description: >-
  The Vakataka kingdom of Vatsagulma governed part of the Deccan from a capital identified
  with modern Washim in Maharashtra. Its separate branch began under Sarvasena around 330,
  following the division of the wider Vakataka realm. Hereditary kings issued land grants
  and worked through ministers and local families. Sarvasena also gained a reputation as a
  poet writing in Prakrit. The branch's inscriptions and copper plates illuminate its
  rulers more clearly than its uncertain beginnings and ending. Under Harishena, the
  kingdom expanded its influence and supported the Buddhist monasteries and paintings at
  Ajanta, notably through royal officials and dependants. Government weakened after his
  reign, but the identities and fate of his successors remain poorly known. Around 510 is
  an estimated endpoint here.
