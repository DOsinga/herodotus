description: >-
  The Republic of Artsakh was an Armenian governed state in Nagorno-Karabakh that controlled
  territory from 1991 to 2023 without international recognition. Its capital was
  Stepanakert, within land internationally recognised as part of Azerbaijan. It emerged
  during the Soviet Union's collapse and the first Nagorno-Karabakh war, establishing its
  own institutions with extensive Armenian support. Government included an elected
  legislature and a presidency, becoming a fully presidential system in 2017. The first war
  left it controlling surrounding Azerbaijani districts as well as most of Nagorno-Karabakh.
  Defeat in 2020 sharply reduced that territory. Following blockade and Azerbaijan's
  September 2023 offensive, its forces surrendered and almost the entire Armenian population
  fled. Territorial government ended, although later disputes continued over the status of
  institutions abroad.
