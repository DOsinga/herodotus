description: >-
  The Sahrawi Arab Democratic Republic was proclaimed in 1976 as Spain withdrew from Western Sahara.
  Led by the Polisario Front, it claims the former colony but governs only territory east
  of Morocco's defensive wall and Sahrawi refugee camps near Tindouf in Algeria. Its institutions include
  a president, cabinet, courts and a national council, with the independence movement closely tied to
  government. Tifariti is its declared provisional capital, while much administration takes place at Rabuni. War
  with Morocco and Mauritania shaped its beginnings; Mauritania later abandoned its territorial claim. The republic
  belongs to the African Union but has limited international recognition. Its survival reflects an unresolved
  struggle over self determination, displacement and competing claims to sovereignty in the western Sahara today.
