description: >-
  The Duchy of Urbino was a state in the northern Marche, between the Apennines and the
  Adriatic, ruled first by the Montefeltro and then by the della Rovere. The Montefeltro had
  been lords of Urbino since the thirteenth century, and the pope made Oddantonio duke in 1443.
  His brother Federico, a famous condottiero, built the ducal palace and gathered one of the
  great libraries and courts of the Renaissance. Cesare Borgia seized the duchy in 1502 and
  Lorenzo de' Medici held it from 1516 to 1519, but the della Rovere came back each time and
  moved the capital to Pesaro in 1523. The duchy stayed formally a papal fief. When the last
  duke died without an heir in 1631, Pope Urban VIII annexed it.
