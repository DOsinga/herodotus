description: >-
  The Banu Isam were a Berber dynasty that ruled Ceuta, on the African shore of the Strait of
  Gibraltar, for four generations, recorded here from about 850 to 931. The old Byzantine port
  of Septem had been destroyed in the Berber Revolt around 740 and lay largely in ruins. In
  the middle of the ninth century Majakas, chief of the Majkasa tribe, resettled it, and his
  son Isam gave the dynasty its name. The family governed the town and its harbour, the
  crossing point between Morocco and al-Andalus, and in time recognised the Idrisids as
  overlords. When the Idrisids lost Fez, the last ruler, Rida ibn Isam, surrendered Ceuta in
  931 to Abd al-Rahman III, the new caliph of Córdoba.
