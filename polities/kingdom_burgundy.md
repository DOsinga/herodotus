description: >-
  The united Kingdom of Burgundy existed under its own royal government from 933 to 1032.
  It joined Upper and Lower Burgundy under Rudolph II, linking lands around the western
  Alps, the Rhône valley and Provence. Arles represents the realm's southern royal centre,
  although authority also depended on other towns, bishops and regional nobles. Kings of
  the Welf family governed this territory between the French and German kingdoms. Conrad
  the Peaceful maintained the monarchy through much of the tenth century, but his
  successor Rudolph III faced growing pressure from powerful aristocrats and neighbouring
  rulers. Rudolph died without an heir in 1032. Emperor Conrad II inherited the kingdom,
  ending its separate royal court. Burgundy survived afterward as an imperial title and
  region.
