name: "Naiman Khanate"
wikipedia: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Naimans
description: >-
  The Naimans were a powerful people of western Mongolia whose khanate held the Altai Mountains
  and the lands between the Irtysh in the west and the Tamir River in the east during the twelfth
  century. Their name means eight in the Turkic and Mongolic languages, and scholars still debate
  which of the two they spoke. Many Naimans were Nestorian Christians, and their court used the
  Uyghur script and employed Uyghur scribes. They acknowledged the Qara Khitai as overlords until
  about 1175, and later split between the rival khans Tayang Khan and his brother Buyruq. In 1204
  Temüjin, the future Genghis Khan, defeated Tayang Khan, and Tayang's son Kuchlug fled west,
  where he later seized the throne of the Qara Khitai.
