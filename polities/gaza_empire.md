name: Gaza Empire
description: >-
  The Gaza Empire was a kingdom founded in 1824 by Soshangane and his Nguni followers in
  southeastern Africa. It incorporated communities across southern Mozambique and
  neighbouring parts of present day Zimbabwe, building authority through conquest, tribute,
  and military power. Regiments and the adoption of Nguni customs helped unite a population
  that included Tsonga, Ndau, Shona, and other peoples, often under coercive conditions.
  After Soshangane's death, a succession struggle brought Mzila to power. His son Gungunhana
  shifted the royal centre south to Mandlakazi. Relations with Portuguese settlements
  alternated between negotiation and conflict as Portugal pressed inland. Portuguese forces
  captured Gungunhana in 1895, ending the royal government represented here, although
  resistance and the final suppression of Gaza forces continued beyond that date.
