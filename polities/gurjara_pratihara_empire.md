description: >-
  The Gurjara Pratihara Empire was a northern Indian monarchy lasting approximately from
  730 to 1036. Its rulers first built power in western India and Malwa, then established
  their court at Kannauj. Nagabhata I resisted Arab expansion from Sindh, while later
  kings competed with the Palas and Rashtrakutas for control of the northern plains.
  Under Mihira Bhoja and Mahendrapala I, the empire reached its greatest strength,
  relying on regional rulers who acknowledged the imperial court. Royal patronage
  produced notable temples, sculpture, and Sanskrit learning. Succession struggles,
  Rashtrakuta attacks, and increasingly independent subordinate dynasties gradually
  reduced its territory. Mahmud of Ghazni's attack on Kannauj in 1018 further weakened
  the court. A smaller Pratihara government continued until its final ruler disappeared
  around 1036.
