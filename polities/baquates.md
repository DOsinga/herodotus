description: >-
  The Baquates were a Berber people of Mauretania Tingitana in northern Morocco, recorded here
  as a chiefdom centred on Volubilis from 285 to 429. Inscriptions at Volubilis record their
  princes renewing peace and federate status with Roman governors from the second century
  until 280, and some of their rulers held Roman citizenship. They probably lived in the
  Middle Atlas and the plains around Volubilis, wintering in the valleys. When Rome withdrew
  from the interior of Tingitana around 285 and kept only the coast around Tingis, Lixus and
  Septem, Volubilis passed to local tribes, of whom the Baquates are the best known. The town
  kept its Latin-speaking Christian community. Later writers linked them with the Barghawata.
