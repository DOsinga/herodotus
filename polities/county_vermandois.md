description: >-
  The County of Vermandois was a feudal county in Picardy, centred on Saint-Quentin and
  Péronne, recorded here from 896 to 1213. Its counts descended from Charlemagne through
  Pepin, son of Bernard of Italy. Herbert II built a powerful network of counties in northern
  France and held King Charles the Simple prisoner at Péronne until the king's death in 929.
  Later counts were less significant, and around 1080 the county passed by marriage to Hugh
  the Great, brother of King Philip I and a leader of the First Crusade. His son Ralph I added
  Amiens and served as seneschal of France. Philip of Alsace, count of Flanders, held the
  county through his wife, and after his death in 1191 the crown took it piece by piece.
