description: >-
  The Gujarat Sultanate ruled much of western India from 1407 to 1573 under Muslim
  sultans of the Muzaffarid family. Its founder, Muzaffar Shah I, had governed Gujarat
  for Delhi before taking a royal title as Delhi's authority weakened. Ahmad Shah I
  founded Ahmedabad in 1411, making it a centre of government and architecture.
  Coastal ports connected the kingdom to Indian Ocean commerce, while rulers fought
  neighbouring states and local chiefs. Mahmud Begada conquered Junagadh and
  Champaner, establishing another royal capital. Bahadur Shah expanded into Malwa but
  faced both Mughal invasion and Portuguese pressure along the coast. Court rivalries
  weakened later rule. Akbar conquered Gujarat in 1573, incorporating its wealthy
  cities, agricultural lands, and trading ports into the expanding Mughal Empire.
