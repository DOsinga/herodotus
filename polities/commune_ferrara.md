description: >-
  The Commune of Ferrara was the self-governing city state of Ferrara on the Po delta,
  from Matilda of Canossa's conquest of 1101 until the citizens made Obizzo II d'Este
  their lord in 1264. After Matilda's death in 1115 consuls governed the city, which
  controlled the river trade between the Adriatic and the Lombard plain and built its
  cathedral in 1135. Two clans fought over the commune: the Ghibelline Torelli, led in the
  thirteenth century by Salinguerra, and the Guelph Adelardi, whose heirs the Este took
  over their cause. Azzo VII d'Este defeated Salinguerra in 1240 with Venetian and papal
  help and dominated the city afterwards. His grandson's acclamation in 1264 began the
  Este state that ruled Ferrara until 1598.
