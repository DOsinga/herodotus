description: >-
  North Macedonia is a landlocked Balkan republic centered on Skopje, independent since 1991. It emerged peacefully from socialist Yugoslavia after a referendum, although disputes over recognition complicated its early years. Parliament chooses the government, while an elected president serves as head of state. Macedonians form the largest population group, alongside a substantial Albanian community and several smaller minorities. Armed conflict in 2001 ended with the Ohrid Agreement, which expanded minority rights and changed arrangements for local government. A long dispute with Greece over the country's name was settled through the Prespa Agreement, leading to the name North Macedonia in 2019. NATO membership followed in 2020, while relations with neighboring states have continued to shape its path toward European Union membership.
