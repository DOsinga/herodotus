description: >-
  Southern Han was the Liu family's state on China's southern coast, based at Guangzhou
  and covering much of Guangdong and Guangxi. Its separate regional government followed
  the Tang collapse in 907, although Liu Yan proclaimed an imperial state called Yue only
  in 917 and renamed it Han in 918. The rulers claimed a connection with the ancient Han
  dynasty through their shared surname. Guangzhou served as the court's capital and a
  major port linking southern China with overseas commerce. The state also sought control
  over northern Vietnam, but its invasion ended in defeat at the Bạch Đằng River in 938.
  Palace politics and the influence of eunuchs shaped later rule. Song forces conquered
  the kingdom in 971 during China's reunification.
