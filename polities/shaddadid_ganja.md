description: >-
  The Shaddadid Emirate of Ganja was a Muslim monarchy in Arran, in the southern Caucasus, founded when Lashkari captured Ganja from the Sallarids in 971. Its Kurdish ruling family expanded towards Shamkir, Barda, and Armenian lands while competing and sometimes cooperating with surrounding Christian and Muslim powers. Fadl I and Abu'l-Aswar strengthened the emirate, although warfare and dynastic disputes repeatedly threatened stability. Court patronage included the Persian poet Qatran Tabrizi, whose praise preserves glimpses of its rulers and campaigns. Seljuk expansion made the emirs tributaries, but dynastic government continued. In 1075, Malik Shah removed the last ruler and transferred control to a Seljuk officer. The Shaddadid branch at Ani survived separately and does not extend the history of this government.
