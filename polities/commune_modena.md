description: >-
  The Commune of Modena was the self-governing city state of Modena on the Via Emilia,
  from the death of Matilda of Canossa in 1115 until the Este took the city for good in
  1336. Its consuls built the Romanesque cathedral and fought a long border war with
  Bologna, which the Modenese won at Zappolino in 1325 and remembered in the story of the
  stolen bucket. The city was usually Ghibelline. In 1288 it gave itself to Obizzo II
  d'Este, but threw the Este out in 1306. Passerino Bonacolsi of Mantua ruled it from 1312
  to 1327, and papal and Bohemian lords followed. In 1336 the Este returned, and Modena
  stayed with them until the eighteenth century and beyond.
