description: >-
  Austrasia was the eastern Frankish royal realm, centred on the Meuse, Moselle, and Rhine
  regions, with courts at Reims and Metz. It emerged from the division of Clovis's kingdom in 511
  and repeatedly alternated between separate government and reunion with other Frankish lands.
  Merovingian kings ruled through aristocratic households, bishops, and officials; mayors of the
  palace gradually gained much of the practical power. Rivalries with Neustria and disputes
  within the royal family shaped its changing boundaries and succession. This record includes
  only periods when an eastern court governed separately, rather than the whole history of
  Austrasia as a region. The last interval followed Pepin's death in 714. Charles Martel ended
  that separate government by reunifying the Frankish realms in 719.
