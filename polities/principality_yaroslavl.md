description: >-
  The Principality of Yaroslavl was a Rus' principality on the upper Volga with its capital at
  Yaroslavl. It separated from Vladimir-Suzdal in 1218, when the sons of Konstantin Vsevolodovich
  divided his lands and Vsevolod Konstantinovich received the territory around Yaroslavl on both
  banks of the Volga, reaching north to the Sheksna and Lake Kubenskoye. The Mongols sacked the
  city in 1238, and Vsevolod was killed at the Battle of the Sit River the same year. Under
  Mongol overlordship its princes paid tribute to the Golden Horde, and in 1262 the townspeople
  killed the Tatar tribute collectors. In the fourteenth and fifteenth centuries the principality
  split into small appanages whose princes increasingly served Moscow, which absorbed it in 1463.
