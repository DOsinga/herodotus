description: >-
  The Tuvan People's Republic existed from 1921 to 1944 in the mountainous country northwest of
  Mongolia, with its capital at Kyzyl. Emerging from the upheavals after Qing rule and the
  Russian Revolution, it established a socialist republic governed through a ruling party and
  People's Khural. Only the Soviet Union and Mongolia formally recognized its independence.
  Soviet influence strengthened during the 1930s as communist leaders displaced older elites,
  restricted Buddhism and shamanism, and pursued collectivization. The republic developed its own
  distinct currency and written Tuvan language, first using a Latin alphabet and then Cyrillic.
  It supported the Soviet war effort against Germany. In 1944 its parliament formally requested
  incorporation into the Soviet Union, and Tuva became an autonomous region within Russia.
