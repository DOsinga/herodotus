description: >-
  Bharatpur State was a Jat kingdom south of Delhi, in present Rajasthan, ruled by the
  Sinsinwar clan. It grew out of Jat revolts against Mughal taxation in the late seventeenth
  century, and Badan Singh founded it as a state in 1722. His son Suraj Mal built the fortress
  city of Bharatpur, the palaces of Deeg and a large army, and in 1761 took Agra with its
  Mughal fort. At his height he held much of the country between Delhi, Agra and Mathura.
  After his death in 1763 the Marathas and the Mughal general Najaf Khan recovered much of
  this land. Bharatpur withstood a British siege in 1805. In 1826 the East India Company
  stormed the city and installed a ruler under its protection, making Bharatpur a princely
  state.
