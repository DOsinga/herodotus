description: >-
  The Nayakas of Keladi ruled the wooded hills and coast of western Karnataka, with successive
  capitals at Keladi, Ikkeri, and Bidanur. Their family first gained power in 1499 as
  governors under Vijayanagara. Following that empire's defeat at Talikota in 1565, they
  gradually established independent government. The monarchy controlled routes between the
  Western Ghats and Arabian Sea ports, drawing wealth from agriculture and trade. Its rulers
  fought neighbouring kingdoms and negotiated with Portuguese merchants. Shivappa Nayaka
  expanded coastal control and reorganised revenue collection, while Queen Chennamma sheltered
  the Maratha ruler Rajaram against Mughal pressure. Temples, forts, and Kannada literary
  patronage marked their rule. Hyder Ali's conquest in 1763 ended the kingdom and brought its
  lands under the expanding state of Mysore.
