description: >-
  The Kingdom of Cilicia was a native state in southeastern Anatolia, ruled from Tarsus after
  the Assyrian Empire collapsed at the end of the seventh century BCE. Its kings, several of
  whom the Greeks knew by the name or title Syennesis, held the fertile Cilician plain and
  the passes between Anatolia and Syria. One Syennesis helped the Babylonians make peace
  between the Medes and Lydians in 585 BCE. Cilicia backed Cyrus the Great against Lydia
  and from about 542 BCE was a vassal of the Achaemenid Empire, but its kings kept their
  throne, ran the country and acted as satraps for the Persians. That ended when the last
  Syennesis sided with the revolt of Cyrus the Younger, after which Artaxerxes II made
  Cilicia an ordinary satrapy in 401 BCE.
