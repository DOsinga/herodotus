description: >-
  Tyrconnell was a Gaelic kingdom in northwestern Ireland, centred on present County
  Donegal, from roughly the fifth century to 1607. Its name means the land of Conall,
  recalling Conall Gulban, the traditional ancestor of the Cenel Conaill. Kingship belonged
  to ruling families and followed Gaelic succession practices rather than automatic
  inheritance by the eldest son. The O'Donnells became its leading dynasty, governing from
  Donegal while maintaining alliances and rivalries with neighbouring Irish lords. Their
  struggle with the O'Neills of Tyrone alternated competition with cooperation against
  English expansion. Hugh Roe O'Donnell joined the Nine Years' War but defeat weakened
  Gaelic power. Rory O'Donnell's departure in the Flight of the Earls in 1607 ended the
  ruling house's local authority under the crown.
