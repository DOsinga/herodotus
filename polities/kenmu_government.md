description: >-
  The Kenmu government ruled Japan from Kyoto between 1333 and 1336, when Emperor Go-Daigo briefly restored direct imperial government after the fall of the Kamakura shogunate. His victory depended on warriors including Ashikaga Takauji and Nitta Yoshisada, who turned against the military regime. Go-Daigo sought to rebuild the authority of the court, appointing nobles and officials while settling competing claims to confiscated lands. Rewards, taxes, and disputes over provincial offices disappointed many samurai whose support the restoration needed. Takauji became the focus of this discontent and broke with the emperor. Fighting in 1336 divided authority between the emerging Ashikaga government and Go-Daigo's Southern Court, opening a long period of rival imperial courts and renewed military rule based on warrior support.
