description: >-
  The Jolof Empire was a monarchy and confederacy centered in present Senegal,
  conventionally dating from around 1360 until 1549. Its ruler, the buurba Jolof,
  claimed authority over constituent kingdoms whose own rulers retained local power.
  Wolof, Serer, and Fula communities formed part of its population. Traditions credit
  Ndiadiane Ndiaye with founding the union, though stories about his origins and the
  empire's earliest years remain uncertain. Jolof emerged as Mali's influence weakened
  and expanded across the lands between the Senegal and Gambia rivers. Contact with
  Portuguese traders from the fifteenth century increased coastal commerce, including
  horses and enslaved people. In 1549, Cayor defeated Jolof at Danki and broke away.
  Other members followed, leaving the smaller Jolof kingdom as its surviving successor.
