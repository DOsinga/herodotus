description: >-
  Moghulistan emerged in 1347 from the eastern lands of the Chagatai Khanate, covering
  steppe and mountain country around the Tian Shan and parts of the Tarim Basin.
  Dughlat leaders placed Tughlugh Timur on the throne, establishing a khanate ruled by
  descendants of Chagatai alongside powerful tribal chiefs. Almaliq was a royal
  centre. Tughlugh converted to Islam and briefly invaded Transoxiana, but lasting
  control there escaped his successors. Civil wars and Timur's campaigns weakened the
  state, while caravan trade linked its rulers with Ming China. During the fifteenth
  century, conflicts with Oirats, Uzbeks, and rival princes continued. After Yunus Khan
  died in 1487, separate western and eastern governments carried on the dynasty's rule,
  including the khanate centred on Turpan further east.
