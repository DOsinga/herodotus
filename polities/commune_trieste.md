description: >-
  The Commune of Trieste was the self-governing city of Trieste at the head of the
  Adriatic, from the thirteenth century until Venice took it in 1369. Its bishops had held
  the city's comital rights since the tenth century, but sold them to the citizens step by
  step, and by 1295 the commune governed itself fully under a podestà and councils.
  Trieste's small port competed with Venice, which treated it as a rival to be kept in
  check, and the two fought repeatedly. In 1369 Venice besieged and took the city. Trieste
  was handed to the patriarch of Aquileia after the War of Chioggia, and in 1382 it placed
  itself under Leopold III of Austria, beginning five centuries of Habsburg rule.
