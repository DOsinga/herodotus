description: >-
  Broach State was the small kingdom of the nawabs of Broach, the old port of Bharuch at the mouth
  of the Narmada in southern Gujarat. Its rulers began as Mughal officials and in 1736 made
  themselves masters of the town and its district as imperial power in Gujarat fell apart. They
  governed the port and the surrounding cotton country for over three decades, while the Gaekwads
  and the Peshwa took a share of the revenue and pressed them from all sides. The cotton trade
  drew the attention of the East India Company, whose factory at Broach was older than the state.
  A British force attacked in 1771 and took the town in November 1772. The last nawab's family
  received a hereditary pension instead of a kingdom.
