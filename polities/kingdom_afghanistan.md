description: >-
  The Kingdom of Afghanistan governed from Kabul between 1926 and 1973, beginning when
  Amanullah Khan adopted the title of king. He pursued educational, legal and social
  reforms, but opposition helped trigger rebellion and his departure in 1929. After a
  brief upheaval, Nadir Shah restored royal government, followed by his son Zahir Shah in
  1933. The monarchy balanced central authority with tribal and religious interests while
  developing roads, schools and national institutions. Afghanistan remained neutral
  during the Second World War and later accepted development assistance from both Cold
  War blocs. The 1964 constitution introduced a limited parliamentary framework without
  removing the king. In July 1973, Mohammad Daoud Khan then overthrew his cousin Zahir
  Shah and replaced the monarchy with a republic.
