description: >-
  Airgíalla was an Irish overkingdom and confederation in central and southern Ulster,
  represented here from about 600 to 1585. Its foundation by the Three Collas in 331
  belongs largely to legend rather than secure history. Several smaller kingdoms
  recognized an overking, usually drawn from the strongest ruling family, while preserving
  local authority. The confederation's lands included areas around Armagh and Clogher, but
  its reach changed greatly over time. Northern Uí Néill expansion imposed overlordship
  without immediately removing its own rulers. Donnchad Ua Cerbaill strengthened the
  kingdom in the twelfth century. Later, Mac Mathghamhna rulers maintained a reduced
  kingdom around Monaghan. In 1585, surrender and regrant to the English crown and the
  creation of County Monaghan conventionally ended its separate existence.
