description: >-
  Luxembourg is a small European grand duchy between Belgium, France, and Germany,
  governed from Luxembourg City. This record begins in 1839, when the Treaty of London
  divided the earlier grand duchy and established separate administration within its
  present borders. It shared a monarch with the Netherlands until inheritance rules
  ended the personal union in 1890. A constitution and parliament limited the grand
  duke's political role. Steelmaking transformed its economy, and later financial
  services became especially important. Germany invaded during both world wars; Nazi
  control interrupted its government from 1940 until liberation in 1944. Luxembourg
  subsequently helped found European integration institutions and abandoned its earlier
  neutrality. It remains a parliamentary constitutional monarchy, with Luxembourgish,
  French, and German used in public life.
