description: >-
  The Banate of Bosnia was a state in the mountainous western Balkans, governed by rulers bearing
  the title ban. Its recorded history begins with Borić in 1154 and ends with Tvrtko's assumption
  of kingship in 1377. Hungarian and Byzantine rulers claimed authority over Bosnia, but its bans often
  managed their own affairs. Kulin promoted trade with Dubrovnik, while disputes about the Bosnian Church drew
  pressure from neighbouring Catholic powers. Local nobles remained important partners and rivals of the ruling families.
  Under Stephen II Kotromanić, Bosnia expanded into Hum and other adjoining regions, gaining access to the
  Adriatic. Tvrtko survived internal challenges and continued that expansion. His coronation transformed the banate into the
  Kingdom of Bosnia rather than ending Bosnian government.
