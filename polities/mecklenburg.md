description: >-
  Mecklenburg occupied the Baltic coast of northeastern Germany and grew from the lands of the
  Slavic Obotrites. In 1167 Henry the Lion restored Prince Pribislav as a vassal, allowing a
  native ruling family to continue after conquest. His descendants adopted Christianity and
  presided over German settlement, towns, and changing relations with neighboring powers. They
  received ducal rank in 1348 within the Holy Roman Empire. The dynasty repeatedly divided its
  inheritance, and the branches at Werle, Rostock and Stargard are recorded separately until their
  lands returned to the main line. Schwerin was the main seat. The division of 1621 between
  Schwerin and Güstrow ended the united duchy represented here.
