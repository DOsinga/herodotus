description: >-
  The Zaian Confederation was a union of Berber tribes in the Middle Atlas of Morocco around
  Khénifra, recorded here as a chiefdom from 1728 to 1914. The Zayanes, divided into tribes
  such as the Ait Harkat and Ait Bouhou, moved their flocks between the mountains in summer
  and the milder plain of Azaghar in winter. After the death of Moulay Ismail in 1727 the
  sultans lost the garrisons that had held the Middle Atlas, and the tribes governed
  themselves outside the government's control. In 1877 Sultan Hassan I recognised Moha ou
  Hammou Zayani as qaid, and he built the confederation into the strongest power of the
  region. France took Khénifra in 1914, and the Zaian War continued in the mountains until
  1921.
