description: >-
  The Socialist Republic of Vietnam was established in 1976 by the reunification of North
  and South Vietnam after decades of war. It occupies the eastern side of mainland Southeast
  Asia, with Hanoi as its capital and Ho Chi Minh City as its commercial centre. The
  Communist Party directs a socialist state through institutions including the presidency,
  government, and National Assembly. Early reconstruction brought collective farming,
  economic hardship, and large refugee movements. Vietnam intervened in Cambodia in 1978 and
  fought a border war with China in 1979. From 1986, Đổi Mới reforms encouraged markets,
  private enterprise, and foreign investment without ending party rule. Manufacturing and
  international trade expanded. This record follows the republic, not earlier Vietnamese
  kingdoms or divided wartime governments.
