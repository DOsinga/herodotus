description: >-
  The First Philippine Republic was established at Malolos in January 1899, following the
  revolution against Spanish colonial rule. Its constitution created a republic with a
  president, cabinet, representative assembly, and separate judiciary, with Emilio Aguinaldo
  as president. Leaders sought international recognition and claimed sovereignty over the
  Philippines, although their actual control was uneven and some regions remained outside
  their authority. Spain had ceded the islands to the United States without Filipino
  consent, bringing the new republic into conflict with another colonial power. War began in
  February 1899. American advances forced the government to leave Malolos and move, while
  resistance shifted toward guerrilla warfare. Aguinaldo's capture and subsequent submission
  in 1901 ended the republic's central government, though armed opposition continued
  elsewhere.
