description: >-
  The Italian Republic governed parts of northern Italy, chiefly Lombardy and Romagna, from 1802 to 1805, with Milan as its capital. It succeeded the Cisalpine Republic under a new constitution that made Napoleon Bonaparte president. Vice president Francesco Melzi d'Eril handled much of the administration, while electoral colleges representing property owners, scholars, and merchants selected political institutions. The republic remained tied to France but maintained its own officials and diplomatic activity. Its government organized police and military forces, introduced the metric system, and negotiated a concordat with the papacy. Agriculture and silk production supported the economy. In 1805 Napoleon transformed the republic into the Kingdom of Italy, ruling through his viceroy Eugène de Beauharnais and ending this separate republican government.
