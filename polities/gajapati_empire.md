name: Gajapati Empire
description: >-
  The Gajapati Empire was a Hindu monarchy based in Odisha on India's eastern
  coast from 1434 to 1541. Kapilendra Deva founded its ruling house after the
  Eastern Ganga dynasty, governing from Cuttack, then known as Kataka. His
  armies expanded far beyond Odisha, reaching Bengal in the north and deep into
  southern India. The title Gajapati emphasised the king's association with
  elephants, an important part of its military strength. Royal authority drew on
  organised forces, provincial administration, and the religious prestige of
  Jagannath at Puri. Its rulers supported temples, Odia literature, and
  devotional culture. Succession struggles and wars with Vijayanagara and
  neighbouring sultanates reduced its reach. After Prataprudra's death, court
  violence ended the dynasty and brought the Bhoi house to power.
