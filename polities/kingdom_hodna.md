description: >-
  The Kingdom of Hodna was a Romano-Berber monarchy in the interior of present-day Algeria,
  around the Hodna region and former Roman Mauretania Sitifensis. Its emergence is placed
  approximately in 500, although surviving accounts reveal more about sixth-century warfare than
  its beginnings. The known ruler, Ortaias, operated among other regional kings after Roman
  imperial authority had fragmented. He initially allied with the Byzantines and faced opposition
  from Mastigas and Iaudas, rulers farther west and in the Aures. This was a local kingdom, not
  simply a surviving Roman provincial administration. Its capital and borders remain uncertain.
  Solomon's campaigns probably brought its territory back under Byzantine control around 539,
  ending the separate government represented here, though the evidence does not establish every
  detail.
