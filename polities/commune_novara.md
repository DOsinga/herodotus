description: >-
  The Commune of Novara was the self-governing city state of Novara in eastern Piedmont,
  from the end of the eleventh century until the Visconti took it in 1332. It lay between
  Milan and Vercelli, on the road from Lombardy to the western Alps, and its history was
  shaped by both neighbours. Emperor Henry V burned the city in 1110, and it took the side
  of the Lombard League against Frederick Barbarossa later in the century. Consuls and
  then podestà governed the city and its rice and grain lands, and the Tornielli and
  Brusati families led its rival factions. Milan's Della Torre and Visconti lords drew
  Novara into their orbit in the early fourteenth century, and Giovanni Visconti made the
  city his own in 1332.
