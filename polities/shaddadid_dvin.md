description: >-
  The Shaddadid Emirate of Dvin was a government in the Armenian city of Dvin from 951 to
  953. Muhammad ibn Shaddad, a Kurdish leader, took advantage of disorder in the Sallarid
  realm to establish himself there. The city lay in a contested landscape linking Armenia
  with the Muslim states of Azerbaijan and Arran. Muhammad's authority rested on
  possession of this local centre rather than a united realm covering every territory
  later ruled by his family. Renewed Sallarid pressure removed his government, and he fled
  to the Armenian kingdom of Vaspurakan. His descendants later established themselves at
  Ganja, while another branch ruled Ani. Those later emirates had separate beginnings and
  do not extend this short Dvin episode into the following centuries.
