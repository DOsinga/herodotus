description: >-
  Brycheiniog was a small inland Welsh kingdom around Talgarth and the Brecon Beacons,
  traditionally traced to Brychan in the fifth century. Its early royal history mixes genealogy
  with legend, so the foundation and later divisions remain approximate. A marriage joined its
  ruling family to Dyfed in the seventh century, producing a shared government before separate
  rule returned around the middle of the eighth. Kings balanced threats from neighbouring Welsh
  kingdoms, Mercia and Viking raiders. Elisedd sought Alfred's protection in the ninth century,
  while Æthelflæd captured the royal court at Llangorse in 916. The kingdom eventually divided
  into the cantrefs of Mawr, Selyf and Talgarth around 1045. Its lands later fell to Norman
  conquerors and became the marcher lordship of Brecon.
