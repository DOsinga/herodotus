description: >-
  Umma was a Sumerian city state in southern Mesopotamia, within modern Iraq, active
  here from about 2900 to 2350 BCE. Its rulers bore titles such as ensi and governed an
  agricultural territory sustained by canals and irrigated fields. The city's patron
  deity was Shara. Umma is especially known for its long conflict with neighbouring
  Lagash over the fertile borderland called Guedena. Royal inscriptions describe
  boundary agreements, battles, and competing claims, though much of that evidence
  presents the Lagash side. Lugalzagesi began as Umma's ruler before conquering other
  cities and establishing his wider kingship at Uruk. This change ended Umma's separate
  government around 2350 BCE. The city survived under later empires and became an
  important administrative centre, leaving records on tablets.
