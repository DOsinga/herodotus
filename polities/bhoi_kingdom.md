description: >-
  The Bhoi kingdom was the state of Odisha between the end of the Suryavamsa Gajapatis in 1541
  and the Bengal conquest of 1568, ruled from Cuttack. Govinda Vidyadhara, a general and
  minister of Prataparudra Deva, killed the old king's sons and took the throne and the title
  of Gajapati. His short-lived line faced provincial revolts, a declining treasury and wars
  with Golconda in the south, and palace murders followed under his son and grandsons. In 1559
  or 1560 the minister Mukunda Deva, who traced his descent from the Eastern Chalukyas, killed
  the last Bhoi king and crowned himself. He beat back a Bengal invasion and allied with Akbar,
  but in 1568 the army of Sulaiman Karrani took Cuttack and Mukunda Deva died in battle.
