description: >-
  The Uqaylid Emirate of Mosul was an Arab monarchy in northern Iraq and the
  surrounding Jazira from 990 to 1096. Its rulers came from the Banu Uqayl and belonged
  to the Shiite branch of Islam. They displaced the Hamdanids, initially sharing
  regional power with a Buyid governor before taking Mosul outright in 996. Recognition
  by Buyid rulers and Abbasid caliphs did not prevent the emirs from campaigning
  independently, sometimes raiding toward Baghdad. Qirwash's long reign faced attacks
  by incoming Oghuz Turks and struggles within the ruling family. Under Muslim ibn
  Quraysh, the emirate expanded into Aleppo and Harran, but increasing Seljuk pressure
  narrowed its options. Family disputes and warfare weakened the Mosul branch, which
  the Seljuks finally removed in 1096.
