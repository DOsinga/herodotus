description: >-
  The Seljuk Sultanate of Iraq governed Iraq and western Iran from Hamadan between 1118 and
  1194. It emerged when the larger Seljuk realm divided after Muhammad Tapar's death. Its
  sultans acknowledged the senior position of Sanjar in the east while retaining their own
  court and government. Turkish military leaders and Persian administrators supported a
  monarchy whose authority increasingly depended on powerful atabegs. Rival princes,
  ambitious commanders, and the Abbasid caliphs contested its control, especially around
  Baghdad. The atabegs of Azerbaijan became particularly influential in choosing and
  managing rulers. Tughril III tried to restore royal power but faced the expanding
  Khwarazmian state. In 1194, Tekish defeated and killed him near Ray, ending the western
  Seljuk sultanate and taking over its lands.
