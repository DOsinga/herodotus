description: >-
  Mauritania is an Islamic republic on Africa's Atlantic coast, between the Sahara and
  the Senegal River. Independent from France since 1960, it governs from Nouakchott, a
  city built into a national capital near the coast. Its population includes Arabic
  speaking Moors and Haratin, alongside Pulaar, Soninke and Wolof speaking communities.
  Moktar Ould Daddah established a presidential state that became a single party system.
  War over Western Sahara helped bring his overthrow in 1978, followed by successive
  military governments and later elections. Mining, fishing and livestock have shaped its
  economy, while drought encouraged migration into towns. Slavery and inherited social
  inequality remain central issues despite formal legal abolition in 1981. The country
  continues across its changes of rulers and constitutional arrangements.
