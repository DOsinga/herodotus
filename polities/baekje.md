description: >-
  Baekje was a Korean kingdom in the peninsula's southwest, traditionally founded in 18
  BCE and finally defeated in 663 CE. Its kings governed with powerful noble families
  and a ranked official system. The kingdom grew from communities around the Han River,
  then moved its capital south to Ungjin and, in 538, Sabi. Baekje competed and
  sometimes allied with Goguryeo and Silla, while maintaining close maritime ties with
  China and Japan. Its monks, scholars, and craftspeople helped carry Buddhism and
  continental learning to Japan. Tang and Silla captured Sabi in 660. A restored court
  under Buyeo Pung ruled from Juryu in 661–663, but its defeat at Baekgang ended the
  attempt to regain independence, sending further refugees across the sea to Japan.
