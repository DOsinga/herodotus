description: >-
  Wudu was the state of the Yang family of the Di people in the mountains of southern Gansu,
  China, from 443 to 506, continuing the earlier state of Chouchi. When Northern Wei took
  Chouchi in 443, Yang Wende rallied the Di that same year and ruled from the Wudu area as
  king, taking titles from Liu Song while fighting Wei. His successors kept their own
  government and armies between the northern and southern courts, and family quarrels often
  settled the succession. After Yang Wendu was killed in 477 the realm split: Yang Wenhong
  moved the seat east to Wuxing, while another branch held Yinping. Wuxing kept Wudu until
  Yang Shaoxian lost his state to Northern Wei in 506, after which Wei governed the region
  through its own prefectures.
