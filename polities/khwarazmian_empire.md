description: >-
  The Khwarazmian Empire grew from the lower Amu Darya region into a major power across
  Central Asia, Iran, and Afghanistan during the twelfth and early thirteenth centuries.
  This record begins with Atsiz's accession in 1127 and ends in 1231. Its Anushteginid
  rulers held the title Khwarazmshah, commanding Turkic military forces within a
  Persianate administrative and literary world. Initially subject to stronger neighbors,
  they expanded as Seljuk power weakened, sometimes acknowledging Qara Khitai
  overlordship while governing their own lands. Gurganj was an early capital. Muhammad
  II built an empire, but conflict with Genghis Khan brought devastating Mongol
  invasions from 1219. His son Jalal al-Din continued resistance and ruled surviving
  territories farther west until his defeat and death ended the dynasty's state.
