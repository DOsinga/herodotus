description: >-
  The Ganja Khanate was a khanate in the west of present-day Azerbaijan, centred on the city
  of Ganja, which governed itself from 1747 to 1804. Its khans belonged to the Ziyadoghlu
  branch of the Qajar tribe, who had governed Karabakh for the Safavids. After Nader Shah's
  death Shahverdi Khan took Ganja with Georgian help. Squeezed between Georgia and Karabakh,
  the khans often paid tribute to one or the other and acknowledged Karim Khan Zand and
  later Agha Mohammad Khan Qajar as distant overlords. The city was a centre of trade and
  silk production on the road between Tbilisi and Iran. Javad Khan, who ruled from 1786,
  refused to submit to Russia, and General Tsitsianov stormed Ganja in January 1804, killing
  him and annexing the khanate.
