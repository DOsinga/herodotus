description: >-
  Muslim Malta was the self-governing Muslim community of Malta, centred on the walled
  town of Medina, from the fall of the Kalbid emirate of Sicily around 1053 until Roger II
  of Sicily conquered the island in 1127. Malta had been nearly empty after the Aghlabid
  conquest of 870, and Muslims from Sicily resettled it around 1048, building Medina on
  the site of Roman Melite. When Sicily broke up into rival lordships the island was left
  to govern itself, and its people beat off a Byzantine siege in 1053 and 1054. Roger I
  landed in 1091 and forced the town to surrender and pay tribute, but its Muslim leaders
  kept running the island until his son sent a fleet and took direct control.
