description: >-
  The Kathiri Sultanate was the state of the Kathiri tribe in the Hadhramaut of southern
  Arabia. Badr Bu Tuwairik founded it in 1395, and in 1414 Sultan Ali ibn Umar seized Dhofar,
  on the coast of present-day Oman. At its height the sultans ruled from Dhofar to the edge of
  the Empty Quarter and held ports such as al-Shihr and Mukalla. Feuds within the tribe brought
  in Yafa'i soldiers, and in the nineteenth century the Qu'aiti took Shibam, al-Shihr and the
  coast, leaving the Kathiri an inland state around Seiyun and Tarim. Society was ranked, with
  sayyid families and armed tribesmen at the top, and many Hadhramis traded in Southeast Asia.
  In 1918 the Kathiri accepted as binding on them the Qu'aiti's 1888 protectorate treaty with
  Britain.
