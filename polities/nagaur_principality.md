description: >-
  The principality of Nagaur was a Muslim state in the Rajasthan desert, ruled from the fort
  of Nagaur by a line of Khanzada khans. Shams Khan, a brother of Zafar Khan who founded the
  Gujarat Sultanate, established it between 1405 and 1407 as Tughluq authority collapsed,
  and the town sat on the caravan routes from Gujarat and Sindh to Multan and Delhi. The
  khans relied on Gujarat against their Rajput neighbours. Rana Kumbha of Mewar intervened
  in a succession dispute in the 1450s, took and sacked Nagaur, and defeated the Gujarati
  armies sent to restore it, but the khans returned. In 1534 the Rathore ruler Maldeo of
  Marwar attacked Nagaur, forced Khan Daulat Khan to flee to Ajmer and annexed the
  principality.
