description: >-
  Gengshi Han was the short imperial government established by Liu Xuan in China from 23
  to 25 CE. Rebel leaders of the Lülin movement chose him to restore the Han name during
  the collapse of Wang Mang's Xin regime. His forces captured the old imperial centers,
  and his court moved through Luoyang to Chang'an in 24. Initial recognition across much
  of China concealed weak control over commanders and provincial rulers. Rivalries within
  the government, inconsistent appointments, and military defections undermined the
  restoration. Liu Xiu built an independent power base while the Chimei rebels advanced
  westward. They took Chang'an in 25, forcing Liu Xuan to surrender. He was later killed,
  and his brief government failed to reunify China under its authority.
