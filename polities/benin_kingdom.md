description: >-
  The Kingdom of Benin was an Edo monarchy in what is now southern Nigeria, centred on
  Benin City from 1180 to 1897. It was distinct from the modern Republic of Benin
  farther west. The oba ruled with palace officials, titled chiefs, and institutions
  that organised tribute, crafts, and surrounding communities. Under rulers such as
  Ewuare, the kingdom expanded and developed a capital with extensive earthworks.
  Portuguese contact from the fifteenth century linked Benin to Atlantic trade. Court
  artists produced brass plaques, bronze heads, and carved ivories recording royal
  authority and ritual life. In 1897, a British military expedition conquered the
  capital, exiled Oba Ovonramwen, and looted palace treasures. The monarchy later
  continued as a traditional institution without independent state power.
