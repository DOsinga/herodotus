description: >-
  The early Chola kingdom occupied the fertile Kaveri region of southern India, with Uraiyur and the port of Kaveripattinam as important centres. Its span here, approximately 300 BCE to 250 CE, is estimated because the surviving evidence does not yield a firm royal chronology. Hereditary kings belonged to one of the three leading Tamil royal houses, alongside the Cheras and Pandyas. Sangam poems describe rulers, wars, patronage, and disputes within the Chola family. Karikala is especially prominent, though later stories enlarged his achievements. Agriculture supported the kingdom, while coastal trade linked its ports with wider Indian Ocean networks and Roman commerce. Kalabhra expansion ended this phase of Chola rule; the much later imperial Chola state belongs to a separate period.
