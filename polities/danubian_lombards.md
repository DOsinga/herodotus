description: >-
  The Lombards entered the history of the middle Danube when they moved into Rugiland, north of
  the river in present Lower Austria, after Odoacer destroyed the Rugian kingdom in 487 and 488.
  They next settled the plains called Feld, defeated the Heruli in the first decade of the sixth
  century and became the leading power of the region under kings such as Wacho, Audoin and Alboin.
  After the death of Theoderic in 526 they moved south of the Danube into Pannonia, and around 547
  Justinian granted them Pannonia and Noricum as allies against the Gepids. Audoin defeated the
  Gepids in 551 or 552. Alboin destroyed them with Avar help in 567 and in 568 led his people into
  Italy, leaving Pannonia to the Avars.
