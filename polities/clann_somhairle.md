description: >-
  Somerled, a warlord of mixed Norse and Gaelic descent from Argyll, defeated his brother-in-law
  Godred, king of the Isles, in a sea battle in 1156 and took the southern Hebrides for himself.
  He ruled Argyll, Kintyre and the islands as an independent prince, styled king of the Isles,
  until he was killed invading the Scottish lowlands in 1164. His descendants, Clann Somhairle,
  divided the inheritance into lordships held by the MacDougalls of Lorn, the MacRuairis and the
  MacDonalds of Islay. They governed their islands and galley fleets themselves, owing loose
  allegiance to Norway for the Hebrides and to Scotland for mainland Argyll. The Treaty of Perth
  in 1266 transferred the Hebrides to the Scottish crown, binding them to Scottish kings.
