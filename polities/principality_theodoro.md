description: >-
  Theodoro was a Christian principality in mountainous southern Crimea, centred on the fortified
  plateau of Mangup. Its origins are traced to the aftermath of Constantinople's fall in 1204,
  although the named principality is securely documented much later. Its princes governed a
  population speaking Greek, Crimean Gothic and other languages, with Eastern Orthodox churches
  linking it to the Byzantine world. The ruling court maintained close relations with Trebizond
  and marriage connections beyond Crimea. Tribute to northern powers coexisted with local
  government. Competition with Genoese colonies over coastal access and trade shaped its
  expansion, including the port of Kalamita. Ottoman forces conquered Mangup in 1475 after a
  siege, ending the principality and incorporating its territory into their new administration of
  southern Crimea.
