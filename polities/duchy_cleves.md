description: >-
  Cleves was a territory on the lower Rhine, centred on Kleve in what is now western Germany. This
  record follows its separate government from 1250 until its union with neighbouring lands in 1521.
  Its rulers were counts before Emperor Sigismund raised Cleves to a duchy in 1417. Their possessions
  extended across both banks of the Rhine and into areas now belonging to the Netherlands. Inheritance
  joined Cleves with the County of Mark, increasing the dynasty's influence among the princes of the
  Holy Roman Empire. Its rulers disputed borders and alliances with Guelders, Cologne and other
  neighbours. John III's marriage and inheritance brought Jülich, Berg and Ravensberg together with
  Cleves and Mark, forming the United Duchies rather than abolishing regional identities.
