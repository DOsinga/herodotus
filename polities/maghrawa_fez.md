description: >-
  The Maghrawa Emirate of Fez was the state of a Zenata Berber dynasty that ruled northern
  Morocco from Fez between the late tenth century and the Almoravid conquest. Ziri ibn
  Atiyya won control of Fez by 987 and was named emir of the Maghreb by al-Mansur, the regent
  of the Caliphate of Córdoba. He founded Oujda in 994 and briefly broke with Córdoba in
  997. His successors ruled as hereditary emirs, usually recognising the Umayyad caliphs,
  and fought the rival Banu Ifran, who held Fez in the 1030s. Under Dunas the city grew and
  its trade prospered. Succession quarrels and Hammadid raids weakened the dynasty in the
  1060s, and the Almoravids took Fez around 1069, ending its rule.
