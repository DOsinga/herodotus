description: >-
  The Lycian League was a federation of the cities of Lycia, the mountainous coastal region of
  southwestern Anatolia in present Turkey. Rhodes had controlled Lycia after 188 BCE, but in 168
  BCE Rome declared the Lycians free, and their cities formed a common government. The league had
  an assembly in which larger cities such as Xanthos, Patara, Pinara, Tlos, Myra and Olympos cast
  three votes and smaller ones fewer, a system later praised by the authors of the American
  constitution. A lyciarch led the federation. As a free ally of Rome it kept its own institutions
  through the Roman civil wars, though Brutus sacked Xanthos in 42 BCE. Claudius annexed Lycia as
  a Roman province in 43 CE.
