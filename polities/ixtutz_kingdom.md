description: >-
  Ixtutz was a Maya kingdom in the Poxte River valley of southeastern Petén, Guatemala. Its estimated political period runs from 600 to 900, within the Late Classic era, rather than covering the site's entire occupation. Local kings used their own emblem glyph and commissioned carved monuments, showing a court with a distinct political identity. Aj Yaxjal Baak is named in an inscription recording a ceremony in 780 attended by numerous lords. Ixtutz maintained close connections with southwestern Petén and acknowledged an overlord from the Petexbatun region without losing its local ruler. It competed with neighbouring centres in the Dolores valley. Monument building ceased during the ninth century, activity declined, and the settlement was eventually abandoned during the Terminal Classic period.
