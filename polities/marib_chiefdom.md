description: >-
  Marib, the old capital of the Sabaean kingdom east of Sanaa, lies where the Yemeni highlands
  give way to the desert of the interior. After its great dam failed the oasis shrank, and in
  the Islamic centuries the town and its fields were held by the tribes of the eastern desert
  edge, among them Abida and Murad, and at times by local families of sharifs, descendants of
  the Prophet. This regional record covers the long period when no highland state governed the
  district directly. The Zaydi imams and later the Qasimid state counted Marib among their
  lands, but the tribes ran their own affairs. The record ends when Imam Yahya's state brought
  the district under its control in the early twentieth century.
