description: >-
  The Haudenosaunee Confederacy, also known as the Iroquois or Five Nations, united the
  Mohawk, Oneida, Onondaga, Cayuga and Seneca peoples of present-day upstate New York.
  According to oral tradition the Peacemaker and Hiawatha founded it under the Great Law of
  Peace, which set up a Grand Council of fifty chiefs who met at Onondaga and were chosen by
  the clan mothers of their lineages. Each nation ran its own affairs, while the council
  handled war and diplomacy. Armed through the fur trade, the confederacy dispersed the
  Huron, Erie and other neighbours in the Beaver Wars of the seventeenth century. The
  Tuscarora joined in 1722. The confederacy balanced between France and Britain, but split
  during the American Revolution and lost most of its lands after the war.
