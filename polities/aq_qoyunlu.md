description: >-
  The Aq Qoyunlu, or White Sheep Turkomans, formed a tribal confederation and monarchy that
  ruled from 1378 to 1508. Its first base lay around Diyarbakir in eastern Anatolia, where
  the Bayandur ruling family led groups using seasonal pastures. Under Uzun Hasan, victories
  over the Qara Qoyunlu and Timurids transformed the state into an empire spanning much of
  Iran, Iraq and neighbouring Caucasian lands. Tabriz became its principal court, combining
  Turkoman military leadership with Persian administration and cultural patronage. Alliances
  with Trebizond and European powers accompanied rivalry with the Ottomans, who defeated
  Uzun Hasan in 1473. Succession struggles later fractured the realm. Safavid conquests
  removed its Iranian territories, and the capture of Baghdad in 1508 ended its remaining
  rule in Mesopotamia.
