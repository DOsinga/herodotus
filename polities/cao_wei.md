description: >-
  Cao Wei ruled northern China from 220 to 266 as one of the Three Kingdoms. Cao Pi founded the imperial state by taking the throne from the last Han emperor, building on the territory and administration assembled by his father Cao Cao. Its capital moved from Xuchang to Luoyang. The government retained imperial offices while developing systems for ranking officials and organising agricultural settlements to support armies. Wei competed with Shu Han and Eastern Wu for reunification, and its forces also campaigned in the northeast. The Sima family seized authority after Sima Yi's coup in 249, leaving later Cao emperors increasingly powerless. Wei conquered Shu in 263. In 266, Sima Yan replaced its last emperor and established the Jin dynasty.
