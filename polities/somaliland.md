description: >-
  Somaliland is a self governing republic in the Horn of Africa, centred on Hargeisa and the
  former British Somaliland territory. It declared restored independence in May 1991 as
  Somalia's central government collapsed after civil war. The territory had briefly been
  independent in 1960 before joining the Italian territory to form the Somali Republic.
  Somaliland built its institutions through local peace conferences, combining elected
  offices with a chamber of elders. Its government maintains its own administration,
  security forces and currency, although international recognition remains limited and
  Somalia rejects its independence. Livestock exports, remittances and the port of Berbera
  are important to its economy. Control of eastern areas remains disputed. Its continuing
  existence rests on separate government rather than broad diplomatic recognition.
