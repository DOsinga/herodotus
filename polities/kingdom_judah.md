description: >-
  The Kingdom of Judah was an Iron Age monarchy centered on Jerusalem and the highlands
  west of the Dead Sea, conventionally dated 930–587 BCE. Its royal house traced its descent
  to David. Biblical accounts describe Judah as the southern successor to a united
  Israelite kingdom, although the scale and chronology of its earliest government remain
  debated. Farming villages, fortified towns, seals, and inscriptions illuminate
  its development. Judah survived the destruction of the northern Kingdom of Israel and
  retained its own kings while paying tribute to Assyria, then Babylon. Jerusalem's
  temple became central to its religious identity. Rebellions against Babylon brought
  siege and disaster: Nebuchadnezzar destroyed Jerusalem in 587 BCE, deported many
  inhabitants, and replaced the kingdom with a provincial administration.
