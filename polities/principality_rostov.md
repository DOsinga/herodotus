description: >-
  The Principality of Rostov was one of the oldest principalities of northeastern Rus', centred
  on the town of Rostov on Lake Nero. In 1207 Grand Prince Vsevolod the Big Nest gave Rostov to
  his eldest son Konstantin, and after Konstantin's death in 1218 his sons divided the lands,
  Vasilko keeping Rostov while his brothers founded the principalities of Yaroslavl and Uglich.
  Rostov remained the seat of the senior bishopric of the northeast and a centre of chronicle
  writing. Its princes submitted to the Golden Horde after the Mongol invasion and divided their
  lands into smaller shares, among them Beloozero and Ustyug. From the fourteenth century Moscow
  gained influence over them, and in 1474 Ivan III bought the remaining half of Rostov from its
  princes.
