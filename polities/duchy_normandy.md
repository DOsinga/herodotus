description: >-
  The Duchy of Normandy was a principality in northern France, centred on the lower Seine, that
  grew out of the land King Charles the Simple granted to the Viking leader Rollo in 911. Rollo's
  successors extended it west to the Cotentin and governed it with their own court, coinage,
  castles and church councils, owing only loose homage to the French king. Rouen was the main
  seat, with Caen and Fécamp also favoured. In 1066 Duke William conquered England, and for most
  of the next century and a half the dukes were also English kings, yet Normandy kept its own
  exchequer, justiciars and customs. Philip II of France declared the duchy forfeit and conquered
  it in 1204, after which it became part of the French royal domain.
