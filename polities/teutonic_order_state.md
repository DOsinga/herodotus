name: State of the Teutonic Order
description: >-
  The State of the Teutonic Order developed on the southeastern Baltic coast from 1226 and
  lasted in Prussia until 1525. A Catholic military order governed it through an elected
  grand master and subordinate commanders. Crusading armies conquered the native Prussians,
  while settlers, fortified towns, and castles reshaped the region. Marienburg became the
  order's main seat, symbolizing a government that combined religious vows with territorial
  administration. Expansion brought conflict with Poland and Lithuania. Defeat at Grunwald
  in 1410 and the Thirteen Years' War weakened the state, forcing major territorial losses
  and recognition of Polish overlordship in 1466. In 1525 Grand Master Albert adopted
  Lutheranism and secularized the remaining Prussian territory into a hereditary duchy. The
  Livonian branch continued beyond this transformation.
