description: >-
  Bhopal State was a Muslim principality in the Malwa plateau of central India, founded in
  1707 by Dost Mohammad Khan, a Pashtun soldier of the Mughal army who became a mercenary
  for local chiefs after Aurangzeb's death. He leased Berasia in 1709, took over the Gond
  lands of Rani Kamlapati and the principality of Mangalgarh, and in the early 1720s
  fortified Bhopal and took the title of nawab. The Nizam forced him to accept his
  overlordship in 1724, and after the Battle of Bhopal in 1737 the Marathas collected
  tribute, but his Orakzai descendants kept governing the state. Bhopal accepted British
  protection by treaty in 1818, after the Third Anglo-Maratha War. A line of women rulers
  later governed it for over a century.
