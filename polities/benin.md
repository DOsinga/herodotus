description: >-
  Benin is a West African republic extending inland from the Gulf of Guinea between Togo and Nigeria. It became independent from France in 1960 as Dahomey, with Porto-Novo as its capital and Cotonou as the main economic and governmental centre. Regional rivalries and repeated coups unsettled its first decade. Mathieu Kerekou seized power in 1972, adopted Marxism-Leninism and renamed the country Benin in 1975. Economic crisis eventually led to a national conference and a transition to multiparty elections in 1990. The state continued through these changes of name and regime. Its presidential government oversees a diverse population with Christian, Muslim and traditional religious communities. Cotton farming and commerce through Cotonou's port remain important, linking the country closely to its neighbours.
