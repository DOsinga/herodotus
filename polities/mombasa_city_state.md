description: >-
  Mombasa was a Swahili trading city on an island off the coast of present day Kenya. This
  record follows its medieval government from 1300 until Portuguese control in 1593. Local
  Muslim rulers governed a port connected to Arabia, Persia, India, and other African
  coastal towns. Merchants exported ivory and agricultural produce, while mosques and stone
  buildings reflected a wealthy urban community. Ibn Battuta visited in the fourteenth
  century and described its Muslim inhabitants. Relations with Kilwa, neighboring Malindi,
  and overseas powers shaped its politics. Portuguese expeditions repeatedly attacked the
  city during the sixteenth century, and Ottoman intervention briefly challenged their
  influence. Construction of Fort Jesus in 1593 marked a lasting Portuguese military
  presence and the end of this recorded period.
