description: >-
  The Southern Court of Japan was a rival imperial government based at Yoshino between
  1336 and 1392. Emperor Go Daigo established it after his attempt to restore direct
  imperial rule collapsed and Ashikaga Takauji backed another emperor in Kyoto. Go Daigo
  and his descendants asserted their legitimacy through the imperial regalia and the
  claims of their dynastic line. Loyal warrior families supported their cause, but power
  depended on military alliances and control of scattered territories. Fighting between
  the northern and southern camps continued for decades, with temporary gains unable to
  overturn Ashikaga dominance. In 1392, Go Kameyama surrendered the regalia and accepted
  reunification. The court ceased governing separately, although later Japanese
  historical tradition recognised its emperors as the legitimate line.
