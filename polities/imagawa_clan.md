description: >-
  The Imagawa were the Sengoku daimyo house of Suruga province on the Pacific coast, ruling
  from Sunpu, modern Shizuoka, from the Ōnin War in 1467 until 1569. A branch of the Ashikaga,
  they had held Suruga as military governors since the fourteenth century. Imagawa Ujichika,
  helped by his uncle Hōjō Sōun, won the succession in the 1470s and added Tōtōmi. Ujichika
  issued one of the first Sengoku law codes, and Sunpu became a centre of courtly culture.
  His son Imagawa Yoshimoto extended the house's power into Mikawa and was among the
  strongest lords of the east until Oda Nobunaga killed him at Okehazama in 1560. His heir
  Ujizane lost Mikawa and Tōtōmi to Tokugawa Ieyasu and Suruga to the Takeda.
