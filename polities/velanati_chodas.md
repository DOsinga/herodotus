description: >-
  The Velanati Chodas were a Telugu ruling family of the twelfth century who governed Velanadu,
  around Chandavolu in the Guntur country, and at their height all of Vengi between the Krishna
  and Godavari rivers in coastal Andhra. They began as officers and viceroys of the Chola kings,
  whose name they took as an honorific, and later accepted Western Chalukya overlordship before
  returning to the Chola side. Gonka II, who reigned from 1132 to 1161, fought beside Kulottunga
  II to drive the Chalukyas out of Vengi and ruled from Mahendragiri in the north to Srisailam in
  the south. Under his successors the battle of Palnadu and Kakatiya pressure cost them their
  western lands. Prithviswara ruled the remnant from Pithapuram until Ganapatideva of the
  Kakatiyas killed him in 1207.
