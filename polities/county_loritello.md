description: >-
  The County of Loritello was a Norman lordship along the Adriatic coast of Molise and the
  Abruzzi, held by the counts of Loritello from their conquest of Chieti around 1076 until
  Roger II of Sicily drove them out in 1140. Robert I of Loritello, a nephew of Robert
  Guiscard, began in the hills around Loritello in the 1060s and pushed north up the coast
  against the Lombard counts and the pope, taking Ortona and making Chieti his seat. By
  his death in 1107 his lands reached from Bovino to the Tronto, and he styled himself
  count by the grace of God. His son Robert II resisted Roger II's new kingdom and lost
  his lands in the royal conquest.
