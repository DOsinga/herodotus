description: >-
  The Grand Duchy of Tuscany was a monarchy in central Italy, centred on Florence. Created
  in 1569 for Cosimo I de' Medici, it united the Florentine dominions with most of the
  former Republic of Siena. The Medici court supported arts and learning, while Livorno
  developed as an important port. After the dynasty ended, the Habsburg-Lorraine family
  took over. Grand Duke Leopold introduced major administrative and legal reforms,
  including abolishing torture and capital punishment in 1786. The state was replaced by
  the Kingdom of Etruria in 1801, then restored in 1814 after Napoleon's defeat.
  Revolutionary pressures and the movement for Italian unification eventually displaced
  its rulers. In 1860, Tuscany joined the Kingdom of Sardinia, which soon became the
  Kingdom of Italy.
