description: >-
  Ixkun was a Maya kingdom centred in the upper Mopan valley of present Guatemala,
  represented approximately from 600 to 950. Its capital stood north of modern Dolores
  and governed several subordinate settlements. Monumental buildings, residential
  expansion and carved stelae show its importance during the Late Classic period.
  Inscriptions preserve episodes involving kings known as Eight Skull and Rabbit God K,
  including victories over Sacul and Ucanal in 779 and 780. Relations later changed, and
  a visit by Sacul's ruler in 790 marked an alliance. Ixkun's stelae placed local kings
  within wider networks of war and ceremony. Unfinished building projects and reduced
  activity suggest declining authority after 800, although occupation continued. Its
  final political chronology remains an estimate based on archaeology.
