description: >-
  Pakistan became independent in 1947 through the partition of British India, initially
  comprising western and eastern territories separated by India. Created as a homeland for
  Muslims of the subcontinent, it first retained a constitutional monarch before becoming an
  Islamic republic in 1956. Parliamentary institutions have repeatedly alternated with
  military government. Karachi was the first capital; Islamabad later replaced it as the
  federal seat. The eastern wing became Bangladesh after civil war and Indian intervention
  in 1971, leaving the western state. Relations with India, especially the dispute over
  Kashmir, have strongly shaped security policy. The Indus basin supports its main farming
  and urban regions. Pakistan remains a federal republic whose diverse provinces and
  language communities share a contested national political framework.
