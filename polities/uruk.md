description: >-
  Uruk was a Sumerian city in southern Mesopotamia, now the archaeological site of Warka in
  Iraq. This record follows its independent kingdoms rather than the much longer life of the
  settlement. Kings ruled a city whose temples of Inanna and Anu had long been important
  religious and economic centres. Traditions associated Uruk with Enmerkar and Gilgamesh,
  while inscriptions and king lists document later political struggles. Lugalzagesi united
  much of Sumer from Uruk before Sargon defeated him in 2334 BCE. After Akkadian power
  collapsed, Utu-hengal briefly restored a kingdom, succeeded in 2112 BCE by Ur-Nammu's
  state centred on Ur. A later dynasty founded by Sin-kashid governed from 1865 BCE until
  Larsa conquered it in 1802. Uruk continued under regional empires afterward.
