description: >-
  Imereti was a Georgian kingdom in the western Caucasus, ruled from Kutaisi by a branch of
  the Bagrationi royal family. It emerged as a separate monarchy in 1463 during the breakup
  of the united Georgian kingdom. Its rulers faced powerful local nobles, rival Georgian
  princes and repeated Ottoman intervention. Mingrelia, Guria and Abkhazia increasingly
  escaped royal control, leaving the kings with a smaller and more contested domain. The
  kingdom preserved much of its distinct Georgian language and Orthodox Christian traditions
  while seeking outside support against its enemies. Several kings approached Russia, but
  those contacts did not bring lasting security. Solomon II accepted Russian suzerainty in
  1804, resisted growing interference and was deposed when the Russian Empire annexed Imereti
  in 1810.
