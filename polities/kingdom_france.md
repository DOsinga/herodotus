description: >-
  The Kingdom of France grew from the western Frankish realm, with this record beginning at
  Hugh Capet's accession in 987. Its kings gradually enlarged their direct lands and
  strengthened institutions around Paris, overcoming powerful hereditary regional lords.
  Capetian, Valois, and Bourbon rulers shaped a monarchy that became a major European power.
  The Hundred Years' War tested its survival, while religious wars later divided Catholics and
  Protestants. Royal government became increasingly centralised, especially under Louis XIV,
  though provincial privileges and courts remained important. Overseas expansion and costly
  wars added pressure to royal finances. The Revolution abolished the monarchy in 1792.
  Restored in 1814, it continued through the Bourbon restoration and July Monarchy until the
  revolution of 1848 established the Second Republic.
