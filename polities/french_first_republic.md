description: >-
  The French First Republic governed France from the abolition of monarchy in September
  1792 until Napoleon became emperor in May 1804. Centered on Paris, it passed through
  several forms of government during revolution and war. The National Convention executed Louis
  XVI and fought European coalitions and domestic rebellion. Emergency rule by the
  Committee of Public Safety brought the Terror, with executions and repression
  alongside mass military mobilization. After Robespierre's fall, the Directory
  established a five-member executive and two legislative chambers, but faced financial
  crisis, political violence, and dependence on the army. Napoleon's coup in 1799
  created the Consulate, concentrating power in the First Consul. Republican
  institutions remained in name as his authority grew, before the establishment of the
  First Empire.
