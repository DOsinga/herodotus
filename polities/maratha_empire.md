description: >-
  The Maratha Empire grew from Shivaji's kingdom in western India's Deccan, formally established by
  his coronation at Raigad in 1674. Its early monarchy relied on forts, mobile armies, revenue
  administration, and a council of ministers. Long resistance to Mughal conquest survived Shivaji's
  death and helped weaken Mughal authority. During the eighteenth century, hereditary prime ministers
  called peshwas directed expansion from Pune, while the royal house remained at Satara. Powerful
  Maratha families acquired their own regional bases, turning the wider empire into a loose and often
  divided association. Defeat at Panipat in 1761 checked expansion but did not end Maratha power.
  Rivalries later invited British intervention. The East India Company's victory in 1818 ended the
  peshwa's government and the imperial arrangement.
