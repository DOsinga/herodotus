description: >-
  The Sō were the lords of Tsushima, the island between Kyushu and Korea, who ruled it in their
  own right from the Ōnin War in 1467 until they submitted to Toyotomi Hideyoshi in 1587. The
  family had governed Tsushima as deputies and then as military governors since the thirteenth
  century, and also held land in northern Kyushu until the Shōni and Ōuchi wars drove them
  out. The rocky island grew little rice, and the Sō lived from trade with Korea. Under the
  treaty of 1443 they issued the passes that every Japanese ship needed to trade at the Korean
  ports, and Japanese merchants settled in three Korean harbours until the riots of 1510.
  Hideyoshi confirmed the Sō on Tsushima in 1587 as his vassals.
