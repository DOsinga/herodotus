description: >-
  The Kingdom of Nepal was a Himalayan monarchy centered on Kathmandu from 1768 to 2008. Prithvi
  Narayan Shah, ruler of Gorkha, conquered the fertile Kathmandu Valley and laid the foundation of a
  larger state joining many previously separate hill kingdoms. Expansion brought conflict with Tibet
  and the British East India Company; defeat in the Anglo-Nepalese War cost substantial territory.
  From 1846, hereditary Rana prime ministers exercised power while Shah kings retained the throne.
  Their rule ended in 1951, opening a long struggle between royal authority and representative
  government. The monarchy later imposed the partyless Panchayat system before accepting multiparty
  politics in 1990. Civil war, royal upheaval, and mass protests undermined the institution. An
  elected assembly abolished the monarchy in 2008.
