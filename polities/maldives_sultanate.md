description: >-
  The Sultanate of the Maldive Islands was the Muslim monarchy of the Maldives, an archipelago of
  coral atolls in the Indian Ocean south west of India. It began in 1153, when the last Buddhist
  king, Dhovemi, converted to Islam and took the title of sultan. Six dynasties ruled from Malé,
  governing the scattered islands through atoll chiefs and grew rich on cowrie shells, coir rope and
  dried fish. The Portuguese occupied Malé from 1558 until Muhammad Thakurufaanu drove them out in
  1573. Later the sultans paid respect to the Dutch and then the British in Ceylon, but kept their
  own government. An agreement of 1887 made the islands a British protected state, which left the
  sultan ruling at home but gave Britain their foreign relations.
