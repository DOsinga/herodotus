description: >-
  Chetumal was a Maya province on the coast of present-day northern Belize and southern
  Quintana Roo, with its capital at the city of Chetumal, probably the site now called Santa
  Rita near Corozal. It lay beside Chetumal Bay and controlled a trade in cacao and honey
  that reached along the coast to Honduras. Its last known ruler, Nachan Kan, governed
  during the Spanish conquest with the help of Gonzalo Guerrero, a shipwrecked Spanish
  sailor who had become a Maya war leader and married into the ruling family. Chetumal drove
  off Alonso de Ávila's attempt to settle there in 1531. In 1544 the brutal expedition of
  Gaspar and Melchor Pacheco conquered the province and attached it to Spanish Yucatán.
