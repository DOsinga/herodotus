description: >-
  Orchha State was a Rajput kingdom of the Bundela clan in Bundelkhand, in present Madhya
  Pradesh. Rudra Pratap Singh founded it in 1531, moving his seat from Garh Kundar to Orchha
  on the Betwa river. His successors resisted the Sur and Mughal rulers until Madhukar Shah
  agreed to pay tribute and serve Akbar. Bir Singh Deo, a favourite of Jahangir, ruled from
  1605 to 1627 at the kingdom's height and built the Jahangir Mahal and the fort of Jhansi.
  When his son Jhujhar Singh rebelled, Shah Jahan's armies occupied Orchha from 1635 to 1641
  before restoring the dynasty. Orchha was one of the few Bundela states the Marathas did not
  subdue. The capital moved to Tikamgarh in 1783, and a treaty of 1812 placed the state under
  British protection.
