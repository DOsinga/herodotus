description: >-
  The Frankish Kingdom of Paris was one of the royal shares created when Clovis's sons
  divided his lands in 511. Childebert I governed from Paris until 558, ruling territories
  beyond the city and expanding through wars and family partitions. He gained lands around
  Orléans and participated in the conquest of Burgundy and campaigns against the Visigoths.
  Royal power rested on the Merovingian household, military followers and ties with bishops,
  rather than a modern border. Childebert founded the church that became Saint Germain des
  Prés. His death allowed Chlothar I to reunite the Frankish kingdom. A new partition in 561
  gave Paris to Charibert I. When Charibert died in 567, his brothers divided his share,
  ending this second Parisian royal government.
