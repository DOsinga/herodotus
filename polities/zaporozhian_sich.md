description: >-
  The Zaporozhian Sich was the fortified camp and self-governing host of the Zaporozhian
  Cossacks on the lower Dnieper, below the rapids, from the 1550s until 1775. Prince Dmytro
  Vyshnevetsky's castle on Little Khortytsia island is usually taken as its start, and the
  Sich later moved between several island and riverbank sites. The Cossacks elected their own kish otaman and officers in a general council, kept
  their own army and raided the Crimean Khanate and the Ottoman coast, often against the
  wishes of their nominal overlords. Polish kings, the Cossack Hetmanate, the Crimean khans
  and the Russian tsars each claimed it at different times. After the Russo-Turkish war of
  1768 to 1774 brought the steppe under Russian control, Catherine the Great had the Sich
  destroyed in 1775.
