description: >-
  The Lakhmid Kingdom was an Arab monarchy centred on al-Hira in southern Iraq, emerging
  around 293 and lasting until 602. Its rulers, commonly called the Nasrids, governed a
  frontier realm between settled Mesopotamia and Arabian tribal territories. They allied
  with the Sasanian kings but maintained their own court, army, and political interests.
  Lakhmid forces guarded Persian approaches, raided rival lands, and fought the Ghassanids,
  Arab allies of the Roman Empire. Al-Hira also became a meeting place for Arabic poetry and
  Christian communities. Evidence for the earliest kings is limited, and the relationship
  between the dynasty and the Lakhm tribe remains debated. In 602, Khosrow II removed the
  last king, al-Numan III, replacing the monarchy with direct administration on Persia's
  frontier.
