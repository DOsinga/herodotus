description: >-
  Cape Verde is an Atlantic island republic off West Africa, independent from Portugal since 1975 and governed from Praia on Santiago. The uninhabited archipelago developed under Portuguese settlement, with African slavery and Atlantic trade shaping its population and Creole culture. Independence followed Portugal's revolution and the efforts of the movement that also liberated Guinea-Bissau. Aristides Pereira led the new state under single-party rule. Plans for union with Guinea-Bissau ended after that country's 1980 coup. Cape Verde introduced multiparty politics in 1990 and held competitive elections in 1991, beginning transfers of power. Dry conditions limit farming, making migration, remittances, services and tourism important to livelihoods. Portuguese remains the official language alongside spoken Cape Verdean Creole. Cape Verde remains an independent republic.
