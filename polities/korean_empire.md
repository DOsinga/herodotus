description: >-
  The Korean Empire was proclaimed in 1897 when King Gojong assumed an imperial title, asserting
  Korea's equal standing with other empires after the end of its tributary relationship with
  China. Centred on Seoul, it retained the Joseon royal house and placed supreme authority in the
  emperor. The Gwangmu reforms sought to strengthen government, military forces, education,
  industry, and communications while preserving monarchical rule. These efforts unfolded amid
  competition between Japan and Russia for influence over Korea. Japan imposed a protectorate in
  1905, taking control of foreign relations, then forced Gojong's abdication and disbanded the
  army in 1907. This record ends with the protectorate; Sunjong remained emperor until annexation
  in 1910. Resistance to Japanese control continued despite the loss of the imperial state.
