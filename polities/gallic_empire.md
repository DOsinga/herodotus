description: >-
  The Gallic Empire was a breakaway Roman state in western Europe from 260 to 274. Postumus
  established it during the third century crisis after rebellion against the imperial government.
  At its greatest extent it included Gaul, Britain, parts of Germania and Hispania. Its emperors
  defended the Rhine frontier and used Roman institutions, titles and coinage rather than
  declaring a separate national kingdom. Cologne served as an early seat, while Trier became the
  main imperial court. Postumus did not attempt to conquer Italy, concentrating instead on the
  western provinces. His assassination in 269 brought rapid changes of ruler and territorial
  losses. Aurelian defeated Tetricus at Châlons in 274 and restored central Roman control, while
  some former officials continued their administrative careers.
