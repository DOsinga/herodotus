name: "Kingdom of Sindh"
description: >-
  The Kingdom of Sindh governed the lower Indus region from an estimated foundation in 489 until
  the Umayyad conquest of 712. Aror, also called Alor, served as its royal centre, while Debal
  connected the river country with maritime trade. The Rai kings were followed around 632 by
  Chach, whose accession replaced the ruling house without creating a territorial state. Much of
  this early history comes from the Chachnama, written centuries later, so its chronology and
  narratives require caution. The population included Buddhist and Hindu communities, and royal
  authority extended through regional governors and local chiefs. The last king, Dahir, was
  defeated by Muhammad bin Qasim. Later appointments of Dahir's descendants did not preserve an
  independent kingdom after the Arab conquest.
