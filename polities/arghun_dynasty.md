description: >-
  The Arghun dynasty was a Turco-Mongol family that ruled Sindh, in present day Pakistan, from 1520
  until the Mughal conquest. Driven from Kandahar by Babur, Shah Beg Arghun conquered Sindh from the
  Samma jams and governed from the island fortress of Bukkur on the Indus. His son Shah Husayn took
  Multan from the Langah sultans in 1528 and sheltered the fugitive Mughal Humayun for a time. After
  Shah Husayn's death in 1554 his general Mirza Isa Tarkhan took power, and the Tarkhan branch ruled
  lower Sindh from Thatta. Portuguese raiders sacked Thatta in 1555 during the struggles between
  them. Akbar's army forced the last Tarkhan, Mirza Jani Beg, to submit in 1591, and Sindh became
  part of the Mughal Empire, with Jani Beg serving as its governor.
