description: >-
  The Caminesi lordship was the state of the da Camino family in the eastern Veneto, from
  Gherardo III da Camino's lordship of Belluno and Feltre in 1266 until his sons lost
  Treviso in 1312. The da Camino were old counts of the hill country around Ceneda and
  leaders of the Guelphs of the March of Treviso. The bishop of Belluno and Feltre made
  Gherardo lord of both towns in 1266, and in 1283 he was elected captain general of
  Treviso with full power. He governed Treviso firmly, kept a famous court that Dante
  praised, and handed it peacefully to his son Rizzardo in 1306. Rizzardo was murdered in
  1312, and his brother Guecellone was soon driven out.
