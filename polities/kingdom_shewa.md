description: >-
  The Kingdom of Shewa was the Amhara state of central Ethiopia ruled by the princes and later
  kings of the Shewan line, recorded here from about 1682. After the Oromo migrations cut Shewa
  off from the empire, Negasi Krestos united the Amhara families who had held out in the highlands
  of Menz. His successors, with the title meridazmach, reconquered Tegulet, Ifat and neighbouring
  districts, and Amha Iyasus made Ankober their capital. Sahle Selassie defeated the Tulama Oromo,
  rebuilt Debre Berhan and proclaimed himself negus. Emperor Tewodros conquered Shewa in 1855.
  Menelik, Sahle Selassie's grandson, escaped captivity in 1865 and restored the kingdom,
  expanding it southwards, until he became emperor of Ethiopia in 1889.
