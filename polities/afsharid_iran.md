description: >-
  Afsharid Iran began in 1736 when Nader Shah replaced the Safavid monarchy after restoring
  Iranian power through years of warfare. From a base associated with Mashhad, he built an empire
  extending across Iran and into neighbouring regions. His campaigns recovered lands from the
  Ottomans, conquered Kandahar, and carried him into India, where the sack of Delhi brought
  immense captured treasure. Government depended heavily on the shah's army and personal command,
  while taxation and constant campaigning strained his subjects. Nader's assassination in 1747
  triggered struggles among relatives and commanders. Most conquests were lost, but his grandson
  Shahrokh retained a much smaller realm around Mashhad. Qajar conquest in 1796 ended that
  surviving Afsharid government, completing the collapse of Nader's once expansive state.
