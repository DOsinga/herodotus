description: >-
  Colmar was a town in upper Alsace, between the Vosges and the Rhine, that grew from a royal
  estate into a free imperial city, recognised as such in 1226. Its council of patricians and
  guildsmen governed the town, which prospered on the wine of the Alsatian hills. In 1354 Colmar
  joined nine other imperial cities of Alsace in the Decapole, a league for mutual defence under
  the imperial bailiff at Haguenau. The city adopted the Reformation in 1575. During the Thirty
  Years' War it was occupied by the Swedes and then placed under French protection. The Peace of
  Westphalia of 1648 left its status ambiguous, and in 1673 Louis XIV took Colmar, razed its walls
  and ended its independence, a conquest confirmed in 1679.
