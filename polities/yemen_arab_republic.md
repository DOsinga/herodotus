description: >-
  The Yemen Arab Republic governed northwestern Yemen from 1962 to 1990, with its capital at
  Sanaa. It began when military officers overthrew the monarchy and proclaimed a republic.
  The deposed imam's supporters fought an eight year civil war against the new government,
  drawing in Egyptian forces and Saudi assistance on opposing sides. Republican victory did
  not end political instability: coups, assassinations and competing military leaders marked
  the following decade. Ali Abdullah Saleh took power in 1978 and built a more durable
  authoritarian presidency through alliances with military and tribal interests. The republic
  maintained relations with both Western countries and the Soviet Union while competing with
  socialist South Yemen. In May 1990 the two states united as the Republic of Yemen.
