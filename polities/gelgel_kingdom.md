description: >-
  Gelgel was the Hindu kingdom that dominated Bali from around the early sixteenth century until 1686,
  with its palace at Gelgel near the island's southeast coast. Its rulers, the Dalem dynasty,
  descended from the Javanese noble whom Majapahit installed after conquering Bali in 1343. As
  Majapahit weakened and fell to Demak, Gelgel governed on its own and took in Hindu courtiers,
  priests and artisans fleeing Java. Under Dalem Baturenggong in the sixteenth century it controlled
  all of Bali and reached into Blambangan in eastern Java, Lombok and western Sumbawa. Its power
  declined in the seventeenth century through wars with Makassar and Mataram and revolts by its own
  lords. After a usurpation the dynasty moved its seat to nearby Klungkung in 1686.
