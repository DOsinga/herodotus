description: >-
  The Garha kingdom, also called Garha-Mandla, was the first large kingdom of the Gond people, in
  the Narmada valley of central India. Its kings ruled from Garha near present day Jabalpur and
  later from Singorgarh, and Sangram Shah extended it over a wide hilly country in the early
  sixteenth century. Queen Durgavati, regent for her son, fell fighting the Mughal general Asaf Khan
  in 1564, and the Mughals took Garha and much of the realm. The dynasty survived on a smaller
  scale, with Mughal recognition, and governed from Chauragarh and then from Ramnagar near Mandla.
  Bundela and Maratha raids cut it down further in the eighteenth century, and in 1781 the Marathas
  of Saugor deposed the last raja and annexed what remained.
