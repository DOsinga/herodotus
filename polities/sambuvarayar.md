description: >-
  The Sambuvarayar were a family of chiefs who governed northern Tondaimandalam, the country
  around Kanchipuram, Vellore and Tiruvannamalai in Tamil Nadu. They first appear as officers of
  the later Chola kings, holding Oyma Nadu near Tindivanam with a small standing force. As the
  Cholas, Hoysalas and Pandyas declined in the early fourteenth century, they ruled on their own,
  and Venrumankonda Sambuvaraya from 1322 and Rajanarayana Sambuvaraya from 1337 dated records by
  their own regnal years. Their seat was Padaividu, guarded by the hill fort of Rajagambhiram,
  and they also held Kanchipuram. Rajanarayana sided with Vijayanagara against the Madurai
  Sultanate, but Kumara Kampana, son of Bukka Raya I, overthrew him around 1361 and took
  Tondaimandalam into the Vijayanagara Empire.
