description: >-
  Worms was an old city on the left bank of the Rhine, a Roman town, Burgundian capital of legend
  and seat of one of the oldest German bishoprics. Its citizens fought the bishops for control of
  the town from the eleventh century, and by the thirteenth century the council governed Worms as
  a free imperial city, though disputes with the bishops went on for centuries. The city hosted
  many imperial diets, among them the Diet of Worms of 1521, at which Martin Luther refused to
  recant and was placed under the imperial ban. Worms adopted the Reformation, and its old Jewish
  community was one of the most important in Germany. French troops destroyed much of the city in
  1689, and revolutionary France took it in 1794.
