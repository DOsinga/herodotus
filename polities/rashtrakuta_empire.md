description: >-
  The Rashtrakuta Empire ruled much of India's Deccan from 753 to 973, eventually centred at
  Manyakheta in present Karnataka. Dantidurga rose against the Badami Chalukyas, and his
  successors expanded across southern and central India. The monarchy governed through
  officials and subordinate rulers, while its armies repeatedly campaigned north against the
  Palas and Gurjara Pratiharas for influence around Kannauj. Royal patronage supported
  Kannada and Sanskrit writing, Jain scholarship, and Hindu temples. Krishna I is associated
  with the great rock-cut Kailasa temple at Ellora. Amoghavarsha's reign made Manyakheta a
  prominent political and literary centre. Later wars and internal weakness eroded imperial
  power. Tailapa seized the government in 973 and founded the Western Chalukya order,
  although Rashtrakuta claimants and other branches survived separately.
