description: >-
  The Duchy of Nassau occupied the Taunus and Westerwald hills of western Germany from 1806 to
  1866. Created by combining Nassau-Usingen and Nassau-Weilburg under Napoleon's reorganization
  of Germany, it joined the Confederation of the Rhine and later the German Confederation. Its
  dukes ruled from Wiesbaden, with a constitution introduced in 1814 and a parliament whose
  electorate remained very narrow. Early reforms abolished serfdom and simplified taxes, while
  arguments over the ruling family's estates exposed limits on parliamentary power. The
  revolutions of 1848 briefly widened political participation before conservative government
  returned. Nassau supported Austria against Prussia in 1866. After Austria's defeat, Prussia
  occupied and annexed the duchy, incorporating its lands into the province of Hesse-Nassau
  rather than preserving a separate state.
