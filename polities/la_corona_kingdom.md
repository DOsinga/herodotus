name: "Kingdom of Sak Nikte"
description: >-
  Sak Nikte, meaning White Flower, was a Maya kingdom whose residence is now called La Corona in
  Guatemala's Petén region. Its court is represented here from approximately 500 to 900, based on
  royal marriages, inscriptions and the wider collapse of Classic Maya governments. Local kings
  maintained close ties with Calakmul, receiving brides from its ruling dynasty and hosting visiting
  rulers. These relationships placed the kingdom within a powerful diplomatic network without erasing
  its own royal line. Sculpted limestone panels preserve names, ceremonies and political events,
  providing rich evidence for a relatively small court. Many panels were looted before archaeologists
  identified their source as La Corona. Excavation later connected the site with the mysterious Site Q
  known from scattered museum reliefs.
