description: >-
  Hospitaller Malta was the state of the Knights of Saint John on Malta and Gozo from 1530
  to 1798. Charles V granted the islands, together with Tripoli in Libya, to the order after it
  lost Rhodes to the Ottomans, in return for a token yearly falcon. The grand master, elected
  for life by the knights, governed with the order's council, while the Maltese had little say.
  Tripoli fell to the Ottomans in 1551, but the knights held Malta through the great siege of
  1565 and then built Valletta as their new capital. Their galleys fought and raided Ottoman
  and Barbary shipping for two centuries, and the order spent heavily on fortifications,
  churches and hospitals. Weakened by the loss of its French estates, it surrendered to
  Napoleon in 1798.
