description: >-
  Manipur was a Meitei kingdom around the Imphal Valley on the frontier between India
  and Burma. This record follows royal government from 1110, when King Loiyumba's
  administrative arrangements helped consolidate the kingdom. Kangla served as its
  royal center. Expansion brought repeated conflict with neighboring hill communities
  and Burmese rulers, while court patronage developed distinctive religious and
  artistic traditions. Vaishnavite Hinduism gained support alongside Meitei practices,
  and Manipuri dance became closely associated with the monarchy. Burmese occupation
  interrupted local rule from 1819 to 1825. Gambhir Singh restored the kingdom with
  British assistance, after which its rulers continued governing under growing outside
  pressure. Defeat in the Anglo-Manipur War of 1891 ended independent government,
  although a princely title survived until India's later incorporation.
