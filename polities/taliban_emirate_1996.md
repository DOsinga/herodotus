  The first Islamic Emirate of Afghanistan was the government of the Taliban, a movement of
  religious students and fighters from the Pashtun south that emerged in Kandahar in 1994 amid the
  civil war between mujahideen factions. The Taliban took Kabul in 1996 and controlled most of the
  country by 2001, while the internationally recognised Islamic State of Afghanistan and its
  Northern Alliance held out in the northeast. The emirate's leader, Mullah Omar, ruled from
  Kandahar, and its government imposed a strict interpretation of Islamic law, banned girls'
  education and most women's work, and destroyed the Bamiyan Buddhas. It sheltered al-Qaeda, and
  after the September 11 attacks an American-led intervention overthrew it in late 2001.
