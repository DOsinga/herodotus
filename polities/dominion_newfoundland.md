description: >-
  The Dominion of Newfoundland was a self-governing British dominion covering the island
  of Newfoundland and, after a 1927 ruling, the coast of Labrador. The colony had received
  responsible government in 1855, and it took the title of dominion in 1907 alongside New
  Zealand. An elected House of Assembly and a prime minister in St. John's ran its affairs,
  while Britain kept control of foreign policy. The economy depended on the cod fishery,
  and the dominion sent its own regiment to the First World War, which suffered heavy losses
  at Beaumont-Hamel. Debts from the war and a railway, followed by the Depression, brought
  it close to bankruptcy. In 1934 its parliament suspended self-government and a commission
  appointed from London took over. Newfoundland joined Canada in 1949.
