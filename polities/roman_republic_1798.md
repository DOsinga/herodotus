name: Roman Republic (1798–1799)
description: >-
  The Roman Republic of 1798 and 1799 was a revolutionary state established in central Italy after
  French troops occupied Rome. Proclaimed on 15 February 1798, it replaced papal temporal government over much
  of the remaining Papal States. A five member executive and legislative bodies adopted republican forms inspired
  by revolutionary France. The republic also incorporated the Tiberina and Anconine revolutionary administrations. Pope Pius VI
  was removed from Rome and eventually taken to France. French military power sustained the new regime,
  while requisitions, financial pressures and resistance limited its support. The wider war against France brought Neapolitan
  intervention and repeated fighting. French withdrawal and the entry of Neapolitan troops in 1799 ended the
  republic, allowing papal government to be restored afterward.
