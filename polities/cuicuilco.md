description: >-
  Cuicuilco was an early settlement in the southern Basin of Mexico, now within Mexico City. Its growth
  into a regional ceremonial centre is estimated from about 800 BCE, though farming communities
  occupied the area earlier. A circular pyramid, plazas, houses and waterworks show organized labour
  and social differences, supporting its treatment here as a chiefdom without identifying rulers.
  Farmers used the surrounding landscape while artisans and traders connected the settlement with
  other parts of Mesoamerica. Cuicuilco developed alongside the centre of Teotihuacan, and their
  relationship included competition for influence. Lava from the Xitle volcano buried much of the site
  and helped bring its regional role to an end. The endpoint around 150 CE remains an estimate amid
  debated eruption chronologies.
