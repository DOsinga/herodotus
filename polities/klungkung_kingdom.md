description: >-
  Klungkung was a Hindu kingdom in southeastern Bali that continued the Gelgel dynasty after its court
  moved to Klungkung, the modern Semarapura, in 1686. Its rulers, the Dewa Agung, held the highest
  rank among the Balinese princes, but by the eighteenth century Bali had split into several kingdoms
  such as Badung, Buleleng, Gianyar and Karangasem, which governed themselves and often fought each
  other and Klungkung. Klungkung kept the island of Nusa Penida and a strong ceremonial position. In
  the nineteenth century the Dutch conquered the northern kingdoms first. Klungkung signed treaties
  with them but kept its own government until 1908, when a Dutch expedition attacked and the Dewa
  Agung and his court died in a ritual puputan.
