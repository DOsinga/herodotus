description: >-
  The Leopoldine Habsburg lands were the share of the Habsburg inheritance given to Duke Leopold
  III by the Treaty of Neuberg in 1379, when he and his brother Albert III divided their lands.
  Leopold received Styria, Carinthia and Carniola, the County of Tyrol and the scattered western
  possessions known as Further Austria, while Albert kept Austria proper. Leopold governed his
  share himself and pursued expansion in the west, where he was killed fighting the Swiss
  Confederates at Sempach in 1386. His sons ruled the lands jointly at first, with William as
  senior duke, but they divided the inheritance among themselves in 1406, creating separate
  governments for Inner Austria and for Tyrol with Further Austria.
