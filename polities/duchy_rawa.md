description: >-
  The Duchy of Rawa was a small Piast duchy in western Masovia, in central Poland, centred on the
  castle town of Rawa. It was created in 1313 when the Duchy of Płock was divided among the sons
  of Bolesław II of Masovia, and Siemowit II received Rawa. Its dukes governed the Rawa land as
  part of the shifting pattern of Masovian partitions, holding their own court while acknowledging
  the growing power of the Polish crown. Siemowit III reunited the Masovian duchies in 1370, but
  after his death in 1381 his sons divided them again and Rawa became a separate share once more,
  held by the Płock line. In 1442 it was joined back to the Duchy of Płock.
