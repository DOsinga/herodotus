description: >-
  The Kadamba Kingdom of Banavasi ruled parts of present Karnataka and the Konkan from
  approximately 345 until 530. Mayurasharma established its monarchy after resisting
  Pallava authority, according to inscriptions that also preserve a dramatic account of
  his transformation from scholar to warrior. Banavasi became a fortified capital and
  trading center. Kings governed through officials, royal relatives, local landholders,
  and village authorities, while using Sanskrit and Kannada in inscriptions.
  Kakusthavarma strengthened the kingdom through marriage alliances with neighboring
  families. A separate Kadamba branch developed at Triparvata, bringing rivalry within
  the ruling house. Around 530, Triparvata's Krishnavarma II conquered Banavasi and
  killed Harivarma. This ended the native Banavasi court, although other Kadamba
  branches continued to rule under later kingdoms for many centuries.
