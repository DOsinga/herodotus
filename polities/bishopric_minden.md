description: >-
  The Prince-Bishopric of Minden was the territory of the bishops of Minden on the Weser in
  northern Westphalia, where the river breaks through the hills at the Porta Westfalica.
  Charlemagne founded the see around 800 during his conquest of the Saxons. Its bishops became
  princes of the Empire and ruled a small territory around Minden, hemmed in by the counts of
  Schaumburg, Hoya and Ravensberg and the Guelph dukes, while the town of Minden governed many of
  its own affairs and joined the Hanseatic League. The land became Lutheran in the sixteenth
  century, under bishops from the Guelph family. The Peace of Westphalia gave it to Brandenburg in
  1648 as the secular Principality of Minden.
