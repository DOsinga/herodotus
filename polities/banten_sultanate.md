description: >-
  Banten was a Muslim sultanate on western Java, established in 1526 after the advance of Islamic rulers into the Sunda kingdom's coastal lands. Its court developed a busy port facing the Sunda Strait and drew wealth from pepper supplied by Java and southern Sumatra. Sultans governed with ministers and maintained commercial contacts with Asian and European merchants. Banten expanded inland, capturing Sunda's capital Pakuan in 1579, but faced growing competition from Dutch Batavia after 1619. Sultan Ageng Tirtayasa sought to defend the kingdom's trading freedom and strengthen its international ties. Conflict with his son Sultan Haji allowed the Dutch East India Company to intervene. The treaty of 1684 subordinated Banten's external affairs to the company, ending its independent government here.
