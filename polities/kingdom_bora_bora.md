description: >-
  The Kingdom of Bora Bora was a Polynesian monarchy in the Leeward Society Islands, covering
  Bora Bora with Tupai, Maupiti and smaller atolls nearby. In the eighteenth century the
  island's warriors under the chief Puni had conquered Tahaa and Raiatea. In the early
  nineteenth century its chiefs joined Pōmare II of Tahiti, accepted Christianity and in 1820
  adopted a written law code. Tapoa II settled on the island as its high chief in the 1830s and
  governed it until 1860, followed by two queens of the Pōmare and Tamatoa families. The Jarnac
  Convention of 1847, in which France and Britain promised not to take the Leeward Islands,
  protected its independence for four decades. France annexed the islands in 1888 and replaced
  the last queen in 1895.
