description: >-
  The Empire of Thessalonica was a Greek monarchy centered on Thessalonica from 1224 to 1246.
  Theodore Komnenos Doukas created it after conquering the Latin kingdom established following
  the Fourth Crusade. His court claimed the Byzantine imperial succession and hoped to recover
  Constantinople, competing with Nicaea and Bulgaria. Expansion through Macedonia and Thrace
  briefly made it a major regional military power. Theodore's defeat and capture at Klokotnitsa
  in 1230 shattered those ambitions. His brother Manuel ruled a reduced state under Bulgarian
  influence, while Epirus escaped its authority. Returning in 1237, Theodore installed his sons
  as rulers. In 1242 the court abandoned its imperial title and acknowledged Nicaean supremacy.
  Nicaea annexed the city in 1246, ending the Greek dynasty's remaining local government.
