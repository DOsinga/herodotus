description: >-
  Jushi was an oasis kingdom in the Turpan basin of present Xinjiang, represented here from about
  100 BCE until 450 CE. Its principal southern urban centre was Jiaohe, built on a plateau between
  river channels. Chinese accounts describe people who combined agriculture with livestock keeping
  and were skilled archers. Local kings faced pressure from both the Han empire and the Xiongnu,
  whose rivalry made the region strategically important. Around 60 BCE, Jushi lands were divided
  into Nearer and Further kingdoms under differing outside influences. This record follows the
  nearer kingdom at Jiaohe; Further Jushi is recorded separately. In 450, rulers of the Northern
  Liang remnant based at Gaochang conquered Jiaohe and destroyed the surviving local Jushi state
  in this region.
