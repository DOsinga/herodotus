description: >-
  Qara Khitai was a Central Asian empire founded by Yelü Dashi in 1124 after the Khitan
  Liao state's defeat in East Asia. Its rulers carried Liao imperial traditions
  westward and established their capital at Balasagun, in present Kyrgyzstan. They
  governed a diverse population through a mixture of direct rule and tribute from local
  rulers, preserving Chinese administrative forms alongside Khitan practices. Their
  victory over the Seljuks at Qatwan in 1141 made them a leading regional power. Muslim
  communities lived under rulers who generally retained Buddhist and other inherited
  religious traditions. Imperial women also exercised substantial authority as regents.
  The Naiman prince Kuchlug seized power in 1211. Mongol forces defeated him in 1218,
  ending the state and absorbing its remaining territories.
