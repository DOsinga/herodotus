  Cambodia is a mainland Southeast Asian state centred on the Mekong River and Tonle Sap,
  with Phnom Penh as its capital. Independence from France in 1953 began the period
  represented here. Norodom Sihanouk dominated early politics before his removal in 1970 and
  the creation of the Khmer Republic. Civil war ended with the Khmer Rouge takeover in 1975,
  whose forced labour, executions, and famine devastated the population. Vietnam overthrew
  that government in 1979, but fighting and international disputes continued for years. A
  peace settlement and United Nations mission led to elections and restoration of the
  monarchy in 1993. Cambodia subsequently developed a parliamentary constitutional framework
  under strong party control. This record groups these different regimes within the
  country's continuing history.
