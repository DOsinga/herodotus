description: >-
  Han Lin'er's Song was the state of the northern Red Turbans on the Huai plain, lasting from
  1355 to 1363 during the collapse of the Yuan dynasty. Han Lin'er's father, the White Lotus
  leader Han Shantong, had claimed descent from the Song emperors before the Yuan executed
  him. In 1355 the rebel commander Liu Futong enthroned the son at Bozhou as emperor of a
  restored Great Song, and Liu held the real power. Song armies raided as far as Shaanxi,
  Shangdu and Korea, set up provinces in the north and took Kaifeng in 1358. The Yuan general
  Chaghan Temur drove them out in 1359, leaving the court at Anfeng. In 1363 troops of Zhang
  Shicheng killed Liu, and Zhu Yuanzhang took Han under his care.
