description: >-
  Goguryeo was a kingdom spanning northern Korea and parts of southern Manchuria,
  traditionally founded in 37 BCE and conquered in 668 CE. Its earliest royal chronology is
  less secure than its later history. Kings and elites expanded the state from the Yalu
  region, defeating neighbouring powers and competing with Chinese dynasties and Korea's other
  kingdoms. Gwanggaeto and Jangsu greatly enlarged its influence; Jangsu moved the capital to
  Pyongyang in 427. Fortified settlements, painted tombs and inscriptions preserve evidence of
  its court, warfare and beliefs. The kingdom resisted Sui invasions and later Tang attacks.
  Internal succession struggles weakened it, and a Tang alliance with Silla captured Pyongyang
  in 668, ending the royal government despite subsequent resistance and efforts to restore it.
