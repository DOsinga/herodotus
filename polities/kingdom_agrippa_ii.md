description: >-
  Agrippa II's kingdom occupied territories northeast of Judea, with Caesarea Philippi as a royal centre, from 53 CE until his death around 92. Claudius granted him Philip's former tetrarchy in exchange for Chalcis, and Nero later added districts in Galilee and Perea. Agrippa was a Herodian king allied to Rome, but retained his government and military forces. He also supervised Jerusalem's Temple and appointed high priests despite not ruling Judea itself. When rebellion against Rome began in 66, he attempted to discourage it and then supported the Roman army. His troops participated in the war that destroyed the Jerusalem Temple. The kingdom survived that conflict under its client monarch. Rome annexed his lands after his death, whose date remains disputed.
