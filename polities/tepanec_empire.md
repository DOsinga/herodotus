description: >-
  The Tepanec Empire grew around Azcapotzalco on the western shore of Lake Texcoco in the
  Valley of Mexico. Represented here from 1200 to 1428, it developed from a local ruling
  community into the region's dominant tributary power. Its rulers allowed subject towns to
  retain their leaders while demanding goods and military service. The Mexica of
  Tenochtitlan initially served within this system. Under Tezozomoc, Tepanec influence
  reached across much of the valley and into surrounding districts, supported by warfare and
  alliances. His death brought a succession struggle that weakened the empire and provoked
  rebellion among former dependants. Tenochtitlan, Texcoco, and Tlacopan defeated
  Azcapotzalco in 1428. Their Triple Alliance then replaced Tepanec dominance with the
  political order usually called the Aztec Empire.
