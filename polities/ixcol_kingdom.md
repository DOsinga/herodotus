description: >-
  Ixcol was one of the political centres of the upper Mopan valley in the Maya lowlands
  of present Guatemala. Its separate government is placed approximately between 600 and
  950, within the Classic and Terminal Classic periods. Archaeological work identifies
  Ixcol alongside Ixkun, Curucuitz, and Ixtonton as major kingdoms in this regional
  landscape. The English article linked here concerns Ixkun, not Ixcol itself, so
  details about Ixkun's monuments, subordinate settlements, and named rulers cannot
  simply be transferred to this neighbour. Ixcol belonged to a wider network of Maya
  communities whose political relationships changed over time. Its own royal succession
  and precise boundaries remain poorly documented in the accessible summary. Both the
  dates and the representative location should therefore be treated cautiously.
