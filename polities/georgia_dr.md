description: >-
  The Democratic Republic of Georgia governed from Tbilisi between 1918 and 1921, emerging
  after the Russian Empire collapsed and the Transcaucasian federation dissolved. Its
  leadership was dominated by Georgian Social Democrats, commonly called Mensheviks, who
  sought a parliamentary, multiparty republic rather than Bolshevik rule. Elections
  produced a constituent assembly, and the government introduced land reform and expanded
  public education. Independence remained precarious amid territorial disputes, conflict
  with neighbouring states, and competing foreign armies in the Caucasus. Germany
  initially offered protection, followed by a British military presence. Soviet Russia
  recognized Georgian independence in a treaty in 1920, but the Red Army invaded the
  following year. Defeat ended the republic's domestic government, while its leaders
  continued their work in exile in France.
