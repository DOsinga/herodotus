description: >-
  The Palestinian National Authority was established in 1994 under the Oslo peace process to
  administer Palestinian affairs in parts of the West Bank and Gaza. It developed a presidency,
  cabinet, civil service, police, and an elected legislative council, with Ramallah becoming its
  administrative centre. Its powers vary between areas defined by the agreements and remain
  constrained by Israeli occupation and control over many borders, movements, and resources.
  Intended as an interim arrangement, it persisted without the promised final settlement. Hamas's
  electoral victory and conflict with Fatah culminated in separate Gaza government in 2007,
  leaving the Authority governing parts of the West Bank. This record describes that
  administration, not unrestricted sovereignty over all Palestinian territory or the wider
  Palestinian national movement.
