description: >-
  The Kamakura shogunate was Japan's first lasting warrior government, based in Kamakura
  from the late twelfth century until 1333. Minamoto no Yoritomo established its authority
  after defeating the Taira, while the emperor and court remained in Kyoto. Military
  governors and estate stewards linked the new administration to its warrior followers
  across the country. After Yoritomo's death, the Hōjō family gained effective power as
  hereditary regents, leaving later shoguns largely ceremonial. The government defeated an
  imperial challenge in 1221 and resisted Mongol invasions in 1274 and 1281. Defence costs
  and difficulties rewarding warriors strained loyalty. Emperor Go-Daigo's uprising gained
  military support, and the capture of Kamakura in 1333 destroyed Hōjō rule, opening the
  short period of restored imperial government that followed.
