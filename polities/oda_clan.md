description: >-
  The Oda clan record covers Oda Nobunaga's domain from his capture of Kiyosu in 1555 until he
  drove the last Ashikaga shogun from Kyoto in 1573. The Oda were deputies of the Shiba
  governors of Owari, divided into rival branches, and Nobunaga's father Nobuhide had built up
  the strongest of them. Nobunaga took Kiyosu in 1555 and had united Owari by 1559. He killed
  Imagawa Yoshimoto at Okehazama in 1560, allied with Tokugawa Ieyasu and conquered Mino in
  1567, moving his seat to Gifu. In 1568 he marched on Kyoto and installed Ashikaga Yoshiaki as
  shogun, then fought a coalition of the Asakura, Azai, Takeda and the Hongan-ji. When he
  expelled Yoshiaki in 1573, his domain became the national regime.
