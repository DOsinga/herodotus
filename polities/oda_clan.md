description: >-
  The Oda clan record covers the Oda deputies who governed lower Owari from Kiyosu after the
  Ōnin War, and then Oda Nobunaga's domain until he drove the last Ashikaga shogun from Kyoto
  in 1573. The Oda served the Shiba governors of Owari, who lost real power during the Ōnin
  War, and split into rival houses at Kiyosu and Iwakura. Oda Nobuhide, of a junior Kiyosu
  line, built up the strongest force in the province from the 1530s. His son Nobunaga took
  Kiyosu in 1555, united Owari by 1559, killed Imagawa Yoshimoto at Okehazama in 1560 and
  conquered Mino in 1567. In 1568 he installed Ashikaga Yoshiaki as shogun in Kyoto, and when
  he expelled Yoshiaki in 1573, his domain became the national regime.
