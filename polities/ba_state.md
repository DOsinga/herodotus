description: >-
  Ba was an ancient kingdom in the upper Yangtze region, centred at different times in eastern Sichuan and neighbouring Hubei. Its beginnings are uncertain; this record places them around 1000 BCE. Rather than a tightly unified bureaucracy, Ba consisted of diverse clans and communities recognising a king. Expansion by Chu pushed its centres westward, with Jiangzhou, now Chongqing, among its seats. Fishing, hunting, and limited cultivation supported communities, while distinctive bronzes and burial practices provide archaeological evidence. Ba warriors also served neighbouring states, and alliances shifted during conflicts among Chu, Shu, and Qin. In 316 BCE, Qin conquered Ba alongside Shu. The royal government ended, though local people and cultural traditions continued within the expanding Qin state and later empires.
