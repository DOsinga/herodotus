description: >-
  The Kingdom of Romania existed from 1881 to 1947, with Bucharest as its main royal capital. It
  developed from the union of Moldavia and Wallachia and their successful struggle for
  independence from Ottoman rule. Carol I became its first king, heading a constitutional
  monarchy with a parliament. After the First World War, the kingdom expanded to include
  Transylvania, Bessarabia and Bukovina, creating a larger and more ethnically diverse state.
  Parliamentary politics eventually gave way to royal dictatorship and then Ion Antonescu's
  military regime. Allied with Nazi Germany, Romania participated in the Holocaust and the war
  against the Soviet Union. King Michael removed Antonescu in 1944, but Soviet influence
  strengthened communist power. His forced abdication in December 1947 ended the monarchy.
