description: >-
  The Emirate of Erzincan was a Turkmen principality in eastern Anatolia, ruled from the town
  of Erzincan on the upper Euphrates. Its emirs had long been autonomous vassals of the
  Eretnids, until Mutahharten took power in 1379, struck his own coins and had the Friday
  sermon read in his name. He drove off an Eretnid campaign and fought Kadi Burhan al-Din of
  Sivas for years, leaning on the Qara Qoyunlu and then on Timur, whose overlordship he
  accepted. Erzincan was largely Christian, and Mutahharten favoured its Christians for the
  sake of trade and drew tribute from Trebizond. Bayezid I seized the town and its emir in
  1401, but Mutahharten was soon restored. After his death in 1403 his grandson held Erzincan
  until the Qara Qoyunlu took it in 1410.
