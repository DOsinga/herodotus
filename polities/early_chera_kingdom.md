description: >-
  The early Chera kingdom occupied parts of present day Kerala and western Tamil Nadu, with
  its approximate span here running from 300 BCE to 250 CE. Karur was an inland royal and
  commercial centre, linked through the Palghat Gap to ports on the Malabar Coast. Chera
  rulers appear in Tamil poetry, inscriptions, coinage, and Mediterranean accounts of Indian
  Ocean trade. Their courts supported poets, fought neighbouring Chola and Pandya rulers,
  and profited from exchanges involving pepper and other goods. Inscriptions near Karur
  identify generations of the Irumporai royal family, while Roman coins reveal distant
  commercial connections. These early kings should not be confused with later Chera
  governments. Their decline belongs to a poorly documented transition associated here with
  Kalabhra expansion.
