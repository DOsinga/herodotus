name: "Southern Yan"
description: >-
  Southern Yan was a short lived imperial state in northern China from 398 to 410, centred
  eventually on Guanggu in present Shandong. Murong De founded it after Northern Wei conquered
  the heartland of Later Yan and separated him from the northern court. A member of the
  Xianbei Murong family, he first ruled from Huatai before securing a base farther east. His
  government drew on the administrative traditions of earlier Yan states and claimed imperial
  authority over Chinese and Xianbei subjects. His nephew Murong Chao succeeded him in 405,
  but court conflict and raids into Eastern Jin territory undermined the kingdom. The Jin
  commander Liu Yu invaded and besieged Guanggu. Its capture in 410 ended Southern Yan, and
  Chao was executed.
