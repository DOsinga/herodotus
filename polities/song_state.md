description: >-
  Song was a hereditary state centered on Shangqiu in present-day Henan, China, governing
  its own affairs here from 770 to 286 BCE. Its earlier foundation followed the Zhou
  conquest of Shang, when surviving members of the defeated royal house received a fief
  where they could preserve ancestral sacrifices. This ancestry gave Song a ritual position
  among Zhou states. Dukes ruled through a noble court and competed with neighboring powers
  during the Spring and Autumn period. Duke Xiang sought leadership among the states but
  suffered defeat against Chu. Song later endured the increasingly destructive struggles of
  the Warring States era. Its final ruler adopted the title king and pursued expansion, but
  Qi, Wei and Chu partitioned the state in 286 BCE.
