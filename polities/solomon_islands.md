  Solomon Islands is an independent Pacific country in Melanesia, established in 1978
  after British protectorate rule. Its capital, Honiara, lies on Guadalcanal, but the
  state includes many islands with distinct languages and local communities. It became
  a constitutional monarchy with the British monarch separately serving as its
  sovereign, represented by a governor-general. An elected parliament supports a prime
  minister and cabinet responsible for government. Early leaders balanced national
  administration with demands for island autonomy. Tensions between communities on
  Guadalcanal and migrants from Malaita escalated into armed conflict around the turn
  of the century. A regional assistance mission arrived in 2003 to restore security and
  support institutions. The country continues independently, with agriculture, fishing,
  logging and external partnerships shaping its economy.
