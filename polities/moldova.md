  Moldova is a landlocked republic between Romania and Ukraine, independent since 1991, with its
  capital at Chișinău. Much of its territory belonged historically to eastern Moldavia before
  Russian annexation, Romanian rule, and incorporation into the Soviet Union. Independence came
  during the Soviet collapse, but conflict over Transnistria left the government without control
  of much of the territory east of the Dniester. Gagauzia received autonomous status within
  Moldova. A parliamentary system combines an elected legislature, president, and government
  headed by a prime minister. Romanian language and cultural ties coexist with communities shaped
  by several other regional traditions. Agriculture and wine production remain distinctive
  economic features. Political debate has repeatedly centred on national identity, relations with
  Russia, and closer integration with Europe.
