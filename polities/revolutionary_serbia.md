description: >-
  Revolutionary Serbia was the insurgent government created during the First Serbian Uprising against Ottoman rule between 1804 and 1813. It grew from resistance to abusive Janissary leaders in the Belgrade region into a struggle for independent political authority. Karađorđe led its armies and dominated its government, alongside local commanders, assemblies and a Governing Council established in 1805. The rebels captured towns and fortresses, built courts and schools, and made Belgrade their main centre. They sought foreign support, especially from Russia, rather than accepting Ottoman offers of limited autonomy. Russian withdrawal from the conflict weakened their position. Ottoman forces reconquered Serbia in 1813, forcing leaders into exile. The uprising nevertheless laid foundations for the renewed revolt and Serbian principality that followed.
