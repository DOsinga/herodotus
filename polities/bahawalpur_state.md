description: >-
  Bahawalpur State was a Muslim principality on the left bank of the Sutlej and the edge of
  the Cholistan desert, in present Pakistani Punjab. Its rulers, the Abbasi nawabs of the
  Daudpotra tribe, came from Shikarpur in Sindh. Bahawal Khan founded the city of Bahawalpur
  in 1748, and his successors settled their kinsmen on new canal lands along the river. The
  state recognised the Durrani kings in name while governing itself, and it kept its
  independence between the Sikh Empire and the emirs of Sindh. A treaty of 1833 opened the
  Indus and Sutlej to British traffic and confirmed the nawab in his lands. A further treaty
  in 1838 bound him to act in subordinate cooperation with the British, making Bahawalpur a
  princely state.
