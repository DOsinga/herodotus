  Grenada is an island state in the southeastern Caribbean, independent of Britain since
  1974. It includes the main island, Carriacou, Petite Martinique and smaller islands, with
  government based at St. George's. Its parliamentary constitution combines an elected
  government with a monarch represented by a governor general. French and British colonial
  rule left an economy shaped by plantations, slavery and spice cultivation, especially
  nutmeg. Independence initially brought continued rule by Eric Gairy. Maurice Bishop's
  revolutionary movement took power in 1979, but internal conflict led to his killing in
  1983 and an invasion led by the United States. Parliamentary elections resumed in 1984.
  The country has continued within the Commonwealth, with tourism and agriculture important
  and hurricanes a recurring threat to development.
