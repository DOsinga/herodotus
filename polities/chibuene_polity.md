description: >-
  Chibuene was an trading settlement on the coast of present Mozambique, south of Vilanculos. This
  record represents its occupation from approximately 600 to 1000, not the later phases of settlement
  at the same site. Archaeological deposits contain local pottery, shell and glass beads, imported
  ceramics, iron fragments, and glass vessels. These finds show connections with the Indian Ocean
  trade network and communities far inland, including the Limpopo basin and Zimbabwe plateau. Glass
  beads passing through coastal settlements helped link farmers and traders across southern Africa.
  Fishing and other coastal resources contributed to local subsistence alongside farming. No evidence
  identifies Chibuene's rulers or system of government. Its importance rests on material traces of
  exchange, rather than a documented kingdom or dynasty.
