description: >-
  Cologne was a self-governing city on the Rhine from 1288 until French occupation in
  1794. Its burghers helped defeat the archbishop at Worringen, securing practical
  political independence while his surrounding ecclesiastical territory remained separate.
  Merchant families initially dominated the city council, before a new constitution in
  1396 gave civic groups a larger role. Cologne prospered through its river harbour,
  manufacturing, and long-distance trade, including strong links with England through the
  Hanseatic League. Its university and churches made it an important centre of learning
  and religion. The emperor formally recognised it as a free imperial city in 1475.
  Cologne remained Catholic during the Reformation. Revolutionary French forces occupied
  it in 1794, ending the council's independent government after centuries of civic rule.
