description: >-
  Connacht was a kingdom west of Ireland's River Shannon, represented here from
  the conventional early king list beginning around 406 until 1474. Its earliest
  rulers and dates belong partly to genealogy and tradition rather than securely
  documented history. Rathcroghan was a royal centre; later kings used other residences
  and inauguration places. Rule depended on leading dynasties, subordinate kings, and succession
  within royal kindreds rather than a fixed modern administration. The Ó Conchobair
  family became dominant, and some of its kings also claimed the high
  kingship of Ireland. Norman settlement and warfare reduced their territory after the
  twelfth century, but did not immediately end their government. The last provincial
  overking died in 1474; smaller Gaelic lordships survived beyond that conventional endpoint.
