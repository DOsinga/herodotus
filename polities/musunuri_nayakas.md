description: >-
  The Musunuri Nayakas were Telugu warrior chiefs who led the revolt against the Delhi Sultanate in
  the former Kakatiya lands of Telangana and Andhra in the fourteenth century. Prolaya Nayaka rose
  around 1330 at Rekapalle in the Godavari hills, and his cousin Kapaya Nayaka, backed by many
  allied chiefs and by the Hoysala king, drove the sultanate's governor out of Warangal in 1336.
  From Warangal Kapaya governed eastern Telangana, kept the sultans' palace and called himself
  sultan of the Andhra country. His rule was soon squeezed by the new Bahmani Sultanate, to which
  he ceded Golconda and the turquoise throne in the treaty of 1364. The Recherla Nayakas, allied
  with the Bahmanis, killed him at Bhimavaram in 1368.
