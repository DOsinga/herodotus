description: >-
  Osroene was a kingdom centred on Edessa, now Şanlıurfa in southeastern Turkey, conventionally
  founded in 132 BCE. Its Abgarid rulers governed an Aramaic speaking region at an important
  junction between Mediterranean and Mesopotamian routes. The court navigated rivalry between
  Rome and Parthia, changing alliances and sometimes fighting its powerful neighbours. Roman
  client status did not always remove the kings' control of their own armies and affairs. Edessa
  became a major centre of Syriac culture and early Christianity, although stories linking King
  Abgar directly with Jesus are legendary rather than established history. Caracalla annexed the
  kingdom in 214. A brief restoration under Abgar X is placed here in 239 to 242, after which
  Roman government replaced the separate royal court again.
