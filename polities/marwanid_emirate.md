description: >-
  The Marwanid Emirate was a Kurdish state in the Diyar Bakr region of Upper Mesopotamia,
  now southeastern Turkey, that governed from about 990 to 1085. Its capital was
  Mayyafariqin, the modern Silvan, and its other main city was Amid, the modern Diyarbakır.
  The dynasty took power after the Kurdish chief Badh, who had seized the region from the
  Buyids and Hamdanids, was killed, and his nephews of the Marwan family carried on his
  rule. Nasr al-Dawla, who reigned for half a century from 1011, made Mayyafariqin a
  prosperous court known for its scholars and buildings. The emirs balanced the Byzantine
  Empire, the Fatimids and the Buyids, and later acknowledged the Seljuks. In 1085 a
  Seljuk army led by Fakhr al-Dawla ibn Jahir, once a Marwanid vizier, ended the emirate.
