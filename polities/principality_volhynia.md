description: >-
  The Principality of Volhynia was a western Rus' monarchy founded in 987, in lands
  now shared by Ukraine, Poland and Belarus. Rurikid princes governed from Volodymyr
  and other regional centers, including Lutsk. Dynastic competition within Rus'
  repeatedly changed its rulers and divided its lands. Roman the Great reunited much
  of Volhynia and joined it with Halych in 1199, forming Galicia-Volhynia. Later,
  struggles between Poland and Lithuania restored a separate Volhynian government
  under Gediminid princes. This record includes its renewed periods from 1349 to
  1392, 1434 to 1438 and 1440 to 1452. Liubartas and later Švitrigaila maintained
  regional courts amid succession conflicts. After Švitrigaila's death in 1452,
  Lithuania absorbed the principality, ending the last interval of separate princely
  government in Volhynia.
