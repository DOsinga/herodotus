description: >-
  The Serbian Empire was a Balkan monarchy proclaimed in 1346 when Stefan Dušan was
  crowned emperor of the Serbs and Greeks at Skopje. Building on the Serbian kingdom,
  Dušan conquered extensive Byzantine lands in Macedonia, Albania, Epirus, and Thessaly.
  His court adopted Byzantine titles and practices, while the Serbian church became a
  patriarchate. Dušan's Code sought to order relations among rulers, nobles, clergy, and
  subjects within this multilingual realm. His death in 1355 left the throne to Stefan
  Uroš V, whose weaker authority allowed provincial lords to govern increasingly on their
  own. Military defeats and competing noble interests hastened fragmentation. Uroš died in
  1371, ending the imperial line and leaving several successor lordships rather than a
  single united Serbian state.
