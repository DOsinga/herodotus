description: >-
  Dacia was a kingdom north of the lower Danube, centred in the Carpathian region of
  present Romania. Burebista brought Dacian and neighbouring communities under his
  authority from around 82 BCE, extending his power towards the Black Sea and central
  Europe. His death in 44 BCE led to divisions, but this record treats the later
  reunified kingdom as continuing the same Dacian state. Royal authority was associated
  with mountain strongholds and religious centres, including Sarmizegetusa Regia.
  Decebalus consolidated power and confronted Rome, first negotiating with Domitian and
  later resisting Trajan's invasions. Fortresses, sanctuaries, metalworking, and
  ancient accounts provide evidence for its society. Roman victory in 106 ended the
  kingdom, and much of its territory became a province of the Roman Empire.
