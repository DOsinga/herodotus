description: >-
  The Salghurids were a Turkmen dynasty of atabegs who governed Fars in southwestern Iran
  from 1148 to 1282. Sunqur ibn Mawdud took Shiraz during the decline of Seljuk power and
  founded a line that ruled as nominal vassals of the Seljuks, then the Khwarazmshahs, and
  from the 1230s the Mongols. By paying tribute and submitting early, Abu Bakr ibn Sa'd
  spared Fars the destruction suffered elsewhere, and Shiraz flourished as a centre of trade
  and learning; the poet Saadi dedicated his Bustan and Gulistan to the family. The atabegs
  also held the Persian Gulf coast and islands for a time. After 1260 disputed successions
  brought growing Mongol interference, and Ilkhanid officials took over the government after
  Abish Khatun married a Mongol prince.
