description: >-
  Wetzlar was a small town on the Lahn in Hesse that Frederick Barbarossa made a free imperial
  city in the late twelfth century. In the thirteenth century it flourished on iron working and
  the cloth trade, and its council governed the town, which was surrounded by the lands of the
  counts of Nassau and Solms and the landgraves of Hesse. A quarrel with the lords of the region
  and heavy debts brought it close to ruin in the 1370s, and it never regained its importance.
  From 1689 to 1806 Wetzlar was the seat of the Imperial Chamber Court, the supreme court of the
  Empire, where the young Goethe served as a trainee lawyer. In 1803 the town was given to Karl
  Theodor von Dalberg.
