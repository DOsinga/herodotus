description: >-
  Jin was a principality based at Taiyuan in the Hedong region of northern China, principally covering
  present Shanxi. Its rulers were the Shatuo military leaders Li Keyong and his son Li Cunxu. Li
  Keyong held a Tang military governorship before receiving the title Prince of Jin in 896, the
  starting point used here for his autonomous government. When Tang collapsed in 907, Jin rejected the
  successor Later Liang and claimed continued loyalty to the former dynasty. Its armies fought for
  territory across northern China, captured Yan in 913, and expanded beyond their base. Li Cunxu
  assumed the imperial title in 923, presenting himself as Tang's rightful successor. This transformed
  Jin into Later Tang, which defeated Later Liang later that year.
