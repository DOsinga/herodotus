name: "Sharabhapuriya Kingdom"
description: >-
  The Sharabhapuriya Kingdom governed part of Dakshina Kosala in central India, mainly present
  day Chhattisgarh and neighbouring Odisha. Its estimated span, 475 to 590, follows a
  reconstruction of the royal succession rather than securely dated founding and conquest events.
  Kings initially associated with the Gupta Empire increasingly exercised their own authority as
  Gupta power declined. Copperplate land grants and a few gold coins provide most of the evidence
  for their rule. The grants name rulers, officials, villages, and Brahmin recipients, revealing
  a monarchy that supported Vishnu worship and used Sanskrit for formal records. Sharabhapura was
  the earlier court, although its location remains disputed; Shripura became the later capital.
  The Panduvamshis succeeded this dynasty, retaining aspects of its inscriptional and traditions.
