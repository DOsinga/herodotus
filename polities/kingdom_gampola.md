description: >-
  The Kingdom of Gampola was a Sinhala monarchy in central Sri Lanka from 1341 to 1408.
  Bhuvanaikabahu IV shifted the royal center from Kurunegala to Gampola, in the hill
  country near modern Kandy. Kings continued to draw authority from Buddhism and custody
  of the sacred tooth relic, while royal relatives and military leaders contested power.
  The Alagakkonara family became especially influential, defending southern interests
  against pressure from the northern kingdom of Jaffna and building a fortified center at
  Kotte. Gampola's era left important religious buildings, including the temples of
  Lankatilaka, Gadaladeniya, and Embekke. Political authority increasingly moved away from
  the original capital toward other regional courts. The kingdom gave way to the rise of
  Kotte in the early fifteenth century.
