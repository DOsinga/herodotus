description: >-
  The Kingdom of Jolof was the rump of the Jolof Empire in the interior of what is now Senegal,
  after its coastal vassals broke away at the battle of Danki in 1549. Its rulers, the buurba,
  were chosen by an electoral college from the royal lineages and governed from successive
  capitals including Ceng, Warkhokh and Yang-Yang. Cut off from Atlantic trade, Jolof was poorer
  than Cayor and Waalo but kept some of its imperial prestige. It fought Muslim reformers from
  Mauritania in the 1670s, went through long civil wars around 1700 and suffered raids by the
  Trarza Moors in the nineteenth century. Reformers from Saloum and Futa Toro converted its
  leaders to Islam in the 1860s and 1870s. France conquered Jolof in 1890.
