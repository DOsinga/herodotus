description: >-
  The Odrysian Kingdom arose in Thrace around 480 BCE, joining communities across parts of present
  Bulgaria, Greece, and European Turkey under powerful kings. Teres and Sitalces expanded its
  influence, drawing tribute from subjects and cultivating relations with Greek cities. Royal
  authority depended on leading families and could fragment among competing rulers. Philip II of
  Macedon conquered the kingdom in the fourth century BCE; this record marks the interruption in
  342. Seuthes III restored a smaller kingdom around 330 and founded Seuthopolis. Later Odrysian
  kings ruled under Hellenistic and then Roman pressure. By the middle of the first century BCE
  the Sapaean kingdom, recorded separately, had taken over royal government in Thrace.
