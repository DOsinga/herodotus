description: >-
  Ndongo was a Mbundu kingdom in the highlands between the Kwanza and Lukala rivers of
  present Angola. Its rulers bore the title ngola, which gave Angola its name. This
  record begins with the kingdom's embassy to Portugal in 1518 and ends with the
  Portuguese capture of Pungo Andongo in 1671. The royal court at Kabasa governed
  through advisers and territorial nobles called sobas, whose tribute and military
  support sustained the monarchy. Relations with neighbouring Kongo and Portuguese
  settlers shifted between negotiation, trade, and war. Portuguese expansion and the
  slave trade disrupted the kingdom. Queen Nzinga resisted Portuguese power while also
  building a base in Matamba. Later rulers struggled against increasing intervention,
  until conquest ended the remaining kingdom's separate political control.
