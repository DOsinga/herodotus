description: >-
  Chan Santa Cruz was the state of the rebel Maya in the east of the Yucatán Peninsula
  during the Caste War. It grew around a shrine founded in 1850, where a cross was said to
  speak and give orders to the faithful, and the town of the same name became its capital.
  Leadership combined military commanders with religious offices tied to the cult of the
  cross, and a garrison system drew men from the surrounding villages. The Cruzob, as its
  followers were called, took the old fortress town of Bacalar in 1858 and traded arms
  and goods with British Honduras. For half a century Mexico could not reconquer it.
  Internal splits and an agreement fixing the border with Britain weakened it, and
  a Mexican army occupied the capital in 1901.
