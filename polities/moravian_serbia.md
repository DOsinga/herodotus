description: >-
  Moravian Serbia was the principality built by Prince Lazar Hrebeljanović around the Morava
  river valleys after the Serbian Empire fragmented in 1371. From Kruševac, Lazar expanded his
  lands through warfare and alliances, becoming the strongest of the Serbian regional rulers. His
  realm included important mining towns such as Novo Brdo and Rudnik, whose wealth supported his
  government. Eastern Orthodox monasteries and refugees from Ottoman advances helped shape its
  cultural life. Lazar died fighting the Ottomans at Kosovo in 1389. His widow Milica and son
  Stefan Lazarević subsequently accepted Ottoman overlordship, supplying troops while retaining
  their own court. After fighting for Bayezid at Ankara, Stefan received the Byzantine title of
  despot in 1402, marking the principality's continuation as the Serbian Despotate.
