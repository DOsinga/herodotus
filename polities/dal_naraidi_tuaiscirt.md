description: >-
  Dal nAraidi in Tuaiscirt was a northern Irish kingdom in Eilne, between the Bann and
  Bush rivers in present County Antrim. It developed after a branch of the Uí Chóelbad
  dynasty settled there following conquest in the middle seventh century. This record
  begins approximately in 650. Its kings belonged to the wider Dal nAraidi political world
  but maintained a distinct northern territory, sometimes winning the overlordship of Dal
  nAraidi and Ulaid. Annals record warfare, royal deaths, and links with neighboring
  powers and churches. Rathveagh represents a place associated with its kings, not a
  securely established permanent capital. The last named northern king appears in 883,
  used here as a last attestation end rather than evidence of a conquest that year.
