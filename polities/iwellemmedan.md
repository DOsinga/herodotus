description: >-
  The Iwellemmedan were one of the great Tuareg confederations of the Sahel, led by a paramount
  chief, the amenokal, chosen by the heads of the noble clans. Established south and east of the
  Adrar des Ifoghas by the seventeenth century, they came to dominate the Niger bend between
  Timbuktu and the Azawagh. Noble clans led the confederation in war, trade and seasonal
  migration, with tributary and servile groups farming and herding under them. The Iwellemmedan
  fought the Arma pashas of Timbuktu and imposed their own indirect rule over the river towns,
  including Gao, in the eighteenth century. They divided into the western Kel Ataram and the
  eastern Kel Dinnik. French columns defeated them in the 1890s, and they formally submitted in
  1903.
