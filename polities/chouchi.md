description: >-
  Chouchi was a small state ruled by the Yang family of the Di people in the mountains of
  present Gansu, China. Its recorded independent periods run from 296 to 371 and from 385 to
  442. Mount Chouchi offered a defensible centre near the upper Han River, between northern
  Chinese powers and the routes towards Sichuan. Yang rulers accepted titles from larger
  dynasties while maintaining their own government, and family struggles repeatedly
  unsettled succession. Former Qin conquered the state in 371. Yang Ding restored it in 385
  as Former Qin collapsed, and later rulers expanded into nearby river valleys. Liu Song
  took control in 442. Later Yang governments at Wudu and Yinping belong to a different
  phase of this regional history.
