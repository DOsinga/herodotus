name: "Dachanghe"
description: >-
  Dachanghe was a monarchy in the Yunnan region of southwestern China from 902 to 928, with its
  capital at Yangjumie, near modern Dali. Chief minister Zheng Maisi founded it by overthrowing
  the last Nanzhao ruler and destroying the former royal family. The new regime inherited
  Nanzhao's territorial government while claiming succession to both Nanzhao and the Tang
  dynasty. Zheng's family ruled through a royal court and senior ministers, maintaining Buddhist
  patronage and diplomatic links with neighbouring states. Zheng Renmin attacked Former Shu in
  914 but suffered defeat, and later missions sought marriage alliances, including one with
  Southern Han. In 928, Yang Ganzhen helped overthrow the Zheng house and establish Datianxing.
  This ended Dachanghe's rule without ending government in the region.
