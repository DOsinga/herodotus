name: "Orontid Armenia"
description: >-
  Orontid Armenia was a kingdom in the Armenian highlands after Alexander's conquests,
  represented here from 321 to approximately 200 BCE. Its royal family had earlier
  served within the Achaemenid Empire, and Iranian traditions remained important
  alongside local Armenian culture. Armavir was a principal royal centre. The kings
  maintained autonomy despite the claims and influence of Alexander's successors,
  especially the Seleucids. Greek inscriptions and court practices reveal growing
  Hellenistic connections without a wholesale replacement of older customs. Related
  Orontid rulers also governed neighbouring territories, which should not all be treated
  as this same kingdom. Antiochus III moved to replace local dynastic rule with his own
  commanders. The removal of the last Orontid around 200 ended this kingdom's
  independent phase in Armenia.
