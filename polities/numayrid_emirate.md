description: >-
  The Numayrid Emirate was an Arab principality in Diyar Mudar, the western part of Upper
  Mesopotamia, ruled by emirs of the Bedouin Banu Numayr tribe. Waththab ibn Sabiq founded
  it in 990, taking Harran, Saruj and Raqqa as Hamdanid power declined, and for a time
  also held Edessa until the Byzantines took it in the early 1030s. Most emirs stayed with
  their tribe in the countryside and left the towns to deputies, often slave soldiers, but
  Mani' ibn Shabib, under whom the emirate was largest, lived in Harran and turned its old
  Sabian temple into a palace. The Numayrids recognised in turn the Abbasids, the Fatimids
  and Byzantium. They lost Raqqa to the Mirdasids in 1062, and in 1081 the Uqaylids of
  Mosul, backed by the Seljuks, took Harran.
