description: >-
  Argos was a Greek city state in the northeastern Peloponnese, ruling the fertile plain of the
  Argolid near the old Mycenaean centres. In the seventh century BCE, under King Pheidon, it was
  one of the strongest powers in Greece. Argos was Sparta's oldest rival for control of the
  Peloponnese, and its defeat at Sepeia around 494 BCE cost it most of its citizen army. It stayed
  neutral during the Persian Wars, later became a democracy and allied with Athens against Sparta.
  In the Hellenistic period tyrants supported by the Macedonian kings ruled the city, and Pyrrhus
  of Epirus died in its streets in 272 BCE. In 229 BCE Argos joined the Achaean League, ending its
  separate government.
