description: >-
  Thaton is traditionally understood as a Mon kingdom centered on a coastal town in Lower
  Burma, now Myanmar. This record places an urban court around 500 and follows the traditional
  endpoint of 1057, while acknowledging that both its antiquity and conquest are disputed. Accounts describe
  a monarchy linked through maritime trade with southern India and Sri Lanka, and associate it
  with the spread of Theravada Buddhism. Later chronicles tell of King Manuha refusing Anawrahta's request
  for Buddhist scriptures, prompting Pagan to conquer Thaton and remove captives and texts northward. Those
  stories were written centuries after the supposed events. Some historians question whether an independent kingdom
  existed at all before Pagan's expansion, so its political history cannot be treated as secure.
