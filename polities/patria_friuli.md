description: >-
  The Patria del Friuli was the temporal state of the patriarchs of Aquileia in Friuli, in
  the far northeast of Italy. Emperor Henry IV granted the patriarch the county of Friuli in
  1077, and later grants added Istria and Carniola. The patriarchs, usually German or Italian
  churchmen chosen with imperial or papal backing, ruled from Cividale and from 1238 from
  Udine. From the thirteenth century they governed with a parliament of nobles, clergy and
  towns, one of the oldest in Europe. The state was squeezed between the counts of Gorizia,
  the Habsburgs and Venice, and its nobles were often at war with each other. Venice conquered
  Friuli in 1418–1420 and ended the patriarchs' temporal rule, though the parliament kept
  meeting under Venetian rule.
