description: >-
  The Saltukid Emirate was a Turkish principality centred on Erzurum in eastern Anatolia,
  which governed the high plateau around the city from about 1072 to 1202. Its founder Emir
  Saltuk was one of the Turkmen commanders of the Seljuk sultan Alp Arslan, and received the
  region after the Byzantine defeat at Manzikert in 1071. At its height the emirate covered
  the present provinces of Erzurum and Bayburt, lands east of Erzincan and much of Kars. Its
  emirs acknowledged the Great Seljuks but ran their own affairs, and fought repeated wars
  with the Kingdom of Georgia over Kars and Ani. Georgian armies besieged Erzurum in 1184.
  The Saltukids left mosques, tombs and a three-domed mausoleum in Erzurum. The Seljuk
  sultan of Rum, Süleymanşah II, annexed the emirate in 1202.
