name: Sikh Empire
description: >-
  The Sikh Empire grew from the confederated Sikh military communities of Punjab after Ranjit Singh
  captured Lahore in 1799. He consolidated a monarchy that extended across Punjab into Kashmir and
  territories toward the Khyber Pass. Lahore served as the political center, while Amritsar retained
  major religious importance. The administration and army included Sikhs, Muslims and Hindus, and
  European officers helped develop infantry and artillery alongside cavalry forces. Ranjit Singh
  balanced powerful chiefs, expanded revenue collection and negotiated with the British East India
  Company. His death in 1839 brought violent succession struggles and increasing army influence. Two
  wars with the Company followed. After the second war, British authorities annexed the empire in
  1849, removing the young Maharaja Duleep Singh from power.
