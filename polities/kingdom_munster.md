description: >-
  Munster was a Gaelic kingdom in southern Ireland, centred on the royal seat of
  Cashel. This record begins around 450, an estimate for early medieval kingship rather
  than the much earlier rulers named in legend. Kings governed through relationships
  with subordinate kingdoms, powerful families and church centres, not a uniform modern
  administration. The Eóganachta dominated much of its early history. Viking
  settlements at Limerick, Waterford and Cork introduced new rivals and trading centres
  from the ninth century. The Dál gCais later displaced the Eóganachta, most famously
  through Brian Boru, who also became Ireland's high king. Rival dynasties and outside
  intervention eventually divided the kingdom. In 1118, Munster split into Thomond
  under the O'Brien family and Desmond under the MacCarthy family.
