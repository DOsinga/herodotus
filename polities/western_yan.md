description: >-
  Western Yan was a short lived state in northern China from 384 to 394, ruled mainly by
  members of the Murong family of the Xianbei. It arose when Former Qin weakened after its
  defeat at the Fei River. Murong Hong rallied displaced Xianbei communities, and Murong
  Chong then captured Chang'an in 385. Their followers wanted to return east rather than
  settle in the conquered capital. A series of murders and rapid successions followed as
  the population moved toward Shanxi. Murong Yong established his court at Zhangzi and
  claimed the imperial title. His claim competed with that of his relative Murong Chui in
  Later Yan. In 394, Later Yan conquered Zhangzi and killed Murong Yong, bringing Western
  Yan to an end.
