description: >-
  The Lordship of the Isles was the domain of the MacDonald lords over the Hebrides and much of
  the western Highland coast of Scotland. John of Islay, descended from Somerled, began styling
  himself Lord of the Isles in 1336 and gathered most of the old Hebridean inheritance through
  marriage and royal grants. The lords held their court and council at Finlaggan on Islay,
  commanded fleets of galleys and patronised Gaelic poetry, law and learning. Although nominally
  vassals of the Scottish crown, they fought their own wars, claimed the earldom of Ross and even
  negotiated with England, as in the Treaty of Westminster-Ardtornish of 1462. James IV declared
  the lordship forfeit in 1493 and absorbed it into the Scottish crown.
