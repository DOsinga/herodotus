description: >-
  Phong Châu was a separate domain ruled by Kiều Công Hãn in the northern Vietnamese heartland
  during the tenth century's struggle among the Twelve Warlords. Its appearance is placed around
  965, after the collapse of central Ngô authority, and its independence ended in 967. Hãn
  controlled the region around modern Việt Trì and Lâm Thao in Phú Thọ province, using the title
  Kiều Tam Chế. He belonged to the Kiều family: his grandfather was Kiều Công Tiễn, and his
  younger brother Kiều Thuận held another domain. The surviving account describes a regional
  military ruler rather than a clearly documented royal administration. Đinh Bộ Lĩnh defeated Hãn
  in 967 while bringing the competing domains under the government of his emerging dynasty.
