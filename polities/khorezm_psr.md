description: >-
  The Khorezm People's Soviet Republic governed the former Khanate of Khiva in Central Asia
  from 1920 to 1924. Its capital remained Khiva, amid the irrigated settlements of the lower
  Amu Darya. The republic followed the khan's overthrow and was formally proclaimed by an
  assembly in April 1920. Revolutionary committees, representative assemblies, and executive
  bodies replaced royal institutions, with communist leaders increasingly shaping
  government. Local reformers worked within a political setting influenced by Soviet Russia,
  while armed resistance complicated efforts to consolidate authority. In 1923, the state
  adopted the name Khorezm Socialist Soviet Republic. The Soviet national delimitation of
  Central Asia dissolved it in 1924. Its territory was divided among Uzbek, Turkmen, and
  Karakalpak units rather than preserved as one republic.
