description: >-
  The Ōtomo were the Sengoku daimyo house of Bungo province in northeastern Kyushu, ruling from
  Funai, modern Ōita, from the Ōnin War in 1467 until 1586. They had held Bungo as military
  governors since the Kamakura period. Ōtomo Yoshiaki extended their power into Higo and
  Chikugo, and his son Ōtomo Sōrin added Buzen and Chikuzen with the port of Hakata after the
  fall of the Ōuchi in 1557, ruling six provinces at his height. Sōrin welcomed Portuguese
  traders and Jesuits and was baptised in 1578, the year the Shimazu crushed his army at
  Mimikawa, after which his vassals fell away. With the Shimazu closing in, Sōrin
  went to Osaka in 1586 to submit to Hideyoshi and ask his help.
