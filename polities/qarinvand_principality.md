description: >-
  The Qarinvand Principality occupied mountainous parts of Tabaristan, south of the Caspian Sea
  in northern Iran. Its beginnings are estimated around 550, when Karen received lands from the
  Sasanian king Khosrow I. Hereditary rulers used the title ispahbadh and claimed descent from
  the noble House of Karen. Mountain strongholds helped them preserve local government after the
  Arab conquest and the fall of neighbouring dynasties. Their religious traditions were
  Zoroastrian, although rulers navigated the Islamic political world. Mazyar gained control with
  Abbasid recognition, then challenged the Tahirids and resisted outside authority. His defeat
  and capture in 839 ended the government represented here. Descendants appear in later accounts,
  but their survival does not establish an uninterrupted principality continuing into the
  eleventh century.
