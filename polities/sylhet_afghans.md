description: >-
  Afghan Sylhet was the domain of the Afghan chiefs who held the Sylhet region of northeastern
  Bengal after the Karrani sultanate fell to the Mughals in 1576, until the Mughal conquest of
  1612. Its best known ruler was Bayazid, often called Bayazid Karrani II, who governed the
  eastern half of Sylhet from the city and from Pratapgarh, with his brother Yaqub. He counted
  among the Baro-Bhuiyan chiefs and sheltered Khwaja Usman when the Mughals drove him from
  Bokainagar in 1611. In 1612 the governor Islam Khan sent Shaikh Kamal against Sylhet while
  his main army fought Usman. Bayazid held out with help from the Kachari king, but after
  Usman's death he surrendered and Sylhet became a Mughal province.
