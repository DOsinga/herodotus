description: >-
  Wu Zhou was the Chinese imperial government established by Wu Zetian in 690 and ended in
  705. Its capital was Luoyang, renamed Shendu, the Divine Capital. Wu had already
  exercised power within Tang government as empress and regent, but her adoption of the
  Zhou name claimed a distinct imperial identity. She ruled as emperor rather than as a
  consort, the only woman to hold that position in China's recognised imperial succession.
  Her government drew legitimacy from Buddhist and Daoist religious imagery, court
  construction, and claims connecting her family to ancient Zhou. Officials were recruited
  beyond established aristocratic networks, although coercion also sustained her
  authority. A palace coup in 705 removed her and restored her son Zhongzong and the Tang
  regime.
