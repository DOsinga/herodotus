description: >-
  The Gepid Kingdom occupied much of the eastern Carpathian Basin, including
  lands in present-day Romania, Hungary, and Serbia, from about 454 to 567. Its
  emergence followed the collapse of Hunnic power, when King Ardaric led former
  subjects of Attila against his sons at Nedao. Gepid kings ruled a Germanic
  population alongside other communities and maintained relations with the
  Eastern Roman Empire, receiving subsidies while pursuing their own interests.
  Sirmium became an important later royal center, although control of the city
  changed repeatedly. Competition with Ostrogoths and Lombards shaped the
  kingdom's history. In 567, a Lombard and Avar alliance destroyed it. Surviving
  Gepids entered neighboring territories or remained under Avar rule, but their
  communities did not preserve an independent Gepid state.
