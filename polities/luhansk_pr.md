description: >-
  The Luhansk People's Republic was a Russian-backed separatist administration in eastern
  Ukraine from 2014 until Russia's claimed annexation in 2022. Armed groups seized buildings
  and settlements amid the unrest following Ukraine's change of government, then proclaimed
  a republic based in Luhansk. It claimed the entire Luhansk oblast but initially controlled
  only part of it. A head of state and People's Council provided its formal governing
  institutions, while military and economic dependence tied it closely to Russia. Fighting
  with Ukrainian forces devastated towns, displaced residents and divided an industrial
  region. Russia recognized it shortly before the full-scale invasion of Ukraine in 2022.
  After a widely rejected referendum, Russia claimed incorporation of the territory; Ukraine
  and most countries continued to reject annexation.
