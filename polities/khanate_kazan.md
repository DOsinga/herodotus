description: >-
  The Khanate of Kazan was a Tatar state on the middle Volga, founded in 1438 as the Golden Horde fragmented. Its capital, Kazan, stood within lands previously associated with Volga Bulgaria. A khan headed the monarchy, but leading nobles and religious figures exercised considerable power through a council. Sunni Islam shaped the court, while the population included Tatars, Chuvash, Mari, Udmurts, and other peoples. Farming, crafts, and river trade sustained its towns. Relations with Moscow alternated between warfare and intervention in royal succession, with rival aristocratic factions favouring Moscow or Crimea. Russian armies repeatedly attempted to impose control. Ivan IV captured Kazan after a siege in 1552, incorporating the khanate into Russia, though resistance continued in the countryside for years.
