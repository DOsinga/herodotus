description: >-
  Rwanda is a landlocked republic in Africa's Great Lakes region, independent from Belgium since 1962. Kigali became the capital of the state after the monarchy had been abolished. Colonial policies and struggles over political power sharpened divisions among Hutu, Tutsi, and Twa communities. The first republic was followed by military takeover and a second authoritarian government in 1973. Civil war began in 1990 when the Rwandan Patriotic Front invaded from Uganda. In 1994, extremist authorities and militias organised the genocide against Tutsi, also killing moderate Hutu. The RPF defeated that government and began a new political order. Reconstruction, justice efforts, and economic development followed, alongside restrictions on opposition and involvement in neighbouring Congo's wars. Rwanda has continued as a republic.
