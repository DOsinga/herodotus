description: >-
  Bundi State was a Rajput kingdom of the Hada branch of the Chauhans in the Hadoti region of
  present Rajasthan. Rao Deva took Bundi from the Meena and Bhil tribes in 1242 and made it
  his capital. For about two centuries the Hadas served the Sisodias of Mewar as vassals. In
  1569 Rao Surjan Singh surrendered the fortress of Ranthambore to Akbar, and the rulers
  became Mughal nobles who led their troops in imperial wars while governing Bundi themselves.
  Rao Ratan Singh gave Kota to his son in 1631, creating a separate state. Rao Chhatar Sal
  died fighting for Dara Shikoh at Samugarh in 1658. Ravaged by Maratha and Pindari raids,
  Bundi made a treaty with the British in 1818 that placed it under their protection.
