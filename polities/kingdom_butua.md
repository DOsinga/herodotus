description: >-
  The Kingdom of Butua, or Butwa, was a Kalanga state of the Torwa dynasty in what is now
  southwestern Zimbabwe, from about 1425 to about 1683. It arose as Great Zimbabwe declined
  and trade routes moved, and its capital at Khami, near the goldfields, was built with
  terraced stone walls in the tradition of Great Zimbabwe. The mambo ruled with a court of
  officials and drew wealth from cattle and from gold traded to Arab and later Portuguese
  merchants. Butua was a rival and sometimes an ally of Mutapa. Civil war divided the royal
  family in the 1640s, and the king regained power with Portuguese help. Around 1683 the Rozvi
  leader Changamire Dombo conquered the kingdom and made its lands the core of the Rozvi
  Empire.
