description: >-
  The Yadava kingdom of Bayana was ruled by a Jadaun Rajput dynasty from the fort of Bayana,
  also called Shripatha or Thangir, in the Braj country south-west of Mathura. Tradition
  makes its founder Vijayapala a ruler who came from Mathura and built the fort around 1040,
  and a Mathura inscription of 1150 names a king Vijayapala. Under Timanpala, who built the
  nearby fort of Tahangarh, the kingdom spread over Mathura, Alwar, Bharatpur, Dholpur and
  the Chambal ravines, between the Tomaras and Chahamanas of Delhi and the Kachchhapaghatas
  of Gwalior. In 1196 Muhammad of Ghor and Qutb al-Din Aibak took Bayana and Tahangarh from
  Kumarapala. His descendants later founded Karauli, which became a separate princely state.
