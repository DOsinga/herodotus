description: >-
  Western Shu was a short-lived kingdom in Sichuan, southwestern China, governed from
  Chengdu between 405 and 413. It arose when soldiers serving the Eastern Jin resisted an
  expedition far from their homes and forced the officer Qiao Zong to lead their mutiny.
  After killing the provincial governor and his brothers, the rebels established Qiao as
  Prince of Chengdu. His government relied heavily on relatives, including the military
  commander Qiao Daofu. Seeking support against Jin, Qiao acknowledged Later Qin, whose
  ruler Yao Xing granted him the title Prince of Shu in 409. The alliance did not secure the
  kingdom's survival. Liu Yu's general Zhu Lingshi invaded in 413, took Chengdu and restored
  Jin authority; Qiao fled and killed himself after defeat.
