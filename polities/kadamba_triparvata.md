description: >-
  The Kadamba kingdom of Triparvata was a separately governing branch of the early
  Kadamba realm in present Karnataka, southern India. Its beginning around 455 is an
  estimate linked to Krishnavarma I receiving the southern part of the kingdom. The
  capital's exact location remains disputed. Hereditary kings governed in a setting of
  rivalry with the Kadambas of Banavasi, the Pallavas, and other regional powers.
  Inscriptions issued by Krishnavarma's descendants describe royal achievements,
  religious patronage, and claims of authority, although parts of the succession remain
  unclear. Pallava pressure affected the branch's independence and alliances.
  Krishnavarma II conquered Banavasi around 530, reuniting the Kadamba lands. Early
  Chalukya expansion then ended its separate rule around 540, with alternative
  chronologies placing the final conquest later.
