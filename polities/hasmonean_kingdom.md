description: >-
  The Hasmonean Kingdom was a Jewish state centred on Jerusalem, recorded from Simon's recognition
  as ruler in 140 BCE to Herod's conquest in 37 BCE. It grew out of the Maccabean struggle against
  Seleucid rule. Its hereditary rulers combined political leadership with the high priesthood, and
  later adopted the title king. John Hyrcanus and Alexander Jannaeus expanded its territory into
  neighbouring regions, incorporating communities with different religious traditions. Internal
  religious conflicts and rival claims to succession weakened the monarchy. Pompey captured
  Jerusalem in 63 BCE, leaving a tributary state under Hyrcanus II rather than ending local
  government outright. Parthian intervention installed Antigonus as king. Roman backed Herod then
  took Jerusalem in 37 BCE, replacing the Hasmonean government with his own.
