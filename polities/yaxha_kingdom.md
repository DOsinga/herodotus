description: >-
  Yaxha was a Maya kingdom beside Lake Yaxha in northern Guatemala's Petén region. Its
  recorded span runs approximately from 300 BCE to 900 CE, marking a Preclassic governing
  center and the later disappearance of Classic royal institutions rather than the whole
  history of settlement. Temples, palaces, causeways and carved monuments reveal a
  substantial city connected to trade across the lakes region. Its emblem glyph and royal
  monuments distinguish its rulers from neighboring kingdoms. Early Classic imagery shows
  influence from distant Teotihuacan, while later architecture included a twin pyramid
  complex. Yaxha fought repeatedly with Naranjo, whose king captured and sacrificed a Yaxha
  ruler in 710. Settlement continued beyond the royal period, but nearby Topoxte's later
  government belongs to a different history.
