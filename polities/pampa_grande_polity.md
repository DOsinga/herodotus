description: >-
  Pampa Grande was a late Moche governing centre in the Lambayeque valley of northern
  Peru, represented here from approximately 650 to 750. Its rapid growth brought a large
  population together around monumental adobe platforms, especially Huaca Fortaleza, with
  residential compounds and workshops spread across the surrounding city. Excavated
  buildings, storage areas, and craft production suggest elites organized labour and
  controlled important resources, though rulers' names and precise offices are unknown.
  Irrigated farming supplied maize, beans, cotton, and other crops, while specialists made
  pottery, textiles, and metal objects. This was a northern regional centre, not a
  government for every Moche settlement. Burning and abandonment ended its main
  occupation. The date remains debated, with some chronologies placing that collapse
  closer to 700.
