description: >-
  Aidhne was an Irish kingdom in southern County Galway, between Galway Bay, the Burren and the
  Slieve Aughty hills. Its territory broadly matched the later diocese of Kilmacduagh. The Uí
  Fiachrach ruling community emerged in the early historical period; the beginning around 450 is
  an estimate rather than a recorded foundation. Its kings included figures who also competed for
  the kingship of Connacht. By the eighth century Aidhne's rulers had lost much of their
  influence, but local kingship continued under Connacht's stronger dynasties. Families including
  the O Hynes, O Shaughnessys and O Clearys belonged to this political landscape. Anglo Norman
  expansion under the de Burghs broke up the wider kingdom around 1267, while smaller native
  lordships continued governing separate areas.
