description: >-
  Sumedang Larang was a Sundanese principality in the Parahyangan highlands of West Java, with its
  seat at Kutamaya near the modern town of Sumedang. Local tradition traces its rulers back to the
  eighth century, but it only stands out as a state of its own in the late sixteenth century. Its
  rulers converted to Islam under Prince Santri and kept close ties with Cirebon. When Banten
  destroyed the Sunda Kingdom in 1579, the Sundanese crown regalia were carried to Sumedang, and
  King Geusan Ulun claimed the inheritance of Pajajaran over the eastern Sundanese lands. He broke
  with Cirebon in 1585. In 1620 his successor submitted to Sultan Agung of Mataram, and the Priangan
  was afterwards governed by regents under Mataram.
