description: >-
  Ur was an Early Dynastic Sumerian city state in southern Mesopotamia, near the former
  coast of the Persian Gulf, represented here from 2900 to 2350 BCE. Its kings and
  temples drew wealth from farming, trade, and connections reaching far beyond the
  region. The moon god Nanna was central to the city's religious life. Surviving
  inscriptions and the Sumerian King List identify rulers such as Mesannepada, although
  the sequence of dynasties remains uncertain. The Royal Cemetery contains burials,
  jewellery, musical instruments, and imported stones, revealing powerful elites and
  skilled craftspeople. Ur competed with other Sumerian cities and sometimes fell under
  their authority. Lugalzagesi ultimately brought it into his realm. The later empire of
  Ur's Third Dynasty is a separate historical entity.
