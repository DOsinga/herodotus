description: >-
  The Kalhora dynasty ruled Sindh, in present day Pakistan, in the eighteenth century. The Kalhoras
  began as leaders of a Sufi following in upper Sindh, and from 1701 Mian Yar Muhammad Kalhoro
  governed the country around Khudabad with Mughal titles. His successors extended their rule down
  the Indus and took over Thatta from the last Mughal governor in 1737. After Nadir Shah's invasion
  of 1739 they paid tribute first to Persia and then to the Durrani Afghans, but they ran Sindh
  themselves and dug many of its irrigation canals. Mian Ghulam Shah Kalhoro moved the capital to
  the new city of Hyderabad in 1768. Quarrels with the Talpur Baloch, whose leaders the Kalhoras had
  killed, led to the battle of Halani in 1783, which brought the Talpurs to power.
