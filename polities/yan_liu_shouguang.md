description: >-
  Liu Shouguang's Yan was a state around modern Beijing at the start of the Five Dynasties,
  lasting from 907 to 913. Liu's father Liu Rengong had governed the Lulong circuit from You
  Prefecture since 895. In 907, while Later Liang troops attacked the city, Liu Shouguang
  drove them off, imprisoned his father and took power. He accepted Later Liang titles as a
  nominal vassal, defeated his brother Liu Shouwen and added the Yichang circuit around
  Cangzhou. In 911 he proclaimed himself emperor of Yan, and his cruelty earned the state the
  nickname Jie Yan, after a tyrant of legend. Jin armies under Zhou Dewei took the Yan
  cities one by one, and You Prefecture fell at the end of 913. Liu was executed early in
  914.
