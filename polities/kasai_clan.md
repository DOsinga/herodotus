description: >-
  The Kasai were a warrior house that governed several districts of central Mutsu province in
  northeastern Honshu, from the Kitakami valley around Hiraizumi to the Sanriku coast, until
  Toyotomi Hideyoshi dispossessed them in 1590. Their founder Kasai Kiyoshige, a retainer of
  Minamoto no Yoritomo from the Kantō, received lands here after the fall of the Northern
  Fujiwara in 1189, and his descendants settled in the north. Their seat moved from Ishinomaki
  to Teraike in Tome district. In the Sengoku period they fought a long war with the
  neighbouring Ōsaki and drew close to the Date, who sent them heirs, at the cost of quarrels
  among their retainers. Kasai Harunobu failed to join Hideyoshi at Odawara in 1590, and his
  lands were confiscated.
