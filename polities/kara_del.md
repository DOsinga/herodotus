description: >-
  Kara Del was a kingdom centred on the oasis of Hami, in eastern Xinjiang, that lasted from
  1389 to 1513. The Buddhist Chagatayid prince Gunashiri broke with the Northern Yuan in 1389
  and established himself at Hami, ruling a mostly Uyghur population. Ming armies soon forced
  him to submit, and from 1404 his successors held the Ming title of Obedient Prince while
  Hami was counted as a Ming guard. The Ming did not tax or govern the oasis directly, and
  its princes also bowed at times to the Oirats. From the 1460s succession disputes and
  attacks by the rulers of Turpan unsettled the kingdom, and the Ming repeatedly restored its
  princes. Mansur Khan of Turpan conquered Hami in 1513.
