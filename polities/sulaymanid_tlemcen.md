description: >-
  Sulaymanid Tlemcen was a principality in western Algeria ruled by descendants of Sulayman, a
  brother of Idris I, recorded here from 829 to 931. Idris I took Tlemcen from the Banu Ifran
  around 790, and his nephew Muhammad ibn Sulayman became its governor. When Idris II died in
  828 and the Idrisid lands were divided among his sons and kinsmen, the Sulaymanid branch
  governed Tlemcen and neighbouring towns of the central Maghreb as an independent line. The
  towns grew in connection with the Sunni Arab culture of al-Andalus, while the surrounding
  Zenata tribes kept their Kharijite beliefs. The Fatimids and their Miknasa allies ended the
  Sulaymanid principality in 931, and the region then passed between Fatimid and Umayyad
  clients.
