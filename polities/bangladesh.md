  Bangladesh is a state in the Bengal delta of South Asia, with its capital at Dhaka.
  It became independent in 1971 after a war separating East Pakistan from the
  government based in Pakistan's western wing. Bengali language activism, economic
  grievances, and demands for political representation helped drive the independence
  movement. Pakistani military repression was followed by resistance from the Mukti
  Bahini and intervention by India. The new republic adopted a constitution in 1972,
  but its subsequent history included assassinations, military governments, and the
  restoration of parliamentary rule. Agriculture, river transport, garment
  manufacturing, and overseas labour have shaped its economy. Dense settlement and
  recurring floods remain important conditions of national life. Changes of government
  have not ended the country's separate existence.
