description: >-
  The Archbishopric of Magdeburg was an ecclesiastical principality on the middle Elbe, founded
  by Otto I as a missionary see in 968 and ruling its own lands as an imperial principality from
  1180. Its territory took in the Magdeburg Börde, the Jerichow Land east of the river and the
  Saale country around Halle, where the archbishops moved their residence in the sixteenth
  century. The cathedral chapter elected the archbishops, who were often princes of the houses of
  Brandenburg, Saxony and Anhalt. The city of Magdeburg itself, a leading Hanseatic town, largely
  governed itself and turned Lutheran early. After the Reformation the see was held by Lutheran
  administrators. Tilly's army destroyed the city in 1631, and the Peace of Westphalia promised
  the principality to Brandenburg, which took it in 1680.
