description: >-
  Tarquinii was a leading Etruscan city in central Italy, near the Tyrrhenian coast, represented here from about 750 to 281 BCE. Its wealthy families shaped civic life, religious practice, and regional expansion; the Spurinna family appears prominently in surviving inscriptions. Access to metals in the Tolfa Hills and maritime trade helped the city prosper. Greek goods and craftsmen influenced its art, while traditions connected Tarquinii with Rome's early kings. The painted tombs of Monterozzi preserve scenes of banquets, games, music, and aristocratic display. Tarquinii fought repeated wars against Rome, interrupted by long truces. Its final conflict ended around 281 BCE, when it became a Roman ally and lost independent control of war and foreign relations. The precise timing remains uncertain.
