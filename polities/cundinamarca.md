description: >-
  The Free and Independent State of Cundinamarca was the government of Bogotá and its
  surrounding province during the first years of the independence wars in New Granada. On 20
  July 1810 a quarrel in the main square led to the creation of a Supreme Junta that removed
  the viceroy. In 1811 the province adopted a constitution as a constitutional monarchy
  under Ferdinand VII, and under Antonio Nariño it declared full independence in 1813.
  Nariño wanted a strong central state and refused to join the federation of the United
  Provinces of New Granada, which led to civil war between Bogotá and the federalists in
  Tunja. In December 1814 federal troops under Simón Bolívar took Bogotá, and Cundinamarca
  became part of the United Provinces.
