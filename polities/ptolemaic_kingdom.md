description: >-
  The Ptolemaic Kingdom was a monarchy centred on Egypt and Alexandria from 323 to 30 BCE.
  Ptolemy, one of Alexander's generals, took charge of Egypt after Alexander died and
  adopted the royal title in 305 BCE. His successors presented themselves as Greek kings
  and Egyptian pharaohs, supporting temples while maintaining a Greek-speaking court and
  administration. Control of Nile agriculture financed fleets, armies, and wars against
  other Hellenistic powers. Alexandria became a major port and centre of scholarship,
  famous for its library and royal patronage. Dynastic marriages, court violence, and
  Egyptian revolts weakened the kingdom, while Roman involvement increased. Cleopatra
  VII's alliance with Mark Antony ended in defeat by Octavian. His conquest in 30 BCE
  turned Egypt into a Roman possession.
