description: >-
  The Riograndense Republic, also called the Piratini Republic, was a breakaway state in
  southern Brazil that roughly covered the present state of Rio Grande do Sul. Ranchers and
  officers of the province rose against the imperial government in the Ragamuffin War of
  1835, angered by taxes on dried beef. After a victory at Seival, Antônio de Sousa Neto
  proclaimed the republic in September 1836, and Bento Gonçalves became its president. Its
  government moved between Piratini, Caçapava and Alegrete while the empire held Porto
  Alegre and the coast. The republic briefly joined with the Juliana Republic in Santa
  Catarina in 1839. Exhausted by war, its leaders accepted the Treaty of Ponche Verde in
  1845, which brought the province back into the empire with an amnesty.
