description: >-
  Tambralinga was a kingdom on the Malay Peninsula, centred near present Nakhon Si Thammarat
  in southern Thailand. Its early history is reconstructed from inscriptions and foreign
  accounts whose identification is disputed; this record uses approximately 150 to 1277.
  Access to routes across the peninsula and maritime trade connected its rulers with India,
  China and neighbouring Southeast Asian states. Hindu and Buddhist traditions shaped its
  courts and religious monuments. Relations with Srivijaya included influence and tribute,
  but do not necessarily establish direct outside government. In the thirteenth century King
  Chandrabhanu conducted ambitious campaigns in Sri Lanka, showing the court's ability to
  pursue its own wars. Defeats and growing pressure from neighbouring powers accompanied its
  decline, followed by a different political order.
