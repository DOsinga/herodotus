description: >-
  Farrukhabad was a state in the central Ganges–Yamuna Doab ruled by the Bangash nawabs, a
  family of Pashtun soldiers. Muhammad Khan Bangash, a mercenary commander in Mughal
  service, founded the town of Farrukhabad in 1714 and named it after the emperor
  Farrukhsiyar. He served as Mughal governor of Malwa and Allahabad, but his own lands, held
  by an army of adopted slave disciples, grew into a hereditary state reaching from Koil to
  Kora at his death in 1743. His successors fought the nawabs of Oudh, the Rohillas and the
  Marathas, who besieged Farrukhabad in 1751, and lost much territory to Oudh. In 1802 the
  nawab ceded his remaining lands to the East India Company in return for a pension.
