description: >-
  The Principality of Turov, later called Turov and Pinsk, was a Rus principality on the Pripyat
  river in the marshes of present southern Belarus. For two centuries Turov was an appanage that
  the princes of Kiev gave to their sons and allies. In 1158 Yury Yaroslavich, a grandson of
  Sviatopolk II, seized Turov and defended it against Kiev, and his descendants ruled it as their
  own patrimony. The land was divided among his sons, and Pinsk became the seat of a junior
  principality. Turov's army fought with Smolensk at the Kalka river in 1223. In the thirteenth
  century its princes depended on Galicia–Volhynia and leaned on Lithuania to escape it. Early in
  the fourteenth century the principality passed to the Grand Duchy of Lithuania.
