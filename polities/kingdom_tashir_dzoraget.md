description: >-
  The Kingdom of Tashir-Dzoraget, also called the Kingdom of Lori or the Kiurikian kingdom, was a
  medieval Armenian kingdom in what is now northern Armenia and the neighbouring parts of Georgia
  and Azerbaijan. In 979 the Bagratid king Smbat II gave the province of Tashir to his brother
  Kiurike with a royal title, and the Kiurikian branch went on to outlive the main Bagratid line
  at Ani. Kiurike's son David I Anhoghin, who reigned from 989 to 1048, took land from the
  emirates of Tbilisi and Ganja and ruled from Samshvilde. The kingdom lost Samshvilde to the
  Georgians in 1065, and its later kings ruled from the fortress of Lori. In 1118 King David IV
  of Georgia took Lori, and the Kiurikians kept only small domains around Tavush and Matsnaberd.
