description: >-
  The Sultanate of Ternate arose in 1486 on a volcanic island in the northern Moluccas, now
  part of Indonesia. Muslim sultans governed a maritime kingdom whose wealth and influence
  depended on cloves. Ternate competed with nearby Tidore and extended its authority and
  alliances across eastern Indonesia and parts of the southern Philippines. Portuguese
  traders arrived seeking spices, but their interference and the killing of Sultan Hairun
  provoked war. Sultan Baabullah expelled them in 1575 and presided over the kingdom's
  greatest influence. Later struggles involved Spanish forces and the Dutch East India
  Company. A treaty in 1683 subordinated Ternate to the company, ending its independent
  external affairs in this record. The royal institution survived under colonial rule and
  later ceremonial arrangements.
