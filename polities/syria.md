description: >-
  Syria is a republic in the eastern Mediterranean and northern Middle East, with its capital at Damascus. Independent government began after French troops withdrew in 1946. Parliamentary politics and repeated military coups preceded union with Egypt in the United Arab Republic from 1958 to 1961, when separate Syrian government ceased. A coup restored independence, followed by Baath Party rule from 1963. Hafez al Assad took power in 1970, and his son Bashar succeeded him in 2000. Protests in 2011 developed into a civil war involving rival Syrian authorities and foreign powers. Opposition forces overthrew Assad in December 2024, opening a transitional political period. This record follows the Syrian state across regime changes, while recognising the interruption caused by Egyptian union.
