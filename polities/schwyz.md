description: >-
  Schwyz was a free valley community in central Switzerland, north of Lake Lucerne, from which the
  whole of Switzerland later took its name. Its people were largely free peasants under the
  Habsburg counts, who held the local jurisdiction. In 1240, during his war with the pope, Emperor
  Frederick II granted Schwyz a charter that took it under the Empire's direct protection, a
  status the Habsburgs refused to accept. In practice the valley governed itself through an
  assembly of its free men and their elected leaders, and it fought a long border quarrel with the
  abbey of Einsiedeln. When Rudolf of Habsburg died in 1291, Schwyz joined Uri and Unterwalden in
  the Federal Charter, the founding alliance of the Swiss Confederacy.
