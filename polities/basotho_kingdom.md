description: >-
  The Basotho Kingdom emerged in southern Africa under Moshoeshoe I in 1822, bringing together communities displaced by warfare and migration. Moshoeshoe built his authority through alliances, protection, and the incorporation of former rivals, rather than relying on conquest. Thaba Bosiu became the kingdom's mountain stronghold and royal centre, helping its people withstand attacks. Missionaries provided diplomatic advice, introduced writing in Sesotho, and helped obtain firearms. Relations with British officials and expanding Boer settlements became increasingly difficult as outsiders claimed fertile lowland territory. Wars with the Orange Free State cost the kingdom much of its western land. Facing losses, Moshoeshoe requested British protection. The protectorate established in 1868 ended its independent foreign relations, while preserving the monarchy and Lesotho's distinctive identity.
