description: >-
  The Mar'ashis were a dynasty of Shi'ite sayyids who governed Mazandaran, on the southern
  shore of the Caspian Sea, from 1359 to 1596. Mir-i Buzurg, a religious leader with a
  following of dervishes, overthrew the Afrasiyabid rulers of Amol and founded a state ruled
  by his descendants from Amol and Sari. Timur conquered the province in 1392 and deported
  the family, but his son Shahrukh allowed them to return, and they governed again from
  about 1405. The Mar'ashis paid tribute to the Timurids, the Aq Qoyunlu and then the
  Safavids while governing the forested lowlands themselves. Rival branches fought over Amol
  and Sari for much of the sixteenth century. Shah Abbas I, whose mother was a Mar'ashi,
  used these disputes to annex Mazandaran in 1596.
