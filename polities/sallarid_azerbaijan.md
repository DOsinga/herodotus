description: >-
  The Sallarid Emirate of Azerbaijan began when Marzuban ibn Muhammad conquered the region
  in 941. A ruler of Daylamite origin, he established authority over cities including
  Ardabil and extended influence into Arran and Armenia, while his brother governed Tarom
  separately. The emirate faced the Hamdanids, Buyids, and raids by Rus forces, whose
  occupation devastated Barda. Marzuban's capture in 948 triggered struggles among
  relatives and rivals, but he later recovered power. Following his death, succession
  disputes and intervention from Tarom weakened the government. The Shaddadids took Ganja,
  and the Rawadids expanded from Tabriz. Ibrahim's deposition around 979 ended its
  standing as a major regional power. A smaller Sallarid territory survived until Marzuban
  ibn Isma'il was captured by the Rawadids in 984.
