name: Ségou Empire
description: >-
  The Ségou Empire was a Bamana monarchy in the middle Niger valley of present Mali, lasting
  from 1712 to 1861. Biton Coulibaly transformed a youth association into a army and made Ségou
  the centre of an expanding state. Kings bearing the title faama governed through military leaders, provincial
  authorities and councils, while traditional religious institutions supported royal power. River canoes, cavalry and infantry helped
  collect tribute and conduct campaigns. Trade flourished, but warfare and the enslavement of captives were central
  to the economy and army. After instability following Biton's death, Ngolo Diarra established a lasting royal
  line. Nineteenth century defeats by the Massina state weakened Ségou. Umar Tall captured its capital in
  1861, incorporating the kingdom into his Toucouleur Empire.
