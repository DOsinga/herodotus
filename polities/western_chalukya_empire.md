description: >-
  The Western Chalukya Empire governed the western Deccan in India from 973 to 1189. Tailapa II
  established it by overthrowing the Rashtrakutas, and its kings later made Kalyani their capital.
  This monarchy relied on provincial administration and subordinate houses, several of which
  eventually became independent kingdoms. Rivalry with the Cholas repeatedly drew it into wars
  over Vengi and the fertile eastern river valleys. Vikramaditya VI's long reign marked a high
  point in royal power and patronage. Kannada and Sanskrit writing flourished, while builders
  developed temples that linked earlier Chalukya forms with later Hoysala architecture. Although
  Kalachuri rulers seized Kalyani in 1157, Chalukya government continued at Annigeri. A recovery
  of Kalyani in 1183 proved temporary; Seuna conquest ended it in 1189.
