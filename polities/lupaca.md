description: >-
  The Lupaca were an Aymara people on the southwestern shore of Lake Titicaca, whose kingdom
  emerged after the decline of Tiwanaku in the eleventh century. Their lords, remembered in
  colonial sources under the names Cari and Cusi, ruled from the area of Chucuito, and the
  pre-Inca royal residence was probably at the hilltop site of Cutimbo with its stone burial
  towers. The Lupaca kept large herds of llamas and alpacas and controlled colonies in
  warmer valleys towards the Pacific coast. They fought their northern neighbours the Colla
  and sided with the Incas against them. In the mid fifteenth century the Lupaca came under
  Inca rule, and Chucuito became the capital of an Inca province and later a Spanish crown
  province.
