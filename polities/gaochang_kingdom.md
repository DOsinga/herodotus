description: >-
  Gaochang was an oasis kingdom in the Turpan basin of present Xinjiang, governing from
  460 until the Tang conquest in 640. Its rulers came successively from the Kan, Zhang, Ma
  and Qu families, rather than founding separate states at each succession. Chinese
  settlers and local elites maintained institutions shaped by Chinese administrative and
  literary traditions. The kingdom's location on trade routes connected it with
  neighbouring oases and the powers of the steppe. Its kings acknowledged Rouran and later
  Turkic overlords while continuing to run local affairs. Buddhism flourished, and King Qu
  Wentai welcomed the pilgrim Xuanzang on his journey west. Conflict with Tang China ended
  in military conquest. Tang authorities abolished the monarchy and replaced its
  government with a prefecture.
