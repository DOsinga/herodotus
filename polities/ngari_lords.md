name: "Lords of Ngari"

description: >-
  After the Tibetan Empire broke apart in the civil wars that followed the death of Langdarma
  in 842, Ngari in the far west of Tibet, the old heartland of Zhangzhung around Mount Kailash
  and the upper Sutlej, was left to local lords. Little is known of them: the later chronicles
  of western Tibet begin only with Kyide Nyimagon, a descendant of the imperial line who fled
  from central Tibet, married into a local noble family and was accepted as ruler of the region
  in the early tenth century. His sons divided his lands into Maryul in Ladakh, Guge and
  Purang. Until then the valleys and the pastures of the Changtang were held by clan chiefs and
  by remnants of the imperial administration.
