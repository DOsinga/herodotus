description: >-
  Paraguay is a landlocked South American republic that became independent of Spain in
  1811, with its capital at Asuncion. The Paraguay River divides its more densely
  settled eastern region from the Chaco to the west. Early governments under Jose Gaspar
  Rodriguez de Francia and the Lopez family concentrated power and limited outside
  influence. The devastating war against Brazil, Argentina, and Uruguay from 1864 to
  1870 caused enormous population losses and territorial concessions. Paraguay later
  fought Bolivia in the Chaco War, gaining much of the disputed region. Alfredo
  Stroessner's dictatorship lasted from 1954 to 1989, followed by constitutional
  government and competitive elections. Today its institutions include a president and
  Congress. Spanish and Guarani are official languages, reflecting the country's
  bilingual society.
