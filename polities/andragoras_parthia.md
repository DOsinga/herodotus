description: >-
  Parthia under Andragoras was a independent kingdom in northeastern Iran, conventionally
  dated from about 245 to 238 BCE. Andragoras had been a Seleucid satrap responsible for
  Parthia and neighbouring Hyrcania. During the instability surrounding the Third Syrian
  War, he broke with Seleucid authority and ruled on his own account. His coins bear his
  name in Greek and show royal imagery, providing important evidence for a ruler whose
  background remains obscure. Independence also removed the military support previously
  available from the Seleucid kings. The Parni, led by Arsaces, invaded from the north and
  progressively seized his territory. Andragoras was killed during their conquest around
  238 BCE. His brief kingdom was replaced by the Arsacid state that became the wider
  Parthian Empire.
