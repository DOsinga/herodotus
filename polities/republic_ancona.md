description: >-
  The Republic of Ancona was a maritime city state on the Adriatic coast of the Marche,
  from the early twelfth century until Pope Clement VII took it in 1532. Its elders,
  elected by the three districts of the city, governed under the high jurisdiction of the
  papacy, and its sea statutes regulated a trade that reached Constantinople, Alexandria,
  Syria and the Black Sea. Ancona was an ally of Ragusa and Hungary and a rival of Venice,
  and it twice held out against Frederick Barbarossa's sieges. The Malatesta held the city
  in the middle of the fourteenth century, and Cardinal Albornoz built a papal citadel
  there in 1355. The citizens tore it down in 1383, and the republic lasted until the
  papal takeover.
