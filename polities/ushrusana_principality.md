description: >-
  Ushrusana was a principality in the mountains and valleys of present northern Tajikistan, represented from about 600 to 892. Its
  hereditary rulers bore the title afshin and governed from Bunjikath near modern Shahriston. Coins bearing rulers' names and excavated palace
  paintings provide evidence for a court connected with the Iranian and Turkic worlds of Central Asia. Its inhabitants used Sogdian
  writing, and religious traditions included Zoroastrianism. The principality resisted the spread of Arab authority for generations. An Abbasid campaign in
  822 compelled its ruler to submit and convert to Islam, but the afshins initially retained local government. Members of the
  ruling house also entered caliphal military service. Ismail's Samanid conquest in 892, sometimes dated 893, brought the principality to extinction.
