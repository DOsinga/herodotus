description: >-
  Alodia was a Nubian kingdom in present day Sudan, centred on Soba near the meeting of the Blue and White Nile. Its formation is estimated in the sixth century, and it adopted Christianity during that century. A powerful king governed with appointed provincial officials, while churches and literacy in Nubian and Greek formed important parts of public life. Soba prospered through farming and trade connecting the Nile region with distant markets. Archaeology and medieval travellers describe substantial buildings, gardens and furnished churches. The kingdom declined as trade shifted and pressures from neighbouring peoples increased, although the causes remain debated. Soba's fall ended its central government around 1500; this record follows the conventional 1504 date for conquest associated with the Funj.
