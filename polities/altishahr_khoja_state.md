description: >-
  The Altishahr Khoja state was the short rule of the White Mountain Khoja brothers over the
  oasis cities of the western Tarim Basin, from 1755 to 1759. The brothers Burhan al-Din and
  Khwaja-i Jahan descended from Afaq Khoja and had been held captive by the Dzungars. When Qing
  armies destroyed the Dzungar Khanate in 1755, they were released and sent to win over
  Kashgar and Yarkand, which they took over with local support. Amid the revolt of Amursana
  they ruled on their own account, and in 1757 Khwaja-i Jahan killed the Qing envoy Amindao.
  Qing armies advanced on Kucha, Yarkand and Khotan in 1758. In 1759 the brothers fled over
  the Pamirs to Badakhshan, whose ruler killed them.
