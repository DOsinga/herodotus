description: >-
  Nanzan occupied southern Okinawa during the island's Sanzan period, from 1314 to 1429.
  Its rulers governed from the fortified centre at Ozato, commanding their own estates,
  retainers, and allied local lords. Although conventionally called a kingdom, its
  political structure was smaller and less unified than that label might suggest. Nanzan
  emerged as Okinawa divided into three competing realms, alongside Chuzan and Hokuzan.
  Its coastal position and dock supported trade with China and Southeast Asia, while
  tribute missions to the Ming court brought commercial opportunities and recognition.
  Succession disputes weakened its rulers, and their precise lineage remains uncertain.
  In 1429, Sho Hashi of Chuzan conquered Nanzan, completing Okinawa's political
  unification. The name survived as a regional designation within the Ryukyu Kingdom.
