description: >-
  Hesse-Marburg was a landgraviate in central Hesse, created in 1567 when Philip the Magnanimous
  died and his lands were divided among his four sons. Louis IV, the second son, received the
  upper county around Marburg and Giessen, about a quarter of Hesse, and ruled it from Marburg
  Castle until his death. He was a careful Lutheran ruler who strengthened the university of
  Marburg, founded by his father in 1527, and kept his land out of the religious wars of the age.
  He had no children. When he died in 1604 his lands were divided between Hesse-Kassel and Hesse-
  Darmstadt, and the dispute over Marburg, sharpened by Kassel's turn to Calvinism, lasted until
  the Peace of Westphalia.
