description: >-
  The Chandravalli chiefdom stands for the local rulers of the valley below the hill of
  Chitradurga in central Karnataka between the Maurya withdrawal and the arrival of the
  Satavahanas. Chandravalli had been settled since the Iron Age, and its megalithic burials and
  painted pottery show a farming population with a recognisable centre. Ashoka's edicts at
  Brahmagiri and Siddapura nearby mark the southern edge of Maurya rule, and nothing shows
  another state taking its place for the next half century or more. Excavations found coins of
  the Han emperor Wu and of Augustus, so the valley lay on long trade routes. Later coins name
  local Maharathi commanders who married into the Chutu family, the kind of chiefs who
  probably held it in these years.
