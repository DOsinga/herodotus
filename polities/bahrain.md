description: >-
  Bahrain is an island state in the Persian Gulf, centred on Bahrain Island with its capital at Manama. It became independent in 1971 when British protection ended. The Al Khalifa family continued to rule, first under an emir and, from 2002, a king. A constitution provides an elected lower chamber and an appointed upper chamber, while the monarchy retains substantial power. Earlier pearling and maritime trade gave way to oil production, refining, banking and other services. A causeway links Bahrain with Saudi Arabia. Political tension has often involved demands for broader representation and discrimination alleged by the Shia majority against the Sunni ruling establishment. Protests in 2011 were suppressed with support from neighbouring Gulf states. Bahrain remains a sovereign monarchy.
