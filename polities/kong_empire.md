description: >-
  The Kong Empire was a commercial and military state centred on Kong in present northeastern Ivory Coast from 1710 to 1898. Seku Watara established its ruling house by uniting Dyula leaders and taking control of the city. Expansion linked settlements across neighbouring Burkina Faso and beyond, protecting routes carrying gold, kola nuts, and other goods. Government was decentralised: branches of the Watara family and their commanders controlled territories, collected tribute, and raised troops. Kong itself became an centre of Islamic scholarship, although rulers also maintained older religious practices. Family divisions weakened central authority without ending cooperation. During the late nineteenth century, pressure from Samori Ture and France disrupted commerce. Samori's conquest and destruction of Kong ended the empire's independent existence.
