description: >-
  The Kingdom of Majorca was a Mediterranean monarchy that governed separately from 1276 to
  1344. James I of Aragon left its crown to his younger son, James II, creating a realm that
  combined the Balearic Islands with Roussillon, Cerdanya and possessions around
  Montpellier. Its rulers used both Palma and Perpignan, connecting island commerce with
  lands north of the Pyrenees. The widely scattered territories left the kingdom exposed to
  pressure from France and Aragon. Recognition of Aragonese overlordship did not end its own
  royal government, but disputes over allegiance, succession and trade repeatedly brought
  war. Peter IV conquered the islands in 1343 and the mainland counties in 1344. Majorca
  then retained a separate title and institutions within the Crown of Aragon.
