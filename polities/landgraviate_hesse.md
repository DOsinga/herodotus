description: >-
  The Landgraviate of Hesse was a principality in central Germany from 1264 to 1567. It
  emerged from the Thuringian succession war, when Sophia of Brabant secured the Hessian lands for
  her son Henry. Landgraves governed within the Holy Roman Empire while building their own territorial
  authority around centres including Marburg and Kassel. Dynastic divisions and reunifications altered the administration over
  time. Philip the Magnanimous made Hesse an important supporter of the Protestant Reformation, founded the
  University of Marburg, and helped organise alliances against imperial religious policy. His political and family
  decisions also brought conflict and imprisonment. After his death in 1567, the territory was divided
  among four sons. Kassel and Darmstadt became the two enduring successor states from that partition.
