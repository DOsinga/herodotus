description: >-
  Mozambique is a republic on southeastern Africa's Indian Ocean coast, independent from
  Portugal since 1975. FRELIMO led the liberation struggle and established a socialist
  government under Samora Machel, with Maputo as the capital. A civil war followed between
  the government and RENAMO, supported by neighboring white minority regimes. Fighting,
  displacement, and damaged infrastructure severely affected rural communities.
  Constitutional reforms in 1990 introduced multiparty politics and a market economy, and a
  peace agreement ended the war in 1992. FRELIMO remained the dominant governing party
  through subsequent elections. Agriculture, ports, mineral resources, and later natural gas
  development shaped economic prospects, while poverty and uneven growth persisted. Renewed
  violence and an insurgency in the north have challenged security within the continuing
  independent state.
