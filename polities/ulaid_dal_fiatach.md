description: >-
  The Dal Fiatach kingdom occupied eastern County Down in northeastern Ireland, with
  Downpatrick as its royal center. Its emergence around 450 is an estimate for the
  historically attested ruling line, rather than a literal date from legendary genealogies.
  Dal Fiatach kings frequently held the title king of Ulaid, competing with neighboring
  dynasties, especially Dal nAraidi, for regional leadership. This record follows their own
  governing territory, not every kingdom associated with Ulster. Bangor Abbey became an
  important religious center, while kinship, warfare, and alliances shaped royal succession
  and authority. Early ancestry stories mix tradition with uncertain history. John de
  Courcy's conquest in 1177 ended this independent government. Descendants of its rulers
  survived, but their later presence does not establish uninterrupted kingship.
