name: "Guiyi Circuit"
description: >-
  The Guiyi Circuit governed Dunhuang and neighbouring oases in the Hexi Corridor from 848
  to 1036. Zhang Yichao founded its government by seizing Shazhou from Tibetan control,
  receiving Tang recognition several years later. Although styled a Chinese military
  district and nominally attached to successive dynasties, its rulers directed local affairs
  themselves. The Zhang family initially held power, followed by the Cao family in the tenth
  century. Brief changes to the Jinshan and Dunhuang kingdom titles did not create separate
  governments. Its position on the Silk Road required diplomacy with neighbouring Uyghur
  kingdoms and other powers. Court patronage enriched the Buddhist caves at Mogao, whose
  paintings and manuscripts preserve evidence of this society. Tangut conquest finally ended
  the circuit in 1036.
