description: >-
  The Massina Empire governed the Inner Niger Delta in present day Mali from 1818 until
  1862. Seku Amadu founded this Fulani Sunni Islamic state after defeating forces allied
  with the Bambara kingdom at Noukouma. Its new capital, Hamdullahi, became the seat of an
  almami assisted by a council of religious scholars and judges. The government applied
  Islamic law, supported religious schools, collected taxes, and regulated access to
  seasonal grazing grounds and farmland. These rules helped coordinate the seasonal
  movements of cattle herders with the needs of settled farmers. Massina also contested
  control of Timbuktu and surrounding trade routes. Rivalry with Umar Tall's expanding
  Toucouleur Empire ended in the capture of Hamdullahi in 1862, although resistance
  continued after the capital fell.
