description: >-
  The Aetolian League was a federal union of communities in western and central Greece,
  represented here from approximately 370 to 189 BCE. Its assembly met at Thermon, and elected
  officials, including an general, managed common affairs while member communities retained local
  institutions. The league gained prestige by helping repel the Gallic invasion in 279 BCE and
  expanded its influence around Delphi. It became a rival of Macedon and the Achaean League,
  combining military strength with a widening network of members. Allied with Rome against
  Macedon, it later sought support from Antiochus III against Roman expansion. Defeat forced a
  treaty in 189 BCE binding it to Rome's friends and enemies. Its institutions survived, but
  independent foreign policy ended under Roman control.
