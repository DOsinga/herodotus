description: >-
  The Empire of Trebizond ruled the southeastern Black Sea coast from 1204 to 1461, centred on
  the port now called Trabzon in Turkey. Alexios and David Komnenos established it with Georgian
  military support just before crusaders captured Constantinople. Its emperors claimed Byzantine
  legitimacy, although their surviving realm became mainly a narrow coastal strip backed by
  mountains. The Grand Komnenoi maintained an Orthodox Christian court and used marriage
  alliances, diplomacy, and tribute to navigate pressure from Georgian, Turkish, and Mongol
  powers. Trebizond connected Black Sea shipping with routes into Iran, attracting Italian
  merchants, while agriculture remained vital to royal income. Civil struggles weakened the court
  at times. Ottoman sultan Mehmed II captured the capital in 1461, ending its separate imperial
  government.
