description: >-
  Later Liang ruled much of northern China from 907 to 923, opening the Five Dynasties period after
  the Tang collapse. Its founder Zhu Wen rose from rebel commander to powerful military governor, took
  control of the Tang court, and forced the last emperor to abdicate. He claimed imperial authority
  without bringing the regional governments under control. The court initially used Luoyang before
  settling at Kaifeng in 913. Rivalry with the Shatuo-led state of Jin dominated its wars, while
  succession struggles weakened the ruling house: Zhu Wen was murdered by a son, who was subsequently
  overthrown. Li Cunxu, Jin's ruler, proclaimed Later Tang and captured Kaifeng in 923. The last Liang
  emperor died, and his government was absorbed into Later Tang.
