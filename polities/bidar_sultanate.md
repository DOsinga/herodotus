description: >-
  The Bidar Sultanate was a small kingdom in India's central Deccan, ruled from Bidar by the
  Barid Shahi family from around 1490 until 1619. It emerged as Bahmani authority broke
  apart, with Qasim Barid and his successors controlling the old imperial court while
  Bahmani sultans retained little real power. Amir Barid continued this arrangement without
  taking a royal title; Ali Barid Shah adopted one in 1542. Bidar was one of the five Deccan
  sultanates and joined their alliance against Vijayanagara at Talikota in 1565. Its rulers
  added buildings to Bidar Fort and constructed distinctive royal tombs. Succession
  struggles weakened the dynasty after Ali's death. Ibrahim Adil Shah II of Bijapur defeated
  the last ruler and annexed the kingdom in 1619.
