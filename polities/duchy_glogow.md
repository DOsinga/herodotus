description: >-
  The Duchy of Głogów was one of the duchies of Silesia, with its capital at Głogów. It was formed
  in 1251, when the sons of Henry II the Pious divided Lower Silesia, and Konrad I received the
  northern part. His son Henry III of Głogów was among the most powerful of the Silesian Piasts,
  claimed the Polish crown and gained much of Greater Poland in the early fourteenth century. His
  sons lost the Greater Polish lands to Władysław the Elbow-high and divided the duchy among
  themselves. In 1331 King John of Bohemia took Głogów, and the remaining dukes became vassals of
  the Bohemian crown. Later dukes of the line held the town again for periods, until the duchy
  passed to the Bohemian kings for good in the sixteenth century.
