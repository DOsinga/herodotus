description: >-
  The Duchy of Głogów was one of the duchies of Silesia, with its capital at Głogów. It was formed
  in 1251, when the sons of Henry II the Pious divided Lower Silesia. When the last son of Duke
  Henry III died in 1331, John of Bohemia seized Głogów; he granted it to Henry I of Jawor until
  1346, and in 1349 Charles IV kept half of the town for the crown and gave the other half to
  Henry V the Iron of Żagań, whose line ruled as dukes of Głogów and Żagań under Bohemian
  overlordship. Matthias Corvinus took the duchy in 1488. The Jagiellonian princes John Albert and
  Sigismund then ruled it until it passed to the Bohemian crown in 1506.
