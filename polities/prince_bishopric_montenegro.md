description: >-
  The Prince-Bishopric of Montenegro was a mountain polity centred on Cetinje from 1516
  to 1852. Orthodox bishops, known as vladikas, combined religious leadership with
  political authority among clans that resisted Ottoman control. Their power depended on
  negotiation with tribal leaders as well as military cooperation. From the late
  seventeenth century, the Petrović-Njegoš family made the office hereditary through
  succession among relatives, since bishops could not marry. Russian support
  strengthened the principality's position against the Ottomans. Petar I expanded its
  territory and worked to reduce internal feuding, while Petar II developed central
  institutions and became a poet. The impostor Šćepan Mali briefly exercised secular
  rule in the eighteenth century. In 1852 Danilo abandoned episcopal office, creating a
  hereditary secular principality instead.
