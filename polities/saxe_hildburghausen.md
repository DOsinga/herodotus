description: >-
  Saxe-Hildburghausen was a small Ernestine duchy in southern Thuringia, created in 1680 when the
  lands of Ernest the Pious of Saxe-Gotha were divided among his sons, and Ernest received
  Hildburghausen and Heldburg. Its dukes moved their court to Hildburghausen in 1684 and built a
  palace and a planned new town for Huguenot refugees. Their ambitions outran their small
  revenues, and in the eighteenth century the duchy's debts were so large that an imperial
  commission took over its finances. Duke Frederick, who ruled from 1780, saw his daughter Therese
  marry the future King Ludwig I of Bavaria, an occasion celebrated by the first Oktoberfest. In
  1826 Frederick exchanged Hildburghausen for Saxe-Altenburg, and Hildburghausen passed to Saxe-
  Meiningen.
