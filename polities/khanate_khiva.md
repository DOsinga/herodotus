name: Khanate of Khiva
description: >-
  The Khanate of Khiva was an Uzbek monarchy in Khorezm, around the irrigated lower Amu
  Darya south of the Aral Sea. Established in 1511, it initially centred on Urgench before
  Khiva became the capital. Its rulers governed settled farming districts alongside
  territories inhabited by Turkmen and other mobile communities. Dynastic struggles,
  changing river courses, and rivalry with neighbouring Bukhara shaped its history. Nader
  Shah conquered the khanate in the eighteenth century, after which local rule returned. The
  Qungrat dynasty consolidated power in the nineteenth century. Russian conquest in 1873
  reduced its territory and made it a protectorate, although the khan remained.
  Revolutionary upheaval overthrew the monarchy in 1920, replacing it with the Khorezm
  People's Soviet Republic under growing Bolshevik influence.
