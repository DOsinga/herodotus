description: >-
  The Kingdom of Kartli-Kakheti united two eastern Georgian kingdoms in 1762 under Heraclius II,
  with Tbilisi as its capital. The Bagrationi monarchy sought to strengthen central government,
  limit powerful nobles, and improve economic resources. Its position between Russia, Iran, and
  the Ottoman Empire made independence difficult to defend. Heraclius signed the Treaty of
  Georgievsk with Russia in 1783, seeking protection while abandoning allegiance to Iran.
  Russian support proved unreliable when Agha Mohammad Khan invaded in 1795, defeated Georgian
  forces, and devastated Tbilisi. After Heraclius's death, George XII faced succession disputes
  and renewed outside pressure. Russia annexed the kingdom in 1801 and replaced its monarchy
  with imperial administration. The union nevertheless became an important stage in the history
  of modern Georgia.
