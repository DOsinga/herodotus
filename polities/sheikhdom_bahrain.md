description: >-
  The Sheikhdom of Bahrain was the state of the Al Khalifa family in the Bahrain islands
  of the Persian Gulf from their conquest in 1783 until Britain took charge of its defence
  and foreign affairs. The Al Khalifa, a clan of the Bani Utbah, had settled at Zubarah on
  the Qatar peninsula, and in 1783 they drove out the governor who held Bahrain for Zand
  Iran. For decades the new rulers fought to keep the islands against Oman, the Emirate of
  Diriyah, the Ottomans and Persia, and Muscat briefly occupied them in 1800. Pearling and
  date palms were the base of the economy. The General Maritime Treaty of 1820 and the Perpetual Truce of 1861 bound
  Bahrain to Britain, which from 1861 guaranteed its defence.
