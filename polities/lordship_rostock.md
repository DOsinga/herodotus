description: >-
  The Lordship of Rostock was a principality on the Baltic coast of Mecklenburg, formed in the
  division of the Mecklenburg lands after Henry Borwin II's death. Its lords, a branch of the
  Obotrite dynasty, ruled the area around the growing Hanseatic town of Rostock, which itself won
  wide self-government from its rulers. Weak and indebted, the lords struggled against the town,
  neighbouring princes and the kings of Denmark, whose influence on the southern Baltic coast was
  strong. Nicholas the Child, the last lord, placed the lordship under Danish overlordship in
  1300. After his death in 1314 the Danish king took control, and in 1323 Rostock passed as a
  Danish fief to the dukes of Mecklenburg, who incorporated it.
