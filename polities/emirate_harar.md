description: >-
  The Emirate of Harar was a Muslim city-state in the eastern highlands of present-day
  Ethiopia, governed from the walled city of Harar. It was founded in 1647, when the people of
  Harar refused the authority of the Imamate of Aussa, successor to the Adal Sultanate, and
  chose their own ruler, Ali ibn Daud. Emirs of his line governed the city and its farmland
  and traded coffee, cloth and other goods between the Somali coast, Shewa and the Oromo
  country, while Harar remained a centre of Islamic learning with its own coinage. Oromo
  groups settled the surrounding countryside and paid tribute to the emirate. Egyptian forces
  occupied Harar from 1875 to 1885. The restored emirate fell to the Shewan army of Menelik
  after the battle of Chelenqo in 1887.
