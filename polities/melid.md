description: >-
  Melid was an Iron Age kingdom centred at Arslantepe near modern Malatya in eastern
  Turkey. Emerging after the collapse of the Hittite Empire around 1180 BCE, it preserved
  Luwian language and royal traditions within the network of Neo-Hittite states. The
  kingdom is also associated with the name Kammanu. Its rulers built a palace and
  commissioned monumental sculptures of lions and kings, making the city a prominent
  regional centre. Early royal connections linked Melid with Carchemish, while Assyrian
  campaigns eventually imposed tribute. Paying tribute did not immediately extinguish its
  local kingship. The decisive change came in 712 BCE, when Sargon II sacked and annexed
  the city. The much older archaeological layers at Arslantepe belong to earlier
  societies, not this particular kingdom.
