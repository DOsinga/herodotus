description: >-
  Buleleng was a Balinese kingdom on the north coast of the island, with its palace at Singaraja.
  Its founder, I Gusti Anglurah Panji Sakti, united the quarrelling lords of north Bali in the
  seventeenth century as the Gelgel kingdom broke up, and extended his power to Jembrana and across
  the strait to Blambangan in Java. After his death in 1704 the state weakened, and Mengwi
  controlled it from 1732 until 1752. From about 1780 princes of the Karangasem dynasty ruled
  Buleleng, and in the nineteenth century they in turn dominated Karangasem. Buleleng resisted Dutch
  expeditions in 1846 and 1848 under its minister Gusti Ketut Jelantik, but the Dutch conquered it
  in 1849 and governed it directly.
