description: >-
  Đường Lâm was a Vietnamese domain governed by Ngô Nhật Khánh during the collapse of Ngô
  authority, approximately from 965 to 968. Situated near present-day Sơn Tây, it was one
  of the competing territorial powers remembered in the Period of the Twelve Warlords.
  Khánh belonged to the Ngô ruling family and adopted princely titles, but his government
  was local rather than a restored kingdom over all Vietnam. Đinh Bộ Lĩnh defeated him in
  968 as part of the campaigns that reunited the country. Marriage ties linked the
  defeated family with Đinh's household without restoring the domain. Khánh later fled to
  Champa and supported an attempted invasion in 979, dying at sea. Those later events did
  not prolong his separate government.
