description: >-
  Epirus was an ancient Greek state in northwestern Greece and southern Albania,
  recorded here from 330 to 167 BCE. The Molossian royal house brought neighbouring
  communities into a united kingdom, linked by marriage and politics to Macedonia. Its
  best known king, Pyrrhus, made Ambracia his capital and campaigned in Italy and Sicily
  against Rome and other powers. His costly victories gave rise to the expression
  Pyrrhic victory. The sanctuary and oracle at Dodona remained an important religious
  centre. After the monarchy ended in 231 BCE, the Epirote League governed through
  federal institutions. Rival factions later supported different sides in Rome's wars
  with Macedonia. Following the Roman victory at Pydna, Roman forces devastated Epirus
  in 167 BCE and enslaved many inhabitants.
