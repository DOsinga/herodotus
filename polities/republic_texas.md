description: >-
  The Republic of Texas emerged from rebellion against Mexico in 1836 and governed until the
  transfer of power to the United States in 1846. Its elected presidents and two-house
  Congress operated under a constitution that protected slavery and restricted citizenship.
  Sam Houston's victory at San Jacinto secured practical independence, but Mexico never
  accepted the republic's separation. Texas claimed more territory than it controlled,
  including lands occupied by Indigenous peoples and disputed regions toward the Rio Grande.
  Austin became its capital, although Houston challenged that location. Debt, border
  warfare, settlement, and relations with Native nations shaped domestic politics. After
  years of debate over annexation, Texas entered the United States in December 1845; its
  republican government formally surrendered authority the following February.
