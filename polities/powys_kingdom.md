description: >-
  Powys was a Welsh kingdom that emerged after Roman rule in Britain, around the fifth
  century. Its early lands stretched from the Cambrian Mountains into areas now within the
  English West Midlands. Hereditary kings defended its valleys against expanding Anglo-Saxon
  neighbours, especially Mercia. The eastern frontier gradually contracted, while dynastic
  links drew Powys into the struggles of other Welsh kingdoms. Rhodri's family absorbed its
  government after Cyngen's death in 855, with the takeover completed by 886. A separate
  kingdom reappeared under Maredudd ap Bleddyn around 1124. Mathrafal represents the later
  royal seat rather than an established early capital. Madog ap Maredudd was its last ruler.
  His death in 1160 brought division into northern and southern kingdoms, ending a united
  Powys.
