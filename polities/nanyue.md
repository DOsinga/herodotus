description: >-
  Nanyue was a kingdom spanning southern China and northern Vietnam from 204 to 111
  BCE. Zhao Tuo, a former Qin commander, founded it after the Qin empire collapsed,
  ruling from Panyu, now Guangzhou. His successors combined Chinese administrative
  practices with the customs of the region's Yue populations. Relations with the Han
  emperors alternated between tribute, confrontation, and recognition of Nanyue's own
  rulers. Its kings sometimes used imperial titles at home while accepting a
  subordinate position in diplomacy. The wealthy tomb of Zhao Mo preserves evidence of
  court life and trade. A dispute over closer submission to Han prompted killings
  within the royal court. Emperor Wu's armies then conquered the kingdom and
  incorporated its lands into the expanding Han imperial administration.
