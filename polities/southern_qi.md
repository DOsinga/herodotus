name: "Southern Qi"
description: >-
  Southern Qi was an imperial monarchy in southern China from 479 to 502, with its
  capital at Jiankang, modern Nanjing. Xiao Daocheng founded the state by forcing the
  last Liu Song emperor to surrender his throne, taking the imperial title himself. Its
  rulers belonged to the Xiao family, distinguishing this Qi from the later northern
  state ruled by the Gao family. Northern Wei remained its main rival across the divided
  Chinese world, with frontier fighting followed by a peace agreement in 490. After the
  first two emperors, disputed succession and killings within the royal family
  undermined the court. Xiao Baojuan's harsh rule provoked rebellions. General Xiao Yan
  captured the capital and replaced Southern Qi with the Liang dynasty in 502.
