description: >-
  The Kingdom of Jessore was a principality in the southern Ganges delta of Bengal, ruled by a
  Kayastha family from about 1576 until the Mughal conquest of 1612. Srihari, an official of the
  last Karrani sultan, withdrew into the marshy Sundarbans with part of the royal treasury when
  the Bengal Sultanate collapsed, and took the title of maharaja. His son Pratapaditya succeeded
  him in 1584 and expanded the realm across Jessore, Khulna, Barisal and the country south of
  Calcutta, ruling from Dhumghat and later Ishwaripur. He built river forts, a large fleet of war
  boats and gun foundries, often with Portuguese help. As one of the Bara-Bhuiyan lords, he
  submitted to the Mughal governor Islam Khan in 1609, broke his pledge, and was defeated at Salka
  and Jessore.
