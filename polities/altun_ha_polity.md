description: >-
  Altun Ha was a Maya political centre in northern Belize, represented here from about
  200 to 900. Earlier settlement existed at the site, but monumental construction and
  elite burials distinguish the governing community of the Classic period. Its rulers'
  names and succession are poorly known, leaving archaeology as the main guide to its
  organisation. Temples and plazas formed a ceremonial core supported by nearby
  settlements and access to coastal resources. Imported obsidian and other objects
  demonstrate links with distant regions, including central Mexico. Tombs contained
  abundant jade, most famously a large carved head associated with the sun god.
  Construction and elite institutions declined near the end of the Classic period,
  although smaller communities continued living among the former city's buildings.
