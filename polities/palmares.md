description: >-
  Quilombo dos Palmares developed from 1605 in the forested hills of Pernambuco, in what is now Brazil's Alagoas state. Escaped enslaved Africans formed settlements that also welcomed Indigenous people and other outsiders. These communities farmed, traded, and defended themselves against Portuguese and Dutch expeditions. Their government combined central African traditions with local conditions, joining several settlements under rulers such as Ganga Zumba and Zumbi. Officials, judges, and fortified centres supported this political network. Ganga Zumba accepted a Portuguese peace offer, but Zumbi rejected its restrictions and continued resistance. A colonial army captured the main settlement at Serra da Barriga in 1694. Zumbi survived until 1695, and smaller communities endured, but the central government was destroyed by the Portuguese colonial forces.
