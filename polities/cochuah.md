description: >-
  Cochuah was a Maya province of the central Yucatán Peninsula, in the forested interior
  east of Sotuta and south of Cupul, with its capital at Tihosuco. Like the other provinces,
  it emerged after the fall of Mayapan around 1441 as an independent jurisdiction under a
  halach uinik, or supreme lord, with town chiefs below him. Its lands lay along routes
  leading to the Caribbean coast and towards the province of Chetumal, and it took part in
  the wars between the provinces. Cochuah fought the expeditions of Francisco de Montejo in
  the 1530s and was conquered by his nephew in 1543 and 1544, and it joined the great Maya
  revolt of 1546. Tihosuco later became the starting point of the Caste War in 1847.
