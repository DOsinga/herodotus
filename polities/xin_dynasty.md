description: >-
  The Xin dynasty was Wang Mang's Chinese imperial government from 9 to 23 CE, based at
  Chang'an. A powerful relative of the Han imperial family, Wang rose through the regency
  before taking the throne himself. He presented his rule as a renewal of ancient
  Confucian ideals and attempted sweeping changes to landholding, currency, commerce, and
  public administration. These measures met resistance from established officials and
  powerful families, while repeated policy changes caused disruption. Border conflict with
  the Xiongnu consumed resources. Floods, famine, and popular rebellion further weakened
  the regime, and rebel armies joined Han claimants in challenging it. Gengshi forces
  entered Chang'an in 23 and killed Wang Mang, ending Xin rule before the later
  consolidation of the Eastern Han empire.
