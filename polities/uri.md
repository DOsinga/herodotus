description: >-
  Uri was a valley community on the upper Reuss in central Switzerland, astride the route that led
  from Lake Lucerne to the Gotthard pass. The opening of that route in the early thirteenth
  century made the valley valuable, and in 1231 King Henry (VII) redeemed it from the Habsburgs
  and placed it directly under the Empire. With no lord between them and the distant king, the
  free valley people governed themselves through an assembly of landholders and an elected
  Landammann, keeping order, settling feuds and raising troops. The abbey of the Fraumünster in
  Zurich held much of the land but did not rule it. In 1291, after the death of Rudolf of
  Habsburg, Uri swore the Federal Charter with Schwyz and Unterwalden, the founding alliance of
  the Swiss Confederacy.
