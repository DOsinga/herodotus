description: >-
  The County of Henneberg was the territory of the counts of Henneberg in southern Thuringia and
  northern Franconia, between the Thuringian Forest and the Rhön. The counts had been burgraves of
  Würzburg, and after losing that office they built up their own lands, which they divided in 1274
  among the lines of Schleusingen, Aschach and Hartenberg. The Schleusingen line was the
  strongest. It held the lordship around Coburg, apart from a period under the margraves of
  Brandenburg from 1291 to 1312, and lost it to the Wettins in 1353. Count Berthold VII was made a
  prince in 1310. In 1542 the counts took Meiningen from Würzburg in an exchange. When the last
  count died in 1583, the Wettins took over under an inheritance treaty.
