description: >-
  Sotuta was a Maya province of the northern central Yucatán Peninsula, founded by survivors
  of the Cocom family after the fall of Mayapan around 1441. The head of the Cocom served as
  its halach uinik, the supreme lord, who claimed descent from Kukulkan and ruled the town
  chiefs from the town of Sotuta. The province fought its neighbours Ah Kin Chel, Hocaba and
  above all the Xiu of Maní, whose ancestors had massacred the Cocom at Mayapan, and in 1536
  the Cocom lord Nachi Cocom had a Xiu pilgrimage party killed. Sotuta resisted the Spanish
  longer than most provinces. Nachi Cocom submitted only after a campaign in 1543, and the
  province joined the great Maya revolt of 1546 against Spanish rule.
