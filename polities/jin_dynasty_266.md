description: >-
  The Jin dynasty governed China from 266 to 420 under the Sima imperial family. Sima Yan replaced Cao Wei and conquered Eastern Wu in 280, briefly reuniting the country after the Three Kingdoms period. The court initially ruled from Luoyang, but struggles among imperial princes weakened its armies and administration. Northern rebellions and new rival states followed. Luoyang fell in 311 and Chang'an in 316, driving many officials and families south. Sima Rui continued Jin government at Jiankang from 317, beginning the Eastern Jin period. Powerful aristocratic clans limited the emperors' authority, while generals fought repeated northern campaigns. Victory at the Fei River in 383 secured the southern regime. Liu Yu took the throne in 420 and founded Liu Song.
