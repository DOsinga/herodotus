name: "Idrisid Kingdom of Fez"
description: >-
  The Idrisid Kingdom began at Walili in northern Morocco in 788, when Idris I gained the support of
  local Berber allies after fleeing Abbasid persecution. His son Idris II developed Fez as the royal
  seat, attracting immigrants and strengthening its role as an Islamic urban center. The ruling family
  claimed descent from the Prophet Muhammad through Hasan, giving its monarchy important religious
  standing. After Idris II died in 828, his sons divided their lands; this record follows the
  government at Fez rather than every Idrisid branch. Rivalries and intervention by tribes allied with
  the Fatimids weakened that court. Miknasa rule interrupted it in 919. Hassan al-Hajam recovered Fez
  in 925, but lost the city again in 927, ending this government.
