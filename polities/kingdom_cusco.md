description: >-
  The Kingdom of Cusco was the Andean state from which the Inca Empire developed, centred on the Cusco
  valley in present Peru. Its beginnings are conventionally placed around 1200 and associated with
  Manco Capac, although accounts of the earliest rulers combine later traditions with foundation
  legends. Leaders governed communities organised through kin groups, known as ayllus, and
  strengthened their authority through warfare, marriage, and alliances with neighbouring peoples. The
  royal succession was remembered in two divisions, Hurin and Hanan Cusco, with Inca Roca associated
  with the latter. Farming, terraces, and irrigation supported the valley's population. In 1438
  Pachacuti's victory over the Chanka marked a turning point, as the local kingdom became the
  expanding imperial state known as the Inca Empire.
