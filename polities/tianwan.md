description: >-
  Tianwan was the state of the southern Red Turbans in the middle Yangtze valley, lasting
  from 1351 to 1360 during the collapse of the Yuan dynasty. The cloth seller Xu Shouhui and
  the blacksmith Zou Pusheng rose in eastern Hubei in 1351, drawing on the White Lotus hope
  that the Buddha Maitreya would come to save the poor. They took Qishui, made it their
  capital and enthroned Xu as emperor. Their armies spread quickly through Hubei, Jiangxi and
  Hunan, but Yuan forces drove them back in 1353 and Xu went into hiding. He returned in
  1355 and moved the capital to Hanyang, while his generals took Jiangxi and Sichuan. In
  1360 his general Chen Youliang killed him and replaced Tianwan with his own state of Han.
