description: >-
  The Netherlands began in 1813, when William of Orange returned after French rule and
  became sovereign prince. In 1815 his state became a kingdom incorporating the southern
  Netherlands, which separated as Belgium after the revolution of 1830. The monarchy
  developed parliamentary government, especially through the constitutional reforms of
  1848. Amsterdam remained the capital, while the government sat in The Hague. Overseas
  possessions formed a large colonial empire, most notably in Indonesia. During German
  occupation from 1940 to 1945, the government continued in exile and retained authority
  over overseas territories. Decolonisation reshaped the kingdom after the war. The
  Netherlands remains a constitutional monarchy, closely involved in European integration,
  with Dutch and Caribbean territories forming different parts of its continuing political
  structure.
