description: >-
  The Kingdom of Syunik, also called the Kingdom of Baghk or of Kapan, was a medieval Armenian
  kingdom in the mountains of southeastern Armenia, ruled by the Siunia dynasty from the fortress
  town of Kapan. Its princes had long recognised the Bagratid kings of Armenia, but in 987, as
  Bagratid power weakened, Smbat of Syunik took the royal title for himself. At its height the
  kingdom reached over Syunik, parts of Artsakh and the lands around Lake Sevan, and its
  monasteries, above all Tatev, were centres of Armenian learning. It outlived all the other
  Armenian kingdoms of Greater Armenia, surviving the Seljuk invasions as a reduced mountain
  state. Turkish forces took its last fortress, Baghaberd, in 1170, and the region passed under
  the Eldiguzid atabegs of Azerbaijan.
