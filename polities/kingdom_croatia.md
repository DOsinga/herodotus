name: "Kingdom of Croatia"
description: >-
  The Kingdom of Croatia governed lands beside the eastern Adriatic from the tenth century
  until Hungarian control in 1102. Its beginning follows the recognition of Tomislav as king
  around 925, rather than a securely documented coronation ceremony. Kings of the
  Trpimirović family ruled through nobles, regional officials, and church connections, while
  relations with Byzantine Dalmatian cities complicated authority along the coast. Under
  Peter Krešimir IV and Demetrius Zvonimir, the crown expanded its influence and
  strengthened ties with the papacy. Knin served as a royal centre. Disputed succession
  after Zvonimir's death brought warfare and Hungarian intervention. Coloman established
  rule in 1102; Croatia retained its name and institutions, but government passed under the
  Hungarian crown through appointed bans rather than independent kings.
