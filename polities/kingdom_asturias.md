description: >-
  The Kingdom of Asturias arose in the mountains of northern Iberia after the Muslim
  conquest of the Visigothic kingdom. Its traditional beginning around 718 is associated
  with Pelayo, whose victory at Covadonga became central to later accounts of Christian
  resistance. Kings expanded west into Galicia and south toward the Duero, while also
  negotiating with Muslim rulers and confronting local rivals. Oviedo became an important
  royal and religious centre under Alfonso II, and churches built by later kings reflect
  the court's resources and ambitions. Royal succession gradually became hereditary. In
  910, Alfonso III's lands were divided among his sons, creating separate governments in
  León and Galicia. Fruela II retained Asturias until 924, when its remaining government
  joined the kingdom of León.
