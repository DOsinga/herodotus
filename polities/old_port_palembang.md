description: >-
  The Old Port Pacification Superintendency was the Chinese name for Palembang in southern Sumatra
  while a Chinese Muslim community governed it under Ming recognition. After Majapahit crushed the
  old kingdom of Palembang in the late fourteenth century, the city fell to Chinese settlers led
  first by Liang Daoming and then by the pirate chief Chen Zuyi. In 1407 Admiral Zheng He's treasure
  fleet defeated Chen, and the Ming court appointed Shi Jinqing, a Muslim of Hui descent who had
  served Majapahit, as pacification commissioner. Shi governed the port until his death around 1421,
  after which his son and daughter disputed the succession. The office faded by about 1440, and
  Palembang came back under Javanese influence.
