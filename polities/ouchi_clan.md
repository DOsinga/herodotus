description: >-
  The Ōuchi were the Sengoku daimyo house of western Honshu and northern Kyushu, ruling from
  Yamaguchi from the Ōnin War in 1467 until 1557. They had been military governors of Suō and
  Nagato since the fourteenth century, and at their height held six provinces. Ōuchi Masahiro
  was a leading general of the western army in the Ōnin War, and Ōuchi Yoshioki restored the
  shogun Ashikaga Yoshitane to Kyoto in 1508. The house controlled much of the trade with Ming
  China and Korea, and Yamaguchi became a wealthy centre of learning that welcomed Francis
  Xavier in 1551. That year the vassal Sue Harukata forced Ōuchi Yoshitaka to kill himself and
  set up a puppet heir. Mōri Motonari defeated Sue in 1555 and overran the Ōuchi lands by
  1557.
