description: >-
  The Principality of Valona was a lordship on the southern Albanian coast, centred on
  Valona and the fortress of Kanina, that governed itself from the death of Stefan Dušan
  in 1355 until the Ottoman conquest of 1417. Dušan had given it to his brother-in-law
  John Komnenos Asen, who styled himself despot and heir of the Epirote rulers, and who
  kept it as his own after the Serbian emperor died. His heir Alexander ruled until about
  1371, after which Balša II of Zeta held Valona through his marriage to Comita Muzaka.
  After Balša fell at Savra in 1385, Comita and her daughter Rugina governed it, offering
  it to Venice in vain, until the Ottomans took Valona and Kanina.
