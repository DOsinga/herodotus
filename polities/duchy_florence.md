description: >-
  The Duchy of Florence was a Medici monarchy in Tuscany from 1532 to 1569. It replaced the Florentine Republic after imperial forces restored Medici control and Alessandro received the ducal title. New councils retained some civic forms, but political power rested with the hereditary duke. Alessandro's assassination in 1537 brought Cosimo I to power. Cosimo defeated opponents, strengthened administration and defenses, and expanded the state's naval capabilities. His conquest of Siena, completed after prolonged warfare, enlarged Florentine territory. The court also supported art and building projects, linking Medici authority with the city's cultural prestige. In 1569 Pope Pius V granted Cosimo the title Grand Duke of Tuscany. The duchy continued under that broader identity rather than disappearing through foreign conquest.
