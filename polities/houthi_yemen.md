description: >-
  The Supreme Political Council represents the Houthi government based in Sanaa, governing most of northwestern Yemen during the civil war. This record begins with the Revolutionary Committee that assumed authority in February 2015, after the Houthis seized the capital and displaced the existing executive. In 2016, the Houthis and allies from the General People's Congress established the council as their governing body. It appoints cabinets and claims national authority, but is not the internationally recognised government of Yemen. The alliance fractured when former president Ali Abdullah Saleh broke with the Houthis and was killed in 2017. Council institutions continued under Houthi dominance. War, outside intervention and competing governments have left Yemen divided, with Sanaa remaining the centre of this administration.
