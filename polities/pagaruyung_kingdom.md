name: "Pagaruyung Kingdom"
description: >-
  Pagaruyung was a kingdom centered in the Minangkabau highlands of western Sumatra, beginning with
  Adityawarman's inland court in 1347. Its early royal culture combined Hindu and Buddhist traditions
  with local customs, and inscriptions record irrigation works and claims to authority. Islam spread
  through the region during the sixteenth century and later influenced royal titles and government.
  The monarchy coexisted with strong local communities and customary leaders rather than controlling
  every village directly. Recognition of Acehnese overlordship did not remove its own kings. In the
  nineteenth century the Padri War set religious reformers against defenders of customary practice and
  devastated the royal family. Dutch intervention then displaced royal government. The arrest and
  exile of a claimant in 1833 marks its end.
