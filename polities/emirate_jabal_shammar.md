description: >-
  The Emirate of Jabal Shammar was a Rashidi monarchy centered on Ha'il in northern Arabia from 1836 to 1921. Its rulers drew support from Shammar tribal networks and the oasis town's position on caravan routes. Abdullah ibn Rashid founded the dynasty, and later emirs expanded their influence across Najd. The emirate became the principal rival of the Saudi family, defeating them and taking Riyadh in 1891. Connections with the Ottoman Empire provided support but did not remove the Rashidis' own government. Succession disputes and murders within the ruling house weakened the state as Ibn Saud rebuilt Saudi power. During the First World War the Rashidis remained aligned with the Ottomans. Ha'il surrendered to Ibn Saud in 1921, ending the emirate.
