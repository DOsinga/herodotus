description: >-
  The Chola Empire ruled from southern India's Kaveri valley between 848 and 1279.
  Vijayalaya's seizure of Thanjavur began the medieval kingdom, distinct from the earlier
  Cholas known in Tamil literature. Hereditary kings expanded through warfare and governed
  with officials, revenue districts, and influential village assemblies. Rajaraja I and
  Rajendra I brought much of southern India and parts of Sri Lanka under their power. Rajendra
  established Gangaikonda Cholapuram after a campaign reaching the Ganges, and his fleet
  attacked Srivijaya's ports across the Bay of Bengal. Stone temples, bronze sculpture, and
  inscriptions reflect royal patronage and local economic life. Later rulers faced growing
  pressure from neighbouring states. The revived Pandyas defeated the last Chola ruler in 1279
  and absorbed the remaining kingdom.
