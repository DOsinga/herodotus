description: >-
  The Mōri were the Sengoku daimyo house that rose from a small estate in Aki province to rule
  most of western Honshu, from their castle of Yoshida-Kōriyama, between 1554 and 1585. Mōri
  Motonari inherited the house in 1523 as a minor vassal caught between the Ōuchi and Amago.
  In 1554 he broke with Sue Harukata, who had seized control of the Ōuchi, and destroyed him at
  Itsukushima in 1555. He took the Ōuchi provinces of Suō and Nagato by 1557 and the Amago
  stronghold of Gassantoda in 1566. His sons Kikkawa Motoharu and Kobayakawa Takakage
  supported his grandson Terumoto, who fought Oda Nobunaga for years. After peace with
  Hideyoshi in 1582, the Mōri accepted his authority in 1585.
