description: >-
  The Maukhari Kingdom of Kannauj ruled in the Ganges plain of northern India during the
  sixth century. Its start around 510 estimates the emergence of Harivarman's separate
  court, rather than a dated declaration of independence. The dynasty had earlier ties to
  Gupta authority, but later kings asserted wider sovereignty as that empire weakened.
  Ishanavarman adopted the title maharajadhiraja and fought neighbouring powers, while his
  successors extended their influence across much of the region. Royal inscriptions and
  literary accounts reveal rivalries with the Later Guptas and marriage connections with
  the Pushyabhuti family of Thanesar. Grahavarman married Rajyashri, Harsha's sister. His
  death in 606 ended the governing line, and Harsha incorporated its realm. This record
  excludes the separate Maukhari branch around Gaya.
