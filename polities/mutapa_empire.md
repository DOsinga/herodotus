name: Kingdom of Mutapa
description: >-
  Mutapa was a Shona kingdom on the northern Zimbabwean plateau and adjoining Zambezi
  lands, represented here from 1430 to 1760. Tradition connects its foundation with
  Nyatsimba Mutota, who moved north from the Great Zimbabwe region. Kings bearing the
  title mwenemutapa governed through court officials and regional chiefs, with royal
  authority supported by ancestral religious traditions. Farming, cattle, gold, and
  ivory linked the interior to Indian Ocean trade through coastal merchants. Portuguese
  traders and soldiers became involved in succession struggles, seeking access to mines
  and influence over rulers. These interventions did not erase local political life, and
  royal power revived at times. Rozvi expansion and civil wars weakened the kingdom,
  which fragmented around 1760, although smaller Mutapa successor rulers continued
  afterward.
