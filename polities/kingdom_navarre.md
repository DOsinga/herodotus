description: >-
  The Kingdom of Navarre grew around Pamplona in the western Pyrenees, traditionally beginning with Íñigo Arista's rise in 824. Its monarchy emerged between Frankish expansion and the Muslim emirate of Córdoba, drawing on local Basque communities and shifting alliances. Later kings extended their authority toward the Ebro, while partitions and succession disputes repeatedly reduced the realm. Royal marriages connected Navarre with French and Iberian dynasties, but its laws and representative institutions remained distinctive. Ferdinand of Aragon conquered most territory south of the Pyrenees in 1512, leaving a smaller northern kingdom. Henry of Navarre inherited the French crown in 1589. The surviving kingdom retained separate government until Louis XIII incorporated it into France in 1620, ending its independent political existence there.
