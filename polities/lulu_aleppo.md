description: >-
  This emirate was the government of Aleppo in northern Syria under Lu'lu' and his son
  Mansur from 1002 to 1016. Lu'lu' had risen from military service to become chamberlain and
  guardian of the Hamdanid emir, gradually exercising real power. After Sa'id al-Dawla died
  in 1002, he displaced the young Hamdanid heirs and established his own family's rule.
  Aleppo occupied a contested frontier between Byzantine power and the Fatimid caliphate.
  Lu'lu' balanced both neighbours, recognizing Fatimid supremacy while continuing relations
  and tribute payments to Byzantium. His son succeeded him in 1008 or 1009 but lacked his
  political skill. Opposition from local groups and military rivals undermined Mansur's
  government. A revolt in 1016 ended the regime, followed by administration under Fatimid
  appointees.
