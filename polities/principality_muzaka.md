description: >-
  The Principality of Muzaka was the realm of the Albanian Muzaka family, centred on
  Berat, which ruled central and southern Albania and the district of Devoll independently
  from the breakup of the Serbian Empire until the Ottoman conquest of 1417. The Muzaka
  had been Byzantine lords with the title of sebastokrator since the late thirteenth
  century, and submitted to Stefan Dušan in the 1340s. After his death they revolted, and
  by 1372 Andrea II Muzaka governed lands from the Adriatic to Korçë and from the Devoll
  to the Vjosa. He allied with the Balšić and the Angevins through marriage. His heirs
  faced growing Ottoman pressure, and the Ottomans took their lands in the campaign of
  1415 to 1417.
