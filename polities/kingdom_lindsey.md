description: >-
  Lindsey was a small Anglian kingdom in what is now northern Lincolnshire, emerging around 500. Its lands lay between the Humber and the Wash, with rivers and marshes marking much of their boundary. Lincoln, formerly Roman Lindum, was probably its principal centre. Settlement evidence points to both incoming Angles and continuing British communities. A royal genealogy survives, but it cannot securely date the rulers or show which ancestors actually held power. Lindsey became caught between Northumbria and Mercia, whose influence and control changed over time. Christian missionaries reached the region during the seventh century. The final Mercian acquisition after the Battle of the Trent in 679 ends its separate government here, although earlier stages of subjection remain difficult to reconstruct.
