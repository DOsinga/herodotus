description: >-
  The Taifa of Carmona was a Muslim kingdom in the Guadalquivir valley east of Seville, ruled by
  the Birzalid family of the Zenata Berbers. It emerged from the civil war that broke up the
  Caliphate of Córdoba after 1009, when local commanders and families took power in their own
  districts. The Birzalids held the fortified hilltop city of Carmona and the fertile countryside
  around it,. From the 1040s the expanding taifa of Seville under al-Mu'tadid absorbed one
  neighbour after another. Carmona fell to Seville in 1066. When Almoravid rule in al-Andalus
  collapsed in the 1140s, Carmona briefly governed itself again, as a second wave of small taifas
  appeared, until the Almohads from Morocco took it in 1150.
