description: >-
  The Sabir Kingdom represents a mobile governing community in the northern Caucasus during
  approximately 500 to 560. Sixth century accounts describe leaders including Balaq and his
  widow, Queen Boareks, with their own armed followers and diplomatic choices. Their origins
  and political boundaries remain uncertain, and the map point marks a region rather than a
  fixed capital. Sabir groups participated in the struggle between the Byzantine and
  Sasanian empires, sometimes raiding frontiers and sometimes serving either side. Boareks
  developed an alliance with Justinian and acted against rival leaders supporting Persia.
  Their military reputation included skilled siege equipment and service in the Lazic War.
  Avar pressure brought destruction and dispersal around 560; later Sabir mercenaries do not
  necessarily represent the same government.
