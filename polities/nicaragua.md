description: >-
  Nicaragua is a Central American state between the Pacific Ocean and Caribbean Sea,
  independent of the Central American federation since 1838. Its republican government
  developed amid rivalry between Liberal León and Conservative Granada; Managua later became
  the capital between these competing centres. Foreign intervention shaped its politics,
  including William Walker's seizure of power and United States military occupations. The
  Somoza family dominated much of the twentieth century through the presidency and National
  Guard. The Sandinista revolution overthrew that regime in 1979, followed by war against
  the Contras and an electoral transfer of power in 1990. This record treats those changes
  as stages within the same country. Nicaragua combines volcanic Pacific landscapes with
  large lakes and a culturally distinct Caribbean coast.
