description: >-
  Baden was a hereditary territory along the Upper Rhine in southwestern Germany, ruled
  by margraves of the Zähringen family. This record begins its separate government in
  1250 and follows the realm until its partition in 1535. The rulers expanded their
  lands through inheritance, purchase, and local political struggles, with Baden-Baden
  an early seat. Separate Baden-Baden and Baden-Durlach branches then governed for more
  than two centuries. Charles Frederick reunited their territories in 1771 after the
  Baden-Baden line died out. The restored state pursued reforms and gained lands during
  the restructuring of the Holy Roman Empire. Baden became an electorate in 1803, then
  joined Napoleon's Confederation of the Rhine in 1806. Its elevation to a grand duchy
  marks the successor recorded separately.
