description: >-
  Macedonia was a kingdom north of the Greek city states, traditionally dated from 808
  BCE until Rome ended its monarchy in 168 BCE. Its early royal centre was Aegae, later
  replaced by Pella. Kings governed alongside powerful nobles, military companions, and
  assemblies whose precise authority remains debated. Philip II transformed its army and
  brought much of Greece under Macedonian leadership. His son Alexander conquered the
  Persian Empire and advanced into Central Asia and India. After Alexander died,
  competing commanders divided his wider empire, while Macedonia remained a kingdom
  under successive ruling houses, eventually the Antigonids. Wars with Rome ended when
  Perseus lost at Pydna. Roman authorities removed the king and reorganised Macedonia
  into separate districts, ending the independent monarchy's history.
