description: >-
  The Motoyama were a warrior house of Tosa province in southern Shikoku, one of the seven
  lords who divided the province beside the Ichijō court nobles in the Sengoku period. Of old
  Tosa stock, they took their name from the mountain district of Motoyama in the upper Yoshino
  valley. In 1508 they led the coalition that destroyed the Chōsokabe at Okō and became the
  strongest house in Tosa. As the authority of the Hosokawa governors faded, Motoyama Shigemune
  came down to the coastal plain, made Asakura Castle his seat and ruled central Tosa around
  the site of modern Kōchi. Chōsokabe Kunichika defeated them at Nagahama in 1560, and in 1563
  Motoyama Shigetoki burned Asakura and retreated into the mountains.
