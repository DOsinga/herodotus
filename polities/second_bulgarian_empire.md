description: >-
  The Second Bulgarian Empire emerged in the Balkans in 1185 through the rebellion of Peter
  and Asen against Byzantine rule. Its tsars governed from Tarnovo, renewing Bulgarian
  statehood after more than a century under Byzantium. Kaloyan challenged the Latin Empire,
  while Ivan Asen II brought the state to a peak of influence during the thirteenth century.
  The monarchy supported an Orthodox church, and Tarnovo became a major centre of writing,
  art, and religious learning. Wars, Mongol pressure, internal conflict, and regional nobles
  later reduced royal authority. During the fourteenth century the lands fragmented as
  Ottoman armies advanced. Tarnovo fell in 1393, and Ivan Shishman's remaining government
  ended in 1395. Other Bulgarian rulers survived, but the Tarnovo centred empire was gone.
