description: >-
  Sparta was a Greek city state in the Eurotas valley of the southern Peloponnese, emerging
  around 900 BCE. Its unusual constitution combined two hereditary kings with elders, an assembly
  of male citizens and elected ephors. A small citizen body trained for war from childhood
  through state education, supported by the labour of enslaved helots and by free communities
  without full citizenship. Sparta conquered Messenia and led the Peloponnesian League, becoming
  Athens' chief rival. Victory in the Peloponnesian War brought brief dominance across Greece,
  but defeat at Leuctra in 371 BCE ended its military supremacy and cost it Messenia. Later kings
  attempted reforms and renewed expansion. Its separate government ended when the Achaean League
  forced Sparta into membership in 192 BCE.
