description: >-
  Broerec was an early Breton realm around Vannes in southern Armorica, now part of
  Brittany in France. Its beginnings are placed approximately around 490, drawing on
  uncertain traditions rather than a securely dated founding event. Migrants from
  Britain settled among the region's existing population, bringing Christian
  communities and links across the Channel. Vannes, the former Roman town of
  Darioritum, served as an episcopal centre and a seat of local rulers. The realm's
  name is associated with Waroch, though the identities and succession of its earliest
  kings remain disputed. It faced recurring pressure from Frankish rulers without
  becoming part of a securely established united Breton kingdom in 635. Pepin's capture
  of Vannes in 753 brought the area into the Breton march.
