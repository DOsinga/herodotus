description: >-
  The Kufichis, or Koch, and the Baloch were mountain tribes of southern Kerman in southeastern
  Iran, living in the ranges around Jiroft and the Jebal Barez. They were known as raiders of
  caravans and had defied earlier rulers of the province. As Saffarid power over Kerman faded
  in the early tenth century, and after Muhammad ibn Ilyas made himself ruler of the province
  in 932, the tribes kept control of their mountains and the southern lowlands and recognised
  outside authority at most loosely. Their chiefs are barely named in the sources, and they are
  known mainly from the campaigns against them. In 968 the Buyid ruler 'Adud al-Dawla conquered
  Kerman and sent his armies into the mountains to break the tribes' resistance, though they
  rose again in the following years.
