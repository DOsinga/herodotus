name: "Nabataean Kingdom"
description: >-
  The Nabataean Kingdom was an Arab monarchy centred on Petra, in present day Jordan,
  represented here from 168 BCE to 106 CE. Emerging from communities with a background
  in desert pastoralism, it grew wealthy by directing caravan routes between Arabia and
  Mediterranean markets. Its territory also included centres such as Hegra and Bosra,
  and briefly Damascus. Nabataean builders combined local traditions with outside
  influences in elaborate tombs and temples, while water management supported settlement
  in dry landscapes. Kings negotiated and fought with neighbouring powers, including the
  Hasmoneans and Romans. Roman client status still left them their own armies and
  decisions. After the death of Rabbel II, Emperor Trajan annexed the kingdom in 106,
  creating the Roman province of Arabia Petraea.
