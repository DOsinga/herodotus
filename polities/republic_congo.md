description: >-
  The Republic of the Congo gained independence from France in 1960, with Brazzaville
  as its capital. It occupies west central Africa between the Congo River and the
  Atlantic coast, distinct from the Democratic Republic of the Congo across the river.
  Presidential government succeeded colonial administration, but coups and struggles
  reshaped the state. From 1969 to 1992 it operated as the Marxist Leninist People's
  Republic of the Congo under a single ruling party. Multiparty elections followed,
  then civil wars disrupted the transition and returned former president Denis Sassou
  Nguesso to power in 1997. Petroleum exports became central to revenues and the
  economy. This record treats successive regimes as one continuing country rather than
  separate states, despite changes in constitutions and institutions.
