description: >-
  The Almoxarifate of Menorca was the Muslim state that governed Menorca as a tributary of
  the Crown of Aragon, from the Treaty of Capdepera in 1231 until Alfonso III of Aragon
  conquered the island in 1287. After James I took Majorca, the Menorcans agreed to
  recognise him and pay tribute in exchange for keeping their own government, law and
  religion. Abu Uthman Said ibn Hakam ruled from Medina Manurqa, the present Ciutadella,
  from 1234 until 1282. He was a scholar and poet who kept a learned court and managed the
  island's relations with Aragon, Majorca and North Africa. His son Abu Umar succeeded
  him, but his contacts with the Hafsids of Tunis gave Alfonso III a pretext to invade.
