description: >-
  The County of Saarbrücken was a lordship on the middle Saar, in present Saarland, governed from
  the castle of Saarbrücken. Its counts descended from a family that held the castle from the
  eleventh century and acted as advocates of the bishops of Metz, whose fief it was. When the male
  line died out in the 1270s the county passed through an heiress to the lords of Commercy in
  Lorraine, who ruled it with their French lordship from Saarbrücken. The counts served the kings
  of France and the dukes of Lorraine as often as the emperors. In 1353 the heiress Johanna
  married Count John I of Nassau-Weilburg, and on her death in 1381 their son Philip inherited
  Saarbrücken, uniting it with the Nassau lands.
