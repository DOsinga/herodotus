description: >-
  The Kingdom of Pakbul was a Maya court centred at Pomona in today's Tabasco, Mexico,
  near the Usumacinta River. Its approximate span of 500 to 900 covers the Classic
  period kingdom known through inscriptions, royal accessions and warfare. Pomona
  occupied fertile hills and comprised several dispersed groups of buildings rather than
  one compact centre. Local kings governed within a region contested by larger
  neighbours. In 751, a king took the throne under Palenque's supervision, showing how
  royal authority depended partly on outside alliances. Piedras Negras attacked in 792
  and 794, taking captives and defeating King Kuch' Bahlam. These defeats do not
  establish permanent foreign administration. Finely carved reliefs preserve courtly
  imagery, while the kingdom's final disappearance remains less clearly documented.
