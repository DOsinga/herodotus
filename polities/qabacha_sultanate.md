description: >-
  The Qabacha Sultanate was the state that Nasir ad-Din Qabacha, a Kipchak Turkish slave
  commander of Muhammad of Ghor, ruled from Uch and Multan after his master's death in 1206.
  Appointed governor of Multan in 1203, he governed upper Sindh and the southern Punjab on his
  own account, declared himself independent by 1210, and held the island fortress of Bhakkar on
  the Indus. He fought Taj al-Din Yildiz of Ghazni and Iltutmish of Delhi for Lahore, holding it
  more than once, and took in scholars and refugees fleeing the Mongols. Jalal ad-Din of
  Khwarazm ravaged his lands in the early 1220s. In 1228 Iltutmish's armies took Multan and Uch,
  and Qabacha drowned in the Indus while besieged at Bhakkar, ending the state.
