description: >-
  Tyrol and Further Austria formed the western share of the Habsburg lands after the division of
  1406, ruled by a separate branch of the Leopoldine Habsburgs. Frederick IV, known as Frederick
  of the Empty Pockets, lost most of his Swiss and Swabian lands after siding with the antipope
  John XXIII in 1415, but kept Tyrol and moved his residence to Innsbruck. His son Sigismund the
  Rich profited from the silver mines of Schwaz, which made Tyrol one of the wealthiest Habsburg
  lands, and he struck the first large silver coins. Sigismund's wars and extravagance led his
  estates to restrict his power, and in 1490 he handed Tyrol and Further Austria to his cousin
  Maximilian, reuniting the Habsburg lands.
