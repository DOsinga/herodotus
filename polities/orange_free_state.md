description: >-
  The Orange Free State was an independent Boer republic in southern Africa from 1854 to
  1902, centred on Bloemfontein between the Orange and Vaal rivers. Britain recognised its
  independence through the Bloemfontein Convention after withdrawing from the Orange River
  Sovereignty. An elected president and a legislature called the Volksraad governed the
  republic, with political rights reserved for white men. Farming sustained its economy,
  while disputes over land brought repeated wars against Moshoeshoe's Basotho kingdom.
  Diamond discoveries created further conflict over the western border and British claims.
  The republic allied with the neighbouring South African Republic against Britain in 1899.
  Despite resistance, the Second Boer War ended with surrender under the Treaty of
  Vereeniging in 1902 and incorporation into British territory.
