description: >-
  The Second Turkic Khaganate was a nomadic empire centred on the Orkhon region of present
  day Mongolia from 682 to 744. Ilterish of the Ashina clan established it through revolt
  against Tang rule, restoring a Turkic royal government after decades of Chinese
  domination. Its khagans governed through relatives, commanders, and alliances with subject
  tribes, while their mobile court was associated with the sacred region of Otuken. Qapaghan
  expanded the empire, and Bilge Khagan, his brother Kul Tigin, and the adviser Tonyukuk
  became central figures in its history. The Orkhon inscriptions preserve their accounts of
  war, leadership, and relations with China. Succession struggles weakened the ruling house.
  A coalition involving Uyghurs, Karluks, and Basmils overthrew it in 744, enabling Uyghur
  rule.
