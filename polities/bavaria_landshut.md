description: >-
  Bavaria-Landshut was a Wittelsbach duchy governing much of Lower Bavaria from Landshut. This record includes the territory's first separate government after Bavaria's partition in 1255, its reunion under Louis IV in 1340, and renewed separation in 1349. Further family divisions reshaped its lands, with Munich and Ingolstadt eventually governed by other branches. The Landshut dukes exercised territorial authority within the Holy Roman Empire and became wealthy during the fifteenth century. They absorbed Bavaria-Ingolstadt in 1447, while Louis the Rich founded the university at Ingolstadt. George the Rich's marriage to the Polish princess Hedwig inspired the celebrated Landshut Wedding. His death without a surviving son in 1503 triggered a succession war, after which Bavaria-Munich obtained most of his disputed Bavarian inheritance.
