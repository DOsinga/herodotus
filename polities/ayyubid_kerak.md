description: >-
  The Ayyubid Emirate of Kerak was a principality east of the Dead Sea, centred on the
  castle of Kerak and covering much of Transjordan. It began in 1229, when an-Nasir Dawud,
  son of the Ayyubid ruler of Damascus, lost that city to his uncles al-Kamil and al-Ashraf
  and was left with Kerak. From there he played a long game of shifting alliances
  between his Ayyubid relatives and the Crusaders, briefly occupying Jerusalem in 1239 and
  holding the future sultan as-Salih Ayyub prisoner. He lost his lands west of the Jordan in
  1245 and was pushed out of Kerak in 1248. His son al-Mughith Umar took the castle again
  around 1250 and held it against the new Mamluk regime in Egypt until Baybars captured him
  and annexed Kerak in 1263.
