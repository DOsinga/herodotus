description: >-
  The County of Schwerin was a county in western Mecklenburg, created in 1160 when Henry the Lion
  conquered the Obotrites and made his follower Gunzelin of Hagen count at the old Slavic
  stronghold of Schwerin. Gunzelin's descendants ruled it for two centuries beside the Obotrite
  princes of Mecklenburg. Count Henry the Black captured King Valdemar II of Denmark in 1223,
  which broke Danish power on the southern Baltic. The counts later divided their land into the
  lines of Schwerin and Wittenburg, and their power declined. The cathedral town of Schwerin grew
  up beside the castle. In 1358 Duke Albert II of Mecklenburg bought the county, and Schwerin
  became the capital of the Mecklenburg dukes.
