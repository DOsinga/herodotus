description: >-
  The Mahameghavahana kingdom was a monarchy in Kalinga, on India's eastern coast,
  represented here between an estimated 225 BCE and about 100 CE. Its history is known
  chiefly through Kharavela's Hathigumpha inscription near present Bhubaneswar, rather
  than a sequence of records. The inscription celebrates military expeditions, repairs to
  public works, entertainments and religious gifts. Kharavela is associated particularly
  with Jainism, and the nearby caves preserve evidence of patronage for ascetics. These
  claims show an ambitious royal court, but their dates, geographical references and
  interpretation remain debated. Later kings such as Vakradeva are known from short cave
  inscriptions, and the Sada kings of the Krishna delta claimed descent from the family.
  Kalinganagara serves as the representative capital. The Murunda kings followed in the
  second century.
