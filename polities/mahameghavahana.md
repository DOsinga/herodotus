description: >-
  The Mahameghavahana kingdom was a monarchy in Kalinga, on India's eastern coast,
  represented here between an estimated 225 BCE and the beginning of the Common Era. Its
  history is known chiefly through Kharavela's Hathigumpha inscription near present
  Bhubaneswar, rather than a sequence of records. The inscription celebrates military
  expeditions, repairs to public works, entertainments and religious gifts. Kharavela is
  associated particularly with Jainism, and the nearby caves preserve evidence of
  patronage for ascetics. These claims show an ambitious royal court, but their dates,
  geographical references and interpretation remain debated. Kalinganagara serves as the
  representative capital in this record. The dynasty article also discusses later
  developments; this entry follows the narrower, uncertain early kingdom whose
  subsequent political history is poorly documented.
