description: >-
  The Beylik of Constantine was the eastern province of the Regency of Algiers, centred on the
  city of Constantine, and it governed itself after the French took Algiers in 1830. Its bey,
  Ahmed Bey, had been appointed in 1826 and had begun to modernise the army and administration
  on the model of Muhammad Ali's Egypt. He refused to accept the dey's surrender, led the
  resistance in the east and in 1833 claimed the title of dey of Algiers. The province was the
  richest and most populous of the regency, and its bey ruled through alliances with the great
  tribal families. Constantine beat off a French assault in 1836, but fell to a second siege
  in 1837. Ahmed Bey continued fighting from the Aurès until 1848.
