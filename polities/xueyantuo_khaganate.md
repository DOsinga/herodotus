name: "Xueyantuo Khaganate"
description: >-
  The Xueyantuo Khaganate ruled a steppe confederation in Mongolia and neighboring northern
  regions from 628 to 646. Yi'nan established its royal government as the Eastern Turkic
  Khaganate weakened, receiving recognition from Tang China. The Xueyantuo belonged to the
  Tiele grouping and initially cooperated with the Tang against their former Turkic
  overlords. After the Turks' defeat, Yi'nan became a major power north of China, governing
  pastoral peoples through a khagan and subordinate leaders. Relations with the Tang
  deteriorated over control of the steppe and attempts to restore Turkic communities.
  Yi'nan's death brought succession struggles and rebellion among subject peoples. Tang
  armies and Uyghur forces destroyed the royal government in 646; later references to
  Xueyantuo people do not indicate continued independent rule.
