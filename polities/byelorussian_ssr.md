description: >-
  The Byelorussian Soviet Socialist Republic was reestablished in 1920 with its capital at Minsk, after earlier Soviet experiments collapsed during
  the Polish Soviet War. This record covers its separate existence before joining the Soviet Union in 1922. Communist leaders governed
  through soviets and a party organisation closely tied to Soviet Russia. War and diplomacy shaped its small initial territory: the
  Treaty of Riga left western Belarus under Polish rule, while other Belarusian lands remained within Russia. The new government faced
  damaged towns, economic disruption, and opposition to Bolshevik power, including the Slutsk uprising. In December 1922, Byelorussia joined Russia, Ukraine,
  and the Transcaucasian republic as a founding member of the Soviet Union. Its republican institutions continued within that larger state.
