description: >-
  Maranga was a center in Peru's Rimac valley, within the area now occupied by
  metropolitan Lima. This record represents an estimated regional chiefdom from about
  100 to 750 CE, rather than every community that used Lima-style pottery. Large
  ceremonial buildings and the labor required for construction and irrigation point to
  organized leadership, although no named rulers or detailed government survive. People
  in the surrounding desert valleys farmed irrigated land and drew food from the Pacific
  coast. Distinctive ceramics and textiles link Maranga with neighboring Lima culture
  centers and changing regional networks. Later artistic influences associated with Wari
  do not by themselves establish conquest. The endpoint marks the estimated end of
  Maranga's governing role in the terminal Lima sequence, not abandonment.
