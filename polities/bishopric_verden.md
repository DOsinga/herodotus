description: >-
  The Prince-Bishopric of Verden was the territory of the bishops of Verden on the lower Aller in
  northern Germany, between the archbishopric of Bremen and the Guelph duchy of Lüneburg. The see
  was founded in the time of Charlemagne. Its bishops ruled a small territory around Verden and
  Rotenburg, where they usually lived, and were overshadowed by their powerful neighbours. In the
  sixteenth century the see passed to Protestant administrators, often the same princes who held
  Bremen, and the land became Lutheran. During the Thirty Years' War Danish and imperial forces
  fought over it. The Peace of Westphalia of 1648 turned it into the secular Duchy of Verden and
  gave it to Sweden.
