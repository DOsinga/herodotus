description: >-
  The Duchy of Naples was a coastal state in southern Italy whose practical independence
  developed around 770. It originated as a Byzantine province, but local dukes
  increasingly managed their own affairs as imperial assistance declined. Stephen II's
  turn toward the papacy marked this shift, while Latin gradually replaced Greek in
  official use. The duchy included Naples and nearby lands and islands, although
  neighbouring ports such as Amalfi and Gaeta developed separate governments. Ducal rule
  became hereditary, supported and contested by prominent city families. Naples survived
  through trade, diplomacy, and alliances against Lombard and later Norman pressure,
  sometimes cooperating with Muslim powers. Norman expansion finally prevailed. After the
  last duke died in 1137, Roger II brought Naples under Sicily's royal government.
