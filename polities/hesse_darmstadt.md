description: >-
  Hesse-Darmstadt was a hereditary landgraviate in central Germany from 1567 to 1806. It
  arose when Philip the Magnanimous divided Hesse among his four sons, with George I
  receiving the southern Hessian lands around Darmstadt. The ruling branch of the House of
  Hesse made that town its residence. Its territory later included several disconnected
  districts, rather than one compact province. Lutheran Hesse-Darmstadt disputed the
  inheritance of Hesse-Marburg with Calvinist Hesse-Kassel, a rivalry intensified by the
  Thirty Years' War. Louis V founded the University of Giessen in 1607 to provide a
  Lutheran alternative to Marburg. Subsequent rulers expanded their court and lands within
  the Holy Roman Empire. Napoleon's reorganisation of Germany transformed the landgraviate
  into the Grand Duchy of Hesse in 1806.
