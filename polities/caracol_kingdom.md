description: >-
  Caracol was a Maya kingdom in the forested uplands of present Belize, with a royal foundation
  traditionally dated to 331 CE. Its kings ruled from a sprawling centre on the Vaca Plateau, where
  palaces, temples, reservoirs and agricultural terraces supported a large population. Inscriptions
  record royal successions, ceremonies and warfare with other Maya courts. Caracol defeated Tikal in
  562, then expanded its influence through further campaigns and alliances, including connections with
  Calakmul. Roads linked the ceremonial core with surrounding neighbourhoods and outlying centres,
  showing how widely settlement spread. Monument building and royal activity continued into the ninth
  century. Central government probably collapsed around 900, although occupation lasted beyond the end
  of the kingdom and the abandonment of its royal buildings.
