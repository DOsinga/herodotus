description: >-
  The Kingdom of Holland was a short-lived monarchy in the Netherlands from 1806 to 1810,
  created when Napoleon replaced the Batavian Republic and installed his brother Louis
  Bonaparte as king. Although closely tied to France, Louis tried to govern in Dutch
  interests, taking disaster relief and the country's economic difficulties seriously.
  Amsterdam became the capital after earlier royal seats at The Hague and Utrecht. The
  kingdom also acquired East Frisia and Jever. Conflict with Napoleon grew over military
  demands and restrictions on trade, which hurt an economy dependent on shipping. French
  troops occupied territory, and Louis abdicated in favour of his young son. Napoleon
  instead annexed the kingdom in July 1810, bringing its separate administration under
  direct French imperial rule.
