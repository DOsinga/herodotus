description: >-
  Ekron was a Philistine city kingdom on the southern coastal plain of the Levant, identified with Tel Miqne in present Israel. Its Philistine settlement is placed around 1175 BCE, following an earlier Canaanite town. One of the five principal Philistine cities, Ekron had its own ruler and stood near the contested frontier with Judah. Distinctive excavated pottery reflects connections with the Aegean world, while extensive later buildings and inscriptions document a wealthy urban centre. Under Assyrian influence, its kings participated in regional struggles and its economy expanded dramatically. Numerous presses reveal large scale olive oil production during the seventh century BCE. A royal dedicatory inscription confirms the city's identity. Nebuchadnezzar's Babylonian forces destroyed Ekron in 604 BCE, ending this kingdom.
