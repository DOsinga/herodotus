description: >-
  Early Adal stands for the Muslim port state that governed Zeila on the Gulf of Aden coast of
  present Somaliland before the Sultanate of Ifat. Muslims settled in the town soon after the
  Hijra, and its two-mihrab mosque is among the oldest in Africa. In the late ninth century the
  geographer al-Yaqubi described a kingdom of Adal with its capital at Zeila, and later Arab
  writers knew the Muslim lands of the interior collectively as the country of Zeila. Local
  dynasties of Arab and Somali descent seem to have ruled, trading incense, myrrh, hides and
  slaves with Arabia and the Ethiopian highlands. Little else is known of its government or its
  rulers. In the late thirteenth century the Walashma sultans of Ifat brought Zeila and Adal under
  their rule.
