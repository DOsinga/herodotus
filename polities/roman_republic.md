description: >-
  The Roman Republic began, according to tradition, with the expulsion of Rome's kings in 509 BCE and ended with Octavian's settlement in 27 BCE. Elected magistrates, especially two annual consuls, shared government with the Senate and citizen assemblies, although wealthy families dominated political office. Conflicts between patricians and plebeians shaped its institutions and expanded access to public and political rights. From its base in central Italy, Rome built alliances, conquered the peninsula, defeated Carthage, and established provinces across the Mediterranean. Expansion brought wealth, enslaved labour, and sharp disputes over land and citizenship. Increasingly powerful commanders turned political rivalries into civil wars. Julius Caesar's dictatorship and assassination preceded Octavian's victory, which preserved republican forms while placing effective power in one ruler.
