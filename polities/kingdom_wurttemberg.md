name: Kingdom of Württemberg
description: >-
  The Kingdom of Württemberg was a German monarchy centred on Stuttgart, represented as independently governed from
  1806 to 1871. Frederick gained royal rank during Napoleon's reorganization of Germany, after territorial expansion had
  added former church lands and imperial cities. The kingdom joined the Confederation of the Rhine and
  supplied troops for French campaigns before changing sides against Napoleon. It subsequently belonged to the German
  Confederation. William I introduced a constitution in 1819, establishing representative institutions alongside the crown. Agricultural reforms,
  railways and membership in the Zollverein helped economic development. Württemberg supported Austria against Prussia in 1866
  but joined the German Empire in 1871. Its monarchy survived until 1918, while imperial control of
  war and foreign policy ended its international government.
