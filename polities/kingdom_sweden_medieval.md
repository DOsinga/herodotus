description: >-
  The medieval Kingdom of Sweden developed across the lands of the Svear and Götar in Scandinavia. Its beginning here around 970 is an estimate associated with Eric the Victorious; his son Olof Skötkonung is the first king clearly known to have ruled both peoples. Royal power grew unevenly alongside regional assemblies, great families and an expanding Christian church. Uppsala represents an important religious and political centre rather than an unchanging capital. Dynastic struggles gave way to stronger institutions, while expansion eastward brought parts of Finland under Swedish rule. Medieval kings developed law codes, taxation and a privileged nobility, supported by castles and towns. In 1397 Sweden entered the Kalmar Union with Denmark and Norway, ending the separate period recorded here.
