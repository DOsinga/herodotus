description: >-
  Karamanli Tripolitania was a hereditary government based in Tripoli, in present day Libya,
  from 1711 to 1835. Ahmed Karamanli seized power from the Ottoman governor and secured
  recognition as pasha, while directing local affairs independently. His family's influence
  extended into Fezzan and Cyrenaica, though its reach varied. Maritime raiding, tribute
  payments from foreign shipping powers, and trade helped support the state. Dynastic
  conflict allowed the Ottoman officer Ali Burghul to interrupt Karamanli rule in 1793,
  before the family expelled him in January 1794. Yusuf Karamanli later fought the United
  States in the First Barbary War. Debt, declining revenues, and renewed succession conflict
  weakened his successors. Ottoman troops removed the dynasty in 1835 and restored
  provincial government over Tripoli and surroundings.
