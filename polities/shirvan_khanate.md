description: >-
  The Shirvan Khanate was a khanate in eastern Transcaucasia, centred on Shamakhi in
  present-day Azerbaijan, which governed itself from the 1760s until it accepted Russian
  protection in 1805. After Nader Shah's death the old Shirvan region broke up among rival
  lords, and the brothers Muhammad Said Khan and Aghasi Khan established themselves at Old
  and New Shamakhi, the modern Aghsu. Fath Ali Khan of Quba overran the khanate in 1768 and
  held it for two decades. After his death in 1789 Muhammad Said's son Mustafa Khan restored
  the khanate and moved its seat to the mountain fortress of Fit-dagh. He fought the
  neighbouring khans and Qajar Iran in turn. Mustafa Khan accepted Russian protection in
  1805 but later fled to Iran, and Russia abolished the khanate in 1820.
