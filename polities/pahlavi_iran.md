description: >-
  Pahlavi Iran was the Iranian monarchy governed from Tehran by Reza Shah and his son
  Mohammad Reza Shah between 1925 and 1979. Reza replaced the Qajar dynasty and pursued a
  central government, new roads, railways and secular education. Britain and the Soviet
  Union invaded in 1941, forcing his abdication in favour of his son. Constitutional
  institutions and a parliament existed, but royal power increasingly dominated them. The
  struggle over oil nationalisation culminated in the 1953 coup against Prime Minister
  Mohammad Mosaddegh. Oil income later funded rapid development and the Shah's White
  Revolution reforms, while political repression and unequal gains fuelled opposition.
  Mass protests overthrew the monarchy in 1979, and the Islamic Republic replaced the
  Pahlavi state after the Iranian Revolution.
