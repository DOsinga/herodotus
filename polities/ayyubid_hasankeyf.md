description: >-
  The Ayyubids of Hasankeyf were the longest-lived branch of Saladin's family, governing the
  fortress town of Hisn Kayfa on the upper Tigris, in what is now southeastern Turkey, from
  the fall of the Ayyubid sultanate in Egypt in 1250 until 1524. Al-Kamil had taken the town
  from the Artuqids in 1232 and given it to his son as-Salih Ayyub, whose descendants kept
  it after the family lost Egypt and Syria. Their small principality survived by accepting
  the overlordship of the Mongol Ilkhans, the Qara Qoyunlu, the Aq Qoyunlu and the Safavids
  in turn, while running its own affairs. The emirs patronised scholars and built mosques
  and tombs in the town. The Ottomans took Hasankeyf after their conquest of the region and
  ended the dynasty in 1524.
