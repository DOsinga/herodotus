description: >-
  The Empire of Great Fulo, or Denianke Kingdom, was a Fula state that dominated Futa Toro in the
  middle Senegal valley from the early sixteenth century to 1776. After Songhai defeated and
  killed his father Tenguella in 1512, Koli Tenguella led an armed migration from Futa Jallon, re-
  established his family's rule in Futa Toro and fixed his capital at Tumbere-Jiinde. His
  campaigns against the Jolof Empire hastened its breakup into rival kingdoms. The Denianke
  rulers, with the title satigi, governed a large realm and claimed Jolof and Waalo as vassals,
  but later reigns were marked by succession struggles, Moorish intervention and instability. In
  1776 Sulayman Bal led a Muslim revolution that overthrew the dynasty and founded the Imamate of
  Futa Toro.
