description: >-
  The Sultanate of Lakhnauti was the first independent Muslim kingdom of Bengal, ruled from
  Lakhnauti, the old Gauda, between 1287 and 1324. Nasiruddin Bughra Khan, son of the Delhi
  sultan Balban, had governed Bengal for his father and declared himself sultan when Balban
  died. His own son Kaiqubad, now sultan of Delhi, accepted this when the two armies met on
  the Saryu. Bughra Khan gave way to his son Rukunuddin Kaikaus in 1291, and Shamsuddin Firuz
  Shah, probably one of Bughra Khan's officials, took the throne in 1301. Firuz Shah carried
  the kingdom east to Sonargaon, Satgaon, Mymensingh and Sylhet, conquered in 1303. Quarrels
  among his sons let Ghiyasuddin Tughlaq of Delhi invade in 1324 and divide Bengal into
  provinces.
