description: >-
  Li Maozhen's Qi was a principality in the Wei valley west of Chang'an at the end of the Tang
  dynasty and the start of the Five Dynasties, centred on Fengxiang and lasting from 887 to
  924. Li Maozhen, an officer of the imperial guards, became military governor of Fengxiang in
  887 and soon dominated the Tang court, at times holding the emperor himself. The rival
  warlord Zhu Quanzhong besieged Fengxiang in 902 and 903 and took much of his land. Li
  refused to recognise Zhu's Later Liang in 907 and ruled as Prince of Qi with a court of
  imperial style, though he never claimed the throne. Wars with Former Shu reduced Qi to the
  country around Fengxiang. Li submitted to the Later Tang in 924 and died that year.
