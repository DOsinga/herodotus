description: >-
  The southern government in Guangzhou was the rival Chinese republican government set up in 1917
  by Sun Yat-sen and southern military leaders, who refused to recognise the Beiyang government in
  Beijing after it dissolved the parliament. Its first form was the Constitutional Protection
  junta, a military government backed by the warlords of Guangdong, Guangxi and Yunnan. After
  quarrels with these allies drove Sun out and back again, he led a military government from 1921
  and, with Soviet help, reorganised the Kuomintang and founded the Whampoa Military Academy. In
  1925 it became the National Government. Its Northern Expedition from 1926 defeated the northern
  warlords, and in 1928 it took Beijing and became the government of all China.
