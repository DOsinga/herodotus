description: >-
  Characene was a kingdom at the head of the Persian Gulf, mainly in modern southern Iraq,
  from 141 BCE to about 222 CE. Hyspaosines established its royal government as Seleucid
  authority weakened. Its capital, Charax Spasinou, developed from a Hellenistic foundation
  into an important port connecting Mesopotamia with India and other Gulf destinations. The
  kingdom often acknowledged Parthian overlordship while retaining its own rulers and coinage.
  Coins, inscriptions and scattered written accounts provide much of the evidence for its
  history. Greek appeared on early coins, while Aramaic became prominent later; royal names
  reflected Iranian connections. Characene also supported merchants travelling toward inland
  cities. Ardashir of Persis conquered it around 222, before his decisive overthrow of the
  Parthian Empire further north.
