description: >-
  The Banu Hilal were a confederation of Arab Bedouin tribes who migrated from Upper Egypt
  into Ifriqiya in the eleventh century, recorded here as a chiefdom from 1057 to 1159. The
  Fatimid caliphs sent them against the Zirids after the Zirids renounced Fatimid authority,
  and they defeated the Zirid army at Haydaran in 1052 and sacked Kairouan in 1057. Their
  clans, such as the Riyah, Athbaj and Zughba, held the plains of central Tunisia and eastern
  Algeria under their own chiefs, taking tribute from towns and fighting both for and against
  the Zirids and Hammadids. Their arrival spread nomadism and Arabic across the countryside.
  The Almohads defeated them at Sétif in 1153 and conquered Ifriqiya in 1159, moving many of
  their tribes to Morocco.
