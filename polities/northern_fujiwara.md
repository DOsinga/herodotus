description: >-
  The Northern Fujiwara were a warrior house that ruled the far north of Honshu, the
  provinces of Mutsu and Dewa, as their own realm from 1087 to 1189. Their founder Fujiwara
  no Kiyohira, of mixed court and Emishi descent, emerged as master of the north after the
  Later Three Years' War and built a capital at Hiraizumi. Gold mining, horses and trade with
  the far north and the continent made the house rich, and Kiyohira, Motohira and Hidehira
  filled Hiraizumi with temples such as Chūson-ji in the style of Kyoto. Governors sent from
  the capital had little say. The family stayed neutral in the Genpei War but sheltered
  Minamoto no Yoshitsune, and in 1189 Minamoto no Yoritomo invaded and destroyed it.
