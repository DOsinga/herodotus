description: >-
  The Kingdom of Ireland existed from 1542 to 1800, interrupted by Commonwealth rule during the mid-seventeenth century. Henry VIII replaced the older lordship with a royal title, sharing Ireland's crown with England and later Great Britain. Administration centred on Dublin Castle, a royal viceroy and an Irish parliament. Tudor conquest, plantations and religious conflict transformed landholding and political power across the island. The seventeenth century brought rebellion, Confederate government, Cromwellian conquest and restoration of the monarchy in 1660. Protestant elites dominated the later parliament, while laws restricted the Catholic majority. Legislative independence expanded in 1782, but the rebellion of 1798 renewed pressure for union. The Acts of Union abolished the separate parliament and created the United Kingdom from January 1801.
