description: >-
  The early Duchy of Swabia emerged in southwestern East Francia from struggles among counts and
  church leaders after Carolingian authority weakened. This record covers its separate ducal
  government from 915 to 926, not the much longer history of the ducal title. Erchanger asserted power
  after defeating King Conrad's forces, but was executed in 917. Burchard II then secured the duchy,
  defending its lands against Upper Burgundy and negotiating recognition from Henry I. The region
  included areas around the upper Rhine, Lake Constance, and upper Danube, with Hohentwiel
  representing an early stronghold rather than a permanent capital. Burchard died campaigning in Italy
  in 926. Henry installed Hermann, bringing ducal appointment under royal control, although Swabia
  continued as a major German duchy.
