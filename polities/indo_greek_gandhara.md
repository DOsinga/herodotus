name: "Indo-Greek kingdom of Gandhara"
wikipedia: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Archebius
description: >-
  This Indo Greek kingdom governed Gandhara around Taxila, in present day Pakistan, approximately
  from 100 to 80 BCE. Its chronology comes mainly from coins rather than a continuous written
  political history. Archebius, its last recorded king, ruled near the end of this period and
  issued silver and bronze money showing royal portraits and Greek gods. Bilingual legends used
  Greek and Kharoshthi, linking the monarchy's Hellenistic claims with local traditions of
  kingship. Coin designs and overstrikes help scholars place Archebius among neighbouring rulers,
  although his family connections remain uncertain. This was one of several Indo Greek
  governments, not a single kingdom embracing all their territories. The Saka ruler Maues
  conquered Taxila around 80 BCE, ending the separate Gandharan court represented here.
