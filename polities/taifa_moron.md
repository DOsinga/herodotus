description: >-
  The Taifa of Morón was a small Muslim kingdom south of Seville, centred on the fortified town of
  Morón de la Frontera. Its rulers were the Banu Dammar, a family of the Zenata Berbers. It
  emerged from the civil war that broke up the Caliphate of Córdoba after 1009, when local
  commanders and families took power in their own districts. The Banu Dammar governed Morón and
  its farming country as one of a cluster of Berber principalities around the lower Guadalquivir,
  which together resisted the ambitions of Seville's Arab rulers. From the 1040s the expanding
  taifa of Seville under al-Mu'tadid absorbed one neighbour after another. In 1066 Seville took
  Morón, ending the Banu Dammar's government.
