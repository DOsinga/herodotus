description: >-
  The Beylik of Tunis governed much of present Tunisia from 1705 until the French
  protectorate of 1881. Hussein ibn Ali founded its Husainid ruling house after
  military and political turmoil in the Ottoman province. The beys acknowledged the
  Ottoman sultan but ran their own army, finances, and diplomacy from Tunis. Rivalries
  within the dynasty and intervention from Algiers disrupted its early decades.
  Nineteenth century rulers pursued reforms, including the abolition of slavery and the
  adoption of a constitution in 1861. Reform expenses, heavy borrowing, and taxation
  helped produce rebellion and foreign financial supervision. French invasion brought
  the Treaty of Bardo in 1881. The dynasty remained on the throne afterward, but France
  took control of the country's external affairs and administration.
