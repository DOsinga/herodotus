description: >-
  Tarentum, known to its Greek inhabitants as Taras, was a city state on the gulf of
  present day southern Italy bearing its name. Tradition dates its foundation by settlers
  from Sparta to 706 BCE. Its sheltered natural harbour supported trade, fishing, crafts,
  and a wealthy urban community within Magna Graecia. Government changed over time,
  including democratic institutions and elected military commanders. The philosopher
  Archytas repeatedly served as general during the fourth century BCE. Tarentum fought
  neighbouring Italian peoples and sought outside Greek military help when its position
  weakened. Conflict with Rome brought Pyrrhus of Epirus to Italy in 280 BCE. After his
  departure, Tarentum surrendered in 272 BCE and became a Roman ally, losing independent
  control of war and diplomacy.
