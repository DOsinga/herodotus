name: "Kingdom of Bodeok"
description: >-
  Bodeok was a small Korean kingdom maintained by Goguryeo refugees under King Anseung between
  670 and 683. It emerged after Tang and Silla destroyed Goguryeo, when the general Geom Mojam
  sought to restore a royal court. The first restoration movement operated in the north, but
  Silla recognised Anseung and provided territory near present Iksan. This made Bodeok a
  refuge for displaced Goguryeo people and a useful ally against Tang ambitions on the
  peninsula. Anseung retained kingship under Silla overlordship, although internal tensions
  led to Geom Mojam's death. Silla later abolished the separate court amid political unrest.
  In 683 Anseung moved to Gyeongju, received rank and land, and entered Silla's aristocracy.
  The refugee population was dispersed among settlements in the south.
