description: >-
  Galuh was a Sundanese kingdom in western Java, centred on the eastern Parahyangan region. Its traditional foundation in 669, following separation from Tarumanagara, comes from later chronicles and remains disputed. Kings governed a region shaped by Hindu, Buddhist and local religious traditions, with changing seats and close dynastic ties to neighbouring Sunda. This record follows the eastern Galuh court where it governed separately, rather than assuming one continuous government for all Sundanese lands. Kawali became an important royal seat in the fourteenth century. Its Astana Gede inscriptions provide firmer evidence than the stories surrounding the kingdom's earliest rulers. In 1482 Sri Baduga united Sunda and Galuh under one ruler, ending the separate eastern kingdom while preserving much of its inheritance.
