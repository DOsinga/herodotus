description: >-
  The united monarchy is the kingdom of David and Solomon in the Hebrew Bible, ruling the
  northern tribes and Judah from Jerusalem. The record starts with David's traditional capture
  of Jerusalem around 1000 BCE and leaves out Saul's earlier kingdom. Whether the state
  existed, and how large, is disputed, and no royal inscription survives. Minimalists such as
  Israel Finkelstein move the buildings credited to Solomon into the ninth century and see at
  most a highland chiefdom in Judah. Maximalists largely accept the biblical account, and
  moderates such as Amihai Mazar a modest tenth century state. The ninth century Tel Dan stele
  names a "House of David", and the fortified town of Khirbet Qeiyafa suggests central power in
  Judah, though both readings are contested. The kingdom split after Solomon's death.
