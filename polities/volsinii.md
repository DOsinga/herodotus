description: >-
  Volsinii, known in Etruscan as Velzna, was a major city of ancient Etruria in central Italy,
  represented here from about 750 to 264 BCE. Its original location is debated, although Orvieto
  is a strong candidate and the marker follows that identification. The city belonged to the
  Etruscan league and may have stood near the important sanctuary of Voltumna, where Etruscan
  communities gathered. Literary accounts describe great wealth and political conflict between
  the governing aristocracy and people identified as slaves or freedmen. Those accounts are later
  and should not be treated as transparent descriptions of its institutions. Roman intervention
  ended with the city's destruction in 264 BCE. Surviving inhabitants were moved to a new
  settlement near Lake Bolsena under Roman authority.
