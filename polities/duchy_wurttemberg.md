description: >-
  The Duchy of Württemberg was a hereditary state around Stuttgart and the Neckar valley
  in southwestern Germany. Raised from a county in 1495, it combined ducal government
  with influential estates representing towns and religious institutions. Duke Ulrich's
  conflicts brought expulsion by the Swabian League in 1519, followed by Habsburg
  administration. Philip of Hesse restored him in 1534, and Württemberg adopted the
  Lutheran Reformation. The duchy suffered heavily during the Thirty Years' War and
  later invasions along the contested frontier between French and Austrian power. Its
  rulers supported universities, music and palace building, while disputes over taxation
  repeatedly tested their relations with the estates. Territorial expansion accompanied
  its elevation to an electorate in 1803. In 1806 it became a kingdom instead.
