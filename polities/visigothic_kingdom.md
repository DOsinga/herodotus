description: >-
  The Visigothic Kingdom began in 418 with a Roman settlement in southwestern Gaul and later
  governed much of Iberia. Its first major court was at Toulouse. Frankish victories drove the
  kingdom south, while Theodoric's direct Ostrogothic administration interrupted separate
  government from 511 to 526. Afterwards, kings consolidated authority in Spain, eventually
  making Toledo their principal seat. Royal power depended on cooperation with nobles and,
  after conversion from Arian Christianity, Catholic bishops meeting in church councils.
  Lawmaking helped unite Gothic and Roman populations, but disputed successions repeatedly
  destabilised the monarchy. Muslim armies defeated the main kingdom in 711. Royal government
  survived briefly under Achila and Ardo in northeastern Iberia and Septimania, before the
  final conquest ended its separate existence in 721.
