  Brunei is a small state on Borneo's northern coast, independent since 1984 after decades
  of British protection. Its two separated territories face the South China Sea and are
  surrounded by Malaysia's Sarawak state. Bandar Seri Begawan is the capital. The sultan
  heads a monarchy whose official identity joins Malay culture, Islam and royal government.
  An appointed legislature and a legal system drawing on English common law and Islamic law
  support that framework. Oil and natural gas exports provide much of the country's income
  and finance public services. Brunei remained outside the Malaysian federation and entered
  ASEAN shortly after independence. The modern state continues an older sultanate, but this
  record begins when Britain relinquished responsibility for its defence and external
  affairs.
