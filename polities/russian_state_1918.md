description: >-
  The Russian State was the anti-Bolshevik government based at Omsk during the Russian Civil
  War, from 1918 to 1920. It grew from the Provisional All-Russian Government formed at Ufa,
  which claimed authority over a reunited Russia. A coup in November 1918 replaced its
  directory with Admiral Alexander Kolchak as Supreme Ruler. Ministers and military commanders
  administered territory controlled by White armies, especially in Siberia, while other White
  leaders recognised Kolchak's seniority without creating close operational unity. Allied
  powers supplied assistance, but pursued their own interests and did not grant it general
  diplomatic recognition. Initial advances against Soviet forces were reversed in 1919. The
  loss of Omsk and retreat eastward destroyed the regime; Kolchak surrendered power and was
  executed in 1920.
