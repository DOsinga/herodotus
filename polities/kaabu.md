description: >-
  Kaabu was a federation of Mandinka kingdoms in the upper Senegambia, centred on the
  northeast of present-day Guinea-Bissau and reaching into the Gambia and the Casamance. It
  began as a western military province of the Mali Empire, and its rulers governed on their
  own account once Mali's power declined in the sixteenth century; 1537 is the conventional
  date of independence. The mansa ba ruled from Kansala, with the kingship rotating among the
  leading provinces, and a warrior aristocracy, the nyancho, supplied its strength. Kaabu sold
  captives and other goods to Portuguese and later traders on the coast and rivers. Wars with
  the Fula of Futa Jallon wore it down in the nineteenth century, and their siege of Kansala
  in 1867 ended the federation, although smaller successor states survived.
