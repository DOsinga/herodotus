description: >-
  Sitawaka was a Sinhalese kingdom in southwestern Sri Lanka from 1521 to 1594. It emerged
  when three brothers divided Kotte after overthrowing their father, with Mayadunne establishing his court near
  modern Avissawella. From this inland base, the monarchy became the principal opponent of Portuguese expansion
  and of Kotte's alliance with Portugal. Mayadunne absorbed Raigama, and his son Rajasinha won an
  important victory at Mulleriyawa before extending control across much of the island. Portuguese possession of
  Colombo and other coastal positions nevertheless prevented complete success. Rajasinha also conquered Kandy, where resistance
  later revived. His religious policies and harsh government deepened opposition. After his death in 1593,
  succession struggles and defections rapidly broke the kingdom apart, ending its brief period of power.
