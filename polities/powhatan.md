description: >-
  The Powhatan Confederacy was a paramount chiefdom of Algonquian-speaking peoples in the
  Tidewater region of Virginia, which its people called Tsenacommacah. Wahunsenacawh, known
  to the English as Powhatan, inherited six chiefdoms and extended his rule over about
  thirty, whose weroances paid him tribute in corn, deerskins and copper. He ruled from
  Werowocomoco on the York River when the English founded Jamestown in 1607, and he moved
  inland to Orapax in 1609. The first Anglo-Powhatan War ended with the marriage of his
  daughter Pocahontas to John Rolfe in 1614. His brother Opechancanough led great attacks on
  the colony in 1622 and 1644. After Opechancanough's capture and death in 1646, a treaty
  made the surviving peoples tributaries of Virginia.
