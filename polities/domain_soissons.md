description: >-
  The Domain of Soissons was a surviving center of Roman military authority in northern Gaul
  during the fifth century, represented here from 461 to 486. Aegidius, appointed commander of the
  Roman forces in Gaul, established a power base around Soissons and opposed the western court
  after Majorian's overthrow. His son Syagrius later governed there, drawing on Roman troops and
  society amid expanding Frankish and Visigothic power. Little evidence survives for its
  institutions or territorial extent. The familiar name Kingdom of Soissons comes from later
  descriptions, and does not prove that its rulers adopted kingship themselves. Clovis defeated
  Syagrius in 486 and incorporated the domain into his Frankish realm. Syagrius fled to the
  Visigoths, who surrendered him to Clovis for execution.
