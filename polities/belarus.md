  Belarus is a landlocked republic in eastern Europe, governed from Minsk since
  independence in 1991. It emerged from the Byelorussian Soviet Socialist Republic as
  the Soviet Union dissolved, retaining borders shaped by earlier wars and Soviet
  territorial changes. A constitution adopted in 1994 created a presidency, and
  Alexander Lukashenko won the first presidential election. Subsequent constitutional
  changes concentrated power around the president, while opposition parties, independent
  media, and public protest faced severe restrictions. Belarus kept state ownership in
  its economy and developed close political, military, and economic ties with Russia,
  including the Union State framework. The disputed presidential election of 2020
  prompted mass demonstrations and repression. Despite its deep dependence on Russia,
  Belarus continues as a separately administered sovereign state.
