description: >-
  Ma'in was a South Arabian kingdom centred on Qarnawu in the Jawf valley of present day
  Yemen. This record places its independent life roughly between 650 and 100 BCE, though its
  chronology remains disputed. Kings governed with councils and powerful urban families,
  while Qarnawu and Yathill formed the core of a wider alliance. Minaean wealth came
  especially from the caravan trade in frankincense and other aromatics. Merchants
  maintained communities across Arabia and reached Egypt and the Mediterranean, leaving
  inscriptions that document their business and worship. Wadd was a leading god, and Minaic
  was their written language. Relations with neighbouring Saba shifted from cooperation to
  rivalry. The kingdom eventually lost its independence to Saba, while Minaean trading
  traditions outlasted its government.
