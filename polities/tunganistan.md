description: >-
  Tunganistan is the name Western writers gave to the territory around Khotan in southern
  Xinjiang that the Hui warlord Ma Hushan ruled from 1934 to 1937. His half-brother Ma
  Zhongying had led the New 36th Division, a Hui army nominally part of the Nationalist
  forces, into Xinjiang and in 1934 crushed the First East Turkestan Republic at Kashgar.
  After Soviet intervention on the side of the provincial governor Sheng Shicai, Ma Zhongying
  crossed into the Soviet Union and never returned. Ma Hushan withdrew to Khotan and governed
  the southern oases from Kargilik to Charkhlik, while Sheng held the rest of the province. His
  officers ruled the Turkic population harshly with heavy taxes and conscription. Sheng's
  forces, with Soviet help, ended his rule in 1937.
