description: >-
  The Autonomous Administration of North and East Syria developed during the Syrian civil war,
  with self-governing cantons proclaimed in 2014. Centred on northeastern Syria, it brought
  Kurdish, Arab, Syriac, and other communities within institutions shaped by democratic
  confederalism. Local communes and councils formed its administrative framework, with paired male
  and female leadership and commitments to linguistic rights and women's participation. The
  Kurdish Democratic Union Party held influence, and critics questioned how far formal
  decentralisation matched political practice. Its territory expanded as the Syrian Democratic
  Forces fought Islamic State, supported by an international coalition. It sought autonomy within
  Syria rather than recognition as a separate country. A government offensive in January 2026 took
  most of its territory, and it was fully integrated into the Syrian state in August 2026.
