description: >-
  The Tahirid Emirate governed Khorasan from 821 to 873, first from Merv and later from
  Nishapur in present day Iran. Tahir ibn Husayn gained the province after helping the Abbasid
  caliph al-Ma'mun win a civil war. His descendants turned the governorship into hereditary government, directing
  its armies, revenues and administration while acknowledging the caliph's authority. That combination makes the degree
  of autonomy a subject of historical debate. The court supported Sunni scholarship and maintained connections
  with Baghdad, where other family members held offices. Those western posts were distinct from its
  Khorasan government. Later rulers faced unrest and expanding regional rivals. In 873, Ya'qub ibn Layth
  captured Nishapur for the Saffarids, ending Tahirid rule in the province despite the family's survival.
