description: >-
  The Principality of Bulgaria existed in the Balkans from 1878 to 1908, following the
  Ottoman defeat in the Russo-Turkish War. The Treaty of Berlin created a smaller
  Bulgarian state than the earlier San Stefano settlement had proposed. Although
  formally owing allegiance to the Ottoman sultan, it established its own government,
  army, constitution, and foreign policy. Sofia became the capital. The Tarnovo
  Constitution provided a constitutional monarchy and an elected assembly, though
  rulers and ministers repeatedly contested political power. Alexander of Battenberg
  was followed by Ferdinand after a turbulent succession. Union with Eastern Rumelia in
  1885 enlarged the state and survived war with Serbia. In 1908 Ferdinand proclaimed
  full independence and assumed the royal title, turning the principality into
  Bulgaria's kingdom.
