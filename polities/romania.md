description: >-
  Romania lies around the lower Danube and Carpathian Mountains, with Bucharest as its
  capital. This record begins in 1947, when King Michael was forced to abdicate and a
  communist republic replaced the monarchy. Party leaders controlled government,
  nationalised industry and collectivised agriculture. Under Nicolae Ceaușescu, Romania
  pursued some independence from Soviet foreign policy while developing a harsh
  dictatorship at home. Debt repayment policies brought shortages and hardship during
  the 1980s. Revolution in 1989 ended his rule and opened a difficult transition to
  multiparty politics and a market economy. The later republic has a president,
  parliament and government sharing executive authority. Romania joined NATO in 2004 and
  the European Union in 2007. These changes are treated here as one Romanian state.
