description: >-
  The Reddi kingdom of Rajahmundry was a branch of the Reddi dynasty ruling the Godavari delta in
  coastal Andhra, in southern India. Kataya Vema Reddi, a relative and general of the Kondavidu
  kings, established it around 1395, and it soon governed as a separate state. Its rulers,
  especially Allada Reddi and his son Veerabhadra Reddi, defended the fertile delta against the
  Bahmani sultans, Vijayanagara and the Gajapati kings of Odisha, shifting alliances between them.
  The court patronised Telugu literature, and the poet Srinatha found favour there after
  Kondavidu's fall. Rice farming and the ports of the Godavari coast supported royal revenue.
  Gajapati expansion under Kapilendra Deva ended the kingdom around 1448, absorbing Rajahmundry
  into the Gajapati realm.
