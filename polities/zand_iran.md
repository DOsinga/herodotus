description: >-
  Zand Iran was an eighteenth century monarchy centered on southern and central Iran
  between 1751 and 1794. Karim Khan emerged from the struggles following Nader Shah's
  death and established his principal court at Shiraz. Rather than normally styling
  himself shah, he used the title meaning representative of the people. His government
  restored some relative stability, encouraged commerce, and supported building
  projects that still shape Shiraz, including its bazaar and mosque. The realm expanded
  beyond its southern core, though Khorasan remained outside its effective rule and
  Caucasian khanates retained considerable autonomy. Karim Khan's death in 1779
  triggered repeated struggles among his relatives. The final ruler, Lotf Ali Khan,
  lost to Agha Mohammad Khan Qajar, whose conquest ended Zand government in 1794.
