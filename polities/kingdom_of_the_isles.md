description: >-
  The Kingdom of the Isles was a Norse-Gaelic kingdom of the Isle of Man and the Hebrides off the
  west coast of Scotland. Godred Crovan established the dynasty that ruled it after his victory at
  Skyhill on Man in 1079. Its kings commanded fleets of galleys, fought in Irish and Scottish wars
  and acknowledged the distant overlordship of the kings of Norway, while governing the islands
  themselves; the Tynwald assembly on Man has roots in this period. In 1156 Somerled of Argyll
  seized the southern Hebrides, and his descendants held them as a rival lordship. After Norway's
  failed expedition of 1263, the Treaty of Perth in 1266 transferred the Isles to Scotland. Magnus
  Olafsson, the last king, had died in 1265.
