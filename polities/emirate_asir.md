description: >-
  The Emirate of Asir, also called the Idrisid Emirate, was a state on the Red Sea coast of
  southwestern Arabia from 1907 until it came under Saudi protection in 1926. Its founder
  Muhammad ibn Ali al-Idrisi, a great-grandson of the Sufi teacher Ahmad ibn Idris, led a
  revolt against the Ottomans from his capital at Sabya. His state held a strip of the
  Tihama plain and reached inland to the scarp of the Asir highlands. Britain supported him
  during the First World War, and after the Ottoman collapse the emirate expanded south as
  far as Hodeidah. After Muhammad died in 1920 his heirs quarrelled and lost Hodeidah
  to Yemen. The Treaty of Mecca of 1926 made Asir
  a Saudi protectorate, and Saudi Arabia annexed it outright in 1934.
