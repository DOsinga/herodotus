name: Kingdom of Thessalonica
description: >-
  The Kingdom of Thessalonica was a crusader monarchy in northern Greece from 1204 to 1224. Boniface
  of Montferrat established it after the Fourth Crusade captured Constantinople and divided Byzantine territory among Latin
  rulers. Its capital was Thessalonica, and it acknowledged the Latin emperor while maintaining its own royal
  government. Boniface extended influence southward through dependent lordships, but the kingdom faced Bulgarian attacks and resistance
  from neighbouring Byzantine successor states. His death in 1207 left the young Demetrius as king. Rivalries
  among Latin nobles, particularly the Lombard faction, complicated the regency and weakened cooperation. Theodore Komnenos Doukas
  of Epirus captured surrounding territory and took Thessalonica in 1224. Latin rule ended there, although western
  claimants continued to use the royal title afterward.
