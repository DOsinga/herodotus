description: >-
  The Commune of Como was the self-governing city state of Como on its lake in northern
  Lombardy, from about 1100 until it gave itself to Azzone Visconti in 1335. Its consuls
  governed the lake, the alpine valleys towards the Saint Gotthard and San Bernardino
  passes, and towns such as Lugano, Bellinzona and Locarno. Milan destroyed Como in 1127
  at the end of a ten years' war, and Frederick Barbarossa rebuilt it as an imperial ally,
  so the city long stayed Ghibelline. In the thirteenth and early fourteenth centuries the
  Rusca and the Vitani fought over the commune, and the Rusca came out on top as lords.
  Franchino Rusca finally sold the city to the Visconti, after which Como belonged to
  Milan.
