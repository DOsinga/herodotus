description: >-
  Lower Burgundy was a kingdom in the Rhône valley and Provence, centred on Vienne and
  reaching the Mediterranean. Charles received it when Middle Francia was divided in 855,
  but his death in 863 brought partition between his brothers. Separate royal government
  returned in 879 when regional bishops and nobles elected Boso, breaking with the
  Carolingian kings of West Francia. His son Louis later held both the regional throne and
  an imperial title in Italy, where his ambitions ended in capture and blinding. Hugh of
  Arles subsequently exercised government before becoming king in Italy. These changes did
  not erase the regional realm. In 933 its lands united with Upper Burgundy under Rudolph
  II, forming the larger kingdom commonly known as Arles.
