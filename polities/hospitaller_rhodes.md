description: >-
  Hospitaller Rhodes was the island state governed by the Order of Saint John from 1310 to 1522.
  After losing their mainland base in the Holy Land, the knights conquered Rhodes and made its
  fortified city their permanent headquarters. An elected grand master led a religious military
  order whose members were organised by language groups, ruling a largely Greek population. Their
  possessions included neighbouring islands and strongholds along the Anatolian coast. Naval
  warfare and crusading brought repeated conflict with Muslim powers, while the order maintained
  links with western European patrons. Rhodes resisted a Mamluk attack in 1444 and an Ottoman
  siege in 1480. Suleiman's renewed assault in 1522 forced surrender; the surviving knights
  departed, and the island passed into Ottoman rule.
