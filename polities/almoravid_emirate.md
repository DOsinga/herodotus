description: >-
  The Almoravid Emirate united much of Morocco and Muslim Iberia under Berber rulers between
  the mid-eleventh century and 1147. Its beginnings are estimated here from the capture of
  Sijilmasa and Awdaghust around 1054, securing important Saharan trade routes. The movement
  grew among Sanhaja tribes under the religious teaching of Abdallah ibn Yasin and promoted
  Sunni Islam through the Maliki legal school. Marrakesh became its capital after its
  foundation later in the century. Yusuf ibn Tashfin defeated a Christian army at Sagrajas
  in 1086 and subsequently annexed several Iberian taifa kingdoms. Almoravid emirs
  acknowledged the Abbasid caliphs without surrendering their own government. Military
  setbacks and the Almohad rebellion weakened their rule; the capture of Marrakesh in 1147
  ended the mainland regime.
