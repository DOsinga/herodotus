description: >-
  The Khanate of Kalat was a Brahui-led state in Balochistan, in present day Pakistan, with its
  capital at Kalat. The Ahmadzai khans formed it in 1666 as a confederacy of Brahui and Baloch
  tribes against Mughal expansion. Under Nasir Khan I in the mid eighteenth century it controlled
  most of Balochistan, from Makran on the Arabian Sea to the edge of Sindh, while sending troops to
  the Durrani Afghans rather than paying tribute. Its government rested on the tribal sardars, who
  met in council with the khan. In the nineteenth century it lost ground to Persia and Afghanistan,
  and the British stormed Kalat in 1839. The treaty of 1876 put it under British protection. In
  August 1947 the khan declared independence, but he acceded to Pakistan in March 1948.
