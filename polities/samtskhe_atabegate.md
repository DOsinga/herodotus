description: >-
  Samtskhe-Saatabago, the Atabegate of Samtskhe, was a Georgian principality in the southwest of
  the country, ruled by the Jaqeli family from Akhaltsikhe. The Jaqelis were marcher lords who
  had resisted the Mongols, and from 1268 they dealt directly with the Ilkhans rather than
  through the Georgian crown, governing Samtskhe, Javakheti and parts of Tao and Klarjeti. King
  George V brought them back under the crown in the 1330s, but they kept their hereditary
  government and the title of atabeg. During the breakup of the Georgian kingdom in the fifteenth
  century the atabegs became fully independent, with their own church and law code. The wars
  between the Ottomans and the Safavids wore the principality down, and in 1578 the Ottomans
  conquered it, later making Akhaltsikhe the seat of an Ottoman province.
