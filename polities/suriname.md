description: >-
  Suriname became independent from the Netherlands in 1975 and continues as a
  republic on South America's northeastern coast. Its capital, Paramaribo,
  stands near the Atlantic, where most of the population lives, while rainforest
  covers much of the interior. Dutch plantation slavery and later immigration
  from India, Java, and elsewhere helped create a society with many languages,
  religions, and communities. Indigenous peoples and Maroon descendants of
  escaped enslaved Africans also maintain distinct traditions. A military coup
  in 1980 interrupted parliamentary government, and civil conflict followed
  during the 1980s. Civilian constitutional government returned, with an elected
  National Assembly choosing the president. Mining, including gold and formerly
  bauxite, has shaped the economy. Independence also brought extensive migration
  and continuing connections with the Netherlands.
