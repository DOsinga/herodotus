description: >-
  Maroboduus established a Marcomannic kingdom in Bohemia around 9 BCE, moving his
  people beyond the immediate reach of expanding Roman power. A nobleman who had spent
  his youth in Italy, he organized neighboring Germanic groups into a confederation
  under his kingship. His kingdom became strong enough that Augustus planned a campaign
  against it in 6 CE. An uprising in Illyria forced Rome to abandon that attack and
  negotiate instead. Maroboduus remained neutral during Rome's later fighting against
  Arminius, but rivalry between the two Germanic leaders led to war in 17. Weakened by
  that conflict, he was overthrown by Catualda in 19 and fled to Roman territory. His
  precise royal seat remains unknown; Bohemia represents the kingdom rather than a
  capital.
