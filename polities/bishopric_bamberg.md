description: >-
  The Prince-Bishopric of Bamberg was an ecclesiastical principality in Upper Franconia, centred on
  the city on the Regnitz that Emperor Henry II made a bishopric in 1007 and where he and his wife
  Cunigunde were buried. The bishops gained imperial immediacy around 1245 and ruled a territory
  in the upper Main valley and the Franconian Jura, as well as scattered lands in Carinthia, such
  as Villach and Wolfsberg, which they kept until selling them to Austria in 1759. The cathedral
  chapter elected the bishops, and several of them also held the see of Würzburg. Bamberg stayed
  Catholic through the Reformation and became a centre of baroque building. In the secularisation
  of 1802 and 1803 it passed to Bavaria.
