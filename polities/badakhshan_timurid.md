name: "Timurid Badakhshan"
wikipedia: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Suleiman_Mirza_(Timurid)
description: >-
  When the Uzbeks under Muhammad Shaybani overran the Timurid lands of Central Asia early in the
  sixteenth century, the mountain province of Badakhshan on the upper Amu Darya remained in
  Timurid hands. Babur's cousin Khan Mirza, also called Sultan Uways, took it around 1507 with
  the support of local chiefs, and after his death in 1520 Babur gave the province to his own son
  Humayun. From 1529 Khan Mirza's young son Sulayman Mirza ruled Badakhshan for more than fifty
  years, nominally under the Mughal emperors but in practice on his own, and he repeatedly tried
  to seize Kabul and Balkh. Quarrels with his grandson Shahrukh weakened the principality, and in
  1584 the Uzbek ruler Abdullah Khan II of Bukhara conquered it.
