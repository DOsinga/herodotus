description: >-
  Numantia was a Celtiberian city on a hill above a crossing of the Duero River, near modern
  Soria in Spain. Its independent period is represented here from approximately 300 BCE
  until Rome destroyed it in 133 BCE. Ancient writers associated its people with the Arevaci
  or Pellendones, but its internal government is poorly documented. The strongly fortified
  settlement became a centre of resistance during the Celtiberian Wars. After sheltering
  refugees from Segeda, it fought repeated Roman expeditions and forced a Roman army to
  accept a treaty in 137 BCE. Scipio Aemilianus then surrounded the city with siege works
  and cut off supplies. Starvation brought surrender and destruction. Later occupation under
  Roman rule did not restore the independent community recorded here.
