description: >-
  The Majeerteen Sultanate was a Somali kingdom on the northeastern tip of the Horn of Africa, in
  what is now Puntland, recorded here from about 1420 until 1889. It was founded by the Majeerteen
  clan of the Darod, and its origins are dated anywhere from the fifteenth to the eighteenth
  century. The sultans, called boqor, ruled a semi-nomadic society with a strong seafaring
  tradition along the coast between Cape Guardafui and the Gulf of Aden, trading frankincense,
  gum, salt and livestock with Arabia. The state reached its height in the nineteenth century
  under Boqor Osman Mahamuud, who ruled from Alula. In 1889 the sultan accepted Italian
  protection, and Italy annexed the sultanate after the Campaign of the Sultanates in 1927.
