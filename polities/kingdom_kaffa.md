description: >-
  The Kingdom of Kaffa was a monarchy in the forested southwestern highlands of present-day
  Ethiopia, lasting from about 1390 to 1897. It lay south of the Gojeb River, with its first
  capital at Bonga and a later royal seat at Anderaccha. The king, the tato, governed with a
  council of seven nobles and through provincial governors, and the court was surrounded by
  elaborate ritual. Its people spoke Kafficho, an Omotic language, and wild coffee grew in its
  forests. Kaffa traded coffee, ivory, musk and captives through Muslim merchants to the
  north, and protected its borders with ditches and fortified gates. Armies of Emperor Menelik
  II conquered Kaffa in 1897 and captured its last king, Gaki Sherocho, after which the
  kingdom was annexed to the Ethiopian Empire.
