description: >-
  Tlaxcala was a confederated Nahua state in central Mexico, represented from 1348 to 1520. Its four
  main communities were Tepeticpac, Ocotelolco, Tizatlan, and Quiahuiztlan. Political authority rested
  with a council whose members could gain office through service, especially warfare, rather than with
  a single dominant king. Nahuatl speakers lived alongside Otomi communities. Surrounded by Aztec
  territory, Tlaxcala resisted incorporation and endured repeated wars and restrictions on trade. In
  1519 its forces initially fought Hernán Cortés, then allied with the newcomers against their
  longstanding enemies. Tlaxcalan armies became crucial to the Spanish conquest of Tenochtitlan and
  later campaigns. Incorporation into Spanish rule ended its independent position, but the alliance
  helped secure privileges and preserve local institutions within the colonial political order.
