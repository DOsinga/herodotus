description: >-
  Middle Francia was the kingdom allotted to Lothair I by the Treaty of Verdun in 843,
  following civil war among Charlemagne's grandsons. It stretched from the North Sea and
  the Low Countries through the Rhine region and Burgundy to northern Italy. Lothair
  retained the imperial title, but his brothers governed East and West Francia separately;
  that title did not make their kingdoms his provinces. His own government relied on royal
  courts, nobles, bishops, and monasteries across a long, varied territory. Aachen was an
  important royal centre, though the court travelled. In 855, shortly before his death,
  Lothair divided the realm among his sons at Prüm. Italy, Lotharingia, and the southern
  Burgundian kingdom then became separate governments, ending unified Middle Francia.
