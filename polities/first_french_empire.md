description: >-
  The First French Empire was Napoleon Bonaparte's state from 1804 to 1814, restored briefly during the Hundred Days of 1815. Governed from Paris, it combined imperial authority with institutions inherited from the Revolution and Consulate. Napoleon concentrated power while maintaining centralized administration and a civil code that influenced much of Europe. Military victories expanded French territory and placed relatives or allies on neighboring thrones, but continual warfare demanded heavy taxation and conscription. The Continental System tried to isolate Britain economically. The disastrous invasion of Russia in 1812 and subsequent coalition campaigns broke French dominance. Napoleon abdicated in 1814, returned from Elba in 1815, and lost at Waterloo. A second abdication ended imperial rule, followed by renewed Bourbon restoration in France.
