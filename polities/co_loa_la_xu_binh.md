description: >-
  Cổ Loa under Lã Xử Bình was a short-lived government at the former royal capital in northern Vietnam
  from 965 to approximately 968. Lã, a subordinate general of Ngô Xương Văn, seized the capital after
  the king died while fighting rebels. The surviving prince Ngô Xương Xí withdrew to Bình Kiều,
  leaving Lã in actual control of Cổ Loa rather than governing for the royal heir. This occurred
  during the fragmentation commonly called the period of the Twelve Warlords, although Lã does not
  appear in its traditional list of twelve names. Details of his administration and territory are
  sparse. Đinh Bộ Lĩnh's campaigns reunited the competing domains by 968, bringing this local
  government into the newly established Đinh royal state.
