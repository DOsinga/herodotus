description: >-
  The Hawadle are a Somali clan of the Samaale line who live along the middle and upper Shabelle
  River, in what is now the Hiran region of Somalia and the neighbouring Somali region of
  Ethiopia. When the Ajuran Sultanate declined at the end of the seventeenth century, the Hawadle
  joined the Gaaljecel and Baadicade pastoralists of the Shabelle valley in campaigns that finally
  drove the Ajuran out of the upper river. From then on the clan held the river crossings and
  pastures around Beledweyne, governed by its elders and religious leaders through customary law
  and councils rather than a sultan. Caravans between the Benadir coast and the Ethiopian
  highlands passed through their country. Italy established a post at Beledweyne in 1908 and
  brought the Hawadle under colonial rule.
