description: >-
  Werle was a lordship in Mecklenburg, created around 1235 when the grandsons of Henry Borwin I
  divided the Mecklenburg lands. Its lords, a branch of the Obotrite dynasty, ruled the area
  around Güstrow and the lakes of central Mecklenburg, with Güstrow as their main seat. The
  lordship itself divided several times among branches based at Güstrow, Parchim, Goldberg and
  Waren, which reunited when lines died out. Its rulers took part in the wars and alliances of the
  Baltic region and promoted towns and German settlement. In the fifteenth century they used the
  title Prince of Wenden. When the last lord, William, died in 1436 without a male heir, Werle
  passed to the dukes of Mecklenburg, who absorbed it into their duchy.
