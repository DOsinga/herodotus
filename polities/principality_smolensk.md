description: >-
  The Principality of Smolensk was a medieval Rus' state on the upper Dnieper, centred
  on the city of Smolensk. It became independently governed in 1125 under Rostislav
  Mstislavich, whose descendants formed a lasting branch of the Rurikid dynasty. Its
  princes governed a territory containing subordinate towns, while an Orthodox
  bishopric strengthened the city's religious standing. River routes connected Smolensk
  with Kiev, Novgorod, Polotsk and Baltic commerce, making its location politically and
  economically valuable. Members of its ruling family repeatedly competed for the
  Kievan throne. The principality avoided the worst immediate destruction of the Mongol
  invasion but later faced mounting pressure from Lithuania and neighbouring powers.
  After struggles over its rulers and allegiance, Lithuania incorporated Smolensk into
  its realm in 1404.
