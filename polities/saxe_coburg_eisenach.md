description: >-
  Saxe-Coburg-Eisenach was an Ernestine duchy in Franconia and western Thuringia, created by the
  Erfurt division of 1572 for the young sons of the deposed Duke John Frederick II. While the boys
  were minors, the elector of Saxony governed for them as guardian. In 1596 the brothers divided
  their inheritance: John Casimir kept Coburg, while John Ernest received Eisenach as a separate
  duchy. John Casimir governed Coburg from its fortress and new palaces, founded the Casimirianum
  grammar school, and promoted Lutheran church order and witchcraft trials. During the Thirty
  Years' War his lands suffered from passing armies and the siege of the Veste Coburg in 1632.
  When he died without children in 1633, Coburg passed to his brother at Eisenach.
