description: >-
  The Principality of Achaea was a crusader state in the Peloponnese, founded in 1205 by
  William of Champlitte and Geoffrey of Villehardouin after the Fourth Crusade. Under the
  Villehardouin princes it became the strongest of the Latin states in Greece, with a court at
  Andravida, a port at Glarentza and castles held by French barons. Prince William II lost
  Mystras, Monemvasia and other fortresses to Byzantium after his capture at Pelagonia in
  1259, and in 1267 the principality passed under the Angevin kings of Naples. In the
  fourteenth century it was ruled by absent princes, bailiffs and finally the Navarrese
  Company, while the Byzantine despots of the Morea took more and more land. The last prince,
  Centurione Zaccaria, lost the remainder to them in 1430–1432.
