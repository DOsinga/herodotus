description: >-
  Anhalt-Dessau was a German state centred on Dessau and ruled by the Ascanian family. A first
  Dessau line ruled from 1396 to 1561; the second began with another division of the Anhalt
  inheritance in 1603. Its princes governed within the Holy Roman Empire, and the state survived
  that empire's dissolution to join the Confederation of the Rhine and later the German
  Confederation. Raised to a duchy in 1807, it retained its own government among the patchwork of
  central German territories. Dynastic succession eventually reversed earlier divisions. After the
  Köthen line ended in 1847, Dessau took over its lands, with formal union following in 1853. The
  extinction of Anhalt-Bernburg in 1863 completed reunification, and its ruler adopted the title
  Duke of Anhalt.
