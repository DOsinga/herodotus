description: >-
  The Kingdom of Raiatea was a Polynesian monarchy on Raiatea and neighbouring Tahaa in the
  Leeward Society Islands. Raiatea was the religious centre of the Society Islands, home of the
  great marae of Taputapuatea, and its Tamatoa line of chiefs was linked by marriage to the
  ruling families of Tahiti, Huahine and Bora Bora. In 1820 Tamatoa III introduced a written law
  code modelled on that of Tahiti. The island later fought off the Mamaia religious movement,
  sheltered Queen Pōmare IV of Tahiti from the French in the 1840s and was recognised as
  independent by the Jarnac Convention of 1847. To keep Germany out, France proclaimed a
  provisional protectorate in 1880 and annexed the islands in 1888, though the chief Teraupoʻo
  resisted until 1897.
