description: >-
  The French Fourth Republic governed France from 1946 to 1958, following the provisional
  government established after liberation in the Second World War. Its parliamentary
  constitution placed strong authority in the National Assembly, while presidents had a more
  limited role. Coalition cabinets changed frequently, but the period also brought
  reconstruction, industrial growth, expanded social institutions, and cooperation with West
  Germany. France helped establish the European Coal and Steel Community and the European
  Economic Community. Overseas, attempts to retain imperial authority led to wars in
  Indochina and Algeria, while Morocco and Tunisia gained independence. The Algerian crisis
  of 1958 brought Charles de Gaulle back to power. A new constitution then replaced the
  Fourth Republic with the Fifth Republic and a stronger presidency.
