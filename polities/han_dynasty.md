description: >-
  Han was a Chinese imperial state founded by Liu Bang, whose kingdom began in 206 BCE
  before he became emperor in 202 BCE. Its emperors governed through ministers, appointed
  officials, commanderies, and increasingly restricted hereditary kingdoms. Western Han
  ruled from Chang'an until Wang Mang established Xin in 9 CE. Emperor Guangwu restored Han
  in 25, beginning Eastern Han with its capital at Luoyang. Expansion, diplomacy, and
  conflict with the Xiongnu extended Chinese influence into Central Asia and supported long
  distance trade. Confucian learning gained a lasting place in administration, alongside
  major advances in historical writing and technology. Court struggles and rebellions
  weakened government, and warlords held much practical power after 190. Cao Pi replaced the
  remaining imperial court in 220.
