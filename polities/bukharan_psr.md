description: >-
  The Bukharan People's Soviet Republic replaced the Emirate of Bukhara in Central Asia
  after a Red Army assault in 1920. Young Bukharan reformers joined the Bolsheviks in
  overthrowing the emir, and Fayzulla Khojaev headed its government. Although organised
  around soviets, the republic initially allowed private property and accommodated
  Islamic principles rather than immediately copying Soviet Russia's system. Its leaders
  sought changes in education, landholding, and administration while facing resistance
  from the Basmachi movement, especially in eastern Bukhara. Soviet military support and
  growing Communist influence narrowed its room for independent action. In 1924 it
  became a Soviet socialist republic before national territorial reorganisation divided
  its lands among new Soviet republics. Those changes ended Bukhara's separate
  government and reshaped modern Central Asia.
