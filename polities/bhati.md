description: >-
  Bhati was the low-lying river country of eastern Bengal that Isa Khan and then his son Musa
  Khan governed from Sonargaon and Katrabo while they resisted the Mughal conquest, from the
  fall of the Karrani sultans in 1576 until 1611. Isa Khan had built up an estate around
  Sonargaon and Mymensingh under the Karranis and by 1571 ruled Bhati. He led the Baro-Bhuiyan
  chiefs, a loose alliance of zamindars and Afghan captains who fought together against the
  Mughals while each held his own lands, and his river fleet beat Man Singh's army in 1597.
  Musa Khan carried on after Isa Khan's death in 1599, until the governor Islam Khan took Dhaka
  in 1610 and forced his surrender the following year.
