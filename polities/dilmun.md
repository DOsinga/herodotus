description: >-
  Dilmun was a Gulf trading kingdom centred on Bahrain, represented here from about 2050 to
  1450 BCE. These estimated dates concern its royal government rather than the much longer
  history of the name. Settlements at Qal'at al-Bahrain, burial mounds, temples, seals, and
  written references reveal a society connected to Mesopotamia and the Indus region. Merchants
  moved copper and other materials through its ports, using distinctive stamp seals in
  transactions. Freshwater springs helped sustain farming and settlement on the island.
  Mesopotamian stories also gave Dilmun a place in their religious imagination, describing an
  ideal land. Letters and royal inscriptions provide glimpses of rulers and administration.
  Its separate kingdom eventually passed under Babylonian control, with Kassite officials
  documented there in later correspondence.
