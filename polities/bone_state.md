description: >-
  Bone was the leading Bugis kingdom of southwestern Sulawesi, with its capital at Watampone near the
  Gulf of Bone. Its chronicle traces the rulers back to the early fifteenth century. In 1582 Bone
  allied with Wajo and Soppeng against the rising Makassar kingdom of Gowa, which nevertheless invaded
  it in 1611, forced it to accept Islam and in 1643 took direct control. The Bone prince Arung Palakka
  allied with the Dutch East India Company, and their joint war led to Gowa's defeat in 1667 and 1669.
  Bone then became the strongest state in South Sulawesi and an ally of the Dutch. After the Second
  Bone War of 1859 and 1860 a new treaty reduced it to a Dutch feudatory.
