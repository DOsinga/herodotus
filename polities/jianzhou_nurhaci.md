description: >-
  Nurhaci's Jianzhou state was the Jurchen polity in the mountains east of Liaodong that he
  built between 1587 and 1616, before he proclaimed the Later Jin. Nurhaci, a chief of the
  Jianzhou Jurchens, began his career in 1583 by avenging his father and grandfather, killed
  in a Ming campaign. He subdued the rival Jianzhou chiefs and in 1587 built a walled seat at
  Fe Ala, where he issued laws and received Korean envoys. Through the 1590s and 1600s he
  conquered the Hada, Hoifa and Ula, moved to Hetu Ala in 1603 and organised his followers
  into the banners. He kept the Ming titles and trade privileges that went with tribute, but
  governed his people himself. In 1616 he declared himself khan of the Later Jin.
