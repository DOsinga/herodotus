description: >-
  The Saindhava kingdom ruled western Saurashtra in present Gujarat from approximately 735 into
  the early tenth century. Its hereditary kings governed from Bhutamabilika, now Ghumli, in the
  Barda hills. Copperplate land grants provide most of the surviving evidence for their
  genealogy, titles and religious patronage. The family associated itself with Sindh, but its
  kingdom was a separate coastal power rather than the state of that region. Early rulers
  probably worked alongside the Maitrakas of Valabhi. Agguka is remembered for defeating Arab
  naval attacks, and the dynasty's fish emblem suited its maritime strength. Later rulers
  encountered Pratihara pressure. The last attested kings leave an uncertain ending: 920 is an
  estimate, and the kingdom may have survived considerably longer than that date.
