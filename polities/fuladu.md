description: >-
  Fuladu was a Fula kingdom in the upper Casamance region, covering parts of present southern
  Senegal, The Gambia and Guinea-Bissau. Its founder, Alfa Molo Balde of Firdu, led the Fula of
  Kaabu in revolt against their Mandinka rulers and, with help from the Imamate of Futa Jallon,
  captured the Kaabu capital Kansala in 1867. He kept Kaabu's decentralised structure but replaced
  its Mandinka chiefs with Fula leaders, grouped the many small territories into five provinces
  and settled Fula in Mandinka communities. In 1874 he made Ndorna his capital. His son Musa Molo
  won a succession struggle with French support and extended the kingdom, but conceded revenue and
  influence in return. In 1903 he fled to British territory, and France annexed Fuladu.
