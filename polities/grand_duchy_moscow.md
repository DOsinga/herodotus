description: >-
  The Grand Duchy of Moscow grew from a small Russian principality founded for Daniel,
  Alexander Nevsky's son, in 1263. Its hereditary Rurikid rulers competed with Tver and
  other principalities while acknowledging the Golden Horde's overlordship. Moscow's
  princes used marriage, purchase, warfare and privileged relations with the khans to
  enlarge their territory. The Russian Orthodox church's leadership also helped strengthen the
  city's standing. Dmitry Donskoy's victory at Kulikovo became an important symbol,
  although Horde domination did not immediately end. Ivan III later annexed Novgorod
  and Tver and consolidated a more centralised monarchy. The confrontation on the Ugra
  in 1480 symbolised its growing independence from the Golden Horde. In 1547, Ivan IV
  took the title of tsar, creating the Tsardom of Russia.
