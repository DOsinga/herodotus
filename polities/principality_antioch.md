description: >-
  The Principality of Antioch was a crusader monarchy in northern Syria and adjoining
  Anatolia from 1098 to 1268. Bohemond of Taranto established it after the First Crusade
  captured Antioch, keeping the city despite Byzantine claims to its restoration. Norman and
  later Frankish princes governed through nobles and a Latin church hierarchy, among
  populations that included Greek and Armenian Christians as well as Muslims. The
  principality fought neighbouring Muslim states and repeatedly negotiated submission or
  alliances with Byzantine emperors and Armenian rulers. Its history also intertwined with
  the County of Tripoli through shared rulers and succession disputes. Later cooperation
  with the Mongols exposed it to Mamluk retaliation. Sultan Baibars captured Antioch in
  1268, destroying the city and ending the principality's rule.
