description: >-
  The County of Auvergne was a feudal county in the mountains of central France, centred on
  Clermont and recorded here from 963 to 1213. After the last Williamite count died in 927,
  the dukes of Aquitaine and the counts of Toulouse disputed the region, until the viscounts
  of Clermont took the title of count in 963 and made it hereditary. The bishops of Clermont
  held strong rights in the city itself. In 1155 Count William VII was ousted by his uncle
  William VIII, and the lands left to the younger branch later became the Dauphiné of
  Auvergne. Count Guy II quarrelled with the bishop and with Philip II, whose army took most
  of his lands, including Clermont, by 1213.
