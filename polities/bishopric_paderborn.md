description: >-
  The Prince-Bishopric of Paderborn was an ecclesiastical principality in eastern Westphalia,
  around the town where Charlemagne held assemblies during his Saxon wars and where the see was
  founded about 799. Its bishops gathered counties and lordships into a compact territory between
  the Teutoburg Forest, the Weser and the Diemel, and from the thirteenth century they ruled it as
  imperial princes. The cathedral chapter elected the bishops and shared power with the estates of
  the land. During the Reformation the town of Paderborn turned Protestant for a time, but the
  bishops restored Catholicism by the early seventeenth century and founded a university. Prussia
  occupied the principality in 1802, and the secularisation of 1803 confirmed its annexation.
