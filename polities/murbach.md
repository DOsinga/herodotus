description: >-
  Murbach Abbey was a Benedictine monastery in a valley of the southern Vosges in Alsace, founded
  in 727 by Count Eberhard of Alsace and Saint Pirmin. Its abbots were princes of the Empire and
  ruled a small territory of their own around Guebwiller, Wattwiller and Saint-Amarin, with
  scattered estates elsewhere. One of these was Lucerne, which grew up around a Murbach monastery
  and which the abbey sold to Rudolf of Habsburg in 1291. From 1560 the abbots also held the abbey
  of Lure in Franche-Comté. Murbach kept its imperial status after France gained the Habsburg
  rights in Alsace in 1648, until Louis XIV's reunions brought it under French sovereignty in
  1680. The abbey moved to Guebwiller in 1759 and was dissolved in the Revolution.
