description: >-
  The Artuqids of Mardin were a Turkmen dynasty that governed Mardin and the surrounding
  part of Upper Mesopotamia, in what is now southeastern Turkey, from about 1106 to 1409.
  They descended from Ilghazi, a son of Artuk Bey, who had served the Seljuks in Syria and
  Palestine. Ilghazi also ruled Aleppo from 1118, where he won a celebrated victory over the
  Principality of Antioch in 1119, and his family held the city until 1128. Mayyafariqin was
  their second seat until Saladin's family took it in the 1180s. The later emirs survived by
  accepting the overlordship of the Ayyubids, the Mongol Ilkhans and the Mamluks in turn,
  while running their own small state and building the madrasas that still mark Mardin. The Qara Qoyunlu ended the dynasty in 1409.
