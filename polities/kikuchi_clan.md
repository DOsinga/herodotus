description: >-
  The Kikuchi were a warrior house of Higo province in central Kyushu, ruling from Kikuchi in
  the north of the province and holding the plain around what is now Kumamoto. Founded by
  Fujiwara no Noritaka, who settled in Kikuchi district in the eleventh century, the family
  fought the Mongols in 1281 and were the main champions of the Southern Court in Kyushu during
  the fourteenth century. They held the governorship of Higo through the Muromachi period.
  After the main line died out, the Ōtomo of Bungo placed their own kin at the head of the
  house from 1520, but Kikuchi Yoshitake, an Ōtomo by birth, turned against his relatives.
  Ōtomo Sōrin drove him from Higo in 1550 and had him killed in 1554.
