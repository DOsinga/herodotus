description: >-
  Li Zitong's Wu was a rebel government in the lower Yangtze region of China during the collapse of the Sui dynasty. It began with Li's occupation of Hailing in 615, before he adopted an imperial title. A former fisherman and hunter, Li gathered followers amid widespread agrarian rebellion and competed with other commanders for control of Jiangsu and Zhejiang. In 619 he captured Jiangdu, modern Yangzhou, after tricking rival relief forces into fighting one another, and proclaimed himself emperor of Wu. His government soon faced attacks from Du Fuwei and other competitors. Defeat in 620 forced the court south to Yuhang. Li surrendered to Du in 621, ending the polity; his later attempted escape and execution occurred after its loss.
