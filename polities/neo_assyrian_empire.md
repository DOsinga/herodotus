description: >-
  The Neo Assyrian Empire expanded from northern Mesopotamia between 911 and 609 BCE, eventually dominating much of the Near East. Its kings commanded large armies and governed through provincial officials, tributary rulers, and an administrative network. Conquests brought wealth but also mass deportations, which moved conquered populations and supplied labour across the empire. Assur, Nimrud, and Dur Sharrukin preceded Nineveh as royal capitals. Sennacherib enlarged Nineveh, while Ashurbanipal's collection of clay tablets preserved works of Mesopotamian literature. At its height, Assyrian power reached Egypt, the Levant, Anatolia, and western Iran. Rebellions and attacks by Babylonian and Median forces destroyed the main cities. Nineveh fell in 612 BCE, and the final royal resistance at Harran ended three years later, in 609.
