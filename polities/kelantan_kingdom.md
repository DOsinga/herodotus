description: >-
  The Kingdom of Kelantan was the Malay state in the Kelantan river valley on the northeast coast of
  the Malay Peninsula before the unification under Long Yunus. Royal lists trace its rulers to a
  prince of Langkasuka who settled there in 1267, and Chinese records name a Muslim ruler, Raja
  Kumar, in 1411. Kelantan sent tribute to Majapahit and Siam, and Malacca forced it into vassalage
  in 1499. After Malacca fell in 1511 the valley was divided among petty chiefs, many of whom became
  subject to Patani, and in 1651 a raja of Kelantan took the throne of Patani itself. Around 1760
  the territories were reunited, and Long Yunus seized power soon after, founding the present
  dynasty.
