description: >-
  The Xianbei were a confederation of nomadic peoples, mostly speaking early Mongolic languages,
  who descended from the Donghu and lived east of the Xiongnu. When the Northern Xiongnu were
  defeated by the Han and their allies around 91 CE, the Xianbei moved onto the Mongolian plateau
  and absorbed many Xiongnu clans. For most of the next three centuries they were led by separate
  chiefs, each with his own following and pastures, who raided and traded with the Chinese
  frontier. Only Tanshihuai in the middle of the second century, and Kebineng briefly after him,
  united most of them. After the Three Kingdoms period many Xianbei groups moved south towards
  China and founded states there, while the northern steppe stayed with lesser chiefs until the
  Rouran rose.
