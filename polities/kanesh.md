description: >-
  Kanesh was a city kingdom on central Anatolia's plateau, near modern Kayseri in Turkey,
  represented here during the roughly 2000 to 1700 BCE trading-colony period. A local
  ruler governed the city while Assyrian merchants maintained a commercial quarter, or
  karum, below the fortified upper settlement. Clay tablets preserve their contracts,
  correspondence, loans, and disputes, revealing extensive trade in tin and textiles for
  Anatolian metals. These merchants did not simply turn Kanesh into an Assyrian province.
  Local politics included warfare and conquest by rulers such as Pithana and Anitta, who
  helped lay foundations for the later Hittite state. Fires destroyed successive trading
  districts, and the colony eventually ceased operating. The Hittites remembered the city
  as Nesha and named their language accordingly.
