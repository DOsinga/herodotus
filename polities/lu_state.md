description: >-
  Lu was a hereditary duchy centred on Qufu in what is now southwestern Shandong, China.
  Established earlier as a Zhou royal fief, it appears here from 770 BCE, when the eastern Zhou
  order allowed its lords to govern separately. Its ruling Ji family traced descent from the Duke
  of Zhou through his son Boqin. Lu fought neighbouring Qi and smaller states, but eventually lost
  ground to stronger powers. Within the duchy, the Three Huan aristocratic families repeatedly
  weakened or displaced ducal authority. Lu's cultural influence exceeded its military strength:
  it was the homeland of Confucius, and the Spring and Autumn Annals followed its rulers' reigns.
  Chu conquered the state in 249 BCE, ending the independent government of its ducal line.
