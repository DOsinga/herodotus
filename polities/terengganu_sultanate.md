description: >-
  Terengganu is a sultanate on the east coast of the Malay Peninsula, centred on Kuala Terengganu at
  the mouth of the Terengganu river. The area had been a dependency of Johor, and an independent
  sultanate emerged in the 1720s under Tun Zainal Abidin, a member of the Johor royal family. Sultan
  Mansur, who ruled from 1741 to 1793, led Malay resistance to the Bugis in Johor and extended his
  influence over Kelantan. Terengganu sent the golden flowers of tribute to Siam but kept its own
  government, and it prospered from trade, boat building and crafts in the nineteenth century. By the
  Anglo-Siamese Treaty of 1909 Siam handed its claims to Britain, which took over Terengganu's foreign
  relations.
