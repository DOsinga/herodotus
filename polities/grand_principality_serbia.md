description: >-
  The Grand Principality of Serbia was a medieval state centred on Ras in the western
  Balkans, represented here from Vukan's independent government around 1091 until 1217. Its
  grand princes ruled alongside local lords and navigated pressure from Byzantium, Hungary
  and neighbouring Serbian lands. Byzantine overlordship sometimes required hostages or
  military service, but did not replace the Serbian ruling government. Vukan's successors
  used alliances and family marriages to strengthen their position. Stefan Nemanja
  consolidated territory in the later twelfth century and founded the Nemanjić ruling line.
  He also supported monasteries, helping connect royal authority with the Orthodox Church.
  His son Stefan secured a royal coronation in 1217, changing the state's standing from a
  grand principality to the medieval Kingdom of Serbia.
