description: >-
  The Prince-Bishopric of Passau was the territory of the bishops of Passau, at the meeting of the
  Danube, the Inn and the Ilz on the border of Bavaria and Austria. The see was organised by Saint
  Boniface in 739, and for centuries its diocese stretched down the Danube to Vienna. In 1217 the
  bishops were made imperial princes, independent of the Bavarian dukes. Their own territory was
  small, the city of Passau and the forested land along the Ilz to the Bohemian border, but they
  drew wealth from salt and the river trade. The citizens of Passau rose against the bishops
  several times without winning independence. The bishopric was secularised in 1803, when Bavaria
  took the city and most of its lands.
