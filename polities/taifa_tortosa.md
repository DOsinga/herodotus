description: >-
  The Taifa of Tortosa was a Muslim kingdom at the mouth of the Ebro on the northeastern coast of
  al-Andalus, in present Catalonia. It was founded by Labib al-Fata, one of the Slavic military
  commanders, the Saqaliba, who seized power in eastern al-Andalus during the collapse of the
  Caliphate of Córdoba after 1009. Tortosa's port, shipyards and river trade gave it importance
  out of proportion to its size, and its rulers changed hands among Slavic commanders and their
  rivals in Valencia and Denia. Pressed between Zaragoza and the counts of Barcelona, it was
  annexed by al-Muqtadir of Zaragoza in 1060. After his death in 1081 it passed with Lleida and
  Denia to his son al-Mundhir.
