description: >-
  The Natchez chiefdom was a society of the Lower Mississippi valley, near the present city
  of Natchez, and the last Mississippian chiefdom to survive well into the colonial era. Its
  paramount chief, the Great Sun, lived beside the temple mound at the Grand Village on St.
  Catherine Creek, where a sacred fire was kept. Rank passed through the female line, and
  society was divided into Suns, Nobles, Honoured People and commoners, with nobles required
  to marry commoners. The French reached the Natchez in 1682 and built Fort Rosalie nearby
  in 1716. Disputes over land led to wars, and in 1729 the Natchez killed most of the
  colonists at the fort. French and Choctaw armies destroyed the chiefdom in 1730 and 1731,
  and survivors joined the Chickasaw, Creek and Cherokee.
