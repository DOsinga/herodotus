description: >-
  The Herat State was an Afghan principality centred on Herat in western Afghanistan that
  governed itself after the Durrani monarchy lost its other provinces. Mahmud Shah, the last
  effective Durrani king, kept Herat after the Barakzai brothers took Kabul and Kandahar,
  and his son Kamran Shah ruled there after 1829. The principality served as a buffer
  between Qajar Iran and the Barakzai rulers of Kabul, and survived a long Persian siege in
  1837 and 1838 with British support. In 1842 the vizier Yar Mohammad Khan Alakozai killed
  Kamran and governed in his own name, extending his authority over the neighbouring Aimaq
  and Uzbek lands. His successors were weaker, and Iran occupied the city in 1856. Dost
  Mohammad Khan captured Herat in 1863 and joined it to the Kabul emirate.
