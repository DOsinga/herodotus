description: >-
  Lihyan was an ancient kingdom centered on Dedan, the oasis now associated with Al-Ula in
  northwestern Saudi Arabia. Its span is estimated here from about 500 to 65 BCE, but the
  chronology remains debated because inscriptions rarely connect local rulers with
  securely dated outside events. Kings and officials are known through Dadanitic texts,
  alongside evidence for temples, offerings, and monumental sculpture. The oasis occupied
  an important position on caravan routes linking southern Arabia with the Levant and
  other northern markets. Trade and agriculture supported its settlements and religious
  institutions. The earlier kingdom of Dedan and later Lihyan are closely connected in
  modern accounts, though their boundaries are uncertain. Nabataean expansion eventually
  brought the kingdom at Dedan under another ruling power.
