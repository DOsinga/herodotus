description: >-
  Mecklenburg-Güstrow was a duchy in northern Germany formed when the Mecklenburg lands were
  divided in 1621 between Duke John Albert II and his brother Adolf Frederick I of Schwerin. Its
  dukes ruled from the Renaissance castle at Güstrow over the eastern half of the duchy, while the
  estates of Mecklenburg kept a common assembly for both lines. During the Thirty Years' War the
  emperor deposed both dukes for siding with Denmark and granted Mecklenburg to his general
  Albrecht von Wallenstein, who governed from Güstrow until Swedish forces restored the dukes in
  1631. Gustav Adolph, the last duke, rebuilt the duchy after the war. When he died without a male
  heir in 1695, Güstrow passed to the Schwerin line after a long inheritance dispute.
