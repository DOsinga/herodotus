description: >-
  Acolhuacan was the kingdom of the Acolhua in the eastern Basin of Mexico, whose rulers traced
  their line to the Chichimec leader Xolotl. Arriving after the fall of the Toltecs, the Acolhua
  settled the lands east of Lake Texcoco, first ruling from Coatlinchan and later from Texcoco.
  Their kings were probably of Otomi descent and adopted Nahuatl under Techotlalatzin. Under
  Quinatzin the kingdom was reorganised into provinces, with Tulancingo heading one, and Acolhua
  rule reached beyond the basin. Rivalry with the Tepanecs of Azcapotzalco ended in war:
  Tezozomoc's forces killed King Ixtlilxochitl in 1418 and took Texcoco. His son Nezahualcoyotl
  won the kingdom back with the Mexica in 1428, as a member of the Triple Alliance.
