name: Kingdom of Pajang
wikipedia: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kingdom_of_Pajang
description: >-
  Pajang was a short-lived Muslim kingdom in the interior of Central Java, with its palace near
  modern Surakarta. Its founder, Jaka Tingkir, was a son-in-law of Sultan Trenggana of Demak who
  held Pajang as a regent. After Trenggana's death in 1546 Demak fell into a succession war. With
  the support of Queen Kalinyamat of Jepara, Jaka Tingkir defeated Arya Penangsang of Jipang and
  took the Demak throne as Sultan Adiwijaya, moving the centre of power inland. He rewarded his
  commanders with the land of Mataram, where Sutawijaya built up his own following. After
  Adiwijaya's death a vassal seized the throne, and Sutawijaya took Pajang in 1586, ending the
  kingdom and making Mataram the leading power.
