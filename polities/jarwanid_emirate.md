description: >-
  The Jarwanid Emirate was a state of eastern Arabia in the fourteenth century, ruled from
  al-Qatif by the Jarwanids, a family of the Bani Malik clan. Whether they belonged to the
  Banu Uqayl, like the Usfurids before them, or to the Abd al-Qays, like the earlier Uyunids,
  is disputed. They came to power after driving out the forces of the Muntafiq chief Sa'id
  ibn Mughamis of Basra. The Jarwanids were Shi'ites: Ibn Battuta and other Sunni writers
  called them extremists or remnants of the Qarmatians, but Twelver scholars held the
  judgeships and other offices under them, and the Shi'ite call to prayer was used in the
  mosques. Early in the fifteenth century the Jabrids, a Bedouin family of the Banu Uqayl,
  ended Jarwanid rule.
