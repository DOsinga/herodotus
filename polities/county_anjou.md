description: >-
  The County of Anjou was a principality on the lower Loire in western France, governed from
  Angers. Its counts grew out of Frankish royal officials, and from Fulk the Red around 930 they
  passed the county from father to son and ran it as their own. Fulk Nerra and Geoffrey Martel
  built a network of stone castles, took Touraine from the counts of Blois in 1044 and made Anjou
  one of the strongest powers in the kingdom. Fulk V added Maine and later became king of
  Jerusalem. His son Geoffrey Plantagenet won Normandy, and Geoffrey's son Henry II joined Anjou
  with England and Aquitaine in what historians call the Angevin Empire. Philip II of France took
  Anjou from King John in 1204.
