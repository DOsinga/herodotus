description: >-
  Estonia is a Baltic republic on the eastern shore of the Baltic Sea, with its
  capital at Tallinn. It declared independence in 1918 as the Russian Empire
  collapsed, then secured its position through war and a peace treaty with Soviet
  Russia. Parliamentary government gave way to authoritarian rule during the
  1930s. Soviet annexation in 1940 interrupted independence; German occupation
  followed during the Second World War, before Soviet control returned. Estonia
  restored its independence in 1991 through a largely peaceful national movement
  associated with the Singing Revolution. It developed a parliamentary republic
  and joined the European Union and NATO in 2004. Its language belongs to the
  Finnic family, and extensive digital public services became a distinctive part
  of its state institutions.
