description: >-
  Tondo was a trading polity on the northern side of the Pasig River delta in Luzon, now
  part of Manila. The Laguna Copperplate Inscription attests its connections in 900, the
  beginning used for this record. Local leaders called datus formed networks under a leading
  lakan, rather than a monarchy organised like European kingdoms. Tondo and neighbouring
  Maynila distributed Chinese goods through the Philippine archipelago, linking river
  communities with overseas merchants. Political relationships rested on trade, kinship and
  negotiated allegiance, and early documentary evidence remains limited. Lakandula was its
  ruler when Spanish forces arrived. Spanish occupation of the Manila area in 1571 ended the
  separate government represented here, although indigenous leaders and later resistance
  continued within the expanding colonial political system.
