description: >-
  The Sanjrani Chiefdom was a Baloch power in Sistan and along the lower Helmand in the
  nineteenth century, with its stronghold at Chakhansur in present day Nimruz province of
  Afghanistan. As the Durrani empire broke up, the Sanjrani and Narui Baloch tribes took over
  much of Sistan, and by 1839 the old Kayanid maliks had lost their authority. Chiefs such as
  Khan Jan, Ali Khan and Ibrahim Khan Sanjrani governed the region through tribal leadership
  and marriage alliances, resisting the Qajar governors of Qaen and the Afghan rulers alike.
  The Goldsmid award of 1872 divided Sistan between Iran and Afghanistan, but Ibrahim Khan kept
  the Afghan side independent in practice until Amir Abdur Rahman Khan defeated him in 1882.
