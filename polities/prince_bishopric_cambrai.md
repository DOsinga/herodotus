description: >-
  The Prince-Bishopric of Cambrai was an ecclesiastical state on the upper Scheldt, recorded
  here from 1250 until Emperor Charles V took control of the city in 1543. King Henry II gave
  the bishops the comital rights over the Cambrésis in 1007, and they ruled it as princes of
  the Empire. The citizens of Cambrai resisted episcopal rule from 1077 and won their own
  privileges, while the counts of Flanders and later the dukes of Burgundy, who placed
  relatives on the episcopal throne, exerted strong influence. The Ladies' Peace between
  France and the Habsburgs was signed in the city in 1529. Charles V built a citadel in 1543,
  after which Habsburg officials governed Cambrai until Louis XIV took it in 1677.
