description: >-
  Tututepec was a Mixtec kingdom on the Pacific coast of Oaxaca, with its capital at the
  city of Yucu Dzaa in the lower Río Verde valley. The Mixtec codices credit its foundation
  as a royal seat to the warlord Eight Deer Jaguar Claw in the late eleventh century, and
  archaeology shows the city growing into one of the largest in Postclassic Oaxaca, covering
  more than twenty square kilometres at its height. Its kings built a tributary state of
  more than 25,000 square kilometres along the coast, taking tribute from Mixtec, Zapotec,
  Chatino and other towns. Tututepec was never conquered by the Aztecs and fought them and
  the Zapotecs of Tehuantepec for control of the coast. Pedro de Alvarado's Spanish force
  took the city in 1522.
