description: >-
  Blambangan was the last Hindu kingdom of Java, in the eastern salient of the island. It grew out
  of the eastern court of Majapahit and stood on its own when Majapahit collapsed in the early
  sixteenth century. Its early centre was the port of Panarukan; later capitals lay further south
  near modern Banyuwangi, such as Macan Putih, founded by Tawang Alun II in 1665. Squeezed between
  the Muslim states of Java and the Hindu courts of Bali, Blambangan fought Demak, Pasuruan and
  Mataram, and often relied on Balinese help, which brought Gelgel, Buleleng and Mengwi influence
  over its court. Sultan Agung's army ravaged it in 1639. The Dutch East India Company conquered the
  kingdom in 1767, though resistance went on into the 1770s.
