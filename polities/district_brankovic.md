description: >-
  The District of Branković was a Serbian lordship centred on Priština and much of present Kosovo from 1371 to 1412. It emerged as the Serbian Empire broke apart, with Vuk Branković expanding his family lands into neighbouring regions. His realm included important towns and rich mining settlements, giving its rulers substantial resources. Government rested with the lord and his household rather than a unified Serbian imperial administration. Vuk fought at the Battle of Kosovo in 1389 and afterwards remained a major regional ruler amid growing Ottoman pressure. His son Đurađ inherited the family's position. Rivalry with Stefan Lazarević eventually gave way to a settlement in 1412, bringing the district into the Serbian Despotate. The Branković family retained considerable influence thereafter.
