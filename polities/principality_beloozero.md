description: >-
  The Principality of Beloozero was a Rus' principality in the forested north around Lake Beloye,
  in today's Vologda Oblast, with its capital at Beloozero, now Belozersk, one of the oldest
  towns named in the Primary Chronicle. The town had long been tied to Rostov, and it became a
  separate principality during the Mongol invasion of 1238, passing to Gleb Vasilkovich of the
  Rostov princely house. His descendants governed it as a distant principality on the fur and
  trade routes towards the White Sea and the Northern Dvina. Prince Fyodor Romanovich of
  Beloozero was killed fighting for Dmitry Donskoy at Kulikovo in 1380. Dmitry then secured the
  principality for his own family and left it to his son, and Ivan III later annexed it formally
  to Moscow.
