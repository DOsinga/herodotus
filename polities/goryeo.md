description: >-
  Goryeo was a Korean kingdom founded in 918 by Wang Geon, who replaced the ruler of Taebong and unified the Later Three Kingdoms by 936. Its principal capital was Gaegyeong, now Kaesong. Kings governed through aristocratic officials, with Buddhism enjoying strong royal support alongside Confucian learning. The kingdom produced celebrated celadon pottery, Buddhist woodblock printing, and early metal movable type. Wars with Khitan and Jurchen neighbours tested its northern frontier. From 1170, military leaders dominated the court for a century. Mongol invasions forced submission to Yuan influence, but the Korean royal house and government continued. As Yuan power declined, Goryeo recovered greater freedom. General Yi Seonggye eventually displaced the dynasty in 1392 and established Joseon, ending its long political tradition.
