description: >-
  The Republic of Florence was a city centred state in Tuscany, developing from the commune
  established after Matilda of Tuscany's death in 1115. Guild members and wealthy families shaped
  its government, whose leading council was the Signoria. Wool production, banking and
  international trade made Florence rich; its gold florin became an important currency far beyond
  Italy. Political life included factional struggles, popular revolts and repeated changes in who
  controlled the republican institutions. From 1434 the Medici usually directed affairs without
  initially abolishing the republic, although they were expelled twice. Florence also became a
  major international centre of Renaissance art and learning. After a siege restored Medici
  power, Alessandro became hereditary duke in 1532, replacing the republic with a ducal state.
