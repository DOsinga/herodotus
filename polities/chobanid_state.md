description: >-
  The Chobanid state controlled Azerbaijan and adjoining parts of Iran from 1338 to 1357, with its centre at Tabriz. Its ruling family descended from the Mongol commander Chupan, whose relatives had served the Ilkhans. After the Ilkhanate fragmented, Hasan Kuchak defeated his Jalayirid rival and established effective control around Tabriz. He retained nominal Ilkhanid monarchs, including Sati Beg and Suleiman, while exercising the real power himself. Family disputes and wars with neighbouring successor states shaped the regime. After Hasan's death, his brother Malek Ashraf prevailed in a succession struggle and continued governing behind puppet rulers. Failed campaigns and harsh rule weakened his position. Golden Horde forces captured Tabriz in 1357 and executed Malek Ashraf, ending Chobanid control of the region.
