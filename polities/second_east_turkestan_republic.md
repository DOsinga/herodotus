description: >-
  The Second East Turkestan Republic emerged from the Ili rebellion against Chinese Nationalist rule in 1944. Based at Ghulja, it controlled the northern Xinjiang districts of Ili, Tarbagatay, and Altay, with Soviet military and political support. Its population included many Kazakhs and Uyghurs, while its leadership also included other groups. Elihan Tore initially served as president of a government seeking independence. Soviet pressure encouraged negotiations with China, and in 1946 its leaders joined a provincial coalition while retaining their own administration and armed forces in the three districts. This record follows that continuing separate government through 1949. Several leading figures died while travelling to talks with Chinese Communists. The surviving leadership accepted incorporation into the People's Republic, ending separate rule.
