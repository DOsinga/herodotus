description: >-
  The Natufian culture occupied the Levant during the late Ice Age, approximately 12500 to 9500
  BCE in this record. Mount Carmel is a representative area with important archaeological sites,
  not a capital. Natufian communities hunted animals and collected wild plants, including
  cereals, while some lived in settlements for long periods before farming became established.
  Stone sickle blades, grinding equipment, buildings, burials, ornaments, and animal remains
  provide evidence of their daily life and social practices. Permanent settlement was not
  uniform, and changing climate affected how groups moved and used resources. These communities
  stand near the transition from mobile hunting and gathering to later agricultural villages.
  Their shared material culture does not identify a single state, dynasty, or known system of
  government.
