description: >-
  The Nakhichevan Khanate controlled the town of Nakhichevan and its surroundings on the middle
  Aras from 1747 to 1828, covering most of today's Nakhchivan Autonomous Republic and the Vayots
  Dzor province of Armenia. Under the Safavids the district had belonged to the province of
  Erivan and was governed by men of the Kangarlu tribe. After Nader Shah's death Heydar Qoli Khan
  Kangarlu made himself khan, and his family held the khanate through the turmoil of the
  following decades, recognising whichever ruler of Iran was strongest. The khans were drawn into
  the wars between Iran, Georgia and the neighbouring khanates, and later between Iran and
  Russia, in which members of the family took opposite sides. The khanate was abolished when the
  Treaty of Turkmenchay gave Nakhichevan to Russia in 1828.
