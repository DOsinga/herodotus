description: >-
  The Federated States of Micronesia is a Pacific island federation comprising Yap, Chuuk,
  Pohnpei and Kosrae. Its constitution took effect in 1979, and independence followed in
  1986 under a Compact of Free Association with the United States. The islands had
  previously formed part of a United Nations trust territory administered by America after
  Japanese rule ended in the Second World War. Palikir on Pohnpei became the national
  capital. An elected Congress chooses the president, while the four states retain
  substantial powers and distinct local traditions. The compact provides American defence
  and financial assistance, alongside rights for Micronesians to live and work in the
  United States. Fishing, subsistence farming and overseas support sustain communities
  scattered across a vast area of ocean.
