description: >-
  East Germany, officially the German Democratic Republic, occupied the eastern part of divided Germany from 1949 to 1990. It emerged from the Soviet occupation zone after the Second World War, with its government based in East Berlin. The Socialist Unity Party controlled political life, while a planned economy directed industry and agriculture. Soviet troops helped crush the workers' uprising of 1953. Large numbers of people fled west before the Berlin Wall closed that route in 1961. The Stasi maintained an extensive surveillance system. Despite industrial strength, shortages and political restrictions remained. Peaceful demonstrations and changing Soviet policy undermined the regime in 1989. The wall opened, competitive elections followed, and the state joined the Federal Republic of Germany in October 1990.
