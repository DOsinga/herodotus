description: >-
  The Commune of Cremona was the self-governing city state of Cremona on the Po, from the
  late eleventh century until the Visconti took it in 1334. Its consuls ruled a rich
  farming territory and the river trade, and the city built its cathedral and the great
  bell tower, the Torrazzo. Cremona was Milan's most loyal enemy: it helped Frederick
  Barbarossa destroy Crema in 1160 and Milan in 1162, and later stood with Frederick II,
  which made it a Ghibelline stronghold in a mostly Guelph Lombardy. In the thirteenth
  century strong men such as Buoso da Dovara ruled through the commune. Henry VII punished
  the city for its revolt in 1311, and after years of faction rule it submitted to Azzone
  Visconti in 1334.
