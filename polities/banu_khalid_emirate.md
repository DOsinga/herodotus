description: >-
  The Banu Khalid Emirate was the state of the Banu Khalid tribal confederation in eastern
  Arabia, centred on the oases of al-Hasa and al-Qatif, from 1670 until the Saudi conquest
  of the 1790s. In 1670 the chief Barrak ibn Ghurayr drove the Ottoman garrison out of
  al-Hasa, and his family, the Al Humayd, ruled the region from al-Mubarraz and al-Hofuf.
  At the height of its power the emirate's influence reached from the borders of Iraq to
  Qatar and deep into Najd, where the towns of the interior paid it tribute. It gave early
  protection to the Bani Utbah settlers of Kuwait. The rise of the Emirate of Diriyah ended its power, and Saudi
  forces took al-Hasa in 1795.
