description: >-
  Anhalt was an Ascanian principality in central Germany, between the Harz mountains and the Elbe.
  This record covers the times when its lands were united: under Henry I until his sons divided
  them in 1252, under Joachim Ernest from 1570 until his sons divided them again in 1603, and as
  the Duchy of Anhalt from 1863. Between those years separate Ascanian lines governed
  Aschersleben, Bernburg, Zerbst, Köthen, Dessau and Plötzkau, each recorded on its own. Joachim
  Ernest, who reunited the lands, gave them a common law code and a Lutheran church order. The
  duchy of 1863, ruled from Dessau, joined the North German Confederation in 1867, which took over
  its military and foreign policy.
