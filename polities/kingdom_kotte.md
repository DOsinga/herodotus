description: >-
  The Kingdom of Kotte was a Sinhalese monarchy in southwestern Sri Lanka, centred on the fortified city of Kotte from
  1412 to 1597. Parakramabahu VI consolidated its authority and briefly brought much of the island under one ruler, including the
  northern kingdom of Jaffna. The court supported Buddhist institutions and a flowering of Sinhala literature. Royal succession disputes later divided
  its territory, producing competing centres at Sitawaka and Kandy. Portuguese involvement in coastal trade increasingly drew the kingdom into foreign
  alliances and warfare. Dharmapala, its final ruler, converted to Christianity and depended on Portuguese support as neighbouring kings seized much
  of his realm. He bequeathed his kingdom to Portugal, and his death in 1597 completed the transfer of its lands.
