description: >-
  Breifne was a Gaelic overkingdom in northwestern Ireland, covering much of present Leitrim
  and Cavan with parts of nearby counties. Its separation from Connacht is conventionally
  placed around 750, although this is not a known founding year. By the tenth century it
  comprised smaller territories under an overking drawn from the Ui Briuin Breifne. Rulers
  were selected within eligible royal families through Gaelic succession practices. The
  O'Rourkes became the leading dynasty, with Dromahair as a principal centre. Under Tigernan
  O'Rourke, Breifne expanded its influence and confronted the Norman newcomers. His killing
  in 1172 intensified struggles over leadership. Rivalry between the O'Rourkes and O'Reillys
  culminated at Magh Slecht in 1256, dividing the kingdom into separately governed western
  and eastern successor states.
