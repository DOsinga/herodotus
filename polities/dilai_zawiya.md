description: >-
  The Zawiya Dila'iya was a Sufi brotherhood of the Middle Atlas in Morocco that became a
  state in the seventeenth century. Founded around 1566 by Abu Bakr al-Majjati, a Sanhaja
  Berber of the Jazuli order, it grew rich and influential as the Saadi state broke apart.
  Under his grandson Muhammad al-Hajj it built a walled capital at Dila, near present Ait
  Ishaq, in 1638 and began conquering the north. By 1641 it held Meknes, Fez and the port of
  Salé, where the corsair republic accepted its suzerainty, and Muhammad al-Hajj was
  proclaimed sultan at Fez. Salé broke away in 1660 and Fez in 1662. In 1668 the Alaouite
  Moulay Rashid took Dila and destroyed it, ending the brotherhood's rule.
