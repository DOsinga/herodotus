description: >-
  The Redarians were a Slavic political community around the disputed site of
  Rethra in what is now northeastern Germany. Their separate government is
  represented here approximately from 789 to 983 and again from 1057 to 1127.
  Medieval writers first name them in the tenth century. Rethra's sanctuary gave
  their community religious importance beyond its own settlements, with priests
  and assemblies playing significant roles in public decisions. The Redarians
  joined the Lutician federation during the uprising of 983 against imperial rule.
  Civil war within that federation in 1056 and 1057 weakened its common
  institutions and restored local political independence. Warfare with Christian
  neighbours then eroded their power. This record ends with Lothar's destruction
  of Rethra, while its exact location remains uncertain.
