description: >-
  The Liao dynasty was a Khitan-led empire in northeastern Asia from 916 to 1125, ruled by the Yelü family. Abaoji created an imperial monarchy from a steppe confederation, and his successors controlled Manchuria, much of Mongolia and parts of northern China. Shangjing was its upper capital within a system of capitals. The government maintained different administrative arrangements for pastoral populations and settled farming communities, combining Khitan institutions with Chinese models. Warfare and diplomacy shaped relations with Goryeo and the Song. The Chanyuan settlement established a lasting peace with Song and payments to Liao. Jurchen rebellion produced the Jin state, which conquered Liao and captured its last emperor in 1125. Yelü Dashi's western successor, Qara Khitai, is a separate record here.
