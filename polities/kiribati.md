description: >-
  Kiribati is an independent Pacific island republic established in 1979 after British
  colonial rule. It joins the Gilbert, Phoenix, and Line island groups with Banaba across a
  stretch of ocean, while South Tarawa serves as the seat of government. Its parliamentary
  system combines an elected legislature with a president chosen through a national vote
  from candidates nominated by parliament. Before colonial unification, the Gilbert Islands
  had separate local political communities. Independence followed the separation of the
  Ellice Islands, which became Tuvalu. Phosphate mining on Banaba left a financial reserve
  but also displacement and lasting disputes for Banabans. Fishing, copra, and overseas
  connections support island livelihoods. Low lying atolls and limited freshwater make
  environmental change a central concern for the country.
