description: >-
  The Kingdom of Rwanda was a monarchy in the highlands of present-day Rwanda and one of the
  most centralised states in the African Great Lakes region. Its Nyiginya dynasty emerged in
  the seventeenth century, with about 1650 as the estimate used here, and expanded by conquest
  from a small core in central Rwanda. The mwami ruled through chiefs responsible for land,
  cattle and armies, and royal power grew strongest under Kigeli IV Rwabugiri in the late
  nineteenth century. The divide between Tutsi cattle holders and Hutu farmers hardened as the
  court extended its control. German officers reached the court in 1894, and Mwami Yuhi V
  Musinga accepted German protection in 1897. The monarchy continued under German and later
  Belgian rule until the revolution of 1961 abolished it.
