name: Merina Kingdom
description: >-
  The Merina Kingdom grew from Madagascar's central highlands, traditionally beginning with Andriamanelo around 1540. Hereditary kings and queens ruled a society organised through hereditary royal lineages, nobles, and dependent labour. Antananarivo became its political capital, while Ambohimanga retained religious and dynastic importance. After periods of division, Andrianampoinimerina reunited the highland kingdom, and Radama I expanded its authority across much of Madagascar during the nineteenth century. Relations with Britain brought military assistance, missionaries, and new formal educational practices, while successive rulers alternately encouraged or restricted foreign influence. Later queens governed alongside powerful prime ministers, notably Rainilaiarivony. French military campaigns eventually overwhelmed the kingdom. The monarchy was abolished and Ranavalona III exiled in 1897, ending Merina rule under direct French colonial control.
