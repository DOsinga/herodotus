description: >-
  The Middle Assyrian Empire developed around Assur in northern Mesopotamia, in present day Iraq, from 1363 to 912 BCE. Ashur uballit I ended Mitanni's overlordship and established Assyria among the region's great powers. Kings conquered former Mitanni territory, campaigned against Babylonia and extended royal administration through governors, taxation and agricultural estates. Military service and forced population movements supported expansion. Tukulti Ninurta I briefly conquered Babylon, while Tiglath Pileser I carried campaigns toward the Mediterranean. The period also produced legal collections that reveal sharply unequal social relations and restrictions on women. Territorial losses and Aramean pressure weakened the empire, but its monarchy and institutions survived. Renewed expansion under later rulers marks the transition to the Neo Assyrian Empire in 912 BCE.
