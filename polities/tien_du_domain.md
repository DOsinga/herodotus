description: >-
  Tiên Du was a independent domain in northern Vietnam during the tenth century Period of the Twelve Warlords. Nguyễn Thủ Tiệp controlled the district, in Bắc Ninh Province, as central authority fragmented following the decline of Ngô rule. This record estimates his government's appearance in 965 and ends it with his defeat in 967. Tiệp used titles asserting lordly and then royal standing, including king of Vũ Ninh, but the surviving account gives little detail about his administration. His brothers Nguyễn Khoan and Nguyễn Siêu also ruled separate domains, so their holdings are not combined here. Đinh Bộ Lĩnh defeated Tiệp while reunifying the region. The conquest absorbed Tiên Du into the emerging Đinh state rather than preserving its government.
