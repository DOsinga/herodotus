description: >-
  The Tomara Kingdom of Delhi was ruled by the Tomaras, a Rajput clan, over parts of present
  Delhi and Haryana between the tenth and twelfth centuries. The family first appears as
  vassals of the Gurjara-Pratiharas, and as Pratihara power declined it set up its own
  principality around Delhi, reaching Hansi and Thanesar. Bardic tradition says that
  Anangapala founded Delhi in 1052, and the fortified enclosure of Lal Kot, later part of the
  Qutb complex, is attributed to him. Much of what is known comes from inscriptions, coins and
  later legends that are not always reliable. The Tomaras fought the Chahamanas of Shakambhari
  and the Gahadavalas. In the 1150s the Chahamana king Vigraharaja IV took Delhi, and the
  Tomaras became his vassals.
