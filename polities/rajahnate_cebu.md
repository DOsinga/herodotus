description: >-
  The Rajahnate of Cebu was a trading polity in the central Philippines, represented here from
  1450 until Spanish conquest in 1565. Its rulers, called rajahs, governed a port linked with
  regional maritime exchange and surrounding communities. Traditions concerning its foundation connect Cebu with migrants and
  wider Southeast Asian networks, although early chronology is uncertain. Rajah Humabon ruled when Ferdinand Magellan
  arrived in 1521. He accepted baptism and an alliance with the visitors, but Magellan died
  fighting on neighbouring Mactan, whose leaders did not simply submit to Cebu's authority. Spanish forces
  returned under Miguel López de Legazpi in 1565 and established a colonial settlement. Cebu's independent
  royal government disappeared, while the city developed into an important centre of Spanish Philippine rule.
