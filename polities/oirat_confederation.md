description: >-
  The Four Oirat were a confederation of western Mongol tribes, usually said to be the Choros,
  Torghut, Dörbet and Khoshut, which dominated western Mongolia, the Altai and the steppe of
  Dzungaria after the fall of the Yuan dynasty. The Oirats had been a forest people west of the
  Mongols who submitted to Genghis Khan in 1207. After 1368 their chiefs broke with the eastern
  Mongol khans of the Northern Yuan, and in the fifteenth century Toghan and his son Esen made
  the Oirats the strongest power on the steppe; Esen captured the Ming emperor in 1449. After
  Esen's death the confederation lost ground to the eastern Mongols. Early in the seventeenth
  century part of the Torghuts moved to the Volga, and the Choros founded the Dzungar Khanate.
