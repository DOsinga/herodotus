description: >-
  The Republic of China began in 1912 after the revolution that ended Qing imperial rule. Its early republican institutions soon faced authoritarian presidents, military factions and regional warlords. The Nationalist Party established a government at Nanjing after its campaigns of the 1920s, although control over China remained uneven. War with Japan forced the government inland and devastated the country. After Japan's defeat, renewed civil war brought Communist victory on the mainland. The republican government withdrew to Taiwan in 1949 and continued there rather than disappearing. Initially governed under Nationalist martial law, Taiwan later developed elected democratic institutions. The republic retains its own armed forces and administration, while its international status and relationship with the People's Republic of China remain contested.
