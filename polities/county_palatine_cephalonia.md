description: >-
  The County Palatine of Cephalonia and Zakynthos was a Latin lordship over the southern
  Ionian islands, held by Margaritus of Brindisi and the Orsini from 1185 and by the Tocco
  from 1357. William II of Sicily granted the islands to his admiral Margaritus after the
  Norman invasion of 1185, and Matthew Orsini followed him in 1195, recognising Venice,
  the pope and Achaea in turn. The Orsini gained Leucas in 1295 and conquered Epirus in
  the early fourteenth century. After the murder of John II Orsini in 1335 the Angevins of
  Achaea held the islands until they granted them to Leonardo Tocco in 1357. The Tocco
  used them to win Epirus, whose despots they became in 1411.
