description: >-
  The Commune of Bergamo was the self-governing city state of Bergamo in Lombardy, from
  the first recorded consuls around 1098 until the Visconti took the city in 1332. Consuls
  and later a podestà ran the city and its valleys, which supplied iron and wool. Bergamo
  joined the Lombard League against Frederick Barbarossa and shared in the victory that
  the Peace of Constance confirmed in 1183. For most of the thirteenth century it fought
  Brescia over the border lands along the Oglio. Inside the walls the Ghibelline Suardi
  and the Guelph Colleoni and Rivola fought for control, and outside lords and emperors
  were invited in to settle the feud. John of Bohemia held the city briefly in 1331, and
  Azzone Visconti took it in 1332.
