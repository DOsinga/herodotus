description: >-
  Kaiserslautern was a town in the western Palatinate that grew up around the palace where
  Frederick Barbarossa came to hunt in the forests of the Palatinate. After the end of the Staufer
  it was governed by the burghers and the imperial bailiff as a royal town, and in 1276 Rudolf of
  Habsburg granted it a charter as an imperial city. Its council governed the town and the
  imperial lands around it, and the collegiate church was built in this period. Like many small
  imperial cities it served the kings as security for loans, and in 1357 it was pledged to the
  Elector Palatine. The pledge was never redeemed, and Kaiserslautern became a town of the
  Electoral Palatinate.
