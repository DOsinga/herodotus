description: >-
  The Deva kingdom was a Hindu Vaishnava state in eastern Bengal during the thirteenth century,
  distinct from the earlier Buddhist Devas of Samatata. Its founder Purushottamadeva rose from
  village headman, and his son Madhusudanadeva was the first to take a royal title. The family is
  known mainly from three copperplate grants of Damodaradeva, dated 1234, 1236 and 1243, which
  show his rule over the Comilla, Noakhali and Chittagong region. A later king, Dasharathadeva,
  extended the kingdom to Bikrampur after the Sena line faded and made it his capital; Muslim
  chroniclers call him Danuj Rai of Sonargaon and record his alliance with the Delhi sultan Balban
  in 1281. His brother moved east in 1294, the last recorded event, and the region later fell to
  Fakhruddin Mubarak Shah of Sonargaon.
