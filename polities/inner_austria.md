description: >-
  Inner Austria was the group of Habsburg duchies of Styria, Carinthia and Carniola, with Gorizia
  and the Adriatic port of Trieste, governed from Graz by a branch of the Leopoldine Habsburgs
  after the family division of 1406. Ernest the Iron ruled it until 1424, and his son Frederick V
  succeeded him. Frederick was elected King of the Romans in 1440 and crowned emperor as Frederick
  III in 1452, while also acting as guardian of his Albertine cousin Ladislaus in Austria and of
  his Tyrolean cousin Sigismund. Inner Austria faced Ottoman raids and quarrels with the Counts of
  Celje. When Ladislaus died in 1457, Frederick inherited Austria, and Inner Austria was joined to
  the archduchy.
