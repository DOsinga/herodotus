  Afghanistan is a landlocked country between Central and South Asia, governed from Kabul. This
  record begins in 1973, when Mohammed Daoud Khan overthrew the monarchy and established a
  republic. Communist rule followed the 1978 coup, provoking rebellion and Soviet military
  intervention. After Soviet withdrawal and the communist government's collapse, rival mujahideen
  factions fought for control. The Taliban established an Islamic emirate in 1996, imposing a
  strict religious order. An American intervention removed that government in 2001, and a new
  republic relied on foreign support while confronting an insurgency. The Taliban returned to
  power in 2021 and restored their emirate. The first Taliban emirate is recorded separately;
  later governments stay in this record. War, displacement, ethnic diversity, and mountain
  geography have shaped the country's political life.
