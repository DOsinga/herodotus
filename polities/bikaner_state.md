description: >-
  Bikaner was a Rajput state in the Thar Desert of northwestern India, founded by Rao Bika
  in 1465. Its Rathore rulers established their seat at Bikaner and expanded through a
  region of scattered settlements and competing local chiefs. The dynasty accepted Mughal
  supremacy under Akbar, supplying soldiers and serving in imperial campaigns while
  retaining its own court and administration. Junagarh Fort became an expression of royal
  power and patronage. After Mughal authority weakened, Bikaner faced pressure from
  neighbouring states and wider struggles over Rajasthan. A treaty with the East India
  Company in 1818 transferred control of external relations and warfare to the British.
  Its maharajas continued ruling internally as princes, but the independent period
  recorded here ended with that agreement.
