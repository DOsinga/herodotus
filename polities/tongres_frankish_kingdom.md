description: >-
  Chararic's Frankish kingdom was one of several separate royal realms that existed before
  Clovis consolidated Frankish power. This record places it approximately between 460 and
  510 and uses Tongeren as a representative point, although both its chronology and its
  association with that city are disputed. Gregory of Tours provides the main narrative,
  identifying Chararic as a king but not defining his territory precisely. He reportedly
  withheld support during Clovis's campaign against Syagrius, waiting to see who would
  win. Clovis later captured Chararic and his son, had them tonsured and placed in
  clerical roles, then ordered their deaths after hearing of renewed ambitions. Their
  treasure and kingdom passed to Clovis. The evidence establishes a distinct ruler more
  clearly than any capital.
