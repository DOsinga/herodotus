description: >-
  Slovakia is a landlocked republic in central Europe, independent since the peaceful
  dissolution of Czechoslovakia on 1 January 1993. Bratislava, on the Danube near Austria
  and Hungary, is its capital. A parliamentary system places executive government under a
  prime minister responsible to the National Council, alongside an elected president. The
  new state inherited institutions formed during the transition from communist rule after
  1989. Its early years brought disputes over democratic practice, economic reform, and
  relations between national leaders. Later governments pursued closer European
  integration. Slovakia joined NATO and the European Union in 2004, entered the Schengen
  area in 2007, and adopted the euro in 2009. Industrial production, especially car
  manufacturing, has become central to its economy, while political competition continues.
