description: >-
  Panna State was a Bundela Rajput kingdom in Bundelkhand, central India, with its capital
  at Panna, a town known for its diamond mines. Its founder Chhatrasal led a long revolt
  against the Mughal emperor Aurangzeb, made Panna his capital around 1675 and, after taking
  Mahoba in 1680, extended his rule over most of Bundelkhand. When the Mughal general
  Muhammad Khan Bangash attacked him in old age, he called on the Maratha Peshwa Baji Rao I,
  and at his death in 1731 he left a third of his kingdom to the Peshwa. The rest was
  divided among his sons, and Panna went to the eldest, Harde Sah. The state passed under
  British protection in the early nineteenth century.
