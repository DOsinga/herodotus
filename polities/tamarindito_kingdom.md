description: >-
  Tamarindito was a Maya kingdom in the Petexbatún region of present northern Guatemala,
  represented here from about 400 to 800. Those dates estimate the life of its royal court
  rather than every phase of settlement. Tamarindito and nearby Arroyo de Piedra served as
  centres of the same dynasty. Inscriptions, monumental stairways and royal burials reveal
  kings who claimed a local lineage. The kingdom came under the influence of Dos Pilas but
  retained its own rulers. In 761 Tamarindito's king Chanal Balam defeated that overlord,
  a victory recorded on the city's stairways. Freedom did not bring lasting security:
  warfare and population decline spread through the region. The court disappeared around
  the end of the eighth century, and the city was abandoned.
