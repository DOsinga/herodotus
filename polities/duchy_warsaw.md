description: >-
  The Duchy of Warsaw restored a Polish government during the Napoleonic Wars, operating
  from 1807 until Russian occupation in 1813. Napoleon created it from lands taken from
  Prussia under the treaties of Tilsit, and Austrian territories enlarged it in 1809.
  Frederick Augustus of Saxony served as duke, while ministers and a parliament governed
  under a constitution shaped by French influence. The Napoleonic Code introduced legal
  changes, although peasant freedom did not remove dependence on landowners. Polish troops
  fought alongside Napoleon, hoping victory would restore a independent Poland. The
  disastrous invasion of Russia in 1812 destroyed those prospects. Russian forces occupied
  the duchy the following year. Its dissolution in 1815 divided its lands between successor
  arrangements imposed by the victorious powers.
