description: >-
  Venad was a kingdom at India's southwestern tip, between the Western Ghats and the
  Arabian Sea, with Kollam as its traditional capital. Its independent period began around
  1102 as the medieval Chera state broke apart, although the precise date is uncertain.
  Kulasekhara rulers governed through royal households and local chiefs, with matrilineal
  succession shaping the dynasty. Kollam's port connected the kingdom to Indian Ocean
  commerce. Venad acknowledged various larger powers and paid tribute while retaining its
  own rulers. Ravi Varma Kulasekhara briefly expanded into Tamil country during the early
  fourteenth century. Later dynastic branches and powerful nobles divided authority.
  Marthanda Varma's accession in 1729 marks its transformation into Travancore, whose
  monarchy consolidated territory and replaced much of the political order.
