description: >-
  Castruccio Castracani was a Ghibelline soldier who made himself lord of his native city of Lucca
  in 1316, after leading a revolt that expelled Uguccione della Faggiuola, the lord of Pisa who
  had taken the city two years earlier. Elected captain and then lord for life, he turned Lucca
  into the leading power of western Tuscany, conquering Pistoia and fighting a long war against
  Guelph Florence, which he defeated at Altopascio in 1325. Emperor Louis IV made him duke of
  Lucca in 1327 and an imperial vicar. He governed through his own officials and built the
  fortress of the Augusta in the city. Castruccio died of fever in 1328, and his young sons soon
  lost the lordship.
