description: >-
  The Kingdom of Hanover occupied much of present northern Germany between 1814 and 1866, with
  its capital at Hanover. The Congress of Vienna raised the electorate to a kingdom after the
  Napoleonic wars. Its rulers belonged to the House of Hanover and shared the British throne
  until 1837, when different succession laws gave Britain to Victoria and Hanover to Ernest
  Augustus. The kingdom had an assembly and a constitution, although royal attempts to
  restrict constitutional rights provoked opposition. It belonged to the German Confederation
  and included North Sea ports, farming districts and university towns. Hanover sided with
  Austria against Prussia in 1866. Despite winning the battle of Langensalza, its army
  surrendered, and Prussia annexed the kingdom as a new province.
