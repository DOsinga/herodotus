description: >-
  After the murder of the emperor Majorian in 461, his general Marcellinus refused to recognise
  the new government in Italy controlled by Ricimer and held Dalmatia, on the eastern Adriatic
  coast, as his own domain. With a fleet and an army he defended the province from his base at
  Salona and acted as an independent ally of the eastern emperor, fighting the Vandals in Sicily
  and Africa. He was murdered in 468, and his nephew Julius Nepos succeeded him in Dalmatia. Nepos
  became western emperor in 474, but was overthrown in 475 and returned to Dalmatia, where he kept
  the imperial title until he was killed in 480. Odoacer took Dalmatia soon after.
