description: >-
  The Wusun were a Central Asian people whose kingdom is recorded from roughly 170 BCE to 436 CE. After earlier struggles in Gansu, they moved west and established themselves in the Ili valley and around Issyk-Kul. Their monarch, called the kunmi, held court at Chigu, a royal settlement whose exact location remains uncertain. Livestock and horses supported their economy, while marriage alliances connected their rulers with both the Han court and the Xiongnu. These ties did not prevent shifting loyalties, wars, or disputes within the royal family. Chinese accounts provide most of the surviving political history. Pressure from the Rouran pushed the Wusun toward the Pamirs during the fifth century, after which their history becomes difficult to trace with certainty.
