description: >-
  The Lordship of Albarracín was an independent Christian lordship in the mountains between
  Castile and Aragon, recorded here from 1170 to 1284. Muhammad ibn Mardanish, the Wolf King
  of Murcia, ceded the former taifa of Albarracín to Pedro Ruiz de Azagra, a Navarrese noble,
  around 1167. Pedro made it independent of both neighbouring kingdoms and had a bishopric
  founded there, and his family, the Azagras, governed it for six generations, claiming to
  hold it of no king. The lords joined the conquest of Valencia in 1233 but also backed rebel
  nobles against James I of Aragon. Peter III of Aragon besieged and took the town in 1284 and
  annexed the lordship to his crown.
