description: >-
  The Helvetic Republic replaced the old Swiss Confederacy in 1798 after a French invasion and
  lasted until 1803. Instead of a loose association of sovereign cantons, it created a single
  state with a central legislature and a five member executive directory. Its constitution
  introduced common Swiss citizenship and removed the subordinate status of territories
  previously ruled by other cantons. These reforms met resistance from communities that valued
  local autonomy, while French influence and the costs of occupation undermined the new
  government. Switzerland became a battlefield for French, Austrian and Russian armies in
  1799. Repeated constitutional changes and civil conflict followed. Napoleon's Act of
  Mediation ended the republic in 1803, restoring a confederation while preserving some gains
  of the revolutionary period.
