name: "Neustria"
description: >-
  Neustria was the western Frankish kingdom centred on Soissons and other cities in present northern
  France. This record follows separate western governments between 511 and 719, interrupted by periods
  when Frankish courts were united. Its beginnings lie in the inheritance division after Clovis's
  death, particularly Chlothar's kingdom. Merovingian rulers repeatedly divided and reunited their
  lands, while queens, nobles, and palace officials fought for influence. Neustria's rivalry with
  Austrasia was especially fierce under Fredegund and her son Chlothar II. Later, mayors of the palace
  increasingly controlled government despite the survival of royal titles. Pepin of Herstal's victory
  at Tertry in 687 established eastern predominance. A final separate government emerged after Pepin's
  death, but Charles Martel defeated its supporters and restored unity.
