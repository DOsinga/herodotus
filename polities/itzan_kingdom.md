description: >-
  Itzan was a Maya kingdom in the Pasion region of northern Guatemala, represented here during the Late Classic centuries, approximately 600 to 900. Its centre occupied a ridge near a tributary of the Pasion River, northwest of Dos Pilas. Large buildings and numerous carved monuments indicate political importance beyond the site's modest size. Inscriptions identify lords and connections with neighbouring royal houses. Calakmul attacked Itzan in 652, and Dos Pilas also claimed a military victory there. A noblewoman from Itzan married the Dos Pilas king Balaj Chan Kawiil, linking the two courts through family as well as conflict. Itzan revived after Dos Pilas collapsed in the eighth century. Its decline belongs to the wider disruption of southern lowland Maya kingdoms.
