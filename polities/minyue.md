description: >-
  Minyue was a kingdom in present Fujian, southeastern China, from 306 to 110 BCE. Its
  origins are associated with refugees from the defeated state of Yue, whose royal
  descendants established authority among communities. Dongye, near modern Fuzhou, is
  identified as a capital, alongside an inland royal center in the Wuyi Mountains.
  Minyue's kings retained their own armies and conducted wars even while seeking
  recognition from the Han emperor. Wuzhu supported Liu Bang during the struggle
  following Qin's collapse and received confirmation as king. Later attacks on Dong'ou
  and Nanyue provoked Han intervention and royal appointments. Yushan attacked Han
  territory, bringing imperial invasion. His killing and the kingdom's defeat in 110
  BCE ended its independence; many inhabitants were ordered resettled northward.
