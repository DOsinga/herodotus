description: >-
  The Gakhars were a Punjabi clan whose chiefs held the Pothohar plateau between the Indus and
  the Jhelum, recorded here from the collapse of Ghurid rule in 1206 until Babur's conquest in
  1526. They had no kingdom with fixed institutions. Chiefs ruled from hill forts such as
  Pharwala, raised followers from their kin and fought the Janjuas, Khokhars and other clans
  for the Salt Range. Delhi sultans, Mongol commanders and Timur crossed the plateau or claimed
  it, but rarely governed it for long. In the early fifteenth century the Khokhar leader Jasrat
  dominated the plateau and much of northern Punjab. By Babur's time the Gakhars had recovered
  their strength, and their chief Hati Khan submitted to him after losing Pharwala, beginning a
  long period of service to the Mughal emperors.
