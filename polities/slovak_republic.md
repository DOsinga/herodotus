description: >-
  The Slovak Republic of 1939 to 1945 was a wartime state centred on Bratislava, created during the breakup of Czechoslovakia under German pressure. It controlled most of Slovakia, excluding southern areas ceded to Hungary. President Jozef Tiso and Hlinka's Slovak People's Party headed an authoritarian, single party government aligned with Nazi Germany. The state joined the Axis and sent forces against Poland and the Soviet Union. Its authorities persecuted Jews, confiscated their property and organised deportations that enabled mass murder. Opposition culminated in the Slovak National Uprising of 1944, followed by German occupation and partisan resistance. Soviet and allied advances ended the regime in 1945, and its territory returned to restored Czechoslovakia. Modern Slovakia does not claim its legal succession.
