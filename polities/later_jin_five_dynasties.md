description: >-
  Later Jin was a northern Chinese imperial state from 936 to 947, one of the Five
  Dynasties following Tang collapse. Shi Jingtang founded it by rebelling against Later
  Tang with military help from the Khitan rulers later known as Liao. He ceded the
  strategic Sixteen Prefectures and accepted tribute obligations, while maintaining his
  own court and administration. The initial seat at Taiyuan shifted through Luoyang to
  Kaifeng. Its territory covered much of northern China but excluded Sichuan and the
  ceded northern frontier. Shi Jingtang's successor, Shi Chonggui, resisted the
  relationship with Liao, bringing invasions. Liao armies defeated his forces and took
  the capital, ending the state in January 947. This regime was unrelated to the
  seventeenth century Manchu Later Jin.
