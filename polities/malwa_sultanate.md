description: >-
  The Malwa Sultanate ruled the plateau of central India, principally in present Madhya
  Pradesh and southeastern Rajasthan. Dilawar Khan established an independent monarchy in
  1401 as Delhi's authority weakened after Timur's invasion. The court moved from Dhar to
  fortified Mandu, where its rulers sponsored mosques, palaces, tombs and illustrated
  manuscripts. Ghurid and Khalji sultans competed with Gujarat and neighbouring Rajput
  powers, while court factions repeatedly unsettled succession. Gujarat conquered Malwa in
  1531, followed by a brief restoration of local rule. Sher Shah's empire took control in
  1542. Baz Bahadur revived independence in 1555 and became famous for his musical
  interests and association with Rupmati. Mughal campaigns finally removed his government
  by 1562, bringing Malwa into Akbar's empire as a province.
