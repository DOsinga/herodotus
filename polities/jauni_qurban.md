description: >-
  The Jauni Qurban were a Mongol tribe settled in northern Khorasan whose chiefs governed Tus and
  the country around it, including the shrine town of Mashhad, in the decades after the fall of
  the Ilkhanate. Their leader Arghun Shah was a commander of the Ilkhanid claimant Togha Temür
  and held Tus in his name. He fought the Sarbadars of Sabzevar, who defeated him in 1340 and
  took Nishapur. After Togha Temür's murder in 1353 the Jauni Qurban amirs governed on their own,
  at times allied with the Kartids of Herat or with Amir Vali of Astarabad against the Sarbadars.
  Arghun Shah's successor submitted to Timur in 1381, but after a later revolt Timur's son Miran
  Shah sacked Tus in 1389, and Mashhad took its place.
