name: Weimar Republic
description: >-
  The Weimar Republic was Germany's first national parliamentary democracy, lasting from the
  revolution of 1918 to the Nazi takeover in 1933. It replaced imperial monarchy after defeat in the
  First World War. Its constitution, drafted at Weimar, created a federal republic with an elected
  president, parliament, and civil rights, while Berlin remained the capital. Governments faced
  revolutionary violence, attempted coups, and the burdens of the Versailles settlement.
  Hyperinflation devastated savings in 1923, followed by economic recovery and a flourishing cultural
  scene. The Great Depression then brought mass unemployment and rising support for extremist parties.
  Presidential emergency rule weakened parliamentary government. Hitler's appointment as chancellor in
  January 1933, followed by repression and the Enabling Act, replaced democratic institutions with
  Nazi dictatorship.
