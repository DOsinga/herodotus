description: >-
  The Ahmadilis were a Turkic family of atabegs who ruled Maragheh and its district in
  northwestern Iran during the decline of the Seljuks. The line began with Aq Sunqur Ahmadili, a
  freedman of the Seljuk commander Ahmadil, who became guardian of a Seljuk prince and held
  Maragheh from about 1122. His successors used their position to back rival claimants to the
  Seljuk throne and competed with the stronger Eldiguzids, who dominated the rest of Azerbaijan.
  Their chronology is uncertain, because the chronicles mention them only now and then and no
  coins of theirs are known. A woman of the family, Sulafa Khatun, held Maragheh when the Mongols
  sacked it in 1221. Her marriage to the Khwarazmshah Jalal al-Din in 1225 handed her lands to
  him.
