description: >-
  The Prome kingdom was a small state in central Burma, governed from the old river city of Prome,
  now Pyay, between 1482 and 1542. It began when Thado Minsaw, governor of nearby Tharrawaddy,
  seized Prome during a succession struggle at Ava and declared himself king. Ava failed to
  recover the city, and the new kingdom held lands as far as Tharrawaddy and Myede. Thado Minsaw
  kept peace with Hanthawaddy, then joined the Shan Confederation in raiding Ava and sacked it in
  1525. The Confederation turned on Prome in 1532 and carried off its king, after which his son
  ruled a reduced realm as a nominal vassal. Prome sided with Hanthawaddy against rising Toungoo
  and fell to Toungoo forces after a five month siege in 1542.
