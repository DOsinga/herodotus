description: >-
  The Third Dynasty of Ur governed southern Mesopotamia from 2112 to 2004 BCE under the
  middle chronology. Founded by Ur-Nammu, the kingdom reunited cities of Sumer and Akkad
  after earlier imperial rule had broken down. His son Shulgi expanded its reach and
  strengthened an administrative system. Kings appointed provincial governors, directed
  temple resources and organised taxation, labour and military campaigns. Thousands of
  surviving clay tablets record deliveries, workers, livestock and legal business, making
  its government unusually visible. Royal building programmes included the great ziggurat
  at Ur. Later rulers faced pressure from neighbouring peoples and declining control over
  provinces. Under Ibbi-Sin the kingdom fragmented; Elamite forces sacked Ur in 2004 BCE,
  captured the king and held the city until Isin intervened.
