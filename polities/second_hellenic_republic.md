description: >-
  The Second Hellenic Republic governed Greece from 1924 to 1935, with Athens as its
  capital. It followed military defeat in Asia Minor, the displacement of large displaced
  refugee populations, and a deep division between royalists and supporters of Eleftherios
  Venizelos. Parliament proclaimed the republic in March 1924, and a referendum endorsed
  the change. Presidents replaced the king, while parliamentary cabinets remained central
  to government. Military intervention repeatedly disrupted this framework, including the
  dictatorship of Theodoros Pangalos. Venizelos later returned to office and sought
  diplomatic reconciliation with Turkey. Serious economic strains and continuing political
  factional conflict undermined stability. After a failed republican military rising in
  1935, Georgios Kondylis overthrew the government. A manipulated referendum restored King
  George II, ending the republic.
