description: >-
  The Gattilusio Lordship of Lesbos was a Genoese-ruled state on the island of Lesbos in the
  northern Aegean, with its seat at Mytilene, from 1355 to 1462. Francesco Gattilusio helped
  the Byzantine emperor John V Palaiologos recover Constantinople in 1354, married the
  emperor's sister Maria, and received Lesbos as her dowry. His family married into the
  Palaiologoi for four generations, spoke Greek and kept the Orthodox Church, while trading
  with Genoa in alum and other goods. They called themselves simply lords. From Mytilene the
  family won Thasos, Lemnos, Imbros, Samothrace and Ainos, but these became separate lordships.
  After the fall of Constantinople the Gattilusi held Lesbos for an annual payment to the
  Ottomans, until an Ottoman fleet took the island in 1462.
