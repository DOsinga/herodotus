name: "Jin (Chinese state)"
description: >-
  Jin was a major Chinese monarchy based in the Fen River basin of present Shanxi. Founded
  earlier as a Zhou fief, it is represented here from 770 BCE, when the eastward movement
  of the Zhou court left regional lords governing their own states. Jin expanded through conquest and
  alliances, and Duke Wen became a leading hegemon during the Spring and Autumn period. Its rulers
  depended on aristocratic families that commanded armies and controlled substantial lands. These families gradually displaced ducal
  authority. In 453 BCE, Han, Zhao and Wei destroyed the Zhi clan and took over most
  of Jin. The Zhou court recognized their separate states in 403 BCE. Jin's duke retained a
  small domain until its partition in 376 BCE.
