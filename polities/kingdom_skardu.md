description: >-
  The Kingdom of Skardu was the Balti state ruled by the Maqpon rajas from the Skardu valley in
  the Karakoram, recorded here from about 1150 until the Dogra conquest of 1840. Baltistan was
  divided among valley kingdoms, chiefly Skardu, Shigar and Khaplu, and the Maqpon of Skardu
  were the strongest of them. Islam spread through the region from the fourteenth century under
  Sufi missionaries from Kashmir. Ali Sher Khan Anchan, who reigned from about 1580 to 1633,
  conquered Khaplu, briefly took Leh and extended his power to Gilgit and Astore. After his
  death his sons fought over the throne, and from 1637 the rajas accepted Mughal overlordship
  while still governing the valley themselves. Zorawar Singh took Skardu for the Dogras of Jammu,
  under Sikh suzerainty, in 1840.
