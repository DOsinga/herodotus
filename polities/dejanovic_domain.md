name: Dejanović domain
description: >-
  The Dejanović domain was a Serbian noble state between the Vardar and Struma rivers from
  1371 to 1395. Its lands lay across parts of today's North Macedonia, Bulgaria, and Serbia,
  with Velbužd as a centre. Brothers Jovan Dragaš and Konstantin inherited estates associated
  with their father Dejan as the Serbian Empire fragmented. They governed their own province
  while acknowledging Ottoman supremacy after the Battle of Maritsa. Their court issued
  charters, supported Orthodox monasteries, and minted coins following Serbian royal
  traditions. Mining at Kratovo and Zletovo and commerce with foreign merchants helped sustain
  its economy. Konstantin eventually ruled alone and served in Ottoman campaigns. His death at
  Rovine in 1395 allowed the Ottomans to take direct control of the family's territory.
