description: >-
  The Ilyasids were a dynasty of Sogdian origin that ruled the province of Kerman in southeastern
  Iran from 932 to 968. Abu Ali Muhammad ibn Ilyas had served in the Samanid army and took part
  in a failed coup against the Samanid amir Nasr II in 929. Fleeing south, he reached Kerman in
  932 and made himself master of its northern districts, ruling from Bardasir while the Koch and
  Baloch held the southern mountains. He acknowledged the Samanids or the Buyids as overlords
  when it suited him but stayed effectively independent for more than thirty years. After a
  stroke he abdicated in 967 in favour of his son Ilyasa, whose border quarrels with the Buyid
  ruler Adud al-Dawla brought a Buyid invasion in 968.
