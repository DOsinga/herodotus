description: >-
  The County of Arnsberg was a county in the Sauerland of southern Westphalia, governed from the
  castle of Arnsberg above the Ruhr. Its counts descended from the counts of Werl, among the most
  powerful families in Westphalia, and moved their seat to Arnsberg in the late eleventh century.
  In the thirteenth and fourteenth centuries they were squeezed between the archbishops of
  Cologne, who held the ducal authority in Westphalia, and the rising counts of Mark, and they
  lost much of their land. The town of Arnsberg received its charter in 1238. Gottfried IV, the
  last count, had no heirs, and in 1368 he sold the county to the archbishop of Cologne, who
  joined it to the Duchy of Westphalia.
