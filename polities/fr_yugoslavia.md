name: Federal Republic of Yugoslavia
description: >-
  The Federal Republic of Yugoslavia united Serbia and Montenegro from 1992 to 2003 after the breakup
  of socialist Yugoslavia. Belgrade housed its federal institutions, but Serbia's size and Slobodan
  Milošević's influence shaped the balance of power. The constitution provided a president,
  parliament, and government while the two republics retained their own administrations. Its claim to
  continue the former Yugoslav state's international identity was rejected. Support for Serb forces
  during neighbouring wars brought sanctions and isolation, compounded by severe economic collapse and
  hyperinflation. Conflict in Kosovo led to NATO bombing in 1999 and international administration
  there. Milošević lost power after protests in 2000. In 2003 a new constitutional arrangement
  replaced the federation with the looser state union of Serbia and Montenegro.
