description: >-
  The Banu Ghaniya governed the Balearic Islands as an emirate after mainland Almoravid
  power collapsed around 1147. Based at Palma, the ruling family descended from Almoravid
  relatives who had originally held the islands as governors. Refugees from the defeated
  dynasty strengthened this island stronghold against the rising Almohads. Its rulers
  gained income from maritime raiding and maintained ties across the western
  Mediterranean. Succession disputes divided the family, while Ali ibn Ishaq launched a
  separate campaign in North Africa in 1184. Almohad intervention in 1185 installed Banu
  Ghaniya princes, followed by further changes driven by island troops. Local dynastic
  government nevertheless continued. In 1203 the Almohads defeated Abdallah ibn Ishaq and
  replaced the emirate with governors, ending its separate island rule.
