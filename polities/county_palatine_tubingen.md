description: >-
  The County Palatine of Tübingen was the lordship of the counts palatine of Tübingen, who held
  the office of count palatine of Swabia and ruled lands on the upper Neckar and in the Black
  Forest from their castle above Tübingen. In the twelfth century they were among the leading
  Swabian nobles and founded the monastery of Bebenhausen. From the middle of the thirteenth
  century the family split into lines at Horb, Herrenberg, Böblingen and Asperg, and their divided
  lands passed bit by bit to others. The town of Tübingen grew around the castle and received a
  charter. In 1342 the impoverished counts sold Tübingen with its castle and lands to the counts
  of Württemberg, who made it their second residence.
