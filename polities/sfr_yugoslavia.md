description: >-
  Socialist Yugoslavia governed a federation in southeastern Europe from 1945 to 1992, with
  its capital at Belgrade. Built by the victorious Partisan movement after the Second World
  War, it replaced the monarchy with a communist led state. Its six republics were Bosnia
  and Herzegovina, Croatia, Macedonia, Montenegro, Serbia, and Slovenia; Kosovo and
  Vojvodina had autonomous status within Serbia. Josip Broz Tito dominated national politics
  until his death in 1980. The break with Stalin in 1948 allowed Yugoslavia to pursue its
  own socialism, including workers' self management, and a prominent role in the Non Aligned
  Movement. Economic crisis and competing national movements later undermined the
  federation. Secessions and war destroyed it, leaving Serbia and Montenegro in a Yugoslav
  state in 1992.
