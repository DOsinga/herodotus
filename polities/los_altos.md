description: >-
  The State of Los Altos was a breakaway state in the western highlands of Guatemala, with
  its capital at Quetzaltenango. The region's creole and mestizo elite resented rule from
  Guatemala City, and a separate state had been discussed since independence. In February
  1838, as the Federal Republic of Central America fell apart, Los Altos proclaimed its
  separation, and the federal congress admitted it that June as a sixth state. It covered
  several western departments and claimed the Soconusco. Its liberal government soon clashed
  with the conservative leader Rafael Carrera, who had taken control of Guatemala. In 1840
  Carrera's army invaded, defeated the forces of Los Altos and occupied Quetzaltenango, and
  the state was annexed to Guatemala. A second attempt at independence in 1848 lasted only
  months.
