description: >-
  The Chiefdom of Bozhou was a hereditary domain of the Yang family centred on Zunyi in
  northern Guizhou, China, from 876 to 1600. Yang Duan took Bozhou from Nanzhao forces and
  the Tang court recognised him as its hereditary ruler in 876. Twenty-nine generations of
  Yang chiefs then governed the region through the Song, Yuan and Ming dynasties, which gave
  them titles as native officials while leaving the government and the army in their hands.
  The Yang built the mountain fortress of Hailongtun in 1257 against the Mongols and ruled
  Han, Gelao and Miao subjects. With Shuixi, Sizhou and Shuidong it counted among the four
  great native chiefdoms of Guizhou. Yang Yinglong's revolt from 1589 ended when Ming armies
  stormed Hailongtun in 1600.
