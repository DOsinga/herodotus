description: >-
  The Carnatic Sultanate, or Arcot State, was the government of the nawabs of the Carnatic in the
  south east of India, between the Eastern Ghats and the Coromandel Coast. It grew out of the Mughal
  province set up after the conquest of Gingee, and from 1710 Saadatullah Khan ruled it as a
  hereditary nawab from Arcot, under the nominal authority of the Nizam of Hyderabad. Disputed
  successions drew in the French and British, whose Carnatic Wars of the 1740s and 1750s made
  Muhammad Ali Khan Wallajah nawab with British support. He moved to Chepauk in Madras and ran up
  huge debts to the East India Company and its servants. The Company took over the Carnatic's
  administration by the treaty of 1801, leaving the nawabs a title and pension.
