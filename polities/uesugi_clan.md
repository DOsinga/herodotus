description: >-
  The Uesugi clan record covers the Sengoku state of Echigo province on the Sea of Japan coast,
  ruled from Kasugayama from the Ōnin War in 1467 until it submitted to Toyotomi Hideyoshi in
  1586. A branch of the Uesugi held Echigo as military governors, with the Nagao family as
  their deputies. In 1507 Nagao Tamekage drove his lord to suicide and took power himself.
  His son Nagao Kagetora was adopted by the fleeing Kantō Uesugi, took their name in 1561 and
  is remembered as Uesugi Kenshin. Kenshin fought the Takeda and the Later Hōjō for decades
  and late in life pushed west into Etchū and Noto. After his death in 1578 his heir Kagekatsu
  won a succession war and later accepted Hideyoshi's rule.
