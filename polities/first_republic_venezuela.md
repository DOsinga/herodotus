description: >-
  The First Republic of Venezuela was the first independent government in Spanish America.
  On 19 April 1810 the cabildo of Caracas deposed the captain general and set up a Supreme
  Junta that governed in the name of the captive Ferdinand VII. Most provinces of the
  captaincy general joined it, although Coro, Maracaibo and Guayana stayed royalist. A
  congress declared independence on 5 July 1811 and adopted a federal constitution in
  December. The republic struggled with divided provinces, paper money and royalist
  uprisings, and the earthquake of March 1812 destroyed Caracas and much of its support.
  Francisco de Miranda was given dictatorial powers, but the royalist commander Domingo de
  Monteverde advanced on the capital, and Miranda surrendered in July 1812.
