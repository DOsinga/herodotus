description: >-
  The Republic of Vietnam governed southern Vietnam from 1955 to 1975, with Saigon as its
  capital. Ngo Dinh Diem replaced the State of Vietnam's monarchy after a referendum and
  established an anticommunist presidential republic. His authoritarian government faced
  opposition over political repression and religious policies, while communist insurgency
  expanded in the countryside. A military coup killed Diem in 1963, followed by unstable
  military governments before another constitutional republic emerged. American aid and troops
  became crucial to its war against communist forces supported by North Vietnam, although South
  Vietnamese leaders pursued their own goals. Fighting devastated communities and displaced
  millions. After American withdrawal, northern offensives overwhelmed the republic. Saigon fell
  in April 1975, ending its government and leading toward Vietnam's reunification.
