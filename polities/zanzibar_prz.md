description: >-
  The People's Republic of Zanzibar governed Zanzibar and Pemba off East Africa's coast between
  January and April 1964. It followed a revolution that overthrew the recently independent sultanate,
  replacing the Arab dynasty with a Revolutionary Council led by Abeid Karume. The Afro Shirazi Party
  and the Umma Party participated in the new administration, while John Okello initially commanded
  armed revolutionaries. Violence against Arab and South Asian residents accompanied the upheaval and
  caused many deaths and departures. Karume consolidated power, sidelined Okello, and pursued social
  and economic reforms. Cold War concerns brought close international attention to the islands. In
  April, the republic united with Tanganyika to create Tanzania, retaining a separate island
  government within the new union rather than full sovereignty.
