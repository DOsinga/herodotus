description: >-
  The Commonwealth of England was the republican government established in 1649 after
  Charles I's execution, lasting until the monarchy returned in 1660. Parliament abolished
  kingship and the House of Lords, initially governing with a Council of State.
  Parliamentary armies conquered Ireland and defeated Scottish resistance, bringing the
  three kingdoms under a structure. The army remained decisive in government, repeatedly
  clashing with parliaments over reform, religion and authority. In 1653 Oliver Cromwell
  became Lord Protector under a written constitution, followed briefly by his son Richard.
  Naval expansion and wars with the Dutch and Spain strengthened England's overseas
  position. Richard's fall brought renewed parliamentary rule but little stability. Charles
  II's restoration ended the experiment and restored separate kingdoms under the Stuart
  monarchy.
