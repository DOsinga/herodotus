description: >-
  The Saudeleur dynasty was the first government to unite the island of Pohnpei in the eastern
  Caroline Islands of Micronesia. It ruled from the megalithic complex of Nan Madol, built of
  basalt columns on artificial islets off the island's southeast coast. Pohnpeian tradition says
  the founders were foreign sorcerers and that one ruler, the Saudeleur, owned the land and its
  people, leasing it to lesser chiefs who collected heavy tribute in food and labour. The island
  was divided into three districts with a court of titled officials at the capital. Later rulers
  are remembered as cruel and greedy. The dynasty ended when the warrior Isokelekel overthrew
  the last Saudeleur, after which Pohnpei divided into separate chiefdoms under the nahnmwarki
  title system that still exists.
