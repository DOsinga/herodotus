name: Federal Republic of Central America
description: >-
  The Federal Republic of Central America united Guatemala, El Salvador, Honduras, Nicaragua, and
  Costa Rica after they separated from Mexico in 1823. A constitution adopted in 1824 established
  a president, federal legislature, and state governments, drawing inspiration from the United
  States. The capital began in Guatemala City and later moved to San Salvador. Liberal leaders,
  notably Francisco Morazán, sought stronger common institutions and reforms that reduced church
  influence, while conservative opponents defended religious authority and local interests.
  Limited revenue, rival regional elites, and repeated civil wars made the federation difficult
  to hold together. Its states withdrew between 1838 and 1841, leaving independent republics.
  Later attempts at reunion recalled this brief experiment but did not restore its government
  across Central America.
