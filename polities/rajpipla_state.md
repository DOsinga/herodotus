description: >-
  Rajpipla State was a Rajput kingdom in the forested hills south of the Narmada in eastern
  Gujarat, ruled by a branch of the Gohil clan. Tradition dates the dynasty to around 1340, when a
  Gohil prince inherited the hill country from his maternal relatives. Its capital moved between
  hill forts before settling at Nandod, the modern town of Rajpipla, in the plain of the Karjan
  river. The rajas held on through attacks by the sultans of Ahmedabad and the Mughals, often
  retreating into the hills and paying tribute rather than giving up their government. In the
  eighteenth century the Gaekwads of Baroda took a heavy share of its revenue. A settlement with
  the British in 1821 made it a protected state, later the largest in the Rewa Kantha Agency.
