description: >-
  The Al Ayedh Emirate was a highland state in Asir ruled from Abha by chiefs of the Mughayd
  tribal group. After the fall of the first Saudi state in 1818, Asiri tribes fought the
  Egyptian forces of Muhammad Ali under Mughaydi leaders: Sa'id ibn Musallat from the 1820s,
  then Ali ibn Mujaththil, and from 1834 the Al Ayedh line. When the Egyptians withdrew in 1840
  the Al Ayedh ruled the highlands; they were usually Saudi allies but never under Saudi
  command. Their attempts to expand into the Tihama plain provoked the Ottomans, who executed
  Muhammad ibn Ayedh and made Abha the seat of a district of the Yemen province. After the
  Ottoman withdrawal in 1918 Hasan ibn Ayedh held Abha until the Saudis took it in 1920.
