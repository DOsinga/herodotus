description: >-
  The Aztec Triple Alliance united Tenochtitlan, Texcoco, and Tlacopan in 1428 after their
  victory over Azcapotzalco. From the Basin of Mexico, the allied city states expanded a tribute
  empire across much of central Mexico until 1521. Each partner initially retained its own ruler,
  but Tenochtitlan became dominant and its emperor directed extensive campaigns. Conquered
  communities generally kept local governments while delivering goods and labour, rather than
  becoming uniformly administered provinces. Markets, farming, and tribute supported the island
  capital, while ritual and warfare reinforced royal power. Enemies and reluctant subjects
  survived around the empire. Hernan Cortes and his Indigenous allies exploited these divisions,
  besieging Tenochtitlan in 1521. Its fall ended the alliance's imperial government and brought
  its territories under Spanish rule.
