description: >-
  Qin began as a western frontier lordship and became a recognised Zhou state in 770 BCE after helping escort the
  royal court east. Its rulers gradually expanded across the valleys of present day Shaanxi and adjoining regions. Initially governed by
  hereditary lords, Qin later adopted reforms associated with Shang Yang that strengthened central administration, rewarded military service, and imposed strict
  laws. Agricultural development and irrigation supported its growing armies. Its capitals shifted from Yong to Yueyang and finally Xianyang, reflecting
  expansion and political change. During the Warring States period, Qin defeated vulnerable rivals and absorbed their territories. In 221 BCE,
  King Zheng completed the conquest of the other major states and assumed the title of emperor, beginning the Qin dynasty.
