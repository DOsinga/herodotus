description: >-
  The Lordship of Prilep was a small Balkan state ruled by King Marko from 1371 to 1395, in territory
  now within North Macedonia. It emerged as the Serbian Empire broke apart after the death of Marko's
  father, King Vukašin, at the battle of Maritsa. Marko inherited a royal title, but rival nobles took
  much of his family's former lands, leaving a reduced domain around Prilep. His court maintained his
  own local government and Orthodox religious patronage while accepting Ottoman overlordship. That
  relationship required military service without immediately replacing his own rule. In 1395 he fought
  for the Ottoman sultan against Wallachia and died at Rovine. The Ottomans then annexed his remaining
  territory, ending the separate government associated with his court.
