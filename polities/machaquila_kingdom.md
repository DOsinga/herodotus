description: >-
  Machaquila was a Maya kingdom on the lower Machaquila River in Guatemala's Petén region,
  represented here from about 650 to 900. Its location linked routes from the Maya Mountains to
  the Pasión and Usumacinta rivers. Sculpted monuments and inscriptions provide the main evidence
  for its royal court, which flourished during the Late and Terminal Classic periods. Shared royal
  titles connected it with Cancuen, and an inscription there depicts King Tajal Chan Ahk
  installing rulers at Machaquila. Whether these links describe joint government or subordination
  remains debated. Machaquila's court continued after Cancuen's destruction and gained prominence
  as other regional powers weakened. Its temples, palaces, stelae and altars mark a royal centre
  whose estimated endpoint reflects the end of that governing tradition.
