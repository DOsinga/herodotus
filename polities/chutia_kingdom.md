description: >-
  The Chutia Kingdom was a late medieval state in the far east of the Brahmaputra valley,
  centred on Sadiya in present Assam and reaching into the foothills of Arunachal Pradesh. Its
  people were Bodo-Kachari groups who settled the plains around Sadiya, probably before the
  Ahoms arrived in 1228. The kingdom becomes clear in the second half of the fourteenth
  century, when inscriptions name King Nandin and his son Satyanarayana, seated at Sadhyapuri.
  Historians describe it as the most advanced of the states that rose after the fall of
  Kamarupa, with crafts, trade and strong Hindu influence. It fought the Ahoms for decades.
  Around 1523 the Ahom king Suhungmung conquered it, and its nobles were taken into Ahom
  service.
