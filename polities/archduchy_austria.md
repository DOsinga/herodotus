description: >-
  The Archduchy of Austria was the Habsburg monarchy's central hereditary territory along
  the Danube, with Vienna as its city. This record covers 1453 to 1804, beginning when
  Emperor Frederick III recognised the archducal title previously claimed through the forged
  Privilegium Maius. Habsburg archdukes governed the Austrian lands while often holding the
  imperial crown and ruling additional kingdoms. The archduchy itself was not identical to
  all those wider possessions. Upper and Lower Austria formed its core, and divisions among
  branches of the family complicated its earlier administration. Religious conflict, Ottoman
  wars and reforms under Maria Theresa and Joseph II shaped its later history. Francis
  proclaimed the Austrian Empire in 1804, placing the archduchy within a new imperial
  framework that retained Vienna.
