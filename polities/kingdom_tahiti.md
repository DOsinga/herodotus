description: >-
  The Kingdom of Tahiti formed under Pōmare I around 1788 or 1791 in the Society Islands of the
  South Pacific. Through alliances, warfare and contact with European visitors, the Pōmare family
  strengthened its position among competing chiefs. Pōmare II's victory at Te Feipī in 1815
  consolidated royal authority, while Protestant missionaries encouraged Christianity, literacy
  and new laws. The monarchy governed Tahiti and associated islands, with Papeete later becoming
  its capital. French pressure produced a protectorate in the 1840s and a war that ended in 1847.
  The royal title continued, but French administration sharply limited the rulers' control. This
  record ends with the protectorate of 1842; in 1880 Pōmare V ceded the kingdom to France. Its
  islands thereafter formed part of the colonial territory that became modern French Polynesia.
