description: >-
  The Toungoo state grew from a Burmese kingdom at Taungoo, independent of Ava in 1510, into a
  Southeast Asian empire. Kings Tabinshwehti and Bayinnaung reunited much of Burma and brought
  Shan states, Siam and other neighbouring territories under their authority. Government
  combined administration with tributary rulers, making the empire dependent on its king's
  ability to command loyalty. After Bayinnaung's death in 1581, many dependencies broke away.
  Nyaungyan and Anaukpetlun rebuilt a smaller kingdom, eventually centred at Ava. Restored
  Toungoo rulers replaced hereditary chiefs with appointed governors in the Irrawaddy valley
  and strengthened administration. Later raids and rebellions weakened the state. The Mon
  uprising in Lower Burma created Restored Hanthawaddy, whose armies captured Ava in 1752 and
  ended Toungoo royal government.
