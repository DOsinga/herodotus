description: >-
  The Khazar Khaganate was a Turkic state between the Black and Caspian seas, emerging
  around 650 after Western Turkic power fragmented. Its rulers controlled steppe
  peoples and routes connecting the Volga, Caucasus, and wider Eurasian trading
  networks. A sacred khagan shared authority with an active military ruler, commonly
  called the bek. The capital eventually moved to Atil on the lower Volga, whose exact
  location remains disputed. Khazar armies fought Arab advances through the Caucasus
  and maintained relations with Byzantium. Part of the ruling elite adopted Judaism,
  while subjects followed several religions; the extent of conversion is uncertain.
  Rus' campaigns destroyed major centres during the 960s, conventionally ending the
  central government in 969. Later Khazar communities do not establish state
  continuity.
