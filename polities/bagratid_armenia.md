description: >-
  The Bagratid Kingdom of Armenia emerged from Arab domination in the Armenian highlands, with Ashot I recognized as king in 885. The Bagratuni monarchy balanced relations with the Abbasid caliphate and Byzantium while drawing authority from powerful Armenian noble families. Its rulers gradually strengthened the kingdom, although rival dynasties and regional branches established kingdoms of their own. Ashot III made Ani the capital in 961. The city became a major commercial and religious center, famous for its churches, fortifications, and urban growth. Political divisions and Byzantine expansion weakened the kingdom during the eleventh century. Earlier agreements promising its territory to Byzantium did not immediately remove its government. Byzantine annexation in 1045 ended the Bagratid kingdom, though other Armenian courts survived.
