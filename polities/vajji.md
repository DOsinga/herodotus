description: >-
  The Vajjika League was a confederation of republican communities in northeastern India,
  centred on Vaishali in present Bihar. This record places its beginnings around 700 BCE
  and its conquest around 468 BCE, although early chronology remains uncertain. The
  Licchavis led the league, joined by groups including the Videhas and Nayikas. Government
  rested with aristocratic assemblies and councils rather than a single hereditary
  monarch; member communities retained their own institutions. The region was closely
  associated with early Buddhism and Jainism, whose traditions preserve accounts of its
  leaders and political life. Its wealth and position north of the Ganges brought it into
  conflict with Magadha. Ajatashatru's prolonged campaign broke the confederation and
  absorbed its territory, ending this independent alliance of neighbouring communities.
