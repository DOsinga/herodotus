description: >-
  The County of Aversa was the first Norman lordship in southern Italy, founded north of Naples in
  1030 when Duke Sergius IV of Naples granted the town of Aversa and its district to the Norman
  mercenary Rainulf Drengot. Rainulf had served Naples and its rivals in the wars between the
  Lombard princes and the Byzantines, and his new county gave the Normans a permanent base from
  which they soon expanded. Rainulf and his successors played Naples, Capua, Salerno and the
  empire against each other, recruiting more knights from Normandy. In 1058 Count Richard of
  Aversa conquered the Lombard principality of Capua and moved his court there, uniting Aversa
  with Capua under Norman princes.
