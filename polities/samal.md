description: >-
  Sam'al was an Iron Age kingdom centred on present-day Zincirli in southeastern
  Turkey, near the routes connecting northern Syria with Anatolia. Its separate royal
  government is represented here from approximately 920 to 680 BCE. Aramaean rulers
  established authority in an area with older Luwian traditions, creating a mixed
  cultural setting. A circular fortified city surrounded a raised citadel containing
  palaces and administrative buildings. Royal inscriptions, carved stone reliefs, and
  monuments preserve the names and ambitions of kings including Kilamuwa and Panamuwa.
  They describe dynastic struggles, prosperity, and relations with powerful
  neighbours. Sam'al increasingly relied on Assyrian support while maintaining local
  kings. Eventually Assyria replaced the monarchy with provincial administration. Both
  boundaries are estimates within the city's much longer settlement history.
