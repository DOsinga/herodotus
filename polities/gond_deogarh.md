description: >-
  The Gond Kingdom of Deogarh was a Gond state in central India, in present Madhya Pradesh and
  the Vidarbha region of Maharashtra. Jatba founded it around 1580 at the hill fort of
  Deogarh, after overthrowing the Gauli princes he had served. Akbar left it alone in return
  for tribute, and the Ain-i-Akbari credits Jatba with a large army and many elephants. A
  Mughal army besieged Nagpur in 1637, and in 1669 Aurangzeb placed the kingdom under direct
  imperial rule. Bakht Buland Shah restored it in 1686 after converting to Islam, founded the
  city of Nagpur and invited settlers to the plains. His son Chand Sultan moved the capital
  there. Succession quarrels after 1739 drew in Raghoji Bhonsle, who took power in 1743.
