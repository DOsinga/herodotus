description: >-
  The United Principalities joined Moldavia and Wallachia into the state that became
  Romania. In 1859, both principalities elected Alexandru Ioan Cuza as their ruling
  prince, using a shared monarch to advance union despite Ottoman overlordship and
  European diplomatic constraints. Common institutions followed in 1862, with Bucharest
  becoming the capital. Cuza pursued land, education and church reforms before
  opponents removed him in 1866. Carol of Hohenzollern then became prince under a new
  constitution establishing parliamentary institutions and hereditary monarchy.
  Romanian forces fought alongside Russia against the Ottoman Empire during the
  independence war of 1877. International recognition followed in 1878, with
  territorial exchanges including Northern Dobruja. In 1881, Carol assumed the title of
  king, and the principality became the Kingdom of Romania.
