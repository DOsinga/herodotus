description: >-
  The Frankish Kingdom of Orleans was Chlodomer's separate share of Clovis's realm,
  governed from 511 until 524. Centred on Orleans, it included important districts around
  Tours and Poitiers rather than matching any modern regional boundary. Chlodomer ruled as
  a Merovingian king, alongside three brothers who held their own courts and territories.
  Their common ancestry did not prevent competing dynastic interests, though they
  cooperated in campaigns against Burgundy. Chlodomer captured the Burgundian king
  Sigismund and had him and his sons killed. He then died fighting at Vezeronce in 524.
  His brothers divided his lands, and two of his young sons were murdered to remove rival
  claims. The surviving son, Clodoald, entered religious life, leaving no lasting separate
  royal government at Orleans.
