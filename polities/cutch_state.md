description: >-
  Cutch State, or the Kingdom of Kutch, was a kingdom in the Kutch region of western Gujarat, India,
  between the Gulf of Kutch and the salt marshes of the Rann. Its rulers belonged to the Jadeja
  branch of the Samma Rajputs from Sindh, and the kingdom dates its foundation to 1147. Its capital
  was at Lakhiarviro until Khengarji I founded Bhuj as the capital in 1549. Cut off from the
  mainland by the Rann, its rulers kept their own government under Gujarati and Mughal overlordship,
  while its sailors traded across the Arabian Sea. Wars with Sindh and quarrels at court weakened it
  in the eighteenth century. Treaties with the East India Company in 1816 and 1819 made it a
  protected princely state.
