description: >-
  Togo is a West African republic extending inland from the Gulf of Guinea between Ghana and
  Benin. It became independent from French administration in 1960, with Lomé as its capital
  and port. Its territory formed the eastern portion of German Togoland, divided after the
  First World War into British and French mandates. Independence brought Sylvanus Olympio to
  the presidency, but his assassination in 1963 began a period of military intervention.
  Gnassingbé Eyadéma took power in 1967 and governed for decades; his son Faure later
  succeeded him. Agriculture, phosphate production, and commerce through Lomé have shaped
  the economy. Constitutional changes have altered the presidency and parliamentary system,
  while opposition movements have challenged concentrated power. The republic continues
  despite these regime changes.
