description: >-
  The Nayaka Kingdom of Chitradurga was a principality in eastern Karnataka, ruled from the
  great hill fort of Chitradurga by a family of Bedar chiefs. Its rulers had served the
  Hoysala and Vijayanagara kings as local governors. After the Vijayanagara empire broke up,
  Obanna Nayaka ruled Chitradurga on his own from 1588, and his son Kasturi Rangappa enlarged
  the state against its neighbours and the Bijapur sultanate. The Nayakas were at times
  independent and at times vassals of Mysore, the Mughals or the Marathas, while governing
  their lands themselves. Bharamappa Nayaka, who ruled from 1689 to 1721, fought the Mughals
  and built temples and tanks. Haidar Ali of Mysore took the fort in 1779 after a long siege,
  ending the dynasty.
