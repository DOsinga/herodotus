description: >-
  The Kadamba Kingdom of Goa was ruled by a branch of the Kadamba dynasty over Goa and the
  southern Konkan coast from the tenth to the fourteenth century. Shashthadeva, a feudatory of
  the Western Chalukyas, took Chandrapura, modern Chandor, from the Shilaharas around 960 and
  later the port of Gopakapattana. Around 1054 Gopakapattana, now Goa Velha, became the
  capital, and it grew into a trading port linked to Gujarat, Bengal, Sri Lanka and East
  Africa. The kings served the Chalukyas and later the Yadavas as overlords but governed their
  own lands, used Kannada and Sanskrit, and built many Shiva temples. Khalji forces sacked
  Gopakapattana in 1312, and Muhammad bin Tughluq took Chandor in 1327. The dynasty ended
  around 1345.
