description: >-
  The Amago were the Sengoku daimyo house of Izumo province in western Honshu, ruling from the
  mountain fortress of Gassantoda from 1486 to 1566. Amago Tsunehisa was the deputy of the
  Kyōgoku governors of Izumo, who dismissed him in 1484 for refusing to collect the
  shogunate's taxes. In 1486 he retook Gassantoda by surprise and made himself master of the
  province, and over the next decades he extended his power across the San'in coast and into
  the Chūgoku mountains, with the Iwami silver mines in dispute. His grandson Amago Haruhisa
  was named military governor of eight provinces in 1552 and fought the Ōuchi and then the
  Mōri. Mōri Motonari besieged Gassantoda and took it in 1566.
