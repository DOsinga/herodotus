description: >-
  The Kingdom of Dublin was a Norse and later Norse Gaelic monarchy on Ireland's eastern
  coast, attested from 853 until the Norman capture of 1170. Its royal government developed
  after Viking settlement beside the River Liffey, and its territory usually included Dublin
  and a surrounding coastal hinterland. Rulers were sometimes jointly reigning kings, and
  some also held power at York. The port became a major centre for trade, including the sale
  of enslaved people, while its settlers gradually adopted Irish language and customs.
  Expulsion by Irish forces in 902 interrupted the kingdom until its restoration in 917.
  Tribute and intervention by Irish kings changed its political position without always
  ending its court. Norman conquest finally displaced that local royal government.
