description: >-
  Ulm was a city on the Danube in Swabia, at the point where the river becomes navigable, which
  grew from a royal palace into one of the leading free imperial cities of southern Germany. Its
  patrician and guild council governed the town and built up a territory north of the Danube that
  was larger than that of any imperial city except Nuremberg. Ulm grew rich on fustian, a cloth of
  linen and cotton, and on the river trade, and in 1377 it began the great minster church. It led
  the Swabian League of Cities in the fourteenth century and was a pillar of the later Swabian
  League. Ulm adopted the Reformation in 1530 and remained an imperial city until 1803, when it
  was given to Bavaria.
