description: >-
  The Gabalas Lordship of Rhodes was an independent Greek domain on the island of Rhodes and
  some nearby Aegean islands, formed when the Fourth Crusade broke up the Byzantine Empire in
  1204. Rhodes had already slipped out of imperial control by then, and its ruler is usually
  identified as Leo Gabalas, from an old Byzantine aristocratic family. Leo took the title of
  Caesar and minted his own copper coins. He may have recognised the Empire of Nicaea as
  overlord, but he acted on his own, and John III Vatatzes sent a fleet against him in 1233.
  The next year Leo made an alliance with Venice. His brother John, styled lord of Rhodes,
  followed him in the 1240s. The Genoese seized the island in 1248, and Nicaea recovered it.
