description: >-
  The Emirate of Sicily was the Muslim government of the island under the Kalbid emirs, who
  ruled from Palermo from 948 until the 1050s. The island had been conquered from Byzantium
  by the Aghlabids of Ifriqiya in a long war that began in 827, and passed to the Fatimids in
  909. After revolts against Fatimid governors, the caliph appointed al-Hasan al-Kalbi, whose
  family then held the office by inheritance and governed on its own account while still
  naming the Fatimid caliph in prayers. Palermo grew into one of the largest cities of the
  Mediterranean, and new crops, irrigation and trade made the island rich. Family quarrels
  and revolts broke the emirate up in the 1040s, and after the last Kalbid fell in 1053
  rival local rulers divided the island.
