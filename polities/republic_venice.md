description: >-
  The Republic of Venice developed from lagoon communities on Italy's northeastern coast,
  with 697 traditionally marking the beginning of its doges. It gradually loosened
  Byzantine ties and became a maritime republic governed by an elected doge
  and councils dominated by noble families. Trade, shipbuilding, and a fleet supported
  possessions around the Adriatic and eastern Mediterranean. Venice profited from the Fourth
  Crusade and the seizure of Constantinople in 1204, while competing with Genoa.
  It later expanded across the Italian mainland. Wars with the Ottoman Empire
  cost important overseas territories, although the city remained a major commercial and
  cultural centre. Napoleon's advance forced the republic's surrender in 1797, and its
  territories were divided between France and Austria, ending its long aristocratic government.
