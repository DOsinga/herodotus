description: >-
  El Mirador was a major Preclassic Maya city in northern Guatemala's Petén region, represented here from approximately 600 BCE to 150 CE. Its enormous ceremonial buildings and raised causeways show the resources its rulers could command, although their names and political arrangements remain poorly known. The La Danta and El Tigre complexes rose above a dense settlement connected with other centres across the Mirador Basin. Monumental sculpture and religious imagery place the city within an already sophisticated Maya world centuries before the better documented Classic kingdoms. Its great urban phase ended with declining construction and abandonment. Environmental damage associated with intensive building and farming has been proposed as one cause. Later occupation does not imply that this early kingdom survived.
