description: >-
  Ah Kin Chel was a Maya province of the northern Yucatán Peninsula, stretching from around
  Izamal to the north coast. According to Maya tradition it was founded in 1441 by Mo-Chel,
  a priest of Mayapan, when the league of Mayapan collapsed into a number of rival
  provinces. The Chel family supplied its halach uinik, or supreme lord, and other lineages
  governed its towns as batabs. Ah Kin Chel controlled valuable salt beds on the coast and
  the old pilgrimage centre of Izamal. It fought with neighbouring Sotuta and Ceh Pech over
  land and trade. Its lords received the Spanish expedition of Francisco de Montejo the
  Younger peacefully and submitted in the early 1540s, after which Izamal became a major
  Franciscan mission.
