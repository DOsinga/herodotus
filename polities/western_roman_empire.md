description: >-
  The Western Roman Empire was the western imperial government that followed the division of
  authority after Theodosius I died in 395. Its emperors governed Italy and, with diminishing
  control, provinces across western Europe and North Africa. The court moved from Milan to
  Ravenna in 402, while Rome remained a religious and political centre. Civil wars, powerful
  military commanders and the settlement of armed migrant groups weakened imperial authority.
  The loss of North Africa to the Vandals deprived the government of crucial revenue and
  supplies. Successive rulers struggled to recover territory or restrain their generals. In
  476 Odoacer deposed Romulus Augustulus and took control of Italy. Julius Nepos retained the
  imperial title in Dalmatia, but no longer governed the Italian centre.
