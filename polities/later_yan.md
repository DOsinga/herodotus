description: >-
  Later Yan was a monarchy in northern China founded by Murong Chui in 384, during the
  fragmented Sixteen Kingdoms period. Its Xianbei royal family sought to restore the
  earlier Yan realm after the weakening of Former Qin. From Zhongshan, Murong Chui
  recovered a large territory and defeated rival states, but expansion gave way to
  conflict with the rising Northern Wei. Military losses and succession struggles
  undermined the kingdom after his death. In 397, the court withdrew northeast to
  Longcheng, leaving much of its former territory behind. Wars with Goguryeo further
  reduced its position. Murong Xi's overthrow in 407 marks the endpoint used here: Gao Yun
  and Feng Ba continued the remaining state under the identity recorded separately as
  Northern Yan.
