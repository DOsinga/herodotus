description: >-
  Senegal is a West African republic on the Atlantic coast, independent since 1960, with its
  capital at Dakar. It first gained independence from France within the Mali Federation, then
  withdrew when that union broke apart later the same year. Léopold Sédar Senghor became its
  first president and shaped a state combining African socialism with close French ties. The
  constitution provides a president, prime minister, and elected National Assembly. Political
  competition broadened over time, and electoral transfers of power became a feature of public
  life. A confederation with neighbouring Gambia lasted from 1982 to 1989 without erasing
  Senegal's government. The Casamance separatist conflict has challenged its southern region.
  French remains the official language, while Wolof is widely spoken throughout the country.
