description: >-
  The Italian Social Republic was Benito Mussolini's final Fascist regime, established in German
  occupied northern and central Italy in 1943. Germany restored Mussolini after his removal by
  the monarchy and Italy's armistice with the Allies. Although it claimed Rome as its capital,
  ministries operated around Salò and other northern towns. The Republican Fascist Party
  controlled its government, but German military power severely limited its authority. The regime
  raised armed forces, fought Italian partisans, and collaborated in the persecution and
  deportation of Jews. Promised economic socialization had little practical effect during the
  war. Allied advances and the partisan uprising destroyed it in April 1945. Mussolini was
  captured and killed, and its remaining armed forces surrendered as the Kingdom of Italy
  returned.
