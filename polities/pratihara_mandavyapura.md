description: >-
  The Pratihara Kingdom of Mandavyapura was a regional monarchy in present Rajasthan,
  approximately from 550 to 900. Its rulers traced their line to Harichandra, whose sons were
  credited with capturing and fortifying Mandavyapura, now Mandore. A later ruler, Nagabhata,
  moved the court to Medantaka, identified with Merta, although Mandore retained importance. The
  family's history is reconstructed chiefly from ninth century inscriptions at Jodhpur and
  Ghantiyala, which record ancestry, victories, religious gifts, and public works. These texts
  praise individual kings rather than describing a complete system of administration. Connections
  with the larger imperial Pratihara dynasty remain uncertain, and this local court is treated
  separately. Kakkuka's inscription of 861 is a late secure reference; the kingdom's ending
  around 900 is an estimate.
