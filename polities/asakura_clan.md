description: >-
  The Asakura were the Sengoku daimyo house of Echizen province on the Sea of Japan coast,
  ruling from the valley fortress of Ichijōdani from 1471 to 1573. Asakura Toshikage was a
  deputy of the Shiba military governors who changed sides during the Ōnin War, received the
  governorship from the shogun in 1471 and drove out his former lords. His house code is one
  of the best known of the period. Under his successors Ichijōdani grew into a town of
  several thousand people, with courtiers and artists who fled war-torn Kyoto. The Asakura
  fought the Ikkō-ikki of neighbouring Kaga for decades. Asakura Yoshikage joined the
  alliance against Oda Nobunaga, who defeated him at Anegawa in 1570 and burned Ichijōdani in
  1573.
