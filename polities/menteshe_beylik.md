description: >-
  The Beylik of Menteshe was a Turkish principality in southwestern Anatolia, centred on Milas and the lands of ancient Caria.
  Established during the decline of Seljuk authority, it is represented here from 1260, with a later restoration between 1402 and
  1413. Hereditary beys governed a region whose harbours connected inland farming and forests with the Aegean. Menteshe became a notable
  naval power, combining trade with raiding and attacks on nearby islands. Venetian merchants, especially those based in Crete, bought grain,
  horses and alum. Its rulers also sponsored substantial stone mosques and other buildings at Milas and Balat. Ottoman expansion interrupted
  the dynasty's government, before Timur restored Ilyas Bey after Ankara. Renewed Ottoman control eventually ended the principality's separate political life.
