description: >-
  The Shazhou Guard was a Mongol tribal chiefdom at the oasis of Shazhou, the modern
  Dunhuang, at the western end of the Hexi Corridor. Its chiefs came from Mongol houses that
  had held titles under the Yuan, and after the Ming conquered the Hexi Corridor in 1372
  they stayed beyond the new frontier at Jiayu Pass. In 1404 the Ming made their chief
  commander of the Shazhou Guard, one of the Seven Garrisons west of Jiayu Pass, which held
  Ming titles but kept their own leaders and herds. The guard sent horses and tribute to the
  Ming court and shielded it from the Oirats. In 1445 the Oirat leader Esen forced its
  chiefs to accept his titles, and in 1446 most of the tribe fled into Ming territory.
