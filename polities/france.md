description: >-
  France's Fifth Republic began in 1958 after the Algerian crisis and the collapse of
  the Fourth Republic's parliamentary system. Governed from Paris, it combines a
  president with a prime minister responsible to parliament, comprising the National
  Assembly and Senate. Charles de Gaulle helped shape the constitution and became its
  president. Direct presidential elections were adopted in 1962, strengthening the
  office's mandate. Decolonization transformed France's overseas position, including
  Algerian independence that year, while metropolitan France remained involved in
  European integration. Periods of cohabitation placed presidents and parliamentary
  governments from different parties in office together. Constitutional changes later
  shortened presidential terms from seven years to five. The republic continues across
  changes of president and party government, retaining the framework established in
  1958.
