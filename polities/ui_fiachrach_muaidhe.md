name: "Kingdom of Ui Fiachrach Muaidhe"
description: >-
  Ui Fiachrach Muaidhe was a Gaelic kingdom centred on the Moy valley in
  northwestern Ireland, represented here from approximately 450 to 1603. Its
  early lineage traced descent from Fiachnae, brother of the high king Ailill
  Molt, though the founding date rests on genealogical traditions rather than a
  recorded establishment. Its territory extended across parts of modern Mayo and
  Sligo, including districts around Killala. Members of the dynasty sometimes
  became kings of Connacht, while local rulers maintained their own court and
  authority. The O'Dowd family later dominated the kingdom, with a seat at Carn
  Amhlaidh. Connacht overlordship did not remove their local government. This
  record ends with the defeat of the native governing order after the Nine
  Years' War in 1603.
