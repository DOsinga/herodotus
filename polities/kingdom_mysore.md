description: >-
  The Kingdom of Mysore became an independent power in southern India after Vijayanagara's
  defeat at Talikota in 1565. Its Wodeyar rulers expanded their territory from a small
  feudatory base, governing from Srirangapatna. During the eighteenth century real authority
  passed to military leader Hyder Ali and then his son Tipu Sultan, while the older royal
  house lost effective control. Their armies enlarged Mysore across parts of present
  Karnataka, Kerala and Tamil Nadu, and challenged the East India Company in successive wars.
  French connections, administrative reforms and military rockets distinguished this period.
  Tipu died defending Srirangapatna in 1799. The British restored a Wodeyar ruler under a
  subsidiary alliance, ending Mysore's independent war and foreign policy, although the
  princely state continued until 1950.
