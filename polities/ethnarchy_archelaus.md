description: >-
  The Ethnarchy of Archelaus governed Judea, Samaria and Idumea from 4 BCE to 6 CE, with
  Jerusalem as its principal centre. It was one of the territories created when Augustus
  divided Herod the Great's kingdom among his surviving sons. Archelaus received the title
  ethnarch rather than king, while his brothers ruled other districts separately. His
  authority rested on Roman approval but operated through a local dynastic government. The
  reign began amid unrest, including a violent suppression of crowds at the Jerusalem Temple
  described by Josephus. Further complaints accused Archelaus of misrule and brought his
  position before Augustus. The emperor eventually deposed him and banished him to Gaul.
  Roman prefects then governed his former lands as the province of Judaea instead.
