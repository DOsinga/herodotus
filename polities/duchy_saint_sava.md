description: >-
  The Duchy of Saint Sava was the state of the Kosača family in Hum, the land that took
  its later name of Herzegovina from their title, from 1435 until the Ottomans took its
  last fortress, Herceg Novi, in 1482. Stjepan Vukčić Kosača inherited his uncle Sandalj's
  lands in 1435 and governed them independently of the Bosnian kings, with whom he fought,
  and made himself a vassal of Alfonso V of Aragon in 1444. In 1448 he took the title of
  herceg, or duke, of Saint Sava. From Blagaj and Herceg Novi he traded with Ragusa and
  fought Venice, Ragusa and his own son. The Ottomans took most of his lands in the 1460s,
  and his son Vlatko lost the rest.
