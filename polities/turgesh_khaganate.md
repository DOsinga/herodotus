description: >-
  The Türgesh Khaganate was a Turkic state in Central Asia, established by Üch Elig in
  699 and conquered by the Karluks in 766. Its rulers drew authority from a tribal
  confederation that had belonged to the Western Turkic world. Suyab and Taraz served as
  royal centers amid the towns and pastures of the Seven Rivers region. Under Suluk,
  Türgesh armies became opponents of Umayyad expansion into Transoxiana,
  working with Sogdian rulers and rebels against Arab forces. They also negotiated and
  fought with Tang China, Tibet, and neighboring Turkic powers. Suluk's murder triggered
  struggles between rival Black and Yellow factions. These weakened the khaganate, but
  did not immediately end it; surviving rulers continued until the Karluks took over its
  regional centers.
