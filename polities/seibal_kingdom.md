description: >-
  Seibal was a Maya urban center above the Pasión River in northern Guatemala. Its early polity is
  represented from about 400 BCE to 200 CE, followed by a long decline before a renewed court emerged
  around 600. Archaeology reveals plazas, temples, and changing settlement patterns; later
  inscriptions identify individual kings and political alliances. Dos Pilas defeated Seibal in 735,
  but local rulers continued under its overlordship until that power collapsed. The city revived in
  the ninth century with Watul Chatel, whose monuments displayed connections to other surviving Maya
  courts and varied artistic influences. Its river position linked it to regional exchange routes.
  This revival did not last: population and institutions declined again, and the site was finally
  abandoned around 950.
