description: >-
  The Emirate of Sfax was a city principality on the eastern coast of Tunisia, centred on Sfax
  and recorded here from 1058 to 1099. When the Zirids lost control of Ifriqiya to the Banu
  Hilal, their governor of Sfax, Mansur al-Barghawati, declared himself independent. After his
  murder his cousin Hammu ibn Malil al-Barghawati took power and ruled the city for about
  forty years. Sfax was a rich centre of olive oil production, and Hammu at times recognised
  the Hammadid ruler al-Nasir as his overlord. He fought the Zirids of Mahdia and besieged
  their capital in 1063. The Zirid emir Tamim finally took Sfax in 1099, and Hammu ended his
  days as a refugee in Gabès.
