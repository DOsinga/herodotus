description: >-
  The Middle jüz was the central of the three Kazakh hordes, occupying the steppes of central and
  northeastern Kazakhstan between the Irtysh, the Ishim and the Syr Darya. After Tauke Khan's
  death around 1718 it followed its own khans of Genghisid descent, governing pastoral clans
  through judges and war leaders. The Dzungar invasions of the 1720s brought famine and flight,
  but Kazakh forces fought back. Ablai Khan, its most famous ruler, balanced between Russia and
  Qing China after the Dzungar state fell in 1758 and was acknowledged across much of the steppe
  before his death in 1781. Russian forts and trade spread along the Irtysh. Speransky's reforms
  abolished the khan's office in 1822 and placed the jüz under Russian district administration.
