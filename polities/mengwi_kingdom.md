description: >-
  Mengwi was a Balinese kingdom in the southwest of the island, between Badung and Tabanan. It was
  founded in the early eighteenth century by I Gusti Agung Made Agung, a son of the Gelgel usurper
  Gusti Agung Maruti, whose family had fled to Jimbaran after his defeat. The new court built the
  royal temple of Taman Ayun and soon became one of the strongest powers in Bali, controlling
  Buleleng from 1732 to 1752 and claiming influence in Blambangan on Java. Its rulers fought
  repeatedly with Badung and the Bugis settlers of the south coast, and Mengwi briefly came under
  Badung in 1829. In 1891 Badung and its allies defeated Mengwi and divided its lands with Tabanan,
  ending the kingdom.
