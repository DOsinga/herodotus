description: >-
  The Kingdom of Jerusalem was a Latin Christian monarchy established after the First Crusade
  captured Jerusalem in 1099. Its rulers governed through nobles, church institutions and courts,
  while military orders and Italian trading communities gained power. The population included
  Muslims, eastern Christians and other communities alongside Latin settlers. Royal authority
  depended on cooperation with barons and on support arriving from Europe. Saladin's victory at
  Hattin in 1187 led to the loss of Jerusalem and much of the kingdom. Coastal recovery during
  the Third Crusade allowed government to continue from Acre, despite intermittent recovery of
  Jerusalem itself. Rival claims and internal conflicts weakened the remaining state. Mamluk
  conquest of Acre and the last coastal strongholds in 1291 ended its territorial existence.
