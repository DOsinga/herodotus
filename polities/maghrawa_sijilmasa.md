description: >-
  The Maghrawa Emirate of Sijilmasa was ruled by the Banu Khazrun, a family of the Zenata
  Berber Maghrawa, in the oasis city of Sijilmasa on the edge of the Sahara in southeastern
  Morocco. In 976 or 977 their chief Khazrun ibn Fulful took the city from the Midrarid
  dynasty and sent the last Midrarid ruler's head to Córdoba. The Umayyad caliphs then
  recognised him and his descendants as their governors, but the family governed the city
  itself. Sijilmasa was the northern end of the caravan routes to the gold of West Africa, and
  its trade gave the emirs their wealth. A Zirid expedition drove them out for a few years
  after 979. The Almoravids captured Sijilmasa in 1054 or 1055, ending the emirate.
