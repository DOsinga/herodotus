description: >-
  Cuan was the regime of the Cuan family in central and eastern Yunnan, China, from 339
  until Nanzhao destroyed it in 748. The family had served the Shu Han and Jin governors,
  and when Ningzhou passed from Cheng-Han back to Jin in 339, Cuan Chen took control of the
  Lake Dian region as a hereditary ruler. Jin and the southern dynasties named the Cuan
  governors but sent no officials of their own. The core, later called Western Cuan, was run
  from Weixian near Qujing, while the chiefs of Eastern Cuan in northeastern Yunnan and
  western Guizhou paid tribute and governed themselves. Sui armies broke Cuan power in 597
  and 602 without setting up direct rule, and Tang made Cuan chiefs hereditary prefects.
  Nanzhao killed the last leaders.
