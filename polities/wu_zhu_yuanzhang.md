description: >-
  Zhu Yuanzhang's Wu was the rebel state on the lower Yangtze that grew into the Ming
  dynasty, lasting from 1355 to 1368. Zhu, a former monk and soldier of the Red Turban
  leader Guo Zixing, took command of Guo's army at Hezhou in 1355 and captured Nanjing the
  following year, making it his capital. He formally served the Red Turban Song of Han
  Lin'er, but governed his province on his own, recruited Confucian advisers and built a
  disciplined army and civil administration. He took the title Duke of Wu in 1361 and King
  of Wu in 1364. Victory over Chen Youliang at Lake Poyang in 1363 made him the strongest
  rebel, and he conquered Zhang Shicheng and Fang Guozhen in 1367. In January 1368 he
  proclaimed the Ming dynasty.
