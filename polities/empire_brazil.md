description: >-
  The Empire of Brazil began with independence from Portugal in 1822 and ended with a military
  coup in 1889. Pedro I and his son Pedro II ruled from Rio de Janeiro under a constitution
  combining monarchy, elected representation, and substantial imperial powers. The state
  preserved most of Portuguese America's territory despite provincial revolts and the loss of
  Cisplatina, which became Uruguay. Coffee exports, immigration, railways, and urban growth
  transformed its economy, while slavery remained central to plantations and social inequality.
  Brazil fought major regional wars, most notably against Paraguay. Slavery was abolished in
  1888, after a long struggle. Conflicts with military officers, republican activists, and
  sections of the elite weakened the monarchy; Pedro II's removal brought the First Brazilian
  Republic.
