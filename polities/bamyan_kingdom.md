description: >-
  The Kingdom of Bamyan was a Buddhist monarchy in the high Bamyan valley of central
  Afghanistan, on the route between Bactria and the Kabul region. After the Sasanians and
  Turks destroyed the Hephthalite empire around 560, a local dynasty of Hephthalite or
  Kushan descent governed the valley from Bamyan. Its kings, later known to Muslim writers by
  the title Shir, patronised the cave monasteries and the two colossal Buddhas carved into the
  valley's cliff. Xuanzang was received by the king about 630, and in 727 the Korean monk
  Hyecho described Bamyan as independent and strong although Arab armies operated to its north
  and south. A Shir accepted Islam under the Abbasids. Yaqub ibn al-Layth's Saffarid conquest
  ended the kingdom in 870.
