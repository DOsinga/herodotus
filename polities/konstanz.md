description: >-
  Konstanz was a city on the Rhine where it leaves Lake Constance, the seat of the largest diocese
  in Germany. The bishops held the town's lordship until the citizens won the status of a free
  imperial city in the thirteenth century, and the council governed it from then on while the
  bishops moved to Meersburg across the lake. Konstanz grew rich on linen and on the trade between
  Swabia and Italy. It hosted the Council of Constance from 1414 to 1418, which ended the Western
  Schism and burned Jan Hus. The city took up the Reformation and joined the Schmalkaldic League.
  After the league's defeat it refused the Augsburg Interim, and in 1548 Charles V placed it under
  the imperial ban and handed it to Austria.
