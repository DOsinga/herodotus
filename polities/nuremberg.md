description: >-
  Nuremberg was an independently governed imperial city in Franconia, represented from 1250 until its
  annexation by Bavaria in 1806. Frederick II's charter of 1219 had granted important privileges,
  and the city gradually reduced the authority of its neighbouring burgraves. A council dominated
  by wealthy patrician families controlled government, excluding artisans from political power
  after an unsuccessful uprising. Trade routes, metalworking and merchant networks made Nuremberg
  one of Germany's richest medieval urban centres. Its territory extended beyond the walls
  through purchases and military settlements. Imperial diets met there, and the city guarded the
  imperial regalia. Renaissance artists and printers flourished during its commercial peak. War,
  changing trade patterns and debt weakened its position before Napoleonic restructuring brought
  the end of independence.
