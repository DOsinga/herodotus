description: >-
  The Livonian Order was the self-governing branch of the Teutonic Order in Livonia, formed
  in 1237 from the survivors of the Brothers of the Sword. Its master, chosen by the Livonian
  knights and confirmed by the grand master in Prussia, ruled the order's lands in present
  Latvia and Estonia from Riga and from castles such as Wenden, Fellin and Segewold. The
  order fought the Lithuanians, Semigallians and Curonians for decades, and lost a famous
  battle against Novgorod on the ice of Lake Peipus in 1242. It bought northern Estonia from
  Denmark in 1346. It quarrelled for most of its history with the archbishops and
  citizens of Riga, and took the city by force in 1330. In 1435 the order and the Livonian
  bishops bound themselves together in the Livonian Confederation.
