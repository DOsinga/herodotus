description: >-
  The Hazaraspids were a Kurdish dynasty that governed Greater Luristan, the mountainous
  Zagros country of southwestern Iran, from the mid-twelfth century until 1424. Abu Tahir
  ibn Muhammad, sent by the Salghurid atabegs of Fars, made himself independent around Idaj,
  the modern Izeh, which remained the dynastic seat. His successors, styled atabegs,
  controlled important routes between the Iranian plateau and Khuzestan. They submitted to
  the Mongols and served the Ilkhans as vassals while keeping their own government, and some
  of them fought both for and against their overlords. After the Ilkhanate fell they dealt
  with the Muzaffarids and then with Timur, who accepted them as tributaries. Internal
  disputes weakened the line, and in 1424 Shahrukh removed the last atabeg and annexed
  Luristan.
