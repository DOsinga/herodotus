description: >-
  The Shimazu were the Sengoku daimyo house of southern Kyushu, ruling Satsuma and its
  neighbours from Kagoshima from the Ōnin War in 1467 until they submitted to Toyotomi
  Hideyoshi in 1587. Military governors of Satsuma since the Kamakura period, they spent the
  early sixteenth century in wars between branches of the family. Shimazu Takahisa reunited
  the house and moved its seat to Kagoshima, and his reign saw the arrival of firearms on
  Tanegashima in 1543 and of Francis Xavier in 1549. His son Shimazu Yoshihisa conquered
  Ōsumi and Hyūga, crushed the Ōtomo at Mimikawa in 1578 and the Ryūzōji in 1584, and nearly
  conquered all of Kyushu. Hideyoshi's great invasion of 1587 forced him to submit.
