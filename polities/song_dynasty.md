description: >-
  The Song dynasty ruled a Chinese imperial state from 960 to 1279, first from Kaifeng
  and later from Lin'an, present-day Hangzhou. Its emperors strengthened civilian administration and expanded recruitment through
  examinations, giving educated officials a prominent role in government. Agricultural growth, busy cities, printing, overseas
  commerce, and paper money supported a active economy. Scholars and artists developed influential forms of
  painting, literature, and Confucian thought. The state nevertheless faced powerful northern neighbours, including Liao, Western
  Xia, and Jin. Jurchen armies captured Kaifeng in 1127, forcing the court south while it
  retained prosperous territories beyond the Yangtze. Mongol campaigns eventually overwhelmed this Southern Song government. The
  final resistance ended at the naval battle of Yamen in 1279, completing the Yuan conquest.
