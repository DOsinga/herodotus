description: >-
  Tundama was a Muisca confederation in the highlands of present Boyacá, Colombia, centred on the
  town that became Duitama. Its ruler, known by the same title, governed a group of chiefdoms in
  the valleys north of Sugamuxi and maintained independence from the zaque of Hunza. Muisca
  farmers there grew maize and potatoes and traded cloth and salt. When Spanish forces under
  Gonzalo Jiménez de Quesada entered the northern highlands in 1537, the cacique of Tundama
  organised resistance and fought them in a series of encounters around the lagoons and marshes of
  his territory. He held out longer than the other Muisca rulers, until Spanish captains defeated
  his forces in 1539 and the confederation came under Spanish control.
