description: >-
  The Ukrainian People's Republic emerged around Kyiv during the Russian Revolution of
  1917, seeking Ukrainian autonomy before declaring independence in January 1918. Its
  Central Rada combined national self government with social reform and rights for
  minorities. War with Bolshevik forces drove its leaders to seek assistance from the
  Central Powers, whose intervention helped restore their government but also undermined
  it. Pavlo Skoropadsky's Hetmanate replaced the republic during 1918. A Directorate
  restored republican rule that year, and the renewed government fought amid overlapping
  Soviet, White Russian, and Polish campaigns. Symon Petliura allied with Poland against
  Soviet forces. Defeat forced the army and government abroad in November 1920. An exile
  government continued to claim the republic's legitimacy long after territorial control
  ended.
