description: >-
  Karasahr, known in Chinese sources as Yanqi, was an oasis kingdom near Lake Bosten in the
  Tarim Basin. Its beginnings are estimated around 200 BCE from accounts of the region.
  Kings governed a settled farming community linked to the routes between China and Central
  Asia, with mountains and narrow passes shaping its defences. Ancient inhabitants spoke a
  Tocharian language, and Buddhism flourished alongside other religious traditions. Chinese
  histories describe agriculture, walled towns, wine, and music, as well as conflicts with
  Han officials. Tang forces conquered the kingdom in 644 after its alignment with the
  Turks. A restored local government briefly followed, but another Tang defeat in 648 ended
  it. Karasahr subsequently became an imperial garrison rather than an independent kingdom.
