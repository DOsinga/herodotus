description: >-
  The Principality of Iberia was an aristocratic government in the Georgian region of Kartli, led by presiding princes rather than crowned kings. It began around 588, after a period of direct Persian administration, and survived amid rivalry between Persia, Byzantium, and later the Arab caliphates. Princes held Byzantine titles and negotiated with outside powers, but their authority over hereditary nobles was limited. Arab control of Tbilisi during the eighth century pushed the princely government into a reduced territory. Suppression around 786 interrupted its existence. Ashot restored a court at Artanuji in 813, strengthening the Bagrationi family and linking rule with monastic life and Georgian Christian culture. Adarnase's assumption of kingship in 888 ended the principate and began a renewed monarchy.
