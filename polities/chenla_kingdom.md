name: "Chenla Kingdom"
wikipedia: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chenla
description: >-
  Chenla names an early Khmer political centre in mainland Southeast Asia,
  conventionally traced from around 550 to an estimated fragmentation by 713. Its
  rulers expanded across parts of present Cambodia and neighbouring regions, displacing
  Funan's authority. Isanapura, now represented by the temples of Sambor Prei Kuk,
  became a seventh century capital under Isanavarman. Sanskrit and Old Khmer
  inscriptions record kings, religious foundations, land, and service obligations.
  Hindu worship, especially devotion to Shiva, stood beside Buddhist traditions. Royal
  authority depended on ties with local elites rather than clear modern borders.
  Historians dispute whether Chinese accounts describe one unified kingdom or several
  linked courts. Queen Jayadevi's last dated inscription in 713 provides a cautious
  endpoint here; other courts continued governing afterwards independently.
