name: "First Turkic Khaganate"
description: >-
  The First Turkic Khaganate was a steppe empire founded in 552 when Bumin defeated the
  Rouran. Governed by the Ashina ruling clan, it expanded from Mongolia across Central Asia,
  with a mobile royal court associated with Otuken. Bumin's brother Istami directed western
  campaigns under the title yabgu, while the senior khagans ruled the eastern heartland.
  Mounted armies, alliances, and control of trade routes helped the Turks defeat the
  Hephthalites and negotiate with Persia, Byzantium, and Chinese states. Sogdian merchants
  played an important role in commerce and diplomacy. Succession disputes and Chinese
  intervention weakened the empire after 581. Its final division in 603 produced separate
  Eastern and Western Turkic khaganates, preserving Turkic power while ending the united
  empire's shared royal government.
