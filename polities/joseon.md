name: Joseon
description: >-
  Joseon governed the Korean peninsula from 1392 to 1897 under kings of the Yi family. Founded
  by the general Yi Seong-gye after overthrowing Goryeo, it made Hanseong, present Seoul, its
  capital. The monarchy worked through a civil bureaucracy shaped by Confucian learning,
  examinations, and scholar officials. King Sejong's reign produced the Korean alphabet,
  alongside advances in science and administration. Japanese invasions in the 1590s devastated
  the country, and later Manchu invasions brought tributary relations with Qing China without
  ending Korean government. Farming, social hierarchy, and disputes among elite factions
  marked much of its history. In the nineteenth century, foreign pressure and domestic reform
  unsettled the court. Gojong proclaimed the Korean Empire in 1897, replacing Joseon's royal
  status with imperial status.
