description: >-
  Saudi Arabia grew from Ibn Saud's capture of Riyadh in 1902 into a kingdom covering most of
  the Arabian Peninsula. That victory restored a Saudi foothold after the fall of an earlier
  family state. Further campaigns secured Najd, the eastern coast, and the Hejaz, including
  Mecca and Medina. The united kingdom took its present name in 1932. It is a hereditary
  monarchy governed by the House of Saud, with Islamic law and royal authority central to its
  institutions. Oil discovered in the twentieth century transformed finances, cities, and
  international influence. Pilgrimage to Islam's holiest cities gives the country a
  distinctive religious role. Its government has continued through successive kings, while
  managing economic change and close but sometimes contested foreign partnerships.
