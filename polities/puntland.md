description: >-
  Puntland is a self governing region of northeastern Somalia, established in 1998 after
  the collapse of national government. A conference of local leaders, elders and other
  representatives created an administration with its capital at Garowe. It has an
  executive president, a parliament and security forces, drawing on both constitutional
  institutions and clan based political arrangements. Unlike Somaliland, Puntland has not
  sought separate international independence. It joined Somalia's federal system in 2012
  while continuing to run its own affairs. Bosaso's port and livestock trade are important
  to its economy, alongside remittances from Somalis abroad. Disputes with neighbouring
  administrations and the federal government have repeatedly tested its position. Its
  history illustrates regional state building within a fragmented but continuing Somali
  national framework.
