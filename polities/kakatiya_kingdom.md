description: >-
  The Kakatiya Kingdom governed much of the eastern Deccan in India from 1163 to 1323, with its
  capital at Orugallu, now Warangal. Formerly subordinate to dynasties, Rudradeva established
  independent royal power as Western Chalukya authority weakened. Ganapati Deva expanded the
  kingdom into the Telugu-speaking coastal lowlands, linking them with the inland plateau.
  Rudrama Devi ruled as queen and defended the realm against neighbouring rivals. Kings worked
  through military chiefs, while irrigation tanks supported cultivation and temples expressed
  royal and local patronage. The kingdom helped strengthen shared Telugu cultural life across
  distinct regions. Delhi's armies forced tribute after the siege of 1310, but native government
  continued. Ulugh Khan's conquest of Warangal in 1323 ended Kakatiya rule and brought direct
  sultanate control.
