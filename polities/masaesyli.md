description: >-
  The Masaesyli were a Berber kingdom in western Numidia, largely within modern Algeria,
  whose known ruler was Syphax. This record represents his kingdom from around 220 to 202
  BCE, with Siga as a principal royal centre. The rulers commanded tribal forces and issued
  coins showing royal authority. During the Second Punic War, Syphax sought advantage
  between Rome and Carthage while competing with the eastern Numidian Massylii. He initially
  favoured Rome but later allied with Carthage, marrying Sophonisba, daughter of the
  Carthaginian commander Hasdrubal Gisco. His rival Masinissa moved in the opposite
  direction and supported Rome. Defeat and Syphax's capture destroyed his position.
  Masinissa acquired the kingdom and united Numidia, although Masaesylian names and claims
  outlasted that decisive transfer of power.
