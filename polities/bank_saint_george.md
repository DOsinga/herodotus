description: >-
  The Bank of Saint George was the Genoese public bank that governed Corsica from 1453
  until the Republic of Genoa took the island back in 1562. The bank had been founded in
  1407 by the republic's creditors to manage its debt, and Genoa handed it territories in
  payment, Corsica among them. It governed the island through its own governors at Bastia,
  collected taxes, built coastal towers and fortresses, founded Ajaccio in 1492, and
  fought the Corsican lords who resisted it. Its government ran on even while Genoa itself
  was under Milanese or French rule. In 1553 French troops and Sampiero Corso's Corsicans
  took most of the island; the Peace of Cateau-Cambrésis returned it, and the bank gave it
  up to the republic.
