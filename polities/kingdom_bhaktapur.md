description: >-
  The Kingdom of Bhaktapur, also known as Bhadgaon, was one of the Malla kingdoms of the Kathmandu
  Valley in Nepal. Bhaktapur had been the main royal seat of the united Malla kingdom, and when
  Yaksha Malla's sons divided the valley after his death in 1482, the eldest kept it as his own
  kingdom. It soon absorbed the small kingdom of Banepa to the east and controlled the trade routes
  towards Tibet through the eastern hills. Its kings built the palace, temples and squares that
  still mark the city, among them the five storeyed Nyatapola temple of Bhupatindra Malla. Rivalry
  with Kantipur and Lalitpur weakened all three. Bhaktapur was the last of the valley kingdoms to
  fall, when Prithvi Narayan Shah's army took it in 1769.
