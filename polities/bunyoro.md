description: >-
  Bunyoro, or Bunyoro-Kitara, was a kingdom in western Uganda between Lake Albert and the
  Nile. It was founded by the Babito dynasty, traditionally under Rukidi, around 1500, the
  estimate used here. For several centuries it was the strongest state in the region, raiding
  and taking tribute from its neighbours, but it lost provinces such as Buddu to Buganda and
  saw Toro break away in the 1830s. The omukama ruled through appointed chiefs and a royal
  court, and the kingdom controlled much of the regional salt and iron trade. Omukama Kabalega
  rebuilt the army with firearms in the 1870s and 1880s and fought off Egyptian advances.
  British and Ganda forces invaded in 1893 and 1894 and occupied the kingdom, although
  Kabalega fought on until his capture in 1899.
