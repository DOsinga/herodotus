name: "Later Han"
description: >-
  Later Han was a short lived imperial state in northern China from 947 to 951, the
  fourth regime of the Five Dynasties sequence. Liu Zhiyuan, a military governor based at Taiyuan, established
  it during the collapse of the Khitan Liao occupation that had destroyed Later Jin. He moved
  the central court to Kaifeng and governed much of the northern Chinese plain, while separate kingdoms
  remained in the south. His death brought his young son Liu Chengyou to power. Conflict between
  the emperor and senior commanders destabilized the court, and Guo Wei's rebellion overthrew the regime in
  951. Guo founded Later Zhou at Kaifeng. Liu Zhiyuan's brother established Northern Han in Shanxi, preserving
  a dynastic remnant rather than the original government.
