description: >-
  Chiang Hung, also called Moeng Lü or Sipsong Panna, was a Tai Lü kingdom on the upper
  Mekong in what is now Xishuangbanna in southern Yunnan, with its capital at modern Jinghong.
  Tradition dates its founding to 1180, when Phaya Chueang established Tai Lü rule over the
  Akha and other peoples. In the thirteenth century its kings received tribute from Kengtung,
  Chiang Saen and other Tai states. Mongol armies attacked it in 1290, and afterwards it paid
  tribute to the Yuan, Ming and Qing, which recognised its kings as hereditary chiefs of
  Cheli, and at times to Lan Na and Burma, which divided it into twelve districts. Its own
  kings kept governing through succession wars and invasions until Communist rule began in
  1950.
