description: >-
  The North German Confederation united northern German states under Prussian leadership from 1867 to 1871. Created after Prussia's victory over
  Austria, it was a federal state rather than merely an alliance. The Prussian king held its presidency and commanded its
  forces, while Otto von Bismarck served as chancellor. A federal council represented member governments, and the Reichstag was elected by
  universal male suffrage. Authority covered armies and foreign relations, leaving many domestic responsibilities with the states. Legislation established common institutions
  and rules, including postal services, passports, weights and religious equality. Southern German states initially remained outside, except for Hesse's northern
  province. Their participation in the war against France led to accession treaties and the Confederation's transformation into the German Empire.
