name: "Kingdom of Vaspurakan"
description: >-
  Vaspurakan was an Armenian kingdom around Lake Van, in what is now eastern Turkey and
  northwestern Iran, from 908 to 1021. Its Artsruni rulers rose from an older princely
  family as Arab power weakened. Gagik received royal recognition from the Sajid ruler
  Yusuf, initially opposing the Bagratid kings before joining them against Arab forces.
  The court moved among Van, Vostan, and the island of Aghtamar rather than using one
  fixed capital. Gagik's church building and influence over the Armenian church
  strengthened his standing. Family divisions later shared territory while preserving
  the senior king's title. Facing pressure from Byzantium and Turkish raids, Senekerim
  surrendered the kingdom to the Byzantine emperor in exchange for estates around
  Sebasteia, ending independent Artsruni royal government.
