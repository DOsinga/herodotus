description: >-
  The Kingdom of Wak Piit was a Maya state centered at El Palmar in present day
  Campeche, Mexico. Its royal history is recorded here from approximately 150 to 900
  CE, distinct from the settlement's earlier occupation. Inscriptions and painted
  vessels reveal rulers supported by priests, scribes and diplomatic officials. The
  city grew into a large network of ceremonial, residential and agricultural
  complexes, including temples, palaces and ball courts. Officials called lakam
  carried out missions between royal courts. A hieroglyphic stairway records Ajpach'
  Waal's journey to Copán in 726, connected with an alliance involving Calakmul.
  These relationships did not erase Wak Piit's own court. Monumental development
  continued into the ninth century before abandonment around 900, whose political
  circumstances remain incompletely understood.
