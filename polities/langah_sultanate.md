description: >-
  The Langah Sultanate was a Muslim kingdom of the southern Punjab, in present day Pakistan, with
  its capital at Multan. It arose in 1445, when the people of Multan, left without protection as the
  Delhi Sultanate weakened, accepted a local leader, and soon afterwards Rai Sahra of the Langah
  tribe took the throne as Sultan Qutbuddin. His successors, notably Husayn Langah, governed the
  lower Doab between the rivers of the Punjab, fought off attacks from Delhi and the Lodis, and held
  the trade route between Sindh and the north. Multan flourished as a centre of learning and Sufi
  shrines. Shah Husayn Arghun of Sindh besieged and took Multan in 1528, ending the sultanate, and
  the Mughals took the city from the Arghuns soon afterwards.
