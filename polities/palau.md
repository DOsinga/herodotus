description: >-
  Palau is an island republic in the western Pacific, independent since 1994 after American
  administration within the United Nations Trust Territory of the Pacific Islands. Its scattered
  islands had previously come under Spanish, German and Japanese colonial rule. Palau chose not
  to join the Federated States of Micronesia and negotiated its own Compact of Free Association
  with the United States. That agreement provides American defence and assistance while Palau
  conducts its own foreign relations. An elected president heads the government, alongside a
  national congress and sixteen states. Traditional chiefs retain recognised roles in society.
  The national capital moved from Koror to Ngerulmud in 2006. Tourism, marine conservation and
  the protection of reefs are important parts of the country's public life.
