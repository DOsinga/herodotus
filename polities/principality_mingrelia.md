description: >-
  The Principality of Mingrelia was a Georgian principality in the lowlands of western
  Georgia, between the Black Sea, the Rioni river and the Caucasus, ruled by the Dadiani
  family from Zugdidi. The Dadiani had been hereditary dukes under the Georgian kings and
  then the kings of Imereti, and from about 1557 they governed in their own right, paying
  only nominal homage to Imereti. Levan II Dadiani made Mingrelia the strongest power in
  western Georgia in the seventeenth century, defeating Imereti and Guria and dealing
  directly with the Ottomans and Persia. Trade in silk and slaves through the Black Sea
  ports supported the court. In 1803 Grigol Dadiani placed the principality under Russian
  protection, and Russia abolished it in 1867.
