description: >-
  The Ligurian Republic existed in northwestern Italy from 1797 to 1805, centred on Genoa and
  largely replacing the Genoese republic. Napoleon's intervention brought revolutionary
  institutions to a territory previously governed by an aristocratic elite. Its first
  constitution created a directory and councils, adopting the language of liberty and equality
  associated with France's sister republics. Political conflict and war repeatedly disrupted the
  new government. Genoa endured a siege in 1800 during the struggle between French and Austrian
  forces. A revised constitution in 1802 restored the title of doge, held by Girolamo Luigi
  Durazzo, alongside a senate, though French influence remained strong. Napoleon annexed the
  republic to the French Empire in 1805, dividing its lands into departments and ending its
  separate institutions.
