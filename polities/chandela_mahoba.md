description: >-
  After the Chandela kingdom of Jejakabhukti fell to the Delhi Sultanate, minor branches of
  the Chandela family kept ruling parts of Bundelkhand, at Mahoba and at the great fort of
  Kalinjar. When Tughluq power collapsed at the end of the fourteenth century, these rajas
  governed the country around their old capitals while the sultans of Kalpi, Malwa, Jaunpur
  and Delhi fought over the region. Little is recorded of their reigns. Rani Durgavati, born
  in 1524 to the Chandela raja of this line, married into the Gond royal house of Garha and
  later led its resistance to Akbar. In 1545 Sher Shah Suri besieged Kalinjar and the
  Chandela raja was killed, although Sher Shah himself died from wounds in the siege.
