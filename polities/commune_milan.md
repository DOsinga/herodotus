description: >-
  The Commune of Milan was a self governing city and surrounding territory in northern
  Italy, beginning around 1097 with its first recorded consuls. Urban leaders challenged
  older imperial and episcopal authority while competing with neighbouring Lombard cities.
  Milan's wars with Lodi, Como and Pavia drew Emperor Frederick Barbarossa into the
  struggle. He destroyed the city in 1162, interrupting its separate government. The Lombard
  League helped rebuild Milan in 1167, and communal rule resumed. Victory over imperial
  forces and the Peace of Constance in 1183 secured local autonomy. Consuls, councils and
  later a podestà directed civic affairs, amid tensions between noble families and popular
  groups. In 1259 government passed toward personal lordship, closing the communal phase
  represented by these two periods.
