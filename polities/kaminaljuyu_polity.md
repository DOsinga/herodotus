name: "Kaminaljuyu"
description: >-
  Kaminaljuyu was a major Maya centre in the Valley of Guatemala, beneath much of Guatemala City.
  This record covers its royal governing centre from approximately 400 BCE to 900 CE, rather than
  the settlement's entire occupation. Monumental platforms, carved thrones, stelae, and rich
  burials show the rise of powerful rulers during the Late Preclassic period. Water channels
  supported cultivation, while nearby obsidian sources and routes between the highlands and
  Pacific coast helped sustain exchange. Its buildings were largely made from earth and adobe,
  leaving remains more vulnerable than stone cities elsewhere. During the Classic period,
  architecture and imported objects reveal strong connections with Teotihuacan, but do not alone
  prove foreign government. The civic centre declined while occupation continued beyond it.
