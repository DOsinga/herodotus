description: >-
  Northern Yan was a kingdom in northeastern China, based at Longcheng in present day
  Liaoning, from 407 to 436. It arose when a coup overthrew the Later Yan ruler Murong Xi
  and installed Gao Yun. Some histories date its beginning instead to Feng Ba's accession in
  409. Its rulers used the title Heavenly King and maintained separate arrangements for
  Chinese agricultural communities and tribal subjects. Feng Ba encouraged farming, reduced
  taxes, and sought alliances to balance the much stronger Northern Wei. His brother Feng
  Hong seized power during a succession crisis in 430. Repeated Wei attacks and internal
  defections then weakened the kingdom. In 436 Feng Hong fled to Goguryeo, and Northern Wei
  took control of the remaining Yan territory.
