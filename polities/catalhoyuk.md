description: >-
  Çatalhöyük was a large Neolithic settlement on the Konya Plain in southern Anatolia, occupied
  approximately from 7100 to 5700 BCE in this record. Its closely packed mudbrick houses were
  usually entered through roofs, which also served as routes between buildings. Repeated
  rebuilding gradually raised the settlement mounds. Residents cultivated cereals, kept
  livestock, hunted, and worked materials such as obsidian. Burials beneath house floors, painted
  walls, modeled reliefs, and animal installations reveal elaborate domestic ritual practices.
  Excavations have not identified a royal palace or a clearly separate governing class, and its
  political organization remains unknown. Differences between houses and burial groups still
  invite debate about social relations. Çatalhöyük therefore represents an archaeological
  settlement and culture, not a documented kingdom or state.
