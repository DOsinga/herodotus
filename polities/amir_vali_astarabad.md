description: >-
  Amir Vali was the son of Shaykh Ali Hindu, who had governed Astarabad for the Ilkhanid claimant
  Togha Temür. When the Sarbadars murdered Togha Temür in 1353 and annexed Astarabad, Vali
  escaped to the Jauni Qurban tribe and then to Mazandaran, from where he raided the Sarbadar
  garrisons. By 1356 he had taken Astarabad, first in the name of Togha Temür's son Luqman and
  soon in his own, and he declared for Shi'ism to win support in Mazandaran. He fought off
  repeated Sarbadar attacks and also clashed with the Jalayirids over Ray and the lands to the
  west. The Sarbadars drove him out in the 1360s, but he regained Astarabad around 1374. Timur's
  arrival ended his rule in 1384.
