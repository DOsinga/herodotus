description: >-
  Khuttal was an Iranian ruled principality in the valleys of present day southern Tajikistan, with its royal centre at Hulbuk near Kulob. Its dynasty emerged around the early seventh century as Hephthalite power weakened. Rulers bore titles meaning king or lord of Khuttal and continued managing their own affairs despite pressure from larger neighbours. An Arab expedition around 676 secured acknowledgement and tribute without establishing direct government. Later invasions drew Khuttal into alliances with the Turkic Turgesh, whose intervention helped resist Umayyad armies. Succession disputes and regencies complicated these struggles, while members of the ruling house also sought support abroad. The Abbasid takeover destroyed the local royal line around 750 or 751, ending the independent principality represented in this record.
