description: >-
  The Congo Free State existed in central Africa from 1885 to 1908 as Leopold II's
  personal monarchy, separate from the Belgian state he also ruled. International
  recognition followed campaigns presented as humanitarian and commercial ventures, while
  his administration expanded control across the Congo basin. Boma became the
  administrative capital, but Leopold directed affairs from Europe. Officials, concession
  companies, and the Force Publique extracted ivory and rubber through coercion and forced
  labour. Hostage-taking, killings, mutilation, hunger, and disease devastated
  communities; uncertain population records make exact losses difficult to establish.
  Reports by missionaries, Roger Casement, and campaigners exposed the system and
  generated international pressure. Belgium annexed the territory in 1908, replacing
  Leopold's personal government with the colony known as the Belgian Congo.
