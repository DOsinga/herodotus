description: >-
  Urkesh was a kingdom in northeastern Syria, near the foothills of the Taurus
  Mountains, represented approximately from 2400 to 2000 BCE. Its remains lie at Tell
  Mozan, where excavations have uncovered a palace, temple buildings and seal
  impressions identifying members of the royal household. Rulers used the title endan,
  and the kingdom is associated with the early Hurrian world, although many surviving
  texts employ Akkadian or Sumerian. A marriage connected its court with Naram-Sin's
  Akkadian royal family, indicating alliance rather than simple subordination in this
  record. The city's religious traditions included the god Kumarbi. The early kingdom's
  chronology remains incomplete. Urkesh continued as a settlement after this period and
  later came under Mari's authority, which local inhabitants continued to resist.
