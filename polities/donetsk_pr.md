description: >-
  The Donetsk People's Republic was a Russian-backed separatist administration that governed part of Ukraine's Donetsk oblast from 2014 to 2022. Proclaimed after activists seized government buildings, it established authorities in Donetsk during the war in the Donbas. Its leaders claimed republican institutions, including a head of state and legislature, while armed groups and Russian support shaped power. It claimed the whole oblast but controlled only part before Russia's full-scale invasion in 2022. The conflict damaged an industrial region and displaced many residents. Ukraine and most countries rejected its claim to independence. Russia recognised it in 2022 and later declared its annexation. This record ends with incorporation into Russia's administration, without treating that internationally disputed annexation as a lawful territorial transfer.
