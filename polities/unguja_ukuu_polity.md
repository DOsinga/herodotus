description: >-
  Unguja Ukuu was a trading settlement on Zanzibar's Unguja island, represented here
  approximately between 500 and 900. Excavations reveal a coastal community connected to
  mainland Africa and the wider Indian Ocean, rather than a state with
  known rulers. Imported pottery from the Middle East, India, and other distant
  regions appears alongside local ceramics, glass, beads, coins, iron objects, and animal
  remains. These finds show how exchange supported an urban centre and brought
  goods and practices to the island. Food remains illuminate fishing, livestock, and
  access to supplies beyond the settlement. Its government remains unknown, so archaeological
  evidence carries the account. Around 900 the centre was abandoned or contracted,
  although the site's history and later traditions extend beyond this early phase.
