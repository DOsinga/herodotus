description: >-
  The Sui dynasty governed China from 581, reunifying northern and southern realms after centuries of division. Emperor Wen founded the monarchy and conquered the southern Chen in 589. His government revised administration, law, and land arrangements, helping establish institutions later developed by the Tang. Daxing served as the first capital; Emperor Yang subsequently centered government at Luoyang. Large construction programs, especially canal works linking major regions, strengthened transport but placed heavy demands on labor and resources. Failed campaigns against Goguryeo and widespread rebellion undermined the regime. Emperor Yang died in 618, when the northern court became Tang. Yang Tong's Sui court nevertheless continued governing Luoyang until Wang Shichong established Zheng in 619, the endpoint of this record's territorial Sui government.
