description: >-
  Rugiland was a small kingdom of the Rugii north of the Danube in present day Lower Austria,
  represented here from approximately 467 to 487. It formed after the collapse of Hunnic power, when
  Germanic groups established their own governments along the former Roman frontier. King Flaccitheus
  and his son Feletheus ruled its people, who practised Arian Christianity. Across the river, Roman
  communities in Noricum lived amid weakening imperial institutions, with the holy man Severinus
  acting as an important intermediary. Relations with Odoacer's Italian kingdom deteriorated amid
  wider imperial rivalries. Odoacer invaded in 487, defeated the Rugian army, and executed Feletheus
  and his wife. The kingdom disappeared, and the subsequent withdrawal of Roman inhabitants left the
  region without a continuing government.
