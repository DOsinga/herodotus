description: >-
  The Kingdom of Kangaba was the northern share of the Mali Empire's heartland after the empire
  broke up, ruled by Keita mansas descended from Mansa Kanda. After defeat outside Djenné in 1599
  and raids by Bamana and Fula war bands, Mansa Mahmud IV took refuge in the mountains, and on his
  death his sons divided what remained. No single Keita ruled Manden again. Kangaba, on the upper
  Niger in what is now Mali, became the seat of the northern branch, which competed with its
  kinsmen in Joma and Hamana for leadership of the Mande. Its rulers kept the title of mansa and
  the prestige of Sundiata's line, and the town became the guardian of the Kamablon shrine. France
  brought Kangaba under its rule in 1888.
