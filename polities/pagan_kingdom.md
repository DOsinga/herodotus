name: Pagan Kingdom
description: >-
  The Pagan Kingdom grew from a settlement on the Irrawaddy River into the first state to
  unite much of present day Myanmar. Founded conventionally in 849, it became a major
  kingdom under Anawrahta in the eleventh century. Kings supported irrigation in the central
  plains, drawing agricultural wealth and manpower into their expanding court. Royal
  patronage helped establish Theravada Buddhism, while thousands of temples made Pagan a
  lasting religious centre. Burmese language and institutions developed alongside influences
  from Mon and other regional communities. Extensive gifts of land to religious foundations
  weakened the crown's resources over time. Mongol invasions and internal division broke
  royal power during the late thirteenth century. The kingdom ended in 1297, when Myinsaing
  rulers displaced its remaining authority.
