description: >-
  The Ili Sultanate, also called the Taranchi Sultanate of Kulja, was a Muslim state in the
  Ili valley of northwestern Xinjiang, lasting from 1864 to 1871. In November 1864 the Hui of
  the Ili towns rose against the Qing, and the Taranchis, Turkic farmers settled there by the
  Qing, joined them. The rebels took the commercial town of Ningyuan at once and, after a long
  siege, the military headquarters of Huiyuan in 1866, where the Qing governor blew himself
  up. The Taranchis then turned on their Hui allies and drove them out, and their leader Abil
  Oghlan ruled as sultan from Kulja. Russia, worried by the unrest on its border and by Yaqub
  Beg's expansion, occupied the valley in 1871 and ended the sultanate.
