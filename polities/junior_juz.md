description: >-
  The Junior jüz was the westernmost of the three Kazakh hordes, roaming the steppes between the
  Ural River, the Caspian Sea and the lower Syr Darya. After Tauke Khan's death around 1718 the
  Kazakh khanate no longer acted as one, and the Junior jüz followed its own khans, drawn like the
  others from the line of Genghis Khan. Its leaders governed through alliances with clan elders
  and judges rather than a settled bureaucracy. Under pressure from the Dzungars and Kalmyks,
  Abulkhair Khan sought Russian protection in 1731, but his successors still ruled the pastoral
  clans themselves. Russian forts along the Ural line grew in influence, and Syrym Datuly's revolt
  challenged both the khans and Russian officials. Russia abolished the khan's office in 1824.
