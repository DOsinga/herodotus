description: >-
  Confederate Ireland governed much of Ireland from 1642 to 1652 during the Wars of the
  Three Kingdoms. Formed after the 1641 rebellion, it united Catholic landowners, clergy,
  and military leaders who sought religious freedom and protection of their lands. A General
  Assembly meeting at Kilkenny chose a Supreme Council, supported by provincial and county
  councils. The Confederates professed loyalty to Charles I while raising armies, collecting
  revenue, and seeking foreign assistance. Disputes between those favouring compromise with
  royalists and those demanding stronger Catholic guarantees divided their leadership.
  Confederates eventually allied with the royalists against Parliament. Cromwell's invasion
  in 1649 and subsequent campaigns destroyed their military position. By 1652, Commonwealth
  conquest had ended Confederate government and brought extensive confiscation and
  displacement.
