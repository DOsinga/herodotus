description: >-
  The Beylik of Candar governed parts of northern Anatolia from 1291 to 1461. Founded by Yaman
  Candar amid the decline of Seljuk power, it developed around Kastamonu and the Black Sea port
  of Sinop. Hereditary Muslim beys ruled the principality, which was also known as Isfendiyar
  after one of its prominent rulers. Dynastic disputes repeatedly divided its lands. Ottoman
  pressure removed much of its inland territory, but its rulers retained a coastal base and
  recovered lands after Timur defeated the Ottomans in 1402. Sinop linked the state to maritime
  commerce, while its rulers alternated resistance, alliances, and submission to larger
  neighbours. Mehmed II captured Sinop in 1461 and absorbed the principality, although members
  of its family later served Ottoman government.
