description: >-
  Modern Hungary emerged in 1918 when Austria-Hungary dissolved, leaving Budapest as the capital of a separate state in central Europe's Carpathian Basin. A republic and brief communist regime preceded a restored kingdom governed by Regent Miklós Horthy without a king. The Treaty of Trianon sharply reduced its territory, shaping later revisionist politics. Hungary fought alongside the Axis during the Second World War before German occupation and Soviet conquest. A communist republic followed, with the 1956 uprising suppressed by Soviet forces. The transition of 1989 established competitive elections and parliamentary government. Hungary subsequently joined NATO and the European Union. This record treats those changes of regime as parts of one continuing state, whose modern borders differ greatly from the earlier kingdom.
