description: >-
  Ula was a Haixi Jurchen state on the Songhua River north of modern Jilin City, one of the
  four Hūlun states of the late sixteenth century. Its ruling Nara clan traced its descent
  to Nacibulu, founder of an earlier Hūlun alliance, and Buyan made Ula a separate state
  with a walled seat at Ula city in 1561. Its beile governed the farming and hunting clans
  of the middle Songhua and extended their influence east into the Warka lands. Ula joined
  the coalition that Nurhaci defeated at Gure in 1593, and its captured prince Bujantai was
  later sent home to rule as Nurhaci's ally. Bujantai then reasserted his independence and
  fought Nurhaci over the eastern tribes. Nurhaci took Ula city in 1613 and absorbed its
  people into his banners.
