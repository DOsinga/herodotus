description: >-
  The Duchy of Surabaya was a Javanese principality centred on the port of Surabaya in the Brantas
  delta of East Java. Its Muslim lords, who claimed descent from Sunan Ampel, held the port under
  Majapahit and submitted to Demak only in 1530. After Sultan Trenggana's death in 1546 Surabaya
  became independent, and by the early seventeenth century it was the leading power of East Java and
  the richest port on the north coast. It led an alliance of eastern principalities against Mataram
  and held Gresik, Sedayu and overlordship in southern Borneo. Sultan Agung of Mataram wore it down
  with years of raids, and in 1625 the starving city surrendered after its water supply was dammed
  and spoiled.
