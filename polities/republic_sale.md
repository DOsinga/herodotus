description: >-
  The Republic of Salé was a corsair state at the mouth of the Bou Regreg river on the
  Atlantic coast of Morocco, centred on the kasbah of what is now Rabat. It was founded in
  1627 by Moriscos expelled from Spain, especially the Hornacheros, who threw off the
  authority of the weakened Saadi sultans. A council of notables and an elected governor
  ran its affairs. Its corsairs raided shipping across the Atlantic, as far as Ireland and
  Iceland, and took captives for ransom, while European states sent envoys to negotiate
  with it. Conflict between the kasbah and the older town of Salé was frequent. From 1641
  the Dila'i zawiya held a loose suzerainty. The Alaouite sultan Rashid took the towns in
  1668.
