description: >-
  Cantref Selyf was a territorial share of Brycheiniog in what is now southern Powys,
  Wales. This record places its separate government approximately between 1045 and the
  Norman conquest in 1093. Medieval genealogical tradition describes Brycheiniog being
  divided among related heirs, with Selyf receiving the portion that bore his name
  alongside the shares of Tewdos and Einon. The surviving accounts concern the wider
  kingdom and do not provide a history of this local court. Its rulers belonged to the
  Welsh tradition of hereditary lordship, but their sequence and the division's timing are
  uncertain. Llanfihangel Talyllyn represents the territory rather than a proven capital.
  Norman expansion under Bernard de Neufmarché ended Welsh government and brought these
  lands into the lordship of Brecknock.
