description: >-
  Nolambavadi was a kingdom across parts of southeastern Karnataka and neighbouring Andhra
  Pradesh and Tamil Nadu. Its Nolamba rulers governed from centres including Henjeru, now
  Hemavati. The chronology here begins with Mangala around 735 and follows separate royal
  government rather than every later use of Nolamba titles. The kings navigated alliances and
  conflicts with the Western Gangas, Rashtrakutas and other regional powers, often
  acknowledging stronger rulers while managing their own lands. Inscriptions preserve royal
  names and grants, while surviving temples and sculpture show royal patronage of southern
  Indian art. Ganga conquest around 968 interrupted the kingdom. A smaller restored government
  followed around 975 under Chalukya overlordship, but its separately governing kings are
  estimated to have disappeared by about 1000 CE.
