description: >-
  The Duchy of Lucca was a small Italian state created by the Congress of Vienna in 1815 for Maria
  Luisa of Bourbon-Parma, former queen of Etruria, as compensation until her family could recover
  Parma. It was centred on the walled city of Lucca and its surrounding countryside in
  northwestern Tuscany. Maria Luisa governed it until her death in 1824, and her son Charles Louis
  followed, a ruler more interested in travel and religion than administration. The duchy kept a
  modest court and government and depended on its larger neighbours for security. As planned in
  1815, when Marie Louise of Parma died, Lucca would pass to Tuscany; Charles Louis handed it over
  early in 1847 and moved to Parma.
