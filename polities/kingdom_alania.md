description: >-
  Alania was a medieval kingdom of the Iranian-speaking Alans in the northern Caucasus. This record begins around 875, an estimate for the emergence of its royal government, rather than for earlier Alan communities. Its rulers controlled settlements and routes across the foothills, especially approaches to the Darial Pass. Byzantine influence helped establish Christianity, while pagan traditions also persisted. Marriage alliances, trade and military service connected Alania with Georgia and Byzantium; Alan cavalry became valued beyond the kingdom. Maghas was its royal centre, but the city's location remains disputed, and the map uses the Arkhyz region as a representative point. The kingdom weakened before Mongol armies attacked. Their destruction of Maghas in 1239–1240 ended central government, although Alan communities survived elsewhere.
