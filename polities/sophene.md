description: >-
  Sophene was a kingdom in southwestern Armenia, in present southeastern Turkey, from
  approximately 260 until 95 BCE. Its beginnings are uncertain, but a branch of the
  Orontid ruling family established a monarchy during the third century BCE. Its rulers
  navigated between the Seleucid kings and neighbouring Armenian kingdoms, sometimes
  accepting stronger rulers' supremacy. After Antiochus III's defeat by Rome in 190 BCE,
  Zariadres asserted independence and expanded his territory. Sophene's kings minted coins
  that combined Hellenistic royal imagery with their own dynastic traditions. Arsamosata
  and Carcathiocerta served as royal centres, though the latter's location remains
  approximate in this record. The kingdom maintained a distinct ruling line until Tigranes
  the Great conquered it in 95 BCE and deposed its final king.
