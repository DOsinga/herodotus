description: >-
  The Palembang Sultanate was a Malay Muslim state on the Musi river in southern Sumatra. Palembang
  had been ruled by a line of Javanese origin under the loose overlordship of Mataram. After Dutch
  East India Company forces burned the city in 1659, Susuhunan Abdurrahman proclaimed an independent
  sultanate and broke with Mataram. The sultanate grew rich from pepper and from the tin of Bangka,
  which it controlled, and it dealt with the company as a trading partner. During the Napoleonic wars
  the British occupied Palembang and quarrelled with Sultan Mahmud Badaruddin II, who later fought the
  returning Dutch. After their victory in 1821 the Dutch dissolved the sultanate in 1823, and its last
  ruler was exiled to the Moluccas in 1825.
