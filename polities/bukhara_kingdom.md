name: "Kingdom of Bukhara"
description: >-
  The Kingdom of Bukhara was a Sogdian state centred on the oasis city in
  present Uzbekistan, represented approximately from 500 to 890. Its local
  rulers bore the title Bukhar Khudah, meaning lord of Bukhara. Early chronology
  is uncertain, relying partly on later accounts by Narshakhi, while coins and
  recorded rulers provide firmer evidence for later centuries. The court
  governed a commercial and farming centre influenced by neighbouring Turkic
  powers. A ruling queen, remembered as the Khatun, managed affairs during her
  son's minority. Arab campaigns under Qutaiba brought conquest and a garrison,
  but native rulers retained reduced territory and government. Religious life
  changed gradually from Zoroastrian traditions towards Islam. Ismail's
  incorporation of Bukhara into the Samanid state ended this local kingdom.
