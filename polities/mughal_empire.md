description: >-
  The Mughal Empire was a South Asian monarchy founded by Babur after his
  victory at Panipat in 1526. Humayun lost power to the Sur rulers in 1540 and
  recovered it in 1555. Under Akbar and his successors, imperial officials,
  military rank holders, and land revenue systems supported expansion across
  much of the subcontinent. Persian court culture blended with regional
  traditions, producing painting, gardens, and buildings such as the Taj Mahal
  at Agra. Aurangzeb's long campaigns extended imperial territory but increased
  pressures on its resources. During the eighteenth century, provincial rulers,
  Marathas, and invading armies reduced central power. This record ends when
  British forces took Delhi in 1803 and made the emperor a Company pensioner,
  although the dynasty's title survived longer.
