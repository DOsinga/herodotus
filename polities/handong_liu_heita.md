description: >-
  Handong was Liu Heita's short lived rebel government in Hebei, northern China, from 621 to 623.
  It arose after Tang forces defeated Dou Jiande's Xia state and executed its ruler. Former Xia
  soldiers and officials rallied around Liu, who restored much of their territory north of the
  Yellow River. He took the title Prince of Handong in 622 and governed from Mingzhou, retaining
  Dou's administrative arrangements and many of his followers. Alliances with other rebels and the
  Eastern Turks strengthened his resistance. Li Shimin defeated him in 622, but Liu returned with
  Turkish support and rebuilt his regional position later that year. A second Tang campaign
  exhausted his army. Betrayed by a subordinate, Liu was captured and executed in 623.
