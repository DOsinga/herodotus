description: >-
  Mecklenburg-Schwerin was a dynastic state in northern Germany, formed by the 1701 settlement dividing Mecklenburg between Schwerin and Strelitz. Its dukes belonged to the House of Mecklenburg, descended from the medieval Slavic rulers of the region. Schwerin served as their residence, while Baltic ports and an agricultural countryside shaped the state's economy. Powerful nobles and towns limited ducal ambitions through the estates. The state survived the end of the Holy Roman Empire and joined Napoleon's Confederation of the Rhine. In 1815 its ruler received the title of grand duke and entered the German Confederation. Membership in the North German Confederation in 1867 transferred military and foreign affairs to the federation. The dynasty retained domestic rule until the revolution of 1918.
