description: >-
  Mecklenburg-Schwerin was a dynastic state in northern Germany, formed when Mecklenburg was
  divided between Schwerin and Güstrow in 1621; it inherited Güstrow in 1695 and ceded Strelitz in
  1701. Its dukes belonged to the House of Mecklenburg, descended from the medieval Slavic rulers
  of the region. Schwerin served as their residence, while Baltic ports and an agricultural
  countryside shaped the state's economy. Powerful nobles and towns limited ducal ambitions
  through the estates. The state survived the end of the Holy Roman Empire and joined Napoleon's
  Confederation of the Rhine. In 1815 its ruler received the title of grand duke and entered the
  German Confederation. Membership in the North German Confederation in 1867 transferred military
  and foreign affairs to the federation. The dynasty retained domestic rule until the revolution
  of 1918.
