description: >-
  Basel, on the Rhine where it turns north between the Jura, the Black Forest and the Vosges, was
  the seat of the prince-bishops of Basel, and a council of nobles and burghers appears there from
  1185. Conflicts between the bishops and the citizens ran through the thirteenth and fourteenth
  centuries. After the earthquake of 1356, and with the bishops deep in debt, the council bought
  up their rights over the city piece by piece, Kleinbasel on the right bank among them in 1392.
  By the late fourteenth century Basel was independent in all but name, while the bishops ruled
  their lands in the Jura from Porrentruy. It hosted the church council of 1431 to 1449, founded
  the first Swiss university in 1460 and joined the Swiss Confederacy in 1501.
