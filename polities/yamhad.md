description: >-
  Yamhad was an Amorite kingdom centred on Halab, modern Aleppo in northern Syria, from about 1810 to 1517 BCE. Its kings combined military force, marriage alliances and diplomacy to dominate neighbouring states. Under Yarim Lim I, Yamhad became a major power opposing the expansion of Shamshi Adad's northern Mesopotamian kingdom and supporting allied rulers at Mari. Aleppo's sanctuary of the storm god Hadad gave the capital religious weight alongside its political importance. Trade linked its lands with Mesopotamia, Anatolia and the Mediterranean. Much of its history survives in tablets from Mari and Alalakh rather than excavations beneath Aleppo. Hittite king Mursili I sacked the city around 1600 BCE without retaining it. A weakened local kingdom continued before incorporation into Mitanni.
