description: >-
  The Setsen Khan khanate was the eastern of the Khalkha Mongol khanates, centred on the Kherlen
  river in present eastern Mongolia. Its rulers descended from Dayan Khan's son Gersenji, and
  Sholoi, who took the title Setsen Khan in the 1620s or 1630s, made it a separate khanate
  alongside the Tüsheet and Zasagt khans. Bordering the southern Mongols, it was the first Khalkha
  khanate to deal closely with the rising Manchu state after Ligdan Khan's defeat. Sholoi resisted
  Qing claims and sheltered refugees from Inner Mongolia, but his successors sent tribute while
  governing their own herding peoples. Like the other Khalkha khanates, it accepted Qing
  sovereignty at the Dolon Nor assembly of 1691, after Galdan's invasion.
