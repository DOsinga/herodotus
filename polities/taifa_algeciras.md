description: >-
  The Taifa of Algeciras was a Muslim kingdom on the Strait of Gibraltar, in the far south of
  present Spain. It was ruled by a branch of the Hammudids, an Arab family of Idrisid descent who
  had briefly claimed the caliphate of Córdoba during the civil war that broke up the caliphate
  after 1009. Their main line ruled Málaga, while relatives governed Algeciras, the port that
  controlled crossings to North Africa and the town of Gibraltar. The kingdom was small but
  strategically placed, and its rulers maintained links with Ceuta across the strait. From the
  1040s the expanding taifa of Seville under al-Mu'tadid absorbed one neighbour after another. In
  1058 Algeciras fell to Seville, which ended the Hammudid government there.
