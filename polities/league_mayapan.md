description: >-
  The League of Mayapan was a Maya alliance on the Yucatán Peninsula, traditionally dated
  from 1007 to 1441. Accounts describe Chichen Itza, Uxmal, and Mayapan as its leading
  centres, associated with the Itza, Xiu, and Cocom lineages. Rather than a single royal
  dynasty, its history concerns cooperation and rivalry among these ruling families and
  their towns. Mayapan eventually became the main political centre, while traditions connect
  Hunac Ceel with conflict against Chichen Itza. These stories combine historical memory
  with legend, and archaeological evidence complicates their chronology. A rebellion led by
  the Xiu against the Cocom in 1441 broke the alliance apart. Yucatán subsequently consisted
  of smaller independent provinces, which faced Spanish conquest in the sixteenth century
  after prolonged local resistance.
