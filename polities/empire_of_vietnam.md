description: >-
  The Empire of Vietnam was a short-lived monarchy established in March 1945 after Japan overthrew the French colonial administration in Indochina. Emperor Bảo Đại ruled from Huế, while Trần Trọng Kim headed a cabinet formed in April. The government proclaimed independence and sought to replace colonial institutions with Vietnamese administration, education, and national symbols. Japanese military occupation limited its room for action, and the famine compounded its difficulties. Initially covering Annam and Tonkin, it gained nominal authority over Cochinchina only in August. Japan's surrender removed the government's sponsor just as the Việt Minh launched the August Revolution. Bảo Đại abdicated later that month, bringing the imperial experiment to an end and opening the way for the Democratic Republic of Vietnam.
