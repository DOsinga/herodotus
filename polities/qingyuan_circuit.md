description: >-
  Qingyuan Circuit was a de facto independent regime in southern Fujian at the end of the Five
  Dynasties and Ten Kingdoms period, governing Quan and Zhang prefectures from 949 to 978.
  After the fall of the Min kingdom, the Min general Liu Congxiao took control of Quanzhou
  and accepted the nominal overlordship of Southern Tang, which created the circuit for him in
  949. He ran its affairs, revenue and army himself, and from 960 also sent tribute to the
  Song. After his death in 962 his adopted son was overthrown by the officers Zhang Hansi and
  Chen Hongjin, and Chen ruled from 963. The circuit was renamed Pinghai in 964. As the Song
  completed the conquest of the south, Chen surrendered his territory in 978.
