description: >-
  Chen Han, officially the Great Han, was a rebel state on the middle Yangtze that lasted
  from 1360 to 1364, in the last years of the Yuan dynasty. Chen Youliang, a fisherman's
  son who rose as a general of the Tianwan state, had governed Jiangxi from Jiujiang. In
  1360 he killed the Tianwan emperor Xu Shouhui, proclaimed himself emperor of Han and took
  over most of Tianwan's lands in Hubei, Hunan and Jiangxi. His large river fleet made him
  the strongest power on the upper Yangtze, and he fought a long war with Zhu Yuanzhang. Chen
  was killed at the battle of Lake Poyang in 1363. His son Chen Li held out in Wuchang until
  he surrendered to Zhu in 1364.
