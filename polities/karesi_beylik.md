description: >-
  The Beylik of Karesi was a Turkoman principality in northwestern Anatolia, centred on
  Balikesir and the former Byzantine region of Mysia. This record places its independent
  existence between 1296 and 1345, during the fragmentation of Seljuk authority. Karesi Bey and
  his family established hereditary rule as Turkish forces expanded into neighbouring Byzantine
  lands. The principality developed naval forces capable of raiding settlements across the sea
  and became involved in Byzantine rivalries. After its founder's death, members of the dynasty
  governed divided domains, and the surviving evidence does not give a simple sequence of
  rulers. Ottoman expansion eventually absorbed the territory. Its coastal position, ships, and
  commanders helped the Ottomans extend their influence toward the Dardanelles and later into
  southeastern Europe.
