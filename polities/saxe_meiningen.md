description: >-
  Saxe-Meiningen was an Ernestine duchy in the southwest of present Thuringia, created in 1680
  when the lands of Ernest the Pious of Saxe-Gotha were divided among his seven sons, and Bernhard
  I received Meiningen. Its dukes governed from the Elisabethenburg palace and gradually enlarged
  the duchy through inheritance from extinct branches, gaining Hildburghausen and Saalfeld in
  1826. In the nineteenth century the duchy was known for the reforms of Bernhard II and
  especially for Duke George II, the theatre duke, whose Meininger company revolutionised European
  stagecraft, and for its court orchestra, led by Hans von Bülow and others. Saxe-Meiningen sided
  with Austria in 1866 and joined the North German Confederation in 1867.
