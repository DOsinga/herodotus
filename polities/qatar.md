description: >-
  Qatar is a Gulf state occupying a peninsula on the eastern Arabian coast, independent since 1971. Doha is its capital and main urban centre. The Al Thani family governs through a hereditary emirate, with the ruler appointing the government and holding extensive authority. Before independence, British protection limited Qatar's foreign relations; the ending of those arrangements opened its separate international role. Oil revenues and especially natural gas transformed an economy once closely tied to fishing and pearling. Large numbers of migrant workers supported rapid construction and population growth. Qatar invested abroad and built influence through diplomacy, mediation, and the Al Jazeera media network. Regional tensions included a blockade by neighbouring states from 2017 to 2021. The monarchy continues in power.
