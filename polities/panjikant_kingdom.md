name: "Kingdom of Panjikant"
description: >-
  Panjikant was a Sogdian kingdom in the Zarafshan valley of present day Tajikistan,
  represented here from an estimated sixth century beginning until 722. Its urban court
  belonged to a landscape of competing Sogdian rulers rather than a single united Sogdian
  state. The last well documented king, Divashtich, succeeded Chukin Chur Bilga around 706
  and shared authority with other princes. He claimed kingship over Sogdia and Samarkand,
  but his effective government centred on Panjikant and its surroundings. Initially
  accommodating the Umayyad conquerors, he later joined resistance against them. Defeat
  drove him to a fortress on Mount Mugh, where documents illuminate his administration and
  diplomacy. Arab forces captured and executed him in 722, destroying his government and
  burning parts of the city.
