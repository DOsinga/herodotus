description: >-
  The Kucha Khanate was a Muslim state in the northern Tarim Basin, formed in the revolt
  against the Qing and lasting from 1864 to 1867. In June 1864 the Dungans of Kucha rose and
  were joined by the local Turkic population, who took the Qing fort and killed its
  garrison. Since no rebel leader had authority over the whole community, they chose Rashidin
  Khoja, a dervish and keeper of the shrine of his ancestor Arshad al-Din, who took the title
  of khan. He sent armies east towards Karasahr and Turpan and west towards Aksu, Kashgar and
  Yarkand, hoping to rule the whole basin, and his forces helped take the fort of Ürümqi. Yaqub
  Beg defeated his armies, and in 1867 conquered Kucha and had Rashidin killed.
