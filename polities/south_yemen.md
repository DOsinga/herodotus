description: >-
  South Yemen occupied southern and eastern Arabia from 1967 until 1990, with its capital at
  Aden. Independence followed an armed struggle against British rule and the collapse of the
  colonial federation and protectorates. The National Liberation Front took power, and its
  Marxist faction established a socialist state after 1969. The Yemeni Socialist Party later
  dominated government, supported by a legislature called the Supreme People's Council. Land
  reform, nationalisation, education, and changes to family law reshaped society, while Soviet
  support helped sustain the country's economy and armed forces. Relations with North Yemen
  alternated between fighting and negotiations. A violent internal leadership struggle in 1986
  weakened the government. In 1990, the two Yemeni republics united to form the present
  Republic of Yemen.
