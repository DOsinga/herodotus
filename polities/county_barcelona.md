description: >-
  The County of Barcelona was a hereditary lordship in northeastern Iberia, developing
  from a Frankish frontier county into the leading power among the Catalan counties.
  This record begins in 897, when Wilfred the Hairy's sons succeeded him locally, and
  ends with the union of Barcelona and Aragon in 1162. Independence from Frankish
  kings developed gradually rather than through one founding declaration. Counts
  expanded their authority through marriages, alliances, and warfare against
  neighbouring Muslim states. Barcelona's sack in 985 exposed the weakness of distant
  Frankish protection. Ramon Berenguer I strengthened comital power and acquired
  interests north of the Pyrenees. Ramon Berenguer IV's marriage to Petronilla of
  Aragon prepared the union inherited by Alfonso II, while Barcelona retained its own
  institutions afterward.
