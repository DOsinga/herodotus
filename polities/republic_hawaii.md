description: >-
  The Republic of Hawaii governed the Hawaiian Islands from Honolulu after the overthrow of Queen
  Liliʻuokalani in 1893. This record includes the provisional government that preceded the
  republic's constitution in 1894. President Sanford Dole and a small political elite, closely tied
  to American business interests, sought annexation by the United States. Voting rules and loyalty
  requirements limited participation, while royalists challenged the new order and mounted an
  unsuccessful restoration attempt in 1895. The government imprisoned the former queen before later
  pardoning her. Sugar exports and Hawaii's position in the Pacific shaped its foreign relations.
  American opposition delayed annexation, but the Spanish–American War strengthened arguments for
  acquiring the islands. Despite widespread opposition, sovereignty passed to the United States in
  August 1898.
