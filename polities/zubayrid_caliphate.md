description: >-
  The Zubayrid Caliphate was the government led by Abd Allah ibn al-Zubayr from Mecca between 683
  and 692 during the second Muslim civil war. Rejecting Umayyad hereditary succession, he claimed
  leadership of the Muslim community after Yazid's death. His firmest base lay in the Hijaz,
  while governors and allies extended his authority elsewhere, especially Iraq under his brother
  Musab. Recognition across provinces did not mean uniform control, and rival Umayyad, Kharijite,
  and pro-Alid movements contested his rule. Remaining in Mecca gave his court religious prestige
  but limited his direct influence over provincial campaigns. The Umayyads defeated Musab in 691,
  then sent al-Hajjaj against Mecca. Ibn al-Zubayr died during its capture in 692, ending his
  government and restoring Umayyad control there.
