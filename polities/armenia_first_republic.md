description: >-
  The First Republic of Armenia governed part of the southern Caucasus from 1918 to
  1920, with Yerevan as its capital. It emerged after the Russian Empire collapsed and
  the short lived Transcaucasian federation broke apart. A parliamentary government,
  dominated by the Armenian Revolutionary Federation, sought to build institutions amid
  war, hunger and disease. Large numbers of refugees had survived the Armenian genocide
  in the Ottoman Empire, creating demands on a poor and damaged country. Armenia
  disputed borders with neighbouring Georgia and Azerbaijan and hoped for international
  support for wider territorial claims. Those hopes exceeded its military resources. In
  1920, Turkish nationalist forces defeated its army while Soviet forces advanced. The
  government surrendered power, and a Soviet Armenian republic replaced it.
