description: >-
  The Seljuk Sultanate of Khorasan was Ahmad Sanjar's eastern government, centred on Merv
  from 1118 to 1153. Although Sanjar claimed senior authority within the Seljuk family,
  western sultans governed separately from Hamadan. His monarchy combined a Turkic
  military ruling house with Persian administration and court culture, drawing resources
  from the cities and agricultural districts of northeastern Iran and adjacent Central
  Asia. Sanjar sought to control neighbouring rulers and restrain the power of Khwarazm.
  Defeat by the Qara Khitai at Qatwan in 1141 weakened his position and loosened his
  influence beyond Khorasan. In 1153, rebellious Oghuz groups defeated and captured him,
  devastating the region and breaking his government. His escape three years later did not
  restore the state before his death.
