  Hamas established a separate government in the Gaza Strip after defeating Fatah forces in June
  2007. The split left the Palestinian Authority governing parts of the West Bank while Hamas
  controlled Gaza's administration and security forces. Its Islamist leadership exercised
  authoritarian power without renewed national elections. Israeli and Egyptian border
  restrictions, Palestinian political division and repeated wars shaped daily government and the
  economy. Hamas's armed wing also remained central to its authority. The October 2023 attacks on
  Israel were followed by a devastating Israeli military campaign, mass displacement and
  extensive physical destruction in Gaza. Hamas lost control of most of the territory during 2025
  but continued administering part after the October peace plan. The endpoint of this government
  remains unsettled.
