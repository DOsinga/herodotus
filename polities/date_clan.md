description: >-
  The Date were the Sengoku daimyo house of southern Mutsu and Dewa in northeastern Japan,
  ruling in their own right from the Ōnin War in 1467 until they submitted to Toyotomi Hideyoshi
  in 1590. Named for the Date district near modern Fukushima, they had held the Okitama basin
  around Yonezawa since the 1380s. Date Tanemune was made military governor of Mutsu in 1522,
  issued a law code and built a network of marriage alliances across the north, though a war
  with his son Harumune in the 1540s weakened the house. Harumune moved the seat to Yonezawa.
  Date Masamune, the one-eyed dragon, conquered the Ashina of Aizu in 1589, but arrived late at
  the siege of Odawara in 1590 and gave up Aizu to keep his house.
