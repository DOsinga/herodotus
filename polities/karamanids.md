description: >-
  The Beylik of Karaman was a Turkish monarchy in central and southern Anatolia, emerging
  around 1250 and surviving until 1487. Its ruling family gained power in the Taurus
  Mountains as Seljuk authority weakened under Mongol pressure. Centres including Larende,
  Ermenek, and Konya supported a state that became one of the Ottomans' strongest rivals.
  Karamanid rulers drew on Turkmen military support and contested cities and trade routes
  with neighbouring powers. Mehmed Bey's occupation of Konya in 1277 is associated with a
  declaration favouring Turkish in official business. Ottoman victories reduced the
  principality, but Timur's defeat of the Ottomans in 1402 restored its fortunes
  temporarily. Later conquests took Konya and the heartland. A smaller surviving government
  disappeared under Ottoman expansion in 1487.
