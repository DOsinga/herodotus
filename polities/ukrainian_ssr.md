description: >-
  The Ukrainian Soviet Socialist Republic was established during the civil wars following the
  Russian Revolution, with a constitution adopted at Kharkiv in 1919. This record covers its
  years before incorporation into the Soviet Union in 1922, not its subsequent existence as a
  union republic. Bolshevik leaders governed through soviets, commissariats, and a party closely
  tied to Moscow. Red Army campaigns fought Ukrainian national governments, White armies, and
  other forces while territorial control repeatedly shifted. Grain requisitions and economic
  disruption provoked rural resistance. The settlement with Poland left western Ukrainian lands
  outside Soviet Ukraine. Although formally sovereign, the republic's military and administrative
  institutions were subordinated to Soviet Russia. It became one of four founding republics of
  the USSR in December 1922.
