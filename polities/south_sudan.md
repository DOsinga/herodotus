description: >-
  South Sudan became independent from Sudan in 2011 after decades of civil war, a peace agreement
  and a referendum supporting separation. This landlocked African republic is centred on Juba and
  the White Nile, with extensive wetlands in the Sudd and communities speaking many languages.
  Its transitional system provides for a president, legislature and state governments.
  Independence brought international recognition but left disputes over borders, oil revenues and
  relations with Sudan unresolved. Rivalries within the former liberation movement escalated into
  civil war in 2013, causing widespread displacement, hunger and destruction. Peace agreements
  created arrangements for sharing power, though their implementation has remained difficult. Oil
  dominates state revenue, while most people rely on farming or livestock. The republic continues
  despite these challenges.
