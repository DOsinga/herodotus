description: >-
  Vulci was one of the twelve leading cities of the Etruscans, on a plateau above the Fiora river
  in present northern Lazio, Italy. It controlled a wide coastal territory and grew rich from
  farming, metalworking and trade with Greece and the eastern Mediterranean. Its tombs have
  yielded thousands of imported Greek painted vases and the wall paintings of the François Tomb,
  which show Etruscan heroes and legends, including the Vulcian hero Mastarna, whom some identify
  with a king of Rome. Bronze workshops at Vulci exported vessels across Italy and beyond. As Rome
  expanded northward, Vulci fought alongside Volsinii, and after its defeat in 280 BCE it lost
  much of its coast and became a Roman ally.
