description: >-
  Northern Han was a small imperial state in Shanxi, northern China, ruled from Taiyuan
  between 951 and 979. Liu Min founded it after the Later Han government at Kaifeng was
  replaced by Later Zhou. He claimed continuity with that fallen dynasty and maintained a
  separate court in its northern stronghold. Northern Han relied on support from the
  Khitan Liao empire, sending tribute without surrendering its own government. Its
  location between Liao territory and the expanding Chinese dynasties made it a persistent
  obstacle to reunification. Song armies repeatedly attacked the state before Emperor
  Taizong mounted a decisive campaign in 979. Liao relief efforts failed, Taiyuan endured
  a prolonged siege, and the last ruler surrendered. Song conquest ended the remaining Han
  court.
