description: >-
  The Early Lê government ruled Đại Cồ Việt in northern Vietnam from 980 to 1009, with its court
  at Hoa Lư. Lê Hoàn took the throne during a crisis following the assassination of the Đinh
  emperor, when a child ruler faced invasion from Song China. His forces resisted the invasion in
  981, and subsequent diplomacy restored tribute without replacing Vietnamese government. He also
  campaigned against Champa and encouraged agriculture, transport, and production at home. The
  monarchy drew support from military officers, court officials, and Buddhist religious life. Lê
  Hoàn's death in 1005 brought fighting among his sons, followed by the reign of Lê Long Đĩnh.
  After that ruler died, Lý Công Uẩn's accession in 1009 established the successor Lý regime.
