description: >-
  Tanganyika was an independent East African state from 1961 to 1964, covering the
  mainland of present day Tanzania. Its capital was Dar es Salaam. Formerly a
  British administered trust territory, it gained independence on 9 December 1961 as
  a Commonwealth monarchy with Elizabeth II as queen. Julius Nyerere and the
  Tanganyika African National Union led the new government. A constitution adopted
  in 1962 replaced the monarchy with a republic and made Nyerere president,
  concentrating authority in the executive. The country inherited borders and
  institutions shaped by German and British colonial rule. On 26 April 1964,
  Tanganyika united with Zanzibar to form a new republic, soon named Tanzania. Its
  territory became the mainland component of that continuing union rather than
  disappearing.
