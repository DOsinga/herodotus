description: >-
  Frankfurt was a council governed city on the Main in central Germany,
  represented from 1250 until 1806 and again from 1815 to 1866. It formally
  became a free imperial city in 1372, answering directly to the emperor rather
  than a territorial prince. Trade fairs, banking, and commerce made its small
  territory influential beyond its borders. The city also hosted imperial
  elections and later coronations. Napoleonic reorganisation ended its
  independence in 1806 and placed it under Karl Theodor von Dalberg's rule.
  Restored after the Napoleonic wars, Frankfurt joined the German Confederation
  and housed its federal assembly. In 1848 it hosted the parliament that
  attempted German unification. Prussia occupied and annexed the city in 1866,
  ending its separate government and republican institutions.
