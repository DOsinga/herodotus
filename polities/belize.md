description: >-
  Belize is an independent country on Central America's Caribbean coast, bordering Mexico
  and Guatemala. It gained independence from Britain in 1981 after a long period as British
  Honduras. Its government is a parliamentary constitutional monarchy, with a prime minister
  directing elected government and a governor general representing the monarch. Belmopan
  serves as the capital, while Belize City remains a commercial center. English is the
  official language, but Creole, Spanish, and Maya languages reflect its diverse population.
  Maya communities, European settlement, African ancestry, and Garifuna migration all shaped
  the country. Guatemala's territorial claim complicated independence and continues to
  affect diplomatic relations. Tourism, farming, and coastal resources support its economy,
  while the barrier reef gives Belize a distinctive place in Caribbean conservation.
