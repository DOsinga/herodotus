description: >-
  Edom was an Iron Age kingdom centred on the highlands south of the Dead Sea, especially in
  present day southern Jordan. Its independent period is represented here approximately from
  1000 to 553 BCE. Kings ruled from a territory associated with Bozrah, while settlements,
  inscriptions, and copper production provide archaeological evidence alongside biblical and
  imperial accounts. Edomite religion included the god Qos, and its language belonged to the
  related languages of the southern Levant. Relations with Judah ranged from conflict to
  contact, while Assyrian expansion brought tribute obligations and wider imperial
  pressures. The kingdom's chronology and early development remain debated. Nabonidus'
  Babylonian campaign ended its government. Edomite communities continued farther west in
  southern Judah, where the later region was called Idumea.
