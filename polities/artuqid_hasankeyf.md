description: >-
  The Artuqids of Hasankeyf were a Turkmen dynasty that governed the upper Tigris valley in
  what is now southeastern Turkey from 1102 to 1232. Their founder Sökmen, a son of Artuk
  Bey, had held Jerusalem for the Seljuks until the Fatimids took it in 1098, and then won
  the fortress town of Hisn Kayfa on the Tigris. His descendants added Amid, the modern
  Diyarbakır, and for a time Harput, which became the seat of a separate branch. The emirs
  fought the Crusader County of Edessa and later acknowledged Zengid and Ayyubid overlords
  while keeping their own government. They built the great bridge over the Tigris at
  Hasankeyf. In 1232 the Ayyubid al-Kamil took
  their lands, and a branch of his family ruled Hasankeyf afterwards.
