description: >-
  The Khasa Kingdom, or Khasa Malla kingdom, was a medieval Himalayan state in the Karnali
  basin of western Nepal, with lands in Kumaon and western Tibet. Its capital was in the Sinja
  valley. Tradition says it was founded by Nagaraja, who came from the Ngari region of Tibet,
  around the eleventh or twelfth century, and the ruling family may have been of Tibetan
  origin. The earliest inscription, of King Krachalla, dates from 1223. Its rulers raided as
  far as the plains, and Ashoka Challa left inscriptions at Bodh Gaya in the 1250s and 1270s.
  Ripu Malla visited Lumbini in 1312. Under Prithvi Malla in the mid-fourteenth century the
  kingdom reached from Guge and Purang to Dullu. It broke up late in that century into the
  Baise states.
