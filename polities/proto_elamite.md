description: >-
  Proto-Elamite names an archaeological period in southwestern Iran and neighbouring parts of the
  Iranian plateau, represented here from approximately 3200 to 2700 BCE. Susa is its reference
  point, with evidence also found at Anshan and other settlements. Clay tablets, numerical
  systems, and cylinder seals reveal organised administration concerned with goods, livestock,
  and labour. The writing remains incompletely deciphered, so the language, rulers, and political
  relationships between sites cannot be reconstructed. Shared accounting practices do not prove
  that all these communities formed one state. Buildings, pottery, and craft remains show links
  with regional developments after the Uruk period. The name refers to a stage before later Elam,
  but a linguistic or political continuity remains uncertain rather than an established dynastic
  succession.
