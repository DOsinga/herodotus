description: >-
  The Ziyarid Emirate ruled parts of northern Iran, especially Gorgan and Tabaristan along the
  southern Caspian Sea. Mardavij founded its government in 931, briefly conquering much wider
  territories and seeking to revive Iranian royal traditions. After his assassination, his
  brother Vushmgir struggled with the Buyids, often relying on Samanid support. The dynasty's
  holdings contracted, but native rulers continued to govern their Caspian lands. Buyid
  occupation drove Qabus into exile in 980; he regained power in 998. His court supported
  scholars and Persian culture, and his monumental tomb tower still stands near Gorgan. Later
  rulers accepted Ghaznavid and Seljuk overlordship without immediately losing their government.
  Nizari expansion conventionally marks its end in 1090, after the loss of its broader
  territorial ambitions.
