description: >-
  The Khandesh Sultanate governed the Tapti valley of central India from 1382 to 1601. Its Faruqi rulers developed a monarchy between Gujarat, Malwa and the Deccan, drawing wealth from agriculture, trade and textile production. Founder Malik Raja had received lands from the Delhi sultan before establishing his own government. His successors held the strong fortress of Asirgarh and built Burhanpur into an important capital and commercial city. The sultanate fought and negotiated with neighbouring states, sometimes acknowledging their supremacy while retaining its own court and army. Succession disputes repeatedly invited outside intervention. Mughal expansion eventually overwhelmed it: Akbar occupied Burhanpur, and Asirgarh fell after a prolonged siege in 1601. Khandesh then became a Mughal province rather than a separate sultanate.
