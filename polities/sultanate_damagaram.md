description: >-
  The Sultanate of Damagaram was a Muslim state in what is now southeastern Niger, centred on
  the city of Zinder. It was founded around 1731 by a Kanuri ruling family as a vassal of
  Bornu, and became the strongest power between Bornu and the Hausa emirates of the Sokoto
  Caliphate. Its sultans governed through titled officials and moved their seat to Zinder in
  the 1730s. Damagaram profited from the caravan route across the Sahara to Tripoli, from the
  trade in captives and from its own muskets and cannon. In the nineteenth century, under
  rulers such as Tanimoun, it conquered neighbouring states and slipped free of Bornu's
  authority. A French column took Zinder in 1899, and the sultanate became part of the colony
  of Niger.
