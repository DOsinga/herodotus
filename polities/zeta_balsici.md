description: >-
  Zeta under the Balšići was a lordship around the southeastern Adriatic, including
  parts of present Montenegro and northern Albania. The family's territory emerged
  during the weakening of the Serbian Empire and is recorded as independently governed
  from 1371. Its rulers sought control of coastal towns, inland districts and trade
  routes, with Shkodër among their principal centres. Their fortunes depended on
  struggles with neighbouring Serbian lords, Venice and advancing Ottoman forces. Balša
  II's defeat and death at Savra in 1385 sharply reduced the family's power. Later
  rulers fought repeated wars over coastal possessions. Balša III ruled with strong
  advice from his mother Jelena, maintaining Orthodox Christianity while tolerating
  Catholics. In 1421, he transferred Zeta to his uncle, Serbian despot Stefan
  Lazarević.
