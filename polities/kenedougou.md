description: >-
  The Kénédougou Kingdom was a Senufo state in what is now southeastern Mali, with lands
  reaching into northern Ivory Coast and western Burkina Faso. It was founded around 1650 by
  Traoré rulers of Dyula origin, and for most of its history it was a modest kingdom with its
  seat at Bougoula. Under the faama Tieba Traoré, who reigned from the 1870s, it expanded and
  moved its capital to Sikasso, which he surrounded with massive earthen walls. Tieba
  withstood a long siege by Samori Touré's army in 1887 and 1888 and allied with the French
  against him. His brother and successor Babemba refused French demands, and a French column
  stormed Sikasso on 1 May 1898, ending the kingdom. Babemba chose to die rather than be
  captured.
