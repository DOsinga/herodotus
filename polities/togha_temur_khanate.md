description: >-
  Togha Temür was a descendant of Genghis Khan's brother Qasar whose family led the Chete nomads
  in the plain of Gorgan, southeast of the Caspian. After the Ilkhan Abu Sa'id died in 1335, the
  governor of Khorasan proposed him as Ilkhan, and most of the princes of eastern Iran accepted
  him. His campaigns to win western Iran failed against the Jalayirid Hasan Buzurg, but from his
  camps around Astarabad and Gorgan he remained overlord of western Khorasan and Mazandaran for
  nearly twenty years. His commander Arghun Shah of the Jauni Qurban held Tus, while the
  Sarbadars of Sabzevar and the Kartids of Herat recognised him when it suited them. In December
  1353 the Sarbadar ruler had him murdered and annexed Astarabad.
