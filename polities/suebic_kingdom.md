description: >-
  The Kingdom of the Suebi arose after Suebi groups entered Roman Hispania in 409 and settled in Gallaecia, principally modern
  Galicia and northern Portugal. Their kings ruled from Braga and built one of the earliest post-Roman kingdoms in western Europe.
  Early rulers expanded beyond their core lands, but Visigothic military pressure curtailed those ambitions. Written accounts become sparse during parts
  of the fifth century, making some royal successions uncertain. Religious allegiance changed between paganism, Catholic Christianity and Arian Christianity before
  Catholicism became established under later kings. Bishops and church councils, including those at Braga, played an important role in the
  realm. In 585 the Visigothic king Leovigild conquered the kingdom and suppressed Malaric's resistance, incorporating its territory into his state.
