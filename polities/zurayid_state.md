description: >-
  The Zurayid State was ruled from the port of Aden in southern Yemen by an Ismaili family of
  the Hamdan tribe. The Sulayhids installed the brothers al-Abbas and al-Mas'ud, sons of al-
  Karam, at Aden in 1083 as tribute-paying governors, and their descendants gradually stopped
  paying. From the death of Queen Arwa in 1138 they governed on their own. That year Saba ibn
  Abi Saud drove a rival branch of the family out of Aden, and his son Muhammad ibn Saba bought
  old Sulayhid fortresses in the highlands, among them Dhu Jibla and al-Takar. The Zurayids
  followed the Hafizi Fatimid line, and Aden's trade with Egypt, Iraq, India and China paid for
  their rule. They beat off the Mahdids, but the Ayyubids took Aden in June 1174.
