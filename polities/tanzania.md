description: >-
  Tanzania is an East African republic formed in 1964 by the union of Tanganyika and
  Zanzibar. The mainland had gained independence from Britain in 1961; Zanzibar followed
  in 1963 before its revolution and union. Julius Nyerere, the first union president,
  promoted African socialism and village-based development through the ujamaa programme.
  Swahili helped connect the country's language communities. The union has a president and
  national parliament, while Zanzibar retains its own government for matters outside union
  responsibilities. Single-party rule gave way to multiparty politics in 1992, though the
  ruling party retained its dominance. Economic policies also shifted away from state-led
  socialism. Dar es Salaam was the capital until 1996, when Dodoma assumed that role. The
  union remains the same continuing state.
