description: >-
  The Free City of Danzig was a Baltic city-state around present day Gdansk, Poland,
  from 1920 to 1939. The Versailles settlement separated its largely German population
  from Germany while securing Polish access to the port. A republican constitution
  established an elected assembly and a governing senate under League of Nations
  supervision. Poland held special rights over customs, transport, harbor facilities,
  and external affairs, creating frequent disputes with local authorities. Trade linked
  the city to the Polish hinterland, although the developing port of Gdynia became a
  competitor. Nazi politicians gained control during the 1930s, undermining
  constitutional safeguards and persecuting opponents and minorities. Germany abolished
  the Free City when it invaded Poland in September 1939, incorporating the territory
  into the Nazi state.
