description: >-
  The Durrani Empire was founded in 1747 by Ahmad Shah after Nader Shah's death, with its
  first capital at Kandahar. Built around Afghan tribal support, its monarchy expanded
  across present Afghanistan, much of Pakistan and parts of Iran and northern India. Ahmad
  Shah's campaigns culminated in victory over the Marathas at Panipat in 1761, but retaining
  distant conquests proved difficult. Royal authority depended on balancing powerful chiefs
  and distributing resources from war and taxation. His successor Timur Shah moved the court
  to Kabul. Later struggles among royal brothers and growing Sikh power weakened the empire
  and stripped away eastern possessions. This record ends in 1823, when Durrani rule at
  Kabul gave way to the Barakzai government that became Afghanistan's emirate.
