description: >-
  The Colla Kingdom was one of the Aymara-speaking polities of the Lake Titicaca basin that
  arose after the decline of Tiwanaku, occupying the northwestern side of the lake with its
  centre at Hatun Colla. Covering some twenty thousand square kilometres, it was among the
  largest of the altiplano chiefdoms, and its people may have spoken Puquina and claimed
  descent from Tiwanaku. Spanish chronicles describe a powerful king ruling from Hatun
  Colla, but archaeological surveys show a fragmented landscape of hilltop forts called
  pukaras, suggesting many lineages under a paramount lord. The Colla fought their southern
  rivals the Lupaca. The Inca ruler Pachacuti conquered them in the mid fifteenth century,
  and the Incas later named the whole southeastern quarter of their empire Collasuyu after
  them.
