description: >-
  Numidia was a North African kingdom centred on Cirta, in present-day Algeria, united by Masinissa in 202 BCE and annexed by Rome in 46 BCE. Masinissa combined rival Numidian territories after assisting Rome against Carthage during the Second Punic War. His troops were important allies, while his long reign encouraged agriculture and enlarged the kingdom at Carthage's expense. Hereditary kings governed a population with local traditions and connections to the wider Mediterranean. Succession disputes later drew Roman intervention, most famously during Jugurtha's war against Rome. Subsequent rulers remained involved in Roman politics. Juba I supported Caesar's opponents in the civil war. After their defeat at Thapsus, Caesar annexed the kingdom, ending its independence and incorporating territory into Roman provincial rule.
