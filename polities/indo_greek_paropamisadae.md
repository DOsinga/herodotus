wikipedia: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hermaeus
description: >-
  This Indo-Greek kingdom occupied the Paropamisadae around the Hindu Kush and Kabul
  valley during the early first century BCE. Alexandria in the Caucasus serves as its
  representative centre. Its history is reconstructed chiefly from coins, so the dates
  around 100 to 70 BCE and the sequence of rulers remain estimates. Hermaeus, its last
  known Greek king, issued silver and bronze money combining Greek royal portraits and
  deities with inscriptions in Kharoshthi. Coins also name his queen Kalliope, giving rare
  evidence of the royal household. His rule ended when incoming peoples took the region,
  identified by different scholars as Yuezhi or Indo-Scythians. Later rulers copied his
  coinage, preserving its prestige without proving that the original Greek monarchy
  continued governing the area.
