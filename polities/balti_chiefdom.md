description: >-
  The Balti chiefs governed the valleys of Baltistan, on the upper Indus around Skardu,
  Shigar and Khaplu, after Tibetan imperial rule over the region ended in the ninth century.
  Baltistan, known as Little Tibet, had come under the Tibetan kings in the late seventh
  century, and its people, of mixed Tibetan and Dardic descent, followed Bon and Tibetan
  Buddhism. When the empire collapsed after 842, no outside power took its place, and the
  valleys were ruled by local chiefs from their own forts, often at war with each other.
  Little is known of individual rulers before the Maqpon family, who according to tradition
  rose at Skardu in the twelfth century and built the kingdom that later united most of
  Baltistan.
