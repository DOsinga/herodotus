description: >-
  The Tungan state of Ürümqi was the government of the Hui rebels in eastern Dzungaria during
  the Dungan revolt, lasting from 1864 to 1870. The Hui soldiers of the Ürümqi garrison rose
  in June 1864, led by Tuo Ming, a New Teaching cleric from Gansu also known as Daud Khalifa,
  and the officer Suo Huanzhang. They took Manas and Wusu, and with help from the rebels of
  Kucha they stormed the Manchu fortress of Ürümqi in October, where the commander blew
  himself up. Tuo Ming took the title Pure and True King and extended his rule to Turpan and
  the oases along the northern foot of the Tianshan. Yaqub Beg declared holy war against him
  and took Ürümqi in 1870.
