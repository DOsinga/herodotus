description: >-
  Tortuguero was a Classic Maya kingdom in what is now southern Tabasco, Mexico, with an
  estimated royal history from 500 to 750. Its rulers used the Baakal emblem title also
  associated with Palenque, but their court pursued its own wars and political ambitions.
  Inscriptions provide the clearest evidence for Bahlam Ajaw, who ruled from 644 to 679. He
  celebrated military victories, including the conquest of Comalcalco in 649, on carved
  monuments. Monument 6 also connects local royal events to a distant calendar ending, later
  widely misrepresented as a prophecy about 2012. Modern quarrying, looting, and a cement
  factory badly damaged the city. These losses leave its earlier succession, territorial
  reach, and final decline less certain than its recorded seventh-century campaigns.
