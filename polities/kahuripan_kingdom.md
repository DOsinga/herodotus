name: "Kahuripan Kingdom"
description: >-
  Kahuripan was Airlangga's kingdom in East Java, founded in 1019 after the collapse of the earlier
  Mataram court. Airlangga gathered support from surviving officials and local rulers, reunited
  territories and established his capital near the mouth of the Brantas River. Its location is lost,
  so the map point represents the proposed capital region. The monarchy supported both Hindu and
  Buddhist worship, restored agriculture and encouraged commerce through river and coastal
  connections. Airlangga's reign brought peace with Srivijaya after earlier conflict between the two
  political centers. Before retiring from royal government, he divided the realm between two sons into
  Janggala and Panjalu, later known as Kediri. This record follows the 1042 partition chronology,
  although the linked article instead dates it 1045.
