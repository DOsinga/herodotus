  Ethiopia is a state in the Horn of Africa, represented here from the military
  overthrow of Emperor Haile Selassie in 1974. Addis Ababa remained its capital through
  successive regimes. The Derg abolished the monarchy, pursued nationalization and land
  reform, and established Marxist rule under Mengistu Haile Mariam. Repression, civil
  war, conflict with Somalia, and devastating famine marked this period. A People's
  Democratic Republic replaced military rule in 1987 without ending Mengistu's power.
  Rebel forces overthrew his government in 1991, leading to a transitional
  administration and the federal constitution of 1995. Eritrea's independence made
  Ethiopia landlocked. The federal republic organized regional governments largely
  around language and ethnicity, while later conflicts exposed deep disputes over
  authority and identity within the continuing state.
