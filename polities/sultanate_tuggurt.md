description: >-
  The Sultanate of Tuggurt was a Saharan state in the Oued Righ valley of southeastern
  Algeria, ruled from the oasis town of Touggourt by the Berber dynasty of the Banu
  Djellab. It took shape in the early fifteenth century and controlled the palm groves and
  oases of the valley, which lay on caravan routes between the Mediterranean and the
  Sahara. The Regency of Algiers sent expeditions against it, beginning in 1552, and at
  times forced it to pay tribute, but the sultans kept governing their own oases. Rivalries
  with Ouargla and the nomad confederations of the region shaped its politics. French
  columns took Touggourt in 1854 and placed the oasis under a ruler of their own choosing,
  and the sultanate was formally abolished in 1881.
