description: >-
  Avanti was an ancient kingdom in central India, corresponding broadly to Malwa and parts of the
  Narmada valley. Its independent period is represented here approximately from 700 to 400 BCE.
  Ujjayini, modern Ujjain, was the northern centre, while Mahishmati served the southern region
  across the Vindhya mountains. Buddhist texts list Avanti among the sixteen great realms of
  early historic India. Kings governed the country, although traditions about its earliest
  dynasties mix history and legend. Pradyota became a rival of neighbouring rulers, and stories
  connect his family with Vatsa. Buddhist and Jain accounts preserve competing memories of royal
  power and religious patronage. Shishunaga of Magadha eventually conquered Avanti; the endpoint
  is estimated, and Ujjayini continued as an important provincial centre afterward.
