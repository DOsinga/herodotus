description: >-
  The Central African Republic is a landlocked state in central Africa, independent from France since 1960, with Bangui as its capital. It developed from the colonial territory of Ubangi-Shari, where forced labour and concession companies shaped French rule. David Dacko became its first president, but military coups repeatedly changed the government. Jean-Bédel Bokassa briefly renamed the country an empire before his overthrow in 1979 restored the republic. These changes are treated here as regimes within one continuing state. Its political institutions include a presidency and legislature. Diamonds, timber, and farming are important to the economy, yet poor infrastructure and armed conflict have limited development. Rebellions, communal violence, and foreign interventions have displaced people and weakened government authority outside the capital.
