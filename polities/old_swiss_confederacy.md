description: >-
  The Old Swiss Confederacy was a loose alliance of Alpine communities and towns that
  developed from 1291 until the French invasion of 1798. Its early core included Uri,
  Schwyz, and Unterwalden; later members included Zurich and Bern. The cantons
  governed themselves through differing systems, from rural assemblies to urban
  councils, and sent delegates to a common diet. There was no permanent national
  capital. Military victories against Habsburg and Burgundian forces strengthened the
  alliance, which reached thirteen full cantons by 1513. Shared subject territories
  and allied communities extended its influence beyond those members. The Reformation
  divided Catholic and Protestant cantons without dissolving their union. Recognition
  of independence came in 1648. French conquest replaced the confederacy with the more
  centralised Helvetic Republic.
