description: >-
  Ek Balam was a Maya royal centre in northern Yucatán, Mexico, represented here by an estimated
  kingdom lasting from about 500 to 1100. Its court is best documented between 770 and 840, when
  rulers sponsored major buildings and inscriptions. The city was associated with the kingdom
  called Talol in later colonial accounts. King Ukit Kan Le'k Tok' commissioned an elaborate
  Acropolis complex, where his tomb and finely preserved stucco sculpture reveal the wealth of the
  ruling elite. Roads, palaces, a ballcourt and defensive walls formed its urban core. Archaeology
  shows a decline in population and construction after its Classic peak, although people continued
  living there. The approximate endpoint describes the fading royal and urban centre, not the
  settlement's final abandonment.
