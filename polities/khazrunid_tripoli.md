description: >-
  The Banu Khazrun were a family of the Zenata Berber Maghrawa who ruled Tripoli and its
  region, in present Libya, from 1001 to 1146. Fulful ibn Said broke away from the Zirid emirs
  of Ifriqiya around 1001 and fought them for years, seeking help from the Fatimids in Cairo
  and the Umayyads in Córdoba. His successors moved between full independence and nominal
  recognition of the Zirids or the Fatimids, playing one against the other. The Zirids
  formally ceded Tripoli to the family in 1022. Al-Muntasir ibn Khazrun held the city through
  the arrival of the Banu Hilal and later raided the Hammadid lands to the west. In 1146 a
  famine led the people to expel the family, and Roger II of Sicily took Tripoli.
