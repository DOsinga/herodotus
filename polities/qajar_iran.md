description: >-
  Qajar Iran was the Iranian state ruled from Tehran by the Qajar royal house between
  1789 and 1925. Agha Mohammad Khan reunified much of Iran after prolonged struggles
  among regional rulers and restored its claims in the Caucasus. His successors lost
  major Caucasian territories to Russia through wars and treaties, while British
  pressure limited Iranian ambitions in Afghanistan. The shah governed through
  ministers, provincial officials, and powerful local interests, with Shia Islam
  central to public life. Foreign concessions and financial dependence provoked
  opposition. The Constitutional Revolution of 1906 established a parliament and
  challenged absolute monarchy, though conflict and intervention weakened the new
  system. After a military coup and growing personal power, Reza Khan replaced the
  Qajars with the Pahlavi monarchy.
