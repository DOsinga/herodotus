description: >-
  The kingdom centred on Kath governed the irrigated lower Amu Darya oasis of Khwarazm until 995.
  Its traditional beginning in 305 comes from al-Biruni's account of Afrig and should not be
  treated as a securely established foundation date. Native rulers used the title Khwarazmshah,
  preserving local government and a distinct Iranian language through changing relations with
  larger empires. Fortresses, settlements, and coins supplement a written history whose early
  centuries remain obscure. Arab campaigns reached the oasis in the eighth century, but local
  shahs recovered authority. The dynasty gradually adopted Islam while older religious practices
  persisted among the population. Rivalry with Gurganj eventually proved decisive: Mamun captured
  Kath in 995, displaced its ruling line, and united Khwarazm under the Mamunid government.
