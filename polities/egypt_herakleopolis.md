description: >-
  The kings conventionally grouped into Egypt's Ninth and Tenth dynasties governed from
  Herakleopolis during the First Intermediate Period, approximately 2160 to 2040 BCE. Their
  authority covered Middle and Lower Egypt rather than a united country. Regional governors and
  competing royal courts had gained power after the decline of the Old Kingdom. The
  Herakleopolitan rulers claimed pharaonic authority, but their succession is poorly known:
  damaged king lists and scattered inscriptions leave many names uncertain. Several kings called
  Khety are attested, and later Egyptian literature preserved memories of this troubled age.
  Their main rivals were the rulers of Thebes in Upper Egypt. Fighting between these centres
  ended with Mentuhotep II's victory and reunification, which established the Middle Kingdom
  under the Theban monarchy.
