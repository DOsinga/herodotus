description: >-
  The Petexbatún Kingdom was a Maya monarchy in northern Guatemala, established by a branch of
  Tikal's royal family in 629. Dos Pilas and nearby Aguateca became twin seats of one dynasty,
  strategically placed near river routes through the Petexbatún region. Its first ruler, B'alaj
  Chan K'awiil, initially served Tikal but later aligned with Calakmul, turning his kingdom into
  an active participant in their rivalry. Military victories brought neighbouring centres under
  its influence, while marriages linked its court with other Maya dynasties. Rebellion and
  warfare drove the royal court from Dos Pilas in 761. Government continued at Aguateca,
  protected by steep terrain and defensive walls. A destructive attack around 810 ended the
  surviving court; the precise date remains an archaeological estimate.
