description: >-
  Dian was a kingdom around Dian Lake in present Yunnan, southwestern China.
  This record begins in 279 BCE, the traditional date when the Chu
  general Zhuang Qiao established a kingdom after being cut off from his
  homeland. Archaeology shows that Dian society and its metalworking traditions were older.
  Kings ruled settled agricultural communities, with elite burials near Jinning providing evidence
  of their authority. Bronze drums, weapons, and decorated cowrie containers depict farming,
  warfare, sacrifice, and courtly life, revealing a society connected to neighbouring cultures.
  Han expansion reached Dian in 109 BCE, when its ruler submitted and
  the Yizhou commandery was established. The king received an imperial seal and
  retained a title, but Dian's territory now formed part of Han government.
