description: >-
  The Heruli were a Germanic people of late antiquity, and their best recorded group built a
  kingdom north of the middle Danube, in or near present Lower Austria and southern Moravia,
  beside the Rugians. It was one of the kingdoms that emerged when the Hunnic empire fell apart
  after the battle of Nedao in 454. Its kings, the last of whom was Rodulf, led a warrior elite
  that raided its neighbours and took tribute from them, and Heruli also served as soldiers under
  Odoacer and the Romans. After the Ostrogoths left Pannonia in 473 the Herulian kingdom was the
  strongest power on this stretch of the Danube. Around 508 the Lombards defeated Rodulf and
  destroyed it; some Heruli moved to Roman territory near Belgrade and others went north to
  Scandinavia.
