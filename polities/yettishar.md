description: >-
  Yettishar was a Muslim state in the Tarim Basin, centred on Kashgar and lasting from 1864
  to 1877. Its name meant Seven Cities, referring to oasis towns across what is now southern
  Xinjiang. It emerged amid rebellion against Qing rule, and the Kokandi commander Yakub Beg
  consolidated an Islamic monarchy through conquest and political manoeuvring. His
  government drew soldiers and officials from Central Asia while ruling a largely local
  population. Russia and Britain sought commercial and diplomatic influence, and Ottoman
  recognition strengthened his standing without transferring everyday government to
  Istanbul. Qing forces under Zuo Zongtang advanced back into the region as Yakub Beg's
  regime weakened. His death accelerated its collapse, and the Qing occupation of Kashgar
  ended it in 1877.
