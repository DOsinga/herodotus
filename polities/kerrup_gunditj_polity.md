description: >-
  The Gunditjmara are an Aboriginal people of southwestern Victoria, Australia, whose country includes
  the volcanic landscape around Budj Bim and Tae Rak. This cultural record begins around 4000 BCE, an
  estimate tied to the long history of eel aquaculture rather than a documented kingdom. Channels,
  weirs, ponds and traps show sustained management of water and food resources, associated with stone
  dwellings and custodial knowledge. Local communities maintained relationships to places through
  kinship, law and ceremony; the evidence does not establish one centralized state. British settlement
  brought dispossession and violent resistance during the Eumeralla conflict. The estimated endpoint
  of 1846 marks loss of control under colonial occupation, not the disappearance of the Gunditjmara,
  whose communities, traditions and connection to country continue.
