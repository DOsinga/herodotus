description: >-
  The Qara Qoyunlu, or Black Sheep Turkomans, built a confederate monarchy across eastern
  Anatolia, Azerbaijan, Iraq, and western Iran between 1374 and 1468. Their rulers combined
  leadership of mobile tribal groups with government over cities, eventually making Tabriz
  their principal capital. Originally connected to the Jalayirids, they displaced their
  former patrons and took Baghdad. Qara Yusuf restored their strength after Timur's
  campaigns, while later rulers faced repeated Timurid invasions and disputes within the
  ruling family. Jahan Shah expanded deep into Iran and briefly occupied Herat. His court
  supported poetry and architecture, including Tabriz's Blue Mosque. Rivalry with the Aq
  Qoyunlu proved decisive: Uzun Hasan defeated and killed Jahan Shah in 1467, and conquered
  the regime the following year in 1468.
