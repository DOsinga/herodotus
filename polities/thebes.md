description: >-
  Thebes was a Greek city-state in Boeotia, represented here from about 700 to 335 BCE
  together with its leading role in the Boeotian League. Its institutions changed over
  time, from oligarchic rule to democracy after a Spartan garrison was expelled. The city
  competed with Athens and Sparta, sometimes choosing alliances that later damaged its
  standing, including support for Persia during the invasion of Greece. Under Epaminondas
  and Pelopidas, Theban armies defeated Sparta at Leuctra in 371 BCE and briefly dominated
  Greek politics. The Sacred Band became a renowned elite unit. Macedonian victories later
  overturned that power. When Thebes rebelled against Alexander in 335 BCE, he captured
  and destroyed the city, ending the independent political history covered by this
  particular record.
