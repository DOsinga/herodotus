description: >-
  Thebes was a Greek city-state in Boeotia, represented here from about 700 BCE together
  with its leading role in the Boeotian League. Its institutions changed over time, from
  oligarchic rule to democracy after a Spartan garrison was expelled. The city competed with
  Athens and Sparta, sometimes choosing alliances that later damaged its standing, including
  support for Persia during the invasion of Greece. Under Epaminondas and Pelopidas, Theban
  armies defeated Sparta at Leuctra in 371 BCE and briefly dominated Greek politics. The
  Sacred Band became a renowned elite unit. When Thebes rebelled against Alexander in 335
  BCE, he destroyed the city. Cassander rebuilt it, and after Macedonian control ended in
  197 BCE it governed itself again until the Roman conquest of Greece in 146 BCE.
