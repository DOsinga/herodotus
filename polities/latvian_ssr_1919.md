description: >-
  The Latvian Socialist Soviet Republic was a Bolshevik state proclaimed in Latvia in December
  1918, during the Latvian War of Independence, with the political, financial and military
  backing of Lenin's government in Moscow. Its head of government was Pēteris Stučka. Its
  forces, the Red Latvian Riflemen and other Red Army units, took Riga in January 1919 and
  most of the country, pushing Kārlis Ulmanis's provisional government back to Liepāja.
  Stučka's government nationalised land and property, and when food ran short in the towns it
  turned to revolutionary tribunals and terror. In the spring the Ulmanis government and
  German Freikorps counter-attacked and retook Riga in May 1919. The republic held out in
  Latgale until Latvian and Polish forces took Daugavpils in January 1920.
