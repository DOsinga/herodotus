description: >-
  The First Brazilian Republic governed Brazil from the overthrow of Emperor Pedro II in
  1889 to the revolution of 1930. Based in Rio de Janeiro, it adopted a federal
  constitution, an elected presidency and a congress. Military leaders controlled its first
  years before civilian presidents took office. In practice, state elites and rural
  landowners dominated elections through patronage, pressure and fraud. São Paulo's coffee
  interests and the political strength of Minas Gerais shaped national leadership. Export
  agriculture remained central despite growing cities, immigration and industrial activity.
  The government confronted naval mutinies, regional civil war, rural religious movements
  and army revolts. Economic crisis and a disputed succession helped unite opponents in
  1930, bringing Getúlio Vargas to power and ending the regime.
