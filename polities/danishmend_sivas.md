description: >-
  The Danishmendid Beylik of Sivas was a Turkish-ruled principality in central Anatolia,
  represented here from the division of Danishmendid territory in 1142 until Seljuk conquest in
  1175. Yaghibasan established his rule at Sivas after the death of Mehmed, while other branches
  governed lands. Its rulers bore titles such as emir and melik and competed with the Seljuk
  sultans of Rum for cities and influence. Sivas belonged to an Anatolian landscape shaped by
  Turkish settlement, older Christian communities, and shifting military alliances. Pressure from
  Kilij Arslan II encouraged the Sivas rulers to seek support from Nur al-Din. His death removed
  that protection, allowing incorporation into Rum. The branch's exact beginning and conquest
  dates vary between accounts; other Danishmendid governments lasted longer.
