description: >-
  The Arab–Byzantine Condominium of Cyprus was the arrangement under which Cyprus stood between
  the Byzantine Empire and the Caliphate from 688 to 965. Arab fleets had raided and occupied
  the island from the 650s. In 688 the emperor Justinian II and the Umayyad caliph Abd al-Malik
  agreed to share it: the island paid tribute to both and neither kept a permanent garrison
  there. Cyprus was run by its own Christian officials and its church, and its ports served
  ships from both sides despite nearly constant war on the mainland. Each power occupied the
  island for short spells, but the arrangement kept returning for almost three centuries. In
  965 a Byzantine fleet under Niketas Chalkoutzes took Cyprus, which became a regular Byzantine
  province, the Theme of Cyprus.
