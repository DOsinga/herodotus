name: Sikh Confederacy
description: >-
  The Sikh Confederacy was a association of military communities and territorial chiefs in
  Punjab from 1748 to 1799. Its component misls grew as Mughal authority weakened and Afghan
  invasions disrupted the region. Rather than one centralized monarchy, the confederacy
  combined independent leaders with shared religious identity and collective gatherings of
  the Sarbat Khalsa, especially at Amritsar. The Dal Khalsa coordinated forces, and Jassa
  Singh Ahluwalia held a leading military role. Mounted troops helped the misls seize
  territory and collect revenue, including payments for protection. Over time, chiefs built
  separate power bases and fought one another as well as outside enemies. Ranjit Singh's
  capture of Lahore in 1799 marked the rise of the Sikh Empire, gradually replacing
  confederate rule with monarchy.
