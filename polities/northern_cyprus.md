  Northern Cyprus governs the northern part of Cyprus, with its capital in North Nicosia.
  This record begins with the Turkish Federated State established in 1975, following
  Turkey's invasion and the island's division the previous year. In 1983 its leaders
  declared the Turkish Republic of Northern Cyprus. It has an elected president,
  parliament and government, but only Turkey recognises it as an independent country. The
  United Nations and most governments regard its territory as part of the Republic of
  Cyprus. Turkish military protection and financial support remain central to its
  position. Population displacement, disputed property and the divided capital shape its
  politics. Tourism and universities support its economy, while repeated negotiations have
  sought an agreed settlement between the island's two communities.
