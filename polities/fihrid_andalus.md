description: >-
  Fihrid al-Andalus was Muslim Iberia under Yusuf ibn Abd al-Rahman al-Fihri, a descendant of
  Uqba ibn Nafi and a former governor of Narbonne. After the Berber revolt and the wars
  between Arab factions, the leaders of al-Andalus agreed in 747 to rotate the governorship,
  and Yusuf took office from Córdoba. When his term ended he refused to hand over power and
  ruled on his own, while the Umayyad caliphate in Damascus fell to the Abbasids in 750.
  Chroniclers note that he governed more like a king than a governor. He ordered a census for
  taxation and crushed a revolt at Zaragoza in 755. That year the Umayyad prince Abd al-Rahman
  landed in the south, and he defeated Yusuf outside Córdoba in 756.
