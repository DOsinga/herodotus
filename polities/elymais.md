description: >-
  Elymais was a kingdom in southwestern Iran, around the mountains and plains of ancient
  Susiana near the Persian Gulf. It emerged from Seleucid rule about 147 BCE and survived
  until Ardashir of Persis conquered it around 221 CE. Its kings frequently acknowledged
  Parthian overlordship while maintaining a local monarchy. Susa represents the kingdom
  here, although its royal seat is uncertain. Coins provide much of the evidence for its
  rulers, including the Kamnaskirid and later Arsacid lines. Their designs drew on Greek and
  Parthian models, while later issues and rock inscriptions used Aramaic writing. The
  people's spoken language remains unknown. Elymaean sanctuaries and skilled archers feature
  in ancient accounts, but surviving evidence leaves many details of the kingdom's internal
  government obscure.
