description: >-
  The Pervaneoglu beylik was a small Turkish principality on the Black Sea coast of northern
  Anatolia, centred on the port of Sinop. It was founded by Mu'in al-Din Süleyman, the pervane or
  chief minister of the Seljuk sultans of Rum, who dominated the sultanate under Mongol
  overlordship and held Sinop as his own possession after its capture from the Empire of
  Trebizond. The Mongols executed him in 1277, but his son and grandsons kept Sinop and its
  district. The last bey, Gazi Çelebi, built a fleet that raided the Genoese colonies in the
  Crimea and the coasts of Trebizond, making Sinop a naval power for a time. Having no sons, he
  left his lands to the neighbouring Candarids, who took Sinop in the 1320s.
