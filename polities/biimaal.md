description: >-
  The Biimaal were a Somali clan of the Dir who held the port of Merca on the Benadir coast and
  its hinterland in the eighteenth and nineteenth centuries. After the decline of the Ajuran
  Sultanate, a group known as the El Amir took Merca and ruled for some thirty years, until the
  Biimaal drove them out and occupied the town. The clan governed through its elders and religious
  leaders, controlled the trade of the port and was strong enough to defeat the Geledi Sultanate,
  killing Sultan Yusuf Mahamud at Adaddey Suleyman in 1848 and his son Ahmed in 1878. Zanzibar
  placed a garrison in Merca in 1871. The Biimaal later led a long armed resistance against
  Italian rule, from 1896 to 1926.
