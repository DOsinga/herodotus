description: >-
  Isenburg-Limburg was the lordship of a branch of the lords of Isenburg, who ruled the town of
  Limburg on the Lahn and its surroundings from the castle above the cathedral. The lords of
  Isenburg held Limburg from the early thirteenth century, and from 1258 a separate line lived at
  the castle and took its name from the town. Limburg was a thriving market town on the road from
  Cologne to Frankfurt, governed by a council of merchant families under the lords. A daughter of
  the house, Imagina, married Adolf of Nassau, King of the Romans. In 1344 the lords pledged half
  the town to the archbishop of Trier, and when their line died out in 1406 Trier took control of
  Limburg.
