description: >-
  West Francia was the western kingdom created by the Treaty of Verdun in 843, when
  Charles the Bald received part of the divided Carolingian empire. It covered much of
  modern France, though neither its borders nor royal control matched those of the later
  country. Kings travelled between royal centres and relied on bishops, counts and
  aristocratic support. Viking attacks and disputes over succession strengthened regional
  leaders and made effective government increasingly uneven. Carolingian and Robertian
  rulers competed for the royal crown, while many dukes governed their own territories.
  Paris was an important royal and aristocratic centre rather than a permanent capital.
  Hugh Capet's election in 987 marks the transition to the Kingdom of France under the
  new Capetian royal dynasty.
