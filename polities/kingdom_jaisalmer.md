description: >-
  The Kingdom of Jaisalmer was a Rajput state in the Thar Desert of western Rajasthan, India. Its
  rulers, the Bhati clan with the title of Maharawal, moved their capital from Lodhruva to the new
  hill fort of Jaisalmer in 1156 because the old seat was exposed to raids. The kingdom lived from
  the caravan trade between Sindh, Gujarat and the Ganges plain, and its merchants built the carved
  sandstone mansions of the walled town. Delhi sultans besieged the fort more than once, and the
  rulers later served the Mughals as allies while governing their own desert lands. Wars with
  Bikaner and Jodhpur cost it much land in the eighteenth century. A treaty with the East India
  Company in 1818 made Jaisalmer a protected princely state, which it remained until 1947.
