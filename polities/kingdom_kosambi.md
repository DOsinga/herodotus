description: >-
  The kingdom of Kosambi was a monarchy in the old Vatsa country on the Yamuna, ruled from
  Kosambi, one of the great trading cities of ancient India, near modern Prayagraj. After
  the Mauryas and Shungas lost control, the city first issued its own cast copper coins, and
  later coins carry the names of kings ending in -mitra, showing that a local dynasty had
  taken charge. In the first century BCE and the first century CE the Mitras appear to have
  extended their power over Magadha and Pataliputra, and possibly over Kannauj. The Kushans
  held Kosambi for over a century. After them the Magha kings ruled from Kosambi and
  Bandhogarh. Samudragupta annexed the kingdom around the middle of the fourth century and
  recorded his victories on the pillar there.
