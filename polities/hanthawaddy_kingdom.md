description: >-
  Hanthawaddy was a Mon-speaking monarchy in Lower Burma, founded by Wareru in
  1287 as Pagan's authority collapsed. Initially centred on Martaban, it united coastal
  and delta regions whose local rulers competed with the royal court. Pegu
  became its capital. King Razadarit strengthened the kingdom and resisted Ava during
  the Forty Years' War, preserving a separate southern state. Prosperous ports connected
  Hanthawaddy with Indian Ocean commerce, and rulers such as Queen Shin Sawbu
  and Dhammazedi supported Theravada Buddhism and religious links with Sri Lanka. Toungoo
  conquered the kingdom in 1539, despite its greater wealth and manpower. A
  brief revival followed Tabinshwehti's assassination in 1550, largely confined to Pegu. Bayinnaung
  defeated that government in 1552, incorporating its centre into the Toungoo empire.
