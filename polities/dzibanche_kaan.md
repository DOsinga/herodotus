description: >-
  Dzibanché was a Maya city in southern Quintana Roo, Mexico, and the first known capital of the
  Kaan, or Snake, dynasty, which became one of the most powerful royal houses of the Classic Maya.
  From its temples and palaces the Kaan kings built alliances and wars across the southern
  lowlands in the fifth and sixth centuries. Under rulers such as Sky Witness they challenged
  Tikal, and the Kaan dynasty backed Caracol in its victory over Tikal in 562. The city's
  monuments include a staircase carved with captives. In the early seventh century the dynasty
  moved its court to Calakmul, where Yuknoom Ch'een II made it the leading power of the Maya
  world, while Dzibanché declined.
