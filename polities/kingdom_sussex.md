name: "Kingdom of Sussex"
description: >-
  Sussex was the kingdom of the South Saxons on England's southern coast, traditionally founded by
  Ælle in 477. Its early history is poorly documented, and the foundation story comes from later
  chronicles rather than contemporary records. Native kings ruled farming communities between the
  coast and the wooded Weald, sometimes sharing power across different districts. Selsey became an
  important royal and church center after King Æthelwealh supported Wilfrid's mission in the seventh
  century. Rivalry with Wessex and Mercia repeatedly threatened the kingdom. West Saxon conquest
  interrupted separate government in 686; native rule returned before Offa's Mercian annexation in
  772. A further period began after 796, but Sussex finally passed under Wessex in 827 and became part
  of the developing English kingdom.
