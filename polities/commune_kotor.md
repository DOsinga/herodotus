description: >-
  The Commune of Kotor was the self-governing city of Kotor on its fjord in the southern
  Adriatic, from 1391 until it gave itself to Venice in 1420. Kotor had long governed its
  own affairs through a council of nobles and its own statute, first under Serbian kings,
  then under Hungary and from 1385 under King Tvrtko I of Bosnia. After Tvrtko's death in
  1391 the city stood alone as a small republic, with a rector and councils, trading with
  Ragusa and the Serbian hinterland. Its independence was precarious: the Balšić lords of
  Zeta pressed it from the land side, and the city offered itself to Venice several times
  before the Venetians accepted in 1420 and kept it until 1797.
