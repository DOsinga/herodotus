description: >-
  The Batavian Republic governed the Netherlands from 1795 to 1806, replacing the Dutch
  Republic after French troops and Dutch Patriots overthrew the old regime. Its name invoked
  the ancient Batavians, presented as ancestors of Dutch freedom. Republican leaders sought
  to end provincial privilege, expand religious equality, and build a unified national
  government. The constitution of 1798 replaced the old confederation with a unitary state
  and introduced democratic institutions. Internal disputes, coups, and French pressure
  repeatedly changed the governing arrangements. War against Britain damaged trade and
  helped France impose financial and military demands. Despite this dependence, reforms to
  administration and public finance left a lasting legacy. Napoleon ended the republic in
  1806, establishing the Kingdom of Holland under his brother Louis.
