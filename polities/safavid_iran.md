description: >-
  Safavid Iran was a monarchy founded by Shah Ismail I in 1501, governing much of Iran and neighbouring lands until 1736. Emerging from a religious order backed by Qizilbash tribal warriors, it made Twelver Shia Islam the state religion and reshaped Iran's religious identity. Its shahs competed with the Ottoman Empire to the west and Uzbek rulers to the northeast. Abbas I strengthened central government and military forces, promoted silk commerce and established Isfahan as a monumental capital. Persian administration worked alongside a court with strong Turkic and Caucasian connections. Afghan forces captured Isfahan in 1722, leaving Safavid authority weakened. Nader largely restored the realm in the dynasty's name before taking the throne himself in 1736 and founding Afsharid rule.
