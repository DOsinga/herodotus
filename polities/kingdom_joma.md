description: >-
  The Kingdom of Joma was the central share of the Mali Empire's heartland after the empire broke
  up, ruled from Siguiri on the upper Niger in what is now Guinea by Keita mansas. When Mansa
  Mahmud IV died, his sons divided the remnant of Mali between them, and according to the
  historian Yves Person the branch descended from Nyamaghan took the Joma region. Its rulers drove
  Fula and Bamana marauders out of the area, and under Fakaba Mansa they rebuilt the old town of
  Niani around 1720. The mansas of Joma competed with their kinsmen in Kangaba and Hamana for
  leadership of the Mande, but their authority remained limited in scope. Samori Touré's armies
  conquered the region around 1881.
