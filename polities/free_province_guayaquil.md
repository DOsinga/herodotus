description: >-
  The Free Province of Guayaquil was a short-lived state on the coast of present-day
  Ecuador, centred on the port of Guayaquil. On 9 October 1820 local patriots and officers
  of the garrison overthrew the Spanish governor and declared the province independent. The
  poet José Joaquín de Olmedo led a provisional government, and a constitution adopted in
  November set up an elected junta. Guayaquil raised an army that tried to carry the
  revolution into the highlands, and it received help from Simón Bolívar's general Antonio
  José de Sucre. The province covered the coast from Esmeraldas to Tumbes. Opinion was
  divided between union with Peru, union with Gran Colombia and continued independence.
  After the victory at Pichincha, Bolívar arrived in July 1822 and annexed the province to
  Gran Colombia.
