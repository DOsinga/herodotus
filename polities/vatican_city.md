description: >-
  Vatican City is a sovereign city-state enclosed within Rome, created by the Lateran
  Treaty between Italy and the Holy See in 1929. It provided a independent territory
  after the loss of the larger Papal States in the nineteenth century. The pope is its
  elected sovereign and holds supreme legislative, executive, and judicial authority,
  with appointed bodies handling administration. The state is distinct from the Holy
  See, the enduring central government of the Catholic Church, which conducts most
  international diplomacy. Saint Peter's Basilica, the Apostolic Palace, museums, and
  gardens occupy much of its territory. Citizenship is generally connected with service
  rather than birthplace. Vatican City continues as a territorial guarantee of papal
  independence, supported by administration, tourism, publications, and other
  activities.
