description: >-
  Shule was an oasis kingdom centred on Kashgar at the western edge of the Tarim Basin, in present Xinjiang. Its
  beginnings are conventionally placed around 200 BCE, and its native royal government endured under changing regional overlords. Water from the
  Kashgar River sustained farming, while routes through the Pamirs and towards the Yumen Pass connected its merchants with the Silk
  Road. The population spoke an Iranian language associated with the Saka. Chinese histories describe its relations with Han officials and
  conflicts among neighbouring oasis kingdoms. Later rulers dealt with Turkic powers, Tang China and Tibet. Tribute and foreign garrisons did
  not always eliminate the local court. The Tibetan conquest around 790 ends this record of the kingdom and its monarchy.
