description: >-
  Chad is a landlocked republic in central Africa that became independent from France in
  1960, with its capital at N'Djamena. Its territory extends from the Sahara through the
  Sahel to wetter southern lands, bringing together communities with different
  languages, livelihoods and religious traditions. The first president, François
  Tombalbaye, introduced one-party rule, and rebellion developed into a prolonged civil
  war. Military coups, competing armed movements and intervention by Libya and France
  shaped later politics. Hissène Habré's dictatorship was followed by Idriss Déby's long
  presidency, while further rebellions repeatedly challenged central authority. Oil
  exports became important from 2003, alongside farming and livestock raising. Despite
  changes of government and periods of violence, the Chadian state has continued under
  the same national territorial identity.
