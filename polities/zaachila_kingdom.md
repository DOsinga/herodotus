description: >-
  The Zapotec kingdom of Zaachila was the leading Zapotec state of the Valley of Oaxaca in
  the Postclassic period, after the decline of Monte Albán. Its kings ruled from Zaachila,
  south of the present city of Oaxaca, and drew tribute from towns such as Teitipac,
  Macuilxochitl and Teotitlán, while Mitla and Tlacolula were looser dependencies. The
  kingdom fought repeatedly with the Mixtec states, whose nobles settled in the valley and
  eventually took over Zaachila itself. After the Aztec ruler Ahuitzotl conquered the valley
  in the 1490s, the Zapotec king Cosijoeza based himself at Tehuantepec, defended the
  fortress of Guiengola and made peace with the Aztecs through a royal marriage. His son
  Cosijopii submitted to the Spanish, who took control of the isthmus in 1522.
