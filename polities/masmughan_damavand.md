description: >-
  The Masmughanate of Damavand was a local Iranian monarchy in the mountains east of Ray, conventionally dated from 651 to
  760. Its ruling family maintained Zoroastrian traditions after the Sasanian Empire collapsed under Arab conquest. Mardanshah, associated with the Karenid
  family, fought alongside the ruler of Ray before making peace and agreeing to tribute. Mountain terrain helped subsequent masmughans preserve
  their own government despite caliphal ambitions. In 748 or 749, local forces ambushed an expedition sent by Abu Muslim and
  forced it to withdraw. Later conflict within the ruling family weakened its position. The final ruler allied with the Dabuyid
  ruler Khurshid when Abbasid forces advanced. In 760, the Abbasids defeated and captured him, bringing the kingdom under caliphal control.
