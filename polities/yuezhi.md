description: >-
  The Yuezhi were a mobile confederation first associated with Gansu in northwestern
  China, represented here from about 300 BCE to 30 CE. Chinese accounts describe a
  powerful pastoral people involved in trade before defeat by the Xiongnu forced most of
  them westward after 176 BCE. These Greater Yuezhi reached the Ili Valley, were displaced
  again by the Wusun, and moved into Bactria around 130 BCE. There they ruled over settled
  communities in a region shaped by Greek kingdoms. Their government was divided among
  five leaders commonly called yabghus. Kujula Kadphises, ruler of the Kushan branch,
  eventually brought the others under his authority. This consolidation produced the
  Kushan Empire, while Chinese writers continued using the broader Yuezhi name for its
  people.
