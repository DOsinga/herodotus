description: >-
  The State of Buenos Aires was a secessionist republic in Argentina from 1852 to 1861, comprising Buenos Aires city and its province. It emerged after the overthrow of Juan Manuel de Rosas, when local leaders rejected arrangements for a new national government under Justo José de Urquiza. Control of the port's customs revenue was central to the dispute with the Argentine Confederation. Buenos Aires adopted its own constitution, elected governors, maintained a legislature, and conducted foreign relations. Its revenue supported schools, urban improvements, and Argentina's first railway. Military confrontations alternated with negotiations over reunion. Defeat at Cepeda in 1859 forced concessions, but victory at Pavón in 1861 reversed the balance. Buenos Aires then rejoined Argentina, shaping the reunited national government.
