description: >-
  Nassau-Usingen was a county of the Holy Roman Empire in the Taunus hills north of Frankfurt,
  which became a principality in 1688. It arose from the division of the Walramian Nassau lands
  agreed in 1629 and carried out in 1659, when Walrad received Usingen. In the early eighteenth
  century the extinction of the Idstein, Ottweiler and Saarbrücken lines brought their lands to
  Nassau-Usingen, which later moved its residence to Biebrich on the Rhine near Wiesbaden. The
  princes promoted the growing spa at Wiesbaden. In 1806, under pressure from Napoleon, Prince
  Frederick Augustus joined the Confederation of the Rhine and united his lands with those of his
  younger cousin in Nassau-Weilburg to form the Duchy of Nassau.
