description: >-
  The Imamate of Futa Toro was a Muslim theocratic state of the Fula-speaking Toucouleur
  people in the middle valley of the Senegal River, in present-day Senegal and Mauritania. A
  jihad led by Sulayman Bal overthrew the Denianke kings in 1776, and his successor Abdul
  Kader Kan became the first almami. Abdul Kader defeated the Moorish emirates of Trarza and
  Brakna and made the French pay customs for their river trade, but after his death in 1806
  real power passed to a few leading Torodbe families, who chose and deposed the almamis. The
  state suffered from French expansion upriver and from the departure of many followers to
  join Umar Tall's Toucouleur Empire. France annexed most of Futa Toro in 1859, though the
  almamis kept nominal authority for some years.
