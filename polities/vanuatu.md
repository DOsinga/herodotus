description: >-
  Vanuatu is a Pacific island republic in Melanesia, independent since 1980, with its
  capital at Port Vila on Efate. Previously called the New Hebrides, the islands had
  been jointly administered by Britain and France under an condominium with parallel
  colonial institutions. Independence under Walter Lini followed political mobilisation
  and disputes between competing movements. A separatist uprising on Espiritu Santo was
  defeated with help from Papua New Guinea. The republic has an elected parliament, a
  prime minister responsible for government and a largely ceremonial president. A
  national council of chiefs advises on culture and customary matters. Numerous
  languages and strong island traditions coexist with national institutions.
  Agriculture, tourism and overseas partnerships support the economy, while cyclones
  and earthquakes pose recurring challenges.
