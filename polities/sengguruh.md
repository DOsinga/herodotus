description: >-
  Sengguruh was a Hindu principality in the upland basin around modern Malang in East Java, with its
  seat near Kepanjen. It was formed by followers of Majapahit who withdrew south after Demak
  captured the old royal centre at Daha in 1527, under a lord with the title Adipati Sengguruh. In
  1535 it attacked Lamongan and Giri on the coast without success, and its ruler later adopted
  Islam, which provoked a revolt by the Hindu party under Raden Pramana. Sultan Trenggana of Demak
  helped put down the revolt in 1545 and confirmed the lord in his post. Mataram took the area in
  1614, when Tumenggung Alap-Alap's army conquered it and, by one story, gave Malang its name.
