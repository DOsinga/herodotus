description: >-
  The Kingdom of Rohilkhand was a state of the Rohillas, Pashtun settlers in the Katehar
  region of the upper Ganges plain, in present Uttar Pradesh. Their chief Ali Muhammad Khan, a
  Jat by birth adopted by the Rohilla leader Daud Khan, received the title of nawab from the
  Mughal emperor in 1737 and, after Nadir Shah's sack of Delhi, ruled Rohilkhand as an
  independent state from Bareilly. After his death in 1748 the regent Hafiz Rahmat Khan led a
  council of Rohilla chiefs. The state fought Oudh and the Marathas and joined Ahmad Shah
  Durrani at Panipat in 1761. Maratha raids in 1772 pushed it into a defensive treaty with
  Oudh. When it failed to pay, Oudh and the East India Company conquered Rohilkhand in 1774.
