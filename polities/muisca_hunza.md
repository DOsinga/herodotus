description: >-
  The zacazgo of Hunza was the northern Muisca confederation in the highlands of present Boyacá,
  Colombia, centred on Hunza, where the Spanish later founded Tunja. Its ruler, the zaque, held
  authority over allied chiefdoms and maintained a court surrounded by palisades, and Muisca
  tradition gave the zaques a sacred origin. The people farmed maize and potatoes, wove cotton
  cloth and exchanged emeralds, salt and gold with neighbouring regions. Hunza's rulers fought
  long wars with the zipa of Bacatá to the south, as each sought supremacy over the Muisca
  highlands. Spanish forces under Gonzalo Jiménez de Quesada reached Hunza in 1537, captured Zaque
  Quemuenchatocha and seized its gold, and Spanish Tunja was founded on the site in 1539.
