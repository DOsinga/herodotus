description: >-
  The United Provinces of New Granada was a federation formed by provinces of the former
  viceroyalty in present-day Colombia during the period known as the Foolish Fatherland. Its
  Act of Federation was signed in November 1811 by provinces such as Antioquia, Cartagena,
  Neiva, Pamplona and Tunja, each of which kept its own government and constitution. A
  congress with a weak executive sat at Tunja and later at Bogotá, and it conducted the war
  and relations with other states. Cundinamarca under Antonio Nariño refused to join, and
  the two sides fought repeatedly until federal forces took Bogotá in 1814. Divided and
  short of money, the federation could not resist the Spanish expedition of Pablo Morillo,
  which took Cartagena in 1815 and Bogotá in 1816.
