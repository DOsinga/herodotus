description: >-
  The Qutlugh-Khanid state was a dynasty of Khitan origin that governed Kerman in
  southeastern Iran from 1222 to 1306. Its founder Buraq Hajib had served the Qara Khitai
  and then the Khwarazmshahs, and seized Kerman as Khwarazmian power collapsed before the
  Mongols. He and his successors submitted early to the Mongol khans and later to the
  Ilkhans, keeping their own government in return for tribute and troops. The best known
  ruler was Terken Khatun, who governed for about twenty-five years after 1257, promoting
  irrigation, building and charitable foundations. Her daughter Padishah Khatun married the
  Ilkhan Abaqa and later ruled Kerman herself. Rival claimants and Ilkhanid interference
  weakened the dynasty, and in 1306 Öljaitü removed the last ruler and appointed his own
  governor.
