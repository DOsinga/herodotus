description: >-
  The Mopan were a Yucatecan-speaking Maya people of southeastern Petén and southern Belize who
  remained independent of Spain until the end of the seventeenth century. Their territory lay
  south of the Itza kingdom of Nojpetén, between the Manche Chʼol and the Kowoj, and their
  population at contact has been estimated at ten to twenty thousand. Little is known of their
  political organisation, which the sources count among the lesser and more obscure groups of the
  Petén forest. Catholic missionaries first entered their lands in 1596, and some Mopan traded
  with the semi-Christian Maya of Tipuj. In 1692 the Council of the Indies ordered their
  subjugation, and war and disease drove the survivors into Spanish reducciones in southeastern
  Petén.
