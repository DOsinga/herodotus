description: >-
  Baden-Baden was a margraviate in southwestern Germany, formed when Baden's ruling family divided
  its lands in 1535. Its core lay along the Upper Rhine, with additional scattered lordships
  farther west. Hereditary margraves governed this territory within the Holy Roman Empire. The
  ruling line became Catholic, distinguishing it from neighbouring Baden-Durlach, although Baden-
  Durlach occupied and governed it from 1594 to 1622. The Thirty Years' War and later French wars
  caused severe destruction. Margrave Louis William and his wife Sibylle supported an ambitious
  Baroque court and building programme. From 1705 the government resided at Rastatt rather than
  Baden-Baden. When Augustus George died without an heir in 1771, Baden-Durlach inherited the
  territory and reunited the two branches' lands.
