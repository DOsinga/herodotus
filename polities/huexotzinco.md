description: >-
  Huexotzinco was a Nahua altepetl, or city-state, in the Puebla valley on the eastern
  slopes of the volcano Iztaccíhuatl. Founded by migrants in the Postclassic period, it was
  among the strongest powers of the valley in the fourteenth and fifteenth centuries, at
  times dominating Cholula and fighting Tlaxcala. Its rulers and nobles governed from a
  capital a short way from the present town. Huexotzinco stayed outside the Aztec empire,
  fighting ritual flower wars with the Triple Alliance, although famine and Tlaxcalan
  attacks forced it to seek refuge with the Aztecs for a time in the early sixteenth
  century. Its warriors joined Hernán Cortés against Tenochtitlan. Franciscans moved its
  people to the new town around their monastery in 1529.
