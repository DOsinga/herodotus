description: >-
  The Tomara Kingdom of Gwalior was a Rajput kingdom in central India, ruled from the hill
  fort of Gwalior by the Tomara dynasty between the 1390s and the early sixteenth century. The
  Tomaras had held a small fief under the Tughluq sultans of Delhi, and Virasimha took Gwalior
  during the disorder that followed Timur's invasion and became independent. His successors
  fought the sultans of Delhi, Malwa and Jaunpur to stay free, and paid tribute to Bahlul Lodi
  to avoid war. Man Singh, who reigned from 1486 to 1516, built the Man Mandir palace and made
  his court a centre of music, where the dhrupad style took shape. Ibrahim Lodi besieged and
  took Gwalior in 1518, after which the family held only lesser fiefs.
