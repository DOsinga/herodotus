description: >-
  The Kingdom of Dambadeniya was the Sinhalese monarchy that re-formed in the southwest of Sri
  Lanka after Kalinga Magha's conquest of Polonnaruwa in 1215. Vijayabahu III established his
  court at the rock fortress of Dambadeniya around 1220, away from the occupied north. His son
  Parakramabahu II drove out Magha's forces with help from local chiefs, recovered Polonnaruwa for
  a time and was celebrated as a patron of Buddhist learning and literature. Later kings moved the
  capital to Yapahuwa, where Bhuvanaikabahu I built a fortified palace, and then to Kurunegala, as
  Pandyan invasions and the growing Jaffna kingdom pressed from the north. The Tooth Relic
  travelled with the court. By 1341 the centre of royal power had shifted to Gampola.
