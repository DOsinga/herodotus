description: >-
  The State of Quito was the self-governing government set up in the highlands of present-
  day Ecuador during the first phase of the independence wars. A junta of Quito's creole
  elite had briefly seized power in 1809, and after its leaders were massacred in prison in
  August 1810, a new Superior Governing Junta took control of the city the following month,
  still nominally loyal to Ferdinand VII. Led by the bishop José de Cuero y Caicedo and
  figures such as Carlos Montúfar, it fought royalist Guayaquil, Cuenca and Pasto. In
  February 1812 a congress adopted a constitution that declared the State of Quito
  independent. Divided between rival factions, its forces were defeated by the royalist
  general Toribio Montes, who took Quito in November 1812.
