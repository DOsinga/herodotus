description: >-
  The Principality of Vitebsk was a Rus principality on the upper Western Dvina in present
  Belarus. It was created in 1101, when the lands of Vseslav of Polotsk were divided among his
  sons, and its princes came from his son Sviatoslav. Vitebsk lay on the trade route between the
  Baltic and the Dnieper, and its princes fought over Polotsk with their cousins of Minsk and
  Drutsk while Smolensk, Chernigov and Kiev intervened in turn. Mstislav the Great of Kiev took
  the Polotsk lands, including Vitebsk, from 1129 to 1132. In the thirteenth century the line of
  princes is poorly known. The principality passed to Lithuania in 1320 through the marriage of
  the future grand duke Algirdas to the heiress of its last prince.
