description: >-
  The Hintata Emirate of Marrakesh was the rule of the Awlad Yunus, the leading family of the
  Hintata, a Masmuda Berber confederation of the High Atlas, over Marrakesh and the south of
  Morocco. The Hintata had been pillars of the Almohad state, and the Hafsids of Tunis came
  from them. Under the Marinids their chiefs governed the Marrakesh region for the sultans,
  and as Marinid power declined they did so with little reference to Fez. After the Marinid
  dynasty ended in 1465 the Hintata emirs ruled Marrakesh as kings in their own right. They
  faced Portuguese attacks from the coast and were reduced to the city and its surroundings.
  In 1525 the rising Saadis took Marrakesh and killed the last emir.
