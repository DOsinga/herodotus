description: >-
  Fu Gongshi's Song was a short lived imperial government in the lower Yangtze region of
  China during 623 and 624. Its capital was Danyang, the city now called Nanjing. Fu had
  been a close associate and deputy of Du Fuwei, whose forces had accepted Tang authority
  after the collapse of the Sui empire. While Du remained at the Tang court, Fu seized
  control of the regional army and proclaimed himself emperor. He claimed to be acting on
  Du's instructions, but established a separate state rather than merely administering Tang
  territory. Tang armies under Li Xiaogong defeated his forces in 624. Fu fled the capital,
  was captured, and was executed, ending this brief attempt to challenge the dynasty's
  expanding imperial rule.
