note: "Yuri Dolgoruky's north-eastern principality, governed from Vladimir from 1157. After the Mongol conquest of 1238 its grand princes paid tribute to the Golden Horde and needed the khan's patent, but governed their own lands, so it stays active. The record follows Vladimir and the grand prince's own domain; of the appanages that split off and governed themselves, only Tver, Moscow and Suzdal-Nizhny Novgorod have records. Ivan I of Moscow received the grand-princely title in 1331, and Moscow governed Vladimir afterwards, except in 1360–1363, when Dmitry Konstantinovich of Suzdal-Nizhny Novgorod held the title and the city."
