description: >-
  Dʿmt was an early kingdom in the highlands of northern Ethiopia and Eritrea during the
  first millennium BCE, represented here by Yeha. Its dates remain uncertain, and
  surviving inscriptions reveal only a few rulers, who used titles translated as king and
  mukarrib. Yeha's great stone temple, palace remains, seals, and elite tombs show a
  society with substantial urban religious and administrative centres. Farming supported
  settlements, while ironworking and contacts across the Red Sea linked the region with
  South Arabia. Scholars disagree about the balance between local traditions and Sabaean
  influence. Monumental buildings were eventually abandoned, and smaller settlements
  followed. Dʿmt's relationship to the much later kingdom of Aksum is unresolved; this
  record does not assume uninterrupted government between the two.
