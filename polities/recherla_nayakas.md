description: >-
  The Recherla Nayakas were a Telugu ruling family who governed Telangana from the fall of the
  Musunuri Nayakas in 1368 until the Bahmani conquest in the 1430s. Allied at first with the
  Bahmani Sultanate, Anapota and Mada Nayaka defeated and killed Kapaya Nayaka at Bhimavaram in
  1368 and took Warangal and the country around it. They built fortified seats at Rachakonda,
  south-east of Golconda, and at Devarakonda, and claimed descent from Kakatiya commanders. Their
  main rivals were the Reddi kings of coastal Andhra. When the Reddis split, the Recherlas went
  over to Vijayanagara and shared in its victory at Pangal in 1419. The Bahmani sultans answered
  by taking Warangal in 1425 and Rachakonda by 1435, after which the family survived only as minor
  chiefs.
