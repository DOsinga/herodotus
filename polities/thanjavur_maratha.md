description: >-
  The Thanjavur Maratha kingdom ruled the Kaveri delta in Tamil Nadu, India, from its capital at
  Thanjavur. Venkoji, a half brother of Shivaji, led a Bijapur army south in 1674, drove out the
  Madurai Nayaks who had taken Thanjavur, and made himself king. His Bhonsle descendants governed
  the rich rice country of the delta independently of the Maratha court in the Deccan, fighting the
  Nayaks of Madurai, the nawabs of Arcot and the kingdom of Mysore. The court was a centre of
  Carnatic music, dance, painting and Sanskrit, Tamil and Marathi learning, and Serfoji II later
  built up the Saraswathi Mahal library. In 1799 Serfoji II handed the government of the kingdom to
  the East India Company and kept only the fort and a pension.
