description: >-
  The Old Babylonian Empire grew from an Amorite monarchy based at Babylon in southern
  Mesopotamia, conventionally beginning in 1894 BCE. Early kings controlled a relatively
  small territory among competing city states. Hammurabi, reigning from 1792 to 1750 BCE in
  the middle chronology, defeated rivals including Larsa and Mari and briefly united much of
  Mesopotamia under Babylonian rule. His famous law collection presents the king as a
  guardian of justice, while surviving letters reveal the work of royal administration.
  Babylon's dominance weakened under his successors as rebellions and new regional powers
  reduced its holdings. The dynasty ended when the Hittite king Mursili I raided Babylon in
  1595 BCE. Its political and literary traditions nevertheless shaped the history of later
  Babylonian kingdoms.
