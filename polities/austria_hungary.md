description: >-
  Austria-Hungary was a Habsburg monarchy in central and southeastern Europe from 1867
  to 1918. The Compromise of 1867 created Austrian and Hungarian halves with separate
  governments and parliaments, joined under one monarch. Common ministries handled
  foreign affairs, military matters, and their shared expenses. Vienna and Budapest
  became major capitals of a state containing many different languages, religions, and
  competing national movements. Railways, industry, and urban growth transformed its
  economy, while disputes over language and political rights strained its institutions.
  It occupied Bosnia and Herzegovina in 1878 and annexed the territory in 1908. War
  against Serbia in 1914 helped begin the First World War. Military defeat and national
  independence movements broke the monarchy into successor states in the autumn of
  1918.
