description: >-
  Shu Han was one of China's Three Kingdoms, ruling the Sichuan basin and neighboring
  southwestern regions from 221 to 263. Liu Bei established his imperial court at
  Chengdu after Cao Pi replaced the last Eastern Han emperor. Claiming descent from the
  Han ruling family, Liu Bei presented his government as the dynasty's continuation,
  not a new empire. Conflict with Sun Quan cost territory in the east and ended in
  defeat at Yiling. Under Liu Bei's son Liu Shan, the minister Zhuge Liang organized
  administration and launched northern campaigns against Cao Wei. Jiang Wei continued
  these campaigns after Zhuge Liang's death, without securing gains. In 263, Wei armies
  penetrated Shu's defenses and reached Chengdu. Liu Shan surrendered, ending the
  kingdom's government.
