description: >-
  Mecklenburg-Stargard was a duchy in northeastern Mecklenburg, formed in 1352 when the brothers
  Albert II and John I divided their inheritance. John received the Lordship of Stargard and other
  eastern lands, ruling from Burg Stargard and later Neubrandenburg. His descendants governed this
  territory as dukes of Mecklenburg within the Holy Roman Empire, while the senior line held
  Schwerin and played a role in Scandinavian politics through Albert of Sweden. The Stargard dukes
  took part in regional wars with Brandenburg and Pomerania over the borderlands. The duchy
  remained small and agrarian, with modest towns. When Duke Ulrich II died without a male heir in
  1471, Stargard returned to the main Mecklenburg line, reuniting the eastern lands with the
  duchy.
