description: >-
  The Sarmatians were Iranian speaking nomadic peoples of the Eurasian steppe, represented
  here from about 400 BCE to 375 CE between the Don and the Urals. They formed many separate
  groups, not a single state with one capital. Archaeological evidence includes burial
  mounds, weapons, horse equipment and ornaments, while Greek and Roman accounts describe
  their migrations and warfare. Horse riding and pastoral life were central, and some groups
  became known for heavily armed cavalry. They displaced or absorbed Scythian communities
  and spread towards the Black Sea and Danube, interacting with neighbouring kingdoms and
  the Roman Empire. The Lower Volga point is representative only. Hunnic expansion ended
  their predominance on the steppe, though related groups, including Alans, continued under
  changing names.
