description: >-
  The Free City of Kraków was a small republic around the old Polish capital, created by the
  Congress of Vienna in 1815 because Austria, Prussia and Russia could not agree which of them
  should have the city. Its territory reached a few towns and villages along the upper
  Vistula. A senate and an assembly of representatives governed it under a constitution that
  the three protecting powers had drawn up, and their residents watched its affairs closely.
  The city kept Polish schools and the Jagiellonian University, and it became a refuge and a
  centre of Polish national life after the other parts of Poland lost their autonomy. The
  powers occupied it more than once. After the failed Kraków uprising of 1846, Austria
  annexed it as the Grand Duchy of Kraków.
