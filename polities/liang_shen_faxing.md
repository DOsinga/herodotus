description: >-
  Shen Faxing's Liang was a short lived government in the lower Yangtze region of
  China between 618 and 620. Shen, a Sui governor from a family connected with the
  earlier Chen dynasty, gathered regional troops after Emperor Yang's assassination.
  He occupied cities in present day Zhejiang and southern Jiangsu and exercised
  authority despite professing submission to the distant Sui claimant Yang Tong. In
  619 he declared himself king of Liang and established his capital at Piling, now
  Changzhou. His court followed the Chen model. Rival commanders fought over the
  same rich southern lands, and Shen's harsh discipline alienated followers. Li
  Zitong defeated his armies and took his cities in 620. Shen fled and drowned
  himself, while Li absorbed the territory.
