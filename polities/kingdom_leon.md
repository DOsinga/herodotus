description: >-
  The Kingdom of León was a Christian monarchy in northwestern Iberia, established
  in 910 when the royal centre shifted south from Asturias to León.
  Its kings governed lands that expanded across the Duero basin, encouraging settlement
  and defending themselves against Muslim rulers, rival Christian kingdoms, and competing nobles.
  Galicia, Castile, and the future Portugal were closely connected to its changing
  borders and dynastic divisions. Royal claims to imperial authority reflected ambitions to
  lead the peninsula's Christian powers. In 1188 Alfonso IX summoned an assembly
  including town representatives, an important development in the history of the Cortes.
  Ferdinand III united León with Castile in 1230. León retained its name
  and institutions within the larger Crown of Castile after separate government ended.
