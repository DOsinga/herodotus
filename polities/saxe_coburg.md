description: >-
  Saxe-Coburg was a small German duchy governed by the Ernestine branch of the Wettin family. It began with the division of Ernest the Pious's lands in 1680, centred on Coburg. Changes of inheritance enlarged and renamed it Saxe-Coburg-Saalfeld in 1699, then Saxe-Coburg and Gotha in 1826. The later state combined separated territories in modern Bavaria and Thuringia. Its nineteenth-century dukes adopted a constitution and joined the German customs union, while the ruling family's marriages linked it to several European monarchies. Coburg and Gotha retained distinct local institutions within their union. In 1867 the North German Confederation took control of military and foreign affairs. The ducal monarchy continued domestically until the German Revolution of 1918 forced the last duke to abdicate.
