description: >-
  The Alamanni were a confederation of Germanic groups who took over the land between the upper
  Rhine and the upper Danube after Rome gave up that frontier around 260. They never formed a
  single state. Several kings and regional leaders ruled from hilltop seats such as the Runder
  Berg near Urach, sometimes acting together against Rome and sometimes serving in its armies.
  Through the fourth century they raided Gaul and Raetia, and Julian defeated them at Strasbourg
  in 357. When Roman rule on the Rhine collapsed in the fifth century they spread into Alsace, the
  Palatinate and northern Switzerland. Clovis beat them around 496 and 506, and the southern
  groups, who had sheltered under Theoderic the Great, passed to the Franks in 536.
