description: >-
  Maguá was a Taíno chiefdom in the northeast of Hispaniola, centred on the fertile Vega
  Real in the Cibao valley of the present-day Dominican Republic. Its cacique Guarionex
  ruled from a town near present La Vega, with lesser caciques and nitaínos governing
  districts under him. Columbus built the fort of Concepción there in 1494, and after the
  Spanish victory at the Vega Real in 1495 Guarionex had to deliver tribute in gold and
  food. He tried to throw off Spanish rule in 1497 with the help of Mayobanex, cacique of
  the Ciguayos, but Bartolomé Columbus defeated and captured both. Guarionex was held
  prisoner for years and drowned in 1502 when the fleet taking him to Spain sank in a
  hurricane.
