description: >-
  Goryeong Gaya is one of the kingdoms named in traditions about the Gaya communities of
  southern Korea. It is usually associated with Sangju, not with the modern town of Goryeong
  that was Daegaya's centre. The record's estimated period, 300 to 562, follows the emergence
  and final absorption of Gaya polities rather than a secure sequence of local reigns. The
  wider Gaya world developed from Byeonhan chiefdoms and consisted of separate centres, not a
  single unified kingdom. Rich burials, iron production and exchange reveal its regional
  setting, but cannot all be assigned to this particular member. Legends place Goryeong among
  six founding kingdoms. Its identification and independent lifetime remain disputed, while
  Silla's conquest ended the last surviving Gaya governments in 562.
