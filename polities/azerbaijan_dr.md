description: >-
  The Azerbaijan Democratic Republic was a short-lived state in the South Caucasus from 1918 to 1920. It declared independence after the Russian Empire and the Transcaucasian federation collapsed. The government initially worked from Ganja because Baku was controlled by rival forces, before moving to the oil-producing Caspian city. A parliament supervised the cabinet and included representatives of several political parties and ethnic communities. Women received equal voting rights, and the republic founded Baku State University. Its leaders sought international recognition while facing disputed borders, warfare with Armenia and pressure from neighbouring powers. British and Ottoman involvement shaped its early survival. In April 1920 the Red Army invaded, ending the republic and establishing Soviet rule over Azerbaijan for the following decades.
