description: >-
  The Thervingi were a Gothic people who held the plains north of the lower Danube and west of the
  Dniester, in present Romania and Moldova, from the late third century. They moved into the land
  Rome gave up when Aurelian abandoned Dacia around 275, and are first named in 291. Their many
  local chiefs could act together under a single leader, whom the Romans called a judge, such as
  Athanaric, who fought Valens from 367 to 369 and persecuted the Christians among his people.
  They traded with the Roman cities across the Danube and served Rome as allies. When the Huns
  attacked in 376, Athanaric withdrew into the hills, and most Thervingi under Fritigern crossed
  into the Roman Empire, where they became a core of the Visigoths.
