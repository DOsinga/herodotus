description: >-
  The Qajar principality of Merv was an independent state in the Merv oasis of Central Asia,
  ruled by the Izz al-Dinlu clan of the Qajar tribe, whose members had governed the oasis for the
  Safavid shahs since 1608. The Qajars of Merv had already defied the declining Safavid
  government early in the eighteenth century, and after Nader Shah's death in 1747 they governed
  on their own. They kept good relations with Daniyal Biy, the ruler of Bukhara, but his son Shah
  Murad turned against them: in 1785 he campaigned against Merv and killed its ruler Bayram Ali
  Khan. In 1788 Bukhara conquered the whole oasis, broke the dam that watered its fields and
  deported much of its population, and the last ruler fled to Iran.
