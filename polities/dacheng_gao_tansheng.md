description: >-
  Gao Tansheng's Dacheng State was a brief rebel court at Huairong in northern China during the
  collapse of the Sui dynasty. Its separate government is placed approximately in 618 to 619,
  although some chronologies assign both its foundation and overthrow to 618. Gao Tansheng was a
  Buddhist monk who seized the town and proclaimed himself Dacheng Emperor, a title referring to
  Mahayana Buddhism. He persuaded the nearby military leader Gao Kaidao to acknowledge him,
  granting that ally the title Prince of Qi. The arrangement lasted only months. Gao Kaidao
  killed Gao Tansheng, took command of his soldiers, and made Huairong his own capital. Surviving
  accounts mainly describe the victor, leaving little evidence about the monk's administration,
  territory, or political programme.
