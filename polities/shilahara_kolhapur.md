name: "Shilahara Kingdom of Kolhapur"
description: >-
  The Shilahara kingdom of Kolhapur governed southern Maharashtra and parts of northern Karnataka from about 940
  until the early thirteenth century. It was the latest of three Shilahara royal branches, with its
  own court rather than a single government shared with the Konkan rulers. Early royal residences included
  Karad, while Kolhapur and the fortress of Panhala became centres. Its kings sometimes acknowledged larger Deccan
  powers, including the Western Chalukyas, while administering their lands locally. Copperplate grants and temple inscriptions preserve
  their genealogy and religious patronage. The dynasty supported Jain and Hindu institutions and claimed the blessing
  of Mahalakshmi. Bhoja II lost his kingdom to the expanding Seuna ruler Singhana; the conquest is
  conventionally dated 1212, though later dates also appear.
