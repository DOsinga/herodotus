description: >-
  The Duchy of Athens was a Latin state covering Attica and Boeotia, set up after the Fourth
  Crusade in 1205 by the Burgundian knight Othon de la Roche. His family ruled it from Thebes
  and Athens for a century, and the duchy prospered from silk and trade. In 1311 the Catalan
  Company, a band of mercenaries, killed Duke Walter of Brienne at the Battle of Halmyros and
  took the duchy, which it then placed under the kings of Sicily and later of Aragon. The
  Florentine banker Nerio Acciaioli took the Acropolis in 1388, and his family ruled Athens
  again as a duchy of its own, paying tribute to the Ottomans. Sultan Mehmed II took Athens in
  1456 and ended the duchy in 1458.
