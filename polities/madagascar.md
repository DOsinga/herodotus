description: >-
  Madagascar became independent from France in 1960, governing the large Indian Ocean
  island off southeastern Africa and nearby smaller islands. Antananarivo, in the
  central highlands, remained its capital. The first republic under Philibert Tsiranana
  kept close French ties, until protests brought military rule in 1972. Didier
  Ratsiraka established a socialist republic in 1975, followed by a return to
  multiparty politics in the early 1990s. Later constitutional changes, disputed
  elections, and military interventions repeatedly reshaped government without ending
  the country. Malagasy and French reflect its varied cultural connections, while
  farming supports much of the population. Exceptional wildlife and forests distinguish
  the island, but poverty, cyclones, and pressure on natural habitats have made
  economic development and conservation difficult throughout its independent history.
