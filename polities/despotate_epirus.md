description: >-
  The Despotate of Epirus arose in western Greece after crusaders captured Constantinople,
  with Michael Komnenos Doukas establishing rule in 1205. Based around Arta, it preserved
  Byzantine institutions and claimed imperial legitimacy against other successor states.
  Expansion under Theodore led to the capture of Thessalonica in 1224 and a separate imperial
  phase. After his defeat by Bulgaria in 1230, an Epirote government again ruled the western
  core. It resisted neighbouring powers until Byzantine conquest in 1337. Restored in 1356,
  the state later passed through changing Greek, Serbian, and Italian ruling houses. Carlo
  Tocco briefly reunited much of its territory in the early fifteenth century. Ottoman
  advances then reduced it to scattered coastal possessions, ending with the conquest of
  Vonitsa in 1479.
