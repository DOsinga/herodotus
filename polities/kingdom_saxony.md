description: >-
  The Kingdom of Saxony was a German monarchy centered on Dresden, established in
  1806 when Napoleon raised its elector to royal rank. The Wettin king joined the
  Confederation of the Rhine and remained allied with France through the Napoleonic
  wars. Defeat brought occupation and the loss of much Saxon territory to Prussia at
  the Congress of Vienna in 1815. The reduced kingdom joined the German
  Confederation and developed into an industrial state. A constitution in
  1831 replaced absolute royal rule with a constitutional monarchy and legislature. Saxony sided
  with Austria against Prussia in 1866, then joined the North German Confederation
  in 1867. Its kings continued reigning until 1918, but the confederation had
  already taken control of military and foreign affairs.
