name: "Palenque Kingdom"
description: >-
  Palenque was a Maya kingdom in the forested foothills of present Chiapas, Mexico. Its
  documented royal history begins with K'uk' Bahlam's accession in 431 and continues to about
  800, an estimated endpoint after the last recorded accession. Kings and occasional queens
  governed a state known as B'aakal, competing with neighbours through warfare and alliances.
  Defeats by Calakmul disrupted its court before K'inich Janaab' Pakal rebuilt its standing
  during his long seventh century reign. Pakal and his successors commissioned palaces,
  temples, sculpture, and extensive inscriptions that preserve dynastic history and religious
  beliefs. His tomb beneath the Temple of the Inscriptions is renowned. Continued conflict
  with Toniná weakened the kingdom. Royal records disappeared around 800, though farmers
  remained after its institutions declined.
