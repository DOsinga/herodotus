description: >-
  Modern Mongolia began with the declaration of independence from Qing rule in 1911 under the
  Bogd Khan, a Buddhist religious leader. Its territory broadly followed Outer Mongolia,
  between China and Russia. Chinese occupation interrupted its separate government from 1919
  to 1921. Revolution and Soviet assistance restored it, and after the Bogd Khan's death the
  country became a socialist republic in 1924. The ruling party built a state closely aligned
  with the Soviet Union, transforming pastoral society while imposing collectivisation and
  severe political and religious repression. Peaceful demonstrations in 1990 brought
  multiparty politics, followed by a democratic constitution in 1992. Ulaanbaatar remains the
  capital. Mongolia now balances relations with its two neighbours while maintaining elected
  government and seeking wider international partnerships.
