description: >-
  The Duchy of Sorrento was a small coastal state on the Sorrentine peninsula south of Naples, one
  of the Tyrrhenian duchies that grew out of Byzantine southern Italy alongside Naples, Amalfi and
  Gaeta. At first a dependency of Naples, it governed itself under its own dukes from the ninth
  century, after resisting a siege by the Lombard prince Sicard of Benevento in 839. Exposed on
  its narrow peninsula, Sorrento often allied with Naples and Amalfi against Lombard and Saracen
  attacks and lived by fishing, farming and sea trade. Its dukes and archbishops formed a small
  local elite. In the eleventh century it came under pressure from the Normans and the princes of
  Salerno, and Robert Guiscard's Normans annexed it in 1078.
