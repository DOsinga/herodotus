description: >-
  The Kalachuri Kingdom of Ratnapura was a Hindu kingdom in present Chhattisgarh, in central
  India, ruled from Ratnapura, modern Ratanpur. It began around 1000 as a branch of the
  Kalachuris of Tripuri, when Kalingaraja conquered the Dakshina Kosala region. His grandson
  Ratnadeva I founded Ratnapura. Jajalladeva I became independent in practice around 1100,
  conquering lands to the south, and Ratnadeva II formally broke with Tripuri and beat back
  the Eastern Gangas. Little is known about the kingdom after the early thirteenth century,
  but the family kept ruling, and around 1407 a junior branch set up its own seat at Raipur.
  In 1741 the Marathas of Nagpur under Raghoji Bhonsle conquered Ratanpur and soon ended the
  dynasty.
