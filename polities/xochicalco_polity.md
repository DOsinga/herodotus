description: >-
  Xochicalco was a fortified city in the hills of present Morelos, Mexico, whose main
  governing phase is estimated between 650 and 900. It flourished after Teotihuacan's
  decline, occupying a strategic position near important trade routes. Terraces,
  defenses, palaces, temples, and ballcourts show a planned urban center with authority
  and labor. Craft production and distant exchange supported its population, while art
  combined influences from central Mexico and the Maya region. Reliefs on the Temple of
  the Feathered Serpent depict figures and places associated with wider political and
  religious connections. The precise structure of its government remains uncertain.
  Around 900, widespread burning and objects abandoned inside buildings mark a rapid
  destruction. Some residents remained nearby, but the city's regional political role
  ended.
