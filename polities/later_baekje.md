description: >-
  Later Baekje was a kingdom in southwestern Korea that emerged as Silla's
  authority collapsed. Gyeon Hwon established his own territorial government at Mujinju in
  892, then proclaimed the revival of ancient Baekje and moved his capital
  to Jeonju in 900. Its fertile Jeolla lands supported an army, and
  its king competed with Silla and the rising Goryeo kingdom during Korea's
  Later Three Kingdoms period. Gyeon Hwon attacked Silla's capital in 927, but
  could not turn military successes into lasting supremacy. A succession dispute became
  decisive: his son Singeom deposed him in 935, and the ruler escaped
  to Goryeo. Goryeo defeated Singeom's government in 936, absorbing the kingdom and
  completing the reunification of much of the Korean peninsula under Wang Geon.
