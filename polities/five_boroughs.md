description: >-
  The Five Boroughs of the Danelaw were the Danish-held towns of Lincoln, Nottingham, Derby,
  Leicester and Stamford in the East Midlands, recorded here from 878 to 918. After the Great
  Heathen Army divided Mercia in 877, Danish armies settled around these fortified towns, each
  governed by its own jarl and army and ruling its own district, the origin of the later
  shires. They had no king of their own but sometimes acted together. Their settlers left
  Scandinavian place names, laws and legal terms across the region. Edward the Elder and his
  sister Æthelflæd took the boroughs one by one between 917 and 920; Lincoln is counted here
  until 918. Olaf Guthfrithson of York briefly retook them in 940.
