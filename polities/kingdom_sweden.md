  The Kingdom of Sweden represented here begins in 1521, when Gustav Vasa became regent; his

  Norway in 1814, while retaining its own government; Norway separated peacefully in 1905. Royal
  power gradually gave way to parliamentary rule and a constitutional monarchy. Industrialization,
  expanded voting rights, and social reforms transformed society. The modern state continues with
  an elected Riksdag and government, while the monarch holds a largely ceremonial role.
