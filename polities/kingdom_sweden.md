description: >-
  The Kingdom of Sweden represented here begins in 1523, when Gustav Vasa became king and
  ended Swedish participation in the Kalmar Union. Centered on Stockholm, the monarchy
  consolidated royal authority and adopted the Lutheran Reformation. Seventeenth century
  victories made Sweden a Baltic power, but defeat in the Great Northern War ended that
  imperial position. Russia took Finland in 1809, reshaping the kingdom's territory. Sweden
  entered a union with Norway in 1814, while retaining its own government; Norway separated
  peacefully in 1905. Royal power gradually gave way to parliamentary rule and a
  constitutional monarchy. Industrialization, expanded voting rights, and social reforms
  transformed society. The modern state continues with an elected Riksdag and government,
  while the monarch holds a largely ceremonial role.
