description: >-
  Iveagh was a Gaelic kingdom in what is now County Down, Northern Ireland, associated
  with the Uí Echach Cobo. Its formation is estimated around 400, although the first
  named king died in 552. It belonged to the political world of Ulaid, where local kings
  maintained their own territories within shifting relationships of allegiance. The Mac
  Aonghusa family, known in English as Magennis, became its leading rulers in the
  medieval period. Their chiefs were based at Rathfriland and inaugurated at Knock
  Iveagh. Norman conquest reduced the native territory without ending its local
  government. The kingdom's independent period conventionally ends with submission under
  surrender and regrant in 1543. Later family estates and hereditary titles survived
  under the crown rather than independent rule.
