description: >-
  The County of Asti was the lordship of the dukes of Orléans over Asti and its district,
  from 1387 until Duke Louis became King Louis XII of France in 1498. Gian Galeazzo
  Visconti gave Asti to his daughter Valentina as her dowry when she married Louis, Duke
  of Orléans, the brother of Charles VI of France. The Orléans dukes governed it through
  their own governors, as a French island in Piedmont that gave them a foothold and a
  claim in Italian politics. Charles of Orléans spent twenty five years as a prisoner in
  England, and Visconti and Montferrat lords held the town for some of that time. When
  Louis of Orléans became king, Asti passed to the French crown.
