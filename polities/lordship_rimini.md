description: >-
  The Lordship of Rimini was the state of the Malatesta family, who ruled Rimini and much
  of Romagna and the northern Marche from 1295 until Cesare Borgia took the city in 1500.
  Malatesta da Verucchio's heirs added Fano, Pesaro, Cesena and other towns, and governed
  them as papal vicars while acting as an independent power. The family were famous
  condottieri: Pandolfo III held Brescia and Bergamo from 1404 to 1421 after the death of
  Gian Galeazzo Visconti. Sigismondo Pandolfo Malatesta, soldier and patron, rebuilt San
  Francesco as the Tempio Malatestiano, but Pope Pius II excommunicated him and stripped
  him of most of his lands. His heirs kept only Rimini until Cesare Borgia's conquest.
