description: >-
  Yelang was an ancient kingdom or alliance of agricultural communities in southwestern China,
  chiefly associated with western Guizhou. It is represented here approximately from 300 to 27
  BCE, although its centre and territorial extent remain uncertain. Chinese accounts describe
  local kings, substantial forces, and relations with neighbouring Dian and Nanyue.
  Archaeological finds attributed to Yelang include bronze weapons, ornaments, vessels, and
  distinctive burial practices, but identifying particular sites with the kingdom is difficult.
  Han expansion brought increasing intervention without immediately eliminating every local
  ruler. The famous story of a king asking whether Yelang exceeded Han in size later became an
  idiom for misplaced pride. Around 27 BCE Han officials killed its last king and suppressed
  resistance, ending the native government.
