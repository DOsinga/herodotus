description: >-
  The Yelahanka Nadaprabhus were a family of chiefs who ruled the country around Bangalore in
  southern Karnataka. They began as vassals of the Vijayanagara Empire at Yelahanka, and their
  best known member, Kempe Gowda I, built the mud fort and market town of Bangalore in 1537 and
  moved his seat there. After the defeat of Vijayanagara at Talikota in 1565 the family governed
  on its own, though inscriptions still named the distant Aravidu emperors. Kempe Gowda II added
  watchtowers, tanks and temples around the town. In 1638 a Bijapur army under Ranadulla Khan and
  Shahaji Bhonsle took Bangalore, and the family withdrew to Magadi to the west, where it held
  the hill country and the fortress of Savandurga for another ninety years. The Mysore army took
  Magadi in 1728.
