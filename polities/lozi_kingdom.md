description: >-
  The Lozi kingdom, or Barotseland, was the state of the Lozi people on the upper Zambezi
  floodplain in what is now western Zambia. It was probably founded in the seventeenth century
  by migrants from the Lunda world, and about 1650 is the estimate used here. Its kings, the
  litunga, governed with a council of nobles, the kuta, and organised the floodplain's people
  to build mounds and canals. Succession wars weakened the kingdom, and around 1840 the
  Makololo, a Sotho group displaced by the Mfecane, conquered it. A rising in 1864 restored
  Lozi rule. Lewanika, who reigned from 1878, sought British protection against the Ndebele
  and the Portuguese and signed the Lochner Concession in 1890, which placed Barotseland under
  the British South Africa Company.
