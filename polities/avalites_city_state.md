description: >-
  Avalites was an ancient trading port near the entrance to the Red Sea, conventionally
  located at Zeila in the Horn of Africa. This record uses approximately 50 to 650 for
  its ancient political identity, rather than a dated foundation or collapse. The
  Periplus of the Erythraean Sea describes a small port whose inhabitants traded across
  the strait with Arabia. Imports included cloth, grain and wine, while local merchants
  exported myrrh and other goods using small boats. Its community appears to have
  managed local affairs, but the surviving accounts reveal little about leaders or
  institutions. Later geographical writers continued mentioning Avalites and its people.
  Djibouti is another proposed location, and continuity between the ancient port and
  medieval Zeila remains poorly documented.
