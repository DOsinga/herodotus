description: >-
  The Dazhong Kingdom was a brief monarchy centred on Dali in present Yunnan, existing from
  1094 to 1096. It replaced the Duan family's Dali Kingdom when the powerful minister Gao
  Shengtai forced Duan Zhengming to surrender the throne. Gao changed the state's name but
  inherited its established territory and court rather than founding a new settlement or
  conquering an unrelated country. The region's political culture combined Buddhism, local
  traditions and connections with the Chinese world. After Gao died, the throne returned to
  the Duan family under Duan Zhengchun, and the name Dali was restored. The Gao clan
  nevertheless retained substantial influence over government. Dazhong therefore marks a
  short transfer of royal power within a longer history of ministerial family dominance.
