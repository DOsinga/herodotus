description: >-
  The Tollensians were a West Slavic people of the lower and middle Tollense valley in
  present northeastern Germany. Their separate political organisation is represented in
  two estimated phases, from 789 to 983 and from 1057 to 1127. They belonged to the
  wider Veletian and later Lutician world, but evidence does not identify a secure
  capital or a continuous line of rulers. In 983 they joined the Lutician federation,
  whose members resisted neighbouring Christian powers. During the federation's civil
  war beginning in 1057, they allied with the Redarians against the Kessini and
  Circipani. Victory nevertheless left the federation weakened. Later resistance to
  German authority brought campaigns by Lothar of Saxony. Their lands were incorporated
  into larger German and Pomeranian political structures.
