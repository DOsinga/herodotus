description: >-
  The Kingdom of Bavaria emerged in southern Germany in 1806, when Napoleon's support allowed the Wittelsbach elector Maximilian Joseph to become king. Munich remained its capital. Minister Montgelas reorganised administration, reduced church power, and helped shape a larger, more unified state. The constitution of 1818 established a parliament alongside the monarchy. Bavaria gained Franconian lands and the Rhenish Palatinate, while royal patronage made Munich a centre of art and architecture. Its rulers tried to preserve room between Austria and Prussia, but defeat alongside Austria in 1866 brought closer dependence on Prussia. Bavaria joined the German Empire in 1871, ending the separate control of war and foreign policy recorded here. Its kings continued within the empire until the revolution of 1918.
