description: >-
  The Principality of Serbia emerged in 1815 after the Second Serbian Uprising and
  negotiations between Miloš Obrenović and Ottoman authorities. It began as an autonomous
  monarchy in the Balkans, retaining tribute obligations and Ottoman garrisons while Serbian
  rulers built their own government. Obrenović and Karađorđević princes alternated in power
  amid disputes over authority and constitutional limits. Kragujevac served as an early
  capital before Belgrade became the seat of government. The withdrawal of Ottoman troops in
  1867 strengthened effective independence. War and diplomacy brought territorial expansion
  and recognition at the Congress of Berlin in 1878. Serbia developed ministries, an
  assembly, and a army during these decades. In 1882 Prince Milan proclaimed the Kingdom of
  Serbia, continuing the state under royal status.
