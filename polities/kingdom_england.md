description: >-
  The Kingdom of England united the English territories under Athelstan in 927 and lasted
  until union with Scotland in 1707, apart from the republican years of 1649–1660. Its kings
  governed through royal officials, courts and, increasingly, Parliament. The Norman conquest
  of 1066 replaced the ruling elite and tied England closely to continental politics. Later
  rulers conquered Wales, fought for the French crown and broke with Rome during the
  Reformation. Civil war ended with Charles I's execution, but the monarchy returned under
  Charles II. The settlement after the Glorious Revolution strengthened Parliament's position.
  England and Scotland shared a monarch from 1603 while retaining separate governments; the
  Acts of Union finally combined their kingdoms and parliaments into the Kingdom of Great
  Britain.
