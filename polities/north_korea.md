description: >-
  North Korea occupies the northern Korean Peninsula, with its capital at Pyongyang.
  Established in 1948 after Japanese colonial rule and the division of Korea into
  Soviet and American occupation zones, it became a socialist state led by Kim Il Sung.
  The Workers' Party dominates government, while leadership has passed through three
  generations of the Kim family. Its invasion of South Korea in 1950 began a
  devastating war involving Chinese forces and a United Nations coalition. The 1953
  armistice left the peninsula divided without a peace treaty. Reconstruction produced
  a centrally planned economy and an ideology of national self-reliance called Juche.
  Later famine, political repression, and nuclear weapons development shaped its
  international isolation, while the state continued through successive leadership
  changes.
