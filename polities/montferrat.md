name: Marquisate of Montferrat
description: >-
  The Marquisate of Montferrat was a hereditary state in northwestern Italy, originating with Emperor
  Otto I's grant to Aleramo in 967. Its lands lay among the competing powers of Piedmont and the Po
  valley, with Casale Monferrato becoming its capital in the fourteenth century. The Aleramici family
  governed through local lordship while maintaining connections with emperors and neighboring Italian
  states. Several marquesses pursued careers in the eastern Mediterranean; Boniface led the Fourth
  Crusade and became ruler of Thessalonica. When the Aleramici line ended, a branch of the Byzantine
  Palaiologos family inherited Montferrat in 1305. Dynastic alliances and warfare continued to shape
  its fortunes. After that line died out, the Gonzaga acquired the territory in 1536 and governed it
  from Mantua.
