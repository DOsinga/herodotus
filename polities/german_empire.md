name: German Empire
description: >-
  The German Empire united most German states under Prussian leadership from 1871 to 1918,
  with Berlin as its capital. Founded during the war against France, it was a federal
  monarchy whose emperor was also king of Prussia. An appointed chancellor directed
  government, while an elected Reichstag and a council representing member states shared
  legislative power. Otto von Bismarck shaped its early institutions, alliances, and social
  insurance laws. Rapid industrial growth made Germany a economic and military power.
  Overseas colonies, naval expansion, and growing international rivalries marked its later
  decades. During the First World War, military leaders gained exceptional influence over
  policy. Defeat, shortages, and revolution brought the monarchy down in November 1918, and
  the empire gave way to republican government.
