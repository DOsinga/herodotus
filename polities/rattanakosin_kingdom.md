description: >-
  The Rattanakosin Kingdom was the Bangkok-centred Siamese monarchy founded in 1782 by
  Rama I, the first Chakri king. It succeeded the Thonburi government and rebuilt
  institutions after the destruction of Ayutthaya. Early kings fought Burma and extended
  influence over Lao, Khmer, and Malay territories. Buddhist temples, court ceremonies,
  and royal patronage helped establish Bangkok as the kingdom's centre. During the
  nineteenth century, expanding British and French colonial powers forced Siam to
  negotiate treaties and surrender peripheral lands. Kings Mongkut and Chulalongkorn
  pursued diplomatic and administrative reforms that helped preserve the core state's
  independence. The monarchy centralised provincial government and ended slavery. The
  revolution of 1932 replaced absolute royal rule with constitutional government, marking
  the transition to the modern Thai state.
