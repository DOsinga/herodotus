description: >-
  Yamatai was a polity in the Japanese archipelago described in the third-century Chinese
  chronicle Records of the Three Kingdoms. According to that account, the people of Wa had
  suffered war among many small countries until they agreed on a female ruler, Himiko, who lived
  secluded in a palace, practised religious rites and governed through her younger brother. She
  sent envoys to the Wei court in 238 or 239 and received the title Queen of Wa Friendly to Wei.
  After her death a male ruler failed to keep the peace, and the girl Iyo took her place; envoys
  from Wa reached China again in 266. Where Yamatai lay, in northern Kyushu or the Nara basin,
  remains disputed.
