description: >-
  The Judicate of Cagliari was one of the four kingdoms of medieval Sardinia and covered the
  south of the island. Its judges ruled from Santa Igia, on the lagoon west of Cagliari, and
  governed thirteen districts through curators, with an assembly of notables. The judicate
  grew out of Byzantine Sardinia, and its rulers first appear clearly in the eleventh century,
  when they gave lands to Italian and Provençal monasteries and began to rely on Pisan and
  Genoese merchants. In the thirteenth century Pisa built the walled castle of Cagliari and
  dominated the judges. When Judge William III allied with Genoa, Pisa and the rulers of
  Arborea and Gallura conquered the judicate, destroyed Santa Igia in 1258 and divided its
  lands between them.
