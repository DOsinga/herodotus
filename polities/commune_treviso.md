description: >-
  The Commune of Treviso was the self-governing city state of Treviso in the Veneto, from
  the first consuls in the twelfth century until Cangrande della Scala took the city in
  1329. Treviso governed the plain between the Piave and the Brenta, the March of Treviso,
  and joined the Lombard League against Frederick Barbarossa. Its government was
  repeatedly taken over by local lords: Alberico da Romano, Ezzelino's brother, from 1239
  to 1259, and the da Camino from 1283, whose court Dante visited. After the da Camino
  were thrown out in 1312, the commune governed itself again, with imperial vicars from
  Gorizia and Austria protecting it against Cangrande. He took it in 1329, and Venice took
  it from his heirs ten years later.
