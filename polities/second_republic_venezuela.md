description: >-
  The Second Republic of Venezuela was the independent government restored by Simón Bolívar
  in 1813. Advancing from New Granada in his Admirable Campaign, Bolívar entered Caracas in
  August 1813, while Santiago Mariño freed the eastern provinces with an expedition from
  Trinidad. Bolívar took the title of Liberator and governed with wide personal powers,
  proclaiming a war to the death against Spaniards who did not join the cause. The republic
  never controlled Coro, Maracaibo or Guayana, and from 1814 it faced the llaneros of José
  Tomás Boves, who raised the cavalry of the plains against the creole patriots. After
  defeats at La Puerta and elsewhere, Bolívar abandoned Caracas in July 1814, and the last
  republican forces in the east were destroyed by the end of the year.
