description: >-
  The Sultanate of Lahej, also called the Abdali Sultanate, was a state in southern Yemen
  centred on the town of Lahij, in the farmland north of Aden. Its Abdali sultans, beginning
  with al-Fadl ibn Ali in 1728, broke away from the Zaydi imams of the highlands as the Qasimid
  state weakened, and were fully independent by 1740. The sultans also held the port of Aden.
  In 1802 Sultan Ahmad ibn Abd al-Karim signed a treaty with Britain, which wanted allies on
  the route to India. A dispute over the plundering of wrecked British ships led Captain Haines
  to bombard and capture Aden in January 1839. Sultan Muhsin signed a bond of peace with
  Britain that year in return for a stipend, and Lahej was drawn into the Aden Protectorate.
