name: Civitas Schinesghe
description: >-
  Civitas Schinesghe names the Piast state centered on Gniezno, called the Duchy of Poland. It emerged
  during the tenth century, with Mieszko I's rule beginning around 960 in this record. The duke
  brought neighboring West Slavic communities under a monarchy supported by fortified settlements and
  military power. His conversion to Christianity in 966 linked the ruling family with Latin
  Christendom and encouraged church institutions. The unusual name survives in a later summary of a
  document placing Mieszko's realm under papal protection. His son Bolesław expanded the state and
  promoted Gniezno's standing as an ecclesiastical center. Relations with German rulers alternated
  between cooperation and warfare. Bolesław's coronation in 1025 changed the duchy's status, marking
  the beginning of the Kingdom of Poland.
