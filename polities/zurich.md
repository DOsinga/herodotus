description: >-
  Zurich was a city at the northern end of Lake Zurich, on the site of the Roman customs post of
  Turicum. The abbesses of the Fraumünster held the town's lordship, but after the Zähringer
  reeves died out in 1218 Zurich became a free imperial city. A council of merchants and knights
  governed it, and the city grew rich on silk and cloth and on the trade route to the Alpine
  passes. In 1336 the knight Rudolf Brun overthrew the old council with the help of the craftsmen
  and set up a constitution of guilds with himself as mayor for life. Threatened by the exiled
  councillors and their Habsburg allies, Brun took Zurich into the Swiss Confederacy in 1351, and
  the city withstood the Austrian sieges that followed.
