description: >-
  The later kingdom of Kosala was a small monarchy in the middle Ganges plain, ruled from
  Saketa, the city now called Ayodhya, after the Shunga empire lost its grip on the region.
  The Yuga Purana and other Puranas speak of seven powerful kings in the capital of Kosala,
  and coins and an inscription of Dhanadeva, who calls himself lord of Kosala, confirm that
  local kings of the Deva family ruled there in the first century BCE. Kings whose names end
  in -datta and -mitra followed, known only from their coins, and the order of their reigns
  is uncertain. The Kushans under Kanishka took Saketa for a time, and Mitra kings ruled
  again after them. The early Gupta kings absorbed the kingdom in the early fourth century.
