description: >-
  Hokuzan was the northern of three competing kingdoms on Okinawa during the Sanzan period, existing from 1314 until 1416. Its
  rulers governed from Nakijin Castle through local lords and their retainers, rather than a tightly organised administration covering the whole
  island. Mountainous land gave the kingdom a large territory but fewer people and less productive farmland than its rivals. Ports
  below Nakijin supported overseas trade, and tribute missions to Ming China opened further commercial opportunities. Competition with Chuzan and Nanzan
  involved diplomacy, commerce, and warfare. Under the last ruler, Hananchi, disputes among his subordinate lords weakened the kingdom. Several defected
  to Chuzan, whose forces took Nakijin in 1416. Hokuzan then became part of the expanding state that eventually united Okinawa.
