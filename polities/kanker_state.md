description: >-
  Kanker was a small hill kingdom in the upper Mahanadi valley of southern Chhattisgarh, north of
  Bastar, with its seat at the town of Kanker. Local tradition, followed by the district history,
  gives it three successive ruling houses: a Somavamshi line from about 1125 to 1344, the short
  Kandra line until 1385, and then the Chandra dynasty, whose founder Veer Kanhar Dev was
  remembered as a king of Puri who settled at Sihawa. The rajas held the Dhamtari country and paid
  tribute to the Kalachuri kings of Ratanpur, and after 1741 to the Bhonsle Marathas, for whom
  they kept a contingent of troops. The land was mostly forested hills inhabited largely by Gonds.
  Nagpur occupied the state in 1809 and deposed Raja Bhup Deo.
