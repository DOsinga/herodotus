description: >-
  Great Bolu, also called Great Palola, was a royal centre in Baltistan, in the mountain valleys of present day northern
  Pakistan. Its beginnings are estimated here around 600, and Skardu serves as a representative location rather than a identified capital.
  Chinese records distinguish Great Palola from Little Palola at Gilgit, but the relationship between their courts remains disputed. Buddhist practice,
  monasteries, and Sanskrit learning linked the region with neighbouring Himalayan communities and routes to India. Great Palola sent embassies to
  the Tang court in 717 and 719 while Tibetan influence was expanding across the mountains. Local rulers navigated competing Chinese
  and Tibetan ambitions. A Tang campaign captured Great Palola in 753, ending the locally governing kingdom represented by this record.
