description: >-
  The Azariqa State was a militant Kharijite government in southwestern Iran and later
  Fars and Kerman, conventionally dated 684–699. Its origins lay among rebels expelled
  from Basra during the Second Islamic Civil War. Nafi ibn al-Azraq and his successors
  led armed communities that rejected rival Muslim rulers and claimed authority through
  a fighting imam. Their movement became notorious for treating other Muslims, including
  women and children, as legitimate targets. Ahvaz was an early center, but repeated
  campaigns forced its leaders eastward. Under Qatari ibn al-Fuja'a, the Azariqa
  maintained territorial rule and issued coins, while facing attacks from al-Muhallab's
  forces. Internal divisions weakened them further. Their final suppression ended this
  territorial government, rather than every surviving Kharijite community or religious
  tradition.
