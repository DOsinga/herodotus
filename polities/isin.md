description: >-
  Isin was a kingdom in southern Mesopotamia, in present day Iraq, governed from a city on
  an important navigable Euphrates canal. It became independent in 2017 BCE when Ishbi-Erra,
  formerly an official of Ur, broke with King Ibbi-Sin. After Ur fell, Isin's kings claimed
  its political and religious inheritance, recovered Ur from Elamite control, and maintained
  many earlier administrative traditions. Royal hymns, inscriptions, legal documents, and
  temple remains reveal a court closely tied to the gods and urban institutions. The healing
  goddess Ninisina was particularly important at Isin. Control of canals, farmland, and
  neighbouring cities made rivalry with Larsa central to its history. Isin gradually lost
  territory and influence before Rim-Sin I of Larsa conquered the kingdom in 1794 BCE.
