description: >-
  The Jabrid Sultanate was a state of eastern Arabia ruled by the Banu Jabr, a Bedouin
  family of the Banu Uqayl, from al-Hasa in the fifteenth and early sixteenth centuries.
  The Jabrids replaced the earlier Usfurid rulers and, under Ajwad ibn Zamil in the late
  fifteenth century, extended their power over Bahrain, al-Qatif, much of Najd and parts of
  Oman. They took Bahrain from the Kingdom of Hormuz, and their wealth came from the dates
  of the oases and the pearls and trade of the Gulf. Ajwad was known as a pious ruler who
  made the pilgrimage to Mecca with a great following. In 1521 the Portuguese and Hormuzis
  took Bahrain and killed its Jabrid governor, and soon after the dynasty lost al-Hasa to
  the Muntafiq tribe.
