description: >-
  The Lemro kingdom was the Arakanese monarchy of the Le-mro period, on the coast of present
  Rakhine State in western Myanmar, from about 818 to 1406. After Waithali declined, its kings
  governed from a series of capitals beside the Lemro River: Pyinsa, Parein, Hkrit, Nyeinzara
  Toungoo and finally Launggyet, founded about 1251. The moves partly followed the shifting course
  of the river. Arakanese chronicles are the main source, and their early dates are traditional
  rather than secure. Pagan exercised influence in the early twelfth century, when Kyansittha
  restored a king to Parein, but local kings continued to rule. Under the Launggyet dynasty the
  kingdom traded rice, ivory and lacquer for Bengal textiles. From the 1370s Ava and Pegu meddled
  in its successions, and Ava conquered Launggyet in 1406.
