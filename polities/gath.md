description: >-
  Gath was one of the five principal Philistine cities in the southern Levant, identified with
  Tell es Safi in modern Israel. This record begins around 1175 BCE, an estimate for Philistine
  settlement, and ends with destruction around 830 BCE. The city had its own ruler and belonged to
  a network of neighbouring Philistine centres rather than one unified state. Biblical traditions
  associate it with Goliath and King Achish, under whom David took refuge. Excavations reveal a
  large settlement, temples, workshops and pottery showing both Aegean connections and growing
  local influences. A major siege system and widespread destruction fit the late ninth century
  conquest by Hazael of Aram Damascus. Later settlement continued, but Gath did not regain its
  earlier political importance.
