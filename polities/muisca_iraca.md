description: >-
  Iraca was a Muisca confederation in the northeastern highlands of present Boyacá, Colombia,
  centred on the sacred town of Sugamuxi, modern Sogamoso. Its ruler, also called the iraca, was
  both the political leader and high priest of the Sun Temple, the most important Muisca religious
  centre. Muisca tradition held that the cultural hero Bochica had chosen Sugamuxi as his seat,
  and the iraca was elected rather than inheriting by descent alone. The confederation stood apart
  from the zaque of Hunza and the zipa of Bacatá, though it sometimes allied with them. Farming,
  weaving and exchange sustained the population. Spanish forces under Gonzalo Jiménez de Quesada
  reached Sugamuxi in 1537, and soldiers looting the Sun Temple set it on fire.
