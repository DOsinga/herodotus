description: >-
  Buyeo was an ancient kingdom in northern Manchuria, in today's northeastern China, represented here from roughly 200 BCE to 494 CE. Its beginnings are uncertain, and later foundation stories connect its rulers with legendary ancestors rather than securely dated events. Chinese accounts describe agricultural communities governed by a king and aristocratic leaders whose titles used animal names. Buyeo exchanged tribute and trade with Chinese dynasties and sometimes allied with them against neighboring powers. These ties also offered its rulers valuable goods and recognition. Xianbei attacks and pressure from Goguryeo caused repeated defeats and movements of the court. Its final remnant surrendered to Goguryeo in 494. Both Goguryeo and Baekje later claimed Buyeo ancestry, giving the kingdom an enduring historical legacy.
