description: >-
  The County of Weimar-Orlamünde was the lordship of the counts of Orlamünde in Thuringia, a
  branch of the Ascanian house of Albert the Bear, who ruled from Weimar and from the castle of
  Orlamünde on the Saale. In 1260 they gained the Franconian lordship of Plassenburg and Kulmbach
  from the inheritance of the dukes of Merania. Through the thirteenth and fourteenth centuries
  they were rivals of the Wettin landgraves of Thuringia. In the Thuringian Counts' War of 1342 to
  1346 the Wettins defeated them, and they had to sell Orlamünde and accept the landgraves'
  overlordship. The Franconian line died out in 1340, leaving Kulmbach to the burgraves of
  Nuremberg, and Weimar passed to the Wettins in 1373.
