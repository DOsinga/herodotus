description: >-
  The Khorshidi dynasty ruled Lesser Luristan, the northern half of the Lur country in the Zagros
  Mountains, from its capital Khorramabad between about 1184 and 1597. Its rulers, who styled
  themselves atabegs and often bore the name Abbasi, came from a Lur clan and took their name
  from the founder Shoja al-Din Khorshid. Their neighbours in Greater Luristan, the Hazaraspids,
  held more land and the main routes, but the Khorshidis outlasted them. They paid tribute to the
  Ilkhans, the Timurids and the Turkmen dynasties in turn while governing their mountain country
  themselves, and the Lur identity took shape under their rule. In the sixteenth century they
  were caught between the Safavids and the Ottomans, and Shah Abbas I removed the last atabeg in
  1597 and replaced him with a governor.
