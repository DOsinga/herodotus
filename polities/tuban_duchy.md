description: >-
  Tuban was a port principality on the north coast of East Java, between Rembang and Gresik. It had
  been one of Majapahit's main harbours and an early centre of Islam, linked with the saint Sunan
  Bonang, while its regents stayed loyal to the Hindu court inland. Demak conquered Tuban around
  1527. As Demak fell apart after the death of Sultan Trenggana in 1546, Tuban's regents governed
  the town and its hinterland on their own and joined the alliance of eastern Javanese
  principalities led by Surabaya. The town was well fortified and drove off Mataram attacks in 1598
  and 1599. Sultan Agung of Mataram conquered it in 1619, one of the steps towards the fall of
  Surabaya.
