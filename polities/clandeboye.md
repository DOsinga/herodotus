description: >-
  Clandeboye was a Gaelic Irish lordship in eastern Ulster, held by a branch of the O'Neills
  and recorded here from 1347 to 1603. As the Earldom of Ulster collapsed after the murder of
  William de Burgh in 1333, the descendants of Aodh Buidhe O'Neill crossed the Bann and took
  over much of the earldom in what are now Antrim and Down. Muirchertach Ceannfada was the
  first king of Clandeboye separate from Tyrone. The lordship stayed independent of both
  Tyrone and the English colony and beat off invasions from Tyrone in 1444 and 1476. It later
  split into Upper and Lower Clandeboye. Elizabeth I granted its land to English colonists
  from 1571, and the lords lost their lands after 1603.
