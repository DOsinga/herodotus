description: >-
  The Kingdom of Naples occupied mainland southern Italy, with Naples as its capital. It
  emerged from the division of the Kingdom of Sicily after the Sicilian Vespers in 1282.
  Angevin kings ruled first, followed by the Aragonese conquest under Alfonso V in 1442.
  Competition between French and Spanish monarchs ended its independent government in the
  early sixteenth century; Spanish and later Austrian rulers administered it through their
  representatives. Charles of Bourbon restored a locally governed monarchy in 1734. During
  the Napoleonic wars Joseph Bonaparte and Joachim Murat ruled as kings, introducing reforms
  and confronting British supported Bourbon resistance. The Bourbons returned in 1815. In
  1816 Naples and Sicily were formally joined as the Kingdom of the Two Sicilies under
  Ferdinand.
