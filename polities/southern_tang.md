description: >-
  Southern Tang ruled part of southern China from 937 until Li Yu's surrender in January
  976. Li Bian founded the monarchy by replacing the Wu regime, presenting his dynasty as a
  continuation of the earlier Tang. Its capital was Jinling, today's Nanjing. The state
  expanded into neighbouring Min and Chu territories, but expensive campaigns strained its
  resources. Productive agriculture, silk making, trade, and court patronage supported a
  flourishing regional culture. Defeat by Later Zhou in 958 cost it northern territory and
  forced submission, yet its rulers retained their government. Tribute continued under the
  Song, which pressed for reunification. Song armies finally captured Jinling. Li Yu, also
  remembered for his poetry, became a captive, and his kingdom entered the Song empire.
