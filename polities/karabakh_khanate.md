description: >-
  The Karabakh Khanate was a khanate in the southern Caucasus, between the Kura and Aras
  rivers, which governed itself from 1748 until it became a Russian protectorate in 1805.
  Panah Ali Khan of the Javanshir tribe seized most of Karabakh after Nader Shah's death and
  built the fortress of Shusha as his capital. He and his son Ibrahim Khalil Khan subdued
  several of the Armenian meliks of the highlands and fought the neighbouring khans, Georgia
  and the rulers of Iran. Ibrahim Khalil defended Shusha against Agha Mohammad Khan Qajar in
  1795, but the shah took the town in 1797 shortly before his murder there. Facing renewed
  Iranian pressure, Ibrahim Khalil accepted Russian protection by the Treaty of Kurakchay in
  1805, and Russia abolished the khanate in 1822.
