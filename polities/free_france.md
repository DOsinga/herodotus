description: >-
  Free France was the wartime government and resistance movement led by Charles de Gaulle
  between 1940 and 1944. Established after France's defeat by Germany, it rejected the
  armistice and challenged the legitimacy of the Vichy regime. Although de Gaulle
  initially worked from London, Free France also governed territories that rallied to him,
  especially French Equatorial Africa and Cameroon. Brazzaville became its African capital
  and a base for administration, recruitment and military operations. Colonial soldiers
  and resources played a central part in the Allied war effort. Its institutions developed
  from the Empire Defense Council into broader national committees. Based in Algiers from
  1943, the leadership became the Provisional Government of the French Republic in 1944 as
  liberation of metropolitan France approached.
