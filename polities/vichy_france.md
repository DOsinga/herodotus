description: >-
  Vichy France was the authoritarian French State led by Philippe Pétain from 1940 to 1944 after
  France's defeat by Germany. Its government operated from Vichy rather than occupied Paris and
  replaced republican politics with personal rule and the National Revolution programme. It
  initially administered an unoccupied southern zone and retained authority over parts of the
  colonial empire, while accepting German demands. The regime pursued collaboration, imposed
  antisemitic laws, and helped deport Jews to Nazi camps. Free France and resistance movements
  challenged its authority. This record ends when German and Italian forces occupied the southern
  zone in November 1942. The regime lingered under occupation until liberation in 1944, when the
  Provisional Government restored republican authority.
