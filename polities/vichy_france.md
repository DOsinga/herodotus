description: >-
  Vichy France was the authoritarian French State led by Philippe Pétain from 1940 to 1944
  after France's defeat by Germany. Its government operated from Vichy rather than occupied Paris and
  replaced republican politics with personal rule and the National Revolution programme. It initially administered an
  unoccupied southern zone and retained authority over parts of the colonial empire, while accepting German
  demands. The regime pursued collaboration, imposed antisemitic laws, and helped deport Jews to Nazi camps.
  Free France and resistance movements challenged its authority. German and Italian occupation of the southern
  zone in 1942 further stripped it of independence. Allied advances and liberation ended its rule
  in 1944, when the Provisional Government restored republican authority and rejected the legitimacy of Vichy.
