description: >-
  The Far Eastern Republic existed in eastern Siberia and the Russian Far East from 1920 to 1922
  during the closing stages of Russia's civil war. Created with Bolshevik support, it served as a
  buffer between Soviet Russia and territory occupied by Japan. Its government maintained
  formally separate republican institutions, a constitution, and an assembly, although Soviet
  influence strongly shaped its decisions and military forces. The first capital was
  Verkhneudinsk, later Ulan-Ude; Chita became the seat in October 1920 after anti-Bolshevik
  forces were driven out. Fighting and diplomacy gradually brought more eastern territory under
  its administration. Once Japanese troops withdrew and the remaining White government fell, the
  buffer no longer served its purpose. The republic joined Soviet Russia in November 1922.
