description: >-
  The Ryukyu Kingdom united Okinawa in 1429 under Shō Hashi and later extended across neighbouring islands between Japan and Taiwan. Its kings ruled from Shuri with court officials and councils, while Naha connected the islands to maritime trade. Tribute missions to China supported royal recognition and commercial privileges, helping Ryukyuan merchants link East and Southeast Asian ports. The kingdom developed a distinctive culture drawing on local traditions and overseas contacts. Satsuma invaded in 1609, imposing heavy obligations while leaving the royal court in place and preserving its Chinese tributary relationship. Japan's Meiji government progressively tightened control, redesignating the kingdom as a domain in 1872. In 1879 it abolished the monarchy's government and established Okinawa Prefecture, ending the kingdom recorded here.
