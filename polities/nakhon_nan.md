description: >-
  Nakhon Nan was a tributary state of Siam in the upper Nan valley of northern Thailand. The
  older kingdom of Nan had been taken by Lan Na in 1449 and later passed under Burmese rule,
  which grew direct from 1714. During the wars of the 1770s the town was deserted. In 1788
  the Burmese were driven out and Siam installed a local prince, Atthawarapanyo, whose line
  ruled Nan from then on. Its rulers resettled the valley, kept their own courts and levies,
  and fought alongside Chiang Mai and Lampang against the Burmese at Chiang Saen in 1804.
  Nan lost much of its eastern territory to French Indochina in 1893. In 1899 the town was
  placed in a Siamese monthon, ending its separate government.
