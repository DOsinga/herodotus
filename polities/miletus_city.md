description: >-
  Miletus was an Ionian Greek city on the coast of western Anatolia, near the mouth of the
  Maeander river. It founded dozens of colonies, most of them around the Black Sea, and was
  home to the philosophers Thales, Anaximander and Anaximenes. Under the tyrant Thrasybulus it
  held out against Lydia for twelve years and kept its independence by treaty. It submitted to
  Cyrus's general in 546 BCE. In 499 its tyrant Aristagoras led the Ionian Revolt, and in 494
  the Persians won at Lade and sacked the city. After the Greek victory at Mycale in 479
  Miletus was rebuilt on a grid plan and joined the Delian League. It went over to Sparta in
  412, and the King's Peace of 387 returned it to Persia.
