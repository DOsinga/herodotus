description: >-
  The Judicate of Arborea was one of the four kingdoms of medieval Sardinia, in the centre
  and west of the island. Its rulers, called judges, appear in records from the eleventh
  century, first at Tharros and from about 1070 at Oristano. The judge governed with an
  assembly of clergy and notables, the Corona de Logu, and the lands were divided into
  districts run by curators. Arborea outlived the other judicates, which fell to Pisa and
  Genoa in the thirteenth century. Under Mariano IV and his daughter Eleanor, whose law code
  stayed in force until 1827, it fought the Crown of Aragon for the island and at times held
  nearly all of it. After defeat at Sanluri in 1409, the last judge sold his rights to Aragon
  in 1420.
