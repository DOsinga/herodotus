description: >-
  The Corsican Republic governed much of Corsica from 1755 to 1769 during the island's struggle
  against Genoa. Pasquale Paoli led its government from Corte under a constitution, supported by
  representative assemblies and a developing administration. Genoa retained several coastal
  strongholds, so the republic never controlled the entire island. Paoli established courts,
  organised armed forces, encouraged economic development, and founded a university at Corte.
  Italian served as the official written language, while Corsican was widely spoken. The
  experiment attracted attention among European thinkers interested in constitutional government
  and national independence. Genoa transferred its claims to France in 1768. After resistance,
  French forces defeated the Corsicans at Ponte Novu in 1769. Paoli went into exile, and France
  took control of the island.
