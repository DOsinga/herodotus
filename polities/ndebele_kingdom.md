description: >-
  The Ndebele Kingdom arose under Mzilikazi in southern Africa from 1823, before moving
  north to Matabeleland in 1838. Built by migrating followers and conquered or incorporated
  communities, it became a multiethnic monarchy in western present day Zimbabwe. The king
  governed through military regiments and chiefs, drawing tribute and manpower from a
  network of settlements. Nguni language and customs shaped court life, while local
  identities persisted within the kingdom. After Mzilikazi's death, Lobengula secured the
  throne following a disputed succession and based his government at Bulawayo. Mining
  concessions and expanding British South Africa Company ambitions increased outside
  pressure. Company forces invaded in 1893, and Lobengula fled. His death in 1894 closes the
  kingdom's government here, before later resistance to colonial rule.
