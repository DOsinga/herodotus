description: >-
  The Austrian Empire united the Habsburg ruler's lands under an imperial title from 1804
  to 1867, with Vienna as its capital. Francis I proclaimed it amid Napoleon's rise,
  preserving his dynasty's position as the Holy Roman Empire approached dissolution. The
  monarchy encompassed German, Hungarian, Czech, Italian, South Slav and other
  populations with differing laws and institutions. It helped defeat Napoleon and shaped
  the European settlement at the Congress of Vienna. Metternich's government opposed
  revolutionary change, but uprisings in 1848 challenged both imperial authority and
  established social arrangements. Franz Joseph restored central control before later
  parliamentary constitutional reforms. Defeat by Prussia in 1866 accelerated
  negotiations with Hungarian leaders. The compromise of 1867 transformed the empire into
  the dual monarchy of Austria-Hungary.
