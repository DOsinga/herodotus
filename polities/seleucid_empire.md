description: >-
  The Seleucid Empire was a Hellenistic monarchy conventionally dated from Seleucus I's recovery
  of Babylon in 312 BCE to Roman annexation in 63 BCE. At its height it stretched from Anatolia
  and Syria across Mesopotamia and Iran into Central Asia. Seleucia on the Tigris and Antioch
  were major royal centres. Kings governed through provincial officials, military settlements,
  and cities, combining Macedonian royal traditions with institutions inherited from earlier
  empires. Greek settlement and cultural exchange accompanied repeated wars against Ptolemaic
  Egypt and other neighbours. Bactrian independence, Parthian expansion, Roman victories, and
  civil conflicts gradually reduced the realm to Syria. Tigranes of Armenia occupied that remnant
  before royal rule returned. Pompey removed the surviving monarchy and incorporated Syria into
  Rome's provincial system.
