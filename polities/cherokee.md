description: >-
  The Cherokee were an Iroquoian-speaking people of the southern Appalachians whose towns
  lay in the river valleys of present-day North Carolina, Tennessee, South Carolina and
  Georgia, grouped into the Overhill, Middle, Valley and Lower settlements. Each town had
  its own council. In the eighteenth century a national leadership grew out of trade and
  diplomacy with the British: Moytoy of Tellico was recognised as head of the nation in
  1730, and the Overhill town of Chota became its capital under leaders such as Old Hop and
  Attakullakulla. The Cherokee fought the British in 1759–1761 and sided with them in the
  American Revolution. Dragging Canoe's followers fought on until 1794, after the Treaty of
  Holston had put the nation under the protection of the United States.
