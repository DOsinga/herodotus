description: >-
  The Emirate of Nekor was a Muslim state in the Rif of northern Morocco, governed by the Banu Salih
  from 710 to about 1015. Its founder, Salih ibn Mansur, gained local authority and encouraged
  neighboring Berber communities to adopt Islam. The rulers first lived at Temsamane, then
  established their seat at Nekor around 760. Their government combined dynastic leadership with
  relationships among surrounding tribal groups. The emirate faced internal disputes, a destructive
  Viking raid in 859, and repeated pressure from Fatimid forces. Its rulers nonetheless recovered
  their position rather than disappearing permanently after each attack. Connections across the
  wider western Mediterranean tied Nekor to wider commercial and political networks. The dynasty's
  government ended before the city's later destruction in 1080.
