description: >-
  Chūzan was the central kingdom of Okinawa during the Sanzan period, from 1314 until the
  island's unification in 1429. Its kings initially governed from Urasoe through relationships with local lords,
  whose cooperation could not always be secured. Hokuzan in the north and Nanzan in the
  south emerged as competing kingdoms after the island's earlier authority fragmented. Under Satto, Chūzan developed
  commercial and diplomatic ties with China, Korea, and Southeast Asia. Tribute relations with the Ming
  began in 1372, helping open markets and strengthen royal standing. Chinese settlers contributed maritime and
  administrative skills. Shō Hashi's family seized power and defeated the rival kingdoms. The conquest of
  Nanzan in 1429 established the Ryukyu Kingdom, although Chūzan remained an important official name afterward.
