description: >-
  The Konbaung dynasty governed Burma from 1752 to 1885, creating the last Burmese monarchy
  before British annexation. Alaungpaya founded the state during resistance to the restored
  Hanthawaddy kingdom and reunited much of the country. His successors expanded into Arakan,
  Assam, Manipur, and neighbouring territories, fought Siam, and resisted Qing invasions. Kings
  ruled through a royal council, officials, provincial governors, and inherited service
  obligations, while supporting Theravada Buddhist institutions. The court moved among several
  capitals, including Ava, Amarapura, and finally Mandalay. Expansion westward brought conflict
  with British India. Three Anglo-Burmese wars progressively stripped away the kingdom's
  territory despite administrative and economic reforms. In 1885 British forces occupied Mandalay
  and deposed Thibaw, ending royal government and absorbing the kingdom into their empire.
