description: >-
  The Dali Kingdom governed much of present Yunnan from its capital near Lake Erhai between
  937 and 1253. Duan Siping founded its monarchy after a series of short lived successors
  to Nanzhao. The Duan court drew on Chinese written culture while ruling a population associated
  especially with the Bai. Buddhism shaped royal patronage, temples, and political legitimacy, and several kings
  retired into religious life. Trade with Song China included prized horses, while tributary relations did
  not remove Dali's own government. Gao Shengtai replaced the dynasty with the Dazhong Kingdom in
  1094; Duan rule returned in 1096, although Gao ministers remained powerful. Mongol invasion under Kublai
  ended the independent kingdom in 1253, but Duan descendants continued as subordinate regional rulers afterward.
