description: >-
  Ui Maine, also called Hy Many, was a Gaelic kingdom in eastern Connacht, chiefly
  around modern Galway and Roscommon. Its origin around 400 is an estimate drawn from
  traditions about Maine Mor, not a dated foundation. Kings from related ruling
  families, especially the O'Kellys, governed a landscape of subordinate lordships and
  communities. Succession followed Gaelic practices such as tanistry rather than a
  fixed rule of eldest sons inheriting. Connacht's greater kings claimed overlordship,
  but Ui Maine maintained its own rulers and political interests. Genealogies, annals,
  and customary texts preserve its families, privileges, and relationships with
  neighbors. Tudor expansion eventually replaced this territorial government. The
  Composition of Connacht in 1585 brought its governing lands under crown
  administration, although chiefly titles survived.
