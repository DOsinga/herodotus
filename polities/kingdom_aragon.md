description: >-
  The Kingdom of Aragon emerged in the Pyrenees of northeastern Iberia after Sancho III of
  Navarre died in 1035. Ramiro received Aragon and expanded his inheritance with neighbouring
  Sobrarbe and Ribagorza, establishing a separate royal power. Its hereditary monarchy drew
  support from nobles and religious institutions while advancing south into Muslim ruled lands.
  Jaca served as an early centre before conquest shifted the capital to Huesca in 1096 and
  Zaragoza in 1118. Alfonso the Battler's death in 1134 created a succession crisis and ended the
  shared monarchy with Navarre. Petronilla's marriage agreement with Ramon Berenguer IV of
  Barcelona in 1137 prepared a dynastic connection. Their son Alfonso II inherited both realms in
  1162, beginning the Crown of Aragon represented separately.
