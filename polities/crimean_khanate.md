description: >-
  The Crimean Khanate ruled Crimea and neighbouring Black Sea steppes from 1441 to 1783. Haci
  Giray established it as the Golden Horde fragmented, and his dynasty supplied its khans.
  Bakhchysarai became their chief seat, while noble clans exercised influence over government.
  Ottoman intervention from 1475 brought dependence, including the sultan's later appointment
  of khans, but the rulers continued conducting their own steppe diplomacy and wars. Cavalry
  campaigns and raids made Crimea important in struggles involving Russia, Poland and the
  Ottoman Empire; enslavement and captive trading were major features of its economy. Russian
  expansion gradually reduced its power. The treaty of 1774 removed Ottoman overlordship
  without securing lasting independence. Russia annexed Crimea in 1783, ending the khanate and
  its royal government.
