description: >-
  The Electorate of Hesse governed lands around Kassel in Germany after its restoration in
  1813, lasting until Prussian annexation in 1866. It continued Hesse-Kassel, whose ruler
  had received electoral rank before Napoleon incorporated the territory into Westphalia.
  The restored princes retained their electoral title even though the Holy Roman Empire no
  longer existed. Membership in the German Confederation left the state with its own
  government. Conflict between conservative rulers and elected representatives dominated its
  politics, particularly over the liberal constitution granted in 1831. Austrian and
  Bavarian intervention helped the elector suppress constitutional resistance in 1850. When
  Hesse backed Austria against Prussia in 1866, Prussian troops occupied Kassel, removed the
  ruler and incorporated the electorate into their expanding kingdom that year.
