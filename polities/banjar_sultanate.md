description: >-
  Banjar was a Muslim sultanate in southern Borneo, centred on the lower Barito river around
  Banjarmasin. It grew out of a civil war in the Hindu kingdom of Negara Daha, which Raden Samudera
  won with help from Demak. He converted to Islam and ruled as Sultan Suriansyah from 1526. Banjar
  became a major pepper exporter and extended its influence along the southern and western coasts of
  Borneo. Under Mustain Billah in the early seventeenth century it beat off a Dutch attack on
  Banjarmasin, and its capital later moved inland to Martapura. Succession quarrels let the Dutch East
  India Company gain economic privileges from 1679. In 1826 the sultanate became a Dutch protectorate,
  and the Dutch abolished it in 1860 during the Banjar War.
