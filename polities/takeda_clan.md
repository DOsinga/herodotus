description: >-
  The Takeda were the Sengoku daimyo house of Kai province in the mountains west of Edo, ruling
  in their own right from the Ōnin War in 1467 until 1582. Hereditary military governors of
  Kai, they spent the late fifteenth century fighting rebellious vassals and their own
  branches. Takeda Nobutora united the province and in 1519 built a new seat at Tsutsujigasaki,
  around which Kōfu grew. His son Takeda Shingen, who deposed him in 1541, conquered Shinano,
  fought Uesugi Kenshin five times at Kawanakajima and took Suruga from the Imagawa in 1569.
  He was famed for his cavalry, his law code and his flood works. His son Katsuyori lost at
  Nagashino in 1575, and Oda Nobunaga destroyed the house in 1582.
