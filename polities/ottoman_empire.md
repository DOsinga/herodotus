description: >-
  The Ottoman Empire began as Osman's frontier principality in northwestern Anatolia around 1299 and
  lasted until the sultanate's abolition in 1922. Its hereditary sultans expanded into the Balkans and
  captured Constantinople in 1453, making the city their imperial capital. At its height the empire
  governed lands across southeastern Europe, western Asia, and North Africa. Provincial officials,
  military households, and religious communities formed a varied system under the sultan and grand
  vizier. Ottoman rulers competed with European and Asian powers while overseeing major trade routes
  and cities. Nineteenth century reforms attempted to reshape administration and citizenship, followed
  by constitutional experiments. Defeat in the First World War and the Turkish nationalist struggle
  ended imperial government, leaving the new Turkish state in control.
