description: >-
  Germiyan was a Turkish beylik in western Anatolia, ruled by hereditary Turkish beys from
  around 1300, with Kütahya as its main centre. It emerged as Seljuk authority weakened
  and initially commanded influence over several neighbouring frontier principalities. Its
  court supported Turkish literature, scholarship, and religious buildings. Pressure from
  Karaman and the Ottomans led Suleiman to arrange his daughter's marriage to Bayezid,
  transferring Kütahya and other lands as her dowry. Ottoman conquest interrupted the
  remaining beylik, but Timur restored Yakub II after defeating Bayezid at Ankara in 1402.
  A further interruption followed Karaman's invasion before Yakub returned in 1414.
  Without a surviving male heir, he left his lands to the Ottoman sultan, ending
  Germiyan's separate government upon his death in 1429.
