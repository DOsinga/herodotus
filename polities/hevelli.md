description: >-
  The Hevelli, also called Stodorani, were a West Slavic people on the middle Havel river in
  present Brandenburg. Their princes ruled from the island fortress of Brenna, the later town of
  Brandenburg an der Havel, with an eastern outpost at Spandau. A Hevellian princess, Drahomíra,
  married the Bohemian duke Vratislaus I. Henry the Fowler took Brenna in his campaign of 928 and
  929, and Otto I founded a bishopric there in 948. The great Slavic rising of 983 threw off
  German rule, and the Hevelli governed themselves again for more than a century and a half, often
  at war with Saxons and other Slavs. Their last prince, the baptised Pribislav-Henry, left his
  land to the Ascanian Albert the Bear, who took it after Pribislav's death in 1150.
