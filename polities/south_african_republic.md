description: >-
  The South African Republic was a Boer state north of the Vaal River, with Pretoria as its
  capital. Britain recognised its independence in the Sand River Convention of 1852. An
  elected president and the Volksraad governed a settler republic whose political rights
  excluded most African inhabitants. British annexation in 1877 interrupted its government,
  but a Boer uprising restored a provisional administration in December 1880 and secured
  victory in the First Boer War. Gold discoveries on the Witwatersrand transformed its
  economy and attracted foreign migrants, creating conflict over citizenship and British
  influence. Under Paul Kruger, the republic sought to preserve its independence. The Second
  Boer War brought prolonged fighting, occupation, and guerrilla resistance before the peace
  of 1902 ended the republic.
