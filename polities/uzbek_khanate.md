name: Uzbek Khanate

description: >-
  The Uzbek Khanate was the realm that Abu'l-Khayr Khan, a descendant of Jochi's son
  Shiban, built from the eastern lands of the Golden Horde between 1428 and his
  death in 1468. He first ruled the tribes of
  western Siberia from Chimgi-Tura, today's Tyumen, and drove out the khan of Sibir. In the
  1430s and 1440s he raided Khwarazm and took the towns of the middle Syr Darya, and from
  1446 Sighnaq became his main seat. The khan governed together with the leaders of the
  nomadic clans, whom Central Asian writers called Uzbeks. A heavy defeat by the Oirats
  weakened him in the 1450s, and the sultans Janibek and Kerei led many clans away to found
  the Kazakh Khanate. After his death the realm fell apart.
