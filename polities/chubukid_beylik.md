description: >-
  The Çubukoğulları Beylik was a small Turkish principality centred on the fortress of Harput,
  above the plain of modern Elazığ in eastern Anatolia, from about 1085 to 1112. Its founder
  Çubuk was a Seljuk commander who took Harput after the Seljuk victory at Manzikert and was
  confirmed as its lord by Sultan Malik-Shah. From Harput he conquered Palu, Genç, Çemişgezek
  and Eğin, ruling them under Seljuk suzerainty. He was known for changing sides in battle, and
  both at the defeat of the ruler of Mosul and at Ain Salm his switch decided the outcome. His
  son Mehmed succeeded him after 1092 and is attested at Harput in 1107. When Mehmed died, in
  1112 or 1113, the Artuqids took over Harput and its lands.
