description: >-
  The Emirate of Homs was a small Turkish principality in central Syria that formed in the
  breakup of Seljuk rule after Tutush I died in 1095. Its first emir, Janah ad-Dawla, had
  been guardian and commander for Tutush's son Ridwan of Aleppo, but the two fell out in
  1097 and Janah made Homs his own seat. He joined Kerbogha's army against the Crusaders at
  Antioch in 1098, while Homs itself held out against Crusader raids and became a base for
  attacks on the coast. Janah was murdered by Assassins in 1103, probably at Ridwan's
  urging. The Turkish emir Qiraja then took the city, and he and his son Khirkhan held it
  between the rival powers of Aleppo and Damascus until the Burids of Damascus took control
  in 1135.
