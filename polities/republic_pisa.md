description: >-
  Pisa emerged around 1000 as a self-governing maritime city in Tuscany, using its fleets
  and merchants to build Mediterranean influence. Its government passed through consuls,
  councils, and powerful individual lords rather than remaining one unchanged republican
  system. Pisan expeditions fought Muslim powers, supported crusading ventures, and
  established commercial privileges overseas. The city competed with Genoa over Sardinia,
  Corsica, and sea routes. Genoa's victory at Meloria in 1284 severely weakened its naval
  position, while Florence threatened it inland. Milan took control in 1399; separate
  government resumed in 1402 before Florence conquered Pisa in 1406. The city recovered
  independence during the French invasion of Italy in 1494. A prolonged struggle followed,
  ending with its final surrender to Florence in 1509 after sieges.
