description: >-
  The Deva kingdom was a Hindu state of southeastern Bengal in the thirteenth century, after
  Sena power had faded and while the Muslim governors of Lakhnauti held the northwest. Its
  founder Purushottamadeva rose from village headman, and his son Madhusudanadeva was the
  first to call himself king. Damodaradeva, who reigned from 1231 to 1243, left copper plates
  showing that he held the Comilla, Noakhali and Chittagong country. A later king,
  Dasharathadeva, known to Muslim chroniclers as Danuj Rai, took Bikrampur and then moved his
  seat to Suvarnagrama, later Sonargaon. In 1281 he agreed to help the Delhi sultan Balban
  against the rebel governor Tughral. His brother Vikramadityadeva withdrew eastwards in 1294,
  and the Lakhnauti sultans took Sonargaon soon afterwards.
