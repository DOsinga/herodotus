description: >-
  The Duchy of Legnica was one of the duchies of Silesia, governed from the castle at Legnica in
  Lower Silesia. It was created in 1248, when Bolesław II the Bald gave up Wrocław to his brother
  and kept Legnica, and a branch of the Silesian Piasts ruled it until 1675. In 1329 the dukes
  paid homage to John of Bohemia and became vassals of the Bohemian crown, but they went on
  governing their duchy. They divided it repeatedly and joined it with Brzeg, which the same line
  held for long periods. Frederick II brought in the Reformation in the 1520s. When the last duke,
  George William, died in 1675, Legnica passed to the direct rule of the Habsburg emperor Leopold
  I.
