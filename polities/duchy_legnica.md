description: >-
  The Duchy of Legnica was one of the duchies of Silesia, governed from the castle at Legnica in
  Lower Silesia. It was created in 1248, when Bolesław II the Bald, eldest son of Henry II the
  Pious, gave up Wrocław to his brother and kept Legnica. A local branch of the Silesian Piasts
  ruled it for more than four centuries. Bolesław's quarrels with his brothers and neighbours were
  constant, and his son Henry V the Fat briefly reunited Legnica with Wrocław. The dukes divided
  the land again and again, splitting off Jawor, Brzeg and other duchies. In 1329 Duke Bolesław
  III paid homage to John of Bohemia, and Legnica became a fief of the Bohemian crown, though its
  Piast dukes ruled on until the line died out in 1675.
