description: >-
  The Kalachuri kingdom of Tripuri governed the Chedi region of central India, with its
  capital at present day Tewar near Jabalpur. Its beginnings around 675 are associated with
  Vamaraja, although the dynasty's origins remain uncertain. Kings built power through
  warfare, marriage alliances and shifting relationships with the Rashtrakutas and
  Pratiharas. Gangeyadeva adopted imperial titles after successful campaigns in the eleventh
  century, and his son Lakshmikarna briefly expanded into several neighbouring Indian
  territories. Royal patronage supported religious establishments, especially those connected
  with Shiva. Later rulers lost northern lands and faced pressure from the Chandelas,
  Paramaras and other neighbours. Trailokyamalla is securely attested in 1212, but the
  kingdom's disappearance around 1220 is an estimate rather than a known conquest or recorded
  abdication.
