name: "Paramara Kingdom of Malwa"
description: >-
  The Paramara Kingdom of Malwa ruled west central India from an uncertain early
  beginning around 800 until Delhi's conquest in 1305. Its early rulers probably
  governed under Rashtrakuta overlordship; secure inscriptions begin only in the tenth
  century. Siyaka's attack on Manyakheta around 972 established an independent royal
  power. Dhara became its principal capital, with Mandu gaining importance later. The
  kingdom reached its greatest prominence under Bhoja, remembered for scholarship,
  building projects, and support for Sanskrit learning. Wars against neighbouring
  Chaulukya, Chalukya, Kalachuri, and Chandela rulers repeatedly expanded and reduced
  its lands. Rival attacks damaged Dhara and weakened the monarchy. Alauddin Khalji's
  forces defeated and killed Mahalakadeva in 1305. Later Paramara chiefs do not
  necessarily represent continued government of this kingdom.
