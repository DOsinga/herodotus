description: >-
  Dou Jiande's Xia was a rebel kingdom in China during the collapse of the Sui dynasty,
  lasting from 617 to 621. Dou gathered agrarian rebels and defeated rival commanders,
  first governing under the title Prince of Changle before adopting Xia in 618. His court
  moved to Mingzhou, and his authority spread across much of present-day Hebei. He
  recruited officials and developed a government rather than remaining simply the leader
  of a roaming army. Competition with the rising Tang and Wang Shichong's Zheng shaped its
  brief existence. When Tang forces threatened Zheng, Dou marched south to help, fearing
  his own kingdom would be next. Li Shimin captured him at Hulao in 621. Xia surrendered,
  ending before Dou's subsequent execution in captivity.
