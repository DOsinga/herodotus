description: >-
  The Emirate of Nejd was the second Saudi state, centred on Riyadh in central Arabia from
  1824 to 1891. Turki bin Abdullah rebuilt Saudi rule after Egyptian forces had destroyed
  the earlier state at Diriyah. Its rulers used the title imam and maintained the alliance
  between the Saudi family and religious scholars associated with Wahhabi Islam. Faisal bin
  Turki strengthened control over oasis towns and tribal groups, sometimes acknowledging
  Ottoman supremacy while governing locally. After his death in 1865, rival sons fought for
  Riyadh and weakened the state. The Rashidi rulers of Ha'il exploited those disputes,
  extending their power across Nejd. Defeat at Mulayda in 1891 ended the emirate, sending
  the surviving Saudi leadership into exile before its later return.
