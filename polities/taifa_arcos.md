description: >-
  The Taifa of Arcos was a Muslim kingdom in the southwest of al-Andalus, centred on the cliff-top
  town of Arcos de la Frontera in present Cádiz province. Its rulers, the Banu Khizrun, were a
  family of the Zenata Berbers who seized the district of Sidonia from its Umayyad governor during
  the civil war that broke up the Caliphate of Córdoba. They governed Arcos and the surrounding
  countryside as one of several small Berber taifas between Seville and the Strait of Gibraltar.
  From the 1040s the expanding taifa of Seville under al-Mu'tadid absorbed one neighbour after
  another. Seville took Arcos in 1068. When Almoravid rule collapsed in the 1140s, the town
  briefly governed itself again before the Almohads took it in 1145.
