description: >-
  Melayu was a Buddhist monarchy associated with the Batanghari River basin of Sumatra, in
  present Indonesia. This record begins with an embassy in 645, conventionally linked to the
  later Jambi kingdom, and resumes with a renewed government in 1028. River routes connected
  coastal trade with gold from the Minangkabau highlands. The Chinese monk Yijing visited Melayu
  while travelling to India and later reported its absorption by Srivijaya, recorded here in 692.
  Its subsequent recovery is less clearly documented than its later royal inscriptions. The court
  shifted inland to Dharmasraya, where rulers maintained ties with Java and received images. In
  1347 Adityawarman established his government at Pagaruyung, marking the transition to the
  successor kingdom rather than the disappearance of Melayu identity.
