description: >-
  Eastern Xia, also called Dongzhen, was a Jurchen kingdom in Manchuria that lasted from 1215
  to 1233, during the Mongol conquest of the Jin dynasty. Its founder Puxian Wannu was a Jin
  commander sent to Liaodong to fight the Khitan rebel Yelü Liuge. After defeat there, and
  with Jin collapsing under Mongol attack, he broke with the Jin and founded his own state at
  Liaoyang in 1215, taking the title Heavenly King and copying Jin institutions. He first
  accepted Mongol overlordship and sent his son as a hostage, but in 1217 he killed the Mongol
  envoys and moved east to a new capital near modern Yanji. From there he ruled the eastern
  Jurchen lands and dealt with Goryeo until a Mongol army under Güyük captured and killed him
  in 1233.
