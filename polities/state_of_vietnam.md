description: >-
  The State of Vietnam was a government based in Saigon, formed in 1949 but represented here
  only during its independent phase from 1954 to 1955. France had previously retained
  control of defence and foreign relations through the French Union. Former emperor Bảo Đại
  served as chief of state, with ministers conducting government under a framework that
  retained elements of monarchy. It claimed all Vietnam while competing with the communist
  Democratic Republic of Vietnam. The Geneva settlement in 1954 left it administering the
  south after regrouping, while large numbers of refugees moved southward. Prime Minister
  Ngô Đình Diệm consolidated power against rivals and challenged Bảo Đại. Following a
  disputed referendum in 1955, Diệm proclaimed the Republic of Vietnam, ending this regime.
