description: >-
  The Taifa of Majorca was a Muslim island kingdom based at Palma in the western
  Mediterranean. This record covers its separate government from 1076 to 1116, after the
  mainland kingdom of Dénia fell and its Balearic possessions became independent. Local
  rulers had already gained considerable freedom before that separation. Emirs governed
  the islands within the political and cultural world of al-Andalus, while their position
  at sea brought them into conflict with neighbouring Christian powers. A Christian
  expedition attacked the islands in 1114 and captured Palma the following year. The
  attackers withdrew rather than establish lasting government. Almoravid forces
  subsequently incorporated the islands into their empire in 1116. The later Banu Ghaniya
  realm belongs to a different phase of island government.
