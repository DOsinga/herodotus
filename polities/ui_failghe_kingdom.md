description: >-
  Ui Failghe was a Gaelic kingdom in the Irish midlands, covering parts of present Offaly, Kildare, and Laois. Its beginning around 500 is estimated from traditions concerning Rus Failge, an early sixth-century ruler. Native kings governed within the wider politics of Leinster, resisting pressure from neighboring Irish powers and later Norman settlers while losing some territory. From the eleventh century the ruling family became known as the O'Connor Faly, distinct from similarly named dynasties elsewhere. Their seat moved from Rathangan to Daingean after the Norman arrival. Tudor conquest and plantation brought the kingdom's separate government to an end in 1556, when the crown created King's County. The family survived that change, and the county name Offaly preserves the kingdom's memory.
