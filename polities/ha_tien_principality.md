description: >-
  The Principality of Hà Tiên was a port state on the Gulf of Thailand founded by Mạc Cửu,
  a Chinese exile who settled there with Cambodian permission late in the
  seventeenth century. In 1707 he put the town under the protection of the Nguyễn lords
  but the Mạc family governed it themselves and
  fought their own wars. Under his son Mạc Thiên Tứ, Hà Tiên beat a
  Cambodian army in 1739, placed its own candidate on the Cambodian throne in 1758 and
  became a centre of Chinese letters. Taksin's Siamese army sacked it
  in 1771 and held it until 1773. When the Tây Sơn overran the south in 1777, Mạc Thiên Tứ
  fled to Siam, and later Mạc rulers were appointed by Siam or the Nguyễn.
