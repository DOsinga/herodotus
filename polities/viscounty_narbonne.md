description: >-
  The Viscounty of Narbonne was a feudal lordship around the port city of Narbonne in
  Languedoc, recorded here from 878 to 1229. The Carolingian counts of Narbonne were gradually
  replaced by viscounts, who from about 878 governed the city in their own right while
  nominally subject to the counts of Toulouse. The archbishops of Narbonne held much of the
  city and shared its lordship with them. Viscountess Ermengarde, who ruled from 1134 to 1192,
  made her court a centre of troubadour poetry and took a leading part in the politics of the
  region. After her death the city passed to her nephews of the Castilian house of Lara.
  Aimery III submitted to the crusaders, and after 1229 royal officials governed the region.
