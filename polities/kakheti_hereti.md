description: >-
  The Kingdom of Kakheti-Hereti occupied eastern Georgian lands from 1014 to 1104, with Telavi as its capital. Kvirike III recovered local power after the death of Bagrat III of Georgia and brought neighbouring Hereti under his rule, a process continuing into the following decade. He organised the realm into seven duchies and worked with neighbouring rulers against shared enemies. Later kings belonged to the Kuirikid family and balanced alliances with Georgia against efforts by Georgian monarchs to absorb their kingdom. Seljuk pressure brought tribute obligations without immediately replacing the royal government. In 1104, David IV of Georgia secured the removal of the last king and defeated opposition to annexation, incorporating Kakheti-Hereti into his kingdom and ending its separate royal government.
