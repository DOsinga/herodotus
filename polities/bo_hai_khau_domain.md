description: >-
  Bố Hải Khẩu was a local domain in northern Vietnam, centred near present Thái Bình and
  governed by Trần Lãm during the tenth century. Its precise foundation is unknown; this
  record begins around 950, when his government was already functioning. Trần was one of
  the powerful regional leaders associated with the Period of the Twelve Warlords,
  following the breakdown of central authority. He held territory and commanded troops
  under the title Trần Minh Công. Đinh Bộ Lĩnh entered his service, swore allegiance and
  became his adopted son. This relationship gave Đinh access to a substantial military
  base for his later campaigns. When Trần died in 967, Đinh inherited his army and
  territory, absorbing the domain into his developing unified state.
