description: >-
  Clusium, modern Chiusi in Tuscany, was one of the leading Etruscan cities, set on a hill above
  the Chiana valley in the inland north of Etruria. Its rich farmland and position on routes
  towards the Tiber made it powerful. Roman tradition made its king Lars Porsenna the leader who
  besieged Rome after the expulsion of its kings around 508 BCE. Clusium was famous for its
  cinerary urns, painted tombs and canopic jars. In 391 BCE a Gallic army attacked the city, an
  episode that led the Gauls on to sack Rome. Clusium later came within Rome's sphere, and after
  the Roman victory at Sentinum in 295 BCE it became a Roman ally.
