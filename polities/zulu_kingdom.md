description: >-
  The Zulu Kingdom arose in southeastern Africa when Shaka became ruler in 1816, expanding across
  much of present KwaZulu-Natal. Its monarchy integrated conquered communities through diplomacy,
  warfare and regiments organised by age. Royal settlements served as centres of government, with
  the king directing military service and controlling important resources. Shaka's successors
  faced succession struggles and growing pressure from Boer settlers and British colonies.
  Dingane's defeat at Blood River in 1838 altered the balance of power, while Mpande and
  Cetshwayo maintained the kingdom through later decades. British invasion in 1879 met a major
  defeat at Isandlwana before victory at Ulundi. That final battle ended independent royal
  government here; Britain divided the country, with subsequent annexation following despite
  attempts to restore kingship.
