name: Wassoulou Empire
description: >-
  The Wassoulou Empire, also called the Samorian state, was founded by Samori Ture in West Africa
  and lasted from 1878 to 1898. Initially centred on Bissandugu in present Guinea, it controlled lands
  in Mali and later expanded east into northern Ivory Coast. Samori ruled as faama and later
  almamy, combining royal authority with Islamic leadership. Provincial war chiefs and a army formed the core
  of government. Trade, gold and enslaved captives helped finance imported firearms, while local smiths repaired and
  manufactured weapons. Expansion brought conflict with neighbouring states and resistance to forced religious change. Facing French
  conquest, Samori moved his government and armies eastward, using scorched earth tactics. French forces captured him
  in 1898, bringing the state to an end.
