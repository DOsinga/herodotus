description: >-
  The Republic of Serbian Krajina was an unrecognised Serb state inside Croatia during the
  Yugoslav wars. Proclaimed in 1991, it governed from Knin through a president, government and
  assembly, with military and financial support from Serbia and Yugoslavia. Its leaders rejected
  Croatian independence and sought union with other Serb territories. Fighting and expulsions
  displaced many Croats and other non Serbs from areas it controlled. United Nations peacekeepers
  separated forces without settling the political dispute. Croatian offensives recovered Western
  Slavonia and most remaining territory in 1995, prompting a large flight of Serb civilians. Local
  Serb government survived in Eastern Slavonia, Baranja and Western Syrmia until United Nations
  administration began in January 1996, ahead of peaceful reintegration into Croatia completed in
  1998.
