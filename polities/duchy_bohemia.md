description: >-
  The Duchy of Bohemia developed around Prague in the late ninth century, with its start
  estimated here at 870. Přemyslid dukes gradually brought Czech communities under their
  rule and built a durable monarchy. Early connections with Great Moravia gave way to
  relations with East Frankish and German rulers. Christianity spread through the ducal
  court, and Prague gained a bishopric in 973. Bohemia became part of the Holy Roman
  Empire, yet its own dukes continued governing the country and pursued wars, alliances
  and expansion into Moravia. Dynastic rivalries repeatedly disrupted succession. Some
  dukes received personal royal titles before the lasting change in 1198, when Ottokar I
  secured hereditary kingship. The duchy then continued as the Kingdom of Bohemia under
  Přemyslid rule.
