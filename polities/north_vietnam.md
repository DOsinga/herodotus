description: >-
  The Democratic Republic of Vietnam was proclaimed by Ho Chi Minh in Hanoi in 1945 after Japan's surrender and the
  August Revolution. Its communist led government claimed all Vietnam, but fought France for control during the First Indochina War. Victory
  at Dien Bien Phu and the Geneva agreements of 1954 left it governing the northern zone, commonly called North Vietnam.
  Party leaders built a socialist state through land reform, state planning, and repression. Supported by China and the Soviet Union,
  its army and southern allies fought South Vietnam and the United States in the Vietnam War. Communist forces captured Saigon
  in 1975. In 1976, the northern republic and southern revolutionary government were formally united as the Socialist Republic of Vietnam.
