description: >-
  The Raj of Sarawak was a kingdom in northwestern Borneo ruled by the English Brooke family. In 1841
  the Sultan of Brunei made James Brooke rajah of the land around Kuching, as a reward for putting
  down a revolt. Brooke and his nephew Charles, who succeeded him in 1868, widened the state step by
  step along the coast at Brunei's expense. The United States recognised Sarawak in 1850 and Britain
  in 1864. The rajahs ruled through a small European service and local chiefs, fought piracy and
  headhunting, and faced several revolts, including a Chinese rising in 1857. Charles Brooke
  encouraged Chinese settlement and pepper and rubber planting. In 1888 Sarawak became a British
  protected state, which ended its control of its own foreign affairs.
