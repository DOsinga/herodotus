description: >-
  Anhalt-Aschersleben was one of the three principalities created in 1252 when the sons of Henry I
  of Anhalt divided their father's lands. Henry II received the share around Aschersleben, on the
  northern edge of the Harz mountains, the old seat from which the Ascanian family had risen and
  taken its name of Askania. Its princes governed a small territory of farms, towns and castles
  and were drawn into the feuds of the neighbouring Saxon princes and bishops. The line was short-
  lived: when Prince Otto II died in 1315 without heirs, the Bishop of Halberstadt seized
  Aschersleben, and despite the claims of the other Anhalt lines the territory was lost to the
  family.
