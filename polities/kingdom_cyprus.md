description: >-
  The Kingdom of Cyprus was a crusader monarchy on the eastern Mediterranean island from 1192 to 1489. Guy of Lusignan acquired it after Richard I's conquest and the Templars' possession. The Lusignan rulers governed from Nicosia through a feudal system, with a Latin Catholic elite above a largely Greek Orthodox population. Cyprus became a base for crusading ventures and a commercial link between Europe and the Levant. Its kings also inherited claims to Jerusalem after the mainland crusader states declined. Genoese intervention weakened the monarchy, while a Mamluk victory in 1426 imposed tribute. Venetian influence subsequently grew around the court. Queen Catherine Cornaro surrendered the kingdom to Venice in 1489, replacing the island's royal government with administration by the republic.
