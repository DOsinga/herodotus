description: >-
  The Uqaylid Emirate of Qal'at Ja'bar was a small Arab principality on the middle
  Euphrates, held by a branch of the Uqaylid family after their main emirate at Mosul had
  fallen. In 1086 the Seljuk sultan Malik-Shah gave the fortress of Qal'at Ja'bar to Salim
  ibn Malik, the Uqaylid commander of Aleppo's citadel, in return for surrendering that
  city. When Seljuk authority broke up in the 1090s, Salim's descendants ruled the castle and
  nearby Raqqa for themselves, apart from a short Crusader occupation in 1102. Zengi took
  Raqqa in 1135 and was murdered by one of his own slaves while besieging the castle in 1146.
  Nur ad-Din finally took Qal'at Ja'bar in 1168 and rebuilt it, giving most of the walls
  that survive today.
