description: >-
  The Chanka were an Andean people of the Late Intermediate Period whose chiefdom centred on
  the Andahuaylas valley in the highlands of present-day Apurímac, Peru. According to
  colonial chronicles they came from the region of Castrovirreyna, conquered the Quechua
  communities of Andahuaylas and became the leaders of a loose alliance of neighbouring
  peoples such as the Vilcas and Huancas. Two chiefs, called the Uscovilca and the
  Ancovilca, are said to have ruled them, and hilltop settlements such as Sondor have been
  linked to them. The chronicles describe a Chanka attack on Cusco around 1438 that was
  defeated by the young prince who became Pachacuti, a victory that launched the Inca
  expansion. Archaeologists question how powerful and unified the Chanka really were.
