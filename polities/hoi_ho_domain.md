description: >-
  Hồi Hồ was the domain held by Kiều Thuận in northern Vietnam during the period of the Twelve
  Warlords. Its stronghold lay in the area of modern Cẩm Khê, Phú Thọ province. This record
  estimates separate rule from 965, as the Ngô government's authority broke down, until Đinh's
  reunification was completed in 968. Thuận was a grandson of Kiều Công Tiễn and the younger
  brother of another local ruler, Kiều Công Hãn. He adopted the title Kiều Lệnh Công and governed
  through his own military following, rather than a documented royal bureaucracy. His biography
  supplies little secure detail about his earlier career or defeat. The domain ended within Đinh
  Bộ Lĩnh's conquest, which replaced local governments with a new monarchy.
