description: >-
  Ixtonton was a Maya kingdom centred near Dolores in the upper Mopan valley of Guatemala. Its
  political history is represented approximately from 200 to 950, within a longer sequence of
  occupation extending beyond those dates. The city dominated the region during the Early Classic
  period, before Ixkun and Ixtutz developed as rivals. Plazas, temples, ballcourts, causeways,
  and residential groups show a regional capital supported by subordinate settlements. Its
  ceremonial core occupied an enlarged limestone hill, joining public architecture with a
  landscape of homes and agricultural spaces. Archaeology establishes the settlement hierarchy
  more clearly than individual rulers or royal succession. Terminal Classic evidence suggests
  continuing importance late in the city's history, but later activity does not prove that its
  kingdom survived.
