description: >-
  The Baku Khanate was a small khanate on the Absheron Peninsula of the Caspian Sea, centred
  on the walled city of Baku, which governed itself from 1747 to 1806. Its khans descended
  from Dargah Quli Bey, a governor under Nader Shah. Baku's harbour, oil wells and salt pans
  gave the khanate an income out of proportion to its size, and its trade linked Iran with
  Astrakhan and the Volga. From the 1760s the khans depended on Fath Ali Khan of Quba, who
  married into their family and kept them in his orbit. After his death they balanced
  between Russia and Qajar Iran. Hossein Qoli Khan had the Russian commander Tsitsianov
  killed at the city gates in 1806, but Russian troops took Baku later that year and annexed
  the khanate.
