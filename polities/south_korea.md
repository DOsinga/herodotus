description: >-
  South Korea, officially the Republic of Korea, has governed the southern Korean Peninsula since
  1948, with Seoul as its capital. It emerged from the division imposed after Japanese colonial rule
  ended in 1945. North Korea's invasion in 1950 began a devastating war, which ended with an armistice
  in 1953 rather than a peace treaty. The republic experienced authoritarian civilian and military
  governments before mass protests helped secure democratic reform in 1987. Its present system
  combines an elected president with a National Assembly and independent courts. Rapid industrial
  growth transformed a poor agricultural economy into a manufacturing and technology centre.
  Relations with North Korea, the security alliance with the United States, and debates about
  democratic accountability remain central to public life.
