description: >-
  The Kanva dynasty was a monarchy associated with Magadha in northern India between 73
  and 28 BCE. Vasudeva Kanva, a minister at the Shunga court, founded it after
  overthrowing the last Shunga ruler, Devabhuti. Later traditions place the dynasty at
  Pataliputra, although coins found around Vidisha suggest that central India also
  mattered to its power. Four rulers are recorded, beginning with Vasudeva and ending
  with Susharman. Coins and literary accounts provide only a fragmentary picture of the
  state, making its exact territorial reach difficult to reconstruct. The Kanvas did not
  inherit every part of the former Shunga realm, and remnants of that family reportedly
  survived. According to the Puranas, Satavahana forces ended Kanva rule in 28 BCE,
  replacing its court.
