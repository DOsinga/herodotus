description: >-
  The Kingdom of Jumla was the strongest of the Baise states, the small kingdoms of the
  Karnali basin in western Nepal that followed the break-up of the Khasa Malla kingdom. It
  began around 1404, when Baliraja married the daughter of the last Malla ruler of the old
  kingdom and succeeded him. His Kalyal dynasty, who later took the name Shahi, ruled from the
  Jumla valley close to the old Khasa capital in Sinja. At its height Jumla reached from
  Mustang, which it took in 1760, westwards towards Kumaon, and it controlled trade routes
  into Tibet. It beat off a first Gorkha attack, but in 1789, during Bahadur Shah's regency,
  Gorkhali troops under Shiva Narayan Khatri conquered it with the help of its neighbours.
