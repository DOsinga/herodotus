description: >-
  The Beylik of Dulkadir was a Turkmen principality in southeastern Anatolia that governed
  the country around Elbistan and Maraş from 1337 to 1522. Zeynüddin Karaca Bey, chief of
  Oghuz clans settled on the frontier between the Mamluk Sultanate and the declining Mongol
  Ilkhanate, founded it with Mamluk support. Elbistan was the first capital and Maraş the
  later one. The beys spent most of their history balancing the Mamluks to the south
  against the rising Ottomans to the west, marrying into the Ottoman house and changing
  sides in their wars. The Mamluks and Ottomans each tried to place a friendly member of
  the family on the throne. After his victory over
  the Mamluks, Selim I killed Bey Alaüddevle in 1515, and Süleyman annexed the beylik in
  1522.
