description: >-
  The Mali Federation was a short-lived independent federation in West Africa,
  joining Senegal and the Sudanese Republic, now Mali, during 1960. A
  self-governing union had formed within the French Community in 1959, with its
  federal government based in Dakar. Modibo Keïta served as premier, Mamadou Dia
  as his deputy, and a federal assembly represented the members. Independence
  from France came on 20 June 1960. Leaders hoped to preserve regional
  cooperation beyond colonial borders, but differed over political authority,
  economic policy, and the balance between federal and member governments. An
  August confrontation involving the army and Senegalese gendarmerie brought the
  crisis to a head. Senegal withdrew on 20 August, leaving two separate states;
  the Sudanese Republic subsequently adopted the name Mali.
