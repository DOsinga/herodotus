description: >-
  The Confederate States of America was a breakaway republic in the southern United
  States from 1861 to 1865. Eleven slaveholding states joined it after secession,
  chiefly to preserve slavery against feared federal restrictions. Jefferson Davis
  headed a federal government with a president and Congress, first at Montgomery and
  then Richmond. Its constitution explicitly protected slavery, while wartime demands
  strained the promised autonomy of states. Confederate forces fought the United States
  throughout the American Civil War, but no foreign government recognised independence.
  A naval blockade, battlefield losses, shortages, and internal opposition weakened the
  republic. Union advances forced the evacuation of Richmond, and military surrenders
  ended its government in 1865. Reconstruction followed, while emancipation destroyed
  the institution central to its founding purpose.
