name: "Sultanate of Dahlak"
description: >-
  The Sultanate of Dahlak governed Red Sea islands and parts of the Eritrean coast until Ottoman
  annexation in 1557. Its emergence around 960 is estimated; Sultan Mubarak's funerary inscription,
  recording his death in 1093, supplies the earliest evidence for a ruler. The island of Dahlak Kebir
  was its principal centre. Muslim sultans profited from trade connecting the African mainland with
  Yemen, Egypt, and India, and elaborate inscribed tombstones preserve traces of this wealthy
  community. Relations with Yemeni dynasties and Ethiopian rulers included tribute without necessarily
  removing local government. New commercial routes reduced Dahlak's importance after the thirteenth
  century, while outside pressure increased. Portuguese attacks damaged the islands during the
  sixteenth century. Ottoman conquest finally incorporated them into the Habesh province.
