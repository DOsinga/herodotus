description: >-
  Yang Wu was a regional monarchy centred on Yangzhou in eastern China during the collapse
  of Tang rule and the Five Dynasties and Ten Kingdoms period. This record begins around
  892, when Yang Xingmi recovered Yangzhou and built his own territorial government. His
  recognition as prince came later, and formal separation followed the Tang dynasty's
  fall. Wu controlled lands along the lower Yangtze, including much of modern Jiangsu,
  Anhui, and Jiangxi. After Yang Xingmi's death, his heirs kept royal titles while the
  commander Xu Wen increasingly directed government. Xu Wen's adopted son Xu Zhigao
  inherited that power. In 937 he removed the Yang ruler and founded the regime later
  called Southern Tang, ending Wu through a change of ruling house.
