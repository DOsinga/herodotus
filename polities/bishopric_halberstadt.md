description: >-
  The Prince-Bishopric of Halberstadt was the territory of the bishops of Halberstadt, on the
  northern edge of the Harz in present Saxony-Anhalt. The see was founded in the early ninth
  century, and its bishops built up a territory in the Harz foreland, adding Aschersleben in 1315
  when the local Ascanian line died out. The cathedral chapter and the towns, above all
  Halberstadt itself, had a large say in its government. In the sixteenth century the see was held
  by princes of the houses of Brandenburg and Brunswick-Wolfenbüttel as administrators, and the
  land became Lutheran. The Peace of Westphalia gave it to Brandenburg in 1648 as the secular
  Principality of Halberstadt, in compensation for Brandenburg's claim to western Pomerania.
