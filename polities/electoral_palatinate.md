description: >-
  The Electoral Palatinate was a princely state of the Holy Roman Empire centred on the
  Rhine, governed here from 1250 to 1622 and again from 1648 to 1777. Its Wittelsbach
  rulers held the title count palatine and became imperial electors, giving them a vote
  in choosing the emperor. Heidelberg was a major court and university centre; Mannheim
  later became the principal residence. The Reformation made the Palatinate an important
  Protestant power. Frederick V's acceptance of the Bohemian crown brought disaster
  during the Thirty Years' War. Bavarian conquest interrupted its government until the
  Peace of Westphalia restored Charles Louis. Later wars devastated its lands again. In
  1777, Charles Theodore inherited Bavaria and governed the Palatinate from Munich,
  ending its separate administration.
