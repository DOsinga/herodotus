description: >-
  The Banu Hafsun of Bobastro were the rebel lordship of Umar ibn Hafsun and his sons in the
  mountains of Málaga, in southern Spain. Around 880 Ibn Hafsun, an outlaw of Muwallad family,
  rebuilt the ruined castle of Bobastro and rallied local converts and Christians who resented
  the taxes of the emirs of Córdoba. By 883 he led the rebels of the south, and his castles
  spread into the provinces of Cádiz, Granada, Jaén and Seville. He lost the battle of Poley
  in 891 but kept fighting, became a Christian in 899 and offered allegiance to the Fatimids
  in 910. Abd al-Rahman III wore down his strongholds with yearly campaigns. Ibn Hafsun died
  in 917, and his sons held Bobastro until its surrender in 928.
