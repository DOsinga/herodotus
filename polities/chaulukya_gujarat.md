description: >-
  The Chaulukya kingdom ruled Gujarat and neighbouring parts of western India from Anahilavada,
  now Patan. Its founding follows the traditional date of 942, when Mularaja displaced the Chavda
  ruler, though other accounts place it around 940. Hereditary kings expanded through warfare and
  alliances, reaching particular strength under Jayasimha Siddharaja and Kumarapala. Their courts
  supported Sanskrit scholarship, Jain teachers and Hindu worship, while temples and waterworks
  displayed royal and elite patronage. Mahmud of Ghazni's raid in 1025 damaged Somnath but did not
  establish lasting foreign government over Gujarat. Later rulers faced invasions and growing
  dependence on ministers. The Vaghela family gradually assumed effective power, and Visaladeva
  replaced the Chaulukya royal line in 1244, continuing a kingdom based in the same region.
