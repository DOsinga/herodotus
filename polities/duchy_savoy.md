name: Duchy of Savoy
description: >-
  The Duchy of Savoy governed lands across the western Alps between France and northern Italy from
  1416 to 1720. Emperor Sigismund raised Count Amadeus VIII to ducal rank, recognizing the House of
  Savoy's growing power. The duchy included Savoy, Piedmont, and Nice, with Chambéry initially serving
  as its seat. Its position between stronger neighbours made warfare and diplomacy central to
  survival. French invasions brought prolonged occupation during the sixteenth century, before
  Emmanuel Philibert recovered much of his inheritance and moved the capital to Turin in 1563. Later
  rulers strengthened administration and expanded their Italian ambitions. Victor Amadeus II gained a
  royal crown through the European succession wars; acquiring Sardinia in 1720 established the state's
  identity as the Kingdom of Sardinia.
