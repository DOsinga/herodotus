description: >-
  North Brega was an Irish kingdom centred on Knowth in the Boyne valley, represented here
  from around 700 until approximately 1172. Its rulers belonged to the Ui Chonaing branch of
  the southern Ui Neill, which competed with a southern court descended from the same wider
  dynasty. The division developed through family rivalry rather than a fixed boundary drawn
  across modern counties. Northern kings sometimes claimed authority over all Brega, while
  alliances and warfare connected them with Tara, Mide and neighbouring kingdoms. Viking
  Dublin and stronger Irish rulers later limited their power. Knowth's importance as a royal
  centre distinguishes this polity from Brega as a broad geographical name. The Norman
  conquest ended its independent government, although local names and families endured.
