description: >-
  The Kingdom of Yugoslavia united Serbia, Montenegro, and former Habsburg lands in southeastern Europe after
  the First World War. Founded in 1918 as the Kingdom of Serbs, Croats and Slovenes,
  it was governed from Belgrade by the Serbian Karađorđević dynasty. Parliamentary institutions struggled with disputes
  over central authority, national identities, land reform, and uneven regional development. King Alexander imposed a
  royal dictatorship in 1929 and renamed the country Yugoslavia, seeking to replace competing national loyalties
  with a common identity. His assassination in 1934 left a regency governing for the young
  Peter II. A 1939 agreement gave Croatia autonomy, but did not settle all conflicts. Axis
  invasion in 1941 destroyed the kingdom's territorial government; its monarchy survived temporarily in exile abroad.
