description: >-
  Barbados is an island country in the eastern Caribbean, independent from Britain since
  1966, with Bridgetown as its capital. Its parliamentary government developed from
  institutions established during centuries of British rule. Sugar plantations, slavery,
  and the labour of enslaved Africans shaped its economy and population before
  emancipation in the nineteenth century. Errol Barrow led the country into independence
  after internal self-government and membership in the short-lived West Indies Federation.
  Barbados initially retained the British monarch as its own head of state, represented by
  a governor-general. In 2021 it became a republic with a president while keeping
  parliamentary government and Commonwealth membership. Tourism and financial services now
  supplement the older plantation sugar industry. Its independent state continues across
  these constitutional changes.
