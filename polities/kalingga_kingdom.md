name: "Kalingga Kingdom"
description: >-
  Kalingga was an early kingdom on the northern coast of Central Java, represented here from
  an estimated 550 to 732. Chinese accounts call it Ho-ling, but its location and historical
  boundaries remain debated. Jepara is one proposed centre rather than an identified capital.
  Reports describe a royal court, wooden fortifications, exports, and connections with China.
  Queen Shima, traditionally placed in the late seventh century, became famous in stories
  about strict justice and honesty. Buddhist travellers also record translation work involving
  local scholars. Sparse inscriptions and later traditions suggest Hindu and Buddhist
  influences without supplying a complete political history. Its replacement by the Medang
  government associated with Sanjaya is conventionally dated to 732, although the relationship
  between these courts remains uncertain.
