description: >-
  The Beylik of Teke was a Turkish principality on Anatolia's southern coast, centred on Antalya.
  A branch of the Hamidid ruling family established it in 1321 as Seljuk authority weakened.
  Hereditary beys governed this coastal territory, whose inhabitants spoke Anatolian Turkish and followed Sunni Islam.
  Its rulers belonged to the wider landscape of Anatolian states competing for land and influence
  before Ottoman expansion. Antalya's port connected the principality with Mediterranean commerce, while surviving buildings reflect
  its local patronage. Ottoman annexation interrupted independent rule around 1390. Timur restored the last ruler
  after his victory over the Ottomans in 1402, but the revived principality remained vulnerable. Ottoman
  forces annexed it permanently in 1423. The Teke name survived in regional administration and geography.
