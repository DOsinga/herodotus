name: "Rouran Khaganate"
description: >-
  The Rouran Khaganate dominated the Mongolian steppe from 402 to 555, extending at times into
  surrounding parts of Inner Asia. Shelun united competing chiefs and assumed the khagan title in
  402, creating a confederation under the Yujiulü ruling family. Its government depended on an
  aristocracy of tribal leaders and military commanders rather than an identified fixed capital.
  Herding, warfare, tribute, and control of routes connected its mobile communities with
  neighbouring states. The Rouran repeatedly fought Northern Wei and also used diplomacy and
  marriage alliances with Chinese courts. Their subjects included groups that later challenged
  their authority. The Turkic revolt began in 552, and continued campaigns defeated the Rouran
  resistance by 555, replacing their steppe rule with the first Turkic khaganate.
