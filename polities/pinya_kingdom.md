description: >-
  Pinya was a kingdom of central Burma, founded by Thihathu in 1313 after the decline of Pagan and the
  rule of the Myinsaing brothers. Its capital stood near the irrigated Kyaukse plain, an important
  source of rice and royal revenue. Kings claimed Pagan's inheritance and supported Buddhism, but
  dynastic disputes and the resources held by religious estates limited their strength. Sagaing
  separated across the Irrawaddy, while governors in places such as Toungoo increasingly acted for
  themselves. Pinya's court struggled to command a unified army or secure its frontier. Raids by the
  northern Maw Shan forces devastated both Pinya and Sagaing in 1364. Thado Minbya then gathered their
  surviving territories into the new kingdom of Ava, replacing Pinya's separate royal government.
