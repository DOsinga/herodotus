description: >-
  The Principality of Murom was a Rus' principality on the Oka River east of Vladimir, with its
  capital at Murom, an old Finnic town. It belonged to the descendants of Svyatoslav of
  Chernigov, and from 1127 it formed a separate principality together with Ryazan. When Ryazan
  split off in the middle of the twelfth century, Murom kept its own line of princes, among them
  Peter and Fevronia, whose legend made them Orthodox saints. The princes often came under the
  influence of the grand princes of Vladimir, and after the Mongol invasion of 1237 to 1239 they
  paid tribute to the Golden Horde. Muscovite influence grew in the fourteenth century, and in
  1392 Grand Prince Vasily I obtained a patent from the khan and annexed Murom.
