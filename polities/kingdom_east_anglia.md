description: >-
  East Anglia was a kingdom in eastern Britain, covering much of modern Norfolk and
  Suffolk. Its formation is estimated around 550, and Rendlesham represents an early royal
  centre. The Wuffing dynasty's most powerful king, Rædwald, exercised influence beyond
  the region, while the burial at Sutton Hoo reflects the world of its early rulers.
  Christianity spread during the seventh century. Mercian expansion later brought periods
  of direct outside government, interrupted by renewed East Anglian kingship. Viking
  forces killed King Edmund in 869 and took control. Guthrum established a settled Danish
  kingdom in 879, continuing a territorial state rather than a loose Danelaw umbrella. In
  918, Edward the Elder's advance brought East Anglia under Wessex, ending the kingdom
  represented in this record.
