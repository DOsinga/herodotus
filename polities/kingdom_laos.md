description: >-
  The Kingdom of Laos governed the landlocked country in mainland Southeast Asia
  from full independence in 1953 until 1975. Vientiane housed the administration,
  while Luang Prabang was the royal capital. The kingdom had received autonomy
  within the French Union in 1947, but the Franco-Lao treaty of 1953 established
  sovereignty. It was a constitutional monarchy, first under Sisavang Vong and then
  Sisavang Vatthana, with governments led by competing political factions.
  Neutralists, conservatives and the communist Pathet Lao struggled for power during
  a civil war entangled with the conflict in Vietnam. Foreign military involvement
  undermined efforts to preserve neutrality. After a coalition government, the
  Pathet Lao took control in 1975. The king abdicated, and the Lao People's
  Democratic Republic replaced the monarchy.
