description: >-
  The Empire of Japan began with the Meiji Restoration in 1868 and continued until the new
  constitution took effect in 1947. Centred on Tokyo, its government replaced the Tokugawa
  shogunate and the domains with a centralised state. The 1889 constitution established an
  imperial monarchy with a parliament, while sovereignty remained with the emperor. Rapid
  industrialisation and military reform enabled victories against China and Russia and the
  acquisition of Taiwan, Korea, and other territories. Parliamentary politics developed but
  later gave way to increasingly militarised rule and expansion in China. Japan's entry into
  the Second World War brought conquest, occupation, and atrocities across Asia. Defeat in
  1945 led to Allied occupation, loss of the empire, and a constitution making the emperor
  symbolic.
