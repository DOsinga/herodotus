description: >-
  Nassau-Weilburg was a county, and from 1688 a principality, of the Holy Roman Empire on the Lahn
  river in present Hesse, ruled by the Walramian branch of the House of Nassau from its castle and
  later palace at Weilburg. It was created in 1344 when the Walramian lands were divided, and its
  counts gradually gathered other Nassau territories by marriage and inheritance, including
  Saarbrücken, which some of them made their seat. Divisions among brothers repeatedly split off
  and reabsorbed smaller counties. In 1803 the secularisation of church states gave it lands of
  the Electorate of Trier east of the Rhine. In 1806 it joined the Confederation of the Rhine and
  merged with Nassau-Usingen to form the Duchy of Nassau.
