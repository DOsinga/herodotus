description: >-
  Larsa was a city kingdom in southern Mesopotamia, at Tell as-Senkereh in present day Iraq.
  This record begins with Naplanum around 2025 BCE, during the weakening of the Third
  Dynasty of Ur, and ends with Babylonian conquest in 1763 BCE. Its kings competed with Isin
  and other neighboring states for farmland, canals, cities, and trade. The city was a major
  center of worship for the sun god Utu, whose Ebabbar temple linked royal power with
  religious life. Under Gungunum and later rulers, Larsa gained territory and wealth at
  Isin's expense. Rim-Sin brought much of southern Babylonia under his control before facing
  Hammurabi. Hammurabi defeated him and absorbed the kingdom, although the city and its
  temple continued under later Mesopotamian rulers.
