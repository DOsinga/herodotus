description: >-
  The United Duchies of Jülich-Cleves-Berg brought several neighbouring territories of the Holy Roman
  Empire under one dynasty from 1521 to 1614. The union joined Jülich, Cleves and Berg with Mark and
  Ravensberg through marriage and inheritance, with Düsseldorf serving as a residence. Dukes of the
  House of La Marck governed lands across the lower Rhine and Westphalia, while the constituent
  territories kept their identities. William the Rich challenged Charles V over Guelders but lost that
  possession after defeat in 1543. The rulers tried to navigate religious divisions without fully
  embracing either confessional camp. John William's death without children in 1609 triggered an
  international succession struggle. The Treaty of Xanten divided the inheritance between Brandenburg
  and Palatinate-Neuburg, ending the united government.
