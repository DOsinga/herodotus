description: >-
  Nangchen was a Tibetan kingdom in the upper Mekong and Yangtze valleys of northern Kham,
  in what is now Nangqên County and the surrounding Yushu prefecture of Qinghai. Its kings
  came from the Nangchen family, and Wa Alu is named as the first king in 1175. Mongol
  armies overran the kingdom in the thirteenth century, and under the Yuan its lords served
  the Mongol administration of Tibet. When Yuan rule ended the kings governed on their own
  again, at the head of a loose union of herding and farming tribes, and they were close
  patrons of the Kagyu and Sakya monasteries that dominate the region. Güshi Khan's Khoshut
  Mongols conquered Kham around 1640. The later Qing made the kings hereditary chiefs, and
  the line lasted until 1955.
