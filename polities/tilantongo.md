description: >-
  Tilantongo was a Mixtec city-state in the Mixteca Alta of Oaxaca and one of the most
  prestigious royal seats of the Postclassic Mixtec world. Painted genealogies such as the
  Codex Bodley and the Codex Zouche-Nuttall trace its dynasties from the tenth century. Its
  first dynasty ended in the late eleventh century, when Eight Deer Jaguar Claw of Tututepec
  seized Tilantongo and founded a new line, whose descendants married into the ruling houses
  of many other Mixtec kingdoms. Like other Mixtec states, Tilantongo was ruled by a
  hereditary lord and noble lineages from a palace town surrounded by dependent communities.
  In the late fifteenth century it paid tribute to the Aztecs but kept its own rulers. Its
  lords submitted to the Spanish in the early 1520s.
