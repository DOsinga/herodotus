description: >-
  Groningen was a self-governing city in the northern Netherlands, recorded here from 1250
  until 1506. The town grew around a trading settlement held by the bishops of Utrecht, and by
  the thirteenth century its burghers governed it largely free of episcopal control. Groningen
  joined the Hanseatic League and used its market rights and grain staple to dominate the
  surrounding Frisian countryside, the Ommelanden. In 1361 it led a league with Frisian lands
  to defend the Frisian freedom. Its struggle with Albert of Saxony and his son George, to
  whom Maximilian I had given Friesland, led the city to accept Count Edzard of East Frisia as
  its lord in 1506. Guelders and then Charles V followed.
