description: >-
  The Thanjavur Nayak kingdom governed the fertile Kaveri delta in southern India, becoming independent after
  Vijayanagara's defeat at Talikota in 1565. Its ruling family had first established itself in 1532
  as Vijayanagara governors, and separation from that empire was gradual. Hereditary Nayak rulers governed from
  Thanjavur, competing and cooperating with neighbouring courts at Madurai and Gingee. Irrigation works and temple
  patronage supported the agricultural economy and helped display royal authority. The court fostered Telugu and
  Sanskrit literature, music, and scholarship, especially under Raghunatha Nayak. Coastal trade brought dealings with European
  merchants, including permission for a Danish settlement at Tranquebar. Madurai's army conquered Thanjavur in 1673,
  ending this kingdom before a later Maratha regime took control of the city and region.
