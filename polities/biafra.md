description: >-
  Biafra was a secessionist republic in southeastern Nigeria from 1967 to 1970, initially
  governed from Enugu. Chukwuemeka Odumegwu Ojukwu declared independence after military
  coups, killings of Igbo civilians and failed negotiations over Nigeria's federal
  arrangements. Its population was predominantly Igbo but included several other
  communities in the former Eastern Region. A centralised military led presidency and
  cabinet directed the new state's administration and war effort. Nigeria rejected
  secession and launched the civil war that steadily reduced Biafra's territory. A
  federal blockade produced catastrophic hunger, drawing international attention and a
  large international humanitarian relief airlift. Only a handful of countries formally
  recognised the republic. After Ojukwu fled, Philip Effiong surrendered in January 1970,
  and the surviving territory returned to Nigerian government.
