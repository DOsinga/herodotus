description: >-
  The Timurid Empire arose in Central Asia in 1370 under Timur, who made Samarkand his capital. His armies conquered Iran and neighbouring regions and campaigned far beyond them, combining devastating warfare with the movement of skilled workers to his court. Timur ruled as a military sovereign using Mongol traditions and Islamic legitimacy, while Persian officials administered settled lands. After his death in 1405, succession struggles divided power among descendants, with Shah Rukh establishing an court at Herat. Timurid patronage supported architecture, manuscript painting, poetry, and astronomy, including Ulugh Beg's work in Samarkand. Rival princes and Uzbek expansion steadily reduced their territories. The Uzbeks captured Herat in 1507, ending this empire. Babur later carried the dynasty's inheritance into the Mughal state.
