name: Madurai Sultanate
description: >-
  The Madurai Sultanate was a Muslim monarchy in southern India, centred on Madurai in
  present day Tamil Nadu. It emerged in 1335 when the provincial ruler Jalaluddin Ahsan Khan
  broke from the Delhi Sultanate during Muhammad bin Tughluq's troubled reign. Coin evidence
  supports that founding date, although later accounts differ. The new government controlled
  Madurai and surrounding districts previously contested by Pandya rulers and northern
  armies. Its short history saw repeated changes of sultan, violent succession struggles,
  and warfare with neighbouring Hindu kingdoms. Ibn Battuta visited the court and recorded
  its rulers and harsh practices. Expansion by Vijayanagara eventually overwhelmed the
  sultanate. The campaigns associated with Kumara Kampana ended its separate rule by 1378,
  bringing Madurai under a southern empire.
