description: >-
  The Cossack Hetmanate emerged in Ukraine in 1648 during Bohdan Khmelnytsky's uprising
  against the Polish Lithuanian Commonwealth. An elected hetman led a government built
  around Cossack military regiments, officers and councils. Chyhyryn was its early
  capital, while later rulers governed from Baturyn and Hlukhiv. Its leaders sought allies
  among Moscow, Poland, the Ottoman Empire and Sweden, trying to preserve authority
  between stronger neighbours. Civil wars and foreign intervention divided the territory
  along the Dnieper, with the lasting Hetmanate concentrated on the left bank. Ivan
  Mazepa's alliance with Sweden ended in defeat at Poltava in 1709. Russian interference
  increased. Catherine II abolished the office of hetman in 1764, replacing its leadership
  with imperial administration and ending the polity's separate governing authority.
