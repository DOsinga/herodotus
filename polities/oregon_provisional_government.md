description: >-
  The Provisional Government of Oregon was a settler government in the Oregon Country, the
  region claimed by both the United States and Britain under their joint occupation.
  American settlers in the Willamette Valley met at Champoeg and set up the government in
  May 1843, adopting Organic Laws that were to apply until the United States extended its
  jurisdiction. It had a legislature, courts and at first a committee of three as executive,
  replaced in 1845 by an elected governor, George Abernethy. Oregon City served as its seat.
  The government raised taxes, registered land claims and raised militia for the Cayuse War
  of 1847 and 1848. The Oregon Treaty of 1846 made the region American, and the government
  ended when the territorial governor arrived in March 1849.
