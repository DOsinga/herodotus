description: >-
  The Republic of Geneva was a city state at the western end of Lake Geneva. Its
  citizens threw off the authority of the prince-bishop and the dukes of Savoy in 1534,
  and the Reformation followed in 1536. John Calvin's work made the city a centre of
  Protestant teaching and a refuge for exiles from France and Italy. Government
  lay with elected syndics and a hierarchy of councils dominated by leading citizen
  families. Geneva was allied with Bern and Fribourg and later with Zurich, but it was
  never a full member of the Swiss Confederacy. It repulsed a Savoyard night attack, the
  Escalade, in 1602. France annexed the republic in 1798; restored in 1813,
  it joined Switzerland as a canton in 1815.
