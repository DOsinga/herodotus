description: >-
  Hazor was a leading northern Canaanite kingdom in Upper Galilee, north of the Sea of Galilee,
  represented here from approximately 1750 to 1250 BCE. Its kings governed a large fortified city with
  an upper citadel and an extensive lower town. Letters from Mari and Egypt place Hazor within
  diplomatic and trading networks stretching through Syria and Mesopotamia. Tin imports supported
  bronze production, while palaces, temples, tablets, and imported objects reveal its wealth and
  connections. During the Late Bronze Age its rulers acknowledged Egyptian overlordship but retained
  their own court. A fierce fire destroyed the Canaanite city in the thirteenth century BCE. The
  attackers remain disputed, and later Israelite settlement belongs to a different political setting
  rather than this earlier kingdom.
