description: >-
  Tamna was an island kingdom on Jeju, south of the Korean Peninsula, with its dated political history beginning in 476. Foundation stories trace it much farther back, but they are legends rather than evidence for these dates. Its native ruling families maintained government while sending tribute to successive mainland powers, first Baekje and later Silla and Goryeo. Maritime contacts connected the island with Korea, Japan and China, and archaeology reveals trade predating the surviving diplomatic accounts. Silla conferred titles on Tamna's leading princes, which became hereditary offices. The kingdom's government ended when Goryeo established a prefecture in 1105. Local hereditary leaders continued within mainland administration afterward, but their survival does not extend this record of Tamna as its own polity.
