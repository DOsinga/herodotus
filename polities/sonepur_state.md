description: >-
  Sonepur was a small Chauhan principality at the meeting of the Mahanadi and Tel rivers in
  western Odisha. Around 1650 Madhukar Deva of Sambalpur took the district from the Bhanja rulers
  of Boudh and gave it to his younger son Madan Gopal, whose descendants ruled it as a separate
  state. The rajas belonged to the family that also held Patna and Sambalpur, and Sonepur stood
  among the hill states that looked to Sambalpur as their senior house, but it was governed by its
  own court. The district around the town is still known for its textiles and terracotta. The
  Bhonsle Marathas of Nagpur took control of the region around 1800, and after their defeat the
  British restored the rajas as feudatory chiefs.
