description: >-
  Sikkim was a Himalayan kingdom founded in 1642 when Buddhist lamas consecrated Phuntsog
  Namgyal as its first chogyal at Yuksom. The Namgyal monarchy governed a society shaped by
  Lepcha and Bhutia communities and strong religious connections with Tibet. Its capitals
  moved from Yuksom to Rabdentse and then Tumlong; Gangtok became the capital only in its
  later protected period. Wars with Bhutan and Nepal reduced its territory and brought
  increasing British involvement. The Treaty of Titalia in 1817 restored lands, while
  Darjeeling was transferred to the British in 1835. The Treaty of Tumlong made Sikkim a
  British protectorate in 1861, ending the independent period recorded here. Its monarchy
  survived under British and later Indian protection until annexation by India in 1975.
