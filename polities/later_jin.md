description: >-
  Later Jin was a Jurchen khanate in Manchuria from 1616 to 1636, founded by Nurhaci of the Aisin Gioro family. Its name recalled the earlier Jurchen Jin empire, though this was a new state. Nurhaci united rival groups and organized households and soldiers into the Eight Banners, linking military service with administration. He challenged Ming authority in 1618 and defeated a large Ming expedition at Sarhu the following year. The court moved through several seats before reaching Mukden in 1625. Hong Taiji succeeded Nurhaci, strengthened government, and drew Mongol allies and Chinese personnel into the realm. After the submission of the last Northern Yuan ruler, he proclaimed the Qing empire in 1636, giving the expanding state a new dynastic identity.
