description: >-
  The Kingdom of Kuku was a Kabyle state in the mountains of Greater Kabylia, east of
  Algiers, ruled by the family of Ahmed ou el Kadhi, a scholar and judge from Béjaïa who
  fled the Spanish capture of that city. From its hilltop capital at Kuku it led the Zwawa
  tribal confederation. After defeating Hayreddin Barbarossa at the Battle of Issers, its
  forces held Algiers for several years in the 1520s and received the allegiance of towns
  along the coast. The corsairs then pushed it back into the mountains. Afterwards the
  kingdom made alliances with Spain and later served the Regency of Algiers with troops
  and mercenaries. Family quarrels and the loss of control over the tribes weakened it
  from about 1618, and it faded away in the 1630s.
