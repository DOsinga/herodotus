description: >-
  Cotzumalhuapa was a large urban centre on Guatemala's Pacific piedmont, with a regional
  governing phase estimated here at 500 to 1000 CE. El Baul, Bilbao and El Castillo formed
  parts of one connected settlement, linked by paved causeways and bridges rather than three
  separate states. Monumental buildings and carved stones suggest concentrated wealth and
  organised authority, although the details of its government remain unclear. Its artists
  created distinctive sculptures showing animated human figures, supernatural beings,
  sacrifice and plantlike speech scrolls. A local writing system has not been deciphered.
  The sculpture style spread along the coast and into the highlands, showing wide
  connections without proving direct political control everywhere. The dates describe its
  monumental development, not its entire history of occupation.
