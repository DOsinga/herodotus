description: >-
  The Valais Republic, also called the Rhodanic Republic, was a small state in the Rhône
  valley created by Napoleon in 1802. France wanted secure control of the Simplon pass,
  where it was building a military road into Italy, and separated the Valais from the
  Helvetic Republic for that purpose. The republic had its own constitution, a diet and
  a council of state at Sion, but it remained under the protection of France and the
  Italian Republic. French influence grew as the road works progressed. In 1810 Napoleon
  annexed the Valais outright as the French department of Simplon. After Napoleon's fall
  the Valais joined Switzerland as a canton in 1815. The episode is the only time the
  valley was a separate state in modern times.
