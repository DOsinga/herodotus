description: >-
  Lydia was a kingdom in western Anatolia, centered on Sardis in present day Turkey. Its
  early origins are uncertain, and the long chronology beginning around 1200 BCE includes
  traditions rather than securely documented rulers. Contemporary evidence becomes clearer
  with Gyges and the Mermnad dynasty around 680 BCE. Hereditary kings expanded inland and
  imposed their authority on many Greek cities along the Aegean coast. Lydia is especially
  associated with early electrum coinage and the wealth of its last king, Croesus. Its
  rulers faced Cimmerian invasions and competed with neighboring powers before confronting
  Persia. Croesus crossed into territory claimed by Cyrus, but the ensuing war ended with
  the capture of Sardis around 546 BCE. Lydia then became part of the Achaemenid Empire.
