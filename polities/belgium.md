description: >-
  Belgium emerged in 1830 when a revolution separated the southern provinces from the Kingdom of the Netherlands. With Brussels as its capital, it became a constitutional monarchy whose elected parliament increasingly shaped government. Industrial growth was concentrated in Wallonia, while language disputes between Dutch and French speakers later drove reforms toward a federal state. German invasions made Belgium a major battlefield in both world wars. During the Second World War, its government in exile continued administering the Belgian Congo despite occupation at home. Colonial rule in central Africa left a legacy of exploitation and violence. Belgium subsequently helped establish European integration, and Brussels became a principal centre of European institutions. Its monarchy and parliamentary institutions continue within a multilingual federation.
