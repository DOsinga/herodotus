description: >-
  Oghuz rule in Kerman was the short government of Turkmen tribal forces in southeastern Iran
  after the fall of the Kerman Seljuk sultans. The Oghuz had devastated Khorasan after capturing
  the Seljuk sultan Sanjar in 1153, and many moved south into Kerman during the succession wars of
  the late Seljuk princes. Their leader Malik Dinar took the city of Bardasir, modern Kerman, in
  1187 and governed the province with his followers, collecting revenue from a region already
  damaged by years of war. Contemporary historians describe famine and disorder during this
  period. After Malik Dinar's death around 1195, his son Farrukh Shah could not hold the province,
  and the Khwarazmshah Tekish annexed Kerman in 1196.
