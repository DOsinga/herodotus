description: >-
  The Kingdom of Upper Mesopotamia was the realm built by Shamshi-Adad I across northern
  Mesopotamia and Syria, represented here from 1808 to 1776 BCE. An Amorite conqueror, he
  took Assur and made Shubat-Enlil his royal seat. He placed his sons Ishme-Dagan and
  Yasmah-Adad at Ekallatum and Mari, coordinating their governments through correspondence
  and family authority. Surviving letters reveal military planning, diplomacy, dynastic
  marriages, and the king's close supervision of his sons. This was a territorial kingdom
  rather than simply an enlarged Assyrian city-state. It competed with Eshnunna and other
  regional powers while seeking allies farther west. After Shamshi-Adad died, its unity
  collapsed: Mari regained its dynasty, and his heirs struggled to preserve control in the
  eastern part of his realm.
