description: >-
  The Bhadra kingdom occupied Samatata in southeastern Bengal, around the region of modern
  eastern Bangladesh. Its court is represented here from approximately 550 to 658, but these
  boundaries remain estimates rather than securely recorded reigns. The royal family is known
  partly through the ancestry of Shilabhadra, the Buddhist scholar at Nalanda whom Xuanzang
  described as coming from Samatata's ruling house. The dynasty itself was of Brahmin origin,
  illustrating connections between royal lineage and different religious traditions. Evidence is
  too sparse to reconstruct a full succession or administrative system. Scholars disagree about
  whether the Bhadras were distinct from the Khadgas or were displaced by them. This record
  follows their estimated replacement during Khadga expansion, without identifying Mainamati as a
  proven capital.
