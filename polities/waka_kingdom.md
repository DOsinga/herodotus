name: "Kingdom of Waka"
description: >-
  The Kingdom of Waka was a Maya polity centred at El Perú in northern Guatemala, near the San
  Pedro River. Its governing centre is represented from approximately 150 to 800 CE, rather than
  from the site's earliest settlement. Inscriptions and royal burials identify a local dynasty
  within the rival networks of Tikal and Calakmul. Waka initially allied with Tikal, then
  strengthened its ties to Calakmul through the marriage of King K'inich Balam and Lady T'abi,
  who held a military title. Its river position made it an important trading centre. Tikal
  defeated Waka in 743, but changing alliances and submission did not erase local kingship.
  Archaeology traces a declining royal centre towards the end of the Classic period, around 800
  CE.
