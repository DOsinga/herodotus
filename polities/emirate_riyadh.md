description: >-
  The Emirate of Riyadh was a town state in Najd, central Arabia, at the oasis then known as
  Hajr or Migrin. It was ruled by the Zara'ah family until Zaid ibn Musa was murdered in
  1737. His slave Khumayyis ruled as regent for the young heir, and after he fled in 1740 his
  aide Dahham ibn Dawwas, a refugee from nearby Manfuhah, took over, first as regent and from
  1745 in his own name. Dahham built a mudbrick palace and a wall around the town, and the
  name Riyadh came into use during his rule. He fell out with Diriyah and its Wahhabi allies
  in 1746, and almost three decades of war followed until Abdulaziz ibn Muhammad took Riyadh
  in 1773 and Dahham fled.
