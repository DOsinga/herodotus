description: >-
  The Inalid Beylik was a small Turkmen principality centred on Amida, the modern Diyarbakır,
  from 1098 to 1183. It grew out of the break-up of the Great Seljuk west after Malik-Shah died
  in 1092. A Turkmen lord named Sadr held Amida as governor, and his son Inal made himself
  independent. Under Inal's successor Ibrahim the beylik had to accept a series of overlords,
  from the Seljuks of Syria to the Ahlatshahs. Ilaldı, who ruled from 1110 to 1142, won back
  territory and held Amida against a Zangid attack in 1133. After his death the Artuqids took
  much of the land, and the Nisanid family, the beys' viziers, ran the government in their
  name. In May 1183 the Artuqids, allied with Saladin, captured Amida and ended the beylik.
