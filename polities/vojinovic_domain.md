name: Vojinović domain
description: >-
  The Vojinović domain was a regional lordship in the western Serbian lands, represented
  here during its separate government from 1371 to 1373. Its ruler Nikola Altomanović
  inherited and expanded holdings developed by his family while the Serbian Empire
  weakened. His territory stretched across parts of modern Serbia, Bosnia and
  Herzegovina, and Montenegro, linking inland districts with approaches to the Adriatic.
  Authority rested on a powerful noble household and its military retainers rather than
  a new royal dynasty. Užice was an important stronghold. Nikola's ambitions brought him
  into conflict with neighbouring lords. Prince Lazar and Bosnia's Ban Tvrtko joined
  forces against him, supported by Hungarian troops. In 1373 Nikola was captured and
  blinded at Užice, and the victors divided his lands.
