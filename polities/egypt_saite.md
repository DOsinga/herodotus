description: >-
  Egypt under the twenty-sixth dynasty was a monarchy centred at Sais in the Nile Delta from
  664 to 525 BCE. Psamtik I began governing under Assyrian overlordship after the expulsion of
  the Kushite kings, then consolidated Egypt and achieved independence. His successors
  maintained an army that included Greek and Carian mercenaries, and renewed Egyptian
  involvement in the eastern Mediterranean. Necho II's ambitions in Syria ended with defeat by
  Babylon at Carchemish in 605 BCE. Later rulers defended Egypt against Babylonian pressure
  while fostering contact with Greek merchants and communities. The period combined revived
  Egyptian artistic traditions with wider foreign connections. In 525 BCE Cambyses II invaded,
  defeated Psamtik III and incorporated Egypt into the Achaemenid Empire, ending Saite royal
  government.
