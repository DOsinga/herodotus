name: "Frankish Kingdom of Le Mans"
description: >-
  The Frankish Kingdom of Le Mans represents the local rule associated with Rignomer, brother of King
  Ragnachar of Cambrai, in late fifth century Gaul. Its existence from about 480 to 509 is an
  estimate, not a securely documented reign. Le Mans provides the geographical anchor because Gregory
  of Tours reports that Rignomer was killed there on Clovis's orders. The surviving account gives
  little detail about boundaries, institutions, or the court, so this kingdom should not be imagined
  as a well mapped territorial state. It belonged to a landscape of competing Frankish rulers whose
  authority depended on family connections and armed followers. Clovis removed related rivals while
  consolidating his monarchy. Rignomer's death marks the absorption of this local government into
  Francia.
