description: >-
  The Republic of Sassari was a self-governing city state in northwestern Sardinia, from
  the end of the Judicate of Logudoro in 1259 until the Crown of Aragon took the city in
  1323. When the last judge, Adelasia, died without heirs, her lands were divided between
  the Doria, the Malaspina and Arborea, but Sassari, the judicate's largest town, kept its
  own government. At first it was ruled by podestà from Pisa, as a semi-autonomous ally of
  the Pisan republic. After Pisa's defeat at Meloria in 1284 the city turned to Genoa,
  which appointed its podestà while leaving it nominally independent. Sassari wrote down
  its laws in the Statutes of Sassari of 1316, and submitted to the Aragonese invaders in
  1323.
