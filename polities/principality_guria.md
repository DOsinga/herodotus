description: >-
  The Principality of Guria was a small Georgian principality on the southeastern shore of
  the Black Sea, in what is now western Georgia, ruled by the Gurieli family. The Gurieli,
  hereditary governors under the united Georgian kingdom, became effectively independent
  during the breakup of the monarchy in the 1460s. They governed from Ozurgeti and other
  fortresses, often as rivals of Imereti and Mingrelia, and some of them briefly held the
  throne of Imereti. Ottoman expansion along the coast took the port of Batumi and the
  district of Chaneti from Guria in the sixteenth century, and the princes paid tribute to
  the sultan. Mamia V Gurieli accepted Russian protection in 1810, and Russia annexed the
  principality in 1829.
