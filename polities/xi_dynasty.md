description: >-
  The Xi dynasty, officially the Great Xi, was the state of the rebel leader Zhang Xianzhong
  during the fall of the Ming, lasting from 1643 to 1646. Zhang came from northern Shaanxi
  and spent the 1630s raiding across central China. In 1643 he captured Wuchang and called
  himself King of the West, then moved into Hunan and held Changsha. In 1644 he invaded
  Sichuan, stormed Chongqing in July and took Chengdu in September, which he renamed the
  Western Capital. There he proclaimed himself emperor and set up a court with officials
  and examinations. Ming loyalists retook Chongqing in 1645 and resistance spread, which
  Zhang met with mass killings. He abandoned Chengdu in late 1646 as Qing armies advanced
  and was killed in January 1647.
