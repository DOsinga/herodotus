name: Zeta under the Crnojevići
description: >-
  Zeta under the Crnojevići was a small principality in the mountains and valleys of present day
  Montenegro, lasting from 1451 to 1496. Stefan Crnojević consolidated the family's rule as the
  Serbian Despotate weakened, balancing local alliances with Venetian support. His son Ivan first
  fought Venice, then cooperated with it against Ottoman expansion. Losses around Lake Skadar
  pushed Ivan towards the mountains, where he founded Cetinje Monastery and established a new
  political and religious centre. The rulers depended on noble families and tribal communities as
  well as their hereditary authority. Ivan's successor Đurađ sponsored a printing press that
  produced decorated Orthodox religious books. Ottoman pressure finally ended the family's
  independent government in 1496, although Cetinje remained central to later Montenegrin history.
