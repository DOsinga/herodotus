description: >-
  The Imamate of Aussa was the successor of the Adal Sultanate, governed from the oasis of Aussa
  on the lower Awash River in what is now the Afar region of Ethiopia. In 1577 Imam Muhammad Gasa
  moved the seat of government from Harar, which was too exposed to Oromo attacks, to the more
  defensible oasis, and Harar was ruled by a representative of the imam. The imamate was troubled
  by conflict between Harla, Arab and Afar factions, and by raids on the caravan routes. In 1647
  the rulers of Harar broke away and founded their own emirate. Afar pressure grew through the
  seventeenth century, and in 1734 the Mudaito leader Kedafu seized power and founded the
  Sultanate of Aussa.
