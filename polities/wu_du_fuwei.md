description: >-
  Du Fuwei's Wu was a military government in the lower Yangtze region during China's
  transition from Sui to Tang. Its separate period here runs from his capture of Liyang
  in 617 to his departure for Chang'an in 622. Du gathered rebel forces, established
  territorial headquarters and competed with nearby commanders for towns and supplies.
  His authority rested on loyal troops and generals, including Fu Gongshi and several
  adopted sons. Although he submitted nominally to Tang in 619, he retained control of
  his own forces and administration. Tang granted him the title Prince of Wu and the
  imperial surname Li. His armies defeated regional rivals and expanded southward. In
  622, concern about Tang suspicions prompted his departure, ending his separate
  government.
