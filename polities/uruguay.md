description: >-
  Uruguay is a republic on the Atlantic coast of southeastern South America, between Brazil
  and Argentina, with its capital at Montevideo. Its independent history begins in 1828,
  when a settlement of the war between Brazil and the United Provinces recognised a separate
  state in the former Banda Oriental. A constitution followed in 1830. Rival Colorado and
  National parties dominated politics and fought civil wars, while European immigration
  transformed the country's towns and countryside. Early twentieth century reforms expanded
  public services, labour protections and social welfare. Economic troubles and political
  conflict later helped bring a repressive civilian-military dictatorship, lasting from 1973
  to 1985. Restored democratic government operates through an elected president and a two
  chamber legislature within the continuing Uruguayan state.
