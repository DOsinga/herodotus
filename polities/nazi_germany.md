description: >-
  Nazi Germany was the German state under Adolf Hitler and the Nazi Party from 1933 to 1945,
  governed from Berlin. Hitler's appointment as chancellor was followed by emergency measures,
  destruction of parliamentary democracy and suppression of rival parties. The regime imposed a
  racist totalitarian dictatorship, persecuting Jews and many other groups through exclusion,
  imprisonment and murder. Rearmament and territorial expansion culminated in the invasion of
  Poland in 1939, beginning the European war. German forces occupied much of Europe, exploiting
  conquered populations and using forced labour. The Holocaust killed six million Jews, alongside
  the mass murder of other civilians and prisoners. Military defeat brought Hitler's suicide and
  Germany's surrender in 1945. Allied occupation dismantled the regime and divided the defeated
  country.
