description: >-
  Siak Sri Indrapura was a Malay sultanate on the Siak river in eastern Sumatra. It was founded in
  1722 by Raja Kecil, a Minangkabau-backed claimant who briefly seized the throne of Johor and
  withdrew to Siak after being driven out by the Bugis. In the eighteenth century Siak expanded by war
  and by controlling the trade between the Sumatran interior and the Strait of Malacca, bringing much
  of the east coast under its influence. Its capital moved for a time to Senapelan, the later
  Pekanbaru. In the nineteenth century the Dutch signed a series of treaties with its rulers, and the
  treaty of 1858 placed Siak under Dutch sovereignty. Its sultans remained as rulers under Dutch
  advisers until 1946.
