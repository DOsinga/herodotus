description: >-
  The Republic of Yucatán was a state on the Yucatán Peninsula that broke with Mexico after
  the central government abolished the federal constitution. Its congress declared
  independence in 1841, with Mérida as capital and Campeche as its main port. A liberal
  constitution drafted largely by Manuel Crescencio Rejón gave it an elected governor and
  legislature and introduced judicial review. Mexico sent an army in 1842 that failed to
  take Mérida or Campeche, and an agreement of 1843 brought the peninsula back on generous
  terms that soon broke down. Yucatán again governed itself from 1845. The Caste War, the
  Maya uprising that began in 1847, drove the creole government close to collapse, and it
  rejoined Mexico in 1848 in return for help against the rebels.
