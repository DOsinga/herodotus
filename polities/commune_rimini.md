description: >-
  The Commune of Rimini was the self-governing city state of Rimini on the Adriatic coast
  of Romagna, from the early twelfth century until Malatesta da Verucchio made himself
  lord in 1295. Rimini guarded the meeting of the Via Emilia and the Via Flaminia, and its
  consuls and podestà governed the city and the hill country inland. The commune was
  usually Ghibelline and sided with Frederick II against the popes, who claimed Romagna.
  The Malatesta, a family of lords from the hills above the city, became citizens and
  leaders of the Guelph faction in the thirteenth century. After decades of struggle with
  the Ghibelline Parcitadi, Malatesta da Verucchio drove them out in 1295 and governed
  Rimini as its lord.
