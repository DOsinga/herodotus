description: >-
  The Kingdom of Upper Burgundy existed from 888 to 933 around the Jura Mountains and
  western Alps, including much of western Switzerland and neighbouring French lands. It
  emerged from the breakup of the Carolingian realm when Rudolph I, a member of the Welf
  family, assumed royal power. Saint-Maurice was a centre associated with his coronation and
  authority. His kingdom drew together territories on both sides of the Jura rather than the
  western duchy also called Burgundy. Rudolph II succeeded him and pursued ambitions in
  Italy, where competing rulers contested the crown. An agreement with Hugh of Arles
  redirected those ambitions towards Burgundian consolidation. In 933 Upper Burgundy joined
  Lower Burgundy under a united royal government, forming the larger Burgundian kingdom.
