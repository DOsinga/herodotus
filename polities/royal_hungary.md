description: >-
  Royal Hungary began in 1526 after the Ottoman victory at Mohacs and the contested succession to
  the Hungarian throne. Habsburg kings initially controlled chiefly northern and western Hungary,
  including much of present day Slovakia, while Ottoman authorities and eastern Hungarian rulers
  held other regions. Pressburg became its main administrative and coronation center. The kingdom
  retained its laws, counties, nobility, and diet within the wider Habsburg monarchy, though royal
  control and local constitutional rights repeatedly clashed. Ottoman retreat expanded its
  territory after 1699. Religious conflict, rural burdens, and demands for political autonomy
  fueled rebellions, including Rakoczi's war and the revolution of 1848. After the revolution's
  defeat in 1849 Vienna governed Hungary directly, until the Compromise of 1867 made it one half
  of Austria-Hungary.
