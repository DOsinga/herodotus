description: >-
  Royal Hungary began in 1526 after the Ottoman victory at Mohacs and the contested
  succession to the Hungarian throne. Habsburg kings initially controlled chiefly
  northern and western Hungary, including much of present day Slovakia, while Ottoman
  authorities and eastern Hungarian rulers held other regions. Pressburg became its
  main administrative and coronation center. The kingdom retained its laws, counties,
  nobility, and diet within the wider Habsburg monarchy, though royal control and local
  constitutional rights repeatedly clashed. Ottoman retreat expanded its territory
  after 1699. Religious conflict, rural burdens, and demands for political autonomy
  fueled rebellions, including Rakoczi's war and the revolution of 1848. After defeat
  and renewed negotiations, the Compromise of 1867 recast Hungary as one half of the
  new Austro-Hungarian monarchy.
