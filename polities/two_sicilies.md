description: >-
  The Kingdom of the Two Sicilies united southern mainland Italy and Sicily under the
  Bourbon monarchy from 1816 to 1861. Ferdinand I combined the former kingdoms of Naples
  and Sicily after the Napoleonic wars, abolishing Sicily's separate constitution.
  Naples became the main seat of a centralised government. Royal authority, the Catholic
  Church and a largely agricultural economy shaped public life, although factories,
  shipping and an early railway also developed. Liberal revolts and Sicilian demands for
  autonomy challenged the monarchy. Constitutions were briefly granted, then withdrawn
  as the kings restored absolute power. In 1860, Garibaldi's volunteers conquered Sicily
  and advanced onto the mainland. Francis II withdrew to Gaeta, which fell in 1861. The
  kingdom was incorporated into the new Italian monarchy.
