description: >-
  The Yarkent Khanate ruled oasis settlements of the Tarim Basin in today's Xinjiang from 1514 to
  1705. Sultan Said Khan founded it by defeating the Dughlat ruler Abu Bakr and taking Kashgar,
  Yarkand, and Hotan. Its Sunni Muslim khans descended from Chagatai, linking their royal claims to
  the Mongol imperial family, while court life used the Turkic Chagatai language. Yarkand served as
  the capital, and irrigated farming and trade sustained its scattered towns. The rulers also
  campaigned towards Tibet and contested neighbouring territories. Over time, rival religious leaders
  known as khojas gained influence, while disputes within the royal family weakened the monarchy.
  Dzungar intervention increasingly constrained its affairs, and the conquest in 1705 ended the
  separate Chagatai royal government there.
