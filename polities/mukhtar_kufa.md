description: >-
  Mukhtar al-Thaqafi established a government at Kufa in Iraq in 685, during the second
  Muslim civil war. He expelled the governor appointed by Abd Allah ibn al-Zubayr and
  ruled much of Iraq and neighbouring Iranian districts for eighteen months. Mukhtar
  claimed to act for Muhammad ibn al-Hanafiyya, a son of Ali, and promised vengeance for
  Husayn's death at Karbala. His government appointed provincial governors and drew
  increasing support from non-Arab Muslim converts, whom he granted military pay and
  shares of booty. These policies alienated many Arab nobles. His general Ibrahim ibn
  al-Ashtar defeated an Umayyad army at Khazir in 686. Musab ibn al-Zubayr subsequently
  invaded from Basra, besieged Kufa and killed Mukhtar in 687, restoring Zubayrid control
  over the city.
