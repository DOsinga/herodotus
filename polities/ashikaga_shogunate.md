description: >-
  The Ashikaga shogunate was Japan's military government from 1336 to 1573, based in Kyoto rather than the former warrior capital of Kamakura. Ashikaga Takauji founded it after breaking with Emperor Go-Daigo's attempted restoration of imperial rule. Shoguns governed through warrior families and provincial military governors, while the emperor retained a separate court. Rival northern and southern imperial lines competed until their reconciliation in 1392. Under Yoshimitsu, the government supported trade with Ming China and helped foster the arts associated with the Muromachi period. Its dependence on powerful regional lords made central authority fragile. The Onin War devastated Kyoto and accelerated provincial warfare. Oda Nobunaga finally expelled the last shogun, Ashikaga Yoshiaki, in 1573, ending the Kyoto government's remaining political authority.
