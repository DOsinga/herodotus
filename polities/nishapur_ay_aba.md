description: >-
  After the Oghuz captured Sanjar in 1153 and his eastern Seljuk government collapsed, Mu'ayyid
  al-Din Ay-Aba, one of Sanjar's former slave commanders, made himself master of Nishapur and much
  of western Khorasan. He governed as an amir, at first in the name of the Seljuk family and later
  recognising the western Seljuk sultans, while competing with Oghuz chiefs and other commanders
  for the region's devastated cities. Nishapur, once a great centre of learning, was rebuilt on a
  new site after the Oghuz sack. Ay-Aba fought the Khwarazmshahs over Khorasan and was killed by
  Tekish in 1174. His son Toghan Shah and grandson Sanjar Shah held Nishapur until Tekish took the
  city in 1187.
