description: >-
  The Emirate of Gabès was a principality centred on the oasis port of Gabès in southern
  Tunisia, recorded here from 1062 to 1159. When Zirid authority collapsed after the Hilalian
  invasion, the Zirid governor al-Mu'izz ibn Muhammad ibn Walmiya declared his independence in
  1062 under the protection of the Hilali chief Mu'nis ibn Yahya. Around 1096 the townspeople
  and the Riyah Arabs replaced his family with Makki ibn Kamil of the Banu Jami', whose
  descendants ruled until the Almohads came. Rafi' ibn Jami' sought Norman protection against
  the Zirids, and later emirs built palaces, minted coins and hosted poets. The last emir,
  Mudafi' ibn Rushaid, surrendered Gabès to an Almohad army in 1159.
