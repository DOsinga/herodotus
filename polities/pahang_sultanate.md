description: >-
  The Pahang Sultanate was a Malay Muslim state on the east coast of the Malay Peninsula, covering the
  basin of the Pahang river with its royal seat near Pekan. Malacca had conquered the older kingdom of
  Pahang in 1454, and around 1470 a Malaccan prince, Muhammad Shah, became its first sultan. Pahang
  soon governed itself and became a rival of Malacca, and after 1511 it fought the Portuguese, the
  Dutch and Aceh by turns. It traded in gold and forest goods and its territory reached into parts of
  modern Selangor and Negeri Sembilan. Acehnese raids in the early seventeenth century devastated the
  state and carried off its royal family. In 1623 its sultan was crowned Sultan of Johor, and Pahang
  became part of Johor.
