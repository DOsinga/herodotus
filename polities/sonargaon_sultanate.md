description: >-
  The Sonargaon Sultanate was an independent Muslim state in eastern and southeastern Bengal from
  1338 to 1352, ruled from the city of Sonargaon near present Dhaka. Fakhruddin Mubarak Shah, an
  armour bearer of the Delhi governor Bahram Khan, declared independence after his master died. He
  beat off an army sent from Lakhnauti, took Comilla and Noakhali, and in 1340 became the first
  Muslim ruler to conquer the port of Chittagong, which became the state's outlet to the sea. He
  built roads, embankments and mosques, and Ibn Battuta visited his capital in 1346, praising his
  hospitality to Sufis. His son Ikhtiyaruddin Ghazi Shah succeeded him in 1349. In 1352 Shamsuddin
  Ilyas Shah, already master of Lakhnauti and Satgaon, took Sonargaon and united Bengal into one
  sultanate.
