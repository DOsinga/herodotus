description: >-
  Al-Jawf is the depression in northern Arabia whose main oasis, Dumat al-Jandal, lies at the
  head of Wadi Sirhan on the routes between Syria, Iraq and the Hejaz. This record covers the
  oasis after the caliphs stopped governing it. The Banu Kalb, the tribe that had dominated the
  area before Islam, still held the region in the tenth century, although the geographers
  report that they were losing ground there to their Tayy allies, and Kalb influence lasted
  into the eleventh. In later centuries the oasis and its fortress of Marid were held by its
  own clans and by the Bedouin who grazed the Jawf, with no lasting outside overlord. The
  record ends when the Rashidi emirs of Ha'il took the oasis in 1838.
