description: >-
  The Tuoba confederation was the union of Xianbei tribes led by the Tuoba chiefs north of
  the Chinese frontier in present Inner Mongolia and northern Shanxi, before the founding of
  Dai. Tuoba Liwei took over the Moluhui tribes in 248 and in 258 moved to the abandoned Han
  city of Shengle, where he held a great sacrifice and forced the neighbouring tribes to
  submit. He kept friendly relations with Cao Wei and Jin and sent his heir to their court,
  but governed his tribes himself. After his death in 277 the confederation fell into
  disorder, and from 294 it was divided between three chiefs. Tuoba Yilu reunited it in 307
  and, after helping the Jin governor Liu Kun, became Duke of Dai in 310.
