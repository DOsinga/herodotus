description: >-
  Kakheti was an eastern Georgian kingdom that emerged during the breakup of the united Georgian monarchy in 1466 and lasted until 1762. Bagrationi kings governed from Gremi and later Telavi, but foreign invasions and periods of direct occupation repeatedly interrupted their government. Agriculture and trade supported a court whose population remained Orthodox Christian despite periods of Persian and Ottoman supremacy. Safavid campaigns under Shah Abbas devastated the kingdom, destroying settlements and deporting many inhabitants to Iran. Later rulers alternated resistance, accommodation, and cooperation with neighboring Kartli. Greater freedom followed the collapse of Iranian central power after 1747. In 1762 Heraclius II inherited Kartli from his father and united the two kingdoms as Kartli-Kakheti, ending Kakheti's existence as a separate state.
