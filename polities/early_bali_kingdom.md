description: >-
  The early Kingdom of Bali governed the Indonesian island before Majapahit's conquest in 1343.
  Its first securely attested ruler, Sri Kesari Warmadewa, issued the Belanjong inscription in
  914, although settlement and religious activity were much older. Kings bearing the Warmadewa
  name supported Hindu and Buddhist institutions, with their royal heartland around present day
  Gianyar and Pejeng. Marriage linked Udayana's court to eastern Java; their son Airlangga became
  a prominent Javanese ruler. Temples, inscriptions, and Chinese coins reveal religious patronage
  and overseas connections across maritime Asia. Singhasari ruled Bali from 1284 until its fall in
  1292. Majapahit forces defeated the old government at Bedulu, replacing its rulers with a new
  political order rather than preserving one continuous island kingdom.
