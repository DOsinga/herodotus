description: >-
  The Neo-Inca State preserved an Inca monarchy in the forested mountains west of Cusco, in
  present Peru, from 1537 to 1572. Manco Inca established his court at Vilcabamba after
  failing to expel the Spanish from the former imperial heartland. His successors alternated
  armed resistance with negotiations, while keeping an Inca royal household and religious
  traditions beyond effective Spanish control. Sayri Tupac left to accept estates near
  Cusco, but Titu Cusi continued governing Vilcabamba and later accepted baptism. Warriors
  learned to use captured horses and European weapons alongside their own methods. After
  Titu Cusi died, Tupac Amaru became ruler. A Spanish expedition conquered Vilcabamba in
  1572, captured him, and executed him, ending the last independent remnant of the Inca
  imperial government.
