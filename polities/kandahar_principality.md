description: >-
  The Principality of Kandahar was an Afghan state centred on Kandahar in southern
  Afghanistan from 1818 to 1856. It arose when the Barakzai brothers rebelled against the
  Sadozai Durrani kings after the blinding and murder of their leader, the vizier Fateh
  Khan. Sher Dil Khan and his brothers, known as the Dil brothers, took Kandahar and
  governed it together, while their half-brother Dost Mohammad Khan established himself at
  Kabul. The Kandahar sardars kept their own government and army, and dealt separately with
  Iran and with the British, who occupied the city during the First Anglo-Afghan War.
  Kohandil Khan, the most prominent of the brothers, returned to power after the war. His
  death in 1855 led to disputes among his heirs, and Dost Mohammad took Kandahar in 1856.
