description: >-
  Kush was a Nubian kingdom along the Nile in present-day Sudan, recorded here from about
  780 BCE to around 350 CE. Its kings first ruled from Napata after Egyptian control of
  Nubia weakened. Kushite rulers conquered Egypt and formed its Twenty-fifth Dynasty
  before Assyrian invasions drove them south. The monarchy continued in Nubia, drawing on
  Egyptian religious traditions while developing distinctive local institutions and art.
  Meroë became the capital around 590 BCE and later the royal burial centre. Its pyramids,
  temples, inscriptions, and ironworking remains preserve evidence of a wealthy society
  connected to African and Mediterranean trade. Powerful royal women also exercised
  authority. The kingdom's final collapse is poorly understood; the approximate ending
  falls around the Aksumite king Ezana's campaign.
