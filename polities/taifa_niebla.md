description: >-
  The Taifa of Niebla was a Muslim kingdom in the southwest of al-Andalus, centred on the walled
  town of Niebla near the Tinto river in present Huelva province. It was ruled by the Arab Banu
  Yahsub family after the breakup of the Caliphate of Córdoba. From the 1040s the expanding taifa
  of Seville under al-Mu'tadid absorbed one neighbour after another. Niebla fell to Seville in
  1053. When Almoravid rule collapsed, Niebla governed itself again from 1145 until the Almohads
  took it in 1150. After the Almohad collapse, Ibn Mahfuz made it a kingdom once more in 1234,
  holding out against the Christian advance under Castilian protection for decades, until Alfonso
  X of Castile besieged and took Niebla in 1262.
