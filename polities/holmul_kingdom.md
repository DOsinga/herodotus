description: >-
  Holmul was a Maya royal centre in the eastern Peten region of present-day Guatemala. The
  kingdom represented here spans approximately 300 to 900, although settlement at the site began
  much earlier. Palaces, elite tombs, inscriptions, and monumental decoration provide evidence
  for its court and connections with neighbouring powers. Nearby La Sufricaya belonged to this
  political landscape; its murals suggest involvement by people linked to Tikal or Teotihuacan.
  Later sculpture connects Holmul with Naranjo and the wider rivalry between Tikal and Calakmul.
  Such ties changed without necessarily replacing local rulers. The city flourished during the
  later Classic period. Defensive walls suggest insecurity near its end, but their precise
  purpose remains uncertain. Abandonment around 900 ended the centre's urban and royal history.
