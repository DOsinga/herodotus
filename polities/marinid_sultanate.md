name: Marinid Sultanate
description: >-
  The Marinid Sultanate ruled much of present Morocco from 1244 to 1465, with Fez as its
  centre. Its royal family came from the Banu Marin, a Zenata Berber group that challenged the
  weakening Almohads. The capture of Marrakesh in 1269 completed their overthrow of Almohad rule in Morocco.
  Marinid sultans built a new administrative quarter at Fez and sponsored madrasas promoting Maliki Sunni learning.
  They intervened in Iberia alongside Granada and briefly extended their power eastward over much of the
  Maghreb. Defeat at Río Salado in 1340 checked their Iberian ambitions. Later succession struggles strengthened viziers,
  especially the Wattasid family, while provincial authority fragmented. A revolt in Fez killed the last Marinid
  sultan in 1465 and ended the ruling dynasty.
