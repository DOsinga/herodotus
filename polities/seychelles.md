  Seychelles is an island republic in the western Indian Ocean, northeast of Madagascar,
  independent from Britain since 1976. Victoria, on Mahé, is its capital. The islands had
  been settled under French colonial rule before passing to Britain, producing a society
  with African, European, and Asian roots and a shared Creole language. Independence
  initially brought a multiparty republic, but France Albert René overthrew President
  James Mancham in 1977. A socialist one party system followed before political
  competition returned in the early 1990s under a new constitution. The state is governed
  by a president and National Assembly. Tourism and fisheries became important economic
  activities after an earlier reliance on agriculture. Independence and state continuity
  survived these changes of government and political system.
