description: >-
  The Kingdom of Syrmia was the realm of the Serbian king Stefan Dragutin in the Sava
  valley, which he ruled from Belgrade and Debrc between 1284 and his death in 1316.
  Dragutin gave up the Serbian throne to his brother Milutin in 1282, and in 1284 his
  Hungarian brother-in-law, King Ladislaus IV, granted him the banates of Mačva, Usora and
  Soli with Belgrade. He began as a Hungarian vassal, but as royal power in Hungary
  collapsed he governed on his own account, added Braničevo and Kučevo in 1291, and made
  Belgrade a Serbian royal seat for the first time. After his death Milutin seized the
  realm and imprisoned Dragutin's son Vladislav, and Hungary retook Belgrade in 1319.
