name: "Wen Dan Kingdom"
description: >-
  Wen Dan was an inland Southeast Asian court known mainly through Chinese accounts of embassies to
  the Tang dynasty. This record places it approximately between 681 and 800 in the middle Mekong and
  Chi basin region, with Kantharawichai as a proposed capital. Its identification with Land Chenla,
  and the extent of the territory it governed, remain disputed. References to a king, crown prince and
  royal companions indicate a court able to conduct diplomacy, but provide little detail about
  internal administration. Missions brought elephants and received Chinese titles; a contingent also
  joined Tang campaigns against Nanzhao. The last recorded embassy arrived in 798 or 799. Wen Dan then
  disappears from the sources, leaving the circumstances of its ending and connections uncertain.
