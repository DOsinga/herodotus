description: >-
  Cirebon was a Muslim sultanate on the north coast of West Java, centred on the port of the same
  name. A trading settlement grew there in the mid-fifteenth century under the Hindu kingdom of Sunda.
  Its ruler Sunan Gunungjati, one of the saints credited with spreading Islam in Java, declared
  independence from Sunda in 1482 and helped found the Sultanate of Banten. Cirebon became a centre of
  trade and Islamic learning and controlled much of the eastern Sundanese country. In the seventeenth
  century it fell under the influence of Mataram while keeping its own rulers. In 1677 the royal house
  divided into separate courts, and in 1681 they accepted the protection of the Dutch East India
  Company, which then controlled their foreign affairs.
