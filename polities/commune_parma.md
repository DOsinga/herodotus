description: >-
  The Commune of Parma was the self-governing city state of Parma in Emilia, from the late
  eleventh century until the Visconti bought the city in 1346. Consuls and then podestà
  governed it, and its Guelph citizens became famous for breaking Frederick II's siege in
  1248, when they stormed and burned his camp town of Vittoria. In the fourteenth century
  native lords such as Giberto da Correggio ruled through the commune, and outsiders kept
  taking the city: the papal legate Bertrand du Pouget, John of Bohemia, the Rossi and
  then the Scaliger lords of Verona. The Correggio threw out the Scaliger in 1341, but
  sold Parma to Obizzo d'Este three years later, who sold it on to Luchino Visconti.
