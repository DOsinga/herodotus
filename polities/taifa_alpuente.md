description: >-
  The Taifa of Alpuente was one of the smallest Muslim kingdoms of al-Andalus, centred on the hill
  town of Alpuente in the interior of the present province of Valencia. Its rulers came from the
  Berber Banu Qasim family. It emerged from the civil war that broke up the Caliphate of Córdoba
  after 1009, when local commanders and families took power in their own districts. The kingdom
  controlled a mountainous district on the routes between Valencia, Albarracín and the upper Turia
  valley, and its lords defended their castle and surrounding villages while more powerful taifas
  fought over the coast and the great cities. The Almoravids, who had crossed from Morocco and
  absorbed the taifas one by one, took Alpuente in 1106.
