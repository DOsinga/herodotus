description: >-
  The Shun dynasty, officially the Great Shun, was the state of the rebel leader Li Zicheng
  during the fall of the Ming, lasting from 1643 to 1645. Li, a former post-station worker
  from Shaanxi, led one of the great peasant armies of the late Ming famines. In 1643 he took
  Xiangyang and then Xi'an, and on the first day of the lunar year 1644 he founded the Shun
  at Xi'an. His army took Beijing in April 1644, and the last Ming emperor hanged himself.
  Within weeks Wu Sangui and the Manchu prince Dorgon defeated Li at Shanhai Pass, and he
  abandoned Beijing in June. Qing armies drove him from Xi'an early in 1645, and he died in
  Hubei later that year. His followers then joined the Southern Ming.
