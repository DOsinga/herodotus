description: >-
  Dai was a Tuoba Xianbei state on the northern frontier of China from 310 to 376. Its
  principal centre was Shengle, near present day Hohhot in Inner Mongolia. Tuoba Yilu
  received the title Duke of Dai from the Western Jin court after helping its forces fight
  rival powers, and his rank was raised to prince in 315. The rulers combined leadership of a
  tribal confederation with growing formal administrative institutions and diplomatic and
  military relations with neighbouring Chinese states. Succession struggles repeatedly
  disrupted the monarchy, but Tuoba Shiyijian restored its strength and governed for several
  decades. Former Qin conquered Dai in 376 amid internal turmoil. Members of the Tuoba ruling
  family later reestablished their power as Northern Wei in 386.
