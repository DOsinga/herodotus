description: >-
  St. Gallen was a town in northeastern Switzerland that grew up around the abbey of Saint Gall
  and made its fortune from linen, which its merchants sold across Europe. The abbots held the
  town's lordship, but they granted the citizens privileges in 1291, and by about 1353 the guilds
  led by the weavers controlled its council. In 1415 the town bought its liberty from King
  Sigismund, and in 1457 it bought out the abbot's last rights, becoming a free imperial city. It
  allied with the Swiss confederates and fought in the Burgundian Wars. Under the mayor and
  reformer Joachim Vadian it adopted the Reformation in the 1520s, and a wall separated it from
  the Catholic abbey after 1566. The Helvetic Republic ended its independence in 1798.
