description: >-
  Caucasian Albania was a kingdom in the eastern Caucasus, chiefly in present Azerbaijan and
  neighbouring areas, emerging approximately around 100 BCE. It is unrelated to modern Balkan
  Albania. Its rulers governed a diverse population between the mountains and Caspian Sea, with
  Partaw becoming an important royal and religious centre. Christianity spread through the
  kingdom, while Iranian influence shaped court politics. Arsacid kings ruled under Sasanian
  overlordship until the monarchy was abolished in 462. Vachagan restored royal institutions
  around 485, but direct Persian administration resumed about 510. A separate Mihranid princely
  court reappeared around 630 after Sasanian power collapsed. Its leaders faced Byzantine,
  Khazar, and Arab pressures. Direct Arab provincial government ended this court in 705; later
  mountain principalities continued separately.
