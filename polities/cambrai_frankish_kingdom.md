description: >-
  The Frankish kingdom centred on Cambrai was one of several local royal governments in northern
  Gaul during the fifth century. Its beginnings around 460 are estimated from the kingship of
  Ragnachar, a relative and eventual rival of Clovis. Ragnachar commanded his own warriors rather than
  serving as an official within a united Frankish state. He joined Clovis against the Roman
  ruler Syagrius in 486, helping expand Frankish power. Later accounts describe discontent among his followers
  and Clovis's use of gifts to win their support. Around 509, Clovis defeated Ragnachar, had
  him and his brother killed, and took their lands. The episode forms part of Clovis's
  wider destruction of competing royal houses, although the surviving narratives were written after the events.
