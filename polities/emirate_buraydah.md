description: >-
  The Emirate of Buraydah was the oasis state of Buraydah in the Qassim region of central
  Arabia in the decades between the decline of the second Saudi state and the rise of the
  Rashidis of Ha'il. Buraydah had long had its own emirs, first from the Al Abu Ulayan, who
  built up the town, and it had served both the first and second Saudi states. In the 1860s
  Muhanna ibn Salih Aba al-Khayl seized the emirate, and his family ruled the town and its
  date gardens as the Saudi family fell into civil war after Faisal ibn Turki's death in
  1865. The emirs of Buraydah balanced between Riyadh, Ha'il and neighbouring Unaizah. After
  the Rashidi victory at Mulayda in 1891 the Rashidis took Qassim and installed their own
  governors.
