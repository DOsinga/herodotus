description: >-
  The Kingdom of Beni Abbas ruled Lesser Kabylia and the Medjana plain in eastern Algeria
  from its fortress capital, the Kalâa of Ait Abbas in the Biban mountains. It was founded
  around 1510 as Hafsid power at Béjaïa collapsed, and was led by the Mokrani family, whose
  chiefs took the titles of sultan and later sheikh of the Medjana. The kingdom resisted
  both the Spanish and the Regency of Algiers, defeating Ottoman armies in the 1550s and
  later charging Algiers a fee to march troops through the pass known as the Iron Gates.
  Andalusian refugees brought crafts to the Kalâa. In the eighteenth century the family
  split into rival branches. After 1830 its leaders became French khalifas, and the Mokrani
  revolt of 1871 ended their role.
