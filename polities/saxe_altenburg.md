description: >-
  Saxe-Altenburg was an Ernestine duchy in eastern Thuringia, centred on Altenburg and its hilltop
  castle. The first duchy was created in 1603 for the sons of Duke Frederick William I of Saxe-
  Weimar, and gained part of Coburg-Eisenach in 1638; when its line died out in 1672, its lands
  passed to Ernest the Pious of Saxe-Gotha. The second duchy began in 1826, when the extinction of
  the Gotha-Altenburg line led to a reshuffling of the Ernestine lands, and Duke Frederick of
  Saxe-Hildburghausen moved to Altenburg as its ruler. The nineteenth-century duchy was small and
  largely agricultural, known for the game of skat, and joined the North German Confederation in
  1867.
