description: >-
  The Duchy of the Archipelago was a Latin island state in the Cyclades, centred on Naxos and
  Paros. The Venetian Marco Sanudo seized the islands around 1207, after the Fourth Crusade,
  and ruled them as duke from a castle above the town of Naxos. His family and, from 1383, the
  Crispo family governed the duchy, while Venetian and other Latin lords held some of the
  smaller islands as fiefs. The dukes lived between Venice, the Latin states of Greece and the
  growing Ottoman navy, and pirate raids repeatedly emptied the islands. Barbarossa forced
  Giovanni IV Crispo to pay tribute in 1537. In 1566 Sultan Selim II deposed the last duke and
  gave the islands to his favourite Joseph Nasi, whose deputy governed them for the Ottomans.
