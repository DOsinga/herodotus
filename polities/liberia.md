description: >-
  Liberia is a republic on the Atlantic coast of West Africa, independent since 1847
  and governed from Monrovia. Its origins lie in settlements sponsored by the American
  Colonization Society for free and formerly enslaved Black Americans, alongside
  settlers from the Caribbean. Their descendants, known as Americo-Liberians, dominated
  the presidency and institutions while indigenous communities faced exclusion. The
  country avoided European colonial conquest, although foreign investment and influence
  shaped its economy, especially rubber production. A military coup in 1980 ended the
  old ruling elite's control. Two civil wars between 1989 and 2003 devastated society
  and displaced many people. Subsequent elections restored civilian government,
  including the presidency of Ellen Johnson Sirleaf. Liberia continues under a
  constitution with an elected president and legislature.
