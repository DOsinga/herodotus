description: >-
  Palistin, also written Walistin, was a Neo-Hittite kingdom in the north of Syria and the
  Amuq plain of Hatay, one of the states that grew out of the collapse of the Hittite
  Empire. It is known almost entirely from Luwian hieroglyphic inscriptions of its king
  Taita and his wife Kupapiya, found at Shaizar, Meharde and in the temple of the storm god
  in the Aleppo citadel. These show a large realm reaching from the Amuq to Aleppo
  and south towards Hama. Its capital was probably Kunulua at Tell Tayinat. The likeness of
  its name to the Philistines has led some scholars to link the two, but this is unproven.
  The kingdom seems to have split into Pattin, Bit Agusi and Hamath, with Pattin keeping
  a short form of the name.
