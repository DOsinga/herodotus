description: >-
  The Berziti were a South Slavic people who settled in western Macedonia, around Lake
  Ohrid and the plain of Pelagonia, at the end of the sixth century, and formed one of the
  sklaviniai that governed themselves beyond Byzantine control. Byzantine writers called
  their land Berzitia and knew their leaders as archons. In the late sixth century they
  sacked Roman towns such as Scupi, and at Heraclea Lyncestis, near modern Bitola, Slav
  settlers built houses in the ruins of the theatre and fortified their village. Byzantine
  emperors campaigned against the sklaviniai in the seventh and eighth centuries without
  lasting success. The Bulgarian khans absorbed Berzitia in the first half of the ninth
  century, and its people became Bulgarian subjects.
