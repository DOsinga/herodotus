  Saint Kitts and Nevis is a federation of two islands in the eastern Caribbean, independent
  from Britain since 1983. Basseterre on Saint Kitts is the federal capital, while Nevis has
  its own assembly and premier. The country is a parliamentary constitutional monarchy, with
  the monarch represented by a governor general and government led by a prime minister. Its
  colonial economy centred on sugar plantations worked by enslaved Africans, creating a
  society shaped by British rule and Caribbean trade. Tourism and other services later
  became important as sugar production declined. Nevis retains a constitutional route to
  secession, although a 1998 referendum fell short of the required majority. The federation
  remains one of the world's smallest independent states by area and population.
