description: >-
  The Commune of Asti was the self-governing city state of Asti in Piedmont, from the
  first consuls in 1095 until it accepted Luchino Visconti as lord in 1342. It was among
  the first Italian communes, and gained the right to mint coins in 1140. Frederick
  Barbarossa burned the city in 1155, but Asti recovered and grew rich in the thirteenth
  century from trade and from its moneylenders, the casane, who worked across France,
  Flanders and the Rhineland. The commune fought the marquises of Montferrat and Saluzzo,
  Savoy and Alessandria, and defeated Charles of Anjou's army at Roccavione in 1275.
  Rivalry between the Solaro and the de Castello split the city, and the Visconti took
  over.
