description: >-
  The Alchon Kingdom represents royal power that developed from Central Asian incursions into
  Bactria around 370 and continued in changing forms until about 670. Its rulers expanded through
  the Kabul region and Gandhara into northern India, where Toramana and Mihirakula became
  particularly prominent. Coins are central to reconstructing this history: they show royal names,
  distinctive symbols and portraits with elongated skulls, often adapting earlier Iranian designs.
  The monarchy's Indian expansion contributed to the fragmentation of Gupta power, but defeats in
  Malwa around 528 checked its reach. Those setbacks did not immediately end Alchon government
  farther northwest. Udabhanda marks a Gandharan royal centre rather than a permanent capital for
  every ruler. Late relationships with the Nezak rulers remain uncertain and disputed.
