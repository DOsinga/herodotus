description: >-
  Byzantium was the Greek city state on the Bosporus that preceded Constantinople, founded
  by colonists from Megara around 667 BCE and annexed to the Roman Empire by Vespasian in
  73 CE. Its walls and harbour on the Golden Horn controlled the passage between the Black
  Sea and the Aegean, and the city lived from fishing, grain shipments and tolls. Persia
  held it from 512 until Pausanias freed it in 478 BCE. It then joined the Delian League,
  revolted against Athens more than once, and spent years under Spartan garrisons. In 340
  BCE it withstood Philip II's siege. Later it paid tribute to the Celts of Tylis, fought
  Rhodes over its tolls, and became a free ally of Rome.
