description: >-
  The Ikkō-ikki were leagues of farmers, priests, merchants and petty warriors who followed the
  Jōdo Shinshū Buddhism of the Hongan-ji temple and governed territory of their own in Sengoku
  Japan from 1488 to 1580. In 1488 the leagues of Kaga province overthrew the military governor
  Togashi Masachika, and for nearly a century Kaga was ruled by its temples and leagues, later
  under officials of the Hongan-ji at the Oyama temple-fortress on the site of Kanazawa. The
  head temple itself moved in 1533 to the fortified Ishiyama Hongan-ji at Osaka. The leagues
  fought the Asakura, Uesugi and Oda, and held Echizen in 1574. After a ten-year siege the
  Hongan-ji surrendered Ishiyama to Oda Nobunaga in 1580, and Kaga fell the same year.
