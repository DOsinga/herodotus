description: >-
  Anhalt-Bernburg was a small inland hereditary state in central Germany, ruled by the Ascanian
  family from its residence at Bernburg. A first Bernburg line ruled from the division of 1252
  until 1468; the second began with the partition of 1603. Christian I received Bernburg as his
  share of the family lands. Later divisions created smaller related principalities, some of which
  returned through inheritance. The princes governed within the Holy Roman Empire before its
  dissolution, then participated in the Confederation of the Rhine and the German Confederation.
  Their title rose to duke in 1803. Marriage and dynastic succession shaped the state's boundaries
  more than national borders did. When Duke Alexander Karl died without children in 1863, his
  lands passed to Dessau, completing Anhalt's reunification.
