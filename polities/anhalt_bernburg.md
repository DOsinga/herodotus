description: >-
  Anhalt-Bernburg was a small inland hereditary state in central Germany, ruled by the
  Ascanian family from its residence at Bernburg. The government covered here began with
  the renewed partition of Anhalt in 1603 and lasted until 1863. Christian I received
  Bernburg as his share of the family lands. Later divisions created smaller related
  principalities, some of which returned through inheritance. The princes governed within
  the Holy Roman Empire before its dissolution, then participated in the Confederation of
  the Rhine and the German Confederation. Their title rose to duke in 1803. Marriage and
  dynastic succession shaped the state's boundaries more than national borders did. When
  Duke Alexander Karl died without children in 1863, his lands passed to Dessau,
  completing Anhalt's reunification.
