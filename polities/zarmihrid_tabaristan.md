name: "Zarmihrid Principality"
description: >-
  The Zarmihrid Principality occupied mountainous parts of Tabaristan, south of the Caspian
  Sea in present Iran, from roughly 550 to 785. Its beginnings are reconstructed from
  traditions about grants made during the reign of the Sasanian king Khosrow I. The ruling
  house claimed descent from Sukhra, a noble of the Karen family. Zarmihr supposedly received
  lands and the titles marzban and masmughan for helping defend the empire's eastern frontier.
  Local hereditary rulers shared the highland region with Karenid and other dynasties,
  preserving Iranian traditions after the Arab conquest of the lowlands. Their precise capital
  remains unknown. In 783 they joined neighbouring rulers in rebellion against Abbasid
  authority. The alliance's defeat in 785 ended their recorded political existence in the
  region.
