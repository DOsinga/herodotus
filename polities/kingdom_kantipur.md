description: >-
  Kantipur was one of the Malla kingdoms of the Kathmandu Valley in Nepal, with its capital at the
  city now called Kathmandu. It arose after the death of Yaksha Malla in 1482, when his sons divided
  the valley between them. Its kings ruled from the Hanuman Dhoka palace and lived from the trade
  between India and Tibet that passed through the valley, and they competed with the neighbouring
  kingdoms of Lalitpur and Bhaktapur in war and in building temples. Pratap Malla, in the
  seventeenth century, was its best known king and a patron of poetry and architecture. The rising
  Gorkha kingdom blockaded the valley for years, and Prithvi Narayan Shah captured Kathmandu during
  the festival of Indra Jatra in 1768, making it the capital of the new Kingdom of Nepal.
