description: >-
  Bern was a city on a bend of the Aare in western Switzerland, founded in 1191 by Duke Berthold V
  of Zähringen. When the Zähringer died out in 1218 it became a free imperial city, governed by
  its council and a Schultheiss chosen from leading families. During the interregnum it put itself
  under the protection of Peter of Savoy, from 1255 to 1268, while keeping its own government.
  Bern built up a territory in the Aare valley by buying lordships, taking nobles as citizens and
  making alliances with neighbouring towns and valleys. With help from the Forest Cantons it
  defeated Fribourg and the nobles of the region at Laupen in 1339. In 1353 it joined the Swiss
  Confederacy, in which it became the largest and strongest canton.
