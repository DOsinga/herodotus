description: >-
  Koch Hajo was the eastern half of the Koch kingdom of Kamata, ruled by Raghudev and his son
  Parikshit Narayan from 1581 to 1613. Raghudev, son of the general Chilarai, had expected to
  inherit from his uncle Nara Narayan until a late son was born, and he declared himself king
  of the east at Barnagar near the Manas river. The division was settled peacefully, with the
  Sankosh river as the border, and after Nara Narayan's death in 1587 Raghudev stopped paying
  tribute. The two Koch kingdoms fought, Koch Bihar sought Mughal help, and Raghudev moved his
  capital to North Guwahati. Parikshit continued the struggle until Mughal armies conquered
  the kingdom in 1613, opening a long Mughal and Ahom contest over Kamrup.
