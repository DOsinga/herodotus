description: >-
  The Judicate of Logudoro, also called Torres, was the largest of the four kingdoms of
  medieval Sardinia and covered the northwest of the island. Its judges first ruled from Porto
  Torres and later from Ardara, and divided their lands into twenty districts under curators.
  Logudoro is the earliest judicate to appear clearly in the records, in the eleventh century,
  when its rulers invited Benedictine monks and Pisan and Genoese traders. Sassari grew into
  its largest town. In the thirteenth century the judges were pulled between Pisa, Genoa and
  the emperor; Adelasia married Enzo, son of Frederick II, who took the title king of
  Sardinia. When she died in 1259 without heirs, the Doria, the Malaspina, Arborea and Sassari
  divided the judicate.
