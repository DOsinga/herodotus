description: >-
  The Duchy of Prussia occupied the southeastern Baltic coast, with its capital at
  Königsberg. It began in 1525 when Albert of Hohenzollern secularised the remaining
  Prussian lands of the Teutonic Order and became their hereditary duke. Adopting
  Lutheranism made the duchy an early centre of Protestant government. The population
  included German, Polish and Lithuanian speakers, and local estates played a part in its
  political life. Initially a fief of the Polish crown, Prussia retained its ducal
  administration. The Brandenburg electors inherited it in 1618, creating a personal
  union. Treaties in 1657 ended Polish overlordship. The duchy's position outside the Holy
  Roman Empire enabled its ruler to take a royal title in 1701, beginning the Kingdom of
  Prussia under Hohenzollern rule.
