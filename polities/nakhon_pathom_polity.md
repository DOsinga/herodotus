description: >-
  Nakhon Pathom was a major early urban center in the central plain of present
  Thailand. Its principal political phase is estimated here between 500 and 1000,
  within the wider Dvaravati cultural world. Large moats, extensive settlement remains,
  and monumental religious buildings indicate organized local government, although
  rulers and royal dates are poorly known. Buddhist monuments made the city an
  important religious center, while imported objects show connections beyond the plain.
  Its exceptionally large enclosure and surrounding settlements suggest a regional
  hierarchy rather than merely an isolated town. Dvaravati should not be treated as one
  unified kingdom governing every associated city. Nakhon Pathom's transition toward
  Khmer influence is uncertain, and occupation continued after the main governing phase
  represented by this record.
