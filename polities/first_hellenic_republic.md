description: >-
  The First Hellenic Republic was the Greek revolutionary state that developed during the struggle against Ottoman rule from 1822 to
  1832. Its provisional administration brought together representatives through national assemblies and written constitutions, although local military leaders retained considerable power.
  Nafplio became an important seat of government. Fighting against Ottoman and Egyptian armies, coupled with disputes among Greek factions, repeatedly
  threatened the new state. Britain, France and Russia intervened, and their victory at Navarino helped secure independence. Ioannis Kapodistrias, chosen
  governor in 1827, attempted to rebuild administration and the economy but clashed with regional elites. His assassination in 1831 renewed
  political disorder. The international settlement of 1832 established the Kingdom of Greece with the Bavarian prince Otto as its king.
