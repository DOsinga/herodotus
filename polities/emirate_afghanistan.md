description: >-
  The Emirate of Afghanistan developed from a Barakzai principality in Kabul established in
  1823 and became a kingdom in 1926. Its hereditary rulers gradually reunited territories
  divided after the decline of the Durrani Empire. Dost Mohammad Khan consolidated power and
  recovered Herat shortly before his death. Afghanistan's position between British India and
  Russian expansion brought repeated interventions and three Anglo-Afghan wars. The first
  British invasion failed, but the 1879 Treaty of Gandamak placed foreign affairs under
  British control. Abdur Rahman Khan strengthened central authority through military
  campaigns, forced resettlement and repression, while negotiating boundaries with
  neighboring powers. Amanullah regained independent foreign relations following the 1919
  war and pursued reforms. In 1926 he adopted the royal title, continuing the same dynasty.
