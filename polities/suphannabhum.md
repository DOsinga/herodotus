description: >-
  Suphannabhum was a city-state in the western Chao Phraya basin of central Thailand, with
  its seat at present Suphan Buri and older centres at U Thong and Nakhon Pathom. This
  record runs from 913 to 1351. Thai chronicles give a line of rulers from the early tenth
  century, but most of the early chronology is legendary. Chinese records of the period
  name nearby polities such as Chen Li Fu, and from 1349 call the Tai state of the lower
  basin Xian. Jayavarman VII of Angkor listed Suvarnapura among cities receiving his Buddha
  images around 1191, but inscriptions show no Khmer administration in the west. In 1351
  Suphannabhum joined Lavo and Ayodhya in forming the Ayutthaya Kingdom, whose throne its
  royal house later held. Suphan Buri itself became a provincial town.
