description: >-
  The Tangier International Zone was the city of Tangier and its surroundings in northern
  Morocco, governed under international control from 1923 to 1956, apart from a Spanish
  occupation during the Second World War. A convention signed in Paris in 1923 by France,
  Spain and Britain, later joined by Italy and other powers, kept Tangier out of both the
  French and Spanish protectorates. A committee of control made up of the consuls of the
  signatory powers and an international legislative assembly governed the zone, and the sultan
  was represented by an official called the mendoub. The zone had its own courts and a free
  port and became a centre of banking and smuggling. Spain occupied it from 1940 to 1945, and
  it returned to Morocco in 1956.
