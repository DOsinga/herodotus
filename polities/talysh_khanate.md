description: >-
  The Talysh Khanate was a khanate of Talysh origin on the southwestern shore of the Caspian Sea,
  with its capital at Lankaran. It covered the southeastern corner of today's Azerbaijan and the
  adjoining strip of northwestern Iran, a humid lowland backed by the forested Talysh Mountains.
  The khanate took shape in the middle of the eighteenth century, when Iranian authority weakened
  after the death of Nader Shah, and its khans balanced between the rulers of Iran, the
  neighbouring khans of Quba and Shirvan, and Russia. Mir Mustafa Khan sought Russian protection
  early in the nineteenth century, and the Treaty of Gulistan placed the khanate under Russia in
  1813. The last khan went over to Iran during the war of 1826 to 1828, and Russia then abolished
  the khanate.
