description: >-
  The Kingdom of Sardinia here means the Savoyard state after its rulers acquired Sardinia in 1720, not the earlier Aragonese kingdom on the island. Its mainland heart lay in Piedmont, with Turin serving as the seat of government. The House of Savoy ruled initially as an absolute monarchy over territories with differing institutions. During French occupation of the mainland from 1798 to 1814, the court governed from Sardinia. Restoration brought mainland recovery and the addition of Genoa. Charles Albert granted a constitution in 1848, establishing parliamentary institutions that survived military defeat. Under Victor Emmanuel II and Cavour, the kingdom led Italian unification through diplomacy, war, and annexation. In 1861 it became the Kingdom of Italy, carrying its monarchy and institutions.
