description: >-
  The Russian Republic was proclaimed in Petrograd in September 1917, after the February Revolution had
  removed the imperial monarchy. Alexander Kerensky's Provisional Government declared the republic while preparing for an
  elected Constituent Assembly to decide the country's future. Its authority was contested by workers' and
  soldiers' soviets, national movements and political opponents within the army. Continuing participation in the First
  World War intensified shortages, military exhaustion and anger. Liberal reforms had widened political freedoms, but
  the government struggled to resolve demands for peace and land. In November, the Bolsheviks seized
  power in the October Revolution and overthrew the provisional administration. This record ends with that
  transfer of actual power, rather than the later legal claims attached to the republican name.
