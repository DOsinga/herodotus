description: >-
  The Kingdom of Thanesar arose in northern India around 500, centred on Sthanvishvara in present day
  Haryana. Its Pushyabhuti rulers, also called the Vardhanas, began as a royal house in the political
  world that followed Gupta decline. Prabhakara Vardhana strengthened the kingdom through warfare and
  marriage alliances. After the deaths of his father and brother, Harsha inherited the throne in 606
  and expanded his authority across much of northern India. He later governed from Kannauj, joining
  the older Thanesar state to a wider royal realm. Court literature and the traveller Xuanzang
  describe his campaigns, religious patronage, and assemblies. Expansion southwards was checked by the
  Chalukyas. Harsha's death in 647 ended the larger government, although a successor court continued
  at Kannauj.
