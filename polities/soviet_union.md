description: >-
  The Soviet Union was a communist state spanning much of Eurasia from 1922 to
  1991, with its government in Moscow. Formally a federation of socialist
  republics, it was governed through central institutions dominated by the
  Communist Party. It emerged from the Russian Revolution and civil war, then
  transformed agriculture and industry through state planning. Stalin's rule
  brought forced collectivisation, mass repression, and famine. Victory over
  Nazi Germany came at enormous human cost and established Soviet influence
  across eastern Europe. During the Cold War, the country became a nuclear
  superpower and led several pioneering space missions. Economic stagnation and
  Gorbachev's reforms weakened party control. National movements and political
  crises led to dissolution in 1991, leaving independent republics across its
  former territory.
