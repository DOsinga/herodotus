description: >-
  The County of Aragon was a small Frankish marcher county in the central Pyrenees, in the
  valley of the Aragon river around Jaca, recorded here from about 800 to 922. The
  Carolingians set it up to guard the passes against the Muslims of the Ebro valley, and its
  first known ruler, Oriol, appears in 807. Count Aznar Galíndez I, a vassal of Charlemagne,
  was driven out by his son-in-law García the Bad with help from Íñigo Arista of Pamplona, and
  from 844 its counts were vassals of the Pamplonan kings. The monastery of San Pedro de
  Siresa kept a notable library. When Count Galindo Aznárez II died without a legitimate son,
  Aragon passed to the crown of Pamplona in 922.
