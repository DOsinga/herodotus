description: >-
  The Kingdom of Bohemia developed around Prague in the lands of today's Czech Republic, gaining
  lasting royal status under Ottokar I in 1198. Its kings belonged successively to the Přemyslid,
  Luxembourg, Jagiellon and Habsburg houses. Although part of the Holy Roman Empire, Bohemia
  retained its own government and estates, and its king became an imperial elector. Under Charles
  IV, Prague grew into an imperial capital and centre of learning. The Hussite movement and
  ensuing wars made religion a central political issue during the fifteenth century. Habsburg
  kings inherited the crown in 1526 but faced powerful local estates. Their victory over the
  Bohemian revolt at White Mountain in 1620 ended this period of separate political control and
  strengthened rule from Austria.
