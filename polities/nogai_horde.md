description: >-
  The Nogai Horde was a confederation of pastoral communities north of the Caspian Sea,
  conventionally dated from 1480 to 1634. It emerged from the weakening Golden Horde around
  the Manghit leadership descended from Edigu. Its rulers held the title bey rather than
  claiming descent from Genghis Khan, and leading mirzas governed mobile groups with
  considerable freedom. Saray-Jük on the lower Ural River served as a trading centre and
  winter seat. Herding, horse trading, tribute and raids, including the capture and sale of
  enslaved people, sustained its economy. Rivalries divided the confederation during the
  sixteenth century, producing a Lesser Nogai Horde farther west. Russian expansion and Kalmyk
  attacks broke its power, though Nogai communities continued in other regions after its
  collapse.
