description: >-
  Sierra Leone has been an independent state on West Africa's Atlantic coast since 1961,
  with its capital at Freetown. British rule had joined a coastal colony, associated with
  settlement by freed Africans, to an inland protectorate. Independence initially retained
  the British monarch and a parliamentary government; a republic followed in 1971. The
  country later experienced single party rule, military coups and a civil war from 1991 to
  2002. Diamond revenues helped sustain the conflict, while regional and international
  intervention supported its eventual end. Sierra Leone continued as the same state through
  these changes of regime. Its presidential republic has an elected parliament. Temne, Mende
  and other communities share the country, with English official and Krio widely used across
  linguistic boundaries.
