description: >-
  Sumenep was a principality on the eastern end of the island of Madura, off the north coast of East
  Java. Its rulers counted Arya Wiraraja, the regent of Sumenep who helped Raden Wijaya found
  Majapahit in 1293, as their forerunner. After Majapahit collapsed, Sumenep was one of several
  Madurese courts, alongside Arosbaya and Pamekasan, that governed themselves, adopted Islam and
  traded across the Java Sea. Little is recorded about its sixteenth-century rulers. In the early
  seventeenth century the Madurese courts sided with Surabaya against the rising power of Mataram.
  Sultan Agung invaded Madura in 1624, and the island was afterwards placed under one princely line,
  the Cakraningrat, who governed it as vassals of Mataram.
