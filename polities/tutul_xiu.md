description: >-
  Tutul-Xiu, also known as Maní, was a Maya province of the central Yucatán Peninsula ruled
  by the Xiu family from their capital at Maní. The Xiu claimed descent from lords who had
  come from the west and had long been rivals of the Cocom rulers of Mayapan. Around 1441
  the Xiu led the revolt that massacred the Cocom and destroyed Mayapan, after which they
  governed their own province under a halach uinik, a supreme lord with military, judicial
  and religious authority over the town chiefs. Their relations with neighbouring Sotuta
  stayed hostile, and in 1536 the Cocom killed a Xiu pilgrimage party. In 1542 the ruler Ah
  Kukum Xiu submitted to Francisco de Montejo the Younger and became his ally in the
  conquest of Yucatán.
