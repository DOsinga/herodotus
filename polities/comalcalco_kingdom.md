description: >-
  Comalcalco was a Maya kingdom centred on Joy Chan, an city in the lowlands of modern
  Tabasco, Mexico. Its separate court is represented here from about 500 until Tortuguero's
  conquest in 649. River access connected the settlement with Gulf Coast trade routes and
  the inland Maya world, while the surrounding region supported cacao production. Comalcalco
  is especially known for buildings made with fired clay bricks rather than the limestone
  masonry usual at major Maya centres. The named ruler Ox Balam appears in an inscription
  recording his defeat by Tortuguero's Balam Ahau. Construction and occupation continued
  after the conquest, and later inscriptions used the Baakal emblem associated with Palenque
  and Tortuguero. Those later remains do not establish restored independent royal
  government.
