description: >-
  Elmet was a Brittonic kingdom in what is now Yorkshire, surviving after Roman government
  withdrew from Britain. Its emergence around 450 is estimated, and its boundaries and royal
  centers remain uncertain. Barwick-in-Elmet is a proposed seat rather than an identified
  capital. The kingdom lay near the expanding Anglian realm of Deira and the Midlands territories
  that became Mercia. Welsh literary traditions associate the region with rulers such as Gwallog,
  while later historical accounts name Ceretic as its final king. Edwin of Northumbria expelled
  Ceretic around 616 or 617, ending Elmet's separate government. Brittonic inhabitants did not
  simply disappear with the conquest: their identity continued in later references and local
  place names. That survival does not demonstrate a restored independent kingdom.
