description: >-
  Metztitlán was an independent lordship in the Sierra Alta of present-day Hidalgo, north of
  the Basin of Mexico, centred on a fertile canyon valley. Its people were mostly Nahuatl
  and Otomi speakers, and its rulers governed a string of towns in the valley and the
  surrounding mountains. Protected by rugged terrain, Metztitlán was one of the few states
  that the Aztec Triple Alliance never conquered. The Aztec ruler Tizoc led a campaign
  against it in the 1480s that ended in defeat, and the lordship remained a hostile enclave
  on the empire's northern edge, comparable to Tlaxcala in the east. After the fall of
  Tenochtitlan its lords submitted to the Spanish in the early 1520s, and Augustinian friars
  later built a large monastery there.
