description: >-
  The Toucouleur Empire grew from Umar Tall's religious and military community at Dinguiraye in
  1849 into a major power across the western African interior. Tall, a leader of the Tijaniyya
  Sufi brotherhood, joined Islamic authority with conquest, recruiting followers from several
  peoples rather than only the Toucouleur. His armies defeated Kaarta, captured Ségou, and
  overthrew the Muslim Massina state, provoking resistance as well as expansion. After his death
  in 1864, his son Ahmadu governed at Ségou amid competing family territories and revolts. Trade,
  farming, tribute, and slavery supported the regime. French advances gradually destroyed its
  remaining centres. The endpoint here is the French capture of Bandiagara in 1893, rather than
  later dates sometimes used for the empire's formal colonial annexation.
