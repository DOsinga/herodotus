description: >-
  The Cao polity represents a Moche ruling center in Peru's lower Chicama Valley, approximately from 200 to 650 CE. Its seat at Cao Viejo formed part of the El Brujo complex, where monumental adobe platforms, reliefs, and burials reveal political and religious power. Repeated rebuilding required labor organized by local elites, while images of captives and sacrifice express the ceremonial roles of authority. The richly furnished burial known as the Lady of Cao shows that a woman could hold exceptional status within society. Its relationship with the southern Moche valley state remains debated, so this record treats the local court separately. The dates estimate its elite institutions rather than the entire occupation of El Brujo, which continued through later cultures.
