description: >-
  The Marshall Islands became an independent republic in free association with
  the United States in 1986. Located in the central Pacific, its many low coral
  atolls and islands form the Ratak and Ralik chains, with government based at
  Majuro. A president chosen by the elected Nitijela leads the parliamentary
  government, while traditional chiefs retain an advisory role. Earlier German,
  Japanese, and American administration shaped the islands' modern history.
  American nuclear tests at Bikini and Enewetak displaced communities and left
  lasting health and environmental problems. The Compact of Free Association
  provides American assistance and defence responsibilities while preserving
  Marshallese sovereignty. Fishing, overseas employment, and external support
  matter greatly to the economy. Rising seas threaten the land on which island
  communities depend.
