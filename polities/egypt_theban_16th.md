description: >-
  The Theban kingdom of the Sixteenth Dynasty is a reconstruction of an Upper Egyptian state during the Second Intermediate Period, approximately 1650 to 1580 BCE. It follows the interpretation that these pharaohs ruled from Thebes independently of the Hyksos kings at Avaris. Other scholars identify the dynasty differently, including as rulers subordinate to the Hyksos. Surviving royal names, monuments, and the damaged Turin king list provide incomplete evidence for its rulers and chronology. In this reconstruction, northern armies reduced Theban territory, although some reigns may have included intervals of peace. Famine compounded the kingdom's difficulties. A Hyksos conquest of Thebes ended this government, before the later Seventeenth Dynasty renewed Theban power. Its boundaries and exact ending remain matters of debate.
