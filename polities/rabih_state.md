description: >-
  Rabih az-Zubayr built a military state across the eastern Sahel in the late nineteenth century.
  A former soldier in the Egyptian army and lieutenant of the Sudanese slave trader al-Zubayr
  Rahma Mansur, he led a raiding army westward after his patron's power collapsed in 1879. Rifles
  and disciplined troops let him dominate Dar al-Kuti and the borderlands of Bagirmi and Wadai,
  and in 1893 he overthrew the al-Kanemi rulers of Bornu, making Dikwa his capital. He governed
  through military commanders, taxing farmers and trading captives and ivory. His expansion
  collided with French columns advancing towards Lake Chad. French and allied forces defeated and
  killed him at Kousséri in 1900, and his son Fadlallah was killed the following year, ending the
  state.
