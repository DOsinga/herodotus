description: >-
  The Theban kingdom of the eleventh dynasty governed southern Egypt during the First Intermediate Period, beginning here with Intef I's
  royal claim around 2134 BCE. Its ruling family developed from local governors at Thebes into pharaohs who asserted authority beyond
  their district. The Intef kings expanded northward and fought the rival rulers based at Herakleopolis. Control of Abydos was especially
  important in this contest for the Nile Valley. Tombs and contemporary inscriptions help reconstruct the dynasty's succession and claims, although
  exact dates remain dependent on chronology. Mentuhotep II eventually defeated the northern kingdom and reunited Egypt around 2040 BCE. That
  victory ends this regional record and begins the Middle Kingdom, while the same dynasty continued ruling the newly unified country.
