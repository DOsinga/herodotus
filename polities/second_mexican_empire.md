description: >-
  The Second Mexican Empire was a monarchy set up in Mexico during the French intervention,
  with Mexico City as its capital. After French troops took the city in 1863, an assembly of
  conservative notables proclaimed an empire and offered the crown to Archduke Maximilian of
  Austria, who arrived in 1864 with his wife Carlota. Maximilian ruled through Mexican
  ministers and an imperial army alongside a large French force, and his government was
  recognised by several European powers. He disappointed his conservative backers by keeping
  liberal reforms such as the sale of church lands. Benito Juárez's republican government
  never left the country and fought on from the north. When France withdrew its troops under
  American pressure in 1866 and 1867, the empire collapsed, and Maximilian was captured at
  Querétaro and shot.
