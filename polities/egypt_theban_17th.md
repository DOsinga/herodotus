description: >-
  The Theban kingdom of the Seventeenth Dynasty ruled part of Upper Egypt during the Second
  Intermediate Period, approximately 1580 to 1550 BCE. Its pharaohs governed from Thebes while the
  Hyksos controlled the north and Kush was powerful to the south. Their emergence followed the loss
  and recovery of Theban independence, although the chronology and duration of outside occupation
  remain uncertain. Tombs, temple works, inscriptions, and damaged king lists illuminate a sequence of
  rulers whose order is still debated. The later kings Seqenenre Tao and Kamose fought the Hyksos,
  turning a monarchy into the base for reunification. Kamose's brother Ahmose completed that struggle
  and inaugurated the New Kingdom. The dynasty's end therefore marks an expansion of Theban rule, not
  its disappearance.
