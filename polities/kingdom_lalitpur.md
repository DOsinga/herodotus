description: >-
  The Kingdom of Lalitpur, also known as Patan, was one of the Malla kingdoms of the Kathmandu
  Valley in Nepal. It took shape after the division of the valley among the sons of Yaksha Malla in
  1482. For its first century Patan was governed largely by powerful local nobles, and a line of
  Malla kings of its own ruled from the late sixteenth century, starting a great age of building
  around the Patan Durbar Square under Siddhi Narasingha Malla. The city was known for its Buddhist
  monasteries, metalworkers and craftsmen, and it competed and allied in turn with Kantipur and
  Bhaktapur. Its last years were troubled by rival nobles who made and unmade kings. Prithvi Narayan
  Shah's Gorkha army took Lalitpur in 1768, soon after the fall of Kathmandu.
