description: >-
  The Commune of Vicenza was the self-governing city state of Vicenza in the Veneto, from
  the first consuls in the twelfth century until Padua took the city over in 1266. Vicenza
  joined the Veronese and Lombard leagues against Frederick Barbarossa and spent much of
  the next century at war with Padua and Verona over the plains and hills around it. Its
  nobles fought among themselves, and in 1236 Ezzelino da Romano, whose family came from
  the hills north of the city, made it part of his lordship with Frederick II's backing.
  After Ezzelino's fall in 1259 the commune recovered for a few years, but in 1266 it
  submitted to Padua, which governed it until Cangrande della Scala took it in 1311.
