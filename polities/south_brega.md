description: >-
  South Brega was a small kingdom north of Dublin, centred on the royal settlement of Lagore,
  or Loch Gabhair. Its rulers belonged to the Uí Chernaig branch of the southern Uí Néill,
  distinct from the rival court at Knowth in North Brega. Separate government is estimated
  from around 700, although the royal title first appears in surviving annals in 729. Family
  rivalries and wider struggles for the kingship of Brega shaped its politics. The region
  occupied a contested landscape between Mide and the growing Viking kingdom of Dublin.
  Lagore's excavated crannog provides evidence of an elite residence and worked bronze
  objects. The destruction of this centre and absorption into Mide are placed around 1075,
  though the institutional chronology remains uncertain.
