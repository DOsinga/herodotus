description: >-
  Tibes was an Indigenous ceremonial and settlement centre near modern Ponce in southern Puerto
  Rico. The chiefdom represented here spans approximately 400 to 1000, following excavated
  occupation rather than a documented royal chronology. Stone-lined ballcourts, plazas, burials,
  ceramics, and shell middens reveal a community capable of organizing spaces and ceremonies.
  They support an interpretation of local political leadership, but do not identify rulers or
  establish their succession. Changes between Igneri and later Taino material traditions raise
  questions about population movement and continuity. Tibes should therefore not represent all
  communities associated with either culture. Its large cemetery provides evidence about diet,
  health, and ritual as well as social differences. The site's archaeological importance does not
  establish continued government beyond this period.
