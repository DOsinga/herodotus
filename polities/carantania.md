description: >-
  Carantania was a Slavic principality in the eastern Alps, covering parts of present
  southern Austria and northeastern Slovenia. Its conventional beginning in 658 follows
  Samo's death, although regional Slavic leadership existed earlier. Native dukes
  governed from the Karnburg area, drawing authority from local elites and communities.
  Threats from the Avars encouraged an alliance with Bavaria around 745. Bavarian and
  later Frankish supremacy did not immediately abolish the local ruling system, but it
  brought closer political dependence and Christian missionary activity directed
  especially from Salzburg. The principality's ducal installation customs later became
  an regional tradition. After unrest in the early ninth century, Frankish rulers
  replaced native administration with appointed counts in 828. Carantania thereafter
  formed part of the Carolingian frontier system.
