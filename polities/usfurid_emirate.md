description: >-
  The Usfurid Emirate was a state in eastern Arabia, centred on the oasis of al-Hasa, that
  took power in 1253. Its rulers, named after their founder Usfur ibn Rashid, came from the
  Banu Uqayl, whose clans had earlier ruled in Iraq and Syria. They had first been allies of
  the Uyunid rulers of the region, and overthrew them after a Salghurid invasion from Fars in
  1235 had weakened Uyunid power. At its height the emirate held al-Hasa, al-Qatif and the
  Bahrain islands, with influence into central Arabia and parts of Oman. Its power rested on
  the tribes and on the date oases and Gulf trade. The Usfurids were later pushed aside by
  the Jarwanids, who in turn gave way to the Jabrids, another Uqayli family.
