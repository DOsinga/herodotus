name: Margraviate of Brandenburg
description: >-
  Brandenburg was a principality centred on the region around Berlin,
  represented here from 1250 to 1701. It developed from a frontier march
  established among Slavic communities and was governed by hereditary margraves.
  The Golden Bull of 1356 confirmed their place among the electors who chose the
  Holy Roman Emperor. After periods under Ascanian, Wittelsbach, and Luxembourg
  rulers, the Hohenzollerns secured the territory in the fifteenth century. They
  expanded their possessions, adopted the Reformation, and strengthened central
  administration. In 1618 the elector inherited the Duchy of Prussia, creating a
  personal union while the territories retained distinct institutions. Recovery
  from the Thirty Years' War supported a stronger army. In 1701 Brandenburg and
  Prussia entered the monarchy known as the Kingdom of Prussia.
