description: >-
  The Grand Duchy of Würzburg was a Napoleonic-era state in Lower Franconia, created in 1805 when
  the Peace of Pressburg gave the secularised lands of the former prince-bishopric of Würzburg to
  Ferdinand III, the Habsburg grand duke of Tuscany, who had lost his Italian state to Napoleon.
  Ferdinand ruled it first as an elector and from 1806 as grand duke, joining the Confederation of
  the Rhine and supplying troops to Napoleon's wars. He governed from the Würzburg Residence,
  keeping much of the old administration and making the city a centre of music and the arts under
  his patronage. After Napoleon's defeat, the Congress of Vienna restored Ferdinand to Tuscany,
  and Würzburg passed to the Kingdom of Bavaria in 1814.
