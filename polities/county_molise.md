description: >-
  The County of Molise was a Norman lordship in the mountains of the central southern
  Apennines, from about 1053 until Roger II of Sicily brought it under royal control in
  1140. Rodulf de Moulins, a Norman knight whose family name gave the region its name,
  took Bojano and the lands of the Lombard gastaldate there in the years of the Norman
  conquest. His successors extended their lands between the Matese and the upper Volturno,
  built castles and monasteries, and acted independently of the dukes of Apulia and the
  princes of Capua, making alliances and war on their own account. When Roger II of Sicily
  forced the mainland barons into his new kingdom, Molise became a royal county and later
  a justiciarate.
