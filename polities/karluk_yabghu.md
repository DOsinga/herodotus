description: >-
  The Karluk Yabghu State was a Turkic government in Central Asia, conventionally dated
  from 742 to 840. Its leaders bore the title yabghu and headed a confederation of Karluk
  groups. Originally involved in struggles against the Eastern Turkic rulers, the Karluks
  moved west as the Uyghurs gained predominance. Their expansion into the region of the
  western Tian Shan brought Suyab into their political orbit and displaced earlier Türgesh
  authority. Pastoral communities interacted with settled towns and trade routes
  connecting China, Iran and the steppe. Uyghur overlordship did not eliminate the Karluk
  leadership's own government. The collapse of Uyghur power in 840 marks the conventional
  transition toward the Karakhanid identity, although the dynasty's origins and the
  relationship remain debated by historians.
