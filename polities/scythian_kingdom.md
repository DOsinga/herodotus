name: "Scythian kingdom"
description: >-
  The Scythian kingdom represented here occupied the Pontic steppe north of the Black
  Sea from approximately 650 to 339 BCE. Its royal elite governed a population combining
  mobile pastoralists with farming communities farther inland. Horses, cattle, and river
  routes sustained an economy connected to Greek coastal settlements through trade and
  political dealings. Monumental burial mounds along the lower Dnieper reveal wealthy
  rulers, elaborate goldwork, and contacts across the wider ancient world. Scythian
  power did not mean every people called Scythian belonged to this kingdom. In the
  fourth century BCE, King Ateas extended influence westward toward the Danube. His
  conflict with Philip II of Macedon ended in defeat and his death in 339. Smaller
  Scythian powers continued after this kingdom's endpoint.
