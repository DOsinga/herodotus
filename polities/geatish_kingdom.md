description: >-
  The Geatish Kingdom represents the separately governed Geats of southern Scandinavia,
  inland Götaland in present Sweden. Its beginning around 500 rests on evidence for
  kingship in the age of Hygelac, whose raid against the Franks around 516 is historically
  attested. Beowulf and Norse traditions preserve royal names and conflicts, but literary
  accounts cannot supply a secure continuous list of rulers. The Geats remained distinct
  from the Swedes, with their own identity and regional institutions, while the process
  joining them into one kingdom remains debated. Götaland is a representative region
  rather than an identified capital. The working end of 970 marks gradual incorporation
  into medieval Sweden; common royal rule becomes securely visible under Olof around 995,
  not through one documented conquest.
