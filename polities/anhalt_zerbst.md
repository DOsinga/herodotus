description: >-
  Anhalt-Zerbst was a principality in Germany, represented here from the
  division of Anhalt in 1603 until its ruling line ended in 1793. Ascanian
  princes governed from Zerbst within the Holy Roman Empire, sharing a dynastic
  background with neighboring Anhalt states. The principality adopted Reformed
  religion before returning to Lutheranism in 1644. Later inheritance brought
  the distant lordship of Jever, while further family partitions created smaller
  branches. Its best-known family member was Sophie Auguste Fredericka, who
  married the Russian heir and became Catherine the Great. Prussian occupation
  during the Seven Years' War drove the prince's household into exile. After
  Frederick Augustus died without an heir, the core lands were divided among
  Anhalt-Dessau, Anhalt-Bernburg, and Anhalt-Köthen, with Dessau receiving the
  largest share.
