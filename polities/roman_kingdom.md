description: >-
  The Roman Kingdom was the earliest royal government of Rome and its surrounding territory
  beside the Tiber in central Italy. Tradition places its foundation in 753 BCE and the
  overthrow of its last king in 509 BCE. Later Roman writers described seven kings,
  beginning with Romulus and ending with Tarquinius Superbus. Their accounts associate the
  monarchy with the Senate, religious institutions, military organisation, drainage works,
  and the city's growing public spaces. Archaeology supports Rome's development from hill
  settlements into a substantial urban centre, but cannot confirm the traditional sequence
  of reigns in detail. Kings exercised military, judicial, and religious authority, with
  aristocratic advisers and assemblies playing supporting roles. The expulsion of Tarquinius
  became the founding story of the Roman Republic.
