description: >-
  The Judicate of Gallura was the smallest of the four kingdoms of medieval Sardinia and lay
  in the northeast of the island, with its main town at Civita, the later Terranova and modern
  Olbia. Its judges ruled ten districts through curators and appear in the records late in
  the eleventh century. Lying closest to Tuscany, Gallura was the most loyal of the judicates
  to Pisa, and from 1207 it passed by marriage to the Pisan Visconti family, who ruled it from
  Pisa as well as from the island. Nino Visconti, the last judge, was a leader of the Guelphs
  in Pisa and a friend of Dante. After he was driven from Pisa in 1288, the Pisan commune took
  Gallura for itself.
