description: >-
  The Latin League was a confederation of communities in Latium, central Italy, whose recorded
  period here begins around 500 BCE. Their shared language, religious traditions, and military
  cooperation linked cities such as Lavinium, Tusculum, Aricia, and Tibur without making them one
  centralized kingdom. Its relationship with Rome changed from rivalry to alliance and increasing
  Roman dominance. According to Roman tradition, defeat at Lake Regillus led to the treaty of 493
  BCE, providing cooperation in war and a division of spoils. Joint colonies and campaigns extended
  the allies' influence, but unequal power brought renewed tensions. The Latin War ended in 338 BCE
  with Rome victorious. Rome dissolved the league and dealt separately with its former members,
  granting different rights and obligations.
