description: >-
  Tairona is the name for the cultural tradition of the Sierra Nevada de Santa Marta in
  northern Colombia, where the mountains rise steeply from the Caribbean coast. Archaeology
  shows dense settlement from about 200 BC, and the tradition took its definite form by the
  tenth and eleventh centuries AD. Its people built towns of stone terraces, stairways and
  paved paths, such as Ciudad Perdida and Pueblito, and were skilled goldsmiths. The coast,
  slopes and high valleys formed one system of exchange between farmers, fishers and
  craftsmen. Spanish accounts describe several separate chiefdoms rather than one state.
  Contact after the founding of Santa Marta in 1525 was at first tolerated, but conflict
  grew, and by 1600 survivors had withdrawn into the high Sierra, where their descendants
  include the Kogi.
