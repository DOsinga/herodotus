description: >-
  The Miskito Kingdom was a monarchy of the Miskito people on the Caribbean coast of
  present-day Nicaragua and Honduras. Allied with English buccaneers and the colony of
  Jamaica, the Miskito obtained firearms in the seventeenth century and raided Spanish
  settlements and neighbouring peoples for captives, from Yucatán to Costa Rica. Their
  kings, together with a governor and a general who ruled other parts of the coast, were
  crowned with English support. In 1740 they accepted British sovereignty, and a
  superintendent at Black River directed the coast. After the Convention of London, Britain
  withdrew its settlers in 1787, and the kings governed alone until Britain declared a
  protectorate again in 1844. The Treaty of Managua of 1860 turned the kingdom into the
  Mosquito Reserve within Nicaragua.
