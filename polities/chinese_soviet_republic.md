description: >-
  The Chinese Soviet Republic was a Communist revolutionary state within China from 1931 to
  1937. Its main centre was Ruijin in Jiangxi, although it claimed authority over several
  separated territories held by Communist forces. Mao Zedong headed both the state and its
  government, supported by the Red Army and local soviet institutions. The administration
  redistributed land, developed taxation and currency systems, and organised peasants during
  the civil war against the Nationalist government. Nationalist encirclement campaigns
  forced the leadership to abandon Jiangxi in 1934 and undertake the Long March. Survivors
  established a new base in northwestern China. Negotiations for resistance against Japan
  led to its formal dissolution in 1937, with the Communist controlled Shaan-Gan-Ning Border
  Region continuing under a different framework.
