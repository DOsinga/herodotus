  Slovenia is a parliamentary republic in Central Europe, independent since 1991, with its capital
  at Ljubljana. Its territory links the Alps, the northern Adriatic, and the western Pannonian
  plain, bordering Italy, Austria, Hungary, and Croatia. Previously a constituent republic of
  socialist Yugoslavia, Slovenia developed a strong independence movement amid the federation's
  political and economic crisis. A referendum in 1990 backed sovereignty, followed by a declaration
  of independence and the comparatively brief Ten-Day War in 1991. The new constitution established
  elected institutions, with government led by a prime minister responsible to parliament. Slovenia
  joined the European Union and NATO in 2004 and adopted the euro in 2007. Its independent state
  continues despite changing governments and the transition to a market economy.
