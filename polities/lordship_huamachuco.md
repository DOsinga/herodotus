description: >-
  The Lordship of Huamachuco was a Culli-speaking highland polity of the Late Intermediate Period
  in the northern Andes of Peru, in what is now La Libertad. Colonial chroniclers describe the
  region as united under a single political entity before the Inca conquest. Its chief deity was
  Catequil, a thunder god and oracle whose shrine at Namanchugo, at the foot of Cerro Icchal, drew
  worshippers from the Huamachuco and the neighbouring Conchucos. Archaeologists call this period
  the Tuscan phase, after the decline of the earlier centre at Marcahuamachuco and the Wari
  presence at Viracochapampa. The Inca conquered Huamachuco in the fifteenth century and took
  Catequil into their pantheon, though Atahualpa later destroyed the shrine when the oracle went
  against him.
