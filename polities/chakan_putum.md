description: >-
  Chakán Putum was a Maya province on the southwestern coast of the Yucatán Peninsula, with
  its capital at the port that the Spanish called Champotón. The city was a major trading
  port with thousands of buildings, and its people traded by canoe along the Gulf coast
  towards Tabasco. It was ruled by a lord known to the Spanish as Moch Couoh. In 1517 his
  warriors defeated the expedition of Francisco Hernández de Córdoba, who died of his
  wounds, and the bay became known as the Bay of the Evil Fight. The town also resisted Juan
  de Grijalva in 1518. Francisco de Montejo's forces made Champotón their base in the late
  1530s, and from there they conquered the rest of the peninsula.
