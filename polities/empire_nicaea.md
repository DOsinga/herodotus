description: >-
  The Empire of Nicaea was a Byzantine successor state in western Anatolia from 1204 to
  1261. It formed after the Fourth Crusade captured Constantinople and established a
  Latin empire there. Theodore Laskaris organised a new imperial court, and Nicaea
  became the seat of a restored Orthodox patriarchate, while emperors also governed from
  Nymphaion. Its rulers claimed to continue Roman imperial authority, competing with
  other Greek successor courts as well as Latin and Turkish neighbours. John III
  Vatatzes strengthened the state's economy and extended its control into the Balkans.
  Michael VIII Palaiologos gained power late in its history. In 1261 his forces
  recovered Constantinople, allowing the court to return and continue as the restored
  Byzantine Empire rather than an exile state.
