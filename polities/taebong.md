description: >-
  Taebong was Gung Ye's kingdom in central and northern Korea from 901 to 918, during the
  Later Three Kingdoms period. It arose as Silla's authority weakened and military leaders
  gained territory. Initially called Later Goguryeo, the state became Majin in 904 and
  Taebong in 911. Its court moved from Songak to Cheorwon in 905. Gung Ye governed as king
  and promoted Buddhism, eventually claiming a sacred identity that reinforced his personal
  authority. Accounts describe increasingly harsh punishments and alienation among his
  followers. His commanders overthrew him in 918 and raised Wang Geon to the throne. Wang
  established Goryeo, which inherited much of Taebong's territory, personnel and ceremonial
  practices. The earlier names belong to this same short-lived kingdom, not separate states.
