description: >-
  Former Yan was a northern Chinese monarchy ruled by the Murong branch of the Xianbei from 337 to 370. Murong Huang founded the state from a strong northeastern power base, initially retaining recognition from the Eastern Jin court. His armies defeated neighbouring groups and attacked Goguryeo and Buyeo. Under Murong Jun, Yan exploited Later Zhao's collapse, defeated Ran Wei, and expanded across the North China Plain. Its rulers then claimed imperial status, and Ye became the capital in 357 after earlier northern seats. Chinese officials and institutions served alongside the ruling military elite. Effective leadership under Murong Ke gave way to internal court rivalry and fiscal weakness. Former Qin conquered the kingdom in 370 and captured its ruler, Murong Wei.
