description: >-
  The Sultanate of Agadez, also called the Sultanate of Aïr, is a Tuareg monarchy in the Aïr
  Mountains on the southern edge of the Sahara in present-day Niger. Its traditions place the
  first sultan at Tadaliza in 1405, and the seat moved to Agadez around the middle of the
  fifteenth century. The sultan, usually chosen from a family of mixed Tuareg and Hausa
  descent, mediated between the Tuareg confederations, collected tolls on the caravans between
  the Hausa cities and North Africa, and dealt with the salt trade of Bilma. Songhai forces
  under Askia Muhammad took Agadez in 1500 and demanded tribute, but the sultans continued to
  rule. French troops occupied Agadez in 1906, after which the sultanate survived as a
  traditional office within the colony.
