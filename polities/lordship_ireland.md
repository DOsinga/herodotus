description: >-
  The Lordship of Ireland was the Anglo-Norman political order established in 1177 after the
  invasion of Ireland. Henry II assigned the lordship to his son John; when John became England's
  king, the titles remained linked. Government operated from Dublin through royal
  representatives, councils, courts, and a parliament, alongside powerful landed families. Its
  claim extended across Ireland, but actual control never covered the whole island. Gaelic
  kingdoms and lordships continued governing elsewhere, while many Anglo-Norman families adopted
  local customs. Scottish invasion, famine, plague, and renewed Gaelic power weakened the
  lordship during the fourteenth century. English authority became concentrated around the Pale
  and dependent on regional magnates. Henry VIII's assumption of the title King of Ireland in
  1542 inaugurated its successor kingdom.
