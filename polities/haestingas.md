description: >-
  The Haestingas were a distinct local political community around Hastings in southeastern England,
  between the marshes near Pevensey and the borderlands of Kent. Their separate political history is
  poorly documented; this record estimates its beginning around 500 and follows a later chronicle's
  conquest date of 771. Marshes and the Weald helped distinguish the district from neighboring South
  Saxon territory. Charters naming a second king, Watt, have prompted suggestions that he ruled the
  Haestingas, but this identification is not established. Neither their origins nor the details of
  their government are securely known. Simeon of Durham later reported that Offa of Mercia defeated
  them. Their name and local identity survived absorption, appearing again in accounts of Danish
  attacks during the eleventh century.
