description: >-
  Junagadh State was a kingdom in southern Saurashtra, ruled from the old hill town of Junagadh
  below Mount Girnar by the Babi nawabs, a Pashtun family. Mohammad Sher Khan Babi held Junagadh
  as an officer of the Mughal government of Gujarat and declared himself independent around 1730,
  as Maratha armies broke Mughal power in the province. His successors conquered a large share of
  southern Saurashtra and fought the Gaekwads, the Jadeja rajas and rebellious Koli chiefs, while
  paying tribute to the Marathas. They kept their own court, army and revenue. After the Second
  Anglo-Maratha War the nawab accepted British protection in 1807, and Junagadh became a princely
  state of the Kathiawar Agency. Its accession to Pakistan in 1947 led to Indian intervention.
