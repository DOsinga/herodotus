description: >-
  Maguana was a Taíno chiefdom in the centre of Hispaniola, around the San Juan valley and
  the gold-bearing mountains of the Cibao. Its cacique Caonabo was the most powerful
  opponent of the Spanish among the island's rulers, and his wife Anacaona was the sister of
  Behechío of Jaragua. The great stone plaza now called the Corral de los Indios near San
  Juan de la Maguana is linked to his seat. Caonabo's warriors destroyed Columbus's fort of
  La Navidad in 1493 and besieged the fort of Santo Tomás in 1494. Alonso de Ojeda captured
  him by a trick in 1495, and he died at sea on his way to Spain. His brother Manicaotex
  continued to resist until the Spanish subdued the chiefdom.
