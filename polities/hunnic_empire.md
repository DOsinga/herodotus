name: "Hunnic Empire"
description: >-
  The Hunnic Empire grew from the arrival of Hunnic groups north of the Black Sea around 370
  into a European confederation. Its early unity is uncertain; a single royal government
  becomes clear under Rua and Attila during the fifth century. Royal camps on the Hungarian
  Plain anchored a mobile court that commanded Huns and many subject peoples. Mounted warfare,
  tribute, diplomacy, and service in Roman armies supported its power. Attila and his brother
  Bleda initially shared rule before Attila became sole ruler. Campaigns devastated the
  Balkans and reached Gaul and Italy. After Attila died in 453, succession struggles and
  rebellions shattered the confederation. Smaller Hunnic forces survived, but Dengizich's
  death in 469 marks the final endpoint used for this record.
