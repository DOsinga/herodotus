description: >-
  The Kingdom of Brittany developed on the Armorican peninsula in northwestern France
  after Nominoe defeated Charles the Bald at Ballon in 845. His son Erispoe secured
  royal recognition following another victory in 851, and subsequent kings expanded
  Breton authority into neighbouring Frankish lands. Government rested on regional
  nobles as well as the royal court. Salomon's assassination brought succession
  struggles, while Viking attacks repeatedly disrupted settlements and monasteries. Alan
  the Great restored some unity, but after his death native government weakened. Viking
  conquest removed the court by 914, creating a break in this record. Alan Twistedbeard
  returned to recover Brittany from 936. His victory in 939 established renewed Breton
  government under a ducal title, beginning the separate Duchy of Brittany record.
