description: >-
  Argentina began with the May Revolution of 1810 in Buenos Aires and declared independence
  in 1816 as the United Provinces of the Río de la Plata. Buenos Aires became its capital,
  but disputes between that port and the provinces shaped decades of civil war and
  struggles over federal government. The republic expanded across the Pampas and
  Patagonia, often through campaigns that displaced Indigenous peoples. Agriculture,
  exports, and large immigrant communities helped make it a major regional economy.
  Presidential and federal institutions endured repeated interruptions by military
  governments during the twentieth century. The dictatorship that began in 1976 carried
  out repression and disappeared thousands of people. Civilian democracy returned in
  1983 after the Falklands War, while recurring economic crises continued to shape
  public life.
