description: >-
  Malawi is a landlocked state in southeastern Africa, stretching along the western and
  southern shores of Lake Malawi. It became independent in 1964 when British Nyasaland left
  colonial rule after the dissolution of the Federation of Rhodesia and Nyasaland. Hastings
  Banda led the independence government and became president when Malawi adopted a
  republican constitution in 1966. His Malawi Congress Party established a one party system,
  and he later assumed a life presidency. A referendum in 1993 opened the way to multiparty
  politics; elections in 1994 removed Banda from office. The country has since continued as
  a presidential republic, with Lilongwe as its capital. Agriculture, especially smallholder farming and
  export crops, remains central to its economy and rural society throughout Malawi.
