description: >-
  Bavaria-Munich was a Wittelsbach duchy in southern Germany from 1392 to 1505. It arose when members of the ruling family partitioned Bavaria into separate governments, leaving John II with the Munich share. The dukes governed from Munich and repeatedly competed with relatives ruling Ingolstadt, Landshut and Straubing. Joint rule and family disputes complicated succession, while towns and territorial estates helped shape domestic politics. The duchy gained lands when the Straubing line ended and later recovered a separated Dachau share. Albert IV ultimately challenged the disputed inheritance of Bavaria-Landshut, drawing neighbouring powers into a succession war. The settlement of 1505 reunited most Bavarian lands under his government. Bavaria-Munich ended as a separate polity and continued within the restored Duchy of Bavaria.
