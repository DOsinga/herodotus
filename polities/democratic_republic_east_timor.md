description: >-
  The Democratic Republic of East Timor was proclaimed by FRETILIN on 28 November 1975 after
  Portugal's withdrawal and a civil war. It claimed the former Portuguese colony on eastern Timor and
  nearby territories, with Dili as its capital. Francisco Xavier do Amaral became president and
  Nicolau Lobato prime minister under a declared republican government. Indonesia invaded on 7
  December, quickly taking Dili, but FRETILIN continued governing support bases in the interior. These
  areas combined armed resistance with efforts to organize civilian life while enduring bombardment,
  displacement, and hunger. The fall of the Mount Matebian base in late 1978 ended the territorial
  government represented here. Resistance and the independence claim survived, eventually contributing
  to the emergence of independent Timor-Leste in 2002.
