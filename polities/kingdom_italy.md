description: >-
  The Kingdom of Italy was proclaimed in 1861 when Victor Emmanuel II of Sardinia became king
  of a newly united state. The Savoy monarchy inherited a parliamentary constitution,
  although voting rights initially covered only a small share of the population. Turin served
  as capital before Florence and then Rome, which became the seat of government in 1871. The
  kingdom completed further territorial gains through wars and built an overseas colonial
  empire. After the First World War, social conflict and political violence helped Benito
  Mussolini establish a Fascist dictatorship beneath the surviving crown. Defeat in the
  Second World War discredited both the regime and monarchy. A referendum in 1946 abolished
  the kingship and created the modern democratic parliamentary Republic of Italy.
