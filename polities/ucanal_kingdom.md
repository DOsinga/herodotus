description: >-
  Kanwitznal was a Maya kingdom centred at Ucanal on the Mopan River in present Guatemala. Its governing court is represented here from about 600 to 950, with both endpoints estimated. Kings maintained connections with Tikal and Caracol, while Naranjo became a powerful rival. Attacks led by Lady Six Sky in 693 were followed by the capture of Ucanal's ruler in 695. Naranjo imposed overlordship, but a local court continued. Another defeat by Caracol in 800 preceded changes among the ruling elites. During the ninth century, Kanwitznal regained influence and installed a ruler at Seibal. Temples, palaces, ballcourts, water channels, and carved monuments reveal an enduring urban centre whose political institutions survived well into the Terminal Classic period before eventually disappearing.
