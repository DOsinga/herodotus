description: >-
  The Free Imperial City of Besançon was a self-governing city of the Holy Roman Empire,
  recorded here from 1250 until 1654. Besançon was the seat of an archbishop, and from 1184 it
  held imperial privileges that set it apart from the surrounding County of Burgundy. After a
  century of conflict with the archbishops, the emperor confirmed the citizens' self-
  government in 1290, and governors elected by the city's districts ran its affairs. As an
  enclave inside the Franche-Comté, the city depended on whoever held the surrounding county,
  first the dukes of Burgundy and then the Habsburgs. The Peace of Westphalia allowed the
  emperor to cede it, and in 1654 it passed to Spain in exchange for Frankenthal, keeping some
  privileges until France took it in 1674.
