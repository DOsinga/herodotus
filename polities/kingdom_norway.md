description: >-
  Norway occupies the western Scandinavian Peninsula, with Oslo as its capital. This
  record begins with the constitution of 1814, when separation from Denmark was followed
  by a union with Sweden. Norway kept its constitution, parliament and domestic
  government despite sharing a monarch. The union ended peacefully in 1905, and Haakon
  VII became king. Parliamentary government developed within a constitutional monarchy.
  German invasion in 1940 interrupted government at home; the king and cabinet continued
  in exile until liberation in 1945. Postwar Norway joined NATO and developed an welfare
  system. Fishing, shipping and hydroelectric power were important before offshore
  petroleum transformed its economy. State management of oil income helped fund public
  services and savings, while the monarchy and elected parliament continued together.
