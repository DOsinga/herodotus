description: >-
  The Great Horde was a khanate centred on Sarai along the lower Volga, surviving here
  from 1466 to 1502. It represented the remaining core of the Golden Horde after rival
  khanates separated. Its hereditary khans claimed the authority of the earlier Mongol
  rulers, but competing dynasties and tribal leaders limited their power. Crimea became
  a enemy, while relations with Lithuania offered possible support against Moscow. Khan
  Ahmed tried to restore Moscow's submission in 1480, but withdrew after the
  confrontation on the Ugra River. His death the following year deepened struggles among
  his successors. The Horde continued campaigning without recovering its former
  position. In 1502, the Crimean khan Mengli Giray defeated its forces and sacked Sarai,
  ending the Great Horde's independence.
