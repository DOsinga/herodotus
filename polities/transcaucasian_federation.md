name: Transcaucasian Democratic Federative Republic
description: >-
  The Transcaucasian Democratic Federative Republic briefly united much of present Georgia, Armenia and Azerbaijan in April
  and May 1918. Based in Tiflis, it was a parliamentary federation governed through the Transcaucasian Seim
  and a cabinet led by Akaki Chkhenkeli. Regional institutions had already rejected Bolshevik authority after the
  Russian Revolution and administered the southern Caucasus through the Transcaucasian Commissariat. Ottoman advances and the territorial
  concessions of Brest-Litovsk prompted a declaration of independence on 22 April. The federation sought peace with
  the Ottoman Empire, but its Georgian, Armenian and Azerbaijani leaders had different security needs and foreign
  alliances. Renewed fighting exposed these divisions. Georgia declared independence on 26 May; Armenia and Azerbaijan followed,
  dissolving the federation after more than one month.
