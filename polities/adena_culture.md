description: >-
  Adena culture names related Early Woodland communities in the Ohio Valley and neighbouring parts
  of eastern North America. The dates here, roughly 500 BCE to 100 CE, are estimates rather than
  the lifespan of a single government. Archaeologists recognise Adena through burial mounds,
  pottery, stone tools and shared ceremonial practices. Communities combined hunting and gathering
  with cultivation of native plants and lived in small settlements. Elaborate burials and objects
  made from distant materials reveal differences in status and exchange networks. Mounds were
  sometimes built over burned mortuary structures through repeated ceremonies. The Adena mound
  near Chillicothe gives the culture its name. These traditions overlapped with and contributed to
  later Hopewell developments, without demonstrating one united state.
