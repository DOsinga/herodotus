description: >-
  Mannaea was a kingdom south and east of Lake Urmia in northwestern Iran, known chiefly
  through Assyrian and Urartian records. Its existence is represented here approximately
  from 850 to 616 BCE, with Izirtu as the royal centre, although the capital's location
  remains uncertain. Kings governed a region of settlements and competing local powers
  between larger neighbours. Assyria and Urartu repeatedly fought for influence there, and
  Mannaean rulers sought alliances while facing rebellions and external attacks. The
  population probably included several linguistic and cultural groups rather than one
  uniform people. Archaeological finds at Qalaichi include decorated bricks and an Aramaic
  inscription associated with Mannaean elites. Scythian incursions and Assyrian warfare
  weakened the kingdom before Median expansion brought it to an end.
