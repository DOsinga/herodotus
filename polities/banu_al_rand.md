description: >-
  The Banu al-Rand were a Berber dynasty that ruled Gafsa and the oases of the Djerid in
  southern Tunisia, recorded here from 1053 to 1159. Abdullah ibn al-Rand was the Zirid
  governor of Gafsa when the Banu Hilal invaded Ifriqiya; he protected the town by paying
  tribute to the Bedouins and declared himself independent in 1053. Tozeur, Nefta, Nefzaoua
  and the other Djerid oases accepted his rule. His son Abu Umar al-Mu'tazz extended the
  state, but went blind and left the government to his grandson Yahya ibn Tamim. The dynasty
  attracted poets and scholars and kept the caravan towns prosperous. In 1159 Yahya
  surrendered Gafsa to the Almohad caliph Abd al-Mu'min.
