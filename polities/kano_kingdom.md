description: >-
  The Kingdom of Kano was a Hausa monarchy centred on the city of Kano in present-day northern
  Nigeria. Its traditional beginning around 999 comes from the Kano Chronicle's account of
  Bagauda. Early rulers used Sheme before establishing Kano as their main seat, and later
  kings built the first city walls around the hill of Dala. The kingdom grew around farming,
  iron working, crafts and commerce linking the savanna with Saharan routes. The kings, or
  sarki, contended with the priests of an older local cult and with neighbouring Hausa towns.
  Muslim traders and scholars reached the city in the fourteenth century. This record ends
  around 1350, when the Islamic phase of the monarchy begins, recorded here as the Sultanate
  of Kano.
