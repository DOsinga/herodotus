description: >-
  The Mongol Empire began in 1206 when an assembly of steppe leaders
  proclaimed Temüjin as Genghis Khan. From Mongolia, he and his successors conquered
  northern China, Central Asia, Iran, and lands reaching into eastern Europe. The
  great khan ruled through relatives, commanders, and officials drawn from many conquered
  peoples, with Karakorum becoming the centre under Ögedei. Mobile cavalry, coordinated campaigns,
  and severe violence made its expansion rapid. Imperial communications and protection for
  merchants encouraged exchanges across Eurasia. Succession struggles weakened unity, especially after Möngke's
  death in 1259, when Kublai and Ariq Böke fought for power. This
  record ends in 1271 with Kublai's proclamation of the Yuan dynasty, while
  western Mongol khanates increasingly governed their own lands and pursued separate interests.
