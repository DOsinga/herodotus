description: >-
  The Restored Hanthawaddy Kingdom ruled Lower Burma from Pegu between 1740 and 1757. It began when
  a rebellion overthrew the governor appointed by the Toungoo court at Ava. Mon leaders rallied a
  mixed population and revived the name of an earlier southern kingdom. Its first king, Smim Htaw
  Buddhaketi, was followed by Binnya Dala in 1747. French support helped strengthen the monarchy as
  its armies advanced north and captured Ava in 1752, ending Toungoo rule. Victory proved
  short-lived: Alaungpaya organized armed resistance in Upper Burma and founded the Konbaung
  dynasty. Hanthawaddy failed to crush his movement, then lost the delta and the French-supported
  port of Thanlyin. Pegu fell in 1757, and reprisals drove many Mon people to neighboring Siam
  afterward.
