description: >-
  The Ifranid Emirate of Tlemcen was a Kharijite state of the Banu Ifran, a Zenata Berber
  tribe, in what is now northwestern Algeria. During the Berber revolts against Arab rule the
  tribe proclaimed its leader Abu Qurra caliph, and he made Tlemcen, on the site of the Roman
  town of Pomaria, the seat of a Sufri state around 765. Little is known about how it was
  governed, but it was a serious military power. Between 767 and 776 Abu Qurra fought the
  Abbasid governors of Ifriqiya, reaching Tobna and besieging Kairouan. Sufri teaching did not
  allow him to found a dynasty. He later allied with the Maghrawa and recognised Idris I, who
  took over Tlemcen around 790 and joined it to the Idrisid realm.
