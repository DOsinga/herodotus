description: >-
  The Kingdom of Prussia began in 1701 when the Hohenzollern ruler of Brandenburg and Prussia assumed a royal title. Governed
  mainly from Berlin, it grew from scattered territories into a major European power. A disciplined army and central administration supported
  expansion, especially Frederick II's conquest of Silesia and participation in Poland's partitions. Defeat by Napoleon in 1806 prompted military and
  administrative reforms, followed by recovery and territorial gains at Vienna. The monarchy later adopted a constitution, but the crown and
  landed elites retained influence. Under Wilhelm I and Otto von Bismarck, Prussia defeated Austria and led northern German unification. Its
  separate foreign and military authority ended with the North German Confederation in 1867, though its monarchy survived within federal Germany.
