description: >-
  The Nandivardhana kingdom was the senior Vakataka branch in central India, represented
  approximately from 330 to 480. Its rulers governed from Nandivardhana near modern Nagardhan and
  later Pravarapura, rather than sharing one court with the separate Vatsagulma branch. Rudrasena
  I inherited this line after Pravarasena I's death. Marriage connected the dynasty to the Guptas,
  and Queen Prabhavatigupta acted as regent for her sons after Rudrasena II died. Copperplate land
  grants document royal authority, religious patronage and local administration. Pravarasena II
  also supported literature and established Pravarapura as a capital. The last known ruler,
  Prithivishena II, restored the kingdom after difficulties. Its end around 480 conventionally
  marks absorption by Harishena of Vatsagulma, although the precise circumstances remain uncertain
  in surviving evidence.
