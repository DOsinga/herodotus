description: >-
  The Lordship of Argos and Nauplia was a crusader lordship in the northeastern
  Peloponnese that survived the Catalan conquest of Athens and was held by the Brienne and
  Enghien families from 1311 until Venice bought it in 1388. The de la Roche dukes of
  Athens had held the two towns as a fief of Achaea, and when the Catalan Company killed
  Duke Walter of Brienne at Halmyros in 1311, his widow and heirs kept them. Walter VI of
  Brienne and then the Enghien lords governed through bailiffs, while their family lived
  in the west. Maria of Enghien, the last heir, sold the lordship to Venice in 1388, but
  Theodore Palaiologos of the Morea seized Argos before the Venetians arrived.
