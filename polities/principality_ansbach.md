description: >-
  The Principality of Ansbach, or Brandenburg-Ansbach, was a Hohenzollern state in Middle
  Franconia, west and south of Nuremberg. It grew out of the lands of the burgraves of Nuremberg,
  which were divided in 1398 into a lowland share around Ansbach and an upland share around
  Kulmbach. When Burgrave Frederick VI became Elector of Brandenburg in 1415, his Franconian
  descendants took the title of margrave, and the principality was often ruled by younger sons or
  cadet lines of the Brandenburg house. The margraves introduced the Reformation early and built a
  baroque residence at Ansbach. Their long feud with the city of Nuremberg shaped the politics of
  Franconia. The last margrave, Charles Alexander, also ruled Bayreuth and sold both lands to
  Prussia in 1791.
