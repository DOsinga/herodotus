description: >-
  The Kachchhapaghatas were a Rajput dynasty who ruled Gwalior and the surrounding country
  in northern Madhya Pradesh. Vajradaman took the Gwalior fort from the Pratiharas in the
  late tenth century as a vassal of the Chandela kings. After the death of the Chandela king
  Vidyadhara around 1035, and under the strain of Ghaznavid raids, the family threw off
  Chandela overlordship and ruled in their own right. Branches governed from Gwalior,
  Dubkund and Narwar. The Gwalior kings built temples such as the Sas-Bahu temple of
  Mahipala, and inscriptions and coins attest their rule into the twelfth century, when
  Parihara chiefs held the fort for them. Qutb al-Din Aibak took Gwalior from Sulakshanapala
  in 1196.
