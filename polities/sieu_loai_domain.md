description: >-
  Siêu Loại was the domain governed by Lý Khuê in northern Vietnam during the struggle known as
  the Period of the Twelve Warlords. Centred in the area of modern Thuận Thành district in Bắc
  Ninh province, it appears here from an estimated 965 until 968. Lý Khuê used the title Lý Lãng
  Công and held his territory as a military ruler amid the fragmentation of central authority.
  The account identifies his base and defeat but offers little detail about his court or
  administrative institutions. In 968, Lưu Cơ, a general serving Đinh Bộ Lĩnh, defeated him
  during the campaigns that reunified the country. This local government was absorbed into the
  new Đinh state, rather than surviving as a separate principality.
