description: >-
  Kandy was a monarchy in Sri Lanka's central highlands, recorded here from 1469 until British
  absorption in 1815. It developed from Kotte's weakening authority and became the island's
  principal independent kingdom as European powers occupied the coasts. Kings governed through
  court officials and provincial chiefs, drawing support from Buddhist institutions and custody of
  the sacred Tooth Relic. Mountain passes, forests and ambush tactics helped Kandyan forces resist
  Portuguese and Dutch invasions. Sitawaka held it from 1582 until Vimaladharmasuriya I restored
  independence in 1591. From 1739 the kings belonged to the Nayak dynasty, with South Indian
  family connections. Disputes between the last king and powerful nobles aided the British
  advance. The Kandyan Convention deposed the monarch in 1815 and transferred sovereignty to
  Britain.
