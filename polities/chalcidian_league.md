description: >-
  The Chalcidian League was a federation of Greek cities on the Halkidiki peninsula in northern Greece, centred on Olynthus from 432 to 348 BCE. It emerged when communities rebelled against Athenian control and concentrated population at Olynthus. Member cities shared political and military institutions, and the league issued coinage bearing a common Chalcidian identity. Its early organisation remains debated. During the fourth century, expansion brought conflict with neighbouring cities, Sparta, and Macedonia. Some members complained that participation had been imposed rather than freely chosen. Although Spartan intervention checked its power, the federation survived and later allied with Athens against Philip II. In 348 BCE, Philip captured and destroyed Olynthus, ending the league and bringing the peninsula under Macedonian royal control.
