description: >-
  Urartu was a kingdom of the Armenian Highlands, centered on Lake Van, from about 860
  to 590 BCE. Its kings governed from Tushpa and expanded across parts of present
  Turkey, Armenia, and northwestern Iran. Fortresses, irrigation canals, storehouses,
  and cuneiform inscriptions reveal a state able to organize agriculture, collect
  tribute, and move populations. The kings promoted the god Haldi and built temples
  alongside their fortified centers. Urartu repeatedly fought Assyria, its southern
  rival, while rulers such as Menua and Argishti I enlarged its territory and founded
  new cities. Assyrian attacks and pressure from northern peoples weakened the kingdom.
  Its final collapse remains poorly documented; this record uses an estimated Median
  conquest around 590 BCE rather than a precisely established date.
