description: >-
  The Archontate of Sardinia was the self-governing island state that grew out of
  Byzantine Sardinia in the ninth and tenth centuries, before it divided into the four
  judicates around 1000. Cut off from Constantinople by the Arab conquests of Africa and
  Sicily, the island's governor, the iudex provinciae or archon residing at Caralis, took
  over its defence and government. Sardinians beat off Arab attacks in the early ninth
  century, and in 851 Pope Leo IV asked the judge of Sardinia for help in defending Rome.
  The archons kept Byzantine titles and Greek in their documents. Lieutenants from the
  Lacon-Gunale family governed districts for them, and their shares became the judicates
  of Cagliari, Arborea, Torres and Gallura.
