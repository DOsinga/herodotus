description: >-
  Medang was a Hindu and Buddhist kingdom in Java, recorded from the eighth century until its destruction in the early eleventh. The Canggal inscription of 732 names Sanjaya, providing a conventional starting point rather than a precise foundation date. Kings governed through court officials and local lords, drawing wealth from rice agriculture and later expanding maritime trade networks. Their religious patronage produced Borobudur, Sewu and Prambanan, among the greatest monuments of early Java. In 929, Mpu Sindok transferred the royal centre from Central to East Java and founded a new ruling line. Dharmawangsa later challenged Srivijaya. An attack on his palace in 1016 or 1017 killed the king and broke the kingdom apart, leaving Airlangga to establish a successor state.
