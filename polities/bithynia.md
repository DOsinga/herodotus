description: >-
  Bithynia was a kingdom in northwestern Anatolia, between the Black Sea and the
  Sea of Marmara, from 297 to 74 BCE. Zipoetes adopted the royal title after
  maintaining local power against larger neighbouring kingdoms. His successors
  developed a hereditary monarchy within the Hellenistic world, founding cities
  and issuing Greek coinage while retaining a regional identity. Nicomedes I
  established Nicomedia, which became the royal capital. The kingdom competed with
  Pergamon and Pontus, drawing Rome increasingly into its disputes. In its final
  decades, Mithridates VI of Pontus repeatedly displaced Nicomedes IV, who
  recovered his throne with Roman support. When Nicomedes died in 74 BCE, he left
  Bithynia to Rome. Annexation provoked renewed war before Roman provincial
  administration became established across the kingdom.
