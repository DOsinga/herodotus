description: >-
  Gojoseon was an early kingdom in northern Korea and neighbouring parts of Manchuria,
  represented here from about 400 BCE to 108 BCE. That starting date estimates the
  historically attested state rather than accepting the legendary founding by Dangun in 2333
  BCE. Its extent and early development remain debated, and later traditions mix history
  with origin stories. Kings governed a realm linked to bronze and iron cultures and
  increasingly involved with Chinese frontier states. Around 194 BCE, Wiman displaced King
  Jun and established a new ruling line. Wanggeom-seong, associated with modern Pyongyang,
  became its royal centre. The kingdom controlled regional trade and relations with
  neighbouring peoples. Han armies conquered it in 108 BCE and replaced its monarchy with
  commanderies administering territory.
