description: >-
  Arwad was a Phoenician island kingdom off the coast of present Syria, near modern Tartus. Its
  independent governing life is estimated here from about 1200 to 332 BCE. The small island
  supported a densely built city whose ships, sailors, and mainland connections gave it influence
  beyond its shores. Local kings ruled while navigating the demands of larger Assyrian,
  Babylonian, and Persian powers. Tribute and naval service did not always prevent them from
  making their own decisions. Under Persian rule, Arwad joined Tyre and Sidon in a confederation
  with a common council at Tripolis. When Alexander invaded in 332 BCE, its royal family submitted
  and provided ships for his siege of Tyre. It was an autonomous city again from 259 BCE until 64
  BCE.
