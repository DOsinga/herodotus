description: >-
  The medieval Kingdom of Serbia was a Balkan monarchy ruled by the Nemanjić family from 1217 to 1346. It grew from the earlier Serbian grand principality when Stefan received a royal crown, becoming known as Stefan the First-Crowned. Ras was an early political centre, although later rulers also used other capitals. In 1219, Stefan's brother Sava established a self-governing Serbian Orthodox archbishopric, linking royal authority with a church organisation. Kings founded monasteries, promoted mining and trade, and negotiated with Hungary, Byzantium and neighbouring states. Dynastic quarrels disrupted succession. Under Milutin and then Stefan Dušan, territorial expansion carried Serbian power deeper into formerly Byzantine lands. Dušan's imperial coronation in 1346 transformed the kingdom into the Serbian Empire, now recorded separately here.
