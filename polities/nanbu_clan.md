description: >-
  The Nanbu were a warrior house that governed much of northernmost Honshu, in the north of
  Mutsu province, from the Ōnin War in 1467 until they submitted to Toyotomi Hideyoshi in 1590.
  Descended from the Minamoto of Kai, they received horse-ranching lands in the far northeast
  after the conquest of Hiraizumi in 1189 and moved their seat there in the fourteenth century.
  Their wealth came from warhorses bred on fortified ranches numbered Ichinohe to Kunohe, each
  held by a branch of the family. From Sannohe Castle the main line ruled seven districts by
  the time of Nanbu Harumasa, but the branches often went their own way. The revolt of Ōura
  Tamenobu in 1571 cost them Tsugaru. Nanbu Nobunao joined Hideyoshi at Odawara and kept the
  rest.
