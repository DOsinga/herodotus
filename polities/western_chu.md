description: >-
  Western Chu was Xiang Yu's short lived kingdom in eastern China from 206 to 202 BCE, during the
  struggle following the Qin dynasty's collapse. Its capital was Pengcheng, now Xuzhou, and Xiang
  styled himself Hegemon King rather than founding a unified imperial administration. He divided
  the former Qin territories among eighteen rulers, expecting to dominate this political
  settlement. The arrangement soon unravelled as displaced claimants and ambitious commanders
  fought for territory. Liu Bang, assigned the kingdom of Han, became Xiang's main opponent in
  the Chu Han contention. Xiang won dramatic battlefield victories but failed to secure lasting
  alliances and supplies. Defeat at Gaixia ended his military position; his subsequent death in
  202 allowed Liu Bang's Han dynasty to consolidate control.
