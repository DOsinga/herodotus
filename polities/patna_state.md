description: >-
  Patna was a Chauhan kingdom in western Odisha, founded around 1360 by Ramai Deva, who seized
  power from a council of eight local chiefs after the Eastern Ganga administrator of the region
  died. Its first seat was Patnagarh, and in the sixteenth century the capital moved to Balangir.
  The kingdom lay between the Mahanadi in the north and the Tel in the south and headed a cluster
  of eighteen fortified districts, the Garhjat. Much of its history comes from seventeenth-century
  court poetry such as the Kosalananda Kavya. Around 1570 a younger branch took the lands north of
  the Ang River and founded the kingdom of Sambalpur, which soon overshadowed the parent state.
  The Bhonsle Marathas of Nagpur overran the region around 1800.
