description: >-
  The Shaan–Gan–Ning Border Region was a Communist governed base in north central
  China, named for Shaanxi, Gansu, and Ningxia. Reconstituted in 1937 under the Second
  United Front, it formally belonged within the Republic of China but retained its own
  party leadership and administration. Yan'an became the political headquarters from
  which Mao Zedong and his colleagues rebuilt the Communist movement after the Long
  March. Rural government, military organization, education, and campaigns for economic
  self-sufficiency supported the base during war with Japan. The Yan'an Rectification
  Movement strengthened Mao's authority and imposed ideological discipline, including
  coercive purges. Renewed civil war brought Nationalist attacks, but Communist forces
  recovered the region. In 1949 its revolutionary government became part of the new
  People's Republic of China.
