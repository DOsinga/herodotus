description: >-
  Schaffhausen was a town on the Rhine in northern Switzerland, just above the falls where boats
  had to be unloaded. It grew around the Benedictine abbey of All Saints, founded by the counts of
  Nellenburg about 1050, and was a free imperial city by 1208. Its council gradually limited the
  abbot's powers, and Rudolf of Habsburg granted a charter of liberties in 1277. In 1330 Louis the
  Bavarian pledged the town to the Habsburgs, who held it until 1415, when King Sigismund outlawed
  Duke Frederick of Austria at the Council of Constance and Schaffhausen bought back its freedom.
  It allied with the Swiss confederates in 1454, joined them as a full member in 1501, and adopted
  the Reformation in 1529.
