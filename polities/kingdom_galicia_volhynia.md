description: >-
  Galicia and Volhynia formed a Rus' monarchy in eastern Europe when Roman Mstislavich
  united their principalities in 1199. Its heartland lay in present western Ukraine, with
  important centres at Halych, Volodymyr, Chełm and later Lviv. Princes of the Rurik family
  competed with boyars and faced intervention from Poland and Hungary. Daniel restored the
  union after prolonged succession struggles, survived the Mongol invasion and accepted the
  Golden Horde's supremacy. His coronation by a papal envoy in 1253 gave the state its royal
  title, without replacing its Orthodox religious traditions. Trade connected its towns with
  the Baltic and Black Sea regions. Succession crises weakened the kingdom in the fourteenth
  century. Poland took Galicia in 1349, while Volhynia came under Lithuanian control
  thereafter.
