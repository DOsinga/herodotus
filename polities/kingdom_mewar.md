description: >-
  Mewar was a Rajput kingdom in southern Rajasthan, northern India, traced here from 734
  until its British protection treaty in 1818. Guhila rulers and their Sisodia successors
  governed a landscape of hill forts, farming districts, and competing noble houses. Chittor
  became its chief stronghold, while Udaipur later served as the royal seat. Rulers such as
  Kumbha and Sanga expanded Mewar's influence, and Maharana Pratap became famous for
  resisting Mughal conquest. Warfare with Delhi, Gujarat, Malwa, and the Mughals repeatedly
  threatened its territory without extinguishing its governing line. A settlement with the
  Mughals in 1615 allowed the kingdom to retain its own administration. The 1818 agreement
  transferred external affairs to the East India Company; its princely monarchy survived
  afterward until 1949.
