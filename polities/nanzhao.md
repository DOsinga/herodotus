description: >-
  Nanzhao was a kingdom centred on the Erhai valley in present day Yunnan, China, from
  738 to 902. Piluoge united neighbouring chiefdoms under the Meng ruling family,
  establishing a monarchy that grew into a major power between Tang China, Tibet, and
  northern Southeast Asia. Its capital moved from Taihe to Yangjumie, near modern Dali,
  in 779. The kings alternated alliances with Tibet and the Tang, defeated very large
  Chinese invasions, and sent armies into Sichuan, Burma, and northern Vietnam.
  Officials borrowed Chinese administrative practices while governing a population with
  several languages and traditions. Buddhism became important at court. Changes of the
  kingdom's official name did not interrupt its government. In 902, Zheng Maisi
  overthrew the Meng dynasty and established Dachanghe.
