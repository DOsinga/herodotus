description: >-
  The Commune of Padua was the self-governing city state of Padua in the Veneto, from the
  first consuls in 1138 until Jacopo da Carrara became its lord in 1318. Padua joined the
  Lombard League, and in 1222 its university was founded by scholars from Bologna.
  Ezzelino da Romano held the city from 1237, and his cruelty there became legendary,
  until a papal crusade freed it in 1256. The restored commune expanded: it governed
  Vicenza from 1266 and built the Palazzo della Ragione, while Giotto painted the
  Scrovegni Chapel. War with Cangrande della Scala over Vicenza went badly, and in 1318
  the commune handed power to Jacopo da Carrara, beginning the lordship of the Carrara
  family.
