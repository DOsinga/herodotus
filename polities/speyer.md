description: >-
  Speyer was a city on the left bank of the Rhine, whose cathedral was the burial place of the
  Salian emperors. Henry V granted its citizens their first charter of liberties in 1111, and in
  1294 the bishop gave up his remaining rights, leaving the council to govern the free imperial
  city while the bishops moved to Udenheim and later Bruchsal. Speyer hosted many imperial diets,
  among them the diet of 1529 at which the Lutheran princes protested against the majority and so
  gained the name Protestants. From 1527 to 1689 it was the seat of the Imperial Chamber Court.
  French troops burned the city in 1689 during the Nine Years' War, and it fell to revolutionary
  France in 1794.
