description: >-
  The Lordship of Padua was the state of the Carrara family, who ruled Padua from 1318
  until Venice conquered the city in 1405. Jacopo da Carrara was elected lord to defend
  the city against Cangrande della Scala, but in 1328 his nephew had to hand Padua to the
  Scaliger. Marsilio da Carrara won it back in 1337 with Venetian help. Under Francesco il
  Vecchio from 1350 the lordship reached its height, adding Feltre, Belluno and, for a few
  years, Treviso, and fighting Venice in the War of Chioggia. Gian Galeazzo Visconti took
  Padua in 1388, but Francesco Novello retook it in 1390. His attempt to expand after Gian
  Galeazzo's death brought war with Venice, which took Padua and executed the last
  Carrara.
