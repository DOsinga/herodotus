description: >-
  The County of Champagne was a feudal principality on the plains east of Paris, governed by its
  counts from Troyes. It grew from the counties of Meaux and Troyes, held from 956 by Herbertian
  counts, which Odo II of Blois inherited in 1022; his descendants joined them with other lands
  into a single county. The counts kept their own courts, mint and officials and owed only homage
  to the French king. Under Theobald II and Henry the Liberal the fairs of Troyes, Provins, Lagny
  and Bar-sur-Aube drew Flemish and Italian merchants, and the court of Henry and Countess Marie
  supported poets such as Chrétien de Troyes. From 1234 the counts were also kings of Navarre.
  Countess Joan married the future Philip IV of France in 1284.
