description: >-
  The County of Champagne was a feudal principality on the plains east of Paris, governed by its
  counts from Troyes. Odo II of Blois acquired Troyes and Meaux in 1022, and his descendants
  joined these and neighbouring lands into a single county by the early twelfth century. The
  counts kept their own courts, mint and officials and owed only homage to the French king. Under
  Theobald II and Henry the Liberal the fairs of Troyes, Provins, Lagny and Bar-sur-Aube became
  the main meeting place of Flemish and Italian merchants, and the court of Henry and Countess
  Marie supported poets such as Chrétien de Troyes. From 1234 the counts were also kings of
  Navarre. Countess Joan married the future Philip IV of France in 1284.
