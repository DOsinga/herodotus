name: Grand Duchy of Baden
description: >-
  The Grand Duchy of Baden was a German monarchy on the eastern bank of the Rhine,
  governing separately from 1806 to 1871. Karlsruhe was its capital. Napoleon's
  reorganisation enlarged Baden and raised its ruler from elector to grand duke as the
  Holy Roman Empire dissolved. It joined the Confederation of the Rhine, then the German
  Confederation after Napoleon's defeat. A constitution introduced in 1818 established a
  representative assembly and gave Baden an important place in German liberal politics.
  Revolutionary movements in 1848 and 1849 briefly challenged the monarchy before
  Prussian troops restored it. Baden later aligned with Prussia and joined the German
  Empire in 1871, transferring war and foreign policy to imperial control while
  retaining its grand duke until 1918.
