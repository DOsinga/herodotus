description: >-
  Baol was a Wolof and Serer kingdom in central Senegal, between Cayor to the north and Sine to
  the south, with its capital at Lambaye. Founded in the eleventh century, it became a vassal of
  the Jolof Empire and won its independence at the battle of Danki in 1549, fighting alongside
  Cayor. Its king bore the title teigne and was chosen by great electors, ruling through a crown-
  slave bureaucracy and representatives of the pastoralists, fishermen, clergy and castes. Baol
  often shared a king with Cayor, and Lat Sukaabe Fall built a strong state armed with muskets
  around 1700, but the two kingdoms also fought often. France began its conquest of Baol in 1859
  and completed it in 1894.
