description: >-
  The Gorkha Kingdom was a hill monarchy in what is now central Nepal, founded by Dravya Shah in
  1559. It belonged to the patchwork of small states between the Himalayan mountains and the
  Indian plains. The Shah rulers governed from Gorkha, supported by nobles, officials, and
  military commanders. Their original territory lay between the Marshyangdi and Trishuli rivers,
  neighbouring Lamjung and the Kathmandu Valley's kingdoms. Under Prithvi Narayan Shah, who
  succeeded in 1743, Gorkha pursued expansion through conquest and blockade. Its forces defeated
  an East India Company expedition sent to aid the valley's rulers. The capture of Kathmandu in
  1768 marks the transition used here to the Kingdom of Nepal, whose further conquests continued
  the same dynasty's expanding political project.
