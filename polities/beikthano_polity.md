description: >-
  Beikthano was an early fortified Pyu city in central Myanmar, west of modern Taungdwingyi. Its governing life is estimated here at about 200 BCE to 600 CE from archaeological evidence, rather than from securely dated royal chronicles. Thick brick walls enclosed a large settlement, with gateways, an inner palace area, and buildings interpreted as Buddhist monasteries and stupas. Pottery, human remains, and other finds reveal an urban community with specialised construction and religious activity. Access to the fertile Kyaukse plains helped connect the city with farming regions. Beikthano is often discussed among the earliest city-states of Myanmar, but its rulers, foundation, and eventual absorption remain largely uncertain today. Later stories of conquest cannot supply reliable dates for its political end.
