description: >-
  Hiyawa, also called Adanawa after its capital Adana and known to the Assyrians as Que, was
  a Luwian-speaking Neo-Hittite kingdom in the Cilician plain of southern Anatolia. It
  emerged after the fall of the Hittite Empire, in a region where migrants from the Aegean
  had settled, and its name may echo the Hittite name for the Mycenaean Greeks. The kingdom
  wrote in both Luwian hieroglyphs and Phoenician, as the bilingual inscriptions of Karatepe
  and Cineköy show. Its king Awarikus paid tribute to Tiglath-pileser III and stayed a loyal
  Assyrian client for decades, building forts across his realm. After an Assyrian governor
  was placed over him, he sought help from Urartu, and Sargon II deposed him and made Que a
  province in 709 BCE.
