description: >-
  Wu Sangui's Zhou was the rebel state of the Revolt of the Three Feudatories in southwestern
  China, lasting from 1673 to 1681. Wu was the Ming general who had opened Shanhai Pass to
  the Manchus in 1644, and the Qing made him a prince governing Yunnan and Guizhou with his
  own army and officials. When the Kangxi Emperor accepted his offer to retire in 1673, Wu
  rebelled from Kunming in the name of restoring the Ming. His armies took Hunan, Sichuan and
  Guangxi, and the feudatories of Fujian and Guangdong joined him for a time. In 1678 he
  proclaimed the Zhou dynasty at Hengzhou but died that year. Qing forces retook Hunan and
  Sichuan, and his grandson Wu Shifan killed himself when Kunming fell in 1681.
