description: >-
  The Varman kingdom ruled southeastern Bengal in the second half of the eleventh century and
  the first half of the twelfth, with its seat at Vikramapura, the Bikrampur region south of
  modern Dhaka. Its founders probably came to Bengal with the Kalachuri king Karna's invasion
  of Vanga and stayed to carve out a state after the Chandra kings lost their hold. Vajravarman
  is the first name known, and his son Jatavarman married Karna's daughter and raided Anga,
  Varendra and Kamarupa. Harivarman reigned for a long time, served by the minister Bhatta
  Bhavadeva. The Varmans were Vaishnava Hindus who favoured Brahmins over the Buddhist
  monasteries of their predecessors. Vijayasena of the Sena dynasty defeated Bhojavarman and
  took the kingdom around the middle of the twelfth century.
