description: >-
  Curucuitz was a Maya kingdom in the upper Mopan valley of present Guatemala, represented here
  during the Late and Terminal Classic periods, approximately 600 to 950. Archaeological
  settlement patterns distinguish it from neighbouring Ixkun, Ixcol and Ixtonton, each identified
  as a separate regional kingdom. The surviving summary evidence does not provide a secure
  succession of Curucuitz rulers or a detailed account of its government. Its identification
  rests principally on the arrangement of centres and subordinate settlements rather than a
  complete written political history. The approximate map point marks the region, not a verified
  royal palace. Likewise, the dates indicate an estimated period of regional political
  organisation, not documented accession or conquest years. Its relationship to neighbouring
  courts remains poorly known.
