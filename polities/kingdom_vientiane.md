description: >-
  The Kingdom of Vientiane governed the middle Mekong region from 1707 to 1828, following the breakup of Lan Xang. Its Lao kings ruled from Vientiane, with Theravada Buddhism central to royal life. Rivalry with Luang Prabang and other neighboring courts drew the kingdom into the wars of Burma and Siam. It accepted Burmese overlordship before Siamese forces captured its capital in 1779 and imposed their own supremacy. The royal court nevertheless continued under Lao rulers. King Anouvong tried to overturn Siamese control through a campaign begun in 1826. Siam's counterattack destroyed Vientiane, deported much of its population, and ended the kingdom in 1828. Its former lands were then administered within the Siamese political system rather than by an Vientiane court.
