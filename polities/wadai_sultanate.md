name: Wadai Sultanate
description: >-
  The Wadai Sultanate ruled eastern Chad and neighboring areas from 1635 to 1912. Abd
  al-Karim founded it by overthrowing the Tunjur rulers and establishing an Islamic monarchy
  supported by Maba communities and local alliances. Ouara was its early capital; Abeche
  became the later seat of government. Sultans, also called kolaks, expanded their authority
  through armies, tributary relationships, marriage, and trade. Wadai competed with Darfur,
  Bornu, and Baguirmi, sometimes paying tribute while preserving its own government. Trans
  Saharan routes brought revenue from slaves, ivory, and other goods, and connected the
  court with northern merchants and the Senussi movement. French colonial expansion provoked
  resistance. Conquest ended the independent state in 1912, although the sultan's title
  later survived within Chad as an institution.
