description: >-
  Zwickau was a town on the Mulde in the Pleissenland, the imperial territory around Altenburg in
  present western Saxony. It received a town charter in 1212, and its council governed it under
  the imperial administration. The Wettin margraves of Meissen held the Pleissenland as a pledge
  from the middle of the thirteenth century, but Rudolf of Habsburg won it back for the Empire,
  and from 1290 Zwickau was a free imperial city. The town lived from cloth making and the trade
  roads to Bohemia. In 1323 Louis the Bavarian granted the Pleissenland, including Zwickau, to his
  son-in-law Frederick of Meissen, and the town became Wettin for good in 1348. It later became
  the first city after Wittenberg to adopt Lutheranism.
