description: >-
  The Ifranid Emirate of Salé was a principality of the Banu Ifran, a Zenata Berber tribe, on
  the Atlantic coast of Morocco in the eleventh century. After the Fatimids destroyed their
  earlier centre in western Algeria, one branch of the tribe took the Tadla region and then
  settled at Salé and Chellah at the mouth of the Bou Regreg. Around 1029 its leader Abu al-
  Kamal Tamim conquered Tamesna from the Barghawata. In 1033 he captured Fez from the rival
  Maghrawa and held it for several years, persecuting its Jewish community, until the Maghrawa
  retook the city around 1038 and he fell back on Salé. The emirs built the Great Mosque of
  Salé. The emirate declined in the 1050s, before the Almoravid conquest.
