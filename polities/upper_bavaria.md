description: >-
  Upper Bavaria was a Wittelsbach duchy created by Bavaria's partition in 1255, with Munich as a
  principal seat. Louis II governed this western share, which initially included the Palatinate as
  well as Bavarian lands. It was a dynastic monarchy whose borders did not match the modern
  administrative region called Upper Bavaria. The Palatinate separated in 1329, while Louis IV
  reunited Bavaria in 1340, ending the duchy's first period. A new partition in 1349 restored
  Upper Bavaria as a separate government. In 1363 it passed to the Landshut dukes, who governed it
  with Lower Bavaria until their partition of 1392 created the Munich and Ingolstadt duchies.
