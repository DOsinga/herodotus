description: >-
  Upper Bavaria was a Wittelsbach duchy created by Bavaria's partition in 1255, with
  Munich as a principal seat. Louis II governed this western share, which initially
  included the Palatinate as well as Bavarian lands. It was a dynastic monarchy whose
  borders did not match the modern administrative region called Upper Bavaria. The
  Palatinate separated in 1329, while Louis IV reunited Bavaria in 1340, ending the
  duchy's first period. A new partition in 1349 restored Upper Bavaria as a separate
  government. Further inheritance disputes and transfers brought it under the Landshut
  dukes from 1363, without a separate break in this record. In 1392 the territory was
  divided again, producing Bavaria-Munich and Bavaria-Ingolstadt and ending the united
  Upper Bavarian duchy described here.
