description: >-
  Venezuela occupies the northern coast of South America, with Caracas as its capital. This record begins in 1830, when it separated from Gran Colombia after the wars against Spanish rule. The republic's nineteenth century was marked by regional strongmen, civil wars, and struggles over central and federal power. Petroleum transformed its economy during the twentieth century, replacing agricultural exports as the main source of state income. Military governments alternated with civilian rule before the democratic settlement of 1958. Hugo Chávez's election in 1998 brought a new constitution and the Bolivarian political project. Subsequent economic collapse, repression, and mass emigration deeply changed society. The record follows the country across these regimes, rather than treating each government as a separate historical polity.
