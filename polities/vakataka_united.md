description: >-
  The United Vakataka Kingdom represents the early hereditary royal rule of Vindhyashakti
  and Pravarasena I in central India and the northern Deccan, approximately from 250 to
  330. Its origins and exact boundaries remain uncertain. Later inscriptions remember
  Vindhyashakti as the family's founder, but provide few details about his government. His
  successor Pravarasena expanded the realm and adopted the imperial title samrat.
  Elaborate Vedic sacrifices, including horse sacrifices, helped proclaim his royal
  standing. Purika is associated with this early kingdom, although its location is
  disputed and the map point is approximate. After Pravarasena's death, his descendants
  ruled separate branches, particularly those centred on Nandivardhana and Vatsagulma.
  Their later achievements, including Ajanta patronage, belong to successor governments
  rather than this united realm.
