description: >-
  Mehrgarh was an farming settlement on the Kacchi Plain near the Bolan Pass in present day
  Balochistan, Pakistan. This record represents its Neolithic and later prehistoric
  development approximately from 7000 to 3300 BCE, with both boundaries treated as
  estimates. Excavations reveal mud brick houses, grain storage, burials, and a gradual
  expansion of crafts. People cultivated barley and wheat and kept sheep, goats, and cattle,
  combining agriculture with other resources. Early inhabitants lived without pottery; later
  phases produced painted vessels, figurines, beads, and copper objects. Distant materials
  show exchange networks beyond the settlement. Human remains even preserve evidence of
  early dental drilling. Mehrgarh is an archaeological community, not a documented kingdom.
  Its traditions helped shape the background of the Indus civilization.
