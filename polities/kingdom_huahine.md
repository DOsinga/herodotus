description: >-
  The Kingdom of Huahine was a Polynesian monarchy on the island of Huahine and nearby Maiao in
  the Leeward Society Islands. Its rulers came from a line of chiefs that was already in place
  when James Cook met Queen Tehaʻapapa I in 1769, and later from families related to the Tamatoa
  house of Raiatea. Protestant missionaries arrived from 1809, and the island became Christian
  in the following years. Huahine resisted French attempts to extend the Tahitian protectorate
  in 1846, and the Jarnac Convention of 1847 between France and Britain recognised it as
  independent. In the 1870s it signed its own treaty of friendship with Germany. France
  proclaimed a protectorate and annexed the island in 1888, met resistance and bombarded its
  villages, and deposed the last queen in 1895.
