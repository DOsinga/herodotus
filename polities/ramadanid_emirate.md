description: >-
  The Ramadanid Emirate was a Turkmen principality in the Cilician plain of southern
  Anatolia, with its capital at Adana, which took over the region from the Armenian Kingdom
  of Cilicia in the fourteenth century. Ramadan Bey led the Yüreğir Turkmen who had settled
  in the plain, and his family established its rule around 1352 under the overlordship of
  the Mamluk Sultanate. For more than a century the beys were in practice independent,
  holding the frontier between the Mamluks, the Karamanids and the Dulkadirids and building
  mosques and bridges in Adana. Unlike most Anatolian emirates, it grew out of the Mamluk
  sphere. When Selim I
  conquered the Mamluks in 1517, the Ottomans made Adana a province and kept the family as
  its hereditary governors until 1608.
