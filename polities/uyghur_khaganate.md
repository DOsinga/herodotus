description: >-
  The Uyghur Khaganate ruled the Mongolian steppe from 744 to 840, after a tribal alliance overthrew
  the eastern Turkic rulers. A khagan governed the confederation from Ordu-Baliq in the Orkhon valley,
  combining mounted military power with a permanent capital and trading connections. Uyghur armies
  assisted China's Tang dynasty during the An Lushan rebellion and received payments and commercial
  advantages in return. Sogdian merchants helped link the court with Asian networks and introduced
  Manichaeism, which became a royal religion. Rivalries with Tibet and internal struggles strained the
  state. In 840 a Kyrgyz attack destroyed the imperial centre and ended its government. Displaced
  Uyghur groups later established new kingdoms farther south, rather than preserving the same steppe
  empire under a continuing khagan.
