description: >-
  The Sapaean kingdom was the last Thracian monarchy, ruling much of Thrace in the southeastern
  Balkans from the middle of the first century BCE until 46 CE. Its kings came from the Sapaean
  tribe and governed from Bizye, modern Vize in European Turkey, succeeding the older Odrysian
  kingdom. At first of limited importance, the kingdom grew as a client of Rome. After the Battle
  of Actium in 31 BCE, Octavian backed a new line of Sapaean kings, including Rhoemetalces I, who
  proved loyal and expanded their rule across Thrace with Roman support. Royal coins combined
  Thracian rulers with Roman emperors. Dynastic murders and revolts by Thracian tribes troubled
  the later kings. Claudius annexed the kingdom in 46 CE and made Thrace a Roman province.
