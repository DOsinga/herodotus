description: >-
  The Principality of Piombino was a small state on the Tuscan coast, made up of Piombino,
  part of Elba and nearby islands, from 1399 until France annexed it in 1799. Gherardo
  Appiani founded it when he sold Pisa to the Visconti and kept Piombino for his family.
  It was a lordship until 1594, when Emperor Rudolf II made it a principality, and its
  iron mines on Elba made it valuable to its larger neighbours. Cesare Borgia, the Medici
  and Spain each took it for some years, and Spain kept garrisons there. After the Appiani
  it passed to the Ludovisi in 1634 and then to the Boncompagni, absentee princes who left
  local councils to govern. French troops ended it during the revolutionary wars.
