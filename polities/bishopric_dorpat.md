description: >-
  The Bishopric of Dorpat was a church principality in southeastern Estonia, centred on the
  town now called Tartu. Hermann of Buxhoeveden, brother of Bishop Albert of Riga, took the
  Estonian hill fort of Tarbatu in 1224 and made it the seat of his see, and the German king
  enfeoffed him with its lands the next year. The bishops governed through their castles and
  a nobility of German vassals, with the cathedral chapter beside them. Dorpat was a
  Hanseatic town and an important stop on the trade route to Novgorod and Pskov. Its troops
  fought beside the Livonian knights when Alexander Nevsky defeated them on Lake Peipus in
  1242. The bishops often opposed the Livonian Order, and from 1435 the bishopric was a
  member of the Livonian Confederation.
