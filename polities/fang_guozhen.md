description: >-
  Fang Guozhen's domain was a maritime regime on the coast of Zhejiang during the collapse of
  the Yuan dynasty, lasting from 1356 to 1367. Fang came from a family of smugglers in
  Huangyan and began as a pirate in 1348, raiding the grain fleets that fed the Yuan capital.
  The Yuan court failed to defeat him and instead bought his nominal submission with titles.
  By 1356 he held the prefectures of Qingyuan, Taizhou and Wenzhou, which his relatives
  governed, and his fleet was said to number over a thousand ships. He traded diplomatic
  gifts with Zhu Yuanzhang and shipped grain north for the Yuan, keeping his independence by
  caution. Zhu's armies took Taizhou, Wenzhou and Qingyuan in late 1367, and Fang
  surrendered.
