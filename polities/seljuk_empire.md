description: >-
  The Great Seljuk Empire arose in 1037 from the expansion of an Oghuz Turkic ruling family
  across Iran and neighbouring lands. Its sultans combined military power with Persian
  administration and Sunni Islamic patronage. Tughril entered Baghdad in 1055, leaving the
  Abbasid caliph's religious position intact while taking political command. Alp Arslan's victory
  over Byzantium at Manzikert in 1071 helped open Anatolia to Turkish settlement and conquest.
  Under Malik Shah and the vizier Nizam al Mulk, the empire expanded and supported schools,
  cities and monumental building. Isfahan became a principal royal seat. Succession conflicts
  weakened the central government after Malik Shah's death. This unified record ends in 1118,
  when eastern and western sultanates governed separately, not when all Seljuk rule disappeared.
