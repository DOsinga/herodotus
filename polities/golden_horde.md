description: >-
  The Golden Horde was a Mongol successor state governing the western Eurasian steppe, recorded
  here from 1260 to 1466. Descendants of Jochi ruled as khans from centres including Sarai on the
  lower Volga. Their authority joined mobile pastoral populations, commercial towns, and
  tributary principalities, including the Rus' lands. Long-distance trade connected Black Sea
  ports with inland routes across Asia. Over time the ruling society became increasingly
  Turkic-speaking, and Islam gained official prominence under Öz Beg Khan. Succession struggles
  after the mid-fourteenth century weakened central power, while Tokhtamysh briefly reunited much
  of the realm before destructive wars with Timur. Crimea, Kazan, and other khanates emerged from
  the fragmentation. The remaining central state continues separately here as the Great Horde
  from 1466.
