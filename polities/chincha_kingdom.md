description: >-
  The Chincha Kingdom was a polity of the Late Intermediate Period in the fertile Chincha
  valley on the southern coast of Peru, with its centre at the adobe complex of La
  Centinela. Its society was known for its specialists, among them thousands of fishermen
  and farmers and a large class of merchants who traded by sea along the coast, possibly
  reaching as far as Ecuador for spondylus shell, and by land into the highlands for copper.
  Spanish accounts describe a powerful lord of Chincha who ruled the valley's population.
  The kingdom came under Inca rule around 1476, apparently largely by agreement, and its
  lord kept a privileged place: at Cajamarca in 1532 he was the only noble besides Atahualpa
  carried in a litter.
