description: >-
  The Dimasa Kingdom, also called the Kachari kingdom, was a state of the Dimasa people in the
  hills and valleys south of the Brahmaputra, in present Assam and Nagaland. Ahom chronicles
  place the Dimasa between the Dikhou and Kolong rivers from the late thirteenth century.
  Their first known capital was Dimapur, a walled brick city on the Dhansiri. After wars with
  the Ahoms, Dimapur fell in 1536, and the kingdom lapsed for over twenty years. Nirbhaya
  Narayan revived it around 1558 at Maibang in the North Cachar hills. It paid tribute to the
  Koch and later moved its capital to Khaspur in the Cachar plains. Burmese invasions in the
  1820s led to British protection in 1824 and annexation in 1832.
