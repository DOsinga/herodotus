description: >-
  Rheged was a Brittonic kingdom in northern Britain after Roman rule, conventionally placed between about 500 and 650. Its territory is uncertain, but proposed heartlands include Cumbria and southwestern Scotland. Poetry associated with Taliesin celebrates King Urien and his son Owain, providing a rare glimpse of its royal family, warfare, and courtly ideals. Urien fought the expanding Anglian kingdom of Bernicia during the later sixth century. Genealogies and later accounts preserve further traditions, though they cannot establish firm borders or a complete sequence of rulers. Archaeologists have proposed Trusty's Hill in Galloway as a royal centre; this remains an interpretation rather than a proven capital. Rheged was eventually absorbed into Bernicia's realm, conventionally around the middle of the seventh century.
