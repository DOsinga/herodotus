description: >-
  The medieval Kingdom of Hungary occupied the Carpathian Basin from Stephen I's coronation
  around 1000 until the crisis following Mohács in 1526. Its kings established a Christian
  monarchy with counties, bishops and royal officials, joining the political world of Latin
  Europe. The Árpád dynasty ruled until 1301, followed by rulers from several other houses.
  Nobles gained privileges through the Golden Bull of 1222, and assemblies increasingly
  influenced government. Mongol invasion devastated the kingdom in 1241 and 1242, prompting
  defenses and stone castles. Later kings expanded influence abroad, while Matthias Corvinus
  strengthened royal administration and maintained a standing army. Ottoman victory at
  Mohács killed Louis II and opened a contested succession, dividing government between
  Habsburg Royal Hungary and the eastern kingdom.
