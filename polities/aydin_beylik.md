description: >-
  The Beylik of Aydın was a Turkish principality in western Anatolia, founded in 1308
  with Birgi as a principal seat. Mehmed Bey established its independent rule after
  separating from the Germiyanids and conquering lands formerly controlled by Byzantium.
  His son Umur developed a fleet based at Smyrna, carrying raids across the Aegean and
  intervening in Byzantine civil war. These maritime campaigns drew opposition from
  Latin powers and crusading forces. The ruling family also sponsored buildings,
  including the mosque of Isa Bey near Ephesus. Ottoman conquest interrupted the beylik
  in 1390. Timur's victory over the Ottomans allowed its restoration in 1402, but
  struggles followed. Under Junayd it resisted Ottoman consolidation before final
  annexation in 1426 ended its separate government in Anatolia.
