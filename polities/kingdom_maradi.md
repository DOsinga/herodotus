description: >-
  The Kingdom of Maradi was the Hausa state founded by the ruling house of Katsina after the
  Fulani jihad drove it from its capital. When the forces of the Sokoto Caliphate took Katsina in
  1807, members of its ruling class withdrew north, and around 1819 they established themselves at
  Maradi in what is now southern Niger. Their ruler kept the title Sarkin Katsina and claimed to
  govern the true Katsina state in exile. Maradi waged a long war against Sokoto's emirates to the
  south, often in alliance with the exiled Gobir state at Tibiri to the west, while the Sultanate
  of Damagaram at Zinder pressed it from the east. The French Voulet-Chanoine mission sacked
  Maradi in 1899 and brought it under French rule.
