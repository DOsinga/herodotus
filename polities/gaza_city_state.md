description: >-
  Gaza was one of the five Philistine city states on the southern Levantine coast, with its political phase estimated here from 1175 to 604 BCE. Earlier Egyptian control gave way to Philistine settlement during the upheavals at the end of the Bronze Age. Each principal Philistine city had its own ruler rather than forming a single permanent kingdom. Gaza's position near the route between Egypt and the Levant made it valuable for commerce and military movement. Biblical traditions and Assyrian records preserve different views of its rulers and conflicts. Assyrian overlordship brought tribute and intervention while local kings continued governing. The end of this record follows Nebuchadnezzar's campaigns against the Philistine cities, which brought the region under Babylonian authority thereafter.
