description: >-
  Abu Bakr Dughlat's kingdom was a state in the western Tarim Basin, based at Yarkand, that
  lasted from 1465 to 1514. Mirza Abu Bakr belonged to the Dughlat family of amirs, who had
  long governed Kashgar for the khans of Moghulistan. Around 1465 he made himself master of
  Yarkand and Khotan, and in 1479–1480 he rebelled against Yunus Khan, defeated his attacks
  and took Kashgar. A son of Yunus held Kashgar briefly in 1499, but Abu Bakr recovered it and
  went on to raid Ladakh, the Gilgit region and Badakhshan. His rule is remembered as harsh.
  In 1514 the Chagatayid prince Sultan Said Khan took Kashgar and Yarkand, founding the
  Yarkent Khanate, and Abu Bakr was killed while fleeing towards Ladakh.
