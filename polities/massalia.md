description: >-
  Massalia was a Greek city republic founded around 600 BCE at modern Marseille on the
  Mediterranean coast of southern France. Settlers from Phocaea established the port,
  and refugees arrived after Persia conquered their homeland. A closed body of wealthy
  citizens governed through a council of six hundred and a group of elected magistrates.
  Maritime trade linked Massalia with Gaul, Iberia, and the wider Mediterranean, while
  its settlers founded coastal outposts such as Nice and Antibes. The city also produced
  explorers, including Pytheas, who travelled toward northern Europe. Its alliance with
  Rome preserved its own government and fleet for centuries. During Caesar's civil war
  it backed his opponents, endured a siege, and surrendered in 49 BCE, losing
  independence and much territory.
