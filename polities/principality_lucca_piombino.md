description: >-
  The Principality of Lucca and Piombino was a small state in Tuscany that Napoleon created in
  1805 for his sister Elisa Bonaparte and her husband Felice Baciocchi, joining the former
  Republic of Lucca to the principality of Piombino on the coast. Elisa governed it energetically
  herself, reforming the administration and law on the French model, closing monasteries,
  encouraging the Carrara marble quarries and building roads, schools and a public library. Its
  court at Lucca became a centre of music, with Niccolò Paganini in Elisa's service. In 1809
  Napoleon also made her Grand Duchess of Tuscany. The principality's fortunes followed the French
  empire's, and it ended with Napoleon's fall in 1814, after which the Congress of Vienna created
  the Duchy of Lucca.
