description: >-
  The Kingdom of Rarotonga was a monarchy on Rarotonga, the main island of the southern Cook
  Islands, established in 1858. Protestant missionaries had converted the leading ariki, or high
  chiefs, and worked with them to draw up written law codes, which brought a period of unusual
  peace to the island. The kingdom was governed by these chiefs, with the Makea line of Avarua
  at its head. From the 1880s New Zealand and Britain took a growing interest in the islands.
  Fearing a French annexation, Queen Makea Takau asked for British protection, and in 1888 a
  protectorate was declared over the southern group. A British resident then guided a federal
  government of the islands, which New Zealand annexed in 1901.
