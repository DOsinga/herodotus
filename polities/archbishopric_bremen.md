description: >-
  The Prince-Archbishopric of Bremen was an ecclesiastical principality between the lower Weser
  and the lower Elbe in northwestern Germany. The archbishops of Bremen had led the mission to
  Scandinavia since the ninth century, and from the twelfth century they ruled a territory of
  marshes, moors and river towns, with Stade as its main town and the castle of Bremervörde as
  their residence. The city of Bremen, though it gave the see its name, governed itself and stood
  outside the principality. The cathedral chapter elected the archbishops, and the estates of the
  land had a strong voice. After the Reformation, Lutheran administrators from neighbouring princely
  houses held the see. Swedish troops occupied it during the Thirty Years' War, and in 1648 it
  became the Swedish Duchy of Bremen.
