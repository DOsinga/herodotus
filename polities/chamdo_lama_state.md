description: >-
  Chamdo was a monastic principality in western Kham, governed from the Gelug monastery of
  Jampaling at the meeting of the two rivers that form the Mekong. Tsongkhapa is said to
  have visited the site in 1373 and urged that a monastery be built there, and his disciple
  Jangsem Sherab Zangpo founded Jampaling between 1436 and 1444. Its abbots, later the
  incarnate Phagpa Lha lamas, ruled the town and the farms and pastures around it through
  the monastery estates. Chamdo lay on the main road from Lhasa to Sichuan and was one of
  several lama-ruled states in Kham. In 1642 the Dalai Lama's new government in Lhasa took
  over Kham after Güshi Khan's conquest, though the lamas of Chamdo kept much of their local
  power.
