description: >-
  The Commune of Bologna was the self-governing city state of Bologna, from Henry V's
  privilege of 1116 until Pope Julius II took the city in 1506. It grew rich around its
  university, the oldest in Europe, and in 1249 its army captured King Enzo, Frederick
  II's son, at Fossalta. Guelph Bologna usually stood with the papacy, but governed itself
  through its councils and the guilds of the popolo. Papal legates ruled it from 1327 to
  1334 and again after 1360, and the Pepoli and the Visconti held it in the middle of the
  fourteenth century. The commune revived in 1376, and from 1443 the Bentivoglio family
  led it under nominal papal sovereignty, until Julius II drove them out.
