description: >-
  The Chahamana kingdom of Shakambhari governed parts of Rajasthan and neighbouring northern India,
  with its beginnings estimated here around 684. Early rulers were associated with the region of
  Sambhar and Nagaur and later served as vassals of the Gurjara-Pratiharas. Their successors asserted
  stronger royal authority and fought the Chaulukyas, Paramaras, Tomaras, and invading Muslim armies.
  Ajayaraja II shifted the principal court to Ajmer in the twelfth century; Vigraharaja IV extended
  Chahamana rule to Delhi. The dynasty supported Sanskrit literature and Hindu and Jain temples.
  Prithviraja III's defeat by Muhammad of Ghor at Tarain in 1192 did not immediately erase local
  government. Govindaraja and then Hariraja continued ruling before Ghurid conquest in 1194 ended the
  kingdom's separate administration in the region.
