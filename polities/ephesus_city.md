description: >-
  Ephesus was an Ionian Greek city on the coast of western Anatolia, near the mouth of the
  Cayster river. Greek settlers from Attica founded it, by tradition under the Athenian prince
  Androklos, around the tenth century BCE, and it became one of the twelve cities of the Ionian
  League. Its great Temple of Artemis drew worshippers from across the Greek and Anatolian
  world. Ephesus was ruled by tyrants who married into the Lydian royal house, until Croesus
  besieged the city about 560 BCE and brought it under Lydia. After the Persian wars it joined
  the Delian League, paying money rather than ships. It stayed with Athens until 412 BCE, when
  it went over to Sparta, and the King's Peace of 387 BCE returned Ionia to Persia.
