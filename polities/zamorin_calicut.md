description: >-
  The Zamorins were hereditary rulers of Calicut on India's Malabar coast, governing a
  kingdom recorded here from 1124 to 1766. Their Hindu royal house followed matrilineal
  succession and developed Calicut into a major port for the Indian Ocean spice trade.
  Arab and other Muslim merchants were important partners, while the rulers competed with
  neighbouring Kerala kingdoms for inland influence and coastal commerce. Vasco da Gama's
  arrival in 1498 opened a long struggle with the Portuguese over trading privileges and
  naval power. The Zamorins relied on the Kunjali Marakkar naval commanders during that
  conflict. Later European alliances shifted without ending local rule. Hyder Ali's
  invasion from Mysore in 1766 brought the independent kingdom to an end, although the
  royal family survived.
