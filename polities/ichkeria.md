description: >-
  The Chechen Republic of Ichkeria was a separatist government in the North Caucasus,
  centred on Grozny, from 1991 until Russian forces took the city in 2000. Dzhokhar
  Dudayev proclaimed independence during the Soviet Union's collapse, but Russia rejected
  the breakaway. The republic had a president, parliament and constitution, although
  rivalries weakened its institutions. Russian intervention in 1994 began the First
  Chechen War. Separatist victory and Russian withdrawal in 1996 left Ichkeria governing
  Chechnya without broad international recognition. Aslan Maskhadov won the 1997
  presidential election, but devastation, armed factions and kidnappings undermined
  recovery. A second war began in 1999, and Russia captured Grozny in February 2000.
  Resistance and exile organisations continued afterwards, but no longer constituted the
  territorial government represented here.
