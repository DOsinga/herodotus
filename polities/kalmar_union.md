description: >-
  The Kalmar Union joined Denmark, Norway and Sweden under a common monarch from 1397 to
  1523. Sweden then included much of Finland, while Norway brought its North Atlantic island
  possessions. Queen Margaret I helped arrange the union, and Eric of Pomerania was crowned
  at Kalmar. The kingdoms retained separate laws and councils, but the monarch directed their
  shared political affairs. Scandinavian nobles hoped to counter the influence of the
  Hanseatic League, yet disputes over royal taxation, war and Danish dominance repeatedly
  weakened political cooperation. Sweden experienced rebellions and periods of rule by its
  own regents. Christian II's executions in Stockholm in 1520 deepened opposition. Gustav
  Vasa's election as Swedish king in 1523 ended the union, leaving Denmark and Norway
  together.
