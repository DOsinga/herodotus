description: >-
  Đỗ Động Giang was the territorial domain of Đỗ Cảnh Thạc in northern Vietnam during the
  division known as the Period of the Twelve Warlords. Centred in the Thanh Oai area near present
  day Hanoi, it became a separately governed stronghold after the Ngô ruler's death in 965. Thạc
  was a military leader who had previously served in Ngô affairs and helped overthrow the usurper
  Dương Tam Kha. He now claimed the title Duke Cảnh and exercised local power rather than obeying
  a common royal government. His domain was one of several rival regional centres competing
  within the former unified Ngô realm. Đinh Bộ Lĩnh defeated him in 967 during the campaigns that
  reunited the region under a new dynasty.
