description: >-
  Majapahit was a Hindu-Buddhist kingdom in eastern Java, founded in 1293 and recorded
  here until 1527. Raden Wijaya established it after exploiting, then expelling, a
  Mongol expedition sent against Java. Its royal centre at Trowulan supported a court,
  officials, religious establishments, and craft production. Under Hayam Wuruk and the
  minister Gajah Mada, Majapahit became a leading power in the Indonesian archipelago.
  Court literature described a broad network of tributary lands, although those claims
  should not be read as uniform direct administration. Agriculture in Java and maritime
  trade sustained its wealth. Succession struggles and civil war weakened the kingdom,
  while Muslim coastal states gained strength. Demak's expansion ultimately ended its
  political independence, though Majapahit traditions continued to influence Java and
  Bali.
