description: >-
  Gianyar was a Balinese kingdom in the south-central part of the island, east of Ubud. Its rulers
  belonged to the Manggis family, descended from the Gelgel royal house, which first held the
  village of Beng. In 1771 Dewa Manggis Sakti built the palace of Gianyar and made it the centre of
  a sovereign kingdom, one of the nine kingdoms of Bali. Gianyar grew in the nineteenth century as
  neighbouring Payangan collapsed, and the princes of Ubud became its allies. It fought repeatedly
  with Klungkung, which held its king captive in the 1880s. Threatened by its neighbours, Gianyar
  turned to the Dutch, and in 1900 its ruler accepted Dutch authority as a stedehouder, a
  representative of the colonial government.
