description: >-
  Soyo was a coastal province of the Kingdom of Kongo at the mouth of the Congo River that became
  an independent state in the seventeenth century. Its port of Mpinda handled much of Kongo's
  trade with the Portuguese. Álvaro II made its ruler a count in the 1590s, and Count Manuel held
  office for many years with growing autonomy. In 1641 Count Daniel da Silva rejected King Garcia
  II's choice of successor, claiming that Soyo's nobles had the right to elect their count, and
  defeated every royal army sent against him from his forest stronghold. Soyo's rulers then styled
  themselves princes, sheltered the Kimpanzu branch of the royal family and routed a Portuguese
  invasion at Kitombo in 1670. Portugal took control in the 1880s.
