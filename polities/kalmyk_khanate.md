description: >-
  The Kalmyk Khanate was an Oirat Mongol state on the lower Volga steppe from 1630 to 1771.
  Migrants led by Kho Orluk established themselves among lands formerly occupied by the Nogai,
  with herding and mounted warfare central to their society. Hereditary khans and tribal nobles
  governed under a shared nomadic legal code, while Tibetan Buddhism connected them with other
  Mongolian communities. Relations with Russia combined treaties, military cooperation, trade,
  and mutual raids. Ayuka Khan strengthened the state and supplied cavalry for Russian campaigns
  while pursuing his own regional ambitions. Growing Russian interference and settlement later
  restricted its freedom. In 1771 Ubashi led a large and difficult eastward migration toward Qing
  territory; Russia abolished the remaining khanate and incorporated its lands.
