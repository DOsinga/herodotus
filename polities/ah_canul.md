description: >-
  Ah Canul was a Maya province of the northwestern Yucatán Peninsula, between Campeche and
  Mérida, with its main town at Calkiní. The Canul lineage traced its arrival to the time of
  Mayapan, where its warriors had served the Cocom rulers as mercenaries. After the fall of
  Mayapan around 1441 the Canul settled in this region and formed a province governed
  jointly by several lords of the lineage rather than by a single supreme ruler. The Códice
  de Calkiní, written by Maya nobles in the colonial period, records the province's history
  and its lords. Ah Canul submitted to Francisco de Montejo the Younger in 1541 as the
  Spanish advanced north from Campeche.
