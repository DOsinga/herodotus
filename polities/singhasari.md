description: >-
  Singhasari was a Hindu and Buddhist kingdom in eastern Java between 1222 and 1292, centred
  near modern Malang. Ken Arok founded its ruling line after defeating Kediri, though later
  accounts of his rise combine history with legend. Kings governed from Tumapel, which
  became known as Singhasari, and succession was repeatedly marked by violence within the
  royal family. Under Kertanegara the kingdom pursued influence beyond Java, sending
  expeditions to Sumatra and cultivating ties with Champa. His rejection of demands from
  Kublai Khan brought the threat of Mongol intervention. Before that expedition arrived,
  Jayakatwang of Kediri rebelled in 1292, killed Kertanegara and overthrew Singhasari.
  Surviving members of its royal circle subsequently helped establish Majapahit, carrying
  forward the dynasty and its regional ambitions.
