name: Federation of Malaya
description: >-
  The Federation of Malaya governed the Malay Peninsula as an independent state from 1957 to 1963, with Kuala Lumpur as its capital. It joined nine Malay states with Penang and Malacca, following an earlier federation established under British protection in 1948. Independence brought a new parliamentary constitutional monarchy, with a federal king chosen from the hereditary Malay rulers and Tunku Abdul Rahman serving as prime minister. Its constitution balanced federal institutions with state governments, preserved the rulers' positions, and addressed citizenship in a society comprising Malay, Chinese, Indian, and other communities. Singapore remained outside the federation throughout this period. In September 1963, Malaya joined Singapore, North Borneo, and Sarawak to form Malaysia, carrying its institutions into the enlarged federal country.
