description: >-
  The Pomeranians were a West Slavic people on the southern Baltic coast between the Oder and the
  Vistula. Mieszko I and Bolesław I of Poland brought much of their land under Polish rule and
  founded a bishopric at Kołobrzeg in 1000, but a pagan revolt drove the Poles out of western
  Pomerania within a few years, and the east around Gdańsk broke away in the crisis of the 1030s.
  For most of the eleventh century the Pomeranians were governed by their own dukes and the elders
  of strongholds such as Kołobrzeg, Wolin, Szczecin and Gdańsk, of whom few names survive.
  Bolesław III Wrymouth reconquered the land in campaigns from 1102 to 1122. Pomerelia became a
  Polish province, and western Pomerania became the Duchy of Pomerania under Wartislaw I.
