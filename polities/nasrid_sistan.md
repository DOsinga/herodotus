description: >-
  Nasrid Sistan was the principality of the maliks of Nimruz, a branch of the Saffarid family,
  who governed Sistan from Zaranj between the decline of the Ghaznavids and the Mongol
  invasion. Abu'l-Fadl Nasr had been the Ghaznavid malik of the province, and from 1068 his
  line ruled it in its own right. They usually acknowledged a stronger neighbour, first the
  Seljuks, then the Ghurids from 1175 and finally the Khwarazmshahs, but kept their own court,
  army and revenue. Taj al-Din Nasr II reigned for more than sixty years and Taj al-Din Harb
  for more than forty, which gave the province long stretches of stable rule. The Mongol
  invasion ruined Zaranj in 1222, and the Khwarazmian commander Inaltigin ended the dynasty in
  1225.
