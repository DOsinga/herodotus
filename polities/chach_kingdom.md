description: >-
  Chach was an oasis kingdom around present Tashkent in Central Asia, represented here from
  about 500 to 750 CE. Its royal centres included Ming Urik, though the kingdom covered more
  than the modern city. Irrigation canals sustained numerous towns, and trade connected
  settled Sogdian communities with surrounding Turkic pastoral peoples. Chinese accounts
  describe its rulers, while local coins and archaeological remains offer further evidence
  of royal government. Chach operated within changing regional power networks, including
  Turkic influence, without losing local political identity. In 750, the Tang commander Gao
  Xianzhi attacked the kingdom and destroyed its royal government. The ruler's fate helped
  intensify the conflict that led to the battle of Talas. Its starting date and
  representative royal site remain approximate.
