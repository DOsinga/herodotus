description: >-
  The Taifa of Ronda was a Muslim kingdom in the mountains of southern al-Andalus, centred on the
  gorge-top city of Ronda in present Málaga province. Its rulers belonged to the Banu Ifran, a
  Berber family from North Africa who had come to al-Andalus as soldiers of the caliphate. During
  the breakup of the Caliphate of Córdoba, Abu Nur Hilal ibn Abi Qurra made Ronda his own
  principality, and his family held its mountain districts against neighbouring Berber and Arab
  rulers. The kingdom's strong natural defences made it a refuge, but it lacked wealth and allies.
  From the 1040s the expanding taifa of Seville under al-Mu'tadid absorbed one neighbour after
  another. Seville took Ronda in 1065.
