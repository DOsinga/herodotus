description: >-
  Han was a state in central China, governing lands in present Henan and southern Shanxi from 453 to 230 BCE. It emerged when the Han, Zhao, and Wei families defeated the Zhi clan and divided power within Jin; Zhou recognition followed in 403 BCE. Han conquered Zheng and established its capital at Xinzheng in 375 BCE. Its rulers later adopted the title of king, but its position between stronger neighbours made defence difficult. Administrative reforms associated with Shen Buhai sought to strengthen government. The philosopher Han Fei belonged to its ruling family. Han attempted to weaken Qin through an expensive canal project, which instead increased Qin's resources. In 230 BCE, it became the first Warring States kingdom conquered by Qin.
