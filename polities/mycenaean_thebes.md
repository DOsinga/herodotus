description: >-
  Mycenaean Thebes was the Bronze Age palace kingdom on the Kadmeia, the acropolis of Thebes in
  Boeotia, central Greece. Excavations have uncovered parts of its palace, workshops and
  storerooms, together with Linear B tablets that record goods, personnel and offerings, showing
  an administration like those at Mycenae and Pylos. Imported cylinder seals from Mesopotamia
  found in the palace point to wide contacts across the eastern Mediterranean. Greek myth
  remembered Thebes as the city of Cadmus and Oedipus and the target of the Seven against Thebes.
  The palace was destroyed by fire at the end of the thirteenth century BCE, during the wider
  collapse of the Mycenaean palace system, long before the classical city state recorded
  separately.
