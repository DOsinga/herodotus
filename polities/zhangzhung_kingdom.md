description: >-
  Zhangzhung was a kingdom of western Tibet associated with the country around Mount Kailash and
  traditions of the Bon religion. Its emergence around 500 BCE is an estimate, not a securely
  documented foundation. Tibetan accounts describe kings and a royal centre traditionally identified
  with Kyunglung, although the relationship between surviving sites and the royal fortress remains
  disputed. Archaeology reveals settlements, fortifications, and burial grounds in the western
  plateau, but linking every monument to one kingdom is difficult. Zhangzhung had its own language and
  cultural traditions before the Tibetan monarchy extended its power westwards. Its conquest is placed
  here in 644 under Songtsen Gampo, with other chronologies using 625. Incorporation ended the
  separate kingdom, while its cultural and religious legacy continued.
