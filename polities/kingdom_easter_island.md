description: >-
  The Kingdom of Easter Island is the conventional name for the hereditary chiefdom of Rapa Nui,
  the remote island in the southeastern Pacific. Tradition traces its rulers to Hotu Matuʻa, who
  landed at Anakena, and divides the island among clans descended from his children. The clans
  later formed two rival groups, with the Miru of the northwest supplying the ariki mau, a
  sacred paramount chief who claimed divine descent and was surrounded by taboos. The clans
  built the island's ceremonial platforms and moai statues, quarried mostly at Rano Raraku, and
  competed and fought with each other. The Peruvian slave raids of 1862 and the epidemics that
  followed killed most of the islanders, including their chief. Chile annexed the island in
  1888.
