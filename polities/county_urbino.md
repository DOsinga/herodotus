description: >-
  The County of Urbino was the state of the Montefeltro counts, who held Urbino from 1213
  until Pope Eugene IV made Oddantonio da Montefeltro duke in 1443. In 1213 the city was
  granted to Buonconte da Montefeltro, and the counts built up a hill state in the
  northern Marche, governing as imperial and later papal vicars. Guido da Montefeltro was
  a great Ghibelline captain, whom Dante placed among the fraudulent counsellors in hell.
  Cardinal Albornoz drove the family out in the middle of the fourteenth century, but
  Antonio da Montefeltro won Urbino back in 1375 and added Gubbio. His son Guidantonio
  extended the county further and served the popes as a general, and his son became the
  first duke.
