description: >-
  Mazrui Mombasa was the city-state ruled by the Mazrui, an Omani Arab clan, from Fort Jesus on
  the Kenyan coast. The Mazrui governed Mombasa for the Yaruba imams of Oman, and when the Busaidi
  dynasty seized power in Oman in the 1740s, the governor Muhammad ibn Uthman al-Mazrui refused to
  recognise it and ruled in his own name. His successors extended their influence along the coast
  to Pemba and Pate and allied with Swahili towns and the Mijikenda of the hinterland. They
  resisted the Busaidi rulers for nearly a century and in 1824 sought British protection, which
  lasted only two years. Sultan Said bin Sultan finally took Fort Jesus in 1837 and deported the
  leading Mazrui to Bandar Abbas.
