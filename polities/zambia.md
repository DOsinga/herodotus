description: >-
  Zambia is a landlocked republic in south central Africa, independent from Britain since 24
  October 1964. Lusaka is its capital, and an elected president heads the government
  alongside a national legislature. The country emerged from Northern Rhodesia, whose copper
  mines had become central to the colonial economy. Kenneth Kaunda led the independence
  movement and served as the first president. His government supported liberation movements
  in neighbouring countries while attempting to build national unity across many language
  groups. One party rule lasted from 1972 until the return of multiparty elections in 1991,
  when Kaunda lost office. Copper production remains important, leaving the economy exposed
  to world prices. The Zambezi River, Victoria Falls and extensive wildlife areas also shape
  the country's identity.
