description: >-
  Odoacer's kingdom governed Italy from 476 to 493, with its court at Ravenna. It began when
  troops led by Odoacer removed Romulus Augustulus, the young western Roman emperor. Odoacer
  ruled as king while recognising the eastern emperor's formal authority, preserving Roman
  officials, taxation and the Senate instead of replacing them with an entirely new
  administration. His military following included several peoples, and his own ethnic
  background remains debated. Although an Arian Christian, he generally avoided interfering
  with the established church. He also extended his power into Dalmatia and defeated the
  Rugians north of Italy. Relations with Emperor Zeno deteriorated, and Theodoric invaded
  with the Ostrogoths in 489. Ravenna surrendered in 493; Theodoric killed Odoacer and took
  over his Italian realm.
