description: >-
  The Veletian principality was a loose association of Slavic communities between the Elbe
  and Oder, in what is now northeastern Germany. Its beginning around 600 is estimated
  rather than recorded in a founding account. Frankish writers called its people Wilzi;
  the best known ruler was Dragowit. Settlements clustered around rivers and fortified
  centres, separated by wooded areas. Local groups retained strong identities within the
  wider association, making royal authority less uniform than in a centralised kingdom. In
  789 Charlemagne led an expedition against Dragowit, who submitted and gave hostages.
  This defeat marks the end of central royal government here, not the disappearance of the
  communities. Redarian, Tollensian, Circipanian and Kessinian groups continued separately
  and later contributed to the Lutician federation.
