description: >-
  The Earldom of Orkney was a Norse lordship over the Orkney and, for much of its history,
  Shetland islands north of Scotland. Tradition credits King Harald Fairhair of Norway with
  creating the earldom for the family of Rognvald of Møre in the late ninth century. The earls
  were vassals of the Norwegian kings but ruled the islands themselves, raided and fought across
  northern Scotland and the Irish Sea, and at times also held Caithness. The Orkneyinga saga
  records their feuds. Earl Rognvald Kali founded St Magnus Cathedral in Kirkwall in 1137. From
  the thirteenth century the earldom passed to Scottish families, including the Sinclairs. In 1468
  the Norwegian king pledged Orkney to Scotland, and the Scottish crown annexed it in 1472.
