description: >-
  Aram-Damascus was an Aramean kingdom centred on Damascus in the southern Levant. Its
  beginning around 950 BCE is estimated from traditions concerning its early rulers; its
  independent government ended in 732 BCE. Kings such as Ben-Hadad and Hazael fought
  neighbouring Israel and confronted Assyrian expansion. Hazael made Damascus a strong
  regional power, extending its influence towards the Golan and Sea of Galilee. Its
  history emerges from biblical accounts, royal inscriptions and Assyrian records, which
  do not always agree in detail. The final king, Rezin, allied with Israel against Judah
  after earlier paying tribute to Assyria. Judah sought Assyrian help. Tiglath-Pileser III
  conquered Damascus, executed Rezin and annexed the kingdom, deporting inhabitants and
  ending its role as a separately governed state.
