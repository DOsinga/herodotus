description: >-
  The County of Habsburg was the lordship of the counts of Habsburg, named after their castle
  above the Aare in the Swiss Aargau. Their lands lay in the Aargau, the Frickgau, the Zürichgau
  and upper Alsace, built up from counties, advocacies over monasteries and bailiwicks. Rudolf IV,
  count from 1239 or 1240, greatly enlarged them, above all through the Kyburg inheritance of
  1264, which made him the strongest lord between the Rhine and the Alps. In 1273 the electors
  chose him King of the Romans. His defeat of Ottokar II of Bohemia in 1278 won him Austria and
  Styria, which he gave to his sons, and from then on the Swiss and Alsatian lands were governed
  together with Austria as the Habsburgs' western possessions.
