description: >-
  The Principality of Tver was a Russian monarchy centred on the upper Volga from 1246 to
  1485. It emerged from the divided lands of Vladimir and was ruled by descendants of Yaroslav
  Yaroslavich. Its river position supported trade, while refugees from regions devastated by
  Mongol invasion helped its population recover. Tver became Moscow's rival for leadership
  among northeastern Russian principalities. Its princes sought the title of grand prince of
  Vladimir, but appointments and conflicts depended heavily on the Golden Horde. The uprising
  against Mongol representatives in 1327 brought a destructive punitive campaign and
  strengthened Moscow. Later rulers tried to balance Moscow with Lithuanian support. Ivan III
  besieged Tver in 1485, forcing its last prince to flee and annexing his territory.
