description: >-
  The Eshaqvand dynasty ruled Bia-pas, the part of Gilan west of the Sefid-Rud river on the
  southwestern Caspian shore, from their home town of Fuman. They were Shafi'i Sunnis and claimed
  descent from the Sasanians or from the biblical Isaac. Gilan was divided among many local lords
  who paid tribute to the powers south of the Alborz but governed themselves, and the lords of
  Fuman were among the strongest of them. After a period of Ilkhanid rule from 1307 they
  recovered their independence, competed with the Tijaspi lords of Rasht and helped defeat the
  Kar-Kiya sayyids of eastern Gilan in 1389. Their ruler Jamshid Khan moved the capital to Rasht
  in 1572. Shah Abbas I conquered Gilan and ended the dynasty in 1592.
