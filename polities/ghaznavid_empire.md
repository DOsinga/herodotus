description: >-
  The Ghaznavid Empire was a Sunni Muslim monarchy founded by Sebuktegin in 977, initially
  centred on Ghazni in present day Afghanistan. Its rulers came from a Turkic military
  background but adopted Persian court culture and administration. Mahmud expanded the state
  across eastern Iran, Afghanistan, and parts of northern India, undertaking famous and
  destructive campaigns against Indian kingdoms and temples. His court supported poets and
  scholars, helping spread Persian literary traditions into the subcontinent. Defeat by the
  Seljuks at Dandanaqan in 1040 cost the Ghaznavids much of their western territory. Later
  pressure from Ghurids and other enemies drove them from Ghazni, but government continued
  from Lahore. Muhammad of Ghor captured that capital in 1186, ending the state and
  imprisoning its ruler.
