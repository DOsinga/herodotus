description: >-
  The French Third Republic governed France from 1870 to 1940, beginning when Napoleon III's empire collapsed during the Franco-Prussian War. Its early years brought defeat, territorial losses and the violent suppression of the Paris Commune. Although monarchists initially hoped to restore a king, parliamentary republican government took form through the constitutional laws of 1875. A president served as head of state, while ministers depended on elected deputies and the Senate. The republic expanded public education, separated church and state, and built a large overseas colonial empire. The Dreyfus affair exposed deep divisions over nationalism, antisemitism and justice. France survived the First World War at enormous cost. German victory in 1940 led parliament to grant Pétain powers, ending the republic's government.
