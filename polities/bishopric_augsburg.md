description: >-
  The Prince-Bishopric of Augsburg was the territory of the bishops of Augsburg in Swabia, along
  the Lech, the Wertach and the Danube. The bishops held the city of Augsburg until its citizens
  won their freedom in 1276, and they then governed from Dillingen on the Danube, which the last
  count of Dillingen, himself bishop of Augsburg, left to the see in 1258. Their lands lay in
  scattered pieces from the Danube to the Alps around Füssen. In 1551 Cardinal Otto Truchsess von
  Waldburg founded the university of Dillingen, which the Jesuits made a centre of the Counter-
  Reformation. The bishops shared the city of Augsburg with its Protestant council. The bishopric
  was secularised in 1803 and taken by Bavaria.
