description: >-
  Eritrea is a country on the Red Sea coast of northeastern Africa, governed from Asmara.
  Its present state began when the Eritrean People's Liberation Front took control in 1991
  after a long war against Ethiopia; internationally recognized independence followed a
  referendum in 1993. Formerly an Italian colony and then a British administered territory,
  Eritrea was federated with Ethiopia before annexation helped fuel the independence
  struggle. Its government became a highly centralized, single party presidential system,
  with national elections never held. A border war with Ethiopia from 1998 to 2000 shaped
  military service, politics, and migration. The coast, highlands, and western lowlands
  contain several language communities and both Christian and Muslim populations. Eritrea
  remains an independent state despite severe domestic restrictions.
