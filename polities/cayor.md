description: >-
  Cayor, or Kajoor, was the largest and strongest of the Wolof kingdoms that broke away from
  the Jolof Empire, in the region between the lower Senegal River and the Cap-Vert peninsula
  in present-day Senegal. Its ruler Amari Ngoone Sobel Fall defeated Jolof at the battle of
  Danki in 1549 and took the title damel. The damel governed from a traditional capital at
  Mboul with a council of electors and a powerful slave soldiery, the ceddo, and from the
  seventeenth century often ruled neighbouring Baol as well. Cayor traded captives, gum and
  grain with French Saint-Louis and Gorée. French plans for a railway from Dakar to Saint-
  Louis led to repeated wars with the damel Lat Dior. After he was killed in battle in 1886,
  France annexed the kingdom.
