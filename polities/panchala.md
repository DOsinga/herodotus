description: >-
  Panchala occupied territory in northern India's upper Ganges plain, mainly within modern Uttar
  Pradesh. Its beginnings are placed here around 1100 BCE, although the early chronology
  is approximate. Vedic texts describe a kingdom closely linked with the Kurus and
  important in the development of ritual and philosophical traditions. Its territory
  came to have northern and southern divisions, associated with Ahichchhatra and
  Kampilya. Kings such as Keshin Dalbhya and Pravahana Jaivali appear in the literary
  tradition, while the Mahabharata gives Panchala a prominent role through Drupada and
  Draupadi. These accounts require care as historical evidence. Later sources suggest a
  shift toward rule by a republican aristocracy. The Nanda rulers of Magadha absorbed
  Panchala around the middle of the fourth century BCE.
