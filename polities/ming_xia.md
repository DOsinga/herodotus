description: >-
  Ming Xia, officially the Great Xia, was a rebel state in Sichuan that lasted from 1360 to
  1371, at the end of the Yuan dynasty. Its founder Ming Yuzhen was a Tianwan general who
  conquered Sichuan in 1357 and governed it on his own. When Chen Youliang killed the
  Tianwan emperor in 1360, Ming refused to accept him and took the title King of Longshu. In
  1362 he proclaimed himself emperor of Xia, with Chongqing as his capital. Guided by the
  scholar Liu Zhen, he organised his government on the model of the ancient Zhou and won
  over the local gentry, but an attack on Yunnan failed. After his death in 1366 his young
  son reigned. Ming armies invaded in 1371 and took Chongqing and Chengdu.
