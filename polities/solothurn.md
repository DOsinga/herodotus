description: >-
  Solothurn was a city on the Aare in northwestern Switzerland, on the site of the Roman
  settlement of Salodurum. After the Zähringer died out in 1218 it became a free imperial city,
  and from 1252 a council of its citizens appears beside the old monastery of Saint Ursus. Rudolf
  of Habsburg confirmed its rights in 1276 and 1280. The city allied with Bern in 1295, withstood
  an Austrian siege in 1318 and a Habsburg attack in 1382, and in 1344 bought the right to appoint
  its own Schultheiss. Guilds gained places on its councils in the fourteenth century. Through the
  fifteenth century Solothurn bought the surrounding lordships that make up most of the present
  canton, and in 1481 it became a full member of the Confederacy.
