description: >-
  The Commune of Arezzo was the self-governing city state of Arezzo in eastern Tuscany,
  from the end of the eleventh century until Florence bought the city in 1384. Consuls and
  later podestà shared power with its bishops, and the city became the main Ghibelline
  power of Tuscany. Its army was crushed by the Florentines at Campaldino in 1289, where
  Bishop Guglielmino degli Ubertini died. In the early fourteenth century Bishop Guido
  Tarlati governed Arezzo as its lord and extended its territory, and after his death his
  brother sold the city to Florence in 1337. Arezzo regained its freedom in 1343, but
  factional wars and mercenary companies wore it down, and in 1384 Enguerrand de Coucy
  sold it to Florence.
