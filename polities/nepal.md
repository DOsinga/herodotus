description: >-
  Nepal became a federal republic in 2008, replacing the monarchy that had ruled the
  Himalayan country for centuries. Situated between India and China, it ranges from
  southern plains to some of the world's highest mountains. Kathmandu remains its
  capital. The republican settlement followed a decade of civil war, a peace
  agreement and a movement against direct royal rule. Former Maoist insurgents
  entered parliamentary politics alongside established parties. The constitution
  adopted in 2015 organized Nepal into seven provinces, with an elected parliament,
  prime minister and president. Debates over representation, federal boundaries and
  social inclusion have shaped the system. Agriculture, migrant labor and tourism
  remain important to its economy. Despite political crises and changing
  governments, the federal republic continues as Nepal's state.
