description: >-
  Dortmund was the only free imperial city in Westphalia, on the Hellweg, the old trade road
  between the Rhine and the Weser. It grew around a royal palace, and in the thirteenth century
  its council of merchant families governed the town, which became one of the leading members of
  the Hanseatic League and traded as far as England and the Baltic. In the great Dortmund feud of
  1388 and 1389 the city withstood a siege by the archbishop of Cologne and the count of Mark,
  though the war left it deeply in debt. Dortmund declined with the Hanse, took up the Reformation
  in the sixteenth century and suffered in the Thirty Years' War. In 1803 it was given to the
  prince of Nassau-Orange-Fulda.
