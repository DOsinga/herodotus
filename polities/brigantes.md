description: >-
  The Brigantes were a large British confederation centred on the Pennines and Yorkshire in
  northern England. Their political beginnings are uncertain; this record uses an estimated
  start of 50 BCE and ends at the Roman conquest begun in 71 CE. Their territory contained
  several smaller communities, and Stanwick's earthworks mark an important centre of power.
  Roman accounts identify Queen Cartimandua, who maintained an alliance with Rome and
  surrendered the resistance leader Caratacus in 51. Her former husband Venutius challenged
  her authority, turning family conflict into a struggle over Roman influence. In 69 his
  supporters gained control and Roman troops evacuated Cartimandua. The subsequent campaigns
  of Petillius Cerialis brought the kingdom under Roman rule, though resistance and further
  fighting continued afterwards.
