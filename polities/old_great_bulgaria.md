description: >-
  Old Great Bulgaria was a Bulgar confederation in the steppes north of the Black Sea during
  the seventh century. Around 632, Kubrat united groups including the Onogurs, Kutrigurs,
  and Utigurs under his leadership. His realm emerged amid the weakening of older steppe
  powers and maintained relations with Byzantium. Its government rested on a ruler and
  allied tribal groups rather than a uniform administration across fixed borders. Phanagoria
  is traditionally proposed as its centre, but a permanent capital there is not securely
  established. After Kubrat's death, his sons led separate groups while Khazar pressure
  broke the confederation apart. By about 668, the Khazars had subordinated Batbayan's
  remaining realm. Other Bulgar groups migrated toward the Danube, the Volga, and more
  western lands.
