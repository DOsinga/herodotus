description: >-
  The Spanish Monarchy joined Castile and Aragon under Ferdinand and Isabella in 1479, creating a
  composite monarchy rather than erasing their separate laws and institutions. Its rulers
  completed the conquest of Granada, supported Atlantic expansion and built an empire across the
  Americas. Habsburg succession brought additional European possessions, while councils, viceroys
  and local institutions governed widely scattered territories. Madrid eventually became the
  principal royal seat. Catholic identity shaped policy, alongside religious persecution and the
  expulsion of communities considered outside it. Overseas wealth supported costly wars but did
  not prevent financial crises or territorial losses. After the last Spanish Habsburg died, the
  War of the Spanish Succession brought Bourbon rule. Reforms completed in 1716 transformed the
  monarchy into more centralised Spain.
