description: >-
  The Emirate of Granada was the last Muslim state in medieval Iberia, ruled by the Nasrid
  dynasty from 1232 to 1492. Muhammad I established its ruling line during the collapse of
  Almohad authority and made Granada his capital in 1238. The emirate held a defensible
  southern territory with ports, fertile valleys, and populations of refugees from Christian
  conquests. Its hereditary rulers alternated warfare, tribute, and alliances with Castile
  and North African powers to preserve their government. Silk production and Mediterranean
  trade supported a court celebrated for literature and the Alhambra palaces. Dynastic
  struggles weakened Granada as Castile and Aragon coordinated their advance. After sustained warfare,
  the final ruler surrendered the capital in January 1492, ending independent Nasrid rule in
  Spain.
