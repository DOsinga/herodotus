description: >-
  Botswana is a landlocked republic in southern Africa, independent since 1966, when the British
  protectorate of Bechuanaland gained full independence. Its capital, Gaborone, grew near the southeastern
  border, where much of the population lives. A parliamentary system combines an elected National
  Assembly with a president chosen through parliament, while traditional leaders retain an advisory
  role. Seretse Khama led the first government and helped establish institutions that survived
  successive changes of leadership. Diamond mining transformed the economy and funded public services,
  alongside cattle farming and tourism associated with the Okavango Delta and Kalahari landscapes.
  Independence left difficult regional pressures from neighbouring minority regimes. Botswana has
  maintained its statehood through those changes, while confronting inequality, unemployment and the
  severe effects of HIV.
