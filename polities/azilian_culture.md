description: >-
  Azilian culture describes hunter gatherer communities in southern France and northern
  Spain as the last Ice Age gave way to warmer conditions. Named after the cave of Mas
  d'Azil in the French Pyrenees, it is represented here by an estimated span of 12000 to
  9500 BCE. Its characteristic finds include stone projectile points, flat bone harpoons,
  and pebbles painted with dots, stripes, or other abstract patterns. These objects mark
  changing technologies and artistic practices after the Magdalenian tradition, not a
  kingdom with known rulers. Cave and rock shelter deposits reveal groups adapting to
  changing landscapes and animal populations. Similar material elsewhere does not
  necessarily belong to the same culture. Chronologies vary by region and dating convention,
  making these boundaries approximate.
