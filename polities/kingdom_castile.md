description: >-
  Castile was a kingdom in northern and central Iberia from 1065 until its permanent union
  with León in 1230. It grew from a frontier county whose castles gave the region its
  name. Sancho II received Castile when Ferdinand I divided his lands among his children.
  Later kings sometimes ruled León as well, but the kingdoms retained separate identities.
  Royal power depended on nobles, church estates and towns with their own privileges.
  Alfonso VI captured Toledo in 1085, bringing a major centre of Arabic learning under
  Christian rule. Alfonso VIII helped defeat the Almohads at Las Navas de Tolosa in 1212.
  Ferdinand III inherited León in 1230, joining the two kingdoms into the larger Crown of
  Castile while keeping Castile's name.
