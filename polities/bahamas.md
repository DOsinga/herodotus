description: >-
  The Bahamas became independent from Britain in 1973, retaining the monarch as head of state within a parliamentary constitutional system. Its government sits in Nassau on New Providence, among Atlantic islands southeast of Florida. A governor-general represents the crown, while the prime minister and cabinet depend on parliamentary support. Independence followed decades of political change that challenged the dominance of the white merchant elite and brought majority rule in 1967. The country remained in the Commonwealth and developed an economy dependent on tourism and financial services. Its island geography links daily life with the sea but also exposes communities to destructive hurricanes. The independent state continues across elections and changing governments, with local institutions governing its domestic and foreign affairs.
