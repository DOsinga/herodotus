description: >-
  The Republic of Siena was a city state in southern Tuscany that governed itself from about
  1125 until 1555. Consuls led the early commune, and later a series of councils and ruling
  groups, known by the number of their members, took turns in power. Under the Nine, from
  1287 to 1355, the city built its cathedral, the Palazzo Pubblico and much of its walls, and
  its bankers and wool merchants traded across Europe. Siena was the great Ghibelline rival of
  Florence and beat it at Montaperti in 1260, but plague and factional strife weakened it
  after 1348. It gave itself to Gian Galeazzo Visconti from 1399 to 1404. In the Italian Wars
  it allied with France, and an imperial and Florentine army forced its surrender in 1555.
