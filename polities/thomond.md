description: >-
  Thomond was a Gaelic kingdom in northern Munster, Ireland, ruled by the O'Brien dynasty from
  the division of Munster in 1118. Its core lay around modern County Clare and the lower
  Shannon, with Clonroad becoming an important royal residence. Kings governed through
  alliances with local kindreds, while rival O'Brien branches competed for succession and
  support. Anglo-Norman settlement threatened the kingdom, especially through the de Clare
  lordship at Bunratty. Gaelic victory at Dysert O'Dea in 1318 helped end that expansion and
  sustain local rule. Thomond remained a distinct power despite pressure from neighbouring
  Irish rulers and the English crown. In 1543, Murrough O'Brien accepted Henry VIII's
  authority through surrender and regrant, becoming Earl of Thomond within the crown's Kingdom
  of Ireland.
