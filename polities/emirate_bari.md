description: >-
  The Emirate of Bari was a Muslim state in Apulia, centred on the port of Bari, from
  about 847 until the Carolingian emperor Louis II took the city in 871. Khalfun, a Berber
  commander of Aghlabid origin, seized Bari from the Byzantines on his own initiative, and
  his successors Mufarrag and Sawdan raided the Lombard principalities of Benevento,
  Salerno and Capua. Sawdan received formal investiture from the Abbasid caliph's governor
  in Egypt in 864, and under him Bari had a mosque, palaces and a busy trade in slaves,
  wine and pottery. Pilgrims and envoys passed through the city. After years of
  campaigning Louis II starved Bari into surrender with Byzantine naval help, ending the
  only Muslim state on the Italian mainland.
