name: "Duchy of Gaeta"
description: >-
  Gaeta was a coastal duchy in southern Italy whose local rulers governed from 867 to 1032 and
  again from 1064 to 1140. Docibilis established a dynasty as Byzantine influence weakened and
  the town escaped control from Naples. Its rulers used shifting alliances with neighbouring
  powers, Muslim forces, and the papacy to defend the harbour and surrounding lands. The
  family built a palace, encouraged commerce, and adopted the ducal title in the tenth
  century. Divisions among heirs weakened its territorial authority. Capua took control in
  1032, interrupting separate government. Local rule later returned before Norman dukes held
  their own court under changing overlords. Roger II incorporated Gaeta into the Kingdom of
  Sicily in 1140, ending its separate government and ducal autonomy.
