  Montenegro is a Balkan republic on the Adriatic coast, independent since 2006.
  Podgorica is its capital, while Cetinje preserves the country's royal and cultural
  traditions. The modern state emerged peacefully from the union of Serbia and
  Montenegro after a referendum narrowly passed the agreed threshold for
  independence. Parliament declared independence on 3 June 2006. A constitution
  adopted the following year established a parliamentary republic with an elected
  president and a government accountable to parliament. Mountains, coastal towns and
  a diverse population connect its history with both the Mediterranean and the
  inland Balkans. Montenegro joined NATO in 2017 and has pursued membership of the
  European Union. Its political life has included disputes over relations with
  Serbia, national identity and religious institutions.
