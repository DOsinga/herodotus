description: >-
  Cantref Mawr was the core of the Welsh lordship held by the descendants of the Lord Rhys at
  Dinefwr, recorded here from 1198 to 1277. After Rhys ap Gruffudd died in 1197 his sons
  fought over Deheubarth, and Rhys Gryg won Dinefwr, holding Cantref Mawr and, after Llywelyn
  the Great's settlement at Aberdyfi in 1216, Cantref Bychan as well. He and his descendants,
  Rhys Mechyll and Rhys Fychan, ruled the Tywi valley from Dinefwr, siding at times with the
  English crown and more often with the princes of Gwynedd. In the war of 1277 Edward I's
  forces took Dinefwr Castle, and the lands passed to the crown, which made them part of the
  county of Carmarthen.
