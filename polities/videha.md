description: >-
  Videha was an ancient kingdom in the eastern Gangetic plain, centred on Mithila in
  what is now northern Bihar and southern Nepal. Its monarchy is represented here from
  approximately 1100 to 700 BCE, although early chronology depends on literary
  traditions rather than dated royal records. The kingdom lay between rivers and the
  Himalayan foothills, east of Kosala. Kings called Janaka became prominent in Vedic
  literature as patrons of religious learning and philosophical discussion. Traditions
  associate the court with the sage Yajnavalkya, while the Ramayana places Sita's family
  in Videha. These texts preserve cultural memory rather than a straightforward
  political chronicle. The monarchy eventually gave way to an aristocratic republican
  order within the Vajjika League, while Mithila retained its regional identity.
