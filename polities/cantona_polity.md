description: >-
  Cantona was a large fortified settlement in the highlands of present-day Puebla, Mexico. Its
  beginnings are placed around 600 BCE here, although the early political chronology is less secure
  than the history of occupation. By its height between about 600 and 1000 CE, the city controlled
  an important trade route and worked obsidian from nearby volcanic deposits. Dense residential
  compounds, workshops, paved streets, defensive walls, and numerous ball courts reveal a highly
  organized urban community. An elevated ceremonial area housed temples and buildings associated
  with leading groups, but no surviving texts name its rulers or fully explain its government.
  Farmers and merchants sustained its urban economy. The city declined and was abandoned around
  1050; the causes and timing remain debated.
