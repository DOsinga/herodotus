description: >-
  Cheb, in German Eger, was a town on the Ohře in the far west of present Czechia, the centre of
  the Egerland, an imperial territory between Bohemia, Franconia and the Upper Palatinate.
  Frederick Barbarossa built a palace there, and the town grew rich on its position on the roads
  from Nuremberg to Prague. Ottokar II of Bohemia held it from 1266, but after his fall Rudolf of
  Habsburg restored it to the Empire, and from 1277 it was a free imperial city governed by its
  council. Wenceslaus II of Bohemia held it again from 1291 to 1304. In 1322 Louis the Bavarian
  pledged Cheb and the Egerland to John of Bohemia, with a promise of autonomy, and the pledge was
  never redeemed.
