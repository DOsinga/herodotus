description: >-
  The Lutician Federation united several West Slavic peoples in what is now northeastern Germany after the uprising of 983. Its core members were the Redarians, Tollensians, Circipanians and Kessinians. Unlike neighbouring Christian monarchies, their government worked through assemblies and agreement among leading groups rather than a single king. Religious authority centred on the sanctuary of Rethra, whose location remains disputed. The federation expelled imperial officials and missionaries, then alternated warfare with alliances, including cooperation with Emperor Henry II against Poland. Its members could pay tribute without surrendering their own decisions. Rivalry within the alliance eventually undermined collective rule. Civil war in 1056 and 1057 brought outside intervention and left the member peoples governing separately, ending the united polity represented here.
