description: >-
  Kadi Burhan al-Din's state occupied central Anatolia around Sivas and Kayseri between
  1381 and 1398. Its founder had served the Eretnid rulers as judge, minister, and
  regent before seizing their lands and proclaiming himself sultan. He issued coins and
  had the Friday prayer delivered in his name, asserting rule over towns and Turkmen
  and Mongol tribal communities. His court combined Islamic scholarship with poetry in
  Turkic, Persian, and Arabic. Survival required shifting alliances and campaigns
  against local emirs, the Ottomans, Karamanids, Mamluks, and Aq Qoyunlu. He also
  resisted demands for submission from Timur. Burhan al-Din was killed during conflict
  with the Aq Qoyunlu leader Kara Yuluk Osman. His young heir could not hold the realm,
  which passed to Ottoman control.
