description: >-
  The Kingdom of Kongoda lay along the coast of Odisha in eastern India, centred on the Ganjam
  region. Its Shailodbhava rulers had earlier served stronger kings, but this record begins with
  Madhavaraja II's assertion of royal authority around 620. Inscriptions present a hereditary
  monarchy claiming prestigious titles, performing royal sacrifices, and granting land to
  religious beneficiaries. The dynasty's origin legend described an ancestor emerging from a
  rock, linking kingship with the region's sacred landscape. Xuanzang visited in the seventh
  century and described fortified towns, strong soldiers, and valuable coastal goods. Succession
  disputes and weakening authority followed later reigns. The kingdom gave way to Bhauma-Kara
  power, conventionally around 736, though its final chronology is uncertain and other dates
  have been proposed.
