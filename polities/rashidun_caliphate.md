description: >-
  The Rashidun Caliphate arose in Arabia after Muhammad's death in 632 and lasted until 661. Abu
  Bakr, Umar, Uthman, and Ali successively led the Muslim community through consultation or
  nomination rather than an established hereditary dynasty. Medina was the early governing
  centre; Ali later moved to Kufa. Armies reunited Arabia and conquered extensive Byzantine and
  Sasanian territories, including Syria, Egypt, Iraq, and Iran. Governors, taxation, and military
  settlements helped administer an empire expanding far beyond its Arabian base. Disputes over
  authority culminated in Uthman's killing and the first Muslim civil war. Ali's assassination
  did not immediately settle the succession. Hasan briefly governed before reaching an agreement
  with Muawiya in 661, opening the Umayyad period and ending the government represented here.
