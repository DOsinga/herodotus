description: >-
  The Kar-Kiya were a dynasty of Zaydi sayyids who governed eastern Gilan, the Biya-pish
  region on the southwestern coast of the Caspian Sea, from the 1370s to 1592. Sayyid Ali
  Kiya, backed by Zaydi followers in the mountains, took Lahijan and established a family
  government there. The Kar-Kiya fought the Ishaqvand rulers of western Gilan over Rasht and
  the coastal plain and extended their influence into the Daylam highlands. Mirza Ali of
  Lahijan sheltered the young Ismail, the future founder of the Safavid state, between 1494
  and 1499. Later rulers adopted Twelver Shi'ism and ruled as Safavid vassals, keeping their
  own government and revenues. Khan Ahmad Khan's resistance led Shah Abbas I to conquer
  Gilan in 1592 and end the dynasty.
