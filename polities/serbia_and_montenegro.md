description: >-
  Serbia and Montenegro was a state union in southeastern Europe from 2003 to 2006,
  succeeding the Federal Republic of Yugoslavia. The constitutional settlement preserved
  common international representation and defence while leaving most domestic powers with
  the two member republics. Belgrade housed union institutions, and Svetozar Marović served
  as its president. The arrangement reflected Montenegro's growing distance from Serbia,
  including separate economic policies and its use of the euro rather than the Serbian
  dinar. Kosovo, formally within Serbia, remained under international administration. The
  charter allowed either republic to reconsider membership after three years. Montenegro
  voted for independence in a referendum in May 2006 and declared it in June. Serbia then
  continued separately, bringing the last Yugoslav state union to an end.
