description: >-
  The Cimmerians were mobile communities associated with the northern Black Sea steppe
  during the early first millennium BCE. This record uses approximately 1000 to 650 BCE
  for their presence there, with a representative point rather than a capital. They are
  known through surviving external written accounts and disputed connections with
  archaeological remains, not through their own surviving records. Assyrian sources later
  describe Cimmerian groups and named leaders campaigning in the Caucasus and Anatolia.
  Greek authors connected their movements with displacement by the Scythians. Horse
  riding, pastoral life, and warfare feature prominently in reconstructions, but assigning
  particular finds or one political structure to every group is uncertain. Their departure
  from the steppe did not immediately end all Cimmerian activity farther south.
