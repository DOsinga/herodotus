description: >-
  The Bishopric of Ösel–Wiek was a church principality in western Estonia, covering the
  coastal district of Wiek and much of the islands of Ösel and Dagö. It was founded in 1228,
  when a papal legate created the see and the German king made it a principality of the
  Empire. The bishop's seat moved from Leal to Old Pernau in 1251 and to the castle of Hapsal
  in 1279, and later bishops lived at Arensburg on Ösel. The bishops governed through German
  vassals and bailiffs, and shared the islands with the Livonian Order. The Estonian
  islanders rose against their German lords several times, most seriously during the St
  George's Night Uprising of 1343. From 1435 the bishopric was a member of the Livonian
  Confederation.
