description: >-
  The Sufi dynasty was a Turkic family of Mongol Onggirat descent that ruled Khwarazm, the oasis
  of the Amu Darya delta south of the Aral Sea, from the old capital of Urgench. Its founder
  Naghday had been a commander of the Golden Horde khan Öz Beg before he turned to Sufism and
  settled in Khwarazm. During the civil wars that shook the Golden Horde after 1359, his son
  Husayn Sufi made Khwarazm independent around 1361. The Sufis then quarrelled with Timur over
  the southern districts of Khiva and Kath, which Timur claimed for the Chagatai realm. Timur
  campaigned against them repeatedly from 1372, and in 1388 he destroyed Urgench and annexed
  Khwarazm, though members of the family later served there as Timurid governors.
