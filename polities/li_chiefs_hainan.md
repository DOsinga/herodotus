description: >-
  The Li, now called Hlai, are the Kra–Dai speaking people of Hainan Island off the southern
  coast of China. The Han dynasty conquered the island in 110 BCE and set up the commanderies
  of Zhuya and Dan'er, but repeated revolts against its officials led the court to abandon
  Hainan in 46 BCE. For almost six centuries afterwards the island was governed by its own
  village chiefs and headmen. Chinese writers describe the Li of the south as living in
  unwalled villages in the hills, without overall lords, and later dynasties kept a Zhuya
  commandery only on the mainland coast opposite. Lady Xian, a chief of the mainland Li allied
  with the Liang dynasty, brought the island back under Chinese administration about 535.
