description: >-
  The County of Kyburg was the lordship of the counts of Kyburg, named after their castle near
  Winterthur in northeastern Switzerland. A cadet line of the counts of Dillingen, they inherited
  much of the Lenzburg lands in 1173 and a large share of the Zähringer inheritance in 1218, which
  brought them towns such as Fribourg, Burgdorf and Thun. By the middle of the thirteenth century
  they were the strongest lords between the Rhine and the Alps, holding Winterthur, Zug, Baden and
  the advocacy over Glarus. Count Hartmann IV ruled the east and his nephew Hartmann V the west
  from Burgdorf. Hartmann V died in 1263 and his uncle in 1264, and the Kyburg lands passed to
  Hartmann IV's nephew, Rudolf of Habsburg.
