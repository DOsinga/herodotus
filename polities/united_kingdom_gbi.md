description: >-
  The United Kingdom of Great Britain and Ireland joined kingdoms in 1801 and included
  all Ireland until 1922. London housed its monarch, parliament, and government;
  elected ministers exercised power within a constitutional monarchy. Industrial
  growth, finance, and naval strength supported a empire, including government of
  British India through a viceroy after 1858. Britain fought Napoleon and later
  expanded through wars and colonial conquest. Parliamentary reforms widened voting
  rights, though political inclusion advanced unevenly. Ireland's Great Famine, land
  disputes, and campaigns for home rule exposed tensions within the union. The First
  World War brought victory at cost. Irish rebellion and the war of independence led to
  the Irish Free State in 1922, while Northern Ireland remained within the continuing
  United Kingdom.
