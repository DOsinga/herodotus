description: >-
  The Kingdom of the Aures occupied the mountain region of eastern Algeria after Roman authority fragmented in North Africa. Its estimated beginning in 484 follows Berber revolts against the Vandals. Masties presented himself as a Christian ruler of both Berber and Roman subjects, using Roman titles to support his authority. His inscription near Arris is a source for this early kingdom. Later, Iabdas resisted the Byzantines, whose general Solomon conquered the region in 539. Local rule resumed following rebellion in 544. During the Arab conquest, Queen Dihya led resistance from the eastern mountain country. Her defeat and death conventionally mark the kingdom's end in 703, although accounts differ over the date. Its history combines Berber leadership with surviving Roman traditions.
