description: >-
  The Qarmatian state governed eastern Arabia from 899 to 1077, centred on the oasis of
  al-Ahsa in the historical region called Bahrain. Abu Sa'id al-Jannabi founded its ruling
  house within a wider Ismaili religious movement that opposed Abbasid authority and
  rejected Fatimid claims. His successors combined religious leadership with military power,
  supported by agricultural estates, tribute, and enslaved labour. They offered assistance
  and interest free loans to members of their community, but modern labels such as socialism
  obscure the society's inequalities. Abu Tahir's forces attacked Mecca in 930 and carried
  away the Black Stone, shocking other Muslims. Raids and tribute collection ranged beyond
  the state's settled territory. Defeats and coastal revolts weakened it before Uyunid
  rulers finally replaced its government.
