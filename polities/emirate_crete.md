description: >-
  The Emirate of Crete was an independent Muslim island state founded by Andalusian exiles during the ninth century. Its conquest is dated approximately to 824, although other accounts place it several years later. Abu Hafs and his descendants ruled from fortified Chandax, the ancestor of modern Heraklion. They recognised Abbasid authority in principle without becoming ordinary provincial governors. Crete's position across eastern Mediterranean sea routes made it an base for fleets raiding Byzantine shores. Trade and agriculture also supported its economy; surviving accounts provide less detail about everyday government than about warfare. Repeated Byzantine expeditions failed to eliminate the emirate, including a partial reconquest in the 840s. Nikephoros Phokas finally captured Chandax in 961, restoring Byzantine government throughout the island.
