description: >-
  The Median kingdom developed in western Iran, with Ecbatana as its remembered royal
  centre. Its beginning around 678 BCE is an estimate, and accounts of its earliest
  kings depend heavily on later Greek writing. The Medes had previously lived among
  communities exposed to Assyrian campaigns and tribute demands. Under Cyaxares, their
  forces allied with Babylon and helped destroy the Assyrian Empire, including Nineveh
  in 612 BCE. Median power then became important across the Iranian plateau and toward
  Anatolia. How tightly these lands were governed remains debated, so a uniform imperial
  administration should not be assumed. Cyrus of Persia defeated Astyages in 550 BCE and
  absorbed the kingdom. Median nobles and soldiers subsequently held prominent roles in
  the new Persian Empire.
