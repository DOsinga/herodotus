description: >-
  Badung was a Balinese kingdom in the south of the island, around the modern city of Denpasar. Its
  rulers descended from the Arya Kenceng lords installed after the Majapahit conquest, and it became
  one of the independent kingdoms that emerged when the Gelgel kingdom broke up in the late
  seventeenth century. Several branches of the family ruled from different palaces, notably
  Pemecutan and, from 1788, the new palace of Denpasar. Badung traded through Kuta, where the Danish
  merchant Mads Lange later acted for its kings, and it defeated its rival Mengwi in 1891. A Dutch
  claim over a wrecked ship led to an expedition in 1906, when the king and his court died in a
  puputan and the Dutch took over.
