description: >-
  The Erivan Khanate was a khanate of the Iranian lands centred on the fortress and town of
  Erivan, today Yerevan, covering most of central Armenia and neighbouring districts now in
  Turkey and Azerbaijan. It grew out of the Safavid province of Chokhur-e Sa'd. After Nader
  Shah's death in 1747 Iranian authority north of the Aras collapsed, and the khans of Erivan
  governed on their own, though they had to pay tribute to King Heraclius II of Kartli-Kakheti,
  who attacked them whenever they held it back. Karim Khan Zand's nominal restoration of Iranian
  authority changed little. Mohammad Khan of Erivan sought contact with Russia after Georgia
  became a Russian protectorate in 1783. Agha Mohammad Khan Qajar's campaign of 1795 brought the
  khanate back under the Iranian crown.
