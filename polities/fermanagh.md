description: >-
  The Kingdom of Fermanagh governed the lakes and surrounding lands of
  present-day County Fermanagh in northern Ireland. This record follows its
  independent government from 1200 to 1607, although the kingdom's earlier roots
  lay within Airgíalla. Gaelic rulers held power through dynastic leadership and
  customary succession, with the Maguire family dominant from the late
  thirteenth century. Lisnaskea and its nearby inauguration site were important
  royal places, while Enniskillen became a stronghold associated with Maguire
  power. The kingdom faced rivalry from neighboring Irish rulers and growing
  English intervention. Hugh Maguire joined resistance to English rule during
  the Nine Years' War. After defeat, his successor left in the Flight of the
  Earls in 1607, and Fermanagh passed into the crown's Kingdom of Ireland.
