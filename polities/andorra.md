description: >-
  Andorra occupies mountain valleys in the eastern Pyrenees between France and Spain. Its
  political continuity is conventionally traced to the 1278 agreement sharing authority between
  the bishop of Urgell and the count of Foix. The secular share later passed to the French crown
  and ultimately to France's president, preserving the unusual arrangement of two co-princes.
  Local institutions and parish communities nevertheless developed their own administration. The
  constitution of 1993 established a parliamentary system, with an elected General Council and a
  government responsible to it, while retaining the co-princes as heads of state. Andorra la
  Vella is the capital and Catalan the official language. Tourism, winter sports, commerce, and
  finance transformed a rural economy without ending the country's separate political identity.
