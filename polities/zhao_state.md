name: "Zhao (state)"
description: >-
  Zhao was a monarchy in northern China that emerged from the division of Jin. This record
  begins in 453 BCE, when the Zhao, Han and Wei families defeated the Zhi clan and
  governed their territories separately; Zhou recognition followed in 403 BCE. Centred eventually on Handan, Zhao occupied
  parts of present Shanxi, Hebei and adjoining northern regions. King Wuling strengthened its army by adopting
  mounted archery and clothing associated with neighbouring steppe peoples. Zhao became one of the leading Warring
  States, but Qin inflicted a devastating defeat at Changping in 260 BCE. Generals such as Li
  Mu later prolonged resistance. Qin captured Handan in 228 BCE, while a royal remnant survived at
  Dai until its final conquest in 222 BCE.
