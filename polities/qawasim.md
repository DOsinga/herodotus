description: >-
  The Qasimi Sheikhdom was the maritime state of the Qawasim, a tribal confederation on the
  southern shore of the Persian Gulf centred on Ras al-Khaimah and Sharjah. Its ruling family
  rose under Rahma bin Matar in the 1720s, as the Ya'ariba imams of Oman declined. The
  Qawasim also held ports on the Persian coast such as Lingeh, joined the Wahhabi cause as
  allies of Diriyah and fought Oman for control of Gulf shipping. The British called them
  pirates and attacked Ras al-Khaimah in 1809 and again in 1819, after which the sheikhs
  signed the General Maritime Treaty of 1820. They still ran their own affairs until the
  Exclusive Agreement of 1892 placed their foreign relations under Britain. Sharjah and Ras
  al-Khaimah went separate ways in 1869.
