description: >-
  The Commune of Verona was the self-governing city state of Verona on the Adige, from the
  first recorded consuls in 1136 until Mastino della Scala founded the Scaliger lordship
  in 1262. Verona guarded the road from the Brenner into Italy, and in 1164 it led the
  Veronese League of eastern cities against Frederick Barbarossa, a forerunner of the
  Lombard League. Consuls and then podestà governed the city and its territory between
  Lake Garda and the Veneto. Faction wars between the Montecchi and their rivals brought
  Ezzelino da Romano to power in 1226, and he ruled Verona harshly until his death in
  1259. The commune then made Mastino della Scala captain of the people, the first step
  towards the Scaliger state.
