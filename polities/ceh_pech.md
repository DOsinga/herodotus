description: >-
  Ceh Pech was a Maya province of the northern Yucatán Peninsula between Mérida and the
  coast, ruled by the Pech family with its capital at Motul. It emerged as one of the
  independent provinces formed after the fall of Mayapan around 1441, under a halach uinik,
  or supreme lord, with lesser lords governing towns such as Conkal, Chicxulub and Mocochá.
  Its territory included coastal salt works, and families such as the Ek, Chable and May
  were prominent alongside the Pech. The Pech lords received Francisco de Montejo the
  Younger in 1541 and submitted to the Spanish the next year, keeping positions as town
  governors. Their documents, among them the Chronicle of Chicxulub, are some of the few
  Maya accounts of the conquest.
