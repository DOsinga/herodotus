description: >-
  The County of Maine was a feudal county in western France centred on Le Mans, recorded here
  from about 900 to 1051 and again from 1069 to 1110. Hugh I founded a line of hereditary
  counts who held Maine between the powerful counts of Anjou and dukes of Normandy. Herbert
  Wakedog fought off Angevin pressure, but after Hugh IV's death in 1051 Geoffrey Martel of
  Anjou took Le Mans. William of Normandy conquered the county in 1063, and in 1069 the
  citizens of Le Mans rose against the Normans in one of the first urban communes in France
  and recognised Hugh V as count. Elias I defended Maine against William Rufus, and when he
  died in 1110 his son-in-law Fulk V of Anjou inherited it.
