description: >-
  The Turpan Khanate was a Muslim monarchy in eastern Central Asia, centred on the oasis
  of Turpan in present Xinjiang. Emerging from divided Moghulistan in 1487, it was ruled
  by descendants of the Chagatai Mongol dynasty. Ahmad Alaq and his son Mansur developed
  an eastern kingdom distinct from the later Yarkent Khanate in the western Tarim Basin.
  Control of Hami brought repeated conflict with Ming China, mixing military campaigns
  with negotiations over tribute and trade. Mansur eventually reconciled with his brother
  Sultan Said, allowing their neighbouring courts to coexist. After Mansur's death,
  succession disputes weakened Turpan. Yarkent forces captured its ruler in 1570 and took
  control, although later embassies continued to use Turpan's name in dealings with the
  Chinese court.
