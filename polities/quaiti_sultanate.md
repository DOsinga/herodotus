description: >-
  The Qu'aiti Sultanate was a state on the Hadhramaut coast of southern Arabia, founded by the
  Yafa'i family of Umar ibn Awadh al-Qu'aiti, whose sons had made their fortune as jamadars, or
  commanders, in the army of the Nizam of Hyderabad. With money and men from India they took
  the inland town of Shibam from the Kathiri in 1858 and the port of al-Shihr in 1867. They
  then fought the Kasadi naqibs of Mukalla, who ceded half the town in 1873; with British
  backing the Qu'aiti jamadar took the rest in 1881 and the naqib went into exile. The family
  kept close ties to Hyderabad, where several of its rulers lived. On 1 May 1888 Abdallah ibn
  Umar and his brother Awadh signed a protectorate treaty with Britain.
