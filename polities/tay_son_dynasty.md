description: >-
  The Tây Sơn state arose from a rebellion in central Vietnam led by three Nguyễn
  brothers, unrelated to the southern Nguyễn ruling family. It governed from Đồ Bàn by
  1776, before Nguyễn Nhạc's imperial coronation in 1778. Its armies overthrew the
  southern Nguyễn lords, defeated the northern Trịnh regime, and ended the Lê monarchy.
  Nguyễn Huệ, ruling as Quang Trung, defeated a Qing invasion in 1789 and then secured
  Chinese recognition through diplomacy. Government took monarchical form, but the
  brothers' separate bases and competing interests complicated unity. Quang Trung's
  death in 1792 left a young successor and renewed court struggles. Nguyễn Ánh recovered
  the south and conquered the Tây Sơn territory in 1802, founding the Nguyễn dynasty
  that succeeded it.
