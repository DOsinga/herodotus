description: >-
  The Commune of Orvieto was the self-governing city state of Orvieto in southern Umbria,
  from the twelfth century until Cardinal Albornoz brought it under direct papal rule in
  1354. From its rock above the Paglia valley the commune governed a territory that
  reached towards Lake Bolsena and the Maremma, and it fought Siena over the Val di
  Chiana. The city was formally part of the Papal States and popes often lived there, but
  its consuls, podestà and captains of the people ran its affairs. It began its great
  cathedral in 1290. The Guelph Monaldeschi and the Ghibelline Filippeschi fought for
  control, and in the fourteenth century the Monaldeschi ruled as lords until the papal
  reconquest.
