description: >-
  Aachen was a free imperial city in the Rhineland, governed by its own council from the end
  of direct royal government in Germany in 1250 until French revolutionary troops occupied it
  in 1794. Charlemagne's palace chapel made the town the coronation place of German kings
  until 1531, and pilgrims came every seven years to see the relics in its treasury. Frederick
  Barbarossa granted the town its privileges in 1166. A council drawn from merchant families
  and, later, the guilds ran the city, whose wealth rested on cloth, brass working and its hot
  springs. Aachen was too small to influence the policies of its neighbours, Jülich and the
  Spanish Netherlands. A great fire in 1656 and long quarrels between Catholics and
  Protestants slowed its growth.
