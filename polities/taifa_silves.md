description: >-
  The Taifa of Silves was a Muslim kingdom in the western Algarve, in present southern Portugal,
  with its capital at Silves, known in Arabic as Shilb. It emerged from the civil war that broke
  up the Caliphate of Córdoba after 1009, when local commanders and families took power in their
  own districts. From 1048 the Banu Muzayn family ruled it, while Seville's ambitions grew. From
  the 1040s the expanding taifa of Seville under al-Mu'tadid absorbed one neighbour after another.
  Silves fell to Seville in 1063, and its future king al-Mu'tamid governed it as a young prince.
  When Almoravid rule collapsed in the 1140s, Silves governed itself again, under rulers linked to
  Ibn Qasi's revolt, until the Almohads took the Algarve in 1150.
