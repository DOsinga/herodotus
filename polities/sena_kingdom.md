name: "Sena Kingdom"
description: >-
  The Sena Kingdom ruled much of Bengal from Hemanta Sena's assumption of kingship in 1095 until
  approximately 1230. Its rulers came from a family with southern Indian connections that had served
  the Pala kings before taking their lands. Vijaya Sena expanded the kingdom, while Ballala Sena and
  Lakshmana Sena consolidated a Hindu monarchy across the Bengal delta. The court supported Sanskrit
  poets, including Jayadeva, and promoted Brahmanical religious and social institutions. Land grants
  recorded on copper plates illuminate royal patronage and local administration. Bakhtiyar Khalji
  captured Nadia in 1204, forcing Lakshmana Sena eastward. This conquest did not immediately end Sena
  government: his successors continued ruling around Bikrampur, although their authority and territory
  were much reduced during the kingdom's final decades.
