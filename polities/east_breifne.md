description: >-
  East Breifne was a kingdom centred on Cavan in present-day northern Ireland, existing separately from 1256 to 1607. It emerged from the division of Breifne following conflict between the O'Reilly and O'Rourke families. The O'Reilly rulers governed through kinship alliances, subordinate chiefs, and customary law. Kings normally came from the ruling family through tanistry, which selected an adult successor rather than automatically favouring the eldest son. Cavan developed as a market town linking Gaelic territories with the English-controlled Pale. Rivalries within the ruling family and changing relations with neighbouring kingdoms shaped its politics. Tudor attempts to impose English institutions weakened autonomy, while the Nine Years' War disrupted the region. Crown rule finally replaced the kingdom in the early seventeenth century.
