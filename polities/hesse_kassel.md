description: >-
  Hesse-Kassel was a German principality centred on Kassel, created in 1567 when Philip of
  Hesse's lands were divided among his sons. Its hereditary landgraves governed the
  northern territorial share and later expanded through inheritance and war. Calvinism
  shaped its alliances during the Thirty Years' War, when Regent Amalie Elisabeth defended
  the state's interests and secured gains at the Peace of Westphalia. The rulers
  maintained an unusually large army and earned substantial income by supplying troops to
  foreign powers. Hessian soldiers became especially familiar in Britain’s war against the
  American colonies. The landgrave became an elector in 1803. Napoleon's occupation in
  1806 ended this government; restoration followed in 1813, after Kassel had served as the
  capital of his Kingdom of Westphalia.
