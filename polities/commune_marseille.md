description: >-
  The Commune of Marseille was the self-governing lower city of Marseille, recorded here from
  1214 to 1252. The viscounts of Marseille sold their rights to the citizens piece by piece,
  and from 1212 to 1218 a lay confraternity of the Holy Spirit governed the town before a
  council and rectors, and later a podestà, took over. The bishop and the cathedral chapter
  kept the upper town. Marseille's merchants traded across the Mediterranean and held
  privileges in the crusader ports of the Levant. The commune resisted Count Raymond Berengar
  IV of Provence and recognised his suzerainty only in 1243. In 1252 it submitted to Charles
  of Anjou, who confirmed some of its liberties in the Chapters of Peace of 1257.
