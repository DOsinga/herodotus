name: French Second Republic
description: >-
  The French Second Republic governed France from 1848 to 1852 after the February Revolution
  overthrew Louis Philippe's monarchy. Based in Paris, it established an president and
  National Assembly and introduced universal male suffrage. The provisional government
  abolished slavery in French colonies and attempted to address unemployment through
  national workshops. Their closure helped trigger the June Days uprising, which the army
  crushed, deepening divisions between workers and conservative republicans. Louis Napoleon
  Bonaparte won the presidential election in December 1848, drawing support from his family
  name and promises of order. Conflict with the assembly and constitutional limits on
  reelection led him to seize power in December 1851. A year later he became Napoleon III,
  ending the republic and establishing the Second Empire.
