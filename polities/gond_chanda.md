description: >-
  The Gond Kingdom of Chanda was one of the main Gond states of central India, in the Wardha
  valley of present eastern Maharashtra. Local tradition credits Bhim Ballal Singh of the
  Atram clan with founding it in the fourteenth century, with a first capital at Sirpur and a
  stronghold at Manikgarh. A later king, Khandkya Ballal Shah, built the fort of Ballarpur and
  founded the city of Chandrapur, which became the capital. The Gond kings built irrigation
  works and a settled revenue system. The Ain-i-Akbari describes the kingdom as independent,
  but it later paid tribute to the Mughals after they formed the province of Berar. It
  quarrelled with the Gonds of Deogarh. Raghoji Bhonsle of Nagpur conquered Chanda in 1751.
