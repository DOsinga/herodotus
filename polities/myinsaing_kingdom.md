description: >-
  The Myinsaing Kingdom governed central Burma from 1297 to 1313 after the collapse of Pagan's empire. Three brothers, Athinkhaya, Yazathingyan, and Thihathu, established a joint government based on fortified centers in the Kyaukse region. They removed the Pagan king Kyawswa while retaining a nominal royal figure, making their own military leadership decisive. Their defenses resisted a Mongol invasion in 1300 and 1301, and Mongol troops eventually withdrew from Burma. The brothers then strengthened control over the central Irrawaddy valley, but did not restore all Pagan's former territories. Their partnership ended as the elder brothers died and Thihathu claimed kingship. His foundation of Pinya in 1313 marked the successor government. Its later division into Pinya and Sagaing prolonged central Burma's fragmentation.
