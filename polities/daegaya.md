description: >-
  Daegaya was a kingdom centered on present Goryeong in southeastern Korea, traditionally dated from 42 until its conquest in 562. It was one of the Gaya states, not a single government ruling the entire confederacy. The traditional royal genealogy lists sixteen kings, although only a few are known. King Haji's embassy to Southern Qi in 479 securely demonstrates a royal court conducting diplomacy. Iron production helped Daegaya become an important regional power during the fifth century. Rich tombs and historical references show a strongly ranked society. Its rulers negotiated and fought with Baekje and Silla, whose expansion increasingly constrained the Gaya states. Silla conquered Daegaya in 562, ending its royal government and incorporating its territory into the growing Silla kingdom.
