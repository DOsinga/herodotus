description: >-
  The Tsardom of Russia was the monarchy proclaimed when Ivan IV took the title of tsar
  in 1547. Centred initially on Moscow, it expanded across the Volga region and Siberia
  while fighting Poland, Lithuania, Sweden and the Ottoman Empire. Ivan's conquests of
  Kazan and Astrakhan opened routes eastward, but repression and prolonged warfare also
  damaged the country. Dynastic collapse produced the Time of Troubles, with rival
  claimants, foreign intervention and famine. Michael Romanov's election in 1613
  established a new ruling house. Seventeenth century government strengthened royal
  authority and bound peasants more firmly to their landlords. Peter the Great rebuilt
  the army, navy and administration. Victory over Sweden secured Baltic access, and in
  1721 he proclaimed the Russian Empire in succession.
