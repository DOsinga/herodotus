name: Muzaffarid state
description: >-
  The Muzaffarid state emerged in central and southern Iran after the Ilkhanate lost
  effective authority in 1335. Its ruling family had already gained power around Yazd before
  Mubariz al Din Muhammad expanded into Kerman, Fars, and neighbouring regions. Defeating
  the Injuids brought Shiraz under Muzaffarid rule and made it an important royal centre.
  The state was a Sunni Muslim monarchy whose court supported Persian and Arabic literature.
  Shah Shuja, who overthrew and imprisoned his father, became particularly associated with
  literary patronage and the poet Hafez. Rival princes nevertheless fought repeatedly over
  provincial governments and succession. Timur exploited these divisions and invaded. After
  Shah Mansur's defeat in 1393, Timur executed most surviving Muzaffarid princes and
  incorporated their lands into his empire.
