description: >-
  The Burgundian State joined lands in eastern France and the Low Countries under the Valois
  dukes of Burgundy between 1384 and 1482. It was a collection of duchies and counties
  sharing a ruler, not a kingdom with uniform laws. Inheritance, marriage, purchase and
  conquest brought wealthy Flemish and Brabantine towns into the union. Their trade helped
  finance a lavish court, artistic patronage and powerful armies. The dukes built common
  financial and judicial institutions while bargaining or fighting with local estates and
  cities. Charles the Bold sought a royal crown and a continuous territory, but died
  fighting at Nancy in 1477. His daughter Mary married Maximilian of Austria; after her
  death, the surviving northern lands developed into the later Habsburg Netherlands.
