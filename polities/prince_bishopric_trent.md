description: >-
  The Prince-Bishopric of Trent was a church state in the Adige valley and the mountains of
  present Trentino. Emperor Conrad II gave the bishops of Trento the rights of a count in 1027,
  to secure the road from Germany to Italy over the Brenner. The bishops, elected by the
  cathedral chapter, governed the city and much of the valley from the Castello del
  Buonconsiglio, though their advocates, the counts of Tyrol, held much power and later bound
  the see to Tyrol by treaty. Trent hosted the great church council of 1545 to 1563, which
  shaped the Catholic Reformation. The principality survived the wars of the eighteenth
  century but was secularised in 1803, when it passed to Austria and was joined to Tyrol.
