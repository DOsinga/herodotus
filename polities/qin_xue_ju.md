description: >-
  Xue Ju's Qin was a short-lived Chinese state in eastern Gansu, founded during the
  breakdown of Sui rule in 617. Xue Ju, a wealthy local militia commander, seized
  officials and attracted rebels before claiming first a princely title and then the
  imperial throne. His son Xue Rengao captured Tianshui, which became the capital. The new
  monarchy sought to expand towards Chang'an, competing with the rising Tang dynasty and
  other regional contenders. Qin armies inflicted a defeat on Tang forces in 618, but Xue
  Ju died of illness before exploiting the victory. Xue Rengao succeeded him and continued
  the war. Li Shimin defeated the successor, who surrendered in November 618, ending Qin's
  government and bringing its lands under Tang imperial rule.
