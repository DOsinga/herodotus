description: >-
  The Republic of Formosa was a short-lived state on Taiwan, proclaimed on 23 May 1895 to
  resist the island's cession to Japan under the Treaty of Shimonoseki, and ended on 21
  October that year. Its leaders were Qing officials and local gentry, with the governor Tang
  Jingsong as president at Taipei. They hoped that a republic would draw foreign support and
  still professed loyalty to the Qing emperor. Japanese troops landed near Keelung on 29 May,
  and Tang fled to the mainland in early June, after which Taipei fell. The Black Flag general
  Liu Yongfu then led the resistance from Tainan, refusing the presidency but organising its
  defence. Japanese forces fought their way south over five months and took Tainan in
  October.
