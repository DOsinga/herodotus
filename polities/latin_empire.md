description: >-
  The Latin Empire was a crusader monarchy based in Constantinople from 1204 to 1261.
  Leaders of the Fourth Crusade created it after capturing and plundering the Byzantine
  capital, choosing Baldwin of Flanders as emperor. Western nobles received territories and
  fiefs, while Venice secured commercial privileges and ports. The new rulers promoted Latin
  Catholic institutions over a predominantly Greek Orthodox population. Their imperial
  claims exceeded the land they could hold, as Byzantine successor states and Bulgaria
  resisted them. Baldwin's defeat and capture at Adrianople in 1205 exposed this weakness,
  and later emperors struggled with dwindling territory and money. In 1261, forces serving
  the emperor of Nicaea recovered Constantinople, restoring Byzantine government while the
  displaced Latin imperial title survived only in exile.
