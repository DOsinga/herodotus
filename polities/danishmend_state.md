description: >-
  The Danishmendid beylik was a Turkish ruled state in northern and central Anatolia, formed after the Byzantine defeat at Manzikert. Its founding around 1075 is a conventional estimate. From centres including Sivas, Tokat and Kayseri, its hereditary rulers competed with Byzantium, neighbouring Turkish powers and the Seljuk sultans of Rum. Danishmendid forces opposed Crusader armies and captured Bohemond of Antioch, making the state a major participant in the struggles for Anatolia. Emir Gazi extended its power and secured recognition of his royal standing. Coinage using Greek inscriptions reflects the region's Byzantine cultural setting. This record covers the united government, not the dynasty's whole history. Mehmed's death in 1142, sometimes dated 1143, divided authority among lasting Sivas, Malatya and Kayseri branches.
