description: >-
  This Kingdom of Spain covers the centralised Bourbon monarchy from the completion of the
  Nueva Planta decrees in 1716 to the crisis of 1808. Governed from Madrid, it replaced
  the older arrangement of separately governed crowns with institutions shaped largely on
  Castilian models. The kings pursued administrative, military, commercial, and colonial
  reforms across Spain and its overseas empire. Under Charles III, royal policy encouraged
  public works and sought greater control over the church, including the expulsion of the
  Jesuits. Wars with Britain and alliances with France placed heavy demands on finances
  and shipping. Napoleon's intervention and the forced Bourbon abdications in 1808
  shattered royal government. Joseph Bonaparte held Madrid while patriot juntas claimed
  authority, beginning the separate successor Spanish record.
