description: >-
  The Serbian Despotate was a medieval state governed by hereditary rulers bearing the Byzantine title
  despot. It began in 1402 when Stefan Lazarević received that title after the Ottoman defeat at
  Ankara. Based first at Belgrade and later Smederevo, its rulers balanced relations with Hungary and
  the Ottoman Empire while administering their own lands. Mining wealth supported towns, monasteries,
  scholarship, and a cultural revival despite warfare. Đurađ Branković built Smederevo as a fortified
  capital after returning Belgrade to Hungary. Ottoman conquest interrupted the state in 1439, but
  diplomacy restored it in 1444. Renewed pressure and disputed succession weakened the final rulers.
  Smederevo surrendered in 1459, ending territorial government; Serbian despots later appointed in
  Hungary held titles without restoring the state.
