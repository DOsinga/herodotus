description: >-
  Congress Poland was the kingdom created around Warsaw by the Congress of Vienna in 1815 from
  much of Napoleon's Duchy of Warsaw. Its monarch was the Russian emperor, but it retained a
  Polish constitution, parliament, administration and army. Alexander I granted a constitutional
  settlement that promised political rights, although imperial interference increasingly
  undermined them. The kingdom combined Polish national institutions with a personal union that
  left its future tied to Russian policy. Censorship, political repression and breaches of
  constitutional promises helped bring about the November Uprising of 1830. The insurgent
  government rejected the emperor's kingship, but Russians defeated it in 1831. Direct imperial
  government then replaced its political life, although the name Kingdom of Poland remained in use
  much longer.
