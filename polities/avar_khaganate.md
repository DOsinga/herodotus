name: "Avar Khaganate"
description: >-
  The Avar Khaganate was a steppe monarchy established in the Carpathian Basin in 567. Its
  rulers, called khagans, commanded a mixed population that included Avars, Slavs, and other
  communities. The central settlement region lay between the Danube and Tisza, although no
  capital can be located securely. Cavalry warfare, tribute, and alliances made the
  khaganate a powerful neighbor of Byzantium and the Slavic world. Avars participated in the
  unsuccessful siege of Constantinople in 626, after which their influence changed rather
  than disappearing. Rich graves and distinctive equipment reveal elite wealth and wide
  connections. Frankish campaigns devastated their power, especially in 796. Remaining
  communities with khagan leadership continued under Frankish overlordship, with 822
  conventionally marking their last political appearance in written records.
