description: >-
  The Kingdom of Mrauk U ruled the Arakan coast beside the Bay of Bengal from 1430 to
  1785. Min Saw Mon restored his throne with Bengali assistance and established Mrauk U
  as his capital. Its Buddhist kings adopted Islamic court styles and titles, reflecting
  close connections with Bengal and the kingdom's mixed population. Sea trade, rice
  exports, and military cooperation with Portuguese adventurers strengthened the
  monarchy. At its height, it controlled Chittagong and raided around the bay, with
  piracy and the sale of captives forming part of its economy. Monumental temples
  survive from this prosperous period. Mughal conquest of Chittagong in 1666 weakened
  its position. Later internal struggles left it vulnerable to the Burmese Konbaung
  invasion that ended the kingdom.
