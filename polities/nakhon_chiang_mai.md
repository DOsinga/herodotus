description: >-
  Nakhon Chiang Mai was a tributary city-state in northern Thailand that succeeded the Lan Na kingdom
  after two centuries of Burmese rule. Siamese and northern Thai forces took the city from Burma in
  1775, and the Chet Ton dynasty of Kawila governed it under the overlordship of Thonburi and then
  Bangkok. The war-ravaged city was left empty for some years until Kawila resettled it in 1796,
  bringing in war captives from the Shan states and Sipsong Panna. Its rulers were treated as kings at
  home and as governors by the Siamese court. They kept their own army, courts and revenues and led
  campaigns against the Burmese. Bangkok's centralising reforms ended this autonomy in 1899, when
  Chiang Mai became part of Monthon Phayap.
