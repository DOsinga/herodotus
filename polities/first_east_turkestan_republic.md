description: >-
  The First East Turkestan Republic was proclaimed at Kashgar in November 1933 amid rebellion against
  Chinese provincial rule in Xinjiang. Its leaders drew support chiefly from Turkic Muslim communities
  in the Tarim Basin, although they announced a republic for several peoples. Sabit Damolla headed the
  government, which combined Islamic law with reformist plans for education, health, and economic
  development. Claims extended beyond the territory it actually controlled, and rival rebel
  administrations complicated efforts to unite the region. Shortages, inflation, and hostile military
  forces left the republic vulnerable. Attempts to obtain foreign recognition and assistance failed.
  Chinese Muslim forces defeated its defenders at Kashgar in 1934, ending this brief government,
  though its name and ambitions influenced later East Turkestan independence movements.
