name: "Wang Shichong's Zheng"
description: >-
  Wang Shichong's Zheng was a short-lived imperial state centred on Luoyang in northern
  China from 619 to 621. Wang had served the Sui dynasty as a general before gaining
  control of its surviving court under the young emperor Yang Tong. After defeating the
  rival commander Li Mi, he forced Yang Tong to abdicate and proclaimed himself emperor
  of Zheng. His government held Luoyang and surrounding territories but faced the
  expanding Tang dynasty. Tang commander Li Shimin captured its outlying cities and
  besieged the capital. Wang appealed to Dou Jiande's Xia state for relief, recognising
  their shared danger. Li Shimin defeated and captured Dou at Hulao in 621. With
  assistance cut off, Wang surrendered Luoyang, ending Zheng and allowing Tang
  annexation.
