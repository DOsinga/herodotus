description: >-
  Guo Zihe's Yongle state was a short lived rebel kingship around Yulin in northern China
  during the collapse of the Sui dynasty. Guo, a former Sui military officer exiled to the
  region, rebelled in 617 and declared himself King of Yongle. He adopted his own reign title,
  asserting local royal authority amid the wider struggles that brought the Tang dynasty to
  power. The surviving accounts tell more about Guo's career than the institutions or
  territory of his small realm. In 618 he surrendered to the Tang and accepted military
  command at Lingzhou, ending his independent kingship. He subsequently served the new
  dynasty, received noble titles and the imperial surname Li, but these honours did not
  preserve a separate Yongle government.
