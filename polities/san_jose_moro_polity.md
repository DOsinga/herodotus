description: >-
  San Jose de Moro was a Moche ceremonial and political community in Peru's
  Jequetepeque valley, represented here approximately from 400 to 850. Archaeology
  reveals a local court associated with powerful priestesses, whose richly furnished
  graves link them to rituals depicted on Moche pottery. Their headdresses and
  ceremonial goblets suggest that religious authority formed an important part of
  leadership. Elaborate chamber tombs, metal ornaments and evidence of large funerary
  feasts show marked differences in status and the resources devoted to elite burials.
  These remains provide evidence of governing and ritual roles without supplying a
  complete written history or royal genealogy. The late Moche collapse marks this
  record's end. Later use of the cemetery does not establish continuity of the same
  government.
