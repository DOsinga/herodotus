description: >-
  The Republic of the Seven Tithings governed the Valais in the upper Rhône valley from
  the early seventeenth century until 1798. The seven tithes, or Zenden, of the upper
  valley were self-governing communities that had gradually taken power from the prince-
  bishops of Sion. In 1628 they forced the bishop to renounce his temporal rule, and
  from then on a diet of the tithes, led by an elected governor, ran the state. The
  bishop kept a place of honour but little power. The lower Valais, conquered from Savoy
  in the fifteenth century, was ruled as a subject territory. The republic was an
  associate of the Swiss Confederacy rather than a member. French troops invaded in 1798
  and joined the Valais to the Helvetic Republic.
