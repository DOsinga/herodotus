description: >-
  The County of Freiburg was the lordship of the counts of Freiburg in the Breisgau, the
  descendants of Count Egino of Urach, who inherited the town and lands of the dukes of Zähringen
  in 1218. From about 1245 they called themselves after Freiburg, which the Zähringer had founded,
  and ruled the town and the surrounding Breisgau, with its silver mines in the Black Forest.
  Their relations with the citizens were tense: the town had a council and charter of its own, and
  repeatedly clashed with its lords over taxes and debts. In 1366 the citizens fought Count Egino
  III and lost. In 1368 they paid him to give up his rights and placed themselves under the
  Habsburgs, and the counts withdrew to Badenweiler.
