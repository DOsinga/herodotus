description: >-
  The Livonian Confederation joined the rulers and cities of medieval Livonia, covering much of
  present Estonia and Latvia, from 1435 to 1561. It emerged from lands established through the
  Livonian Crusade, but its agreement followed centuries of rivalry among the Livonian Order,
  bishops and towns. The archbishop of Riga, other bishops, knightly vassals and urban
  representatives participated in a common diet meeting at Walk. Riga was a major commercial
  centre linked to Hanseatic trade. This arrangement coordinated separate authorities rather than
  placing them under one strong monarch. Religious reform and competing interests strained its
  cohesion. Russian invasion in 1558 began the Livonian War, during which neighbouring powers
  divided the region. The Order's dissolution in 1561 ended the confederation's political
  framework.
