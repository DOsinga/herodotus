description: >-
  The Tüsheet Khan khanate was the central of the Khalkha Mongol khanates, holding the Orkhon and
  Tuul valleys in present Mongolia. It descended from Dayan Khan's son Gersenji, and its first
  great ruler, Abtai Sain Khan, adopted Tibetan Buddhism and founded the monastery of Erdene Zuu
  near the ruins of Karakorum in 1585. His successors took the title Tüsheet Khan and were the
  senior Khalkha rulers. The khanate's most famous figure was Zanabazar, son of Gombodorj Khan,
  who became the first Jebtsundamba Khutughtu and the leading Buddhist authority of Khalkha.
  Rivalry with the Zasagt khans and the Dzungars led to war in the 1680s. When the Dzungar ruler
  Galdan invaded in 1688, the Khalkha fled south and submitted to the Kangxi Emperor.
