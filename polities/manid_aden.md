description: >-
  The Ma'nid Emirate of Aden was held by the Banu Ma'n, a Himyarite family that took control of
  Aden in the early eleventh century after the power of the Ziyadids of Zabid collapsed.
  Besides the port they held Lahij, Abyan and al-Shihr, and the customs of Aden's trade with
  India and the Red Sea supported them. Little is recorded about how they governed; most of
  what is known comes from the twelfth-century chronicle of Umara. Ali al-Sulayhi defeated them
  in 1062 and made them pay tribute, but they kept running their own affairs and declared
  independence after his death. His son al-Mukarram Ahmad then besieged Aden, ended the
  family's rule, and in 1083 gave the city to the brothers al-Abbas and al-Mas'ud, founders of
  the Zurayid line.
