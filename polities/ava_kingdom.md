description: >-
  The Kingdom of Ava governed much of Upper Burma from the fourteenth century until
  1555. Its formation followed Thado Minbya's consolidation of Sagaing and Pinya in
  1364, with Ava becoming the royal centre. Kings claimed the legacy of Pagan and sought
  to reunite lands that had fragmented after its collapse. Their authority depended on
  provincial rulers whose loyalty often weakened during successions. Long warfare with
  Hanthawaddy in the south exhausted the kingdom, while Shan states pressed from the
  north. Prome and Toungoo broke away. A Shan confederation captured Ava in 1527 and
  installed rulers who continued government from the city. The Toungoo kingdom under
  Bayinnaung conquered Ava in 1555, ending its separate rule and drawing Upper Burma
  into an empire.
