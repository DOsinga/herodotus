description: >-
  The Toltec Empire refers to a debated state associated with Tula in central Mexico,
  represented here by disputed dates of 674 to 1122. Later accounts describe monarchs
  and religious leaders ruling Tollan, but these traditions mix political memory with
  legend and were recorded long after the events. Archaeology establishes Tula as a
  major urban centre, with monumental temples, warrior columns and cultural connections.
  It does not securely establish every claimed conquest or royal genealogy. Traditions
  link the ruler Topiltzin with Quetzalcoatl and portray conflict among competing
  religious groups. Warfare, famine and migration feature in accounts of the capital's
  decline. After Tula lost its prominence, smaller states succeeded it, while later
  rulers invoked Toltec ancestry to strengthen their own political legitimacy.
