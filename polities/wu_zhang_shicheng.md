description: >-
  Zhang Shicheng's Wu was a rebel state in the Yangtze delta during the collapse of the
  Yuan dynasty, lasting from 1353 to 1367. Zhang was a salt shipper from northern Jiangsu
  who led the salt workers in revolt in 1353 and took Taizhou, Xinghua and Gaoyou. In 1354
  he proclaimed the state of Zhou at Gaoyou, and in 1356 he seized Suzhou and made it his
  capital. His lands were among the richest in China, producing much of its grain and salt,
  and the delta's literati backed his government. From 1357 he accepted Yuan titles and
  shipped grain north, while still ruling on his own account. He called himself King of Wu
  in 1363. Zhu Yuanzhang took Suzhou after a ten-month siege in 1367 and ended the state.
