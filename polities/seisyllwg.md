description: >-
  Seisyllwg was a small medieval Welsh kingdom combining Ceredigion with Ystrad Tywi,
  extending across parts of western and southern Wales. Its beginnings are conventionally
  placed around 680, although surviving early written evidence does not firmly establish
  when the combined kingdom formed. The name recalls Seisyll, a king of Ceredigion
  traditionally associated with its expansion. Local kings governed through dynastic
  relationships rather than a documented fixed royal capital; Llanbadarn Fawr represents
  the Ceredigion heartland. After Gwgon died in 872, Rhodri Mawr's family gained control
  and his son Cadell became king. This succession did not automatically unite its
  government with Gwynedd. Cadell's sons Hywel and Clydog inherited the realm. When
  Clydog died in 920, Hywel combined Seisyllwg and Dyfed to create Deheubarth.
