description: >-
  The Kamata kingdom held the western Brahmaputra valley and northern Bengal after the old
  Kamarupa kingdom broke up, until the Bengal Sultanate conquered it in 1498. Its kings first
  ruled from Kamarupanagara, opposite Guwahati, and after Sandhya beat off a Muslim invasion
  in 1257 he moved his seat west to Kamatapur near Cooch Behar and took the title Kamateswara.
  Its land covered Kamrup, Goalpara, Jalpaiguri, Cooch Behar and parts of Rangpur and
  Mymensingh. The court poets of Durlabhnarayan wrote some of the earliest Assamese
  literature. The Bhuyan chief Arimatta seized the throne after Sikandar Shah's raids, and
  the Khen kings replaced his line in the fifteenth century. Alauddin Husain Shah removed the
  last Khen king, Nilambar, after a long siege of Kamatapur.
