name: Sarbadar state
description: >-
  The Sarbadar state governed part of western Khurasan in northeastern Iran from 1337 to
  1381, centered on Sabzevar. It arose during the Ilkhanate's collapse after rebels resisted
  local taxation and seized the city. Its leadership brought together secular military
  figures and religious dervishes, rather than a dynasty. Rival factions disagreed over
  authority and religious policy, and rulers often gained or lost power through violence.
  Shiite movements associated with Sheikh Khalifa and Hasan Juri gave the state a
  distinctive religious character, though its territories included Sunni communities. The
  Sarbadars fought neighboring powers and sometimes acknowledged Mongol claimants for
  practical reasons. Their last ruler, Ali Muayyad, submitted to Timur in 1381, ending
  separate government while avoiding the destruction suffered by many rivals.
