description: >-
  The Kingdom of Serbia was a Balkan constitutional monarchy proclaimed in 1882 when
  Prince Milan became king. Governed from Belgrade, it built on the independence already
  secured by the preceding principality. The Obrenović dynasty initially cultivated
  Austria-Hungary, but the assassination of King Alexander and Queen Draga in 1903
  brought Peter Karađorđević to the throne and a turn toward Russia and France.
  Parliamentary politics developed alongside powerful military factions. Victory in the
  Balkan Wars enlarged Serbia into Kosovo, Macedonia, and neighbouring districts.
  Austria-Hungary's attack in 1914 helped start the First World War. Enemy occupation
  removed the government from Serbia in 1915, though its army continued fighting abroad.
  After returning in 1918, Serbia joined the new Kingdom of Serbs, Croats and Slovenes.
