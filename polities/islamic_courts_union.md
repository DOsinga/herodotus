description: >-
  The Islamic Courts Union governed Mogadishu and much of southern and central Somalia
  for several months in 2006. It grew from courts that used Islamic law to resolve
  disputes and provide security after the collapse of the Somali state. Supported by
  merchants and local communities, the courts combined their militias and defeated a
  rival warlord coalition in June. Their leadership brought together different religious
  and political outlooks rather than a single uniform movement. The new administration
  removed checkpoints, reopened the capital's airport and seaport, and extended its
  authority beyond Mogadishu. Conflict with the Transitional Federal Government and
  Ethiopia followed. Ethiopian forces invaded in December, ending the Union's
  territorial government; former members subsequently followed different paths in Somali
  politics and insurgency.
