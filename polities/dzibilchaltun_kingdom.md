description: >-
  Dzibilchaltun was a Maya royal centre in northern Yucatan, near present Merida in Mexico. This
  record estimates its kingdom's main span as 300 to 1100, distinguishing that government from
  the site's much longer occupation. Its location combined cultivable land with access to coastal
  salt production, while the Xlakah cenote provided water and a place for religious offerings.
  Monumental buildings, plazas, and raised roads show an extensive urban centre. The Temple of
  the Seven Dolls is its best known surviving structure. Although many inscriptions are worn,
  Stela 10 preserves the name of a ruler, K'alomte' Uk'uuw Chan Chaahk. Population and building
  activity changed repeatedly. The later decline of the city does not establish that its Classic
  dynasty survived into subsequent occupation.
