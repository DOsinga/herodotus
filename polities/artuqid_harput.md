description: >-
  The Artuqid Emirate of Harput was a Turkmen principality in eastern Anatolia, ruled from the
  fortress town of Harput above the plain of modern Elazığ from 1185 to 1234. Harput had been
  held by the Artuqids of Hasankeyf since the early twelfth century. In 1185 Imad ad-Din Abu
  Bakr, a son of Kara Arslan who had failed to take the Hasankeyf throne, made it a separate
  principality of his own. His son Ibrahim and grandson Ahmad Khidr followed him. The emirate
  sat between the Ayyubids to the south and the Seljuks of Rum to the west and tried to play
  one off against the other. In 1234 the Seljuk sultan Kayqubad I conquered Harput and ended
  the line.
