description: >-
  The Igawawen, known to Arabic writers as the Zwawa, were a confederation of Kabyle tribes in
  the Djurdjura mountains of Greater Kabylia, recorded here as a chiefdom from 1639 to 1857.
  Its core was eight tribes in two groups, led by the Ait Betrun and the Ait Mengellet. After
  the Kingdom of Kuku faded in the 1630s, the villages governed themselves through their
  assemblies, the tajmaat, and through alliances between tribes, while the Boukhtouche heirs
  of the Kuku rulers kept some standing. The Regency of Algiers built forts in the valleys of
  the Sebaou and Boghni in the 1720s, but the Kabyles destroyed them in the revolt of 1756 and
  never paid regular taxes. France conquered the Djurdjura in the campaign of 1857.
