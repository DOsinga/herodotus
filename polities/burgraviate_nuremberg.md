description: >-
  The Burgraviate of Nuremberg was the territory of the burgraves of Nuremberg, an office the
  Hohenzollern family held from 1192. They lost power over the city of Nuremberg itself, which
  became independent, and built a territory of their own in Franconia, ruled from the castle of
  Cadolzburg. They gained Bayreuth from the Merania inheritance around 1260, bought Ansbach in
  1331 and acquired Kulmbach with the Plassenburg in 1340. Charles IV raised them to the rank of
  princes of the Empire in 1363. In 1398 the brothers John III and Frederick VI divided the
  burgraviate into the lands above the hills, around Kulmbach and Bayreuth, and below the hills,
  around Ansbach. Frederick later became Elector of Brandenburg.
