description: >-
  Saxe-Gotha was a duchy in Thuringia, created for Ernest the Pious when the Saxe-Weimar inheritance was divided in 1640. Ernest governed from Gotha and built Friedenstein Castle as a residence and centre. In 1672, he inherited most of Saxe-Altenburg, giving the combined state its longer name. Further family divisions followed his death, but Gotha and Altenburg remained together under his eldest son's line. The duchy belonged successively to the Holy Roman Empire, the Confederation of the Rhine and the German Confederation, while its dukes managed local government. Primogeniture helped prevent another division within the line. Frederick IV died without an heir in 1825. The settlement of 1826 redistributed the lands, joining Gotha to Coburg and establishing a separate Altenburg duchy.
