name: Ostrogoths in Pannonia
wikipedia: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ostrogoths
description: >-
  After the Hunnic empire broke apart at the battle of Nedao in 454, the Ostrogoths settled in
  Pannonia as federates of the Eastern Roman Empire. Jordanes describes their land as stretching
  from Sirmium to Vindobona, the Roman name of Vienna. Three brothers of the Amal family, Valamir,
  Thiudimir and Vidimir, each ruled a share of the people and the land, and drew yearly payments
  from Constantinople. They fought off the sons of Attila and defeated the Sciri, Suebi and other
  neighbours, though Valamir was killed in battle around 469. Thiudimir's son Theoderic spent his
  youth as a hostage in Constantinople. In 473 the Goths left the exhausted province for the
  Balkans, and Theoderic later led them to Italy.
