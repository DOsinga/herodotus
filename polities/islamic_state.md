description: >-
  Islamic State governed territory across Iraq and Syria between its proclamation
  of a caliphate in 2014 and the loss of its final enclave in 2019. Emerging from
  jihadist organisations in Iraq, it placed Abu Bakr al-Baghdadi at the head of a
  claimed religious and political order. Raqqa became an administrative centre,
  while Mosul was its largest captured city. The terrorist organisation imposed
  its extremist interpretation of Islamic law through courts, police, taxation,
  propaganda, and organised violence. It committed mass killings, enslaved
  Yazidis, persecuted minorities, and destroyed cultural sites. Iraqi forces,
  Kurdish-led forces, and other opponents recovered its territory with
  international support. Its defeat at Baghuz ended this territorial government,
  though surviving militants and affiliated organisations continued insurgent
  activity in countries.
