description: >-
  The Sajid Emirate ruled Azerbaijan and neighbouring parts of the South Caucasus from about
  889 to 929. Its founders came from an Iranian family of Central Asian origin that served
  the Abbasid caliphs. Appointment as governor gave Muhammad ibn Abi'l-Saj the base from
  which he established his own army and regional territorial government. The rulers first
  used Maragha and Barda, then Yusuf moved their main seat to Ardabil in 901. They fought
  Armenian and Georgian rulers, sought to consolidate Azerbaijan, and alternated between
  tribute payments and resistance to Baghdad. Caliphal recognition therefore coexisted with
  considerable local power. Yusuf's campaigns made the emirate an important regional
  military force, but the killing of its last ruler in 929 ended the ruling line.
