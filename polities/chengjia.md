description: >-
  Chengjia was an empire in the Sichuan basin founded by Gongsun Shu after the fall of Wang
  Mang's Xin dynasty, lasting from 25 to 36 AD. Gongsun had governed Daojiang under Wang Mang
  and took control of Shu during the civil war that followed. He called himself King of Shu
  in 24 and emperor of Chengjia in 25, with his capital at Chengdu. Chengjia soon held the
  whole of Yi province, including Hanzhong, Chongqing and parts of Yunnan and Guizhou, and
  the warlord Wei Ao of eastern Gansu submitted to it. Protected by mountains and the Three
  Gorges, it outlasted the other rivals of the restored Han. Guangwu's armies broke through
  in 35, and Chengdu fell after Gongsun was killed in December 36.
