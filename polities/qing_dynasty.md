description: >-
  The Qing was a Manchu imperial state proclaimed in 1636 and ruling China from Beijing after
  1644. Its Aisin Gioro emperors combined Chinese bureaucratic institutions with banner
  armies and different arrangements for governing Inner Asian territories. Conquests under
  Kangxi, Yongzheng and Qianlong brought Mongolia, Xinjiang, Tibet and Taiwan within a vast
  empire. Population growth, farming and commerce expanded during its strongest period. In
  the nineteenth century, internal rebellions and defeats against foreign powers strained its
  finances and authority. Unequal treaties opened ports and weakened control over foreign
  relations. Attempts at military modernisation and institutional reform did not restore
  lasting stability. The revolution of 1911 led to the last emperor's abdication in 1912 and
  the establishment of the Republic of China.
