description: >-
  Kelantan is a Malay sultanate in the northeast of the Malay Peninsula, centred on the Kelantan river
  and the town of Kota Bharu. Several small states had existed there under Patani and Terengganu
  influence. Around 1760 Long Yunus, a warlord of Patani origin, unified the territory and was
  installed as its ruler with Terengganu's support; his descendants still reign. Kelantan was a rice-
  growing and trading state that sent tribute to Siam, while its own rulers ran its affairs. Civil
  wars over the succession in the nineteenth century drew in Siam and Terengganu. Kota Bharu became
  the capital in 1844. Under the Anglo-Siamese Treaty of 1909 Siam transferred its claims to Britain,
  which then controlled Kelantan's foreign affairs.
