description: >-
  The Mengujekid Emirate was a Turkish principality in the upper Euphrates valley of eastern
  Anatolia, founded after the Seljuk victory at Manzikert in 1071 and centred on Erzincan.
  Its founder Mengüjek Ghazi was a Turkmen commander who took Erzincan, Kemah, Divriği and
  Şebinkarahisar from the Byzantines. His descendants governed these towns as a small
  dynastic state between the Saltukids of Erzurum, the Danishmendids and the Seljuks of
  Rum, whose overlordship they accepted at times while keeping their own government. In the
  twelfth century the family divided into a branch at Erzincan and Kemah and another at
  Divriği, known for its Great Mosque and Hospital of 1228. The
  Seljuk sultan Kayqubad I annexed the Erzincan branch in 1228; the Divriği branch survived
  as a minor vassal until 1277.
