description: >-
  The Terramare culture was a Bronze Age culture of the Po valley in northern Italy, named after
  the dark earth mounds, terra marna, that its settlements left behind. Its villages were laid out
  on a regular plan, surrounded by ditches and earth banks and often built on raised platforms.
  Farmers grew cereals and kept cattle, sheep and pigs, and bronze workers produced tools and
  weapons. The population grew dense across the plain of present Emilia and Lombardy, with some
  settlements covering many hectares. Around 1150 BCE the network of villages collapsed and was
  largely abandoned within a short time, perhaps because of climate change, overexploitation of
  the land or social crisis, and the plain was resettled only later.
