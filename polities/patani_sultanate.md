description: >-
  Patani was a Malay sultanate on the east coast of the Malay Peninsula, covering roughly the modern
  Thai provinces of Pattani, Yala and Narathiwat. It rose in the fifteenth century as the successor of
  older states in the area such as Langkasuka, and its rulers converted to Islam around that time.
  Patani became a busy port for Chinese, Japanese and European traders. Its golden age came under four
  successive queens from 1584, when it grew wealthy and beat off several Siamese invasions. Patani
  sent tribute to Ayutthaya but generally governed itself and sometimes fought its overlord. The
  kingdom declined in the late seventeenth century. Siam conquered it in 1786, divided it into smaller
  provinces and deposed its last raja in 1902.
