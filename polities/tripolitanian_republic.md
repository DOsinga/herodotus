description: >-
  The Tripolitanian Republic was an Arab republic proclaimed in the autumn of 1918 in the
  interior of Tripolitania, in present Libya, which resistance to Italian rule had cleared of
  Italian forces during the First World War. Its capital was Aziziya, south of Tripoli, and
  its territory ran from the Nafusa Mountains to Misrata, leaving Italy only Tripoli and Homs.
  A council of four leaders, Sulayman al-Baruni, Ramadan al-Suwayhli, Abd al-Nabi Belkheir and
  Ahmad al-Murayyid, governed it, though they often acted separately. It sought recognition at
  the Paris Peace Conference without success. After a short settlement with Italy in 1919 it
  was re-established at Misrata. Feuds among its leaders weakened it, and Italy reconquered
  the region from 1922.
