description: >-
  The League of Lezhë united Albanian nobles in a military and diplomatic alliance from
  1444 to 1479. Established at a meeting in then Venetian-held Lezhë, it chose Gjergj
  Kastrioti, known as Skanderbeg, to command its combined forces against Ottoman
  expansion. Members contributed soldiers and money while retaining government of their
  own lands; the league was not a fully centralized monarchy. Its armies defended
  strongholds, especially Krujë, and won repeated victories against Ottoman forces.
  Relationships with Venice, Naples, and the papacy brought support but also disputes and
  changing alliances. After Skanderbeg died in 1468, Lekë Dukagjini continued resistance.
  The fall of Shkodër in 1479 marks the league's ending here. Its shared struggle became
  an important part of later Albanian historical identity.
