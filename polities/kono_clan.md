description: >-
  The Kōno were a warrior house that governed Iyo province in northwestern Shikoku from Yuzuki
  Castle at Dōgo, now in the city of Matsuyama, until Toyotomi Hideyoshi's conquest of Shikoku
  in 1585. Of old local stock, they sided with Minamoto no Yoritomo in the Genpei War, became
  military governors of Iyo, and won fame against the Mongol invasions under Kōno Michiari. In
  the Sengoku period their hold on the province was weak. The river valleys of Iyo had their
  own lords, the Hosokawa, the Ōtomo and later the Chōsokabe pressed in from outside, and the
  Murakami pirates of the Inland Sea largely went their own way. The Mōri were their main
  allies. Kōno Michinao surrendered Yuzuki to Kobayakawa Takakage in 1585.
