description: >-
  Opone was a trading port identified with Hafun on Somalia's northeastern coast. Its
  history is represented here from an estimated emergence around 300 BCE to approximately
  500 CE, rather than a documented governmental lifetime. The first-century Periplus of
  the Erythraean Sea describes a market linked to Indian Ocean shipping, particularly
  trade in cinnamon and tortoiseshell. Its position connected routes along the Horn of
  Africa with Arabia, the Red Sea and ports farther east. Imported pottery and remains of
  houses, courtyards, stone walls and a harbour support the identification. Evidence is
  stronger for trade and settlement than for a named ruling dynasty or formal state
  institutions. The endpoint marks the principal Late Antique phase, not the disappearance
  of habitation at Hafun.
