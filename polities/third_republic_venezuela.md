description: >-
  The Third Republic of Venezuela was the independent government that Simón Bolívar re-
  established in 1817, based in the Orinoco basin. After failed expeditions from Haiti,
  Bolívar and Manuel Piar took the province of Guayana, and the city of Angostura fell in
  July 1817. From there Bolívar organised a government, a council of state and the newspaper
  Correo del Orinoco, while llanero forces under José Antonio Páez held the plains of Apure.
  Piar was executed for insubordination in 1817. In February 1819 the Congress of Angostura
  met, elected Bolívar president and heard his plan for a union with New Granada. After his
  crossing of the Andes and victory at Boyacá, the congress proclaimed the Republic of
  Colombia in December 1819, into which Venezuela merged.
