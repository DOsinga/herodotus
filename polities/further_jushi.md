description: >-
  Further Jushi was the northern of the two kingdoms into which the Han divided the Jushi lands
  around 60 BCE, lying north of the Bogda mountains around present Jimsar in Xinjiang. While
  Nearer Jushi held the Turpan basin and its oasis farms, Further Jushi occupied pastures and
  fields on the edge of the Dzungarian steppe, close to Xiongnu territory. Its kings sent envoys
  and hostages to the Han court and housed Han military colonists, but they also changed sides
  when Xiongnu power revived. The Han protectorate of the Western Regions repeatedly intervened in
  its successions, and Further Jushi joined revolts against Han officials in the first and second
  centuries CE. Its separate kingship fades from the Chinese sources in the third century.
