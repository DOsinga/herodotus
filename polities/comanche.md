description: >-
  Comancheria was the territory of the Comanche on the southern Great Plains, covering much
  of present-day western Texas, eastern New Mexico, western Oklahoma and southern Kansas and
  Colorado. The Comanche split from the Shoshone around 1700, took up the horse and drove
  the Apache from the plains. They were not a single state but several divisions, such as
  the Penateka, Kotsoteka, Yamparika and Kwahadi, each with its own chiefs and councils,
  which acted together in diplomacy and war. In 1786 they made a lasting peace with Spanish
  New Mexico. They controlled trade in horses, captives and bison robes and raided deep into
  Mexico in the 1830s and 1840s. Disease, the slaughter of the bison and the Red River War
  broke their power, and the last bands surrendered in 1875.
