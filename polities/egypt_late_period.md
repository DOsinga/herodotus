description: >-
  Egypt recovered native royal government in 404 BCE after Amyrtaeus of Sais rebelled against
  Persian rule. This record covers the independent kingdom of the Twenty-eighth, Twenty-ninth
  and Thirtieth dynasties, ending with Persian reconquest in 343 BCE. Royal seats moved from
  Sais to Mendes and then Sebennytos. Pharaohs governed through Egyptian institutions and
  supported temples, while relying on armies that included Greek mercenaries. Their main
  challenge was defending independence against the Achaemenid Empire. Hakor and Nectanebo I
  resisted Persian invasions, but royal succession remained unstable. Teos launched an
  expedition into the Near East before a family revolt replaced him with Nectanebo II. The
  latter continued building programmes but lost to Artaxerxes III, who restored Persian
  control over Egypt and its government.
