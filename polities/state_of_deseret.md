description: >-
  The State of Deseret was the self-governing settlement founded by members of the Church of
  Jesus Christ of Latter-day Saints in the Great Basin. Brigham Young led the first pioneers
  into the Salt Lake Valley in 1847, while the region still belonged to Mexico, and the
  church's leaders governed the colony. After the Mexican War ceded the region to the United
  States, the settlers organised a provisional State of Deseret in 1849, with Young as
  governor, a legislature, courts and the Nauvoo Legion as militia. It claimed a vast area
  reaching to southern California. Congress declined to admit it and created the smaller
  Utah Territory in 1850 instead. Young became territorial governor, and the Deseret
  legislature dissolved itself in April 1851.
