description: >-
  Ran Wei was a short lived state in northern China, ruled from Ye between 350 and 352 during the Sixteen
  Kingdoms period. Its founder, Ran Min, had served the Jie ruled Later Zhao as a general and adopted member of
  the ruling family. After violent succession struggles, he killed the emperor and claimed the throne himself. His rise was accompanied
  by mass killings directed against Jie and other non Chinese inhabitants, further tearing apart an already fractured region. Ran Min
  fought surviving Zhao forces and sought help from Eastern Jin, without securing a lasting alliance. Former Yan then defeated and
  captured him. His son defended Ye during a devastating, prolonged siege, but the city's surrender ended the state in 352.
