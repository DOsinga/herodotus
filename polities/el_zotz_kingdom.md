name: "Kingdom of El Zotz"
description: >-
  The kingdom of El Zotz was a Maya royal state in Guatemala's Petén region, close to the
  larger city of Tikal. Its history is represented here from about 350 to 800, with both
  boundaries estimated. The ruling dynasty first occupied Bejucal before moving its capital to
  El Zotz. Inscriptions, royal burials, palaces, and temple pyramids show a court embedded in
  the rivalries of the Classic Maya world. Its rulers cultivated links with El Perú and
  Calakmul, balancing the influence of neighbouring Tikal. A shared emblem glyph suggests
  connections with Yaxchilan's dynasty. Tikal's recovery eventually threatened its smaller
  neighbour, and inscriptions record hostile encounters. Later habitation continued, but it
  does not establish that the separate royal government survived beyond this period.
