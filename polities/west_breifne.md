description: >-
  West Breifne was an Irish kingdom ruled by the O'Rourke family from 1256 to 1605, chiefly
  in present County Leitrim. It emerged when conflict between the O'Rourkes and O'Reillys divided the older
  kingdom of Breifne into western and eastern governments. Dromahair was an important royal seat. Kings were
  selected from the ruling family rather than following a fixed succession from father to eldest son,
  and rival branches repeatedly contested authority. Local clans and neighbouring rulers could determine the outcome of
  these struggles. The kingdom maintained Gaelic political and cultural traditions while facing increasing English intervention. Its
  territory was organized as County Leitrim in 1583. After the Nine Years' War, the loss of
  O'Rourke control ended its separate government in 1605.
