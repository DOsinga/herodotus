description: >-
  The Kingdom of Huaylas was a highland polity of the Late Intermediate Period in the Callejón de
  Huaylas, the valley of the Santa River in the Ancash region of Peru. After Wari's hold on the
  valley ended, the communities there formed the kingdom known to the Spanish as Huaylas or
  Wayllas, with Huaraz among its important towns. Little is recorded of its government beyond the
  two kings whose daughters later married into the Inca nobility, and archaeology shows hilltop
  settlements with herding and farming on the valley slopes. Under Pachacuti, the Inca generals
  Capac Yupanqui and Tupac Inca Yupanqui fought the Huaylas and their allied neighbours. The Inca
  then reorganised the kingdom into upper and lower halves, with Huaraz the seat of the lower.
