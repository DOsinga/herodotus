description: >-
  Ailech was the kingdom associated with the Cenél nEógain in northwestern Ireland, whose emergence is
  estimated here around 425. Its name came from the fortified royal site of Grianán of Aileach in
  Inishowen, but the court later moved inland. Kings belonged to the Northern Uí Néill world and
  sometimes competed for Ireland's high kingship. They expanded across the Foyle towards the lands
  later called Tyrone, taking territory and influence from neighboring kingdoms. Rivalry with the
  Cenél Conaill did not make their separately ruled Tír Chonaill part of a united state. Tullyhogue
  became an inauguration site as power shifted southeast. The record ends in 1185 with the continuing
  kingdom identified as Tyrone, although sources retained the Ailech name beyond that date.
