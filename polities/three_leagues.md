description: >-
  The Three Leagues were a federation of Alpine communities in what is now the canton of
  Graubünden in eastern Switzerland. The League of God's House formed in 1367 to resist
  the bishop of Chur, followed by the Grey League in 1395 and the League of the Ten
  Jurisdictions in 1436. By the late fifteenth century they acted together as one state,
  allied with the Swiss Confederacy but not a member of it. Government rested with the
  communes, which sent delegates to a common diet and decided matters by referendum.
  From 1512 the Leagues ruled the Valtellina, Chiavenna and Bormio as subject lands.
  Religious conflict and foreign intervention tore the state apart during the Thirty
  Years' War. The French invasion of 1799 brought it into the Helvetic Republic.
