description: >-
  Zazzau was a Hausa kingdom in what is now northern Nigeria, centred on Zaria. Its early history rests largely on traditions, with this record beginning in 1050. Rulers bearing the title sarkin governed a farming and trading population at the southern edge of Hausaland. Caravan routes brought salt and other goods, while grain and enslaved people moved through its markets. Queen Amina became its most famous ruler, remembered for military expansion and fortified settlements, although details of her reign remain debated. Zazzau also faced pressure from larger neighbours, including Songhai, Bornu and Kwararafa. Fulani forces captured the kingdom in 1808 during the jihad, bringing Zaria into the Sokoto Caliphate while the displaced Hausa ruler established a new seat farther south.
