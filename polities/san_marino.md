description: >-
  San Marino is a small republic surrounded by Italy, centred on Mount Titano near the
  Adriatic coast. Tradition dates its foundation to 301, when the Christian stonemason
  Marinus established a religious community, rather than to a documented modern state.
  Medieval communal institutions gradually developed into an independent republic. Its
  elected Grand and General Council chooses two captains regent, who jointly serve as
  heads of state for six months. The country survived pressure from neighbouring rulers,
  brief occupations and the upheavals of Italian unification. Giving refuge to Giuseppe
  Garibaldi helped secure later respect for its independence. Modern San Marino retains
  its own government while maintaining close economic ties with Italy. Tourism,
  manufacturing and financial services support its continuing existence within Europe.
