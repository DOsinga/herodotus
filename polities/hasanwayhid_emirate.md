description: >-
  The Hasanwayhids were a Kurdish dynasty that ruled the middle Jibal, the mountain country
  around Kermanshah, Dinavar and the fortress of Sarmaj in western Iran, from about 959 to 1015.
  Their founder Hasanwayh ibn Husayn led a clan of the Barzikani Kurds and gathered the castles
  of his relatives after their deaths in 960 and 961 and taking over the holdings of a rival
  family. He and his son Badr acknowledged the Buyid emirs as overlords but governed their own
  lands, and Badr was remembered for his justice and for building mosques, markets and
  caravanserais. The Buyids intervened in the family's quarrels more than once. After Badr was
  murdered in 1014 the dynasty fell apart, and the Annazid Kurds and Buyid governors took over
  its territory.
