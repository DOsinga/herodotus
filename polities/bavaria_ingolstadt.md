description: >-
  Bavaria-Ingolstadt was a Wittelsbach duchy within the Holy Roman Empire from 1392 to 1447. It
  emerged when Stephen II's sons divided their inheritance into Ingolstadt, Landshut, and Munich
  branches. Stephen III received a collection of territories governed from Ingolstadt, including towns
  in southern, eastern, and northern Bavaria. His son Louis VII succeeded him in 1413 and became
  embroiled in bitter conflicts with relatives, especially Henry XVI of Bavaria-Landshut. The duchy
  gained part of the extinct Bavaria-Straubing inheritance in 1429. Its final years were shaped by a
  struggle between Louis VII and his son Louis VIII, who seized power and imprisoned his father. Both
  died without a surviving successor, and Landshut absorbed the Ingolstadt lands in 1447, ending the
  separate court.
