description: >-
  Qian Prefecture under Lu Guangchou was a warlord regime in southern Jiangxi, centred on modern
  Ganzhou, lasting from 885 to 918 at the end of the Tang dynasty and the start of the Five
  Dynasties. Lu was a Nankang man who led a band of rebels during the agrarian revolts of the
  late Tang, with his friend Tan Quanbo as chief strategist. He captured Qian Prefecture in 885
  and governed it as prefect, and in 902 he added Shao Prefecture across the mountains in
  Guangdong. After 907 he sent tribute to both Later Liang and the Wu state while keeping his
  own government. Lu died in 910, and his son and then Tan Quanbo ruled after him. Wu
  conquered the prefecture in 918.
