description: >-
  Arrapha was a Hurrian kingdom east of the Tigris, centred on the city of Arrapha beneath modern
  Kirkuk in northeastern Iraq. The city had belonged to the Akkadian and Ur III states and was
  conquered by Shamshi-Adad, whose kingdom of Upper Mesopotamia broke up after his death around
  1776 BCE. Its later kings are known above all from the clay archives of Nuzi, a provincial town
  of the kingdom, which record land sales, adoptions, family law and the rations of a Hurrian-
  speaking society in the fifteenth and fourteenth centuries BCE. By then Arrapha was a vassal of
  Mitanni, and Mitannian chariot units were stationed in its towns, but its own kings such as
  Ithi-Teshup governed it. When Mitanni collapsed, Assyria conquered Arrapha and made it an
  Assyrian province.
