name: Electorate of Mainz
description: >-
  The Electorate of Mainz was an ecclesiastical principality in central Germany, represented
  here from 1250 until its secularization in 1803. Its archbishop governed scattered
  territories along the Rhine and Main, around Erfurt, and in Eichsfeld. These political
  lands were distinct from his larger religious jurisdiction. Chosen by the cathedral
  chapter, the archbishop also served as an imperial elector and archchancellor of Germany,
  giving Mainz unusual influence in choosing emperors and managing imperial affairs. The
  territory was not a compact nation but a collection of towns, estates, and districts under
  one prince. Revolutionary France occupied Mainz itself, taking it in 1797. The remaining
  eastern lands were governed from Aschaffenburg until the imperial reorganization of 1803
  ended the old ecclesiastical state's government.
