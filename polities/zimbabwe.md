description: >-
  Zimbabwe is a landlocked republic in southern Africa, governed from Harare since internationally recognised independence in 1980. It emerged after the liberation war against Rhodesia's white minority government and the Lancaster House settlement, which briefly restored British administration before elections. Robert Mugabe first led a parliamentary government and later concentrated authority in an executive presidency. ZANU PF became the dominant party, while violence in Matabeleland, restrictions on opposition and contested elections shaped political life. Agriculture, mining and tourism have supported the economy, but land redistribution, economic decline and severe inflation brought major disruption. Mugabe resigned after military intervention in 2017, and Emmerson Mnangagwa succeeded him. The state continues despite these changes of leadership, with institutions and boundaries inherited from independence.
