description: >-
  Dál nAraidi of Magh Line was a kingdom in northeastern Ireland, centered on
  Ráith Mór near Lough Neagh in southern County Antrim. Its early government is
  estimated here from around 450, and its central kingdom ended during John de
  Courcy's conquest around 1177. The Uí Chóelbad dynasty supplied its kings, who
  sometimes also claimed the overkingship of Ulaid. Expansion northward produced
  a separate northern branch, which this record does not combine with the Magh
  Line court. Kings competed with Dál Fiatach, other Ulster dynasties, and
  neighboring peoples, while churches such as Connor formed important local
  institutions. By the twelfth century its territory had contracted
  considerably. Some local dynasts survived the Norman advance, but not the
  earlier kingdom's central political authority.
