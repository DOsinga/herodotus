description: >-
  Fihrid Ifriqiya was the state that Abd al-Rahman ibn Habib, a great-grandson of the
  conqueror Uqba ibn Nafi, ruled from Kairouan after seizing power in a coup at Tunis late in
  744. The Umayyad governor withdrew to Damascus without a fight, and the collapse of the
  Umayyad caliphate soon cut Ifriqiya loose from any outside authority. Ibn Habib put down
  Berber revolts, raided Sicily and Sardinia, and refused the full submission that the new
  Abbasid caliphs demanded. He also sheltered fugitive Umayyad princes, one of whom went on to
  found the emirate of Córdoba. In 755 his brothers murdered him, and a family war followed
  between them and his son Habib. Kharijite Berbers of the Warfajuma took Kairouan in 757 and
  ended Fihrid rule.
