description: >-
  Datianxing, or the Great Tianxing Kingdom, was a brief royal government in the Dali
  region of present Yunnan, conventionally dated here to 928 and 929. It followed
  Dachanghe, one of the states that occupied Nanzhao's former political centre after that
  kingdom's collapse. Zhao Shanzheng, a senior minister, became ruler with the support of
  Yang Ganzhen, a regional military commander and noble. The alliance removed the Zheng
  ruling family but did not produce a stable new court. Within about a year, Yang killed
  Zhao and seized the throne, naming his own state Dayining. Little separate evidence
  survives for Datianxing's institutions or territory. Its importance lies in this
  succession of coups before the durable Dali kingdom emerged under Duan Siping in 937.
