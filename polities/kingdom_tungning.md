description: >-
  Tungning was the Zheng family's state in southwestern Taiwan and the Penghu islands from
  1662 to 1683. Zheng Chenggong, better known as Koxinga, founded it after forcing the
  Dutch to surrender Fort Zeelandia. A Ming loyalist, he intended Taiwan to support
  resistance against the Qing conquest of China. His son Zheng Jing developed a
  government, encouraged Chinese settlement and farming, and maintained overseas trade
  linking Japan and Southeast Asia. The rulers used Chinese administrative institutions
  alongside a military organisation inherited from their mainland campaigns. Their
  expansion changed relations with Taiwan's Indigenous communities. After Zheng Jing died,
  succession struggles weakened the state. In 1683 the Qing admiral Shi Lang defeated its
  fleet at Penghu, and the last ruler surrendered to China.
