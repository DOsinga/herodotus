name: Kingdom of Bosnia
description: >-
  The Kingdom of Bosnia emerged in the western Balkans when Tvrtko I became king in 1377. It grew
  from the earlier Bosnian banate and, at its height, included lands beyond Bosnia towards Serbia
  and the Adriatic coast. The Kotromanić royal family governed alongside powerful nobles whose
  assembly, the Stanak, influenced succession, treaties, and war. Bobovac was an royal
  stronghold, while mining, especially silver, provided income and encouraged trade with
  neighbouring towns. Catholic, Orthodox, and Bosnian Church communities shared the kingdom, and
  religious disputes became entangled with diplomacy. After Tvrtko's death, rival nobles and
  contested successions weakened royal authority. Ottoman expansion culminated in the conquest of
  1463 and the execution of the last king, Stephen Tomašević, ending the independent Bosnian
  monarchy.
