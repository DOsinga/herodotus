description: >-
  The County of Carcassonne was a feudal lordship around the fortified city of Carcassonne in
  Languedoc, recorded here from 878 to 1069 and from 1082 to 1226. Descendants of Count Bello
  ruled it together with neighbouring Razès, and in the tenth century it passed by marriage to
  the counts of Comminges. In 1069 the heiresses sold their rights to Ramon Berenguer I of
  Barcelona, but from 1082 the Trencavel family governed it as viscounts, together with
  Béziers and Albi. Raymond Roger Trencavel's tolerance of the Cathars brought the Albigensian
  Crusade, which took Carcassonne in 1209 and gave it to Simon de Montfort. The Trencavel heir
  recovered it from Amaury de Montfort in 1224, but Louis VIII's army annexed it in 1226.
