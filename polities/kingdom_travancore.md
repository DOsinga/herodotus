description: >-
  Travancore was a monarchy in southwestern India, built from 1729 by Marthanda Varma
  around the kingdom of Venad. From Padmanabhapuram, he defeated local rivals and extended
  royal control across much of southern Kerala. His army defeated the Dutch East India
  Company at Colachel in 1741, limiting Dutch influence over the region's pepper trade.
  Marthanda Varma dedicated the kingdom to the deity Padmanabha, presenting himself and
  his successors as servants of the temple. The royal family followed matrilineal
  succession. His successor Dharma Raja faced pressure from Mysore and relied increasingly
  on British assistance. A subsidiary treaty with the East India Company in 1795 ended
  independent control of war and foreign relations. Travancore continued afterward as a
  protected princely state until 1949.
