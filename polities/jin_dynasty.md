name: Jin dynasty
description: >-
  The Jin dynasty ruled Manchuria and northern China from 1115 to 1234. Its
  founder, the Jurchen leader Wanyan Aguda, rebelled against the Khitan Liao and
  established an imperial monarchy. Jin armies destroyed Liao rule and captured
  the Northern Song capital in 1127, driving the Song court south. The rulers
  combined Jurchen military organisation with Chinese administrative
  institutions, examinations, and court traditions. Zhongdu, near modern
  Beijing, became their capital before Mongol pressure forced a move to Kaifeng.
  Buddhism, Daoism, and Confucian learning flourished alongside efforts to
  preserve Jurchen identity. Genghis Khan invaded in 1211, beginning decades of
  devastating warfare. The dynasty ended when Mongol and Song forces captured
  its final refuge at Caizhou in 1234, completing the conquest of its territory.
