description: >-
  Amber was a Rajput kingdom in the Dhundhar region of northeastern Rajasthan, founded in 1028 and governed by the Kachwaha family. Its early rulers established themselves around Khoh before Amber became the main royal seat. Alliance with the Mughal emperor Akbar transformed its position: marriage ties and military service brought influence, wealth, and opportunities beyond Rajasthan. Bhagwant Das, Man Singh, and later Jai Singh served as imperial commanders while their family continued governing its own territory. Sawai Jai Singh II founded Jaipur in 1727 and transferred the capital there, creating a planned city associated with architecture and astronomy. The kingdom's independent affairs ended with its treaty with the East India Company in 1818, although its princely monarchy survived until 1949.
