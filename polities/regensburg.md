description: >-
  Regensburg, on the Danube at the mouth of the Regen, had been the seat of the Bavarian dukes and
  of the East Frankish kings, and in the twelfth century it was the largest and richest city of
  southern Germany, trading as far as Kiev and Venice. In 1245 Frederick II granted it the status
  of a free city, and its council governed it independently of both the bishop and the Bavarian
  dukes. As trade moved to Nuremberg and Augsburg the city declined, and in 1486 it submitted to
  Duke Albert IV of Bavaria-Munich, until Emperor Frederick III forced it back to the Empire in
  1492. Regensburg adopted the Reformation and from 1663 hosted the Perpetual Diet. In 1803 it
  became part of Dalberg's principality.
