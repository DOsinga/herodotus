description: >-
  Anhalt-Plötzkau was a small Ascanian principality on the Saale in central Germany, centred on
  the castle of Plötzkau. It first appeared in 1544, when the Dessau lands were divided among
  three brothers and George III, a learned churchman and supporter of the Lutheran Reformation,
  received Plötzkau; on his death in 1553 it passed to the Zerbst line. It was created again in
  the Anhalt partition of 1603, with lands taken from Bernburg, and from 1611 Prince August ruled
  it, also serving as regent for relatives in the other Anhalt states during the Thirty Years'
  War. In 1665 his son Lebrecht inherited Anhalt-Köthen and moved there, and Plötzkau was joined
  to Köthen.
