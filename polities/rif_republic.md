description: >-
  The Republic of the Rif governed part of northern Morocco from 1921 to 1926 during resistance to Spanish colonial rule. Based at Ajdir, it united Riffian tribes under Abd el-Krim after their victory over Spanish forces at Annual. Its leaders sought to turn a military alliance into an organised republic, with a council, ministers, and institutions for taxation, justice, and defence. They also tried unsuccessfully to secure wider diplomatic recognition. Expansion into French-controlled territory brought France into the conflict against the republic. Spain and France coordinated offensives, using aircraft and other modern weapons; Spanish forces also employed chemical warfare. Abd el-Krim surrendered in 1926, and colonial forces dissolved the state. Its struggle became an influential example of organised anticolonial resistance.
