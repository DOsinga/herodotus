description: >-
  The Ardiaean kingdom was an Illyrian monarchy along the eastern Adriatic, represented
  here from approximately 260 to 168 BCE. Its power joined coastal strongholds, fleets,
  and inland communities in areas of present Albania, Montenegro, and neighbouring
  lands. King Agron expanded its influence and fielded formidable forces by land and
  sea. After his death, Queen Teuta governed as regent, but conflict over Adriatic
  raiding brought Roman intervention in 229 BCE. Rome's victory restricted the kingdom
  and altered its leadership. Later Illyrian rulers, including Gentius at Scodra,
  belonged to a line often distinguished as Labeatan, making the state's dynastic
  continuity complicated. Gentius allied with Macedonia against Rome and was captured in
  168 BCE. Roman conquest ended the monarchy and reorganised its territories.
