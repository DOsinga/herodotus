description: >-
  The Kumaon Kingdom occupied Himalayan valleys in present-day Uttarakhand and neighbouring areas from approximately 600 until 1791. This record covers both Katyuri and Chand rule rather than treating their dynasties as separate states. The Katyuris governed from the Baijnath region, but their power fragmented into smaller principalities. The Chand rulers later consolidated Kumaon, first using Champawat and then establishing their capital at Almora in 1563. Kings relied on nobles, ministers, local officers, and court factions to administer territory. Agriculture, mining, and trade supported the kingdom, which maintained connections with Tibet and the northern Indian plains. Mughal tribute did not eliminate its ability to conduct wars. Rivalries and internal struggles weakened it before Gorkha forces conquered Kumaon in 1791 for Nepal.
