description: >-
  The Chutu kingdom was a state of the southern Deccan centred on Banavasi, then called
  Vaijayanti, in present-day Karnataka. Its kings probably began as Satavahana feudatories,
  posted to Banavasi in the second century, and the coins found at Chandravalli near Chitradurga
  show them marrying into neighbouring families of local commanders. When Satavahana power broke
  up in the early third century, the Chutus kept Banavasi and extended their rule north and
  east, using the old Satavahana title Satakarni. Inscriptions at Banavasi, Malavalli and
  Kanheri record grants of villages, tanks and monasteries by kings such as Haritiputra
  Vishnukada Chutukulananda Satakarni and Shivaskandavarman. Little else is known about their
  government. Around 345 the Kadamba founder Mayurasharma took Banavasi, and the Kadambas
  absorbed the Chutu lands.
