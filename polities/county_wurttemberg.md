description: >-
  The County of Württemberg was a territory in southwestern Germany, centred on Stuttgart and ruled by
  the House of Württemberg. This record begins in 1250, as the collapse of Hohenstaufen authority
  allowed regional rulers to expand their control within the Holy Roman Empire. The dynasty had older
  roots around its hilltop castle near Stuttgart. Marriage, purchase and military competition enlarged
  the county across Swabia, and a dynastic marriage connected it with Montbéliard west of the Rhine.
  In 1442 the Treaty of Nürtingen divided the territory between family branches based at Stuttgart and
  Urach. Eberhard the Bearded reunited it through the Treaty of Münsingen in 1482. Imperial elevation
  in 1495 made Württemberg a duchy, continuing its government under a higher title.
