description: >-
  The Commune of Mantua was the self-governing city state of Mantua, set among the lakes
  of the Mincio, from the death of Matilda of Canossa in 1115 until the Gonzaga coup of
  1328. Consuls took over from the counts of Canossa, and the city joined the Lombard
  League against Frederick Barbarossa. In the thirteenth century Mantua expanded its
  territory along the Po and the Mincio. Factions of nobles fought over the commune until
  Pinamonte Bonacolsi made himself lord in 1272, and his family then governed the city
  through its councils. Passerino Bonacolsi also ruled Modena from 1312. In 1328 Luigi
  Gonzaga drove the Bonacolsi out with help from the Scaliger of Verona, and the Gonzaga
  state began.
