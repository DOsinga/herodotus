description: >-
  Zhu Can's Chu state arose around Nanyang in southern Henan during the violent collapse
  of China's Sui dynasty. The record covers his territorial imperial government in 618
  and 619, rather than the earlier years of his roaming rebel army. Zhu had been a minor
  official before becoming a rebel leader, and shifted nominal allegiance among
  competing claimants. After taking Nanyang, he proclaimed himself emperor of Chu. His
  forces seized provisions and devastated the countryside; historical accounts describe
  massacres, starvation, and cannibalism. Local resistance and military defeats
  undermined his authority. He briefly submitted to Tang before breaking with it and
  fleeing to Wang Shichong's court at Luoyang. That later service did not preserve an
  independent Chu government or its territorial base.
