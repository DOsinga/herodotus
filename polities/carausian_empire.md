description: >-
  The Britannic Empire of Carausius was a breakaway Roman regime in Britain and northern Gaul from 286 to 296. Carausius had commanded a fleet against Channel raiders before accusations of corruption prompted an imperial order for his execution. Instead, he declared himself emperor, drawing support from ships, legions, and other soldiers. His government remained Roman in form and issued its own coins, appealing both to British loyalties and to recognition by the emperors. Constantius recovered the Gallic territories in 293. That defeat was followed by Carausius's assassination and the accession of his subordinate Allectus. In 296, Roman forces crossed the Channel, defeated Allectus, and retook London. The imperial administration then restored direct control over Britain and reorganised its provincial government.
