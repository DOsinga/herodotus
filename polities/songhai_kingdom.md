description: >-
  The Songhai Kingdom was the state of the Sunni dynasty on the middle Niger, the forerunner of
  the Songhai Empire. The Sunni kings descended from rulers who had held Kukiya, downstream of
  Gao, while the Mali Empire controlled the Niger bend. In the fourteenth century Ali Kulun
  rebelled against Mali without lasting success, but in the first half of the fifteenth century
  Sunni Sulayman Dama threw off Malian rule and made Gao his seat. The kingdom rested on Songhai
  farmers and on the Sorko fishermen and boatmen of the river, whose canoes carried its armies.
  Sulayman Dama raided Mema to the west. His successor Sunni Ali came to the throne in 1464 and
  turned the kingdom into an empire, taking Timbuktu in 1468.
