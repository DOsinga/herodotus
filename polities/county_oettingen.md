description: >-
  The County of Oettingen was a territory in the Ries, the crater basin on the border of Swabia
  and Franconia, ruled by the counts of Oettingen from the twelfth century until 1806. The counts
  built up one of the larger secular territories of Swabia and for a time held Ansbach, which they
  sold to the burgraves of Nuremberg in 1331. The family divided its lands repeatedly. From the
  sixteenth century the main lines were Oettingen-Oettingen, which became Protestant, and the
  Catholic Oettingen-Wallerstein, and the two shared the town of Oettingen, where a street marked
  the confessional border. The Oettingen-Oettingen line became princes in 1674. In 1806 the county
  was mediatised and passed to Bavaria, though the family kept its titles.
