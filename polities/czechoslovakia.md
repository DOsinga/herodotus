description: >-
  Czechoslovakia was a Central European state founded in 1918 from lands of the collapsed
  Austro-Hungarian Empire. Governed from Prague, its first republic combined parliamentary
  democracy with a diverse population of Czechs, Slovaks, Germans, Hungarians, and others. The
  Munich settlement and German expansion dismantled it, and independent government ceased in
  1939. Restored in 1945, it lost its easternmost territory to the Soviet Union. A communist
  takeover in 1948 brought one-party rule and membership in the Soviet bloc. The Prague Spring's
  reform programme was crushed by Soviet-led invasion in 1968. The Velvet Revolution ended
  communist rule in 1989, restoring democracy. Czech and Slovak political leaders subsequently
  agreed to separate; the federation dissolved at midnight on 31 December 1992 into two
  successor republics.
