description: >-
  Pattikera was a kingdom in the Comilla region of southeastern Bengal, on the plains below the
  Tripura hills, from the eleventh to the early thirteenth century. It arose after the Chandra
  kingdom declined, when local rulers took over its eastern lands around the Mainamati hills. Its
  best known king, Ranavankamalla Harikaladeva, granted land to a Buddhist monastery in the city
  of Pattikera in 1219, the seventeenth year of his reign, and scholars regard him as independent
  of the Pala and Sena kings. Burmese chronicles also know Pattikera as an Indian kingdom west of
  Pagan, with whose kings it exchanged marriages and feuds. Its coins continue Chandra types. The
  kingdom seems to have passed to the Deva rulers of the thirteenth century, though its end is not
  recorded.
