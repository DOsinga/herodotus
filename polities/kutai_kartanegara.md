description: >-
  Kutai Kartanegara developed along the Mahakam River in eastern Borneo, with its
  foundation traditionally placed around 1300. Its first centre was Kutai Lama, and the
  court later moved through Pemarangan to Tenggarong. Initially a kingdom shaped by
  Hindu traditions, it became an Islamic sultanate as religious and trading connections
  changed. Its rulers expanded inland and conquered the neighbouring kingdom of Kutai
  Martapura in 1635, combining the names of the two realms. River commerce linked the
  court, coastal settlements, and communities farther upstream. European intervention
  increased during the nineteenth century. A Dutch treaty in 1844 ended the state's
  independent external affairs. Sultans continued to govern locally under colonial
  authority, but that later survival did not restore the kingdom's earlier political
  independence.
