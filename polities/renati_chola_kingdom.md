name: "Renati Chola Kingdom"
description: >-
  The Renati Chola Kingdom ruled Renadu, in the Kadapa region of present-day Andhra Pradesh,
  approximately from 550 to 850. Its kings claimed descent from the ancient Chola ruler Karikala, but
  this court was distinct from the later imperial Cholas and other Telugu Chola branches. Royal power
  rested with a local dynasty whose relationship with the Pallavas varied between independence and
  acknowledgment of their supremacy. Dhananjaya's Kalamalla inscription of 575 is especially important
  as an early record in Telugu. Later inscriptions name kings, grants and territories, including
  Punyakumara's rule over Renadu and Hiranyarashtra. These records reveal a regional monarchy and its
  patronage, not a securely located capital. Bana and Vaidumba expansion eventually eclipsed its
  government, with the ending here necessarily approximate.
