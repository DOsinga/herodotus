description: >-
  Desmond was a Gaelic kingdom in southwestern Ireland, established when Munster divided in 1118.
  Its original territory covered County Cork and much of County Kerry, but Norman conquest reduced
  the lands governed by its MacCarthy rulers. The western kingdom continued around Killarney, Lough
  Leane, and nearby strongholds, alongside powerful local families such as the O'Sullivans. Kingship
  followed Gaelic traditions, including tanistry, rather than simple succession by the eldest son.
  Warfare and negotiation with Anglo-Norman neighbors shaped its survival; the MacCarthy victory at
  Callann in 1261 checked further conquest. Its rulers also supported religious houses and built
  castles. The death of the last reigning MacCarthy Mór in 1596 marks this record's end, during the
  wider collapse of independent local Gaelic government.
