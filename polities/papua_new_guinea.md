description: >-
  Papua New Guinea became independent from Australia in 1975, bringing together the
  eastern half of New Guinea and neighbouring islands. Port Moresby is its capital. It
  retained the monarch as head of state within a parliamentary system, with a prime
  minister responsible to the national parliament. The country's many languages and
  distinct local communities make national government complex. Much land remains under
  customary ownership, and many people rely on village agriculture alongside mining and
  other export industries. Michael Somare helped guide the transition from Australian
  administration. Conflict over the Panguna copper mine contributed to a devastating civil
  war on Bougainville, later followed by an agreement granting autonomy. Papua New Guinea
  continues as a sovereign state within the Commonwealth of Nations.
