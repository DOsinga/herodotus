description: >-
  The Emirate of Abdelkader was the state that the religious leader Abdelkader ibn Muhyiddin
  built in western and central Algeria to resist the French conquest. The tribes around
  Mascara proclaimed him emir in 1832, after the fall of the Regency of Algiers. He
  organised a regular army, appointed khalifas over provinces, collected taxes and minted
  coins. The Desmichels Treaty of 1834 and the Treaty of Tafna of 1837 recognised his rule
  over much of the interior, including Tlemcen and Médéa. His capital moved from Mascara to
  the fortress of Tagdemt in 1835. War resumed in 1839, and French columns destroyed his
  towns in 1841. He fought on with a moving camp and from Morocco until he surrendered in
  December 1847.
