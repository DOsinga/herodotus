description: >-
  The Idrisid kingdom in the Rif was the surviving northern Moroccan realm of the Idrisid
  family after its expulsion from Fez in 927. Its court withdrew to the mountain fortress of
  Hajar an-Nasr, whose location is represented approximately here. Hereditary rulers rebuilt
  support among local communities while Miknasa leaders and rival caliphates contested
  Morocco. The Idrisids alternately recognized the Fatimids and the Umayyads of Córdoba, but
  such allegiance did not necessarily remove their own government. This was a reduced
  regional kingdom, not the earlier Idrisid realm centered on Fez. Its rulers tried to
  preserve authority amid changing alliances and military pressure. Córdoba removed the last
  ruler in 974. A later attempt to return in 985 failed to restore lasting power.
