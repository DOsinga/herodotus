description: >-
  The Thonburi kingdom restored Siamese royal government after Burmese armies destroyed
  Ayutthaya in 1767. Taksin, its only king, established his capital at Thonburi on the
  Chao Phraya River and gradually defeated rival regional rulers. From this base he
  reunited much of Thailand, defended it against Burmese invasions, and expanded
  influence into Lanna, Laos and Cambodia. Government followed a regional pattern of
  royal authority exercised through subordinate rulers rather than fixed modern borders.
  Trade and Chinese connections helped sustain recovery after wartime devastation, while
  the court supported Buddhist institutions. Political unrest ended Taksin's reign in
  1782, and he was executed. His general Chakri became king, moved the capital across
  the river to Bangkok, and founded the succeeding Rattanakosin kingdom and dynasty.
