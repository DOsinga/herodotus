description: >-
  Lesotho is a mountain kingdom entirely surrounded by South Africa, independent from
  Britain since 1966. Its capital is Maseru, and its population is overwhelmingly
  Basotho, sharing the Sesotho language and a political tradition rooted in
  Moshoeshoe's nineteenth century kingdom. The modern state is a constitutional
  monarchy with parliamentary government, though coups, disputed elections, and
  military intervention have repeatedly disturbed that system. Britain had governed the
  territory as Basutoland, preserving its separate identity instead of incorporating it
  into South Africa. Independence retained that separation while leaving close economic
  ties to its larger neighbor. Migrant labor, clothing manufacture, farming, diamonds,
  and the export of water support livelihoods. Despite political crises, the kingdom
  has continued as a distinct state within southern Africa's economy.
