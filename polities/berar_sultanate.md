description: >-
  Berar was one of the Deccan sultanates of India, centred on Ellichpur in what is now
  Maharashtra. It became independent in 1490 when Fathullah Imad ul Mulk broke away from the
  weakening Bahmani Sultanate and founded the Imad Shahi dynasty. The rulers inherited a
  province with its own military establishment and strengthened forts such as Gavilgad.
  Sunni Islam was the court's religion, while Persian, Marathi, and Dakhini formed part of
  the region's linguistic life. Berar's government faced persistent pressure from Ahmadnagar
  and sought allies in Gujarat and Bijapur. During the reign of young Burhan Imad Shah, his
  minister Tufail Khan seized effective power. Ahmadnagar used this usurpation to justify
  invasion and annexed the sultanate in 1574, ending its royal government.
