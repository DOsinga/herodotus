description: >-
  Gao Kaidao's Yan state was a rebel principality in northern China during the collapse of
  Sui authority and the rise of Tang. This record conventionally begins in 618 and ends in
  624. Gao built his power from an armed following, captured towns in present Hebei and
  established a court first at Yuyang, later at Huairong. He called himself Prince of Yan
  and used his own era name. Alliance with the Eastern Turks helped him resist larger
  rivals. Although he accepted a Tang title and imperial surname in 620, he retained his
  army and government, then resumed open warfare in 621. In 624 a coup by his general
  Zhang Jinshu prompted Gao's suicide; his territory subsequently passed under Tang
  government control.
