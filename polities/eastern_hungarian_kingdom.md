name: Eastern Hungarian Kingdom
description: >-
  The Eastern Hungarian Kingdom was the realm of John Zápolya and his son John Sigismund, rival claimants to Hungary's crown after the major Ottoman victory at Mohács in 1526. They governed eastern Hungary and Transylvania, while Habsburg kings controlled the west. Ottoman support helped preserve their monarchy, but the capture of Buda in 1541 forced the royal court eastward, eventually to Gyulafehérvár. Queen Isabella and George Martinuzzi governed during John Sigismund's minority. Habsburg control interrupted the kingdom from 1551 to 1556, before the eastern estates restored the Zápolyas. Religious change culminated in the Edict of Torda in 1568. Under the Treaty of Speyer in 1570, John Sigismund surrendered his formal royal claim and continued as prince of Transylvania and Partium.
