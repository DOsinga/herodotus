description: >-
  Early Pachacamac developed around a religious center in Peru's Lurín Valley, southeast of
  present Lima, approximately between 200 and 1000 CE. This record represents the local governing
  community before the later Ichma political order, whose rulers cannot be projected backward
  into this period. Settlement and temple construction began under the influence of the Lima
  culture. The Old Temple used small adobe bricks, while later buildings and objects show
  connections with the Wari world. A carved wooden idol dated to the eighth or ninth century
  provides evidence for the sanctuary's early religious importance. These connections do not
  establish that Wari officials replaced local government. The community's precise leadership and
  political institutions remain poorly documented, and the transition around 1000 is uncertain.
