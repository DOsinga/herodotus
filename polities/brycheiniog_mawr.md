description: >-
  Cantref Mawr was one of the separately governed portions of Brycheiniog in southeastern
  Wales during the eleventh century. Also called Cantref Tewdos, it is represented here
  from an estimated division around 1045 until the Norman conquest in 1093. Accounts
  describe the older kingdom being shared among heirs, producing the cantrefs of Mawr,
  Selyf, and Talgarth. Surviving genealogies are inconsistent, so neither the sequence of
  rulers nor the date of separation is secure. The territory lay in the area later
  associated with Brecon, though the marker does not identify a proven royal residence.
  Welsh rulers faced pressure from neighboring kingdoms and advancing Norman lords.
  Bernard de Neufmarché's conquest ended its separate government and established a marcher
  lordship under the English crown.
