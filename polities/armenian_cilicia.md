description: >-
  Cilician Armenia grew from a principality founded in 1080 into a kingdom on the
  northeastern Mediterranean coast, in what is now southern Turkey. Armenian migrants and
  nobles built a new political centre outside their ancestral highlands, first under the
  Rubenids and later the Hethumids and Lusignans. Leo received a royal crown in 1198.
  Kings governed through powerful noble families, while Sis became the capital and Ayas an
  important trading port. Close ties with Crusader states brought marriages, commerce, and
  Western court customs. The rulers also sought Mongol support against the Mamluks. These
  alliances could not prevent repeated invasions. Mamluk forces captured Sis in 1375,
  ending the kingdom, although Armenian communities and religious institutions remained in
  Cilicia afterward for many generations.
