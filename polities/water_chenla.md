description: >-
  Water Chenla is the Chinese name for the southern of the two realms into which Chenla
  divided shortly after 707, the northern one being Land Chenla. It held the Mekong delta
  and the lower river around the old centres of Funan. This record runs from 714, after the
  last inscription of the united court, to Jayavarman II's consolidation of the Khmer lands
  in 802. Pelliot placed its capital at Vyadhapura near Ba Phnom, while Coedès preferred
  Aninditapura on the opposite bank. The Tang histories record an embassy that
  probably came from Water Chenla in 750, but its kings and their order are poorly known.
  Javanese fleets raided the coast late in the eighth century, and later accounts suggest
  that Java claimed overlordship before Jayavarman II proclaimed his independence.
