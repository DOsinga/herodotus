description: >-
  The Beylik of Eshref was a Turkish principality centred on Beyşehir in southwestern Anatolia
  from about 1280 to 1326. It arose among the Oghuz frontier lordships that gained room to govern
  as the Seljuk Sultanate of Rum weakened. Suleiman ibn Eshref established the ruling house,
  whose beys maintained a regional monarchy beside other competing Anatolian dynasties. Their
  capital lay near the lake bearing Beyşehir's name, west of Konya. The surviving Eşrefoğlu
  Mosque reflects the court's patronage and the architectural traditions of this period. The
  principality remained exposed to the larger struggle between local rulers and Mongol authority.
  Timurtash's intervention ended the dynasty in 1326, bringing its separate government to a close
  under the Ilkhanate's assertion of control in central Anatolia.
