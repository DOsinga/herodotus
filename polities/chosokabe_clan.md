description: >-
  The Chōsokabe were the Sengoku daimyo house of Tosa province on the island of Shikoku,
  ruling from Okō Castle from 1560 until they submitted to Toyotomi Hideyoshi in 1585. Once
  one of several local houses of Tosa, they lost their head to the Motoyama clan in 1508.
  Chōsokabe Kunichika, raised under the protection of the Ichijō, took revenge on the Motoyama
  and destroyed them in 1560, the year of his death. His son Chōsokabe Motochika overthrew the
  Ichijō in 1574, won all of Tosa at Watarigawa in 1575 and spent the next decade conquering
  Awa, Sanuki and Iyo, so that by 1585 he held nearly all of Shikoku. That summer Hideyoshi
  invaded the island with a huge army and left him only Tosa.
