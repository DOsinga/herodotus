description: >-
  Min was a Wang ruled kingdom in mountainous Fujian on China's southeastern coast during
  the Five Dynasties and Ten Kingdoms period. Its kingdom lasted from 909 to 945, after the
  family's earlier service as provincial governors. Wang Shenzhi governed from Fuzhou and
  initially accepted titles from northern courts while exercising local power. The region's
  mountains encouraged political separation, and coastal commerce connected it with overseas
  markets. Later rulers adopted imperial titles, making the court's claims more ambitious.
  Succession struggles and violence within the ruling family undermined stability. Wang
  Yanzheng created a separate Yin state in the interior, intensifying division. Southern
  Tang conquered the royal government in 945. Fujian's other cities and regional commanders
  did not all lose their autonomy together.
