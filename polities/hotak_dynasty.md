description: >-
  Hotak Afghanistan was a state founded around Kandahar in 1709 by Mirwais Hotak, a Ghilzai
  Pashtun leader who rebelled against Safavid government. Its rulers drew support from local
  tribal networks and Sunni communities resentful of outside rule. Mirwais resisted attempts
  to restore Safavid control, establishing the basis for hereditary government. His son
  Mahmud invaded Iran and forced Shah Sultan Husayn to surrender Isfahan after a devastating
  siege in 1722. Hotak rule over the Persian capital remained unstable, troubled by
  violence, disputed succession and resistance. Ashraf succeeded Mahmud but lost to Nader's
  forces in 1729, ending the dynasty's control of Isfahan. A Hotak government survived at
  Kandahar under Hussain until Nader captured the city in 1738 and ended the state entirely.
