description: >-
  The Second French Empire governed France from 1852 to 1870 under Napoleon III. Louis-Napoléon
  Bonaparte, previously president, restored imperial rule after his coup against the republic. His
  government initially concentrated power in the emperor, restricting opposition and the press while
  using elections and referendums to claim popular approval. Railway construction, banking and
  industrial investment accelerated, and Haussmann's rebuilding transformed Paris. Abroad, France
  fought in Crimea and Italy, expanded colonial possessions and undertook a disastrous intervention in
  Mexico. During the 1860s the regime allowed greater parliamentary authority and political debate.
  War against Prussia in 1870 brought military defeat and Napoleon's capture at Sedan. A revolution in
  Paris proclaimed the republic, replacing the empire while the war itself continued beyond that year.
