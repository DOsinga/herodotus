description: >-
  Mihrabanid Sistan was the principality of the Mihrabanid maliks, a Persian Sunni dynasty that
  governed Sistan, also called Nimruz, from 1236 until the Safavid takeover in the sixteenth
  century. The maliks emerged after the Mongol conquest and recognised the Ilkhans, whose
  distant court left them wide autonomy. They fought the Kartid maliks of Herat and by 1289
  had taken Quhistan. After the Ilkhanate collapsed they were independent until Timur
  devastated Sistan in 1383, and they then ruled as Timurid vassals. A malik could inherit the
  throne or be chosen by the nobles and commanders, and outlying towns were often given to
  relatives. Malik Sultan Mahmud accepted Safavid overlordship and finally handed Sistan over,
  ending the dynasty's government.
