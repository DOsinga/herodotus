description: >-
  Chakla, or Chala, was a Tibetan kingdom in eastern Kham centred on Dartsedo, the modern
  Kangding, on the old border between Tibet and China. A ruling house had established itself
  around the town by the late thirteenth century, and the Ming granted its king a title in
  1407. Dartsedo grew rich as the market where porters from Sichuan brought brick tea to
  trade for Tibetan wool, horses and medicines, and the kings lived off that trade. In the
  seventeenth century the Ganden Phodrang government in Lhasa placed a commissioner in the
  town to tax it. The kings rebelled in 1666 with Qing backing, and Tibetan troops occupied
  the town five years later. After the Qing stormed Dartsedo in 1701 the kings ruled on as
  Qing native chiefs until 1911.
