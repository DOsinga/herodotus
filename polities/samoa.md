description: >-
  Samoa is an independent Polynesian state in the South Pacific, established in 1962 after
  New Zealand administration. Its main islands are Upolu and Savai'i, with Apia on Upolu
  serving as the capital. Independence made Western Samoa the first small island country in
  the Pacific to regain sovereignty after colonial rule. The state adopted a parliamentary
  constitution while preserving an place for Samoan chiefly customs and village life. The
  Mau movement's peaceful resistance had helped shape the struggle against foreign
  administration, including memories of colonial repression and the devastating influenza
  epidemic. Samoa maintained a friendship treaty with New Zealand and joined the
  Commonwealth. In 1997 it dropped Western from its official name. The independent country
  remains distinct from neighbouring American Samoa.
