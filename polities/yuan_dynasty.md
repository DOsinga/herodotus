description: >-
  The Yuan dynasty was the Mongol imperial state proclaimed by Kublai Khan in 1271, with
  its main capital at Dadu, now Beijing. Its conquest of the Southern Song in 1279
  brought China under one government alongside extensive Mongol territories farther north
  and west. Emperors combined Chinese administrative institutions with Mongol court
  traditions, using provincial governments, military commands and officials drawn from
  several peoples. Paper currency, the postal relay network and overseas commerce helped
  connect its cities with wider Eurasian exchange. Expensive campaigns against Japan and
  Southeast Asia achieved limited results. Succession struggles, fiscal problems and
  rebellion weakened later rulers. In 1368, Ming forces captured the capital Dadu, while
  the imperial court withdrew into Mongolia and continued as the Northern Yuan.
