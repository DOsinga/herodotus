description: >-
  The Senior jüz was the southernmost of the three Kazakh hordes, centred on Semirechye and the
  middle Syr Darya around Tashkent and Turkistan. After Tauke Khan's death around 1718 its clans
  followed their own leaders. The Dzungar invasion of 1723 overran its pastures and towns,
  beginning years of devastation that Kazakh tradition remembers as the Great Disaster, and this
  record has a gap while the Dzungars held it. After the Dzungar state fell in 1758 the Senior jüz
  governed itself again under its khans and judges, and Tashkent was for a time ruled by Kazakh
  and local leaders. In the early nineteenth century the Khanate of Kokand extended its forts and
  taxes over much of its land, ending its independence before Russia absorbed the region.
