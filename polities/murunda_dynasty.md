description: >-
  The Murunda kingdom ruled Utkala, the northern part of coastal Odisha, in the second and third
  centuries CE, after the Mahameghavahana kings of Kalinga. Very little is known about it. A
  Prakrit land grant found near Bhadrak, issued in the eighth year of a Maharaja Gana, is the
  main record of its early rule, and a gold coin bearing the name of a later king,
  Dhamadamadhara, was found at Sisupalgarh, the old fortified capital beside modern
  Bhubaneswar. Chinese sources describe an envoy of the Funan kingdom of Cambodia who was
  received in Kalinga in the third century, which shows the region's sea trade with Southeast
  Asia. Jain communities continued to flourish under these
  kings. The Nagas of Vindhyatabi and the Guptas later took over the region.
