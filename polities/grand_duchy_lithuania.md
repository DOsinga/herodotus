description: >-
  The Grand Duchy of Lithuania emerged in the thirteenth century from the union of Baltic communities and became a vast
  monarchy ruled from Vilnius. This record begins in 1236 and follows its separate government until the Union of Lublin in
  1569. Its rulers expanded into former Rus' lands, creating a realm with Lithuanian, Ruthenian and other populations and several religious
  traditions. Pagan Lithuania resisted crusading orders before Jogaila's conversion and marriage established a dynastic connection with Poland in 1386. Under
  Vytautas, the duchy reached its greatest extent and helped defeat the Teutonic Knights at Grunwald. Nobles gained increasing influence as
  wars with Moscow strained the state. The 1569 union created the Polish Lithuanian Commonwealth, although Lithuania kept separate institutions thereafter.
