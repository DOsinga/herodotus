description: >-
  The Kingdom of Vanand, also called the Kingdom of Kars, was an Armenian monarchy from 963
  to 1065. It formed when Mushegh Bagratuni established a separate royal branch at Kars
  after the main Bagratid court moved to Ani. Its kings remained relatives of Ani's rulers
  and shared the wider Armenian political and religious world, but maintained their own
  government. Kars served as their capital in the uplands of present-day northeastern
  Turkey. Following Byzantium's annexation of Ani in 1045, the ruler of Kars inherited the
  Bagratid title King of Kings. Seljuk conquest of Ani in 1064 prompted Gagik-Abas to pay
  tribute. He transferred his kingdom to Byzantium in 1065, ending its separate monarchy;
  Seljuk forces soon gained Kars despite that protection.
