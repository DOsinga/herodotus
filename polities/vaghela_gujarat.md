name: "Vaghela Kingdom of Gujarat"
description: >-
  The Vaghela Kingdom ruled Gujarat in western India from 1244 to 1304, interrupted by a Delhi
  occupation in 1299. Its kings came from a family that had gained power as generals and
  ministers under the weakening Chaulukya monarchy. Visaladeva's accession established their own
  royal line, governing from Dholka and campaigning against neighbouring states in Malwa, Mewar,
  and the Deccan. Wealthy ministers and merchants supported Jain temples, while royal building
  projects included the carved gateways and fortifications at Dabhoi. Coinage and inscriptions
  help trace the kingdom's political and cultural life. Alauddin Khalji's first invasion drove
  out King Karna, who recovered territory after the occupation forces withdrew around 1300. A
  second Delhi conquest in 1304 permanently ended his independent government in Gujarat.
