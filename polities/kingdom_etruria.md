description: >-
  The Kingdom of Etruria occupied much of Tuscany in central Italy from 1801 to
  1807, with its capital at Florence. Created through an agreement between France
  and Spain, it replaced the Grand Duchy of Tuscany and provided a throne for the
  Bourbon family displaced from Parma. Louis I became king, followed after his
  death by his young son Louis II under the regency of Maria Luisa. Its ancient
  regional name did not imply continuity with the Etruscan city states. The
  monarchy operated within Napoleon's influence over Italy, and its room for
  independent action remained limited. In 1807 Napoleon removed the ruling family
  and annexed the kingdom to the French Empire. Its lands were reorganised into
  departments under direct French administration.
