description: >-
  Adab was a Sumerian city-state in southern Mesopotamia, at modern Bismaya in Iraq,
  represented here during the Early Dynastic period until Sargon's conquest around 2334
  BCE. Temples, inscriptions, statues, and administrative tablets provide evidence of its
  rulers and urban institutions. Kings and governors are known, although their order and
  relations with neighbouring powers are often uncertain. Lugal-Anne-Mundu appears in the
  Sumerian King List, but later claims of his enormous empire should not be treated as
  secure contemporary history. Adab maintained temples to deities and participated in the
  wider network of Sumerian cities and trade. Incorporation into the Akkadian empire ended
  its separate government in this record. The city remained inhabited afterward, with
  appointed governors and repeated rebellions against imperial authority.
