description: >-
  The Golden Ambrosian Republic was the short-lived commune proclaimed in Milan in August 1447,
  after Filippo Maria Visconti died without a male heir. Leading citizens and jurists revived
  republican institutions named after the city's patron saint, Ambrose, governing through captains
  and defenders of liberty. Subject cities such as Pavia, Lodi and Piacenza broke away or were
  contested, and Venice sought to exploit the confusion. The republic hired Francesco Sforza,
  Visconti's son-in-law and a leading condottiero, to defend it, but he turned against his
  employers. Factional conflict, war costs and famine weakened the government. Sforza blockaded
  Milan, and in 1450 the hungry city opened its gates and acclaimed him duke, restoring the duchy
  under a new dynasty.
