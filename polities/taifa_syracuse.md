description: >-
  The Taifa of Syracuse was a Muslim lordship in southeastern Sicily, from the collapse of
  the Kalbid emirate around 1053 until the Normans took Syracuse in 1086. Muhammad ibn al-
  Thumna first ruled Syracuse and won Catania by killing its lord. Losing his war with Ibn
  al-Hawwas of Agrigento, he invited the Norman brothers Robert Guiscard and Roger into
  Sicily in 1061, and was killed in 1062. Later Ibn Abbad, known to the Normans as
  Benavert, led the last resistance from Syracuse: he defeated Roger's son Jordan in 1075,
  retook Catania in 1081 and raided Calabria. He drowned in 1086 during the Norman siege
  of Syracuse, which then surrendered. Noto and Butera held out until 1091.
