description: >-
  Kalinga was a kingdom on India's eastern coast, chiefly associated with present Odisha
  and neighboring northern Andhra Pradesh. This record covers its independent phase,
  beginning at an estimated 500 BCE and ending with Ashoka's conquest in 261 BCE.
  Information about its earliest rulers is limited, and the linked regional history also
  describes many later states bearing the Kalinga name. Ancient accounts portray a king
  supported by infantry, cavalry, and elephants. Its coastal location connected inland
  settlements with maritime routes. Ashoka's invasion brought heavy losses, remembered
  in his inscriptions as a cause of remorse over warfare and suffering. Tosali became an
  administrative center under Mauryan rule. Later Kalinga kingdoms revived after Mauryan
  decline, but are separate from the kingdom represented here.
