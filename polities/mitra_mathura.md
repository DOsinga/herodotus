description: >-
  The Mitra dynasty of Mathura was a line of local rulers who governed the city of Mathura
  on the Yamuna between about 150 and 60 BCE, after Shunga rule there had faded. Seven of
  them are known, including Gomitra, Brahmamitra and Vishnumitra, almost entirely from
  copper coins that carry their names without any royal title. Excavations at Sonkh near
  Mathura found their coins in layers dated to this period. Because an inscription at
  Mathura is dated by the reign of the Yavanas, they may have paid tribute to the Indo-Greek
  kings, and they are sometimes linked with a related line of rulers whose names end in
  -datta. The Indo-Scythian Northern Satraps replaced them at Mathura around 60 BCE.
