name: "Kingdom of Usan"
description: >-
  Usan was an island kingdom centred on Ulleungdo in the sea east of the Korean Peninsula. Its beginning around 400 is an estimate, since secure surviving written evidence appears with its submission to Silla in 512. Korean tradition credits General Isabu with frightening the islanders into surrender using wooden lions. Sources describe a small local monarchy, but reveal little about its rulers or internal institutions. Tribute linked the community first to Silla and later to Goryeo, without this record treating those relationships as immediate abolition of local government. Devastating Jurchen attacks in the early eleventh century damaged the island and displaced its people. Goryeo sent agricultural aid, but refugee resettlement and incorporation in 1022 ended the separate community represented here.
