description: >-
  The Commune of Ravenna was the self-governing city of Ravenna in Romagna, from the early
  twelfth century until Venice took it in 1441. The archbishops, once great imperial
  princes, gave way to a commune whose politics were led by noble families such as the
  Traversari. In 1275 Guido da Polenta made himself lord, and his family governed the city
  and nearby Cervia for the next century and a half under nominal papal sovereignty. Guido
  Novello da Polenta sheltered Dante, who finished the Divine Comedy in Ravenna and died
  there in 1321. The later da Polenta leaned on Venice for protection, and in 1441 the
  Venetians deposed Ostasio III and took the city for themselves.
