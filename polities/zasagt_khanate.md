description: >-
  The Zasagt Khan khanate was the western of the Khalkha Mongol khanates, lying between the
  Khangai mountains and the lands of the Oirats in present western Mongolia. Its rulers descended
  from Ashikhai, eldest son of Gersenji, and in the late sixteenth century Laihur took the title
  of khan; his son Subadai called himself Zasagt Khan. The khanate stood on the frontier with the
  Oirat peoples, and its rulers sometimes allied with the Oirats and at other times fought them,
  including the Khotogoid Altan Khans to the northwest. A dispute over subjects between the Zasagt
  and Tüsheet khans in the 1680s drew in the Dzungar ruler Galdan, whose invasion of Khalkha in
  1688 led the khans to submit to the Qing in 1691.
