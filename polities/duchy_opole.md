description: >-
  The Duchy of Opole was a duchy of Upper Silesia, governed by a branch of the Silesian Piasts
  from the castle at Opole on the Oder. Upper Silesia separated from Wrocław in 1172 and 1173,
  when Mieszko Tanglefoot took Racibórz and his nephew Jarosław took Opole, and Mieszko united
  both towns in 1202. In 1281 the land was divided into the duchies of Opole, Racibórz, Bytom and
  Cieszyn. Duke Bolesław of Opole paid homage to John of Bohemia in 1327, and his successors ruled
  on as vassals of the Bohemian crown. Vladislaus of Opole was a powerful figure in Hungary and
  Poland in the fourteenth century. Jan II the Good reunited Opole and Racibórz in 1521, and when
  he died in 1532 the duchy passed to Margrave George of Brandenburg-Ansbach.
