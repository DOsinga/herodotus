description: >-
  The Duchy of Opole was a duchy of Upper Silesia, governed by a branch of the Silesian Piasts
  from the castle at Opole on the Oder. Upper Silesia separated from Wrocław in 1172 and 1173,
  when Mieszko Tanglefoot took Racibórz and his nephew Jarosław took Opole. Mieszko united both
  towns in 1202 as the Duchy of Opole and Racibórz, and his descendants held it through the Mongol
  invasion of 1241. In 1281 the sons of Władysław of Opole divided the land into the duchies of
  Opole, Racibórz, Bytom and Cieszyn. Duke Bolesław II of Opole paid homage to John of Bohemia in
  1327, making the duchy a fief of the Bohemian crown. Opole Piasts ruled on as vassals until the
  last of them died in 1532.
