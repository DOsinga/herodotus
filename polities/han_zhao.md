description: >-
  Han-Zhao was a northern Chinese state founded by Liu Yuan in 304 during the breakdown of
  Western Jin authority. Its ruling family came from Xiongnu communities settled inside China,
  but used Chinese imperial offices alongside institutions linked to the chanyu. Liu Yuan
  called his state Han to claim continuity with the Chinese dynasty through ancestral marriage
  ties. Its armies captured Luoyang in 311 and Chang'an in 316, destroying Western Jin's
  northern government. Pingyang served as an early capital. After violent succession
  struggles, Liu Yao moved the court to Chang'an and renamed the state Zhao in 319. His former
  ally Shi Le established the rival Later Zhao. Shi Le's forces captured Liu Yao and
  eliminated his successors, absorbing Han-Zhao in 329.
