description: >-
  The Ichma Kingdom was a pre-Inca polity on the central coast of Peru, in the Lurín valley and
  later also the Rímac valley where Lima now stands. It formed after the decline of Wari and was
  governed from Pachacamac, the great oracle centre, which its people knew as Ichma. The kingdom
  was organised in two divisions, each split into smaller lordships and moieties, with dominions
  such as Surco, Rimac and Maranga in the Rímac valley and Pachacamac and Manchay in the Lurín.
  Its builders raised at least sixteen pyramids at Pachacamac and many adobe platforms across the
  Lima area, and elite tombs show trade reaching the Amazon. The Inca absorbed the kingdom around
  1469 and made it a province named after Pachacamac.
