description: >-
  The Baloch chiefdom of Bampur stands for the Baloch and Koch chiefs who governed the Bampur
  valley in the interior of Persian Makran, now in Iran's Sistan and Baluchestan province.
  Bampur's fort on its hill controlled the river valley and the routes between Kerman, Sistan
  and the Makran coast. When the Banu Ilyas held Kerman in the tenth century, the southern and
  eastern mountains stayed with the Baloch and Koch until the Buyid campaigns of the 970s.
  After the Seljuks of Kerman fell in 1187, local chiefs again held the valley for centuries.
  Safavid governors and later the khans of Kalat claimed it and took tribute, but the chiefs,
  in the nineteenth century the Rakshani family of Mihrab Khan, ruled until the Qajar conquest.
