name: Russian Empire
description: >-
  The Russian Empire existed from 1721, when Peter the Great adopted the imperial title, until the
  monarchy fell in 1917. Governed by Romanov emperors from Saint Petersburg, it expanded across
  eastern Europe, the Caucasus, Central Asia and Siberia, becoming one of history's largest
  territorial states. Its population included many languages, religions and legal traditions, but
  supreme authority rested with the emperor. Serfdom shaped rural society until its abolition in 1861.
  Industrial growth, military defeats and demands for political reform strained imperial government.
  The revolution of 1905 brought a parliament without removing the monarch's dominant powers. During
  the First World War, economic hardship and unrest culminated in the February Revolution, Nicholas
  II's abdication and the establishment of a republic that year.
