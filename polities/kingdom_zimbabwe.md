description: >-
  The Kingdom of Zimbabwe was a Shona state on the southern African plateau, represented
  here from about 1220 to 1450. Its centre at Great Zimbabwe grew into a major settlement
  marked by impressive stone walls built without mortar. Kings and elites drew authority
  from control of cattle, land, ritual and trade, although the details of government are
  reconstructed largely from archaeology and later traditions. Gold reached Indian Ocean
  markets through connections with Swahili coastal towns, while imported beads and
  ceramics demonstrate distant commercial links. The capital's hill, enclosure and valley
  settlements reveal a urban landscape. From the fifteenth century, regional power shifted
  toward centres associated with Mutapa and Butua. Great Zimbabwe's decline was gradual,
  and occupation continued beyond this period.
