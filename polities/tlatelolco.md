description: >-
  Tlatelolco was a Mexica city-state on the northern part of an island in Lake Texcoco, in the
  Valley of Mexico. It began in 1337 when a group separated from neighbouring Tenochtitlan.
  Dynastic rulers, called tlatoque, led the city, whose temples grew through successive
  building programmes. Its vast marketplace connected local producers and merchants with goods
  from distant parts of Mesoamerica, making it an essential commercial centre even for its
  rival neighbour. Tlatelolco sometimes fought alongside Tenochtitlan against other states,
  but competition between the two cities repeatedly caused conflict. In 1473, Axayacatl
  defeated its ruler Moquihuix and made it subject to Tenochtitlan. The city retained its
  commercial importance afterwards and became the final stronghold of Mexica resistance during
  the Spanish conquest.
