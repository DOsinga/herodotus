description: >-
  The Ukhaydhirid Emirate was a small state in al-Yamama, the region of central Arabia around
  present-day Riyadh and al-Kharj, founded in 867 by Muhammad ibn Yusuf al-Ukhaydhir. He was
  an Alid, descended from the Prophet through al-Hasan, who fled there after a failed revolt
  against the Abbasids, and probably won over the Banu Hanifa, the main local tribe. The
  caliphs had long neglected the area, and the emirate governed itself from its capital at
  al-Khidrima. The family were remembered as Zaydi Shi'ites. The emir Isma'il allied with the
  Qarmatians of al-Hasa and helped them take Kufa in 925, but they killed him in 928 and the
  emirate then fell under their influence. The traveller Nasir-i Khusraw still found the
  dynasty ruling in 1051.
