description: >-
  The Duchy of Kuyavia was a Piast duchy in north central Poland, between Greater Poland, Masovia
  and the Vistula. It belonged to Masovia after the division of Poland in 1138 and became a duchy
  of its own in 1233, when Konrad of Masovia gave it to his son Casimir I. Casimir's sons divided
  it in 1267: Ziemomysł took Inowrocław and Władysław the Elbow-high took Brześć, from which he
  went on to reunite the Polish kingdom in 1320. Later partitions created further small duchies
  around Gniewkowo and Bydgoszcz. The dukes of Inowrocław were briefly vassals of Wenceslaus II of
  Bohemia and then of their royal cousin. In 1327 Przemysł of Inowrocław exchanged his duchy for
  Sieradz, and the Teutonic Order occupied Kuyavia from 1332 to 1343.
