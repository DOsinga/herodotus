description: >-
  Halicarnassus was a Greek city in Caria, on the southwestern coast of Anatolia at the site of
  modern Bodrum. Founded by Dorian settlers, it later became the capital of the Hecatomnid
  rulers of Caria and the site of the Mausoleum. After Alexander took it in 334 BCE it passed
  between his successors and spent most of the third century under Ptolemaic rule. When the
  Ptolemaic hold on Caria collapsed in 197 BCE, Rhodes stepped in to defend the city's freedom
  against the Seleucid king Antiochus III. Halicarnassus then remained outside both Seleucid
  and Rhodian rule, a self-governing city allied first with Rhodes and later with Rome. This
  record covers that free city until the Romans organised their province of Asia after the
  Attalid bequest.
