description: >-
  The Awraba were a Berber tribe of the Baranis branch, recorded here as a chiefdom centred on
  Volubilis from 740 to 788. Under their king Kusaila they led the resistance to the Arab
  conquest of the Maghreb, defeating and killing Uqba ibn Nafi in 683 before Kusaila fell
  around 688. After his defeat part of the tribe moved west to the region of Volubilis, where
  they lived beside the town's Latin-speaking community. When Arab rule in Morocco collapsed
  in the Berber Revolt of 740, the Awraba governed the area under their own chiefs. In 788
  their leader Ishaq ibn Muhammad welcomed Idris ibn Abdallah, a descendant of the Prophet
  fleeing the Abbasids, and made him their imam, founding the Idrisid state.
