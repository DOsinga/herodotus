description: >-
  The Principality of Bayreuth, also called Brandenburg-Kulmbach, was a Hohenzollern state in
  Upper Franconia, covering the upland country of the Fichtelgebirge and the upper Main. It was the
  upland share of the lands of the burgraves of Nuremberg after their division in 1398, and its
  rulers took the title of margrave after the family gained Brandenburg in 1415. Until 1604 the
  margraves resided at the Plassenburg above Kulmbach, then moved to Bayreuth. The principality
  turned Lutheran in the 1520s and suffered badly in the Second Margrave War and the Thirty
  Years' War. In the eighteenth century Margravine Wilhelmine made Bayreuth a centre of court
  culture. The last margrave sold it, with Ansbach, to Prussia in 1791.
