description: >-
  The Pingnan State was the government of the Panthay Rebellion in western Yunnan, based at
  Dali and lasting from 1856 to 1872. In 1856 a massacre of Muslims organised by Qing
  officials around Kunming set off risings across the province. In the west the rebels took
  Dali and made Du Wenxiu, a Muslim from a Han family of Yongchang, their leader. He ruled as
  sultan with a Chinese-style bureaucracy in which Han Chinese held many posts, honoured
  Islam, Confucianism and local religions alike, and called for driving the Manchus out of
  China. His forces repeatedly attacked Kunming, while the Muslim leader Ma Rulong went over to
  the Qing. After the Qing recovered from the Taiping war, their armies closed in, and Dali
  fell at the end of 1872.
