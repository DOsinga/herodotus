description: >-
  The Principality of Minsk was a Rus principality in present Belarus, split off from Polotsk when
  Vseslav of Polotsk died in 1101 and his lands were divided among his sons. Gleb Vseslavich made
  Minsk the seat of his own branch of the family and fought his brothers and the princes of Kiev,
  until Vladimir Monomakh besieged the town and Mstislav the Great carried the Polotsk princes off
  in 1129. Gleb's widow is thought to have ruled for decades afterwards. Later princes of Minsk
  fought those of Drutsk, Vitebsk and Polotsk, sometimes with Lithuanian help, and the
  principality escaped the Mongol invasion. In the thirteenth century its princes came under
  growing Lithuanian influence, and by 1326 Minsk was governed as part of the Grand Duchy of
  Lithuania.
