description: >-
  The Principality of Thessaly was the state of a branch of the Komnenos Doukas dynasty of
  Epirus, which ruled Thessaly from Neopatras between 1268 and 1318. John I Doukas, an
  illegitimate son of the despot Michael II, received Thessaly as his share of the
  inheritance and governed it as sebastokrator. He fought off repeated attacks by Michael
  VIII Palaiologos, who wanted the region back for the restored Byzantine Empire, and
  allied with the Latin lords of Athens and Achaea. His sons Constantine and Theodore
  followed him, and his grandson John II made peace with Constantinople. When John II died
  without heirs in 1318, the Catalan Company took the south, local magnates the north, and
  the Byzantines later recovered it.
