description: >-
  The Lordship of Gjirokastër was the domain of the Albanian Zenebishi family in northern
  Epirus, held by John Zenevisi from about 1386 until the Ottomans took Gjirokastër in
  1418. The Zenebishi came from the Zagoria district between Përmet and Gjirokastër, and
  in the 1380s their clan raided the despotate of Epirus. John submitted to the Ottomans
  after their victory at Savra in 1385, but soon seized the fortress of Gjirokastër and
  took the Byzantine title of sebastokrator. He married into the Shpata family of Arta and
  fought the Tocco and the despots of Ioannina, and for a time held Vagenetia on the coast
  opposite Corfu. The Ottoman campaigns of 1417 and 1418 ended his lordship.
