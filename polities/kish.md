description: >-
  Kish was an Early Dynastic city state in Mesopotamia, near modern Tell al Uhaymir in Iraq,
  represented from about 2900 to 2334 BCE. Its settlement was older, but this record concerns the
  independent kingdom before Akkadian conquest. The Sumerian King List gave Kish the first
  kingship after the flood, mixing historical memory with legendary rulers. Archaeology reveals
  palaces, temples and elite burials, while inscriptions confirm figures such as Enmebaragesi. The
  title king of Kish became a claim to authority, used even by rulers based elsewhere. Kish
  competed with other cities in a shifting landscape of regional power. Tradition describes Sargon
  as cupbearer to King Ur Zababa before his rise. Its incorporation into Sargon's empire ended
  this independent phase of government.
