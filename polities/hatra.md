description: >-
  Hatra was a fortified caravan city and kingdom in northern Mesopotamia, southwest of
  modern Mosul in Iraq. Its local government is represented from about 117 to 241, beginning
  around the city's successful resistance to Emperor Trajan. Lords of Hatra later adopted
  royal titles and governed the surrounding region, sometimes called Araba, within the
  Parthian political sphere. The city occupied a frontier position between empires and
  supported both trade and religious pilgrimage. Its temples combined Mesopotamian, Greek
  and Iranian influences, with Shamash particularly prominent. Strong walls helped repel
  Roman attacks, including those of Septimius Severus. The rise of the Sasanians changed the
  balance of power. Shapur I captured Hatra in 240 or 241, ending its kingdom and leaving
  the city deserted.
