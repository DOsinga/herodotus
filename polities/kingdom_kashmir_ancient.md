description: >-
  The Kingdom of Kashmir before the Karkotas covers the native kings of the Kashmir valley in the
  centuries when no outside empire governed it, recorded here from the end of Mauryan rule in
  185 BCE to the Kushan conquest, and again from the end of Kushan rule to the Alchon conquest
  in the early sixth century. Almost everything known of these kings comes from Kalhana's
  Rajatarangini, a twelfth-century chronicle that groups them into Gonanda and Gonanditya
  dynasties and gives reigns of impossible length. The valley was a centre of Buddhist learning
  in this period, and the scholar Kumarajiva studied there before going to China. Coins confirm
  few of the rulers, so the record stands for the valley's own government rather than a
  documented line of kings.
