description: >-
  The Despotate of Dobruja, also called the Principality of Karvuna, was a Bulgarian
  principality on the western Black Sea coast that broke away from the Second Bulgarian
  Empire in the 1340s and lasted until the Ottoman conquest of 1388. Its first ruler,
  Balik, governed from Karvuna, and his brother Dobrotitsa, despot from 1357, moved the
  capital to Kaliakra and made the principality a naval power. Dobrotitsa received Varna
  for his help against Amadeus VI of Savoy in 1366, intervened in the Byzantine civil wars
  and fought Genoa over Black Sea trade. His son Ivanko made peace with the Ottomans and
  Genoa, but the Ottomans took Dobruja in 1388, and Mircea of Wallachia soon contested it.
