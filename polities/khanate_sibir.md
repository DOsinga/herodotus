description: >-
  The Khanate of Sibir governed western Siberia from 1468 to 1598, developing out of the fragmentation of earlier steppe empires. Its rulers controlled centers including Chimgi-Tura and Qashliq, with competing Taibugid and Shaybanid families contesting the throne. The population included Siberian Tatars, Khanty, Mansi, and Selkup communities. Sunni Islam influenced the ruling court, while many inhabitants retained other religious practices. Under Kuchum, efforts to extend Islamic observance accompanied resistance to Russian expansion. Yermak's Cossacks defeated the khan's army in 1582 and captured Qashliq, but Kuchum continued fighting after that defeat. Russian forts and further campaigns gradually reduced his power. His final defeat in 1598 ended the khanate's government and opened its former lands to more extensive Russian colonization and rule.
