description: >-
  The Yufirid Emirate ruled parts of Yemen's highlands from 847 to 997, based especially
  at Shibam Kawkaban and intermittently at Sanaa. Yu'fir established its authority as
  direct Abbasid administration weakened, conquering a highland area after an earlier
  failed assault on Sanaa. His descendants followed Sunni Islam and initially acknowledged
  the Abbasid caliphs without surrendering their local government. Family murders and
  rivalries repeatedly weakened the dynasty. Zaydi imams, tribal leaders and missionaries
  supporting the Fatimids contested Sanaa, which changed hands frequently. Even when
  expelled from the city, the Yufirids retained strongholds elsewhere. Abdallah ibn Qahtan
  later attacked the Ziyadid capital of Zabid and transferred his formal allegiance to the
  Fatimids. His death in 997 marked the end of effective Yufirid power.
