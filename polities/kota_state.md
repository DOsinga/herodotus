description: >-
  Kota State was a Rajput kingdom of the Hada Chauhans on the east bank of the Chambal river,
  in the Hadoti region of present Rajasthan. Kota had been part of Bundi until 1631, when Rao
  Ratan Singh of Bundi gave it to his second son, Madho Singh, with the approval of Shah
  Jahan, in reward for his service to the Mughals. Its rulers fought in Mughal campaigns and
  became a leading power in Rajputana. In the eighteenth century the regent Zalim Singh
  dominated the state for decades and kept it intact through the Maratha wars. The city was
  famous for its fortifications and its school of miniature painting. A treaty with the East
  India Company at the end of 1817 brought Kota under British protection.
