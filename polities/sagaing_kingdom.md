name: Sagaing Kingdom
description: >-
  The Sagaing Kingdom governed northern parts of Upper Burma from 1315 to 1364, with its court at
  Sagaing beside the Irrawaddy River. Prince Saw Yun established a separate centre of power
  against his father, the king of Pinya, and autonomy gradually became recognised separation. Its
  hereditary monarchy ruled a smaller and less wealthy territory than its southern neighbour.
  Palace struggles weakened successive kings, and the minister Nanda Pakyan exercised influence
  over the court. Rivalry with Pinya later gave way to cooperation as raids from the Shan state
  of Maw threatened both realms. Maw armies sacked Sagaing in 1364. Thado Minbya then seized the
  devastated kingdoms, bringing their territories together in the new political order centred on
  Ava in central Burma.
