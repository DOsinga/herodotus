description: >-
  Yuwen Huaji's Xu was a brief imperial state in northern China during the collapse of the
  Sui dynasty in 618 and 619. Yuwen took command after the mutiny that killed Emperor Yang
  at Jiangdu, initially placing the Sui prince Yang Hao on the throne. His army then moved
  north with court officials and treasure, but faced shortages, desertions and resistance
  from rival leaders. In autumn 618, Yuwen killed Yang Hao and proclaimed himself emperor of
  Xu. Unable to overcome his opponents, he withdrew to Liaocheng, where Tang forces besieged
  him. Dou Jiande's Xia army subsequently arrived and captured the city after a defender
  opened its gates. Dou executed Yuwen and his sons, bringing the short lived government to
  an end.
