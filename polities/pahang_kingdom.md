description: >-
  The Pahang Kingdom was a Malay state on the east coast of the Malay Peninsula, ruled by the
  bendahara family from Pekan on the Pahang river. Pahang had been part of the Johor empire since
  1623, held as a fief by the bendahara, the empire's chief minister. From 1770 Tun Abdul Majid
  ruled it as a raja, and as Johor broke apart after the Anglo-Dutch Treaty of 1824 the bendaharas
  governed Pahang on their own. Tun Ali formally renounced allegiance to Johor in 1853. His death
  led to a civil war that Wan Ahmad won in 1863, and his chiefs proclaimed him sultan in 1881. In
  1888 Britain forced him to accept a British resident, ending Pahang's independence.
