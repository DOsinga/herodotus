description: >-
  Dominica is an island republic in the eastern Caribbean, independent from Britain
  since 3 November 1978. Roseau is the capital and port. The constitution established
  a parliamentary system with a president as head of state and a prime minister
  responsible to the House of Assembly. Independence followed years of internal
  self-government as a British associated state. Early political upheaval brought the
  fall of Patrick John's administration, while Hurricane David devastated the island in
  1979. Eugenia Charles became prime minister in 1980 and led the country through
  attempted coups and economic difficulties. Dominica's mountainous landscape, forests,
  and surviving Kalinago community distinguish it from many neighboring islands.
  Agriculture, including bananas, shaped its economy, with tourism becoming important
  as the independent republic continued.
