description: >-
  Rhodesia was an unrecognised state in southern Africa, occupying present-day Zimbabwe from its unilateral declaration of independence in 1965 until British authority resumed in December 1979. Ian Smith's government broke with Britain to preserve white minority control rather than accept majority rule. It retained parliamentary institutions with a racially restricted electorate and became a republic in 1970. International sanctions and an armed struggle led by African nationalist movements shaped its existence. Agriculture, mining and manufacturing continued despite the embargo, aided by regional connections. A negotiated internal settlement produced Zimbabwe Rhodesia under Abel Muzorewa in 1979, included within this record. That government failed to end the war or win recognition. The Lancaster House agreement restored British administration before Zimbabwe's recognised independence.
