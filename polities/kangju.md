description: >-
  Kangju was a Central Asian kingdom known mainly from Chinese accounts, represented here
  from approximately 200 BCE to 450 CE. Its heartland lay around the middle Syr Darya, with
  connections to the Tashkent region and Sogdiana. Chinese observers described a ruler and
  several subordinate kings, alongside a population combining mobile pastoral life with
  settled communities. Its capital, called Bitian or Beitian, has not been securely located.
  Kangju participated in the shifting alliances linking the Han empire, Dayuan, the Yuezhi,
  and steppe powers. It could support neighbouring states against Chinese campaigns while
  maintaining its diplomatic contacts. Later sources record declining power as new
  confederations advanced across Central Asia. Its final political disappearance is
  uncertain, so this record uses an estimated endpoint.
