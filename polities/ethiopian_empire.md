description: >-
  The Ethiopian Empire began in 1270 when Yekuno Amlak overthrew the Zagwe rulers and established
  the Solomonic dynasty. Centred on the Ethiopian highlands, its Christian monarchy claimed descent from Solomon
  and the Queen of Sheba. Emperors worked with regional nobles and the Ethiopian Orthodox Church,
  though their authority varied greatly across centuries. Wars with neighbouring Muslim states, internal rivalries and
  shifting royal centres shaped its history. Menelik II expanded the empire and defeated Italy at
  Adwa in 1896, preserving independence during European colonial expansion. Italy occupied Ethiopia from 1936 to
  1941, interrupting imperial rule. Haile Selassie returned after liberation and pursued centralisation and reform. In
  1974, revolution and military intervention deposed him, ending the monarchy and establishing a new regime.
