description: >-
  The Sredny Stog culture describes communities of the Black Sea steppe during roughly 4500 to 3500 BCE, especially around the Dnieper, Donets, and neighbouring rivers. Its name comes from an island site in present-day Ukraine, while Deriivka is another important settlement. Archaeologists recognise the culture through pottery, stone tools, dwellings, and cemeteries rather than written accounts of rulers. Mobile communities raised animals and maintained contacts with farming societies farther west, including Cucuteni–Trypillia. Flat graves often contain bodies with bent legs and traces of ochre. Horse remains have prompted debates about early domestication, but evidence for riding is not conclusive. The label covers varied local groups, not a demonstrated unified state, and precedes the later Yamnaya horizon in the same region.
