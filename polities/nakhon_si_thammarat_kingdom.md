description: >-
  The Nakhon Si Thammarat Kingdom was a Tai-ruled kingdom on the east coast of the Malay
  Peninsula, centred on the city also known as Ligor. It took shape in the late thirteenth
  century as Tambralinga faded, and this record runs from 1278 to 1400. Its chronicles
  describe a chain of twelve linked towns, the naksat cities, reaching from Chumphon south to
  Kelantan. The city became a
  centre of Sri Lankan Theravada Buddhism, and its monks carried that tradition north to
  Sukhothai. The Ramkhamhaeng stele lists it among Sukhothai's tributaries, but the tie was
  personal and lapsed after that king's death. Over the following century Ayutthaya drew the
  kingdom into its orbit, and its rulers became Siamese governors.
