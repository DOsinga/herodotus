description: >-
  The Chahamanas of Naddula were a Rajput dynasty who ruled the Marwar country of western
  Rajasthan from Naddula, the modern town of Nadol, between the tenth and twelfth centuries.
  Their founder Lakshmana, a younger son of the Chahamana king Vakpatiraja I of Shakambhari,
  carved out a principality around Naddula in the middle of the tenth century, according to
  legend after its Brahmins hired him to guard the town against raiders. His successors
  fought the Paramaras of Malwa and Mount Abu, the Chaulukyas of Gujarat, the Ghaznavids and
  their own Shakambhari cousins, at times as Chaulukya vassals. A younger branch founded the
  Chahamana principality of Jalor. The last ruler, Jayata-simha, was probably defeated by
  the Ghurid general Qutb al-Din Aibak in 1197.
