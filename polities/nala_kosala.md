name: "Nala Kingdom of Rajim"
description: >-
  The Nala kingdom of Rajim was a small royal government in present Chhattisgarh, represented here approximately
  between 700 and 900. Its history depends heavily on an undated inscription at Rajim, generally assigned
  to the seventh or eighth century. The inscription names a succession of rulers and records royal
  patronage of a Vishnu temple. These kings claimed descent from the legendary Nala and may have
  belonged to a later branch of the earlier Nala dynasty of Bastar. That connection does not
  establish uninterrupted government from the older kingdom at Pushkari. Rajim is therefore a representative centre for
  this later court, not a securely proven capital. The surviving evidence leaves its territorial extent, chronology,
  administrative arrangements and eventual disappearance from power uncertain.
