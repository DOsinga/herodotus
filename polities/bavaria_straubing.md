description: >-
  Bavaria-Straubing was a Wittelsbach duchy formed in 1353 from part of Lower Bavaria around
  Straubing on the Danube, for the sons of Emperor Louis IV who had also inherited the counties of
  Holland, Zeeland and Hainaut through their mother. Its dukes, William I, Albert I, William II
  and John III, often lived in The Hague and governed Straubing through officials while ruling
  their Low Countries counties as well. Straubing itself prospered as a market town and seat of
  ducal administration. When John III died in 1425 without heirs, the Straubing lands were
  disputed among the other Bavarian dukes, and the emperor's arbitration in 1429 divided them
  between the Munich, Landshut and Ingolstadt lines.
