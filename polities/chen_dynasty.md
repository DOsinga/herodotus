description: >-
  The Chen dynasty governed southern China from 557 to 589, with its court at Jiankang, modern
  Nanjing. Chen Baxian, a general who rose during the collapse of Liang authority, took the
  imperial throne and founded the last of China's Southern dynasties. His successors
  maintained an imperial administration over the lower Yangtze region and lands farther south.
  The state recovered from earlier warfare and supported the region's economic and cultural
  development, but faced stronger northern rivals. Emperor Xuan expanded northward against
  Northern Qi before losing those gains to Northern Zhou. Under the last emperor, Chen Shubao,
  the Sui launched a major invasion. Their capture of Jiankang in 589 ended Chen government
  and reunited China, although members of its ruling family survived.
