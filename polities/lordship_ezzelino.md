description: >-
  The Lordship of Ezzelino da Romano was the state that Ezzelino III da Romano built in
  the March of Treviso between 1226 and his death in 1259. Ezzelino became podestà of
  Verona in 1226 and, as Frederick II's ally and son-in-law, added Vicenza in 1236 and
  Padua in 1237, while his brother Alberico held Treviso. He governed through podestà and
  garrisons answerable to him rather than the communes, and his prisons and executions
  made him the model of the cruel tyrant in later Italian writing. After Frederick's death
  he ruled on his own, but a papal crusade took Padua from him in 1256. Wounded and
  captured at Cassano in 1259, he died in prison, and his family was wiped out.
