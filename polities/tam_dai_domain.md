description: >-
  Tam Đái was a domain governed by Nguyễn Khoan in northern Vietnam during the Period of
  the Twelve Warlords. This record places its separate government between approximately
  965 and his defeat in 967, while allowing an earlier local power base. The domain lay
  in present Vĩnh Phúc province, with its mapped centre represented near Vĩnh Tường.
  Khoan adopted the title Nguyễn Thái Bình and held power as a military lord amid the
  fragmentation of central authority. His brothers Nguyễn Thủ Tiệp and Nguyễn Siêu also
  controlled separate territories. Surviving accounts describe Khoan himself rather than
  institutions of his government. Đinh Bộ Lĩnh defeated him during the campaigns that
  reunified the country, ending Tam Đái's rule before the new dynasty's establishment.
