name: "Kingdom of Lazica"
description: >-
  Lazica was a kingdom in western Georgia on the eastern Black Sea, represented here from the
  conventional date of 131 to about 697. Emerging after the fragmentation of ancient Colchis, it
  brought several neighbouring communities under its kings. Archaeopolis, now the ruins of Nokalakevi,
  served as an fortified centre. Roman and later Byzantine influence competed with Sasanian Persian
  ambitions for control of this strategically placed region. Its rulers retained a local monarchy
  despite changing alliances and imperial pressure. Christianity strengthened connections with
  Byzantium, while disputes over trade and military control helped provoke the sixth century Lazic
  War. The kingdom's final decades are poorly documented. Its ending estimates the replacement of
  royal rule by Byzantine administration near the seventh century's close.
