description: >-
  The Dutch Republic emerged from the revolt against Spanish rule, represented here from the rejection
  of Philip II in 1581 until 1795. Seven northern provinces governed through their own estates
  and sent delegates to the States General at The Hague. Urban regents shared and
  contested power with stadtholders, often drawn from the House of Orange. Holland supplied most of the
  republic's money and influence. Maritime commerce, finance, printing, painting, and science flourished during its seventeenth
  century Golden Age. Chartered companies built overseas empires through trade, warfare, forced labor, and slavery.
  Independence received international recognition in 1648. Wars with England and France strained the state, while
  domestic political struggles deepened. French invasion and revolutionary change replaced it with the Batavian Republic.
