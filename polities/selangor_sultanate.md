description: >-
  The Selangor Sultanate was a Malay state on the west coast of the Malay Peninsula, between Perak and
  the Negeri Sembilan states. Bugis settlers from Sulawesi had come to dominate the coast in the early
  eighteenth century, and in 1766 their leader Raja Lumu was installed as the first sultan,
  Salehuddin, with the consent of Perak. The early royal seat was at Kuala Selangor. The state's
  wealth came from tin mined along the Klang and Selangor rivers, which drew large numbers of Chinese
  miners and led to the founding of Kuala Lumpur in 1857. Rival chiefs fought the Klang War from 1867
  to 1874 over control of the mines and river tolls. In 1874 the sultan accepted a British Resident.
