description: >-
  Đằng Châu was a local domain in the Red River region of northern Vietnam governed by
  Phạm Bạch Hổ from 944 to 968. He had served Ngô Quyền as a general and participated in
  the battle of Bạch Đằng against the Southern Han. After Quyền's death, he took control
  of Đằng Châu, in the area of present Hưng Yên, and adopted the title Phạm Phòng Át. His
  government survived the weakening and collapse of the Ngô royal center, becoming one of
  the domains associated with the Twelve Warlords. Đinh Bộ Lĩnh's campaigns brought it
  under a new central monarchy by 968. Bạch Hổ subsequently held military office under
  Đinh, but that service did not preserve his domain's separate political existence.
