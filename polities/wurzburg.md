description: >-
  The Prince-Bishopric of Würzburg was an ecclesiastical state in Lower Franconia, centred on the city beside the Main River. Its bishops combined religious office with territorial rule, exercising government separately from the emperors from the thirteenth century onward. Cathedral canons elected bishops, with papal confirmation, rather than passing power through a hereditary royal family. The bishops also claimed ducal rights in Franconia and belonged to the Holy Roman Empire's network of territorial princes. Their lands lay west of Bamberg and included towns, villages, and estates beyond Würzburg itself. In 1803, the secularisation of church states ended the bishop's political authority and transferred the territory to Bavaria. A grand duchy briefly occupied the area, but did not revive the ecclesiastical government.
