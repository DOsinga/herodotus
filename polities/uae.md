  The United Arab Emirates is a federation on the southeastern shore of the Persian Gulf,
  independent since 1971. Six former British protected sheikhdoms formed the union that
  December, and Ras Al Khaimah joined in 1972. Abu Dhabi is the capital. Each emirate retains
  a hereditary ruler and local authority, while their rulers together form the Federal Supreme

  including defence and foreign relations. Oil revenues helped transform coastal settlements
  once dependent on pearling, fishing and trade. Dubai developed into a major commercial and
  transport centre, while Abu Dhabi holds most of the federation's oil wealth. Large migrant
  communities sustain much of its workforce, alongside the smaller population of Emirati
  citizens.
