name: "Ngô dynasty"
description: >-
  The Ngô royal government ruled northern Vietnam from Cổ Loa between 939 and 965. It
  followed Ngô Quyền's victory over a Southern Han invasion at the Bạch Đằng River in 938,
  where stakes and changing tides helped defeat the attacking fleet. His assumption of
  kingship established a local monarchy beyond direct Chinese rule. After his death in 944,
  Dương Tam Kha seized the throne instead of acting as regent for the royal heir. Ngô Xương
  Văn overthrew him in 950 and shared kingship with his elder brother Ngô Xương Ngập. Royal
  authority remained vulnerable to powerful regional leaders. Xương Văn's death in 965
  brought fragmentation into competing warlord governments; this record ends there, before
  the final Ngô claimant's later submission.
