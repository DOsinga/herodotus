description: >-
  The Principality of Najran was a state in the oasis of Najran, on the northern edge of Yemen,
  ruled by the da'is of the Sulaymani branch of Tayyibi Ismailism. The Sulaymanis split from
  the Indian Bohra community in 1589, and from 1677 their leader, the da'i al-mutlaq, almost
  always came from the Makrami family. The da'is lived at Badr in Najran and governed with the
  armed backing of the Yam tribe, who shared their faith. Their power peaked under Isma'il ibn
  Hibat Allah (1747-1770), who defeated the Wahhabis of Najd and invaded the Hadhramaut. The
  principality began under Zaydi Yemeni suzerainty and later came under Ottoman influence.
  After the Ottoman withdrawal, Yemen and the Saudis fought over Najran until the Saudi
  annexation of 1934.
