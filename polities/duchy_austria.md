description: >-
  The Duchy of Austria was a hereditary principality centred on Vienna and the Danube lands of
  present-day Austria. This record follows its Habsburg government from Rudolf's victory over
  Ottokar II at the Marchfeld in 1278 until 1453. Earlier Babenberg rule and the disputed
  succession after 1246 belong to a different political setting. Rudolf granted Austria to his
  sons in 1282, establishing the dynasty's base. Dukes expanded their holdings while governing
  within the Holy Roman Empire. From 1379 it follows the Albertine line, while the Leopoldine
  Habsburgs ruled the other lands separately. Rudolf IV sought higher rank through the forged
  Privilegium Maius, alongside efforts to strengthen Vienna. In 1453, Emperor Frederick III
  recognised Austria's archducal status, turning the duchy into the Archduchy of Austria.
