description: >-
  The Prince-Bishopric of Chur was the territory of the bishops of Chur, the oldest see north of
  the Alps, in the upper Rhine valley of present Graubünden. The bishops held the town of Chur,
  the Bergell, the Vinschgau and other valleys on the routes to the Septimer and other passes,
  with rights granted by Frankish kings and Otto I, and they ranked as imperial princes from 1170.
  Their power rested on castles and on the ministerial families who served them, and they fought
  constant feuds with the barons of the region. In 1367, during the misrule of Bishop Peter
  Gelyto, the cathedral chapter, the town and the valleys formed the League of God's House to
  govern the bishopric's affairs, the first of the Three Leagues.
