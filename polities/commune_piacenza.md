description: >-
  The Commune of Piacenza was the self-governing city state of Piacenza on the Po, from
  the end of the eleventh century until Galeazzo Visconti took the city in 1313. It stood
  where the Via Emilia met the river and the pilgrim road to Rome, and its merchants
  became some of the leading bankers of Europe. Consuls and later a podestà governed the
  city, which joined the Lombard League against Frederick Barbarossa. In the thirteenth
  century the merchants and the nobles fought over the commune, and lords such as Uberto
  Pallavicino and Alberto Scotto ruled through its councils. Scotto governed the city from
  1290 with brief breaks. In 1313 the Visconti took over, and later papal and Visconti
  rule followed.
