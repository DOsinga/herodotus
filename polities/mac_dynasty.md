description: >-
  The Mạc dynasty ruled a Vietnamese monarchy founded in 1527 when Mạc Đăng Dung displaced the
  Lê emperor. Based initially at Thăng Long, it held much of northern Vietnam while a restored
  Lê court, supported by Nguyễn and Trịnh leaders, contested its authority from the south. Its
  emperors maintained court institutions and sought recognition from China to protect their
  position. Long wars ended with the loss of Thăng Long in 1592. The Mạc then continued as a
  smaller government around Cao Bằng near the Chinese frontier, supported at different times
  by Ming and Qing diplomacy. Trịnh forces took this territory in 1667, but Mạc rule returned
  in 1669. Its final defeat in 1677 ended the dynasty's independent centre of power.
