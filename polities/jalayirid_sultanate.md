description: >-
  The Jalayirid Sultanate ruled parts of Iraq and western Iran between 1335 and 1432
  after the Ilkhanate fragmented. Its ruling family came from the Mongol Jalayir
  tribe, but its court used Persian administrative and literary traditions. Baghdad
  and Tabriz were major seats of government. Hasan Buzurg established the dynasty's
  position amid struggles between rival commanders and claimants to the Ilkhanid
  throne. His son Uways expanded its territory and strengthened royal authority. The
  sultans patronized poets, calligraphers and painters, helping develop Persian
  manuscript art. Timur's campaigns devastated their cities and repeatedly displaced
  their rulers. After his death, surviving Jalayirids attempted recovery, chiefly in
  southern Iraq and Khuzistan. The Qara Qoyunlu finally conquered their remaining
  possessions in 1432, ending the dynasty's rule.
