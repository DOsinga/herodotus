description: >-
  The Tembe Kingdom was a chiefdom of Tsonga-speaking people centred on Delagoa Bay, where Maputo
  now stands, and extending south over the coastal plain of Maputaland into what is now KwaZulu-
  Natal. Its ruling lineage traced its origins to migrants from the Zimbabwe plateau, and Tembe
  himself is the first recorded ruler, in the sixteenth century. The chiefs controlled ivory
  hunting grounds and traded ivory and slaves with Portuguese, Dutch, English and Austrian ships
  for beads, brass and cloth, using the profits to recruit warriors. After King Silamboya's death
  in 1746 the lineage split between branches on either side of the Maputo River, and the eastern
  branch under Mabudu grew strongest. Britain and Portugal divided the kingdom in the 1890s.
