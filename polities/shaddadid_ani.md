description: >-
  The Shaddadid Emirate of Ani was a Muslim emirate in the former Armenian royal capital of Ani.
  After Alp Arslan took Ani from the Byzantines in 1064, the Seljuk sultan Malik-Shah handed the
  city in 1072 to Manuchihr, a member of the Kurdish Shaddadid family that had ruled Dvin and
  Ganja. His descendants governed Ani and the surrounding district of Shirak as Seljuk vassals
  for more than a century, over a largely Armenian Christian population into which the family
  married. The emirate lay on the frontier between the Seljuk world and a resurgent Georgia,
  whose kings seized Ani in 1124 and 1161 and lost it again to Shaddadid rulers supported by the
  neighbouring Muslim princes. In 1199 Queen Tamar's generals, the Zakarid brothers, took Ani for
  good.
