description: >-
  Piedras Negras was a Maya kingdom on the Usumacinta River in what is now northwestern
  Guatemala. Its royal government is recorded here from around 400 to 810, although settlement
  began earlier. Known in ancient inscriptions as Yo'k'ib', the city occupied hills beside a
  major river route. Kings commissioned elaborate stelae, panels, and thrones celebrating
  accessions, marriages, rituals, and victories over neighbouring rulers. Its sculptures were
  central to the recognition that Maya inscriptions recorded dynastic history. Relations with
  Yaxchilan alternated between alliance and bitter rivalry, while smaller communities belonged to
  its political network. Yaxchilan captured its final known king in 808. Destruction at the
  palace and damaged monuments accompanied the royal centre's collapse; continued habitation is
  not evidence of continued kingship.
