description: >-
  The Đinh state began as Đinh Bộ Lĩnh's independent government at Hoa Lư in northern Vietnam,
  resisting the Ngô rulers in 951. Its early years belong to the struggles between regional lords
  rather than an already united empire. Bộ Lĩnh defeated the competing warlords and proclaimed
  himself emperor of Đại Cồ Việt in 968. His court established royal offices, an army and a
  copper coinage while accepting diplomatic recognition and tributary relations with Song China.
  Hoa Lư's surrounding limestone terrain provided a defensible seat. The emperor and his son Đinh
  Liễn were murdered in 979, leaving a child ruler and a dangerous succession crisis. General Lê
  Hoàn took the throne in 980, continuing the state under the Early Lê dynasty.
