description: >-
  The Emirate of Badakhshan was a mountain state on the upper Amu Darya, in what is now
  northeastern Afghanistan and Tajikistan, ruled by the Yarid dynasty. It arose in 1657 from a
  revolt against the Qataghan Uzbeks, who had taken Badakhshan after a Mughal army withdrew. The
  rebels chose Yari Beg, a Naqshbandi Sufi from Bukhara, as their mir, and he built a shrine for
  the cloak of the Prophet at Fayzabad, which became the capital. His descendants paid tribute to
  Nader Shah and later suffered invasions by Ahmad Shah Durrani and the khans of Kunduz, while
  the Pamiri states of Wakhan, Shughnan and Darvaz drifted away. Murad Beg of Kunduz conquered
  Badakhshan in 1829, but the Yarids returned in the 1830s and ruled until the Afghans took over.
