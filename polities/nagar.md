description: >-
  Nagar was a Bronze Age kingdom centred on Tell Brak in the Khabur basin of northeastern Syria.
  Around 2600 BCE, the city expanded and developed large administrative buildings. Records from
  Ebla and neighbouring centres show kings, subordinate towns, diplomatic alliances, conflict,
  and dynastic marriage. Nagar dealt with Ebla and Mari as a major regional power, controlling
  routes across northern Mesopotamia. Its rulers also supplied prized equids to other courts.
  Destruction around 2300 BCE preceded Akkadian control, under which Naram-Sin built a palace.
  After Akkad's collapse Nagar again became an independent kingdom, whose Hurrian royal names
  survive on seals. The city later shrank, and as Nawar it passed between Shamshi-Adad's kingdom,
  small local rulers and finally Mitanni.
