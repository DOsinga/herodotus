description: >-
  The County of Diez was a county on the lower Lahn, centred on the castle of Diez near Limburg.
  It is first recorded in 1073 and probably arose from lands of the Conradine family. In the late
  twelfth century the counts were firm supporters of the Staufer kings, who rewarded them with
  lands such as the Usingen estate in the Taunus, received in 1207, and the county was known as
  the golden county. After the fall of the Staufer its importance waned. In 1302 the county of
  Weilnau was split off for a younger branch, and the counts pledged away land after land to pay
  their debts, the Usingen lands going to Nassau in 1326. When the last count died in 1388, Diez
  passed to the counts of Nassau.
