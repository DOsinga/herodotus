description: >-
  The Grand Duchy of Hesse was a German monarchy centered on Darmstadt, created in 1806 when
  Napoleon elevated the landgrave of Hesse-Darmstadt. It joined his Confederation of the
  Rhine and supplied troops for French campaigns. After Napoleon's defeat, territorial
  exchanges gave it lands west of the Rhine around Mainz, producing the title Hesse and by
  Rhine. A constitution introduced a representative assembly in 1820, while the grand duke
  and his officials retained power. Reform, censorship and popular demands shaped its
  nineteenth-century politics. Defeat alongside Austria in 1866 brought losses to Prussia
  and closer military dependence. The northern province entered the North German
  Confederation, while the whole state joined the German Empire in 1871, retaining its
  monarchy but losing external independence.
