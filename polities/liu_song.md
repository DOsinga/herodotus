description: >-
  Liu Song was an imperial state in southern China from 420 to 479, governed from
  Jiankang, present Nanjing. The general Liu Yu replaced Eastern Jin and took the
  throne as Emperor Wu, drawing on military victories won before his accession. His
  realm included the lower Yangtze valley and substantial lands farther north, where it
  confronted Northern Wei. Emperor Wen's reign brought stable government and cultural
  growth, although northern campaigns failed to reunify China. Court violence, murders
  among imperial relatives, and military revolts undermined later rulers. The state
  supported poetry, historical writing, and scholarship amid this instability. In 479,
  the general Xiao Daocheng forced Emperor Shun to abdicate and established Southern
  Qi, ending the Liu family's rule over the southern empire.
