description: >-
  Lyon was governed by its archbishops as a sovereign lordship in the old Kingdom of Arles,
  recorded here from 1250 until France annexed the city in 1312. Frederick Barbarossa
  confirmed the archbishop's authority over the city in 1157, and an agreement of 1173 with
  the counts of Forez left the archbishop and the cathedral chapter, whose canons bore the
  title of counts of Lyon, as lords of the city and the Lyonnais. The growing merchant
  community demanded a share in government, and its revolts against the clergy in the 1260s
  led the citizens to seek French protection. Royal officials gained a foothold as guardians
  of the city, and the Treaty of Vienne in 1312 brought Lyon into the Kingdom of France.
