description: >-
  Pylos was a Mycenaean kingdom in Messenia, southwestern Greece, represented here from
  roughly 1600 to 1180 BCE. Its royal centre stood at the site known as the Palace of
  Nestor, near modern Chora. The name connects the excavated palace with Homer's legendary
  ruler, not an identified historical king. Linear B tablets provide evidence of a palace
  administration managing land, labour, livestock, production, and religious offerings.
  These records reveal a monarchy with officials and dependent communities across its
  territory. Storerooms, workshops, decorated rooms, and elite burials show the wealth and
  organisation of its centre. Around 1180 BCE, fire destroyed the palace during the wider
  collapse of Mycenaean states. The baked tablets survived, preserving administrative
  details otherwise lost with its royal government.
