description: >-
  The Chandela kingdom of Jejakabhukti governed parts of Bundelkhand in central India, beginning with Nannuka's
  small realm around Khajuraho in the ninth century. Its rulers first acknowledged Pratihara overlordship while
  maintaining their own local government, then expanded as that power weakened. Yashovarman and Dhanga helped
  establish a kingdom, whose courts also used Mahoba, Kalinjar and Ajaigarh. The surviving temples at
  Khajuraho are its best known legacy, representing Hindu and Jain patronage and elaborate stone carving.
  Chandela kings fought neighbouring dynasties and later confronted armies arriving from the northwest. Their territory
  contracted after conflicts with the Chahamanas and Delhi sultans. The record ends around 1315 with
  the last conventionally dated royal government, not with every later estate ruled by Chandela families.
