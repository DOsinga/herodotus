description: >-
  Denkyira was an Akan kingdom in the forest zone of present Ghana. It rose to prominence in the
  early seventeenth century, and the Denkyirahene ruled from Abankeseso, supported by gold
  production, tribute and control of the routes between inland communities and coastal traders.
  Under Boa Amponsem Denkyira became a regional power and brought neighbouring states under its
  authority, until Osei Tutu's Asante defeated it at Feyiase in 1701, killed its ruler Ntim
  Gyakari and sacked the capital. Denkyira then lived as an Asante vassal. After a failed rising
  in 1823 its people moved south of the Ofin, allied with the British against Asante and governed
  themselves around Jukwaa, joining the Fante Confederacy in 1868, until the Gold Coast Colony
  absorbed them in 1874.
