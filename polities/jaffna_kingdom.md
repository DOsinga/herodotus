description: >-
  The Jaffna Kingdom governed northern Sri Lanka from 1215 to 1619, with its court at Nallur.
  Its origins are associated with Kalinga Magha's invasion, though the early history remains
  debated. Aryacakravarti kings later consolidated a Tamil monarchy linked to southern India.
  They drew revenue from land, pearl fisheries and elephant exports, and supported Hindu
  temples and Tamil learning. Pandyan influence, rivalry with other island kingdoms and a
  period of Kotte domination shaped its changing power. Portuguese expansion disrupted
  maritime trade and brought military campaigns, tribute demands and interference in royal
  succession. The kings nevertheless continued governing until the final confrontation under
  Cankili II. Portuguese forces conquered the kingdom in 1619, replacing its royal government
  with colonial rule despite local resistance.
