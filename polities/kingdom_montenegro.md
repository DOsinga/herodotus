description: >-
  The Kingdom of Montenegro was a small mountainous Balkan monarchy governed from
  Cetinje between 1910 and the Austro-Hungarian occupation of 1916. Nicholas I
  proclaimed himself king after five decades as prince. A constitution and elected
  assembly existed, but the monarch retained strong personal control. Montenegro
  joined Serbia, Bulgaria, and Greece against the Ottoman Empire in the Balkan Wars,
  gaining territory while surrendering its claim to Shkoder under pressure from the
  great powers. It entered the First World War alongside Serbia and the Allies. Defeat
  drove Nicholas and his ministers into exile, ending their government at home. The
  kingdom was formally united with Serbia in 1918, a disputed decision that left a
  lasting division between supporters of union and Montenegrin royalists.
