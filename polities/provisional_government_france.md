description: >-
  The Provisional Government of the French Republic governed France during liberation and reconstruction from 1944 to 1946. Formed in Algiers from the French Committee of National Liberation, it moved to Paris after the capital's liberation. Charles de Gaulle headed a coalition drawing on the Resistance and major political parties. It asserted that republican legality had continued despite Vichy, restored French administration and kept France fighting alongside the Allies until Germany's defeat. Its domestic programme included women's voting rights, nationalisation of major businesses and the establishment of social security. Elections and constituent assemblies then debated a permanent constitution. De Gaulle resigned amid disagreements over parliamentary power. The adoption of the Fourth Republic's constitution in October 1946 ended this transitional governmental phase.
