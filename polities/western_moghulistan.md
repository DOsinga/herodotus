description: >-
  Western Moghulistan was the Tashkent based share of the Chagatai Mongol realm after Yunus
  Khan's death in 1487. His son Mahmud Khan ruled the western territories, while another
  son, Ahmad Alaq, governed the eastern branch around Turpan. The khans belonged to a Mongol
  royal line but increasingly used Islamic law and participated in the Turkic speaking
  political world of Central Asia. Their authority rested on relationships with powerful
  tribal leaders as well as control of towns. Uzbek expansion under Muhammad Shaybani
  threatened Mahmud's government. Ahmad marched west to assist him in 1503, but both
  brothers were defeated and captured. Their release did not restore Tashkent, which the
  Uzbeks retained. Mahmud's later attempts to recover power failed, ending this western
  government.
