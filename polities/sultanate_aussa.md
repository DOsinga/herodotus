description: >-
  The Sultanate of Aussa was an Afar kingdom centred on the Aussa oasis of the lower Awash River,
  in what is now northeastern Ethiopia. In 1734 Kedafu, head of the Mudaito clan, seized power
  from the last of the imams of Aussa and founded the Mudaito dynasty. The sultans held a silver
  baton as their symbol of office and extended their influence over the Danakil lowlands. Sultan
  Hanfadhe Aydahis completed the Mudaito hold on the oasis in 1834, and Sultan Mahammad destroyed
  an Egyptian army under Werner Munzinger in 1875. Aussa signed treaties with Italy but was
  defeated by Menelik's forces in 1896 and became a tributary of Ethiopia. Italy occupied the
  sultanate in 1936.
