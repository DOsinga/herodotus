description: >-
  Janggala was a monarchy in northeastern Java, formed when Airlangga divided his realm between two heirs. This record dates its government from 1042 to approximately 1135, following the chronology explained in the note. Its counterpart was Panjalu, better known as Kediri. Hujung Galuh, within present Surabaya, represents Janggala's port and center. Inscriptions preserve rulers including Mapanji Garasakan, Alanjung Ahyes, and Samarotsaha, but much of the kingdom's history remains obscure. Trade and access to the coast distinguished its position from the inland court of Kediri. Rivalry between the successor kingdoms eventually ended with Kediri gaining control. Accounts differ over the date and whether conquest or dynastic union explains the outcome, so the recorded endpoint remains an estimate rather than a certainty.
