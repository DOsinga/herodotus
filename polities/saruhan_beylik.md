description: >-
  Saruhan was a Turkish Muslim principality in western Anatolia, established around 1300 in the
  Gediz valley and governed from Manisa. Its founder, Saruhan Bey, had served the Germiyan rulers
  before taking territory from the Byzantines and building his own hereditary court. The beylik
  controlled towns including Menemen, Gördes and Nif, and became an Aegean naval power under its
  first ruler. Its fleets competed with Genoese interests and the rulers of Naxos. İshak Bey's
  Great Mosque at Manisa remains a major architectural legacy. Ottoman expansion ended its first
  period of rule in the late fourteenth century. The principality returned after Bayezid's defeat
  at Ankara in 1402, but renewed Ottoman consolidation absorbed it permanently in 1412,
  preserving Saruhan as a provincial name.
