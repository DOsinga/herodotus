description: >-
  The Ukrainian State was Pavlo Skoropadsky's short-lived government in Kyiv from April to
  December 1918. Established after the overthrow of the Central Rada, it used the title
  Hetmanate but belonged to the turmoil of the First World War, not the earlier Cossack
  state. Skoropadsky ruled by decree, backed by German and Austrian forces, and favoured
  landowners and industrialists over the previous government's socialist policies. His
  administration built a bureaucracy and supported Ukrainian educational and cultural
  institutions. Its dependence on the Central Powers, grain requisitions, and failure to
  resolve land disputes alienated much of the countryside. Germany's defeat removed its
  military support. An uprising led by the Directorate forced Skoropadsky to abdicate and
  restored the Ukrainian People's Republic in December 1918.
