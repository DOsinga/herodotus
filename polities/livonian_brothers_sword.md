description: >-
  The Livonian Brothers of the Sword were a crusading military order founded in Riga in 1202
  by Bishop Albert to fight and convert the pagan peoples of Livonia, in present Latvia and
  Estonia. In 1207 the bishop granted the brothers a third of the conquered land, which they
  governed under their own master from castles such as Wenden, Segewold and later Fellin.
  Over the next three decades they subdued the Livonians, Latgalians and Estonians, often in
  rivalry with the bishops and with Denmark, whose stronghold at Reval they seized in 1227.
  Their defeat by the Samogitians and Semigallians at the Battle of Saule in 1236
  killed the master and many knights, and the survivors joined the Teutonic Order in 1237.
