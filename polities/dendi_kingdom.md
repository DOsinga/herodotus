description: >-
  The Dendi Kingdom was the Songhai state that survived the Moroccan conquest of 1591 in the lower
  Niger valley, in what is now southwestern Niger and the borderlands of Benin and Nigeria. After
  the defeat at Tondibi, members of the Askia dynasty withdrew to Dendi, the empire's easternmost
  province. Askia Nuh fought a guerrilla war against the Moroccan pashas from his capital at
  Lulami until his followers deposed him in 1599. His successors kept the Songhai royal title and
  court traditions, while the Moroccans set up rival puppet askias upriver. Over the following
  centuries the kingdom suffered unstable reigns and civil wars, and its centre moved south
  towards Gaya. France deposed the last askia in 1901.
