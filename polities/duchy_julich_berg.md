description: >-
  Jülich Berg was the Rhineland government held by the Neuburg branch of the Wittelsbach family
  from 1614 to 1716. The partition of the disputed Jülich Cleves inheritance gave Jülich and Berg
  to Wolfgang Wilhelm, while Brandenburg received Cleves and Mark. Düsseldorf became the court and
  administrative centre of the duchies. Hereditary dukes governed lands within the Holy Roman
  Empire, negotiating with estates and rival princes. The Thirty Years' War brought pressures even
  where battles were absent, and religious disputes prolonged tensions with Brandenburg. In 1685
  the Neuburg rulers inherited the Electoral Palatinate while continuing their Düsseldorf court.
  Under Charles Philip, government shifted away in 1716; the duchies subsequently answered through
  a governor to the elector rather than maintaining this court.
