description: >-
  The Kingdom of Fiji was the first state to unite the Fiji Islands, apart from Rotuma. Ratu
  Seru Cakobau, the paramount chief of the small island of Bau, had come to dominate the east of
  the archipelago and called himself king of Fiji, though rival chiefs saw him as at best first
  among equals. In 1871 the British consul persuaded the chiefs to accept a constitutional
  monarchy with Cakobau as king, while a cabinet and legislature dominated by Australian
  settlers held much of the real power. The legislature met at Levuka. Overspending soon left
  the government in unmanageable debt, and unrest followed. Cakobau and his rival Maʻafu agreed
  to cede the islands to Britain, and the Deed of Cession of 1874 made Fiji a British colony.
