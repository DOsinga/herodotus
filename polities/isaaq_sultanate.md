description: >-
  The Isaaq Sultanate was the state of the Isaaq clans in what is now Somaliland, recorded here
  from about 1600 until 1884. After the fall of Adal the Somali lands broke into clan states, and
  the Isaaq were led by kings of the Tol Je'lo line until a coalition of clans overthrew the last
  of them. In the middle of the eighteenth century the Isaaq chose Guled of the Eidagale as sultan
  after the battle of Lafaruug, and his descendants ruled from Toon. The sultans arbitrated
  between clans, led them in war and dealt with foreign traders. Berbera, run by Habr Awal
  merchant lineages, held a great annual fair that fed the markets of Aden and Mocha. Treaties of
  1884 to 1886 placed the coast under British protection.
