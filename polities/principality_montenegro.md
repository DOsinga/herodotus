description: >-
  The Principality of Montenegro was a Balkan monarchy centred on Cetinje, lasting
  from 1852 to 1910. Danilo ended the older prince-bishop system by becoming
  a secular hereditary prince, while retaining the Petrović dynasty's leadership. His legal
  code helped consolidate authority over a society with clan traditions. Victory over
  Ottoman forces at Grahovac in 1858 strengthened Montenegro's position, and later wars
  under Prince Nikola brought territory. The Congress of Berlin recognised its independence
  in 1878 and confirmed expansion that included access to the Adriatic. Nikola
  promoted schools, administration, and diplomatic ties, especially with Russia, but retained considerable
  personal power. A constitution and parliament followed in 1905. In 1910 he
  proclaimed a kingdom, changing the state's status without replacing its ruling house.
