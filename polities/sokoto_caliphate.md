name: Sokoto Caliphate
description: >-
  The Sokoto Caliphate was a Sunni Muslim state in West Africa from 1804 to 1903, centred in what
  is now northern Nigeria. Usman dan Fodio, a religious scholar, launched the jihad that
  overturned several Hausa governments and created a new political order. His successors governed
  through a network of emirates, with Sokoto and Gwandu serving as major centres of authority.
  Emirs administered their territories under Islamic law while recognising the caliph's
  leadership. Farming, craft production, markets, and long distance trade supported the economy,
  which also relied on enslaved labour. Scholarship and religious reform were central to its
  public life. European conquests dismantled the caliphate, and British forces defeated its last
  resistance in 1903. Sokoto's religious leadership survived under colonial rule.
