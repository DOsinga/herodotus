description: >-
  The Anosy Kingdom was the realm of the Zafiraminia kings of the Antanosy people in southeastern
  Madagascar, around the Efaho valley and the bay where Fort-Dauphin was later built. The
  Zafiraminia claimed descent from Muslim settlers from overseas and held ritual and political
  authority over Anosy's farming and cattle-herding clans. In 1613 Portuguese visitors were
  received by King Andriantsiambany and five hundred of his soldiers, and in the middle of the
  seventeenth century his son ruled from the capital Fanjahira. From 1643 the French East India
  Company's colony at Fort-Dauphin fought, raided and allied with the Anosy kings until the
  Antanosy drove the French out in 1674. The Merina kingdom conquered Anosy in 1825.
