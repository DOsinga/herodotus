description: >-
  Tsongkha, also called Qingtang or the Gusiluo kingdom, was a Tibetan Buddhist
  kingdom in Amdo, in the upper Yellow River and Huangshui valleys of modern
  Qinghai, from 1008 to 1104. Local elders brought Gusiluo, a claimed descendant
  of the old Tibetan royal line, to the region and enthroned him as tsenpo in
  1008. From 1032 he ruled from Qingtang, the modern Xining, with monks and tribal
  chiefs as his main supporters. The kingdom controlled a branch of the trade
  route between Central Asia and Song China, and allied with the Song and the
  Liao against the rising Tangut state of Western Xia. After Gusiluo died in 1065
  his heirs quarrelled and lost outlying towns. Song armies occupied Qingtang in
  1099 and annexed the kingdom in 1104.
