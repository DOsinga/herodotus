name: "Northern Liang at Gaochang"
wikipedia: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Northern_Liang
description: >-
  After the Northern Wei conquered the Northern Liang capital at Guzang in 439, members of the
  ruling Juqu family fled west and kept a remnant state alive. Juqu Wuhui held out at Dunhuang and
  then moved through the desert to the Turpan basin, where he took Gaochang in 442 and continued
  the Northern Liang government there. The Gaochang remnant ruled an oasis of farming and caravan
  trade on the Silk Road, with a population of Chinese settlers and local peoples, and in 450 it
  conquered the neighbouring Jushi kingdom at Jiaohe. Juqu Wuhui's brother Anzhou succeeded him.
  In 460 the Rouran, the steppe power to the north, killed Anzhou and installed Kan Bozhou as king
  of Gaochang.
