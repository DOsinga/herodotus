description: >-
  The Russian Soviet Republic was the Bolshevik state established by the October
  Revolution of 1917, before the creation of the Soviet Union in 1922. Its
  government, led by Vladimir Lenin, claimed authority through councils of workers,
  soldiers and peasants, while Communist Party power increasingly controlled those
  institutions. The capital moved from Petrograd to Moscow in 1918. Withdrawal from
  the First World War was followed by civil war against White armies, foreign
  intervention and rival revolutionary movements. Nationalization and forced grain
  collection under war communism accompanied severe shortages and famine. In 1921,
  the New Economic Policy restored some private trade. Soviet Russia joined Ukraine,
  Belarus and the Transcaucasian republic in forming the USSR, continuing afterward
  as its largest Soviet constituent republic.
