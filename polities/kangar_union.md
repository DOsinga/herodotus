description: >-
  The Kangar Union is a reconstructed medieval confederation associated with the steppes of
  present Kazakhstan and the Syr Darya region. Its conventional dates, 659 to 750, place it
  between the collapse of Western Turkic power and the rise of competing steppe groups.
  Accounts portray a union of pastoral tribes with relationships to the trading towns along
  the river, rather than a closely documented territorial kingdom. Names such as Kangar,
  Kengeres and Kangly have been linked by scholars, but their connections remain uncertain.
  Oghuz, Karluk and Kimek expansion is associated with the union's dissolution. Some Kangar
  groups are also connected to later Pecheneg communities moving westward. Neither these
  identifications nor the location of its governing centre can be treated as settled.
