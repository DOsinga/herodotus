description: >-
  The Obotrite principality grew from a confederation of West Slavic communities in
  present northern Germany, especially Mecklenburg and Holstein, beginning around the
  eighth century. Hereditary princes led the alliance, although its constituent tribes and
  varied religious centres retained distinct identities. The Obotrites supported
  Charlemagne against Saxons and rival Slavic groups, gaining and losing territory as
  Frankish and Danish power shifted. Later rulers faced pressure from Saxon expansion and
  Christian missions, while trade linked their Baltic settlements with neighbouring
  peoples. Niklot resisted the campaigns of Henry the Lion but died in 1160. His sons
  continued ruling around Werle until defeat in 1164 ended their government. Pribislav's
  restoration in 1167 began the separate Mecklenburg principality rather than reviving
  this earlier tribal confederation.
