description: >-
  The Helvetii were a Celtic people or tribal confederation occupying much of the Swiss Plateau before Roman conquest. Their political
  organisation is estimated here from around 200 BCE, rather than dated to a known founding event. Ancient accounts describe several
  constituent groups, including the Tigurini and Verbigeni, alongside leaders and assemblies rather than one monarchy. Farming settlements and fortified towns
  linked them with the wider Celtic world. In 58 BCE, they attempted a large migration westwards with neighbouring peoples, burning
  settlements before departure according to Caesar. His army blocked their route and defeated them near Bibracte. The survivors were sent
  back to their homeland under Roman terms. Their community continued afterwards, but the failed migration ended the autonomy represented here.
