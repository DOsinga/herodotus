description: >-
  The Duchy of Franconia was a short lived Conradine government in central and western
  Germany, represented here from 906 to 939. Its lands lay around the Main and Rhine
  valleys, with the family's power rooted in the Lahngau. Conrad the Younger emerged as
  duke during the weakening of Carolingian kingship and became king of East Francia in
  911. His brother Eberhard continued the family's ducal authority. Franconia differed
  from other stem duchies because it never developed a durable territorial government
  encompassing the whole region. Eberhard rebelled against Otto I and died after defeat
  at Andernach in 939. Otto appointed no replacement duke, bringing its separate
  government to an end. Later Franconian titles belonged to different local rulers and
  religious institutions.
