description: >-
  Cantref Talgarth was one of the separately governed shares of Brycheiniog in southern Wales,
  represented here from about 1045 to the Norman conquest in 1093. Talgarth lay near the older
  kingdom's traditional heartland, between the Welsh uplands and routes towards England. Its rulers
  belonged to the dynastic world of Welsh kings and princes, although surviving genealogies disagree
  about the partition and the relationships between individual rulers. Accounts associate the Talgarth
  share with Einon, alongside the portions called Tewdos and Selyf. These divisions should not be
  mistaken for a securely documented centralized kingdom throughout the period. Norman attacks under
  Bernard de Neufmarché eventually brought the region into the lordship of Brecon, while Welsh
  communities and traditions continued under the new rulers.
