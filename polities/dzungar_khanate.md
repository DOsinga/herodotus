description: >-
  The Dzungar Khanate was an Oirat Mongol state centred in Dzungaria, now northern Xinjiang, from 1634 to 1758. Its rulers united western Mongol groups and built a military monarchy whose leaders usually bore the title khong tayiji. Tibetan Buddhism supplied important religious ties and political legitimacy. Under Galdan and later rulers, Dzungar armies conquered the Tarim Basin, fought the Kazakhs and Khalkha Mongols, and briefly occupied Tibet. The state combined pastoral resources with agriculture, taxation of settled populations and firearm production. Its expansion made it a major rival of the Qing Empire. Succession struggles weakened the ruling house, allowing Qing intervention in the 1750s. Conquest, mass killing, disease and displacement devastated the Dzungar population and ended the khanate in 1758.
