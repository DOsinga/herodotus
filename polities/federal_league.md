description: >-
  The Federal League, or League of the Free Peoples, was a confederation of provinces in the
  Río de la Plata led by José Gervasio Artigas. Artigas, the leader of the Banda Oriental,
  opposed the centralist government in Buenos Aires and demanded a union of equal provinces.
  In 1815 his forces took Montevideo, and the provinces of Entre Ríos, Corrientes, Santa Fe
  and Misiones accepted him as Protector of the Free Peoples, with Córdoba joining briefly.
  Artigas ran the league from his camp at Purificación on the Uruguay River, issued a land
  reform and directed its armies and trade. A Portuguese invasion from 1816 conquered the
  Banda Oriental. After his defeat at Tacuarembó in 1820, his allies in Santa Fe and Entre
  Ríos made peace with Buenos Aires without him.
