description: >-
  The Eldiguzid state was a Turkic atabegate in northwestern Iran and the eastern Caucasus
  that lasted from 1136 to 1225. Its founder Eldiguz, a former Kipchak slave, received Arran
  from the Seljuk sultan of Iraq and married the widow of Sultan Toghrul II. As guardians of
  the young sultans, he and his sons Muhammad Jahan Pahlavan and Qizil Arslan governed in
  their name and soon held the real power in Azerbaijan, Arran and much of Jibal.
  Nakhchivan, Hamadan and Tabriz served as seats. The atabegs fought Georgia and the
  Shirvanshahs over the Caucasian frontier. After the last Seljuk sultan of Iraq died in
  1194 they ruled in their own right, but Georgian raids and the Khwarazmians weakened them,
  and Jalal al-Din captured Tabriz in 1225.
