name: Madurai Nayak dynasty
description: >-
  The Madurai Nayak kingdom governed much of southern Tamil country after Vijayanagara's defeat at
  Talikota in 1565 weakened imperial control. Its ruling family had begun as Vijayanagara governors in
  1529, and independence developed gradually rather than through a single formal break. Nayak kings
  ruled with ministers, military commanders and local chiefs, shifting their court between Madurai and
  Tiruchirappalli. Tirumala Nayaka strengthened the army and sponsored the palace and major temple
  works associated with Madurai. Rivalry with Mysore, Thanjavur and other neighbors repeatedly drew
  the kingdom into war. Rani Mangammal later governed as regent and negotiated with advancing Mughal
  forces. Succession disputes undermined the queen, Meenakshi. Chanda Sahib's intervention in 1736
  ended Nayak government and brought the kingdom under outside control.
