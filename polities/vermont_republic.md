description: >-
  The Vermont Republic was an unrecognised independent state in New England, between Lake
  Champlain and the Connecticut River. Settlers on the New Hampshire Grants, whose land
  titles New York refused to accept, declared independence in January 1777 and adopted a
  constitution that summer, the first in North America to ban adult slavery. Thomas
  Chittenden served as governor for most of the republic's life, and its assembly met in
  towns such as Windsor and Bennington. The Green Mountain Boys under Ethan Allen fought for
  the Revolution, but Congress would not admit Vermont because of New York's claims, and its
  leaders negotiated secretly with British Quebec. After New York gave up its claim in 1790,
  Vermont joined the United States as the fourteenth state in 1791.
