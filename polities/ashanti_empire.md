description: >-
  The Asante Empire was an Akan state centered on Kumasi in present-day Ghana, established after
  defeating Denkyira in 1701. Osei Tutu and his adviser Okomfo Anokye united neighboring communities
  around the Golden Stool, a sacred symbol of collective authority. The Asantehene governed with
  councils and subordinate chiefs, combining royal leadership with institutions that could restrain
  or remove rulers. Gold production, farming, regional commerce, and the trade in enslaved people
  supported expansion beyond the forest heartland. Organized armies fought neighboring states and
  repeatedly confronted British forces on the coast. Britain occupied Kumasi and exiled Prempeh I in
  1896, ending the independent government recorded here. Formal annexation followed later, while
  Asante kingship survived as a traditional cultural institution within modern Ghana today.
