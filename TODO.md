# Follow-ups

Things a PR or a record's note said would be done later, collected from the
descriptions of #1–#30 and checked against the data. Items that later PRs
already dealt with are left out. Remove an item when it is done.

## Questions raised while writing descriptions (#30)

- **Links to more specific articles:** Sophene, Zazzau, Chach, Ashdod,
  Ashkelon, Zand, Chola and the Alawi state link broad or redirected pages.
- **AANES:** its article reports integration into Syria in 2026; the record is
  still open-ended.
- **Equatorial Guinea:** the article reports a new capital in 2026; the point
  is still Malabo.
- **Independence questions:** Royal Hungary, the Kingdom and Lordship of
  Ireland, the Italian Social Republic, the Far Eastern Republic and the early
  Ukrainian SSR.
- **Protectorate periods:** Bukhara, Khiva, Tahiti, Muscat and Oman, Manipur,
  Korea and Afghanistan.
- **Possible occupation gaps:** Kotte over Jaffna, Sitawaka over Kandy,
  Baden-Durlach over Baden-Baden, Vietnam over Cambodia, Singhasari over early
  Bali, Iran and the Ottomans over Kakheti, and outside rule in Milan.
- **Identity and continuity:** Muisca as several confederations; the Sapaean
  kingdom as distinct from the Odrysian; Jushi's split into Nearer and Further
  states; Mecklenburg's narrower reunited periods; the later Reddi branches.
- **Endpoints to recheck:** Kongo, the Kazakh Khanate, Oyo, Theodoro,
  Tuʻi Haʻatakalaua, Vichy France, the Ghurids, Georgia, Lunda and Luba.
- **Dates to recheck:** Sam'al, Hồ, Sidon, Saruhan, Germiyan, Menteshe, Teke,
  Bidar, Rohana, Usan, Seibal, Coosa, Waka, Kaqchikel and Knossos.
- **Notes to recheck:** Soissons (457 and Majorian), Jülich-Berg (the move to
  Mannheim), Czersk (Warsaw's date and the last period), Verona (elections to
  different offices) and Egypt's 24th dynasty (who conquered Bakenranef).
- **Archaeological dates:** Azilian and Capsian may mix BP and BCE; sources
  differ for Adena, Merimde, Majiayao, Yarmukian, Helmand, Mehrgarh, Pukará,
  Jeitun and Bronze Age Ireland.

## Missing polities

- Khalkha Mongolia between Northern Yuan's end in 1635 and its submission to
  the Qing in 1691 (#14).
- The Oghuz successors in Kerman after the Seljuks of Kerman (1187) and in
  Khorasan after Sanjar (1153) (#15).
- The Ghurid chiefs before 1100 (#11).
- Smaller taifas and southern Italian states around 1000–1100, and Mértola,
  whose later periods mix local rule and an Almohad governorship (#18).
- Medieval Scandinavia, a consistent review of the Kalmar Union, the Sri
  Lankan kingdoms, more African states, more of the Americas, and smaller
  European polities (#11, #15, #18).
- Smaller Greek, Etruscan, Phoenician and Anatolian cities and leagues (#26),
  Tabal, the other Mycenaean palaces and the Terramare culture (#27).
- Considered and left out in #24, worth a second look: Wa and Yamatai before
  300, the Kaan dynasty at Dzibanché, small Roman client states annexed around
  70–100, and Julius Nepos' Dalmatia.

## Shares and successors the notes leave out

These notes say a government is not recorded. Some are minor by choice; each
needs a decision rather than silence.

- Anhalt-Plötzkau (1603), Masovia's Rawa share (1310), Ernestine Coburg-
  Eisenach (1572–1638), the Nassau counties before 1806, Nassau-Weilburg
  (1803), the Grand Duchy of Würzburg (1805–1814), and Lucca's commune,
  Castruccio's lordship and its Napoleonic and Bourbon successors.
- Arwad under the Seleucids, the Abydos dynasty in Egypt, Merodach-baladan's
  nine months in 703 BCE, the united monarchy of Israel, the other Mycenaean
  palaces, Xiang Yu's other kingdoms of 206 BCE, the later Yang states after
  Chouchi, Northern Liang's remnant at Gaochang (442–460), the Northern Satraps
  of Mathura, Kidarite rulers in Gandhara, and the Lombard dukes of 574–584.
- Cultures standing in for unrecorded polities: the Iberian towns, the Yayoi
  chiefdoms including Yamatai, and the Coles Creek culture after Troyville.
