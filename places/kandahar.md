note: "Old Kandahar lay a few kilometres west of the modern city, which Ahmad Shah Durrani founded in the 1760s. Indo-Greek rule in Arachosia ended about 100 BCE; Saka kings then held it under the Parthians, and the dates are estimates. Before the Zunbils broke away about 680 the region answered to the kings of Kabul despite Arab raids. In the tenth century Turkish commanders at Bost held Rukhkhaj in the Samanids' name. The Kartids held Kandahar from 1281, though Chagatai princes raised troops there in the 1310s. The Arghun ruled Kandahar from 1479, first as governors for the Herat Timurids and from 1507 in their own right, before taking Sindh."
